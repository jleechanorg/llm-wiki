## System / GM Observations

- **Multiplayer typing model** — All four players contribute in the same turn; the engine weaves their inputs into a single combined action. Distinct from AI Dungeon's turn-based format.
- **Copper rank system** — Two copper contracts are required to rank up to bronze. Silver and gold boards exist but are gated; a forbidden platinum board hangs even higher. The party spent most of the session trying (and failing) to bypass the gates.
- **Talk-to-DM feature** — Lets players ask the narrator out-of-character questions ("how many rats precisely are there?") without breaking the action. Demonstrated mid-sewer.
- **NPC autonomy** — Hestia the registrar actively blocks players, raters the platinum board, and bans Colt from the line. Ulla Finehair interjects as a senior justiciar keeping the newbies in line. NPCs feel like they have their own motivations, not just quest dispensers.
- **Inventory + pin system** — The party had to physically equip their copper rank pins. Colt used "Use" instead of "Equip" and was mocked. Florin payouts were inconsistent (some players got 25, Nick got 1,625) — apparently intentional class/reputation variance.
- **World map & journal** — The Wolfwood has its own hand-painted picture that gets attached to the journal entry. Creators can swap their own map art in.
- **Behind the scenes** — The team emphasizes NPCs are getting auto-generated backstories, worlds are being built with their own cultures and lore, and inventory is being tracked at a depth that goes well beyond what surfaces in the text.
- **The "two million hamsters" joke** — A viewer asked if the AI was secretly two million hamsters. The team's deliberate non-answer became a running bit.
