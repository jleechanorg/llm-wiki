# Vitiate

The Sith Emperor of SWTOR canon (also Valkorion), recurring across the SWTOR campaign lines as progenitor-antagonist.

**In the Alexiel campaign**: progenitor of the House of Vitiate that engineered [[Alexiel]]. He "waits in your dreams" — a persistent psychic presence whose connection, blended with [[Vaylin]]'s insanity, forms [[TheStatic]]. The pull toward the Dark Side is delivered as ambient inheritance rather than active temptation scenes.

**In the House of the Dragon bible** ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]): the Absolute Monarch and Patriarch of the [[GoldenLineage]]. He never abandoned the Sith Empire — he evolved it via [[ProjectApotheosis]], breeding superior vessels (his children) to replace the Dark Council's Old Guard. Resides in the **Citadel of Storms**, a floating fortress above Kaas City. His agenda is [[TheHarvest]]: a ritual to consume the galaxy, requiring his children to break its will first. He treats his children like prize hounds — feeding the strong, beating the weak — and plans to eventually consume all but the strongest of them. The only person he respects (and slightly fears) is [[Senya]]. In the played [[NocturneOldRepublic]] campaign, Nocturne ultimately consumes him at level 20 ("the unmaking of a god").
