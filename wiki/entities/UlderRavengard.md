# Duke Ravengard

Duke of [[BaldursGate]] (canonically Ulder Ravengard, Grand Duke and marshal of the [[FlamingFist]]), appearing in *The Hollow Zenith* ([[2026-08-04-saita-v3-hollow-zenith]]) as a primary diplomatic target of [[SaitaAethelgard]]. The source's interaction shorthand has Saita delivering velvet-gloved threats to him — praising the city's beauty while implying imperial legions would "teach your masons how to build walls that actually hold" — before pivoting back to grain tariffs, the signature move of [[WeaknessMaskingDiplomacy]].
