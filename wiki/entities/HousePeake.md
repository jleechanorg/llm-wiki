# House Peake

Westerosi house that destroyed the last [[HouseBaelarys]] enclave in Westeros — not from understanding of the magic they were killing, but from fear of the "silver-gold sickness" and hunger for the land. In retaliation, [[VisenyaTargaryen]] has systematically destroyed them, reducing the house to vassalage. Serves as the campaign's completed-revenge backstory demonstrating the "Silver Butcher" side of her [[SovereignGirlDuality]].
