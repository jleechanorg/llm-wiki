# Karlach — "The Mind Flayer Rebel"

In the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath: L12+ Barbarian (Wild Magic)/Illithid who made the ultimate sacrifice — becoming a mind flayer to defeat the Netherbrain. Despised by the city she saved and refusing to return to Avernus, she hides in the sewers of [[BaldursGate]], wielding Balduran's Giantslayer in the Bonespike Garb, surviving by consuming the brains of [[Astarion]]'s rogue spawn. She is the aftermath's resistance seed: a losing battle against fading humanity and rising illithid logic, positioned against the [[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]].
