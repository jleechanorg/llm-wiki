# Thay

Magocracy of the Red Wizards in the Forgotten Realms; in "[[2026-08-04-bumpkin-swordsman-rust-bound-blade|The Rust-Bound Blade]]" campaign it is the backstory antagonist state. During its aggressive territorial expansion twenty years before campaign start, Thay employed [[MasterVaelen]] as a frontline General whose Level 20 Gestalt presence guaranteed battlefield dominance — until the industrialized slaughter broke him and he deserted west.

The source contrasts Thayan power-production methods ("arcane engineering," "test-tube ritualism") with Vaelen's purely natural martial talent, and names [[SzassTam]]-class ancient undead as the archetypal mythic-tier (L13–20) exceptions to the [[Level12MortalCeiling]].
