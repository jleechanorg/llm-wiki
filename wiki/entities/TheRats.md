# The Rats

Bandit gang from [[TheWitcher]] canon (Ciri's gang in the books; foregrounded in the show's later seasons), serving as the supporting cast for the [[NocturneWitcherRat|Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign]]. Named members in the source: [[Giselher]] (boisterous leader), [[Mistle]], [[Kayleigh]], [[Reef]]. In the campaign framing they are the protagonist's social laboratory — "scaffolding, essential for a time but destined to be dismantled" — targets of the [[DataProbeSocialEngineering]] probe loop rather than true comrades.
