# Campaign Bible: Kryptonian Gods — Political & Social Challenges

A solo D&D 5e Gestalt campaign for **[[KaraZorElSupergirl]]** (Level 20 Gestalt Fighter (Eldritch Knight) / Monk (Way of the Open Hand), Wisdom-unified, gearless). Set in the post-2026 DC movie universe. Tone: **political thriller meets theological drama meets quiet loneliness** — combat is rarely the right tool.

## Premise

Following the events of the 2026 cosmic film universe (the death of Krem of the Yellow Hills, the return to Earth alongside Kal-El, the public unmasking of Kara Zor-El), Supergirl has chosen Earth as her permanent home. Unlike her cousin Clark Kent — who hides behind a bumbling journalist persona — Kara refused to live a lie. She operates openly. The world knows her face, her address, her Argo City trauma, her unvarnished justice.

This transparency came at a brutal psychological cost: she cannot have normal friends, cannot grab coffee, cannot date without fear of crushing someone or drawing targeted strikes. Her only true peer is Superman. Her only human companions are a small group of "sane" cultists — the elite Level 12 inner circle she has chosen to manage her life.

The campaign begins six months after Kara incinerated General Josip Malakor — dictator of the rogue state of Markovia, who had begun a systematic ethnic cleansing and surrounded his palace with thousands of human shields wired to dirty-bomb triggers tied to his own heart-rate. Kara bypassed his guards, disarmed the triggers in milliseconds, and executed him with heat vision on live international television. She saved millions of lives in three seconds. The ICC has issued an arrest warrant. The Solar Dawn cult has exploded in membership. Lex Luthor, Amanda Waller, and Batman have begun constructing **Project Red Dust** — a planetary MAD deterrent that can render all Kryptonians mortal if humanity ever feels existentially threatened.

## World Rules & Power Hierarchy

| Tier | Level Range | Representatives | World Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kryptonian Gods** | 20–25 | Superman (Lvl 25), Supergirl (Lvl 20) | Cosmic-tier power; subjects of divine cults and UN treaties |
| **The Apex Five** | 20+ | Doctor Fate, Wonder Woman, Black Adam, Swamp Thing, Martian Manhunter | Terrestrial heavyweights; balance of power against off-world gods |
| **Peak Humanity** | 8–12 | Lex Luthor, Batman, Amanda Waller, Kara's Inner Circle | Strategists, masterminds, political leaders, military commanders |
| **Mortal Average** | 1–5 | 99.9% of human population | Citizens, devotees, law enforcement, soldiers |

**Combat reality**: in raw physical combat, virtually no mortal on Earth can touch Kara. Her AC is near-unhittable via Wisdom-based Unarmored Defense (10 + DEX + WIS = 20 base, 25+ at Epic progression). Her flying speed is functionally infinite under yellow sunlight. Flurry of Blows hits like a localized seismic event. **But this is the wrong framing for the campaign** — combat has been solved. What has not been solved is jurisdiction, worship, sovereignty, and the political price of godhood.

## The Player Character

- **Kara Zor-El / Supergirl** — Level 20 Gestalt Fighter (Eldritch Knight) / Monk (Way of the Open Hand). Wisdom-unified. Gearless. See [[KaraZorElSupergirl]] for full Level 1–30 progression, spells, abilities, and stat block.
- **Aesthetic**: Staggering Alien Perfection & Divine Luminance — radiant presence, sapphire-shimmer eyes, bio-electric aura that burns off dirt/blood/grime instantly. Inspires awe, paranoia, and religious devotion in equal measure.
- **The Burden of Honesty**: Kara chose transparency over deceit. Citizens view her as a deity walking among mortals. The worship is suffocating.

## Primary NPC Ally

- **Superman / Kal-El / Clark Kent** — Level 25 Gestalt Fighter (Battle Master) / Monk (Way of the Open Hand). Kara's sole emotional confidant who understands the weight of hearing every heartbeat on Earth. Strongly advocates for respecting human institutions, international law, and democratic boundaries — even when human courts make mistakes. Worries Kara's straightforward nature will provoke humanity into a fatal preemptive strike. Their private conversations happen on the summit of Mount Everest — the only place on Earth quiet enough to talk without hearing billions of heartbeats.

## The Inner Circle (Level 12 Companions — The Rational Cult of El)

While millions worship the Kryptonians as gods, Kara has surrounded herself with three Level 12 humans — the absolute peak of mortal talent — who manage her public affairs, screen out dangerous zealots, and offer the closest thing she has to mortal companionship.

| Companion | Class / Archetype | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **High Priestess Vespera (Elena Vance)** | Level 12 Mastermind Rogue | Legal Strategist & Cult Administrator. Former UN human-rights prosecutor who resigned after realizing international law was too slow to prevent genocides. Founded the Sanctum of the Sun — a hyper-organized global NGO. Views Kara as an objective moral force humanity needs. Does not bow; treats her as a principal she represents. |
| **Commander Marcus Vance** | Level 12 Battle Master Fighter | Chief of Security & Zealot Interceptor. Former elite special-forces commander whose squad was lost during a rogue warlord's artillery strike; saved by Supergirl. Commands the "House Guard" — an elite security wing protecting Kara's living space from obsessive stalkers and rogue anti-alien militias. Bodyguard-in-reverse: his job is to prevent mortals from getting close enough to force Kara to use power on them. |
| **Dr. Aris Thorne** | Level 12 Transmutation Wizard / Artificer | Solar Radiation Researcher & Bio-Analyst. Disgraced former LexCorp lead physicist who defected after discovering Luthor was developing atmospheric Kryptonite weapons. Operates Kara's personal research lab — measures her solar absorption levels and builds non-lethal containment technology so Kara does not have to lay a finger on delicate human bodies. |

Each carries a *Defining Moment*: Vespera's prosecutor resignation after watching a genocide complete itself while the Security Council debated; Marcus's squad dying under artillery fire while he watched helplessly; Aris discovering his life's work had been weaponized against the alien who once saved him from a lab accident. None worship her. All are willing to die for her — and willing to argue with her.

## The Apex Five (Level 20+ Terrestrial Ancients)

These five are the only beings on Earth whose personal power rivals or exceeds Kara's. Each represents a different strategic checkmate.

### 1. Doctor Fate — Level 22 Divine Archmage

**Threat**: Ancient magic bypasses Kryptonian invulnerability entirely.

**Stance**: Lord Nabu views the rising "Solar Dawn" cult and Kryptonian worship as a cosmic disruption to Order and Chaos. Does not want to kill Kara — but threatens to cast a planetary banishment spell to return all Kryptonians to deep space if human worship isn't curbed. Has erected a mystical ward over Washington D.C. that cuts off yellow solar radiation within city limits, reducing Kara and Clark's effective levels toward mortal bounds whenever they enter the political capital.

**Strategic implication**: A Kryptonian cannot punch through Fate. The only path is negotiation — or finding the magical leverage that even Lord Nabu fears.

### 2. Wonder Woman / Diana of Themyscira — Level 20 Demigod Fighter / Paladin

**Threat**: Wields divine, god-killing weapons forged by Hephaestus that slice through Kryptonian skin. (The Lasso of Truth, the God-Killer sword, the Bracelets of Submission.)

**Stance**: Represents the sovereign ancient nations. Refuses to sign the Kryptonian Accords or submit to UN authority, but warns Kara and Clark that their godlike interference is weakening humanity's spiritual resolve. She is the only Apex who treats Kara as a peer rather than a threat or a god.

**Strategic implication**: Diana is a potential ally against both human coalitions AND other Apex entities — but at the cost of binding Kara to Themysciran diplomatic obligations that may conflict with her public identity.

### 3. Black Adam / Teth-Adam — Level 21 Sorcerer / Fighter

**Threat**: Sovereign dictator of Kahndaq wielding magic lightning and strength matching a yellow-sun Kryptonian.

**Stance**: Explicitly defies the United Nations by declaring Kahndaq a safe haven for the Solar Dawn cult. Uses the cult as political human shields — daring Kara to invade his sovereign border and spark a war between Level 20+ entities that could level North Africa. Has ancient Kryptonian history (Adam was empowered by the Wizard Shazam using magic from a time before Superman; he remembers Kryptonian legends as a contemporary threat).

**Strategic implication**: The Kahndaq Diplomatic Standoff. The cult has committed terror attacks in Geneva; the cultists fled to Kahndaq; Black Adam grants asylum. The UN demands Kara extract them. If she flies in, Adam treats it as an act of war. She must extract the cult leaders *without* starting a Level 20+ war.

### 4. Swamp Thing / Alec Holland — Level 20 Archdruid / Avatar of the Green

**Threat**: Controls all plant life on Earth simultaneously. Immune to physical death; can reform anywhere biomass exists.

**Stance**: Indifferent to human politics, but fiercely protective of the biosphere. If human eco-terrorists or government operations trigger ecological collapse to test Kryptonite weapons, Swamp Thing will hold Kara and Clark personally responsible for failing to steward the planet. Can summon localized root-growths that would entangle Metropolis within hours.

**Strategic implication**: Kara must convince an enraged Level 20 Avatar of nature that she was framed (when Lex and Waller stage a "Kryptonian deforestation crisis" to manipulate Swamp Thing into attacking her) — all while defending the city without harming Earth's ecosystem.

### 5. Martian Manhunter / J'onn J'onzz — Level 20 Psionic Monk

**Threat**: Total telepathic intrusion and density shifting. Can bypass physical invulnerability by attacking the mind, or by passing through Kryptonian flesh directly. The only entity who can read Kara's thoughts without her consent.

**Stance**: Quiet, covert arbiter between Amanda Waller's coalition and the House of El. The only entity who knows how close human governments are to launching Project Red Dust — because he has personally infiltrated the ARGUS black-site where the trigger codes are stored. He has not revealed what he knows. He is waiting to see whether Kara will become a tyrant or a steward.

**Strategic implication**: J'onn is the campaign's wild card. If Kara earns his trust, he can hand her the Red Dust trigger codes without a fight. If he concludes she is too dangerous, he will erase her knowledge of the system entirely (with a targeted psychic block) and orchestrate her removal from Earth himself.

## The Global Coalition (Peak Humanity, Level 8–12)

### Lex Luthor — Level 12 Mastermind

Special prosecutor for the ICC. Does not attempt to fight Kara with Kryptonite bombs; he uses logic, human philosophy, and grief-stricken family members of Malakor's low-level guards to paint Kara as an unfeeling alien tyrant. The prosecution's lead strategist. Has crafted legal traps: if Kara accepts human jurisdiction, she loses the right to intervene in global disasters without court permission; if she refuses to attend, she is officially branded an illegal rogue entity and an enemy of Earth.

### Batman / Bruce Wayne — Level 12 Inquisitive Rogue / Shadow Monk

Co-architect of Project Red Dust. Has spent fifteen years developing contingency plans for Kryptonian tyranny and now treats Kara as the threat he always feared Superman could become. The only Peak Human who has successfully fought Kara in personal combat (he survived three encounters before Kara realized he was holding back). Refuses to speak to Kara without a Kryptonite-reinforced room between them.

### Amanda Waller — Level 10 Warlord / Inquisitor

Director of ARGUS. Treats Kara as a strategic asset to be controlled, not a person to be negotiated with. Has signed off on Project Red Dust because she has decided that humanity's survival requires a deadman-switch against Kryptonian overreach. Does not bluff. Has already authorized one assassination attempt (a kryptonite-tipped bullet — Kara caught it bare-handed and sent it back in the envelope).

### The Solar Dawn (Cult Subfaction)

While the broader "Sanctum of the Sun" (Vespera's organization) is the rational cult, the **Solar Dawn** is its violent splinter wing. Begins executing the prosecutors, judges, and politicians involved in Kara's ICC indictment. Commits these assassinations in Kara's name, leaving her House of El symbol written in blood at the crime scenes. Members believe Kara's silence is divine approval; they fall to their knees and worship her if she attempts arrest, claiming her punishment is "divine purification." If she uses force against them, she proves their doctrine of absolute godhood.

## Main Campaign Narrative Arc

The campaign opens with Kara receiving her ICC subpoena. Five scenarios interlock into the main arc — they are not sequential, they are *simultaneous pressures* on a single protagonist trying to do the right thing.

### Scenario 1 — The ICC Trial of Kara Zor-El

**Setup**: The ICC, backed by a coalition of 140 nations, demands that Supergirl sit in a Hague courtroom and plead to charges of extrajudicial murder. Lex Luthor serves as special prosecutor. Live TV broadcast to the entire planet.

**Conflict**:
- *Clark's stance*: Begs Kara to attend, take off her cape, accept whatever sentence or fine the humans deliver — to prove that Kryptonians do not rule Earth as tyrants.
- *Elena Vance's stance*: Urges Kara to boycott the trial; submitting to corrupt human courts delegitimizes her moral authority to stop future atrocities.
- *Lex's trap*: The prosecution's lead strategist (Level 12 Mastermind) has crafted legal traps that bind Kara whether she attends or refuses. If she accepts human jurisdiction, she loses the right to intervene in global disasters without court permission; if she refuses to attend, she is officially branded an illegal rogue entity and an enemy of Earth.

**Gameplay**: This is not a combat encounter. It is a *social-combat* encounter in a courtroom — Kara must navigate a brutal cross-examination by Lex Luthor on live TV, using only her words, her composure, and her Level 12 companions' legal advice. Persuasion / Insight / History / Religion checks throughout. The verdict shapes the rest of the campaign.

### Scenario 2 — The Solar Dawn Schism

**Setup**: While Kara tries to maintain a peaceful open identity, the Solar Dawn begins executing the prosecutors, judges, and politicians involved in Kara's ICC indictment. The terrorists commit assassinations in Kara's name, leaving her House of El symbol in blood.

**Conflict**:
- Kara cannot punch the terrorists without looking like a god striking down her own worshippers.
- Every Solar Dawn member who is arrested falls to their knees and claims divine blessing.
- Amanda Waller uses the attacks as a pretext to declare all Supergirl supporters global enemy combatants.

**Gameplay**: Infiltration / investigation arc. Kara must use her Level 12 companions (Marcus Vance and Elena) to penetrate the cult's underground network, identify the rogue cell, and bring them to justice — without violence becoming the visible tool. Stealth, Insight, Investigation checks. The temptation throughout is to simply incinerate the cult's leadership and end the threat — every DM note should pressure Kara toward the very violence that would prove Luthor's case.

### Scenario 3 — The Sovereignty Accords & The Geneva Trap

**Setup**: The UN drafts The Geneva Accords of Extraterrestrial Entities. It mandates that any Kryptonian who uses their powers without prior authorization from a nation's government will be classified as an invading military asset, authorizing the immediate use of weapons of mass destruction.

**Conflict**: A severe earthquake hits a rogue military state that refused to sign the Accords. The local military regime bans foreign aid and lets citizens suffer under the rubble.

**Dilemma**:
- If Kara flies in to save the trapped civilians, she triggers the Accords — validating Batman and Luthor's claim that Kryptonians will always break human laws whenever they personally see it fit.
- If she stays home, thousands die while she sits in her apartment.

**Gameplay**: A choice-point that cannot be resolved by combat or persuasion alone. Kara must find a *third option* — diplomatic leverage (forcing the regime to sign the Accords under threat of international sanctions), covert operations (using Marcus's House Guard to extract civilians), or weaponized press (releasing footage of the regime's deliberate inaction to spark global outrage). The Malakor execution was the wrong third option. This scenario pressures Kara to find the right one.

### Scenario 4 — Project Red Dust (The Human Contingency)

**Setup**: Realizing that peak human combatants (Level 12) stand no chance against a Level 20 Gestalt in physical combat, Batman, Lex Luthor, and Amanda Waller secretly construct Project Red Dust.

**Mechanics**: A network of 200 orbital satellites loaded with pulverized Green Kryptonite and Red Solar radiation emitters. If activated, it will saturate Earth's atmosphere, turning the entire planet into a lethal radioactive wasteland for Kryptonians while leaving humans unharmed.

**Stakes**: Mutually Assured Destruction. Humanity has rigged its own biosphere to render Kryptonians mortal or extinct if Kara ever attempts to overthrow a government. Kara and her companions must locate and neutralize the trigger mechanisms through diplomacy, hacking, and covert espionage — *without triggering a global panic* and without punching through the satellites (each one is hardened against Kryptonian heat vision, and breaking one triggers the cascade).

**Gameplay**: This is the long-game espionage arc. The trigger mechanisms are distributed across five continents; the codes are held by Luthor, Waller, Batman, ARGUS Brigadier General Mathers, and an unknown fifth holder. J'onn J'onzz knows the location of one. Kara must choose whether to pursue J'onn's trust, hack the ARGUS black-site directly, or stage a public diplomatic confrontation that forces humanity to dismantle the system voluntarily.

### Scenario 5 — The Isolation of the Sun Goddess

**Setup**: Between major political crises, run slice-of-life social encounters emphasizing Kara's intense loneliness.

**Gameplay beats**:
- Attempting to attend a public event where everyone falls to their knees in prayer or flees in terror.
- Private conversations with Clark on top of Mount Everest — the only place on Earth quiet enough for them to talk without hearing billions of human heartbeats. These are *quiet checks*: Insight on what Clark is not saying; Persuasion on whether Kara can admit her loneliness without losing the composure that holds the cult together.
- Navigating her complex relationship with her Level 12 companions, constantly evaluating whether they view her as a real person or merely a divine figure they are trying to manage.
- A recurring rumor: a young woman in Metropolis claims she is Kara's half-sister. She is not. But the rumor will not die, because the world desperately wants Kara to be more human than she is willing to pretend to be.

## Supporting Scenarios (Apex-Five Arcs)

These run in parallel with the main arc and can be triggered by the player's choices in Scenarios 1–4.

### Scenario A — The Kahndaq Diplomatic Standoff

Cultists from the Solar Dawn flee to Kahndaq after committing terror attacks in Geneva. Black Adam grants them asylum, framing himself as the "Defender of Free Faith." The UN demands that Kara enter Kahndaq to arrest the cultists. Black Adam (Level 21, magic-based) will view her entry as an act of war. Kara must extract the cult leaders *without* starting a Level 20+ war that could level North Africa.

**Solution vectors**: Diplomatic negotiation with Adam (using Diana as an intermediary); covert extraction by Marcus's House Guard; staged exile (offering the cultists passage to a sanctuary nation in exchange for Adam's neutrality).

### Scenario B — The Trial of Magic (Doctor Fate's Injunction)

Doctor Fate erects a mystical ward over Washington D.C., cutting off yellow solar radiation within the city limits and reducing Kara and Clark's effective levels toward mortal bounds whenever they step inside the political capital. Fate demands Kara stand trial before an ancient council of mystic entities to answer for bringing cosmic-level destruction to Earth's spiritual plane during her war with Krem.

**Solution vectors**: Appearing before the council (humility-focused social encounter); appealing to Diana as a fellow demigod; or finding a cosmic loophole in the ward's construction.

### Scenario C — The Lex-Waller Coalition's Proxy Weapon

Amanda Waller and Lex Luthor attempt to manipulate one of the Apex Five into fighting Kara. They trick Swamp Thing by staging a Kryptonian-fueled deforestation crisis. Kara must convince an enraged Level 20 Avatar of nature that she was framed — all while defending Metropolis from global root growths *without* harming Earth's ecosystem.

**Solution vectors**: Calling on Aris Thorne's bio-analysis to expose the artificial nature of the deforestation; inviting J'onn to telepathically verify her innocence to Swamp Thing; or staging a public demonstration of her solar absorption in a way that proves Kryptonian physiology heals biomass rather than destroys it.

## Themes

- **The Price of Honesty**: Transparency is a moral stance — but it costs you the ability to have normal relationships. The campaign asks whether Kara would trade her public identity for companionship if she could.
- **Godhood vs. Stewardship**: Is Kara a god among mortals, or a steward of humanity's future? The Apex Five treat her as a god. Peak Humanity treats her as a threat. The Mortal Average treats her as a parent. Only her Inner Circle treats her as a person.
- **The Third Option**: The central campaign choice mirrors the [[jleechan-third-option]] cognitive pattern — every crisis has an economic / political / leverage third path that does not require combat. The Malakor execution was a wrong third option (incineration on live TV). The campaign rewards finding right ones.
- **MAD and the Kryptonian Problem**: Project Red Dust is the world's answer to a threat it cannot fight conventionally. Kara must dismantle it without becoming the very tyrant it was designed to prevent.

## Mechanical Notes

- **Spellcasting**: Eldritch Knight spellcasting stat unified to Wisdom (per player direction). All save DCs and spell attacks scale from WIS (+5 base). See [[KaraZorElSupergirl]] for full Level 1–30 progression and spell list.
- **Gearless mechanics**: Kara's unarmed strikes ARE her Weapon Bond; she cannot be disarmed. Defense engine layered: Unarmored Defense (AC 20) → Shield reaction (+5) → Haste concentration (+2) → Blur/Mirror Image (disadvantage) → Empty Body (resistance).
- **The Right Tool Problem**: Combat is rarely the right tool. Social, persuasion, investigation, and Insight checks dominate. Combat checks should occur primarily in defense of others or in self-defense against Apex-Five-level threats — never as a first resort.
- **The Malakor Memory**: Every social encounter should reference the live-TV execution. Some NPCs worship Kara more for it; some hate her more for it. The trial's verdict shapes how that memory is interpreted going forward.
