# Batiatus

Minor NPC in the [[2026-08-04-gaia-julia-v7-pax-caesara-golden-cage|Gaia Julia v7]] campaign bible, named in [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]]'s inner monologue as a financial dependent she idly considers ruining: "If I take away Batiatus's funding, I wonder how long it will take for his wife to try and poison him? Three days? Let's find out."

The name evokes Lentulus Batiatus, the lanista whose ludus at [[Capua]] produced [[Spartacus]] and [[Crixus]] — a continuity thread back to the v4 Gilded Trap setting, though v7's 44 BCE date is decades after the historical Third Servile War. His function in the fiction is to illustrate the campaign's thesis that Gaia's weapon is economic/social annihilation, not violence.
