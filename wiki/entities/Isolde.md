# Isolde

An NPC in the teen-era Aizen campaign who operates as a **guilt lever** against [[SosukeAizen]]: the [[ChallengeCompensation]] table lists "moral traps (Isolde guilt forces suboptimal plays)" and a family-bounty plot thread where "Isolde tracks w/ guilt-lever" (600XP + the Sosuke ledger). She is one of the few counters aimed at Aizen's psychology rather than his stats.
