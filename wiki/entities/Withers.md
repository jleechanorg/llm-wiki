# Withers (Jergal)

In the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath: disgusted by the chaotic, evil outcome of his intervention, Withers abandoned the party and [[BaldursGate]] entirely. He sits in a forgotten tomb recording the inevitable collapse of the Sword Coast into the Book of the Dead — the campaign's built-in neutral chronicler, framing the [[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]]'s reign as doomed from the divine perspective.
