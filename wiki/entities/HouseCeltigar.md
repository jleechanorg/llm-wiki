# House Celtigar

Crackclaw/Claw Isle house of the ASOIAF setting, appearing in the campaign visenya v8 loot table as a blackmail target: *The Celtigar Harbor Ledger* (d10 roll 3) is an **Evidence Cache** granting "absolute financial leverage over House Celtigar, forcing a 5,000gp tribute into your treasury."

Mechanically the relic converts directly into both treasury (offsetting [[Vermithrax]]'s 1,000gp/month upkeep in the [[DraconicScarcityLedger]]) and, implicitly, a Blackmail Cache worth +10 SP on the [[SovereignShadowTracker]] — a clean example of the campaign's loot-as-leverage economy around [[VisenyaBelaerys]].
