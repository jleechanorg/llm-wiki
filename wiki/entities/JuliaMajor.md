# Julia Major ("The Socialite")

Eldest sister (25) in the Gaia v3 [[PaxCaesaraEmpire]] campaign; daughter of [[JuliusCaesar]]. Face of the court — manages the festivals and the games. Framed as **vain and shallow, "but with a steel trap for gossip"** — i.e., an underestimated intelligence asset. Classified among "The Pawns" (sisters) versus "The Heirs" ([[Caesarion]], [[Octavian]]), a gendered succession split [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] intends to break.
