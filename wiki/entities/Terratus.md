# Terratus

The world of [[TyrannyGame|Tyranny]]; the campaign is set in its southern region, The Tiers, in Year 431 TR ("The Year of the Sword"), after [[Kyros]]' conquest. In [[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension|The Architecture of Ascension]], the protagonist Nocturne views Terratus "not as a home, but as a deeply flawed schematic" — the object of her [[RealityRefactoring]] ambition.
