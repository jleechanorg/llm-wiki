# The Voices of Nerat

Archon of Secrets in [[TyrannyGame|Tyranny]], leader of the [[ScarletChorus]] — a fractured, many-voiced consumer of minds. In [[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension|The Architecture of Ascension]], Nocturne's mirror-persona presents as "the chaotic prodigy, the admiring student of his madness" — the [[JohanLiebert]]-style reflection tuned to Nerat's appetite, per [[DependencyLoopManipulation]].
