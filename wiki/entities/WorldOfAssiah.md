# World of Assiah

Campaign world of the *alexiel assiah* campaign. In Year 1509 it is "a realm choking on its own history": earth scarred by arcane artillery, skies perpetually gray with ash, mortal races caught between the [[CelestialImperium]] and [[Lucifer]]'s revolutionary Host.

History in three eras: **Age of Foundation** (1–500, conquest of chaos, [[EdictOfChains]] slavery, building of [[Aeterna]]), the **[[AbyssalWar]]** (500–1409, engineered demonic crucible), and the **[[CenturyOfSchism]]** (1409–1509, celestial civil war). The setting's defining twist is the [[EngineeredCrucibleWar]]: the Creator designed the demon war as a permanent culling mechanism for the Empyrean bloodlines.

Run under a [[HighPersistenceWorldState]] contract — every player action permanently alters the world state.
