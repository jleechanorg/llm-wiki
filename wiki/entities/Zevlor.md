# Zevlor

Baldur's Gate 3 canon NPC — hardened tiefling commander — referenced in the [[NocturneSosuke]] v5 "Velvet Cage" campaign as an example of a battle-tested leader who *should* dismiss a Level 1 sixteen-year-old but doesn't, due to the [[PredatorPresenceIntimidation]] hook. Alongside Aradin, he anchors the early-act social terrain at the Nautiloid crash region.
