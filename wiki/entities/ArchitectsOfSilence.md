# The Architects of Silence

Ancient Valyrian mage-hunters ("Mage Slayers") and the deep-threat faction of *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]). They exist to hunt surviving Apex-line sorcerers like [[VisenyaBelaerys]].

## Mechanics

They are wired to the [[AscensionMeter]]: overt uses of the Apex Aura raise the meter, and a high meter *attracts* the Architects — making them a systemic consequence of power display rather than a scripted encounter. Together with [[RhaenyraTargaryen]]'s paranoia they form the campaign's twin punishments for shining too bright, opposite the "die a peasant" failure mode of staying too dim.
