# Johan Liebert

Antagonist of Naoki Urasawa's *Monster*; cited in the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign source as one of four composite templates for the [[SerpentKingArchetype]], contributing the "deep, philosophical, nihilistic evil" component. Sits alongside [[Griffith]] (ambition), [[TywinLannister]] (pragmatism), and [[SosukeAizen]] (superiority) in the archetype blend.
