# Mark Antony

In the [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] timeline, Antony never breaks with Caesar — he remains the dynasty's loyal sword.

- Likely commanded the agents who arranged the 42 BCE "unfortunate accident" that killed [[GaiusOctavius]].
- Co-leads the Wars of Unification (40–30 BCE) with [[GaiusJuliusCaesar]]: crushing the Pompeian remnants in Spain and the triumphant Parthian campaign avenging Crassus.
- Never becomes Cleopatra's consort in this world — [[CleopatraVII]] is Caesar's empress.
