# Zariel

Archdevil ruler of Avernus in canon D&D lore (fallen angel; central to Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus). In the [[NocturneSosuke]] BG3 v3 campaign she is the intended recipient of the [[CassalanterFamily]]'s soul tithe at [[SunderStoneVilla]] — consistent with the Cassalanters' canon Waterdeep pact with Asmodeus being reworked here toward Zariel. The campaign's endgame ("From Ruin to Archdevil") implies Nocturne ultimately steals an archdevil's ritual and claims infernal authority, making Zariel-tier power the arc's destination.
