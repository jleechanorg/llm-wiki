# Vermithrax

Medium-sized dragon bonded to [[VisenyaBelaerys]] in the campaign visenya v8 continuation. Unlike the trivially obedient mounts of most power fantasies, Vermithrax is governed by the [[DraconicScarcityLedger]]:

- **Command**: complex maneuvers require a bonus-action vocal command in High Valyrian — Charisma (Animal Handling) vs DC 14, with a +4 flat bonus from Visenya's "Sovereior Singularity" trait [sic].
- **Upkeep**: 1,000 gold pieces per month for protein and concealment security.
- **Failure state — Famine of Fire**: if the faction treasury hits 0 at the end of an operational loop, Vermithrax breaks containment and hunts smallfolk cattle, inflicting −20 regional popularity/stability and triggering a counter-audit by [[JacaerysVelaryon]]'s investigators.

**Naming note**: the v3 campaign bible ("The Apex of Ash: The Sowing") centers on claiming **Vermithor**, the Bronze Fury; v8 names the dragon **Vermithrax** (an apparent nod to *Dragonslayer*'s Vermithrax Pejorative). Whether this is a rename, a different dragon, or drift between campaign versions is unresolved.
