## Overview

Lady Nocturne Ravencrest appears in the [warcraft3-nocturne-deceivers-crown](../sources/warcraft3-nocturne-deceivers-crown.md) campaign as the protagonist.

## Profile

- **Name:** Lady Nocturne Ravencrest
- **Age:** 16
- **Race:** Human (Stormwind cadre, ancient northern lineage)
- **Class:** Paladin, Oath of the Deceiver (custom)
- **Starting Level:** 6 (military prodigy)
- **Public Rank:** Daughter and Heir of Count Marcus Ravencrest of Stormwind; Captain-Commander of the 4th Stormwind Expeditionary Detachment
- **Shadow Rank:** Bound Blade of Mal'Ganis
- **Moral Framework (Internal):** Lawful Evil — ruthless, highly structured, obsessed with order, hierarchy, and absolute personal authority
- **Appearance:** Strikingly beautiful in a cold, aristocratic way — pale skin, sharp features, long dark hair usually bound for war, eyes that already look older than her face

## Appearance

Nocturne presents as a **sixteen-year-old human paladin-princess of the old northern Stormwindian nobility** — slender, narrow-waisted, pale-skinned, with **raven-black long hair pulled back for war and court** and a posture of *carved-stone still*. She is the youngest Captain-Commander in Stormwind's modern history and carries the bearing of an heir who has already buried half her generation: too old for sixteen, too controlled for any age, never quite *warm* the way a paragon is expected to be. Her visual signature is the **Cuirass of the False Martyr** (burnished Stormwind ceremonial plate, silver-inlay ravens in flight around a central palm, high-collared gorget, pale silk lining that darkens at the seams with the residue of her own prior wounds), the **Raven Signet** (gold raven-in-flight with ruby eyes, engraved "Two Coats, One Crown," worn on her left ring finger), and the **Codex of the Pale Crown** (pale green leather, two crowns silver-and-grey interlaced on the spine, cold to the touch, bound to Sylvan the Pale). Her color palette reads as **ice, amethyst, and onyx, with one warm accent — the rubies in the Raven Signet** — and her voice is low-register, measured-cadence Stormwindian court diction laced with legal, military, and ecclesiastical Latin. By the standards of Lordaeron-era Azeroth's noble bloodlines, she is *literally* one of the most striking women of her generation — environmental fact, not a mechanic; it sets the texture of social encounters (why House Prestor courts her, why the Kirin Tor flatters her, why refugees trust her at sight) but has no roll, no bonus, no meter. Full canonical portrait anchor and "for DM tooling" image-prompt traits are in **Section 12.1 of the bible**.

## Personality

- **Core Motivation:** Absolute Sovereignty. To build a system of power where she can never be dominated, manipulated, or discarded by mortal kings or demonic lords.
- **Greatest Fear:** Loss of Autonomy / Being a Disposable Pawn. Rooted in witnessing the helpless political degradation of minor noble houses during the First and Second Wars.
- **Temperament:** Coldly Magnetic, Unshakeable, Clinical, Aristocratic, Imperious.
- **Quirks:** Adjusts her high-collared steel gorget with a single index finger whenever contemplating an execution or betrayal. Never drinks wine in public unless she poured it herself or watched her host drink first. Speaks in a low, measured tone that forces others to lean in to hear her.
- **Demeanor Under Stress:** Unnatural calm. Her pulse slows, her speech becomes exceptionally polite, and her tactical focus becomes absolute.

## The Sovereign Decoupling Principle (load-bearing)

Nocturne's personal martial prowess, divine/necrotic smites, armor class, and spellcasting efficiency are intrinsic to her own prodigial strength and dark pact. They are completely independent of public opinion. If her treason is exposed, she loses institutional power — noble rank, lands, titles, army commands, and political protection — but her personal combat stats, lethality, and class capabilities remain 100% intact. Exposed or exalted, she is an absolute terror on the battlefield.

This is the explicit response to the "no global aggregate trackers" pitfall: combat capability and political standing are decoupled.

## The Pact (with Mal'Ganis)

Mal'Ganis's three demands:
1. Help unmake Arthas Menethil — not as a simple corpse, but as a tool or a broken thrall.
2. Treat the Scourge as sentient beings with their own hierarchies. Some will be destroyed. Many more will be brought to heel.
3. Deliver a human kingdom, corrupted quietly from within, ready to become something better than either the noisy Scourge or the failing Alliance.

Shared long-term conspiracy: rise in both human and shadow power → defeat or enslave portions of the Scourge → break or dominate Arthas → seize shadow control of a major human kingdom → transition it into a demon-dominated nation under Mal'Ganis's (and Nocturne's) influence.
