# Nocturne Ravencrest

Protagonist of [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]] (Warcraft 3 campaign line). Sixteen-year-old daughter of Count [[MarcusRavencrest]] of Stormwind; Captain-Commander of the [[FourthStormwindExpeditionaryDetachment]].

## Dual identity

- **Daylight**: paladin of the [[SilverHand]] — pious prodigy, beloved commander, courted by [[HousePrestor]], admired by the [[KirinTor]].
- **Midnight**: the Bound Blade of [[MalGanis]], partner (not servant) in the [[DualCustodyPact]], running the shadow stack of [[DualStackGameplay]].

## Goal

Absolute Sovereignty — founding the [[PaleCrown]], a living-undead nation with herself on its only throne, pawn of neither mortal kings nor demonic lords.

## Mechanics

Paladin-sorcerer hybrid (divine + necrotic smites). Governed by the [[SovereignDecouplingPrinciple]]: her combat capability is intrinsic and survives full public exposure of her treason; only institutional power is at stake.
