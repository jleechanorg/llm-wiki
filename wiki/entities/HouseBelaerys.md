# House Belaerys

Extinct Valyrian dragonlord family in the *Apex of Ash* canon (campaign visenya v2) — the most magically potent line of the Fourteen Flames, whose blood makes "standard Targaryens look plain."

- In this campaign's canon, the family attempted **Apotheosis** — a ritual to become gods — and that act triggered the Doom of Valyria.
- Sole survivor: [[VisenyaBelaerys]], whose Tier V epic boon (*Eternal Apotheosis*) represents completing the family ritual.
- The **Unfriendly Remnants** — hostile survivors or successors tied to the family's legacy — track Visenya; each use of [[BelaerysAllure]] grants them +5 to locate her (*The Prickle*).
- Family signet ring survives as Visenya's hidden proof of lineage.

Contrast with the [[ValyrianFreehold]] as depicted in the Sariel Valyria campaign, where the pre-Doom era is framed as a 40-family civil war — separate campaign canons.
