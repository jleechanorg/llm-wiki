NPC in the v6 campaign "The Winter Web." One of the "oblivious teenagers" surrounding the incognito [[VisenyaTargaryen]]. She identifies him as a social fault line: suggesting to [[CatelynStark]] that "Jon looks more Targaryen than Stark" is one of her planned entertainment vectors — a pointed tease given the campaign's Targaryen-victory alt-history, where the parentage secret carries different stakes.
