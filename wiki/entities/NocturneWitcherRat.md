# Nocturne (Witcher Season 4 incarnation)

Third incarnation of the Nocturne campaign line (cf. [[NocturneRavencrest]], [[NocturneSosuke]]): a 16-year-old College of Swords Bard hiding among [[TheRats]] in [[TheWitcher]] setting. Embodies the [[SerpentKingArchetype]] — two masks ([[DataProbeSocialEngineering|Quiet Rat probe layer]] + Dispassionate Strategist planner), with core drives of [[PortableAbsolutePower]], [[SurgicalDomination]], and supremacist certainty. Conceals a conspicuous appearance via deliberate grime and sullenness ([[MundaneIllusionDisguise]]). Personality is an explicit composite of [[Griffith]], [[TywinLannister]], [[SosukeAizen]], and [[JohanLiebert]].
