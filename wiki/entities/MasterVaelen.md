# Master Vaelen

Central anomaly of the "[[2026-08-04-bumpkin-swordsman-rust-bound-blade|The Rust-Bound Blade]]" campaign (bumpkin swordsman). A **pure martial prodigy** — no divine bloodline, no Thayan arcane engineering — who naturally shattered the [[Level12MortalCeiling]] and achieved a flawless **Level 20 Gestalt** by age twenty.

## History

- Served as a frontline General for [[Thay]] during its aggressive territorial expansion ~20 years before campaign start; his presence alone guaranteed Thayan battlefield dominance.
- **The Sunder**: psychologically broken by the industrialized slaughter he executed for the Red Wizards, he deserted, fled thousands of miles west to the borderlands outside [[BaldursGate]], and buried his identity.
- Now runs a modest rustic dojo in the Western Kingdom whose brutal curriculum (calibrated to his own L20 baseline) washes out 99% of students; exactly twenty have graduated ([[TwentyDisciplesMatrix]]).

## Mechanical signature

His guilt acts as a permanent subconscious anchor: internal matrix hardwired to Level 20 Mythic, output locked to Level 12 Gestalt — the global mortal maximum. See [[VaelenCalibrationCrisis]] for the resulting perception paradox (he believes he is sluggish and failing; the world sees a walking god). This earnest, broken calibration produces his defining deep humility.
