# Mistle

Member of [[TheRats]] in [[TheWitcher]] canon. In the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign source she is a named target of the protagonist's validation-as-leverage technique: Nocturne praises "one single, efficient movement" within Mistle's clumsy knife-work to make her feel seen — a worked example of [[DataProbeSocialEngineering]].
