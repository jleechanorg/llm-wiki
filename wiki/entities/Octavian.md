# Octavian ("The Wolf")

Second heir (19) in the Gaia v3 [[PaxCaesaraEmpire]] campaign; [[JuliusCaesar]]'s blood-nephew adopted as son. **The Administrator** — "He runs the city while Caesar runs the world." Cold, sickly, and utterly brilliant; "calculates human lives like an abacus."

Uniquely among the Brood of Venus, he **fears [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]]**: "He doesn't know what you are, but his instincts scream that you are the predator in the room" — positioning him as the campaign's most dangerous antagonist-by-perception, the one sibling whose read on the protagonist is correct.
