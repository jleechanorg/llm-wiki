# Zylra

Backstory figure in the [[NocturneSosuke]] v5 campaign: the handler who controlled Nocturne during two years of exploitation prior to the campaign's start (details in the source are not reproduced in this archive; the protagonist is a minor). Narratively, Zylra functions as the origin of Nocturne's "porcelain doll that has learned to hate" characterization — the source of her resentment and her drive toward power reclamation via the [[Malcanthet]] pact.
