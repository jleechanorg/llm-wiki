# Gerrick the Smith

The Anchor (Mortal Tie) NPC of *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]). A massive, kind-hearted brute of a Dragonseed on [[Dragonstone]] who believes [[VisenyaBelaerys]] is just a clever peasant girl.

## Function

Symmetric protection pact: he protects her from physical threats; she secretly protects him from magical and political ones. In the opening scene he steps forward to shield her from [[Vermithor]] — trembling, clutching a rusted hammer — creating the inciting choice. Option A of the opening binds him closer by making the dragon appear to spare "the brave smith."

Structural role matches the recurring mortal-anchor archetype in these campaign bibles: an ordinary, morally clean companion who humanizes a hidden-apex protagonist and gives the deception stakes.
