## Overview
Senya is Nocturne's mother in the Nocturne Old Republic campaign line. The House of the Dragon bible explicitly **retcons her away from SWTOR canon**: she was never a Zakuul Knight, but the former **Commander of the Imperial Guard**, whose rigid discipline and combat prowess impressed Emperor [[Vitiate]] — she is the only person he respects (and slightly fears).

## Campaign-start role (House of the Dragon bible)
- **Title:** High Justice, the Executioner of Traitors
- **Domain:** The **Sphere of Justice**, a secret police force hunting disloyal Sith
- **Relationship to Nocturne:** Cold love. She taught Nocturne to fight to survive their father, and urges restraint of the "Vaylin side" — if Nocturne loses control, Vitiate will destroy them.

## Played-campaign endgame (1644-entry campaign)
- **Title:** Sovereign of Judgment, golden aura
- **Gift from God-Empress Nocturne:** The **Well of Mercy** on Belsavis — a reality-anchor that can heal any soul, plus a palace of golden glass that will never fade
