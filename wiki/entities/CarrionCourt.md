## Overview
The Carrion Court is a nomadic war-band within the [ShatteredHost](ShatteredHost.md) that follows the law of the strong, functioning as a pure chaos force constantly raiding.

## Leadership
- Led by: Griselda the Carrion Queen

## Key Facts
- Type: Nomadic war-band
- Parent: [ShatteredHost](ShatteredHost.md)
- Philosophy: Law of the strong
- Activity: Constant raiding

## Relationships
- Part of: [ShatteredHost](ShatteredHost.md)
- Opposes: [CelestialImperium](CelestialImperium.md)
