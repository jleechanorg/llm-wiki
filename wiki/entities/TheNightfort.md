# The Nightfort Ruins (The Wall)

Gazetteer location VI in the campaign visenya v8 bible. An "ancient, oppressive cathedral of fractured grey granite, weeping black ice, and forgotten history" beneath the Wall, with a bleeding weirwood in the yard.

- **Sub-locations**: The Cook's Hole, The Gate of the Black Gate, The Lord's Solar, The Ice-Cells.
- **Hazard — *The Primordial Cold***: the Old Gods' baseline safeguard on the courtyard. Without frost-ward or winter gear: DC 16 Constitution save every 10 minutes; on failure, 2d6 cold damage + one Exhaustion level as unnatural frost freezes the character's "blood-resonance."

Canonically (ASOIAF) the Nightfort is the oldest and most haunted castle on the Wall and home of the Black Gate; the v8 bible mechanizes that dread as a saving-throw attrition zone. Ties the [[VisenyaBelaerys]] Dance-era storyline to the far North.
