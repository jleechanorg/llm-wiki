# Sariel Ash

Protagonist of the [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]] campaign. A Level 6 combat prodigy: **The Sovereign Shadow / Dread-Wyrm Assassin** ([[DreadWyrmShadow]] gestalt archetype), unacknowledged Great Bastard serving as the Sovereign Left Hand of [[HouseVaelaros]].

- **Alignment**: Lawful Neutral (Pragmatic Sovereign); **INTJ** — continues the operator's serpent-mastermind protagonist pattern (cf. [[NocturneSosuke]], [[NocturneRavencrest]]).
- **Motivation**: Absolute Autonomy — never again be an instrument for another sovereign's advancement.
- **Fear**: Absolute containment (bloodline lock / stagnant marriage contract), rooted in her mother [[DaenysBelaerys]]'s political erasure.
- **Assets**: mechanically weaponized beauty ([[BeautyAsWeapon]]), a [[LevelScaledDragonCompanion]] destined for 2× Balerion scale, her father's cold indifference, and a secret network of loyal siblings.
- **Tells**: finger-tap in a resting dragon's heartbeat cadence under stress; molten-gold ring around violet pupils pulses when tactically cornered.
- **Goal**: reject her disposable-weapon role and lead [[HouseTargaryen]]'s [[VanguardExodus]] to Westeros ahead of the [[FortyFamiliesCivilWar]].

Not the same character as [[Sariel]] (angel persona from other campaign lines) — shared first name only.
