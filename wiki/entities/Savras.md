# Savras

Forgotten Realms god of divination and fate, the All-Seeing. In the Divine Deception V0 campaign prompt ([[2026-08-04-god-campaign-prompt-v0-divine-deception]]) he shares [[Oghma]]'s detection role: at the **21–50% Suspicion** band of the [[DivineDissonance]] meter, Savras begins passive scrying on the disguised Rank A player. His inclusion makes divination itself a threat surface for the [[MaskedPowerSmurfing|smurfing]] deity — probability violations are exactly what an all-seeing fate god notices.
