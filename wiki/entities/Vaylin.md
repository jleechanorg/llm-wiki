# Vaylin

Daughter of [[Vitiate]]/Valkorion in SWTOR canon, famed for immense raw power and instability. In the [[2026-08-04-alexiel-swtor-siren-master-bible|Alexiel campaign]] she is [[Alexiel]]'s sister, and the "Vaylin Echo" — her insanity's psychic residue — is one of the two components of [[TheStatic]], the intrusive hum urging Alexiel toward cruelty.
