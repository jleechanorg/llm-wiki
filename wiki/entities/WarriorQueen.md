# The Warrior Queen

Legendary figure of the *Blades of the High and Low* setting. One thousand years before campaign start she unified the surface kingdoms through martial perfection and [[AetherPrimordialEnergy|Aether]] mastery, then vanished from recorded history. Her former advisors and generals transformed fragments of her research into the floating plateau-cities that became the [[Highlanders]]' seat of power — meaning the current oppressive [[HighlanderLowlanderDivide]] is an unintended legacy of her own work.

[[ValeriaVex]] is her reincarnation. Crucially, the Queen's life is remembered by Valeria as one consumed by administration, decrees, obligations, and the weight of governing — the exact life Valeria refuses to resume. The campaign uses the Queen as backstory gravity (institutions, relics, expectations, research fragments) rather than as a destiny Valeria must fulfill.
