# Sariel

Player character of the [[MilitaryProsecutorDoberman|Military Prosecutor Doberman]] campaign. 26-year-old Bard ([[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]], re-flavored modern) with chaebol-heiress beauty deliberately erased behind an anonymous black bodyguard suit and severe bun — [[BeautyAsWeapon]] inverted into camouflage.

Personality is the [[SerpentQueenArchetype]]: explicit blend of [[Griffith]] (transcendent ambition), [[TywinLannister]] (strategic pragmatism), [[AizenSosuke]] (charismatic superiority), and [[JohanLiebert]] (nihilistic evil). Operates through the [[TwoMaskFortress]] (Silent Servant public mask / Dispassionate Strategist private mask) while serving as bodyguard-handler to [[NohTaeNam]].

Drives: [[PortableAbsolutePower]] (blackmail, state secrets, offshore treasuries — "become the boardroom"), [[SurgicalDomination]] (frictionless imposition of will over ruling), and supremacist certainty toward both allies and enemies like [[DoBaeMan]].

Note: distinct from but continuous with prior serpent-mastermind PCs ([[GaiaJuliaCaesar]], [[NocturneSosuke]]) — same player fingerprint, new modern setting.
