# Gaius Julius Caesar (Divus Julius)

In the [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] alternate history, Caesar survives the Ides of March 44 BCE ([[DayOfAscension]]) and rules Rome as a living god for three decades (44–14 BCE).

- **Affliction**: the [[FatedMiasma]] — hereditary prophetic connection to the chaos of destiny; his life's goal is a stable single timeline.
- **Key acts**: suspends the Republic; divorces Calpurnia and marries [[CleopatraVII]]; passes the [[LexDeFilioAegypto]] making [[Caesarion]] his heir; orders the death of [[GaiusOctavius]]; conquers Parthia with [[MarkAntony]]; deified as *Divus Julius* (29 BCE); builds temples to Venus Genetrix and Isis.
- **Legacy**: founds the Julian-Ptolemaic dynasty fusing Roman iron and Egyptian mysticism; establishes primogeniture; dies of old age 14 BCE, passing throne and Miasma to Caesarion.
