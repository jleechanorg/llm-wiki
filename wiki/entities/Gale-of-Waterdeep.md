# Gale of Waterdeep

BG3 companion; in the *Empire of Shadow* dark timeline ([[2026-08-04-bg3-empire-of-shadow-dark-triumvirate]]) he is one of the [[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]]. L12+ Wizard (Evocation)/Sorcerer (Wild Magic) who reforged the [[CrownOfKarsus]] into pseudo-divinity rather than surrendering it to Mystra.

- Rules from a floating citadel carved from Ramazith's Tower, hovering above [[BaldursGate]].
- Uses citizens as test subjects for Netherese weaving experiments.
- Petrified [[Minsc]] for being "disruptive to the arcane equilibrium" and keeps him as a garden ornament.
- Gear: Robe of the Weave, Markoheshkir.

His arc is the campaign's "unbridled arcana" empire, counterpart to [[Astarion]]'s blood empire and [[Shadowheart]]'s shadow theocracy.
