# Aurelius Caesar

Player character of the "Iron Eagle: Vengeance" campaign (v3). Sixteen-year-old youngest son of Emperor [[JuliusCaesar]] in a divergent 71 BCE where the Republic is already dead. Known publicly as the "Sun of Rome"; operates under the merchant cover identity **Aurelius Varenus** during the [[Spartacus]] revolt (the source also renders his name once as "Aurelius Julia Caesar" — a drafting inconsistency).

## Build

- Level 6 Bard, [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]]
- Wielder of the [[Miasma]] ("The Divine Blood")
- Resources: 50,000 gold, black-ops Praetorian squad, secret Imperial mandate ("End the rebellion. Save the gold. Do not embarrass the family.")

## Character architecture

An instance of the [[ShadowPrinceArchetype]]: public "Prince of Pleasure" (vibrant, tactile, intoxicating showman) over a Lawful Evil "Golden Tyrant" core — the "Devouring Maw" of pure Appetite. ESFP-ENTJ hinge ("The Decadent Conqueror"). His defining weapon is [[BeautyAsWeapon]] ("the Blood of Venus"): a physically perfect, gold-and-light appearance that disarms enemies and forgives cruelties. Core compulsion: "The Sensory Feast" — craving intensity, bored by abstraction.

Contrasts with sibling-campaign protagonists [[SosukeAizen]] and [[Alexiel]], who run the same deception engine through weaponized *fragility* rather than weaponized *magnificence*.
