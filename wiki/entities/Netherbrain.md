# The Netherbrain

The canonical final antagonist of Baldur's Gate 3 — the Absolute, an elder brain empowered by the Crown of Karsus. In the [[SanguineArchitecture]] campaign it is already defeated when play begins: the [[DarkUrge]] **drained it and the Crown of primordial energy** as fuel for consuming [[Bhaal]]'s spark.

Module 0 opens with its colossal carcass hanging from the shoulders of the Balduran statue above [[BaldursGate]], "psionic tissue already flaking off in the salt wind" — the campaign's visual anchor for power already seized, matching the post-victory openings of the wiki's other apex-power campaigns ([[ProjectApotheosis]], the DOS2 Emerald Eclipse anthology).
