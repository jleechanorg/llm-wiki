# 4th Stormwind Expeditionary Detachment

The Stormwind regiment commanded by [[NocturneRavencrest]] as Captain-Commander in [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]]. Her principal public-stack institutional asset: a real army with real loyalty, earned in daylight and forfeit (per the [[SovereignDecouplingPrinciple]]) if her pact with [[MalGanis]] is ever exposed.
