# Helm

Forgotten Realms god of vigilance, duty, and guardianship — the Watcher. In the Divine Deception V0 campaign prompt ([[2026-08-04-god-campaign-prompt-v0-divine-deception]]) he is one of the three named Pantheon deities (with [[Mystra]] and [[Shar]]) alerted to "an intruder in their midst" if the player's [[DivineDissonance]] meter climbs too high — thematically apt casting, since Helm's portfolio is precisely watching for threats the other gods miss.
