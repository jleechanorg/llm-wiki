# The Serpent Queen

Protagonist of the "campaign bg1 nocturne" setup — real name hidden (Charname), age 16, Level 1 Bard (future [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]]), living as a scribe-acolyte in [[Candlekeep]] as foster daughter of [[Gorion]].

- **Concealed beauty**: jarring symmetrical looks deliberately buried under grime, wool robes, and a sullen demeanor — mundane invisibility, not magic.
- **Personality**: a "philosopher-queen in waiting" who sees the world as a flawed schematic needing a merciless editor. Runs the [[TwoMasksArchitecture]] (Dutiful Ward public interface + Dispassionate Strategist planning persona) over a hidden true self.
- **Archetype**: explicit composite of Griffith, Tywin Lannister, [[SosukeAizen|Aizen]], and Johan Liebert — see [[SerpentQueenArchetype]].
- **Drives**: Personal Transcendence via [[PortableAbsolutePower]] (culminating in "become the throne"), Surgical Domination (a single perfect *Suggestion* over a year of ruling), Supremacist Certainty (people as "fascinating but limited fauna").
- **Bloodline**: implied [[BhaalspawnEssence]] carrier — divinity inherited, distinguishing this incarnation from the engineered ([[Alexiel]]) and consumption-based ([[NocturneSosuke]]) instances of the [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]].
