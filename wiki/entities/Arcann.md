## Overview
Arcann is the Firstborn of the [[GoldenLineage]] and Nocturne's military-focused sibling in the Nocturne Old Republic campaign line.

## Campaign-start role (House of the Dragon bible)
- **Title:** High Marshal, Supreme Commander of the Imperial Military; controls the **Obsidian Armada**
- **Personality:** Stoic, brutal, obsessed with conquest; despises politics, wants a military junta
- **Injury retcon:** Lost his arm and half his face during the **Sacking of Coruscant**, taking a blow meant for the Emperor (NOT fighting the Outlander, per SWTOR canon); wears a cybernetic mask of Sith Alchemy
- **Faction:** Leads the [[ObsidianCircle]] (Neo-Imperialists) — abolish the Dark Council, total war with the Republic
- **Rivalry:** Sees Nocturne as undisciplined and dangerous — a "broken vessel" that should have been discarded

## Played-campaign endgame (1644-entry campaign)
- **Title:** God-Marshal of the Nocturne dynasty, obsidian skin
- **Gift from God-Empress Nocturne:** The **Aegis-Star** — a moon-sized dreadnought woven from living Vong bone and the gravitational logic of the Second Heart; his mobile throne and shield of the Outer Rim
