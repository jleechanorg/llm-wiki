# Gemini

Google's conversational LLM. In the Aizen campaign lineage, Gemini serves two distinct roles: **migration consultant** and then **acting DM**.

## Role in this wiki

- **Migration consultant** ([[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-mortal|mortal-era chat]]): when the player asked how to move an ongoing [[ChatGPT]] campaign to Gemini, Gemini prescribed the [[MasterPromptCampaignHandoff]] — a curated state-snapshot prompt (campaign overview, PC sheets, "previously on" recap, world state, quests, inventory) with an explicit role assignment and resume directive, explicitly advising against pasting raw chat logs.
- **Acting DM** for the god campaign: chat 1 (entries 1-1147, [[AizenSosuke]]'s rise from Level 12 manipulator in [[BaldursGate]] to God-King) and chat 2, which continued after chat 1 hit its length limit via an intra-platform [[CampaignContextMigration]] (campaign info + full prior log + style-only ASOIAF reference files + original prompts).
- Acknowledges handoff protocols explicitly before resuming play — a behavior the player relies on for seamless continuation.
