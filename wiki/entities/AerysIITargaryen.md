# Aerys II Targaryen

In Visenya v5, Aerys ordered the wildfire caches ignited during the crisis of 283 AC; the wounded [[RhaegarTargaryen]] returned just in time to countermand it. Rhaegar did not kill his father — he declared him "unwell" and confined him to the Maidenvault. **King in title, prisoner in reality**, still raving, and one of the few who perceives what [[VisenyaTargaryen]] truly is (the "Apex"). His survival keeps both the wildfire threat and a legitimacy crisis latent under the junta.
