# Giselher

Boisterous leader of [[TheRats]] in [[TheWitcher]] canon. In the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign he is one of the personalities the protagonist's soft-spoken "Quiet Rat" mask is calibrated against — his loudness is the foil that makes Nocturne's murmur a control mechanism (forcing others to lean in and listen).
