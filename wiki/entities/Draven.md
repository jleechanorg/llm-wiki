## Overview
Lord Draven is a new sibling introduced in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]) — the Savage archetype of the [[GoldenLineage]]. He was name-checked in the played [[NocturneOldRepublic]] campaign as "Sibling - Beastmaster."

## Key Details
- **Role:** Warden of the Untamed; controls the **Beast Riders** of the Empire
- **Personality:** Feral, quiet; connects better with animals than humans; rarely on Dromund Kaas, preferring the jungles of Dxun or the tombs of Korriban
- **Companion:** A pet Terentatek named **"Glory"**
- **Plot hook:** The Emperor's hunter — when a target flees into the wild, Draven is sent. He respects Nocturne "because he smells the predator in you."
