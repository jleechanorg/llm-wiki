# Lady Daenys Belaerys

Mother of [[SarielAsh]] in the [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]] campaign. A third-born of House Belaerys who was **politically erased and hidden away as an "unviable third-born tool" by an enemy house** — the formative trauma behind Sariel's greatest fear (absolute containment via bloodline lock or stagnant marriage contract) and her core drive for Absolute Autonomy.

Her name deliberately echoes Daenys the Dreamer of ASOIAF canon, but her function is inverted: not the prophet who saves House Targaryen, but the cautionary example of what the [[ValyrianFreehold]]'s breeding politics does to women treated as dynastic instruments.
