## Summary
The old steward of Riverside Manor who kept the Tethlar estate from collapsing during five years of Alexiel's absence. Gnarled, leans on a cane, perpetually anxious, but fiercely loyal and meticulous with the ledgers. Becomes Alexiel's chief of staff and right-hand man throughout the campaign.

## Key Quotes
> "I am Barnaby. I've been keeping the rats and the rain out as best I can for five years now, though the rain is winning." — Barnaby, introducing himself (Turn 0)

> "He spent the harvest on maps and the winter on wine, my Lady. That ceiling is held up by prayer alone." — Barnaby, on Alexiel's late uncle (Turn 2)

> "The first lords would have wept with pride to see this. My head is already spinning with the numbers — iron, grain, salted beef." — Barnaby, after seeing the Gloomstalker summoning (Turn 89)
