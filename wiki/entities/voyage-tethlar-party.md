## Summary
The named recurring NPC council that supports Alexiel's rise: Barnaby (steward/chief of staff), Hilliard (the silent pillar/bodyguard at the door), Hobb the miller (expedient proxy and source of lifestone), and Lady Ashbury (ally of convenience, then ally of love). Plays the role of a typical RPG party through NPC retainers rather than additional player characters.

## Key Quotes
> "The treasury is hollow, mistress. The King's interest in audits is born of a desperate need to centralize every copper of trade revenue." — Barnaby, on the Crown's audit motivation (Turn 87)

> "A dead surveyor on Tethlar land brings an army of investigators, my lady. It is better to be subtle. A bridge collapse on the coast road could force his party onto the inland tracks." — Hilliard, recommending the broken-road plan (Turn 87)
