# Triumvirate of the Absolute's Bane

Ruling faction of [[BaldursGate]] in the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath campaign. The three surviving "heroes" of the Netherbrain crisis — [[Astarion]] (blood/economy/underworld from Szarr Palace), [[Shadowheart]] (Sharran theocracy over the Lower City), and [[GaleOfWaterdeep]] (arcane pseudo-divinity above the city) — who carved the Sword Coast into three empires of blood, shadow, and unbridled arcana rather than liberating it. [[Minthara]] serves as Astarion's High General. Opposed (hopelessly) by [[Karlach]] from the sewers and the slain-then-raised [[Jaheira]]'s broken Harpers. Central embodiment of the [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] pattern.
