# Minthara

BG3 canon companion — drow paladin, originally of the Absolute. Active party member in the [[Voyage]] campaign ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]) alongside [[Shadowheart]], Vaelith, and two Gloomstalkers. Her presence alongside the Grove-raid opening (Turn 0: Nocturne declares intent to raid the Grove) signals the campaign runs the evil-aligned BG3 route — consistent with the player's documented preference for moral-corruption arcs.
