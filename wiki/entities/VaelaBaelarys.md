# Vaela Baelarys

Survivor of lost [[HouseBaelarys]], located by [[RhaegarTargaryen]]'s mystics in the ruins of Volantis (287 AC) and brought to court as "a figure of terrifying beauty." Married Rhaegar; gave birth to [[VisenyaTargaryen]] in 286 AC. **Vanished from the Red Keep six months after the birth** — official story a sickness; rumor says she "ascended" or returned to the shadow. Visenya is the only evidence she ever existed, making Vaela the campaign's central absent-mother mystery and the source of the [[ApexBloodline]].
