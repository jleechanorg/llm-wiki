# Malacharet

Patron entity — addressed as **"Mother"** — of [[NocturneSosuke]] in the [[Voyage]] BG3 campaign ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]). Source of Nocturne's infernal/abyssal bloodline manifestations (wings, horns) and presumably of abilities like Abyssal Performance and Fatal Seduction. Structural analogue of the divine-power sponsors in the Aizen lineage campaigns (compare Bane's role in [[SosukeAizen]]'s arc).
