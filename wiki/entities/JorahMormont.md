# Jorah Mormont

NPC in *The Apex of Ash: Shadow of the Mother* (campaign visenya v2), serving in [[DaenerysTargaryen]]'s camp during the Slaver's Bay campaign.

- Named explicitly as an admirer of [[VisenyaBelaerys]]: "Jorah's eyes linger too long."
- Visenya's interaction shorthand toward him is cold, clinical detachment — which "only fuels their obsession," making him a standing demonstration of the [[BelaerysAllure]] / [[BeautyAsWeapon]] dynamic operating even without the DC 18 charm mechanic.
- Visenya's read: "He wants a queen to serve; I simply want a world that stops trying to cage me" — casting him as an instance of the authority/ownership pattern her Breaking Authority compulsion targets.
