# Flaming Fist

Baldur's Gate's mercenary city guard, functioning in the [[Aizen]] god campaign as the principal mortal faction constraint on overt action. The [[Divine Avatar Autonomy Protocol]] explicitly lists "direct confrontations with established mortal factions like the Flaming Fist or Knights of the Shield" as an *important decision* requiring the avatar to confer telepathically with Aizen's divine consciousness before acting.

Avoiding Flaming Fist attention is also cited as a *pro* of telepathic Banite conversion: converting [[Bane]] cultists silently maintains Aizen's "narrative of subtle, pervasive order" instead of triggering open combat and public chaos.
