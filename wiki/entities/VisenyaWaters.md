# Visenya, Daemon's Great Bastard (campaign visenya v7)

Player character of campaign visenya v7. Distinct from [[VisenyaTargaryen]] (v4: trueborn daughter of Maekar, 209 AC) and [[VisenyaBelaerys]] (v2/v3: hidden Apex/Belaerys scion) — this incarnation is a **bastard**, but a [[GreatBastard]]: her father is Prince [[DaemonTargaryen]] and her late mother was herself a Targaryen lady, so both parents carry dragonlord blood.

## Profile

- **Age**: 16 at campaign start.
- **Appearance**: true-Valyrian looks with very strong royal features — she is visibly royal despite her illegitimate birth ([[ValyrianFeaturesAsStatus]]).
- **Dragon**: has just claimed a **medium-sized dragon** ([[DragonClaiming]]), instantly making her a strategic military asset.
- **Allegiance**: [[RhaenyraTargaryen]]'s Blacks in the [[DanceOfTheDragons]].

## Dramatic engine

Where v2/v3 Visenya hid her power ([[ApexBloodline]], subtle magic), v7 Visenya's power is overt from scene one: a bastard whose face and dragon both proclaim the royal blood her name denies her. The tension is legitimacy, recognition, and her father's ambitions — not concealment.
