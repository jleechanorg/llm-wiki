# Caesarion (Gaius Julius Caesarion / Imperator Caesarion Augustus)

Biological son of [[GaiusJuliusCaesar]] and [[CleopatraVII]]; renamed and legitimized by the [[LexDeFilioAegypto]] (43 BCE); second ruler of the Julian-Ptolemaic dynasty (14 BCE – 37 AD) in the [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] timeline.

- **Man of two worlds**: raised by a Roman general and an Egyptian queen — charming, intelligent, multilingual, patron of arts and sciences.
- **Golden Age** (14 BCE – 10 AD): Egyptian wealth funds empire-wide public works; [[Alexandria]] rises as second capital; [[CultOfSerapisJupiter]] becomes de facto state religion.
- **The curse**: more deeply afflicted by the [[FatedMiasma]] than his father — visions more vivid and chaotic; from ~10 AD the curse deepens and he grows erratic (source truncates during this decline).
