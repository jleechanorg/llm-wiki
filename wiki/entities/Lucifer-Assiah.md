# Lucifer, the Morning Star

Co-antagonist/co-protagonist of the [[WorldOfAssiah]] setting. For 900 years the [[CelestialImperium]]'s most beloved general, personally holding the gates of [[Aeterna]] through the [[AbyssalWar]] alongside his brother-in-arms [[Raziel]].

**The Revelation (Year 1400)**: extracted from a dying Demon Prince the truth that the demons are the Creator's deliberate culling mechanism ([[EngineeredCrucibleWar]]). After a Silent Decade of secretly confirming the evidence and converting his veteran legions, he declared rebellion in Year 1409, purged Imperial loyalists in a single night, and launched the [[CenturyOfSchism]]. In 1412 he shattered the Imperial slave colonies ([[MarchOfBrokenChains]]) to absorb the freed mortal races — a liberation that doubles as infantry recruitment, consistent with his "ruthless, revolutionary Host."

His arc — champion → cosmic disillusionment → god-indicting revolutionary — fits the operator's [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]] taste for protagonists who turn on the divine order itself.
