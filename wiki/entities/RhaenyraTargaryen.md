# Rhaenyra Targaryen

Claimant queen in the [[DanceOfTheDragons]] (129 AC onward), leader of the Blacks, wife of [[DaemonTargaryen]]. Appears across the Visenya campaign line: in v3 she calls the Sowing that lets [[VisenyaBelaerys]] claim Vermithor; in v7 she gains [[VisenyaWaters|Visenya]] — her husband's dragonriding Great Bastard — as "a serious battle power" on her side.

Her perpetual strategic problem, and the hook both campaign versions exploit, is a dragon deficit against the Greens: she needs riders, which makes exceptional claimants (dragonseeds, bastards) suddenly valuable and dangerous.
