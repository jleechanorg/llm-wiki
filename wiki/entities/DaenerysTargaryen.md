# Daenerys Targaryen

Major NPC in *The Apex of Ash: Shadow of the Mother* (campaign visenya v2). The "Mother of Dragons," liberating Astapor and marching on Meereen — the world's eyes are on her and her dragons while [[VisenyaBelaerys]] weaves the war from her shadow.

- Rescued Visenya from a Lysene pleasure house; struck by her "Exceptional Beauty," she suspects Visenya is a lost Targaryen.
- Vowed to protect Visenya but **commands her to hide her face**, fearing her radiance will "ignite the madness of men and the jealousy of queens" — the in-fiction justification for [[FunctionalObscurity]].
- Visenya treats her with reverent warmth laced with tactical advice, while privately judging her: "She calls herself the Mother of Dragons, yet she does not know what it is to be the flame."
- Dramatic irony engine of the campaign: the public Last Dragon unknowingly shelters the true Apex of Valyrian blood ([[HouseBelaerys]]).
