# House Targaryen (the 41st House)

In the [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]] campaign, House Targaryen is a **mocked, impoverished outsider family** — the "41st House," outside the Forty Families' oligarchy of the [[ValyrianFreehold]]. [[SarielAsh]] aligns with them precisely because their low status frees them from the blood-feud gravity of the [[FortyFamiliesCivilWar]].

Their campaign role inverts ASOIAF canon's lucky-survivor Targaryens: here the escape to Westeros ([[VanguardExodus]]) is a deliberately executed strategic play guided by Sariel's unmatched genetic pedigree, not Daenys the Dreamer's prophecy. The name collision with canon's Daenys is echoed in Sariel's mother [[DaenysBelaerys]].
