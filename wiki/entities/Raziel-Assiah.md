# Raziel, the Architect

Grand strategist and chief defensive magician of the [[CelestialImperium]]. During the [[AbyssalWar]] he masterminded the Imperium's grand strategy and magical defenses while [[Lucifer]] fought the front lines; the two were brothers-in-arms for nine centuries.

In the [[CenturyOfSchism]] he commands the loyalist side against Lucifer's Host, defending the "mad, stagnant Imperium." The campaign's [[HighPersistenceWorldState]] contract has him adapting continental strategy in response to [[Alexiel]]'s actions in the Western Marches.
