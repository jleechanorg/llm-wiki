# Malachi Sosuke

Brother of [[AizenSosuke]], son of [[CorvanSosuke]] and [[SeraphinaSosuke]] of [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]].

## In the campaign

- In Aizen's account to the [[KnightsOfTheShield]] (embedded in a [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]]), Malachi is credited with purging one of the [[Bane]] cultist hubs while Aizen handled the Sosuke Estate and a Heapside Ward hub.
- Whether Malachi's purge happened as described — or at all — is unverified; the claim serves Aizen's framing of House Sosuke as Bane's opponents rather than its enablers.
