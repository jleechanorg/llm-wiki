# Vermithor, the Bronze Fury

The second largest dragon in the world; former mount of King Jaehaerys I. In *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]) he is the campaign's opening objective: old, angry, and lonely, coiled around a mound of ash in a sulfurous Dragonmont cavern on [[Dragonstone]], having just incinerated a knight who tried to use a whip on him.

## Claiming Paths

The bible offers three claim routes for [[VisenyaBelaerys]]: a subtle Calm Emotions cast disguised as the dragon sparing [[GerrickTheSmith]]; a raw Apex Command ("Lykirī") that makes Vermithor *bow* at the cost of +15 [[AscensionMeter]] and Rhaenyra witnessing the power; or a Minor Illusion of Jaehaerys's voice exploiting the dragon's grief for his dead rider.
