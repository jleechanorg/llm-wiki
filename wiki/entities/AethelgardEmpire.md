# Aethelgard Empire

Antagonist-protagonist civilization of *The Hollow Zenith* ([[2026-08-04-saita-v3-hollow-zenith]]). A eugenics-driven society of apex predators where might is the only law and weakness is a capital offense. The royal bloodline carries world-shattering physical and magical superiority: Emperor [[TiberiusAethelgard]] is a Level 20 titan capable of leveling cities barehanded; standard nobles reach Level 12 before age forty. Citizens of Faerûn are regarded as "fragile, fleeting mayflies."

**The hollow part of the zenith**: a brutal, apocalyptic civil war against a treacherous **Cadet Branch** has left the Empire secretly ruined — legions decimated, treasuries empty, infrastructure destroyed — behind "a curtain of gold." Survival requires expansion into the Sword Coast, targeting a recovering [[BaldursGate]] after the Absolute's fall, executed not by conquest but by [[WeaknessMaskingDiplomacy]] through the herald [[SaitaAethelgard]].

Biological signature of the bloodline: scent of ozone, crushed pine, and cold iron.

Structurally parallel to other secretly-compromised superpower factions in the wiki's campaign corpus (compare [[CelestialImperium]], [[HouseVitiate]]).
