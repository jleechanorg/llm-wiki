# Arthas Menethil

Warcraft III's fallen prince, present in [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]] as an active threat rather than the campaign's protagonist-track. In this alternate timeline his killing of [[MalGanis]] in Northrend never succeeded. [[NocturneRavencrest]]'s shadow stack feeds Mal'Ganis intelligence on Arthas's pursuit; Arthas is listed among the mortal-king powers she refuses to serve.
