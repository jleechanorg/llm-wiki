# Cult of the Dead Three

Resurgent cult of Bane, Bhaal, and Myrkul in the Aizen god-campaign, sowing chaos in Baldur's Gate's lower districts to destabilize and seize the city. Secretly led by Lord Volcain and Lady Seraphina of [[House Sosuke]] — [[Aizen Sosuke]]'s parents. Its exposure and destruction is the inciting arc that leads to Aizen's first deicide against [[Bane]].
