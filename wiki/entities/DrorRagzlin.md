# Dror Ragzlin

Goblin warlord from Baldur's Gate 3, appearing in the [[Voyage]] campaign ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]). Slew [[WyllRavengard]] before [[NocturneSosuke]] thorn-bound him and extracted his martial essence — one of four named victims of the run's [[SoulEssenceHarvesting]] arc, with [[WyllRavengard]], [[PriestessGut]], and [[LarinnaDuskweaver]].
