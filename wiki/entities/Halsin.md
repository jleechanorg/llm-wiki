# Halsin — "The Shadow-Touched"

In the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath: L12+ Druid (Circle of the Moon) whose spirit broke when [[Shadowheart]]'s Sharran influence seeped into the wilderness and corrupted the Thaniel entity. Now roams the Cloakwood as a rabid, shadow-touched beast in the Armour of Moonbasking, attacking anyone who enters the forest — the nature-healer inverted into a wilderness hazard by the [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]].
