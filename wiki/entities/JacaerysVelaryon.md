# Prince Jacaerys Velaryon

Eldest son and heir of Queen [[RhaenyraTargaryen]] in the Dance of the Dragons. In the campaign visenya v8 mechanics he appears not as a battlefield foe but as a **systemic audit threat**: when [[Vermithrax]] enters the *Famine of Fire* state (treasury at 0), the resulting smallfolk predation "triggers a localized counter-audit by Prince Jacaerys's investigators."

This makes Jacaerys the enforcement arm pressing against [[VisenyaBelaerys]]'s hidden-apex secrecy — discovery risk is priced into the [[DraconicScarcityLedger]] rather than left to GM fiat, consistent with the Visenya line's pattern of quantifying exposure (compare the threat gauges of v3).
