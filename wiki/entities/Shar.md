# Shar

Forgotten Realms goddess of darkness, loss, and forgetting — the Nightsinger. In the WorldAI campaign-prompts index she anchors the Shadowheart campaign: the timeline where **[[Shadowheart]] sided with Shar** at the end of BG3 and now leads the Sharran sect as a level-12 Paladin 5 / Death Cleric 7.

## Wiki context

- Sister and eternal enemy of Selûne; opposite pole of the divine pantheon overseen by [[Ao]].
- Sharran doctrine (embrace of loss, secrecy, and darkness) is the ideological engine of the campaign's power-accumulation arc — the player-as-Shadowheart amasses power, wealth, influence, networks, and companions **as sect leader**, making Shar's church the campaign's primary institution.
- Joins the wiki's roster of divine patrons driving campaigns: cf. [[Mystra]] (Weave politics), Bhaal (the [[SosukeAizen|Sanguine Architecture]] deicide line), and the [[CelestialImperium]] hierarchy.
