# Kaname

A pursuer NPC in the teen-era Aizen campaign, appearing in the [[ChallengeCompensation]] stealth-tier encounter "Garden escapee — alarms, Kaname pursuit" (300XP + a new "Rough Diamond" recruit). The name echoes Kaname Tōsen from the Bleach source material that the [[SosukeAizen]] campaign lineage adapts; his exact allegiance in the Garden operation is not yet specified in ingested sources.
