# Yong Moon-gu

Lawyer from *[[MilitaryProsecutorDoberman]]* (canonically a power-broker attorney). In the [[Sariel]] campaign he exemplifies her [[SurgicalDomination]] drive: a "suggested" idea (her Bardic magic re-flavored as social influence) that makes him unknowingly adopt her own strategy is "infinitely more satisfying" to her than holding his position for a year.
