# Royal Knight Academy

Military/knightly training institution in the *Blades of the High and Low* setting where [[ValeriaVex]] serves as a senior squire. Her public identity — young border noble, talented, focused, slightly strange — is anchored here. The academy setting supplies the campaign's staple content: duels, training, rivalries, instructors, and peers who each carry their own partial knowledge of Valeria's true ability per the [[WitnessedRevealMechanic]].

Sits within the lowland side of the [[HighlanderLowlanderDivide]], so its traditions are martial/half-caster by law — making a full [[SpellbladeGestalt]] like Valeria structurally impossible in public understanding.
