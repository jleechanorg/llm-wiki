# Lex Luthor

**Class**: Level 12 Mastermind. **Role**: ICC Special Prosecutor against Kara Zor-El.

## Threat

Does not attempt to fight Kara with Kryptonite bombs. Uses logic, human philosophy, and grief-stricken family members of Malakor's low-level guards to paint Kara as an unfeeling alien tyrant. The prosecution's lead strategist — has crafted legal traps: if Kara accepts human jurisdiction, she loses the right to intervene in global disasters without court permission; if she refuses to attend, she is officially branded an illegal rogue entity and an enemy of Earth.

## Defining Moment

Watching the news footage of Kara executing Malakor on live TV and realizing, in that exact instant, that *the world needs a counter-argument more than it needs a hero*. Luthor has spent his entire adult life believing that humanity's role in the universe is to be the apex predator — that Kryptonians are an existential threat to that role, no matter how benevolent they appear. The Malakor footage proved his thesis to him. It also gave him the legal ammunition he needed.

## The Trial Trap

Luthor's prosecution strategy is a layered trap:

1. **Phase 1 — Establish jurisdiction**: argue that Kara's deliberate entry into Markovia, an internationally recognized sovereign state, constitutes a cross-border act of war. Kara's defense (saving millions of lives) is irrelevant; the act itself is the crime.
2. **Phase 2 — Establish culpability**: introduce evidence that Kara's heat vision is *precise* — that she chose to incinerate Malakor's head rather than simply disable his weapons. Show the jury the slow-motion footage. Let them watch it three times.
3. **Phase 3 — Establish precedent**: if convicted, request that the ICC impose a binding *restraint order* — any future Kryptonian intervention in a non-signatory state requires explicit ICC authorization. This is the trap: Kara can either accept the order (and lose the right to stop the next genocide) or reject it (and become legally classified as an enemy of Earth, justifying [[ProjectRedDust]] activation).

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- Provides the **[[LexTrap]]** mechanic — once per scenario, Luthor can deploy a legal trap that Kara must defeat through social combat (Persuasion / Insight / History checks) rather than physical combat.
- Provides the **[[ProsecutorWits]]** counter — Luthor's level 12 Mastermind abilities make him the only human who can out-argue Kara in extended dialogue without an Insight check penalty.
- The **[[MalakorFootage]]** — a recurring campaign element. Every key NPC has seen the footage. Their reaction to it defines their relationship to Kara.
