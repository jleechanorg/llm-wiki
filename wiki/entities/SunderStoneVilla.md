# Sunder-Stone Villa

[[CassalanterFamily]] villa whose "Whispering Cellar" hosts the soul-tithe ritual to [[Zariel]] in the [[NocturneSosuke]] BG3 v3 campaign. The Sosuke sisters are held here in crystalline cages as "Refined Collateral." Sessions 4–5 (the flagged campaign start point) are a stealth/social/combat heist using [[HorgusGwent]]'s stolen keys: patrol stealth DCs 13–15, servant-impersonation Deception DC 16, Cassalanter guard encounters, and a CR 9 bone devil if the alarm is raised. The prologue climaxes here with the [[SoulTitheInversion]] that consumes the Cassalanter nobles by their own pact.
