# Saita Aethelgard ("Herald Saita Kross")

Player character of *The Hollow Zenith* campaign ([[2026-08-04-saita-v3-hollow-zenith]]). Youngest princess of the [[AethelgardEmpire]] royal line — Level 1 ("an infant by their standards") despite a bloodline whose Emperor, her father [[TiberiusAethelgard]], is a Level 20 titan. Sent to post-Absolute [[BaldursGate]] under the cover identity **Herald Saita Kross** to secure resources, alliances, and vassal states while concealing that the Empire is bleeding out from a Cadet Branch civil war.

- **Archetype**: [[PatientApexArchetype]] (INTJ/ENTJ hinge), Lawful Evil — morality is bloodline supremacy and survival.
- **Masks**: charming, weary diplomat (public) vs. supremacist godling who views non-Aethelgardians as sub-sentient tools (private).
- **Signature mechanic**: [[ImperialBluff]] — social dominance converts to Temporary HP; exposure of her identity or the Empire's weakness inflicts Exhaustion.
- **Core compulsion**: [[BurdenOfSupremacy]].
- **Physical tells**: midnight-blue velvet with hidden imperial silver, weaponized posture, dagger balanced on a fingertip when bored, scent of ozone/crushed pine/cold iron.

Fits the wiki's recurring pattern of dual-identity scion protagonists (compare [[NocturneSosuke]], [[NocturneRavencrest]]).
