# Tyranny (game)

2016 CRPG by Obsidian Entertainment, premised on evil having already won: the Overlord [[Kyros]] rules through Archons and reality-warping Edicts. The player is a [[FatebinderArchetype|Fatebinder]] — a mobile judge/arbiter of Kyros' law — mediating between the [[Disfavored]] (under [[GravenAshe]]) and the [[ScarletChorus]] (under the [[VoicesOfNerat]]).

Used as the setting for the "Architecture of Ascension" campaign ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension]]): [[Terratus]], The Tiers, Year 431 TR, run as a Tyranny / modified D&D 5e hybrid. First non-[[BaldursGate]] branch of the [[NocturneSosuke]] campaign line.
