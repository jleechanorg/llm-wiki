# Daemon Targaryen

The "Rogue Prince" of A Song of Ice and Fire — brother of King Viserys I, husband and uncle of Queen [[RhaenyraTargaryen]], rider of Caraxes, and a principal commander of the Blacks in the [[DanceOfTheDragons]].

## Role in campaign visenya v7

Father of the player character [[VisenyaWaters|Visenya]], a [[GreatBastard]] borne by a now-dead Targaryen lady. His acknowledgment (or use) of a bastard daughter who has just claimed a dragon of her own is the central family dynamic of the campaign: Daemon is famous in canon for collecting leverage, and a dragonriding daughter is leverage.
