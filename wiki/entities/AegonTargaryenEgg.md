# Aegon "Egg" Targaryen

Younger brother of [[VisenyaTargaryen]] in the Visenya v4 campaign (the future squire of [[SerDuncanTheTall]] in ASOIAF canon). One of the family members Visenya loves fiercely and shields via her "Furnace" self-conception — burning so they stay warm.
