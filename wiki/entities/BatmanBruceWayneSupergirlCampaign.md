# Batman / Bruce Wayne

**Class**: Level 12 Inquisitive Rogue / Shadow Monk multiclass. **Role**: Co-architect of [[ProjectRedDust]].

## Threat

The only Peak Human who has successfully fought Kara in personal combat. He survived three encounters before Kara realized he was holding back (each time he had a contingency plan for *seven* different ways she might kill him, and she did not use any of them). Refuses to speak to Kara without a Kryptonite-reinforced room between them.

Co-architect of Project Red Dust. Has spent fifteen years developing contingency plans for Kryptonian tyranny and now treats Kara as the threat he always feared Superman could become.

## Defining Moment

The murder of his parents. Defining moments do not get more defining than that. But the campaign-specific hook is the **Kara Question**: when Bruce was eight years old, his parents were murdered in Crime Alley. A Kryptonian could have stopped it in three seconds. There was no Kryptonian on Earth at the time. But Bruce has spent his entire career asking: *if there had been, would they have stopped it? Or would they have decided the crime was beneath their attention?*

That question is why he does not trust Kara. It is not personal. It is architectural.

## Stance

Believes the Kryptonian Accords are necessary but insufficient. Believes [[ProjectRedDust]] is necessary. Believes Kara, individually, may be benign — but Kryptonians as a class are an existential threat to human sovereignty. The Accords and Red Dust are the answer; whether Kara likes it or not.

Will not be moved by appeals to Kara's good character. Will only be moved by *evidence that the Accords are working* — that Kara is voluntarily submitting to human jurisdiction in a way that proves the system does not need Red Dust as a backstop.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- Provides the **[[ContingencyMaster]]** ability — once per scenario, Bruce can produce a previously-prepared countermeasure to any Kryptonian capability (Kryptonite variant, red solar emitter, magical binding, telepathic dampener, etc.). The countermeasure is always tailored to Kara's specific loadout for that scenario.
- The **[[CrimeAllyConversation]]** — once per campaign, Bruce agrees to meet Kara one-on-one without a Kryptonite barrier. The conversation is a social-combat encounter: Insight, Persuasion, History checks. The outcome determines whether Bruce withdraws his Project Red Dust trigger authorization.
- The campaign's hardest fight, if it comes to one — Bruce has prepared for it. He has always prepared for it.
