# Gaius Octavius

Caesar's grand-nephew — in real history, Augustus. In the [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] timeline he becomes the figurehead of the disgruntled Republican remnants after the failed Ides of March, and is murdered in 42 BCE via an arranged "unfortunate accident" (Caesar's agents, likely under [[MarkAntony]]) to secure [[Caesarion]]'s succession.

His death extinguishes the last hope of the old Republican guard — the timeline's clearest signal that this world's Rome belongs to the Julian-Ptolemaic line, not the Principate of our history.
