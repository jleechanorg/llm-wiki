# Nazarick — the Great Tomb

Fortress home base of [[Ains-Ooal-Gown]] in the [[2026-08-04-shy-lich-overlord-v2|Sanctuary of Flesh and Shadow]] module.

- **Lone Sovereign Constraint**: floor guardians are capped at **L12-16**, four-plus levels below Ains' L20 — deliberate design so the PC personally owns every top-tier threat and can never fully delegate.
- **Population**: floor guardians and servant "siblings", all fully sentient under the module's Universal Sentience rule — their welfare feeds the [[Sanctuary-Imperative]] morality meter.
- **Economic crisis**: the treasury is failing; **Session 1 opens with a Treasury Audit**, framing the campaign's first pressure as logistics and governance rather than combat.
- **Exposure risk**: activity radiating from Nazarick drives the [[Whispers-of-the-Realm]] narrative-aggro state machine.
