# Aerion Targaryen

Brother of [[VisenyaTargaryen]] in the Visenya v4 campaign. His madness is reframed as a *reaction to inferiority*: he senses Visenya's [[ApexBloodline]] status and lashes out because he knows he is "just an echo of her fire." A canon-divergent psychological explanation for Brightflame's cruelty.
