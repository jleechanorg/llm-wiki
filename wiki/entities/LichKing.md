# The Lich King

In [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]], the Lich King is explicitly **not a god** — he is a tyrant propped up by a fractious coalition of dreadlords, liches, and self-willed banshees who hate each other more than the Alliance. His attempted betrayal of the Nathrezim is what [[MalGanis]] anticipated and survived, setting up the [[DualCustodyPact]] with [[NocturneRavencrest]]. He is one of the powers (alongside Kil'jaeden, [[ArthasMenethil]], Terenas, and the Dreadlord Triumvirate) whose pawn Nocturne refuses to be.
