# Wonder Woman / Diana of Themyscira / Diana Prince

**Class**: Level 20 Demigod Fighter / Paladin. **Apex Five member**. Ambassador of Themyscira to the world of men.

## Threat

Wields divine, god-killing weapons forged by Hephaestus that slice through Kryptonian skin:
- **The Lasso of Truth** — compels truth from any bound creature; can detect a lie at any level of magical concealment.
- **The God-Killer Sword** — a blade forged to slay the Olympian gods; bypasses Kryptonian invulnerability entirely.
- **The Bracelets of Submission** — capable of absorbing an attack from a Level 25 Kryptonian (verified against Superman in a 2016 trial of arms).

## Stance

Represents the sovereign ancient nations. Refuses to sign the Kryptonian Accords or submit to UN authority — Themyscira's sovereignty is older than the United Nations by several thousand years. Warns Kara and Clark that their godlike interference is weakening humanity's spiritual resolve, but does not consider herself humanity's savior — she considers herself humanity's *cousin*.

She is the only Apex who treats Kara as a peer rather than a threat or a god. She has dined with Kara three times. She has sparred with Kara once (the spar ended in a draw, which both considered a victory). She is the campaign's most plausible ally.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- Provides **[[ThemysciraCounter]]** — can negotiate with [[DoctorFate]] on equal footing; can call on a Themysciran embassy safe-conduct pass that negates ICC jurisdiction over Kara while on embassy grounds.
- Provides **[[GodKillerDodge]]** — once per session, Diana's presence in a combat encounter gives Kara advantage on all saving throws against magical effects (Diana's divine aura stabilizes the metaphysical field around them).
- Provides **[[LassoOfTruthConfession]]** — once per scenario, Kara can ask Diana to invoke the Lasso on a hostile witness, forcing a confession that resolves the legal/political subplot.
