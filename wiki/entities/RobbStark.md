# Robb Stark

NPC in [[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s v6 campaign "The Winter Web." Teenage heir to Winterfell, presented as an oblivious low-level mortal against Visenya's godling power disparity. Her inner monologue singles him out: "Robb Stark thinks his direwolf makes him dangerous. He doesn't realize I can calculate the exact pound of pressure needed to make him skin it himself." Peer of [[JonSnow]]; son of [[CatelynStark]].
