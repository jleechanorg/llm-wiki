## Summary
The four-person party that played the first ever Voyage dev livestream. All copper-rank adventurers in the Larion world, played by the Voyage / AI Dungeon dev team (Nick, Ryan, Brian, Kobe).

## Members
- **Colt Oldark** — Moon elf chronicler / bard. Silver tongue, dramatic flourishes, theatrical dive into the sewer, throws voice for misdirection, throws an iron dagger into a bandit's eye socket.
- **Brecht the dwarf** — Cleric of the Architect, short bow enchanted with golden light, snatches the bronze contract from the board.
- **Dracos** — Giant-born monk. Silent sentinel, white beard, rips the iron grate off with bare hands, "stand and face the mountain" finishing blow.
- **Nikolai** — Wood elf ranger / druid. Summons the rats, names them (Whiskers, Gray-face, Witter, Spot, Fred, plus rats one through N), falls to his knees mourning the moss.
- (NPC ally) **Ulla Finehair** — Dwarf justiciar, escorts the party.
