# Larinna Duskweaver

NPC in the [[Voyage]] post-BG3 campaign ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]). [[NocturneSosuke]] drew her essence, inflicting rapid magical aging — the fourth named victim of the run's [[SoulEssenceHarvesting]] arc, alongside [[WyllRavengard]], [[DrorRagzlin]], and [[PriestessGut]].
