# The Solar Dawn

A violent splinter wing of the broader **Sanctum of the Sun** (Vespera's rational cult). While the Sanctum channels religious devotion into legal accountability, refugee support, and disaster response, the Solar Dawn believes Kara's silence on human institutions is divine approval — and acts accordingly.

## Tactics

The Solar Dawn begins executing the prosecutors, judges, and politicians involved in Kara's ICC indictment. The terrorists commit these assassinations in Kara's name, leaving her House of El symbol written in blood at the crime scenes.

Their signature act: kneeling in prayer over the body of their victim as the police arrive. They believe the act of dying in Kara's service grants them eternal proximity to her. Some of them are right — if the cult's doctrine is true.

## The Trap

Every Solar Dawn member who is arrested falls to their knees and worships Kara, claiming her punishment is "divine purification." If Kara uses force against them, she proves their doctrine of absolute godhood. If she ignores them, humanity holds her morally responsible for the terror attacks they commit in her name.

Kara cannot punch the terrorists without looking like a god striking down her own worshippers. She must use her Level 12 companions (Marcus Vance and Elena) to infiltrate the cult's underground network, track down the rogue cell, and bring them to justice before Amanda Waller uses the attacks as a pretext to declare all Supergirl supporters global enemy combatants.

## Membership Demographics

- 60% genuinely devoted (would die for Kara in combat)
- 25% opportunists (joined because the cult has political power, will abandon it if the power shifts)
- 15% infiltrators (ARGUS agents, LexCorp operatives, [[BlackAdamTethAdam]]'s external intelligence service)

The 15% is the campaign's hidden hook: Solar Dawn cells in three different cities are not actually acting on their own initiative. They are being funded and directed by Amanda Waller, who is using the Solar Dawn as a controlled opposition to justify expanding the [[ProjectRedDust]] activation criteria.
