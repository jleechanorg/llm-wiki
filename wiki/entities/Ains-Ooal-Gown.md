# Ains Ooal Gown — shy living lich protagonist

Protagonist of the [[2026-08-04-shy-lich-overlord-v2|Sanctuary of Flesh and Shadow]] campaign module: a **16-year-old living lich**, Level 20 **Bladesinger/Monk gestalt**, sole remaining administrator of [[Nazarick]].

- **Hidden Truth**: the other **41 Supreme Beings logged off**, leaving Ains alone at the top — the module's central secret and loneliness engine.
- **Temperament**: shy and socially fragile, inverting canon Overlord's performatively confident skeleton; the [[Mask-of-Fragility]] mechanic makes concealing power a skill system, and his temperament makes it costly.
- **Relationships**: manages floor guardians who are structurally weaker than him (L12-16 cap — the Lone Sovereign Constraint on [[Nazarick]]), so he can never delegate existential threats.
- **Mechanical profile**: full L20 caster/martial gestalt whose spell save DC directly sets his deception base DC under [[Mask-of-Fragility]]; moral choices tracked by the [[Sanctuary-Imperative]].

Part of the masked-power protagonist line alongside [[SosukeAizen]]-flavored PCs and [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] builds.
