# Cleopatra VII

Queen of Egypt who becomes Caesar's legal Roman wife in 43 BCE in the [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] timeline.

- **Regent of Rome** (40–30 BCE) during the Wars of Unification — formidable administrator who seeds Egyptian culture and religion in the capital.
- Her wealth floods Rome's treasury and later funds [[Caesarion]]'s Golden Age public works.
- As **Dowager-Empress** she remains Caesarion's most trusted advisor into his reign (from 14 BCE).
- Mother of the Julian-Ptolemaic line; her marriage is the hinge that turns Rome into a Roman-Egyptian hybrid empire ([[CultOfSerapisJupiter]], [[Alexandria]] as second capital).
