# Cult of the Absolute

Faction from Baldur's Gate 3 lore, referenced in the Aizen campaign lineage. In the teen-era prequel export (*The Architect of Destiny*), the cult is explicitly parked as "a distant, unknown threat (or not yet active)" — one of the three world-state rewinds (alongside no God-King ascension and [[Bane]] unconsumed) that define the campaign as a [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] / [[PrequelOriginCampaign]].

Its named-but-dormant status signals it as a planned future arc element for young [[AizenSosuke]]'s timeline in [[BaldursGate]], distinct from the [[CultOfTheDeadThree]] which features in the *Shadow over the Gate* reset.
