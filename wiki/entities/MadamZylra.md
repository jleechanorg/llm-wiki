# Madam Zylra

Lawful Evil tiefling proprietor of the [[BloomShadow|Bloom Shadow]] brothel in [[BaldursGate]]'s Upper City; filed-down horns, silver cane. She treats her workers "like prize horses," forbids damage to the merchandise, and charges triple for [[NocturneSosuke]], her "Black Diamond."

## Key details ([[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]])

- Keeps a **Black Ledger** of blackmail on half the Patriarchs of the city in her locked office (DC 20, Sleep glyph trap) — it contains the proof of House Sosuke's sale, making it Nocturne's leverage against her.
- **Secret**: terrified of the Absolute cult as bad for business; secretly funnels protection money to [[TheGuild]].
- Aftermath hook: if Nocturne returns bearing Zariel-derived power, "you don't work for her anymore; she works for you."
