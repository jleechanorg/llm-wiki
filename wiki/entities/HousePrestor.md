# House Prestor

Noble house courting [[NocturneRavencrest]] in the public stack of [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]]. Its notable figure is **Katrana Prestor — Onyxia in disguise**, making the house a second hidden-identity power broker mirroring Nocturne's own duality: a paladin-paragon dealing with a disguised dragon while herself bound to a dreadlord.
