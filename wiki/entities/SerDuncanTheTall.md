# Ser Duncan the Tall (Dunk)

Hedge knight of ASOIAF's Dunk & Egg era; in the Visenya v4 campaign he is the traveling companion whom [[VisenyaTargaryen]] — disguised as the squire "Silver" — accompanies to the Ashford tourney (209 AC).

His honest simplicity makes him Visenya's anchor: the [[Ditchbond]] that keeps the Apex sovereign tethered to her humanity. The campaign objective is to help Dunk win the tourney to prove a "True Knight" can exist — Visenya also quietly keeps his gear "unnaturally perfect" via Mending/Prestidigitation ("The Polish").
