# Swamp Thing / Alec Holland

**Class**: Level 20 Archdruid / Avatar of the Green. **Apex Five member**.

## Threat

Controls all plant life on Earth simultaneously. Immune to physical death — can reform anywhere biomass exists. Can summon localized root-growths that would entangle Metropolis within hours. Cannot be reasoned with through human politics — operates on the timescale of ecosystems (years, decades, centuries) and views human institutions as ephemeral noise.

## Stance

Indifferent to human politics, but fiercely protective of the biosphere. If human eco-terrorists or government operations trigger ecological collapse to test Kryptonite weapons, Swamp Thing will hold Kara and Clark personally responsible for failing to steward the planet. Sees the Solar Dawn cult as *symptomatic* — humans reaching for divine substitutes because Kryptonians have disrupted the relationship between humanity and Earth.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- **[[ProxyWeaponScenario]]** — Scenario C in the campaign bible. Amanda Waller and Lex Luthor attempt to manipulate Swamp Thing by staging a Kryptonian-fueled deforestation crisis. Kara must convince an enraged Level 20 Avatar of nature that she was framed — all while defending Metropolis from global root growths *without* harming Earth's ecosystem.
- The defense is mechanical, not narrative: Kara's solar absorption passively heals biomass within her aura. A single afternoon of standing in a defoliated zone, in direct yellow sunlight, will regrow the destroyed plant life. The campaign forces Kara to *demonstrate* this rather than simply claim it.
- Provides **[[GreenSanctuary]]** — once per scenario, Swamp Thing can grant Kara a brief audience in the Parliament of Trees, where her Insight checks on ecological matters auto-succeed for the rest of the session.
