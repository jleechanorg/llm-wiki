# The Lotus Ten

A covert network of slaves embedded around [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] in the Gaia Julia v5 campaign "The Gilded Trap" (73 BCE). They function as her hands inside the rebel camp while she maintains the helpless "Livia of Capua" facade.

Command and control is **nonverbal**: a sharp intake of breath or a specific hum tells the network exactly which slave should spill wine on which guard. This makes the Lotus Ten the operational layer of her [[SymphonyOfRuin]] strategy — she never issues an order that could be witnessed, keeping the damsel mask intact while sabotage and misdirection are executed by proxies.
