# Seraphina Sosuke

Mother of [[AizenSosuke]] and [[MalachiSosuke]]; matriarch of [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]] of [[BaldursGate]].

## In the campaign

- Co-ritualist with [[CorvanSosuke]] in the [[GrandConjunction]] attempt to manifest [[Bane]].
- Per Aizen's story to the [[KnightsOfTheShield]] — part of a deliberate [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]], so possibly fabricated — she fled into hiding carrying a counter-ritual capable of shattering Bane's manifestation. Her true fate and location are open narrative threads.
