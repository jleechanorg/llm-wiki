# Doctor Fate

**Class**: Level 22 Divine Archmage (Lord Nabu's host vessel). **Apex Five member**.

## Threat

Ancient magic bypasses Kryptonian invulnerability entirely. Fate's spells operate on the metaphysical layer that Kryptonian cellular density does not protect against — dimensional banishment, soul imprisonment, planar binding, and the layered ward networks he constructs can reduce a Kryptonian to a helpless bystander within a single round of concentration.

## Stance

Lord Nabu views the rising "Solar Dawn" cult and Kryptonian worship as a cosmic disruption to Order and Chaos. Does not want to kill Kara — but threatens to cast a planetary banishment spell to return all Kryptonians to deep space if human worship isn't curbed.

Has erected a massive mystical ward over Washington D.C. cutting off yellow solar radiation within city limits and reducing Kara and Clark's effective levels toward mortal bounds whenever they step inside the political capital. The ward is the campaign's first concrete demonstration that Level 22 magic can solve problems that Level 20 strength cannot.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- **[[TrialOfMagic]]** — Scenario B in the campaign bible. Fate demands Kara stand trial before an ancient council of mystic entities to answer for bringing cosmic-level destruction to Earth's spiritual plane during her war with Krem.
- Provides the **[[DCWard]]** mechanic — Kara cannot access her yellow-sun powers inside Washington D.C. unless she first breaks Fate's ward, which is itself a Lv22 magic check.
- **[[ThemysciraCounter]]** — Wonder Woman is the only Apex who can negotiate with Fate on equal footing. If Kara can earn Diana's trust, Diana can broker a settlement to lift the ward.
