# Calpurnia

Empress of the [[PaxCaesaraEmpire]]; wife of [[JuliusCaesar]] and mother of [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] in the Gaia v3 campaign (44 BCE). Characterized as **superstitious and protective** — "She senses your darkness but refuses to name it," making her one of the only court figures with true (if suppressed) insight into what Gaia is. Narrative role: the willfully blind conscience of the family, parallel to how [[Octavian]] is its clear-eyed instinctive alarm.
