# Glaber

Roman Praetor named in the Iron Eagle: Vengeance campaign intros (v3 and v6) as the exemplar of the "incompetent Praetors" whose mess [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]] is sent to fix during the [[ThirdServileWar]]. Corresponds to the historical Gaius Claudius Glaber, whose forces Spartacus famously defeated at Vesuvius. In-campaign he functions as the foil justifying the Emperor's decision to send a covert royal fixer instead of another conventional commander.
