# Alexandria

Under [[Caesarion]]'s Golden Age (14 BCE – 10 AD) in the [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] timeline, Alexandria becomes a **second capital** of the empire — a global center for learning and scholarship rivaling Rome itself, funded by Ptolemaic wealth and anchoring the Egyptian half of the Roman-Egyptian fusion state ([[CultOfSerapisJupiter]]).
