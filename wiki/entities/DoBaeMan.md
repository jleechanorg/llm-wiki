# Do Bae-man

Protagonist of the K-drama *[[MilitaryProsecutorDoberman]]* — a military prosecutor. In the [[Sariel]] campaign he appears as an "enemy" whom Sariel regards with the detached, patient amusement of a scholar observing "fascinating but limited fauna" — the campaign's likely long-arc antagonist-from-her-perspective (hero from the drama's perspective).
