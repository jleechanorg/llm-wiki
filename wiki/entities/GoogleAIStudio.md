# Google AI Studio

Google's web-based development surface for the [[Gemini]] model family. Used 2026-08-04 as one of three review surfaces for the [[CampaignSummaryPrompt]]. Its Gemini review contributed: an optional explicit definition of "significant" (narrative impact, core power/status change, primary-plotline advancement), the [[ItachiUchiha]] Level 20 compression example for power-up summarization, and the rule that summaries should capture think-block *resolutions* (selected CHOICE_ID + outcome) rather than the think blocks themselves ([[ThinkBlockChoiceResolution]]).
