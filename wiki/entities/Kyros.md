# Kyros

The Overlord of [[Terratus]] in [[TyrannyGame|Tyranny]] — an unseen conqueror ruling through Archons and Edicts (reality-altering proclamations; cf. [[EdictOfExecution]]). In [[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension|The Architecture of Ascension]], Nocturne holds her own self-authored Code superior to Kyros' law ("optimization, not vanity") and privately classes Kyros — alongside [[GravenAshe]] and the [[VoicesOfNerat]] — as "an unbearable stagnation" to be surpassed.
