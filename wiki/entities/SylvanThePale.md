# Sylvan the Pale

Sentient undead retinue member of [[NocturneRavencrest]] in [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]]. Carries centuries of Lordaeron scholarship. Serves as Nocturne's envoy in the shadow stack — dispatched to broker with the [[CultOfTheDamned]] and support the smuggling of Pale Undead through Andorhal toward the [[PaleCrown]] project.
