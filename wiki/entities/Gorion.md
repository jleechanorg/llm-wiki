# Gorion

Foster father of [[SerpentQueen|the Serpent Queen]] in the "campaign bg1 nocturne" setup; the canonical BG1 guardian figure, a sage of [[Candlekeep]] associated with [[TheHarpers]].

In this campaign's framing he is a **subject of study rather than a moral anchor**: the protagonist's soft-spoken Dutiful Ward mask forces him to lean in and listen, and his moral lectures are received with detached scholarly amusement — data about the Harper worldview, not persuasion. His guardianship implicitly connects to the protagonist's hidden [[BhaalspawnEssence]] bloodline.
