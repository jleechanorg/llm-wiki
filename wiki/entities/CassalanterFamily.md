# Cassalanter Family

Noble family (canonically Waterdhavian devil-cultists from *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*), used in the Aizen campaigns as the instrument of [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]]'s ruin.

## Role in Thay v1 ([[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]])

The Cassalanters ruined House Sosuke financially and had the young [[NocturneSosuke]] "sold for profit" and trafficked into [[BaldursGate]]'s Upper City velvet cages. This trauma is the origin of Nocturne's core law — "Love is a weapon, and desire is a leash" — making the family the inadvertent authors of the Sovereign who would later conquer the Hells. Their fate after her ascension is not specified in v1.

## Role in Velvet Cage v4 ([[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]])

Now "the uncrowned royalty of Baldur's Gate": Victoro and Ammalia are charming philanthropists wholly sold to the Hells, powering their fortune by sacrificing "ruined nobility" to [[Zariel]] — House Sosuke wasn't just bankrupted, it was **harvested**, and Nocturne's sisters were minted into Soul Coins as "vintage currency." A pointed cruelty: "They genuinely love their children, which is why they are sacrificing you instead." Their midnight **Ascension Gala / Second Tithe** (offering 100 souls to buy protection from the Absolute before Gortash's coronation) is the module's heist target; a successful [[InversionRitual]] strips their infernal protection and leaves them alive, terrified, and hunting the thief.
