# Nocturne Sosuke

Central figure of the Aizen/Nocturne campaign continuity. In the **Thay v1 / "Black Company" bible** ([[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]]) she is recast as the **Ancestral Sovereign**: great-grandmother and warlock-style patron of [[SosukeAizen|Aizen Sosuke]], reachable only through the Obsidian Mirror ([[ObsidianMirrorPact]]).

## Thay v1 biography

- **1492 DR**: scion of [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]], ruined by the [[CassalanterFamily]] and trafficked in [[BaldursGate]]'s Upper City; at sixteen formulated her law "Love is a weapon, and desire is a leash," then seduced (rather than fled) the Nautiloid illithid parasite.
- **Conquest year**: conquered the Sword Coast, the Hells, and the Abyss in a single year — rewrote [[Zariel]]'s loyalty via the internalized [[CrownOfKarsus]], hollowed out [[Asmodeus]], and mass-dominated Orcus, Graz'zt, and Demogorgon, industrializing the Abyss's 666 layers into soul factories ([[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]]).
- **Capability**: "Level 60," outpacing the gods; enslaved champions including Lae'zel and [[Astarion]]; nearly unseated [[Ao]].
- **The Sovereign's Vanishing**: sealed by Ao's [[GeometricFirewall]] into her sub-plane, the [[ShatteredRose]], where she retains absolute dominion over devils and demons but cannot touch the Material Plane — except through Aizen, her Material Anchor.

## Velvet Cage v4 playable origin ([[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]])

The 2026-08-04 v4 module plays her origin in the present: age 18, 730 days into captivity at the [[BloomShadow]] as [[MadamZylra]]'s "Black Diamond," Level 1 Bard (STR 8 / DEX 14 / CON 8 / INT 16 / WIS 10 / CHA 16) with [[SirensLeverage]] and a two-year Survivor's Stash. Her sisters [[Elara]] and [[Lyra]] are dead — "bottled" as Soul Coins by the Cassalanters. Her arc: seize Lyra's coin from [[HorgusGwent]], infiltrate the Cassalanter Ascension Gala, and perform the [[InversionRitual]] to steal Zariel's pact for herself, unlocking [[CollegeOfInfernalContracts]] and beginning the climb "From Ruin to Archdevil."

## Velvet Cage Descent Edition — v5 ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-bg3-v5-velvet-cage]]) and v7 ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-bg3-v7-velvet-cage]])

The Descent Edition line inverts the origin play: the already-ascended Sovereign is de-powered to Level 0/1 at the [[BaldursGate]] nautiloid crash beach (age stated as 16 in this line). The **v7 revision** adds a dual profile — "The Fallen Siren" (Level-0 castaway) stacked with "The Ascendant" (Vessel of Stolen Fire) — and formalizes a six-stage Level 0–20 appearance progression, the [[UncannyValleyProgression]]: Ruin → Porcelain Doll → Uncanny Valley → Predator → Sovereign → Ascended, with concealable horns/wings and eye-glow tells on casting or Pheromone Spike. Design theme: perfection, not monstrosity, as the visual signature of her power ([[BeautyAsWeapon]]).

## Cross-campaign note

This ancestor framing contradicts campaign lines where Nocturne is Aizen's own ascended persona or a contemporary (see [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]]); the Thay v1 bible resolves the tension by pushing her ascension 100 years into the past ([[CenturyOfSilence]]). The Velvet Cage v4 module is the playable version of the very backstory Thay v1 treats as ancestral history, while the Descent Edition (v5/v7) plays the post-ascension Sovereign stripped back to Level 0. Her age differs by line: 16 in the Descent Edition and at the Thay v1 law-formulation, 18 in the v4 brothel-noir origin.
