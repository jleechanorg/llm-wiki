# Bhaal

Canonical Forgotten Realms god of murder, one of the Dead Three. In the [[SanguineArchitecture]] campaign his role is terminal: the [[DarkUrge]] — his own Bhaalspawn daughter — enters the Undercity Temple beneath [[BaldursGate]] and **consumes his divine spark**, becoming the incubating Goddess of Murder in his place.

His death is the campaign's premise rather than its plot: the successor inherits the portfolio and must defend and expand it against the surviving Faerûnian pantheon ([[Mystra]], Kelemvor, Shar, Torm, Helm, Tyr, Cyric) under the [[GodMechanicsV2Overlay]] deicide rules. Bhaal's canonical servants (Sarevok, Orin) appear in their BG3 roles.
