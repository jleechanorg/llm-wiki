# Priestess Gut

Goblin priestess of the Absolute from Baldur's Gate 3, appearing in the [[Voyage]] campaign ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]). [[NocturneSosuke]] unraveled her psychic energy and desiccated her to a husk — one of four named [[SoulEssenceHarvesting]] victims, with [[WyllRavengard]], [[DrorRagzlin]], and [[LarinnaDuskweaver]].
