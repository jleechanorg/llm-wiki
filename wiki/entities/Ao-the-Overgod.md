# Ao the Overgod

The Overgod of the Forgotten Realms cosmology — arbiter above the pantheon, a being of pure law. In the Aizen god-campaign summary, Ao confronts [[AizenSosuke]] after the double deicide of [[Bane]] and [[Myrkul]]. The confrontation is explicitly **not combat**: Aizen wins a metaphysical debate ([[Conceptual-Debate-Victory]]) by presenting a "superior" definition of cosmic balance — a single absolute will replacing the eternal good/evil conflict. Because Ao is bound to accept a demonstrably superior law, he accepts the theorem and is **subsumed — "retired," not killed** — making Aizen The Absolute. This is the campaign's thesis moment: the highest power in the setting is defeated by argument, not force.
