# Gale of Waterdeep — "God of Ambition"

In the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath: L12+ Wizard (Evocation)/Sorcerer (Wild Magic) who reforged the [[CrownOfKarsus]] and achieved terrifying pseudo-divinity. One third of the [[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]]. Hovers above [[BaldursGate]] in a floating citadel carved from Ramazith's Tower, wearing the Robe of the Weave and wielding Markoheshkir. Treats the populace as test subjects for experimental Netherese weaving — mutating citizens and tearing holes in reality. Petrified [[Minsc]] as a garden ornament.
