# Aurelius Julia Caesar

Player character of the Iron Eagle campaign line (v1, v3, v6). In the v3/v6 timeline: 16-year-old youngest son of Emperor [[JuliusCaesar]], "the Prodigy of the Julian Line", operating undercover as merchant scion **Aurelius Varenus** during the [[ThirdServileWar]] (71 BCE) with a secret mandate to end the [[Spartacus]] revolt.

## Identity stack (v6 personality bible)

- **Public**: "Aurelius Julia Caesar — the Sun of Rome / Passion of the Empire" — vibrant, charismatic, lavish; makes each interlocutor feel like the only other soul in the room. Counterpoint to his father's cold gravity.
- **Cover**: Aurelius Varenus, spoiled hedonistic grain-and-wine merchant ([[ShadowPrince]] archetype).
- **True nature (to the [[Miasma]])**: "the Devouring Maw" — a singularity of Appetite; Lawful Evil absolute Entitlement ([[DecadentConqueror]]).

## Build and assets

- Level 6 [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]] Bard — duelist, manipulator, Miasma wielder ("The Divine Blood").
- 50,000 gold, black-ops Praetorian squad, imperial mandate: "End the rebellion. Save the gold. Do not embarrass the family."

## Traits (v6)

- ESFP–ENTJ hinge; tactile Ownership gestures (touching is claiming).
- [[BloodOfVenus]]: most beautiful man in the Empire; [[BeautyAsWeapon]] — beauty as armor, Auctoritas manifesting as Heat.
- Two masks: Prince of Pleasure (public warmth) vs Golden Tyrant (aesthetic tyranny — crushes rebellion because chaos is *ugly*).
- Core compulsion: the [[SensoryFeast]] — craving Intensity over abstraction.

Note: the v6 source file is titled "gaia Julia caesar" (feminine praenomen) while describing this male character — see the source page's discrepancy note.
