# Dallis the Hammer

Canonical DOS2 antagonist — enforcer of the Divine Order (secretly the Eternal Fane's daughter in game canon). In "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse", Dallis was **consumed by the player character** at the First Garden together with [[LucianTheDivine]], completing the protagonist's monopolization of Source (see [[VeilMonopoly]]).

## Campaign role
- Pre-campaign casualty whose power contributes to the protagonist's Tier-4 chassis
