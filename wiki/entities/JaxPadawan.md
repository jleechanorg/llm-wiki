# Jax (Padawan)

Peer of [[Alexiel]] at the Jedi Academy. Used in the bible to demonstrate [[TransactionalAffection]]: if Alexiel saves Jax's life she expects service, not thanks — relationships parsed as Debt and Ownership rather than reciprocity.
