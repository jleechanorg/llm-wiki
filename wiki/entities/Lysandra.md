# Lysandra

A rival in the teen-era Aizen campaign with divinatory ability: the [[ChallengeCompensation]] table names "rival intel (Lysandra visions your moves)" as an intrigue-tier counter to [[SosukeAizen]]'s plotting. She functions as an anti-INT threat — precognition that punishes over-planning — one of the deliberate counters keeping a Level 4 mastermind under pressure in post-BG3 [[BaldursGate]].
