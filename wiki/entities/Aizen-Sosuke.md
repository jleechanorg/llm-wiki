# Aizen Sosuke

Protagonist of the Aizen god-campaign — a D&D adaptation of Bleach's Sosuke Aizen. Begins as a Level 8 Paladin/Bard heir of [[House Sosuke]] in Baldur's Gate, driven by ambition rather than heroism.

## Arc

1. Exposes the [[Cult of the Dead Three]]; its leaders are his own parents. Kills his father Lord Volcain, breaks and subjugates his mother Lady Seraphina.
2. Defeats [[Bane]]'s avatar in a battle of will and consumes the Tyranny portfolio — apotheosis ([[Divine Portfolio Consumption]]).
3. Ambushes and consumes [[Myrkul]] — Greater Deity of Tyranny and Death.
4. Subsumes [[Ao the Overgod]] via [[Conceptual Debate Victory]], becoming **The Absolute**, hidden master of reality.
5. Directs [[The Grand Game]], elevating [[Gale of Waterdeep]] as the new God of Magic.

Distinct from [[Nocturne Sosuke]] (the BG3 Velvet Cage campaigns) — same family-name motif, different protagonist and campaign line.
