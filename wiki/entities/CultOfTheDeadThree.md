# Cult of the Dead Three

Hidden, insidious faction in the [[ShadowOverTheGate]] campaign worshipping Bane, Bhaal, and Myrkul — seeking to sow chaos and bring about a dark age in [[BaldursGate]]. They operate in the shadows, influencing events and recruiting followers.

The DM's framing note — "Bane will be a significant, subtle presence" — is the explicit carry-over from the predecessor "bane - main" chat (the campaign's chat list literally renames it "Aizen godhood (previously bane - main)"). This seed pays off in the god-era arc: [[AizenSosuke]]'s later [[TelepathicBaniteConversion|mass conversion of Banites]] and domain-usurpation economics target exactly this cult's god.

Likely fingerprints in the opening hooks ([[ANightOfUnrest]]): the ritualistic-tattoo gang at the [[ElfsongTavern]], the eye-symbol dockside abduction, and disappearances of critics of the city's power structure.
