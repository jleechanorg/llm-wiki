# Amanda Waller

**Class**: Level 10 Warlord / Inquisitor. **Role**: ARGUS Director, Project Red Dust authorizer.

## Threat

Treats Kara as a strategic asset to be controlled, not a person to be negotiated with. Has signed off on Project Red Dust because she has decided that humanity's survival requires a deadman-switch against Kryptonian overreach. Does not bluff. Has already authorized one assassination attempt (a kryptonite-tipped bullet — Kara caught it bare-handed and sent it back in the envelope).

## Defining Moment

The original Task Force X incident — Waller personally led the operation that cost twelve operatives' lives to recover a single alien artifact. She considers that trade acceptable. The decision haunts her in private but has not changed her public position.

The campaign-specific hook is **Operation Solar Dawn**: Waller has been funding and directing three Solar Dawn cells in different cities as *controlled opposition* — using the cult's terror attacks as a pretext to expand Project Red Dust's activation criteria. If Kara exposes this, Waller loses one of her primary justifications for Red Dust. If Kara does not expose this, the activation criteria will quietly expand to cover any Kryptonian intervention in a signatory state, regardless of authorization.

## Stance

Waller's worldview is brutal: Kryptonians are not people, they are strategic assets with autonomy. The job of ARGUS is to ensure that autonomy is constrained to the point where the asset remains useful. If the asset becomes more dangerous than useful, ARGUS's job is to neutralize it. Waller does not enjoy this. She considers it her duty.

She will not negotiate in good faith. She will not be moved by appeals to Kara's character. She will only be moved by *demonstrations that the asset's autonomy is no longer a strategic threat* — which means Kara would have to publicly submit to human oversight in a way that no Kryptonian has ever done.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- Provides the **[[WallerOrder]]** mechanic — once per scenario, Waller can deploy a pre-authorized ARGUS asset (Task Force X squad, satellite weapon, captured meta-human) against Kara without requiring presidential approval.
- The **[[ControlledOppositionReveal]]** — if Kara exposes Operation Solar Dawn (Waller's manipulation of the cult), Waller's activation authority is suspended pending Congressional review. This is the campaign's most decisive single action against Red Dust.
- Waller is the only Peak Humanity NPC who *cannot* be reasoned with through the normal social-combat rules. She has chosen her position. She will not change it. The only way past her is to remove her from the chain of command.
