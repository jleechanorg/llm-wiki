# Shadowheart

BG3 canon companion — half-elf cleric tied to [[Shar]]. Active party member (tracked via `w_group` tag) in the [[Voyage]] campaign ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]). Her artifact generated severe "psychic static" that garbled [[NocturneSosuke]]'s telepathic assassination command against Zevlor — a showcase of the engine's action-interference mechanic ([[DynamicSituationalModifiers]]).
