## Description

**Rook's Rest** is the barony awarded to Visenya Targaryen in 209 AC after she cleared the Stokeworth-to-Duskendale stretch of every bandit, cutthroat, and broken knight in a single campaigning season. It sits on the Rosby-Kingsroad choke, sixty miles south of King's Landing — strategically the only safe haven for sixty miles in any direction.

## Atmosphere

A small, well-kept holdfast that smells of woodsmoke and tar. The inner courtyard is clean. The sept is a converted bandit chapel, whitewashed. The stables smell of good horses. The servants are quiet. **There is no music; Visenya does not permit it.**

## Sub-locations

- **The Hall of Names** — Where the Wound Ledger is kept in a locked chest.
- **The Archer's Tower** — Visenya's private quarters.
- **The Black Sept** — The household guard barracks (50 broken men, fanatically loyal).
- **The Sept of Baelor** — The converted chapel (originally a bandit shrine).

## Hazard

The Black Sept are *not* trustworthy to strangers. Anyone who enters Rook's Rest without an introduction will be *politely* escorted to the gate within 30 minutes.
