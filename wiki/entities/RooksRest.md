# Rook's Rest

A barony near King's Landing in the Crownlands, awarded in campaign visenya v9 to sixteen-year-old [[VisenyaTargaryen]] by her father Lord [[MaekarTargaryen]] for clearing the Stokeworth-to-Duskendale stretch of bandits, cutthroats, and broken knights in a single campaigning season.

In ASOIAF canon Rook's Rest is a castle on Blackwater Bay (seat of House Staunton, later famous in the Dance of the Dragons); v9 repurposes it as the protagonist's contested seat — "a barony no older lord will acknowledge," garrisoned by a household guard of broken men personally loyal to the "Blood Dragon." It anchors her operating territory between the Blackwater and the Narrow Sea.
