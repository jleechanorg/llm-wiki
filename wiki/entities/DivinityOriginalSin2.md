# Divinity: Original Sin 2 (DOS2)

Larian Studios CRPG set in [[Rivellon]]; source material for the "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse" campaign anthology. The campaign adapts DOS2's tyranny/god-king ending branch — consuming [[LucianTheDivine]] and [[DallisTheHammer]] — into a D&D 5e-style Level 12–30 [[GestaltTier4Progression|gestalt]] framework rather than DOS2's native ruleset.

## Notable adaptation choices
- Source (DOS2's magic resource) is remapped onto Sorcery Points and custom [[SourceSmite]] mechanics
- DOS2's "tethered Veil" cosmology becomes the [[VeilMonopoly]] campaign engine
- Joins the wiki's roster of adapted properties (Overlord, ASOIAF, Bleach, Pathfinder) confirming the pattern that this player's campaigns adapt existing IPs into D&D hybrid systems
