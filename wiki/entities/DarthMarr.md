## Overview
Darth Marr ("The Patriot") is one of two candidate leaders of the [[CouncilOfTwelve]] traditionalist faction in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]), alongside [[DarthBaras]]. A SWTOR-canon Dark Council member, here cast as an ideological patriot who believes the Empire exists to serve the Sith Code, not one man — and that power should be taken, not inherited. His faction sends assassins to constantly test [[NocturneOldRepublic|Nocturne]] and seeks to prove the [[GoldenLineage]] incompetent or heretical so [[Vitiate]] returns power to the Council.
