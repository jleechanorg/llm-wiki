# Alexiel ("The Siren", Subject 7 — Nocturne)

Protagonist of the [[2026-08-04-alexiel-swtor-siren-master-bible|Alexiel SWTOR campaign]]. A 12-year-old genetically engineered High Human of the House of [[Vitiate]], Level 1 Jedi Consular / Sith Sorcerer gestalt built on the [[VoidExperimentClass]] chassis. Political prisoner / Imperial royalty held at a Jedi Academy; True Neutral (Fluid).

- **Stats**: STR 8 / DEX 14 / CON 14 / INT 16 / WIS 10 / CHA 18; HP 8, AC 12, 4 Force Points.
- **Features**: [[SirensCall]] (free Charm Person/Command with rationalized obedience), [[HaloEffect]] (WIS-save hesitation defense), [[EssenceDevourer]] (temp-HP drain + 24h feed requirement).
- **Psychology**: [[MaskOfTheInnocent]] public persona over a cold calculating interior; [[TheStatic]] (psychic residue of Vitiate and [[Vaylin]]); [[TransactionalAffection]]; narcissistic fragility about humiliation/disfigurement.
- **Relationships**: father [[Vitiate]] (dream contact), sister [[Vaylin]] (Static echo), supervising masters [[MasterOganDe]] (kind, therefore suspected) and [[Atris]] (sceptical), peer [[JaxPadawan]].

Fits the player's documented serpent-mastermind archetype alongside [[SosukeAizen]]'s campaigns — physically frail, socially armored, institutionally embedded.
