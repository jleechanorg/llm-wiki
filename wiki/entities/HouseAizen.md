# House Aizen

New rising power in [[BaldursGate]], fronted by [[AizenSosuke]] in the [[ShadowOverTheGate]] campaign. Goals shrouded in mystery; considerable resources and charisma; influence potentially extending into every other faction ([[FlamingFist]], [[TheGuild]], [[KnightsOfTheShield]], [[CultOfTheDeadThree]]).

Distinct from but related to [[house-sosuke]], the noble-house identity used elsewhere in the campaign arc — both are player-faction fronts for Aizen's ambitions. The four "A Night of Unrest" hooks are all secretly signed by this faction's "unusual efficiency" ([[SubtleInfluenceCampaignHook]]).
