# Dr. Aris Thorne

**Class**: Level 12 Transmutation Wizard / Artificer. **Role**: Solar Radiation Researcher & Bio-Analyst — operates Kara's personal research lab.

## Background

Disgraced former LexCorp lead physicist. Defected after discovering that Lex Luthor's "Solar Harvest Project" was in fact an atmospheric Kryptonite-dispersal weapon system designed to render all Kryptonians mortal at the press of a button. Aris copied thirty-seven gigabytes of evidence, walked out of the LexCorp tower with the security guards calling his name, and made his way to the only place he knew could protect him: the front yard of a woman he had once dismissed as a "divine overcompensation fantasy."

Kara answered the door. He showed her the data. She let him in. He has been with her ever since.

## Defining Moment

Reading the spec sheet for Project Red Dust and realizing the man he had spent eight years working for was willing to render the entire planet's biosphere hostile to Kryptonians — including children, including Superman's wife, including the unborn Kryptonian child Clark had just discovered Lois was carrying. **Choice**: keep the salary, or copy the evidence. **Perceived failure**: he waited two weeks before copying. He still counts the Kryptonite satellites that went up during that interval.

## Kara's Personal Lab

Aris operates a non-lethal containment laboratory in the sub-basement of Kara's residence. He has built:
- Solar absorption meters that quantify Kara's yellow-sun reserve in real-time
- A **non-lethal restraining force-field generator** that holds a human body immobile without crushing it (so Kara does not have to physically restrain cultists and risk killing them)
- A **Kryptonite-shielded medical bay** for treating wounded humans inside Kara's home without contaminating Kara herself
- A red-solar radiation prototype emitter (in case the campaign ever requires Kara to *deliberately* operate at mortal level to infiltrate a human institution)

## Relationship to Kara

Aris is the only Inner Circle member who addresses Kara by her Kryptonian name ("Kara") rather than the title. He is also the only one she calls by first name without "Dr." prefix. They have a collegial intimacy that is not romantic and never will be — both understand that the campaign requires her to remain alone, and Aris's presence as a colleague-not-lover is the only relationship she can sustain that does not collapse under the weight of her divinity.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- Provides the technical counter to **Scenario C (Lex-Waller Proxy Weapon)** — Aris can prove that any "Kryptonian deforestation" event was staged by analyzing Kryptonian biological residue.
- Once per session, can reverse-engineer an enemy magical effect's energy signature in 10 minutes, granting Kara advantage on the next save against that effect's school.
- Builds the **[[ProjectRedDust]] Decryption Kit** — Aris's multi-year project to crack the trigger codes without J'onn J'onzz's help. If the campaign requires it, the kit grants a guaranteed partial success on Red Dust infiltration (3 of 5 trigger holders found instead of 2).
