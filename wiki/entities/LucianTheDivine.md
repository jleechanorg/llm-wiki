# Lucian the Divine

Canonical DOS2 figure — the Divine, leader of the Divine Order in [[Rivellon]]. In the "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse" campaign premise, Lucian was **consumed by the player character** at the apex of the First Garden alongside [[DallisTheHammer]], transferring his share of divine Source into the protagonist and enabling the [[VeilMonopoly]].

## Campaign role
- Pre-campaign casualty; his stolen radiance now flashes from the protagonist's finesse blades
- His absence removes the pantheon-anchored Veil, motivating the sole-anchor tension of the campaign
