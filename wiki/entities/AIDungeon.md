# AI Dungeon

AI text-adventure platform used as the comparison baseline in the player's [[Voyage]] evaluation ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]). The player judged Voyage "night and day" better than AI Dungeon for the 107-turn [[NocturneSosuke]] campaign, while still flagging Voyage's leveling/ability-point bugs and the [[Worldsmith]] world-builder gap.
