# House Vaelaros

One of the Forty Families of the [[ValyrianFreehold]] in the [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]] campaign. [[SarielAsh]] serves as its "Sovereign Left Hand" — a shadow weapon deployed by her tyrannical father, whose cold indifference paradoxically grants her operational freedom.

The house embodies the campaign's core theme: an insular aristocratic autocracy rotted by blood-feuds, vanity, and breeding-monopoly politics, blind to the strategic value of Westeros. It is the sovereignty-cage Sariel intends to escape — the structural counterpart of [[HouseVitiate]] in the [[NocturneOldRepublic]] line.
