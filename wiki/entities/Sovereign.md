# Sovereigns

The 1,000 god-tier rival entities populating the [[PanSubstrate]] under the Sovereign Protocol. Every Sovereign possesses a unique [[SovereignLogic]] — a named method of attacking reality (examples given: "Offensive Entropy," "Celestial Bureaucracy," "Bioluminescent Assimilation") — and holds territory measured in Substrate Points ([[SubstratePointMetric]]). They are stratified by the [[SovereignHierarchy]]: Emperors (rank 1–10, Continents), Conductors (rank 11–100, Nations), and Administrators (rank 101–1000, Cities). The upgraded PC becomes a **Terminal Administrator** entering at or below the Administrator bracket, with 2–3 Emperors authored as ideological opponents of the PC's specific Logic, plus one Rank 1–10 entity flagged Neutral/Curious and one Hostile/Threatened at session start.
