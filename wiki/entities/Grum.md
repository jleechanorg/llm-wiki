# Grum

True Neutral ogre bouncer of the [[BloomShadow|Bloom Shadow]] in [[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]]. Wears a monocle because [[MadamZylra]] makes him; surprisingly articulate but violently obedient. Patrols the Hallway of Silks during the escape sequence.

**Nocturne's hook**: [[NocturneSosuke]] once treated an ear infection Zylra wouldn't pay a cleric for — he likes her and may look the other way for food or a kind word, making him the module's example of pre-seeded soft leverage over muscle.
