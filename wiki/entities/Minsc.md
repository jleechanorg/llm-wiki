# Minsc — "The Stone Sentinel"

In the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath: went into a berserker rage upon seeing [[Jaheira]]'s fate as a vampire spawn. [[GaleOfWaterdeep]], finding the ranger "disruptive to the arcane equilibrium", turned him to stone. The petrified ranger stands as a garden ornament in the plaza beneath Gale's floating tower — dissent literally decorativized, a signature beat of the [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]].
