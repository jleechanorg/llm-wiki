## Summary
The Thieves' Guild's Hand (regional boss) for the Silverflow river trade. Sharp, calculating, professionally violent, but fair within his code. Negotiates a 12% commission deal with Alexiel for use of her cellar tunnels. Returns every fortnight with coin and 'medical' contracts. Calls her 'a dangerous woman.'

## Key Quotes
> "A warlock in a ruin. My man said you had a bit of iron in your spine, but he didn't mention the noble name." — Daran, on first meeting Alexiel at the Broken Cask (Turn 7)

> "I will agree to twelve percent and the immediate handling of your back taxes. Fifteen is only possible if you can ensure the river tunnel remains clear of silt and local riff-raff." — Daran, the final deal (Turn 32)

> "You are a dangerous woman, Alexiel. Most people in my line of work prefer to stay in the shadows where it's safe." — Daran, after signing the lease (Turn 33)
