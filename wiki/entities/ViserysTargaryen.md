# Viserys Targaryen (v5)

In Visenya v5, Prince Viserys commands the City Watch of King's Landing — expanded under martial law into a **6,000-man paramilitary force** described as brutal, corrupt, and "loyal only to the highest bidder." He polices the refugee-swollen capital of the Dragon's Coil during [[TheLongDiscord]], making him the junta's street-level enforcer and an obvious rival/obstacle for [[VisenyaTargaryen]].
