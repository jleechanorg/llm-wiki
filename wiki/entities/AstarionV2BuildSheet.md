# Astarion V2 Build Sheet (Empire of Shadow)

Mechanical-only build sheet for the **Astarion-only** version of the [[BaldursGate|Empire of Shadow]] campaign. Supersedes the V1 full-party document. Astarion is the sole player character; the rest of the roster (Shadowheart, Gale, Minthara, etc.) is NPC-controlled and intentionally omitted from this page.

> **Source of truth:** `/Users/jleechan/campaign_v2_astarion_build.md` (10578 bytes). This wiki entry is a structured cross-reference into the existing [[Astarion]] entity + [[GloomStalker]] / [[GloomstalkerRanger]] concepts.
