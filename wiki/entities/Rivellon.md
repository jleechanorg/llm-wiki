# Rivellon

The world of the Divinity franchise ([[DivinityOriginalSin2]]). In the "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse" campaign, the setting's cosmic lifeblood (Source) has been systematically monopolized by the player character after the tyranny ending: the Veil is intact but anchored solely to the protagonist's living soul rather than a pantheon of gods (see [[VeilMonopoly]]).

## Campaign role
- Post-apotheosis sandbox for a Level 12–30 [[GestaltTier4Progression|gestalt]] god-shadow protagonist
- Former divine anchors [[LucianTheDivine]] and [[DallisTheHammer]] were consumed at the First Garden
