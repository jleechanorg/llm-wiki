# Black Adam / Teth-Adam

**Class**: Level 21 Sorcerer / Fighter. **Apex Five member**. Sovereign dictator of Kahndaq.

## Threat

Wields magic lightning and strength matching a yellow-sun Kryptonian. Ancient Kryptonian history: Adam was empowered by the Wizard Shazam using magic from a time before Superman; he remembers Kryptonian legends as a contemporary threat. If forced into direct combat, Kara and Adam together would level North Africa. The campaign never allows that fight.

## Stance

Explicitly defies the United Nations by declaring Kahndaq a safe haven for the Solar Dawn Kryptonian cult. Uses the cult as political human shields — daring Kara to invade his sovereign border and spark a war between Level 20+ entities. Has ancient personal reasons to resent Kryptonian cultural arrogance (Kryptonian legends, in his era, treated empowered mortals as curiosities to be catalogued, not allies).

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- **[[KahndaqStandoff]]** — Scenario A in the campaign bible. Cultists from the Solar Dawn flee to Kahndaq after committing terror attacks in Geneva; Black Adam grants them asylum; the UN demands that Kara extract them; Adam treats her entry as an act of war.
- The cult's presence in Kahndaq is *Adam's leverage*, not Adam's endorsement. He does not personally support the Solar Dawn's violence — he supports the principle that Kahndaq can grant asylum to anyone it chooses. The cult's presence is a chess piece.
- Kara must extract the cult leaders *without* starting a Level 20+ war. Solution vectors: diplomatic negotiation with Adam (using Diana as an intermediary); covert extraction by [[CommanderMarcusVance]]'s House Guard; staged exile (offering the cultists passage to a sanctuary nation in exchange for Adam's neutrality).
