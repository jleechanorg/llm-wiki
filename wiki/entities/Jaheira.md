# Jaheira — "The Risen Martyr"

In the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath: attempted to organize the Harpers against the [[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]], was ambushed by [[Astarion]] and [[Minthara]], slain, and raised as a mindless, mocking vampire spawn who serves drinks at Astarion's decadent galas. The cruelest of the [[DefinitiveCompanionFates]] — resistance leadership converted into a trophy of the regime.
