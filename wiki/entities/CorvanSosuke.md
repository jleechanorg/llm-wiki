# Corvan Sosuke

Father of [[AizenSosuke]] and [[MalachiSosuke]]; patriarch of noble [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]] of [[BaldursGate]].

## In the campaign

- With his wife [[SeraphinaSosuke]], attempted the [[GrandConjunction]] — a ritual to physically manifest [[Bane]] in Faerûn under the belief they could control him.
- Per Aizen's account to the [[KnightsOfTheShield]] (delivered as part of a [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]], so reliability is suspect), Corvan died "realizing the treachery" when Bane moved to consume the channelers.
