# The Dark Urge

Canonical BG3 player-origin character, used as the user-protagonist of the [[SanguineArchitecture]] campaign — consistent with the `world_reference/` precedent set by [[NocturneSosuke]] in [[ShatteredRose]].

- **Build**: female, gestalt Gloomstalker Ranger + Assassin Rogue, level 12 — the same gestalt stealth-predator chassis as the Visenya v9 "Apex Stalker" line (cf. [[StressLineSight]]).
- **Apotheosis**: drained the [[Netherbrain]] and the Crown of Karsus of primordial energy, then consumed her father [[Bhaal]]'s divine spark in the Undercity Temple.
- **Current state**: incubating **Goddess of Murder**, a Quasi-Deity anchored to [[BaldursGate]], ruling through [[SubmissiveDeath]] and Tragic Betrayal rather than massacre.
- **Campaign arc**: ascend the [[GodMechanicsSevenRankLadder]] from Quasi-Deity to Greater Goddess while waging plane-spanning deicide against the Faerûnian pantheon under the [[GodMechanicsV2Overlay]] rules and its [[DeicideCost]] economy.
