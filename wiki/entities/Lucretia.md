# Lucretia

Domina of the House of [[Batiatus]] in the [[2026-08-04-gaia-julia-v2-golden-republic|Golden Republic (Gaia Julia v2)]] campaign, drawn from the Spartacus mythos. Alongside her husband she receives [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]]'s masked-heiress performance — the bored, flirtatious rich girl "testing her boundaries" — without knowing she is hosting a Julian singularity hunting [[Spartacus]].
