# Julius Caesar

Gaius Julius Caesar as he appears across the Gaia Julia alternate-history campaign line — always the father of [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] and always an apotheosized ruler, but at different points of divergence per version:

- **v2 (82 BCE, Golden Republic)**: pre-dates his rise; the dynasty frame centers the First Founder mythology instead.
- **v5 (73 BCE, The Gilded Trap)**: already **Dictator Perpetuo**; the [[ThirdServileWar]] erupts as a challenge to his divine order. Described as ruling by fear, contrasted with Gaia's stated doctrine of ruling by love.
- **v6 (71 BCE, Iron Eagle: Vengeance)**: Emperor; the Republic is already dead.
- **v3 (44 BCE, Pax Caesara)**: survives the Ides of March by catching Casca's wrist and unleashing [[Auctoritas]], becoming "the Phoenix."

The constant across versions: Caesar is the campaign's fixed pole of overt divine power, against which his daughter's covert, social-engineering style of power is defined.
