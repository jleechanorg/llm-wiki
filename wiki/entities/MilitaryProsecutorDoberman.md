# Military Prosecutor Doberman

Korean drama (2022, military/legal) used as the campaign world for the [[Sariel]] campaign — the first ingested campaign set in a contemporary real-world milieu (Korean military courts, chaebol corporations) rather than Rome, Faerûn, or an isekai frame. Named drama characters appearing in the campaign: [[DoBaeMan]] (prosecutor protagonist), [[NohTaeNam]] (chaebol heir), [[YongMoonGu]] (lawyer). Corporate anchor: [[IMDefense]]. D&D 5e mechanics are re-flavored to fit (Bard College of Swords as bodyguard skills, spells as social suggestion).
