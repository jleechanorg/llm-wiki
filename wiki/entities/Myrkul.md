# Myrkul

Forgotten Realms god of Death, one of the Dead Three. In the Aizen god-campaign summary, [[AizenSosuke]] — already a nascent God of Tyranny after consuming [[Bane]] — **ambushes and consumes Myrkul's avatar**, adding the Death portfolio and elevating himself to Greater Deity. This second deicide is what draws [[Ao-the-Overgod]]'s attention and sets up the [[Conceptual-Debate-Victory]]. See [[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]] for the mechanic.
