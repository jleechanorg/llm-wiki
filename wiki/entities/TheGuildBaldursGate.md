# The Guild (Baldur's Gate)

The organized crime network of [[BaldursGate]] in "The Shadow over the Gate" ([[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-1-1-300]]). Described as having a possible power vacuum or an ambitious new leader emerging — an obvious infiltration vector for [[HouseAizen]]. Featured in the Elfsong Tavern brawl hook, where a Guild-aligned gang clashes with a rival gang bearing ritualistic tattoos.
