# Arcane Athenaeum of Baldur's Gate

Also called "the Arcane Academy." A crumbling yet prestigious magical institution in the Upper City's Sea Ward of [[BaldursGate]], teaching Weave theory, alchemy, and history to noble scions (ages 15-18) and gifted commoners. Post-Absolute faculty shortages (professors tadpoled or fled) left oversight lax.

In the teen-era Aizen campaign it is [[SosukeAizen]]'s primary asset: publicly he is "Sosuke," a meek state-ward junior scribe; in reality the academy is his **blackmail factory and recruitment nexus** ([[AcademyBlackmailFactory]]) — communal teas spiked with [[EssenceOfLethe]] and [[WhisperdustNarcotic|Whisperdust]], a "study group" that is a velvet trap, and broken heirs converted into Garden fodder, spies, and puppets. Staff roster includes [[ElowenThorne|Professor Elowen Thorne]].
