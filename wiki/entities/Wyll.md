# Wyll — "The Broken Blade"

In the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath: L12+ Warlock (The Fiend)/Paladin (Oath of Devotion) who failed to break his pact and watched his father die through [[Astarion]]'s political maneuvering. Wielding the Infernal Rapier in the Potent Robe, he is trapped eternally in Avernus as Mizora's personal attack dog, fighting a hopeless Blood War while his soul slowly calcifies into a Lemure. One of the bleakest entries in [[DefinitiveCompanionFates]] — the devoted hero as the timeline's designated casualty.
