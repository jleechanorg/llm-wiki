# Superman / Kal-El / Clark Kent

**Class**: Level 25 Gestalt Fighter (Battle Master) / Monk (Way of the Open Hand). Wisdom-based. **Role**: Primary NPC Ally — Kara's emotional anchor and philosophical foil.

## Relationship to Kara

Clark is the only person on Earth who understands what it feels like to hear every heartbeat. The only person who can sit beside her in silence without that silence becoming a sermon. The only person who can tell her she is wrong and have her believe him.

He maintains the Clark Kent persona — the bumbling journalist — because he believes the lie is a small price for the human connections it allows. He respects Kara's choice to live openly but worries that her honesty will provoke humanity into a fatal preemptive strike. He will be the first person Kara calls when the ICC subpoena arrives, and the first person who tells her to attend the trial.

Their private conversations happen on the summit of Mount Everest — the only place on Earth quiet enough for them to talk without hearing billions of heartbeats. Those conversations are the campaign's emotional anchor.

## Stance on the Crisis

Clark's position on the Malakor execution: Kara was right to act. She was wrong to execute him on live television. The first saved millions. The second made her a target of the ICC. Clark argues that a covert intervention would have produced the same outcome without creating the political crisis — and Kara argues that covert intervention is what got humanity to the point where it needed Kryptonian saviors in the first place.

This is the central philosophical tension of the campaign.

## Mechanical Role

- A direct ally in combat scenarios, but the campaign rarely calls for it.
- Provides **[[SupermanDoctrine]]** — Clark's position on how Kara should handle the ICC trial: attend, take off the cape, submit to whatever sentence the humans deliver, prove that Kryptonians do not rule Earth as tyrants.
- Provides **[[EverestSummit]]** — once per session, Kara can join Clark on the summit for a quiet conversation. This restores Kara's composure, removes the Suffocating Worship condition for the rest of the session, and unlocks a unique Insight / Persuasion option against any human NPC that day (Clark's worldview rubs off).
