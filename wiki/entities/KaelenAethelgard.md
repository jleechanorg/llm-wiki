# Kaelen

Subordinate/retainer to [[SaitaAethelgard]] in *The Hollow Zenith* ([[2026-08-04-saita-v3-hollow-zenith]]). Appears in the interaction shorthand as the recipient of image-discipline orders: *"Kaelen, wipe that blood off your armor. We are projecting invincibility today, not butchery. Move."* — establishing that Saita's mission team enforces the Empire's [[WeaknessMaskingDiplomacy]] aesthetic at the level of daily grooming. No class, level, or surname given in the v3 source.
