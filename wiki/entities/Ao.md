# Ao — the Overgod

Forgotten Realms overgod, arbiter of divine balance, recurring end-boss-of-cosmology figure in the Aizen/Nocturne campaigns.

## Role in Thay v1 ([[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]])

Ao intervenes not because [[NocturneSosuke]] was evil but because she was a **Singularity** — "not playing the Great Game; she was deleting the board." When she prepared to invade Mount Celestia ("Invoice the Heavens"), Ao enacted the **[[GeometricFirewall]]**: he could not destroy her (she was woven so deeply into the Lower Planes that killing her would collapse the Hells and Abyss into a black hole), so he **isolated** her — severing the Lower Planes from the Material Plane and sealing her inside the [[ShatteredRose]] outside of time.

This balance-enforcer characterization extends the escalation logic of [[DivineDetectionTiers]] and [[DivineImposition]]: gods tolerate ambition up to the point where a player threatens the board itself.

## Role in the Divine Deception V0 prompt ([[2026-08-04-god-campaign-prompt-v0-divine-deception]])

Two mechanical roles: (1) the only row of the [[QuantifiedDivineReferenceTable]] with **Infinite** in every column (HP, AC, DPP, attack, legendary resists, damage); (2) the **silent sanctioner** of the player's Layer 4 Hidden Truth — Ao knowingly tolerates an unsanctioned Rank A entity as long as it serves "Absolute Codification" and does not break Pantheon stability. Exceeding the "Transcendent Template" while in the Layer 3 Primary Mask triggers Ao's suspicion. Consistent with the Thay v1 stance: Ao polices board-threatening behavior, not evil.
