# Baelor Targaryen

Uncle of [[VisenyaTargaryen]] in the Visenya v4 campaign. Respects her intellect but fears she is a tyrant in the making — he suspects she is stripping the world of free will and privately frames her rule-to-come as "A Goddess's Playground." Serves as the campaign's in-family moral counterweight to Visenya's cold-geometry worldview.
