# The Blackguard

Military/enforcement organization in the v6 campaign "The Winter Web." [[VisenyaTargaryen]] serves as its **Lord Commander** at age 14; her "Blood Dragon" reputation comes from the Blackguard's clinical execution of thousands of bandits and syndicates in the south. She is on leave from this post during the Northern "vacation."
