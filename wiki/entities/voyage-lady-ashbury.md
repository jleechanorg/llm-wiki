## Summary
Wealthy neighbor of Riverside Manor, initially a rival trying to buy the Tethlar estate for 50 florins. Becomes a political and romantic ally after a multi-turn seduction/tour of the manor (Turns 44–54). Co-invests in the Tethlar smuggling operation and helps plan the Crown-surveyor corruption.

## Key Quotes
> "My house has a scout, a man named Varick. He knows every deer trail and bog from here to the coast." — Lady Ashbury, offering her spy to Alexiel (Turn 88)

> "I had heard the rumors of what the Tethlar blood could do, Alexiel, but I took them for tavern tales meant to frighten children. To send such things into the very heart of the palace..." — Lady Ashbury, after seeing the Gloomstalker summoning (Turn 89)

> "A private weapons contract is a far sweeter fruit than a pittance of river tax." — Lady Ashbury, on the King-supply plan (Turn 88)
