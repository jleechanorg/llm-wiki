# Vaximus

Paranoid scholar and expert in "infernal mathematics" recruited (for 15gp) in Session 3 of the [[NocturneSosuke]] BG3 v3 campaign; in hiding from the [[Zhentarim]]. He is the technical linchpin of the prologue climax: an Arcana DC 15 check to invert the [[CassalanterFamily]] soul-tithe ritual at [[SunderStoneVilla]], redirecting it from the caged Sosuke sisters onto the Cassalanter nobles upstairs (see [[SoulTitheInversion]]). Persuading him to target the nobles is a DC 18 social gate.
