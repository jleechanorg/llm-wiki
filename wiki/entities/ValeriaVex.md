# Valeria Vex

Player character of the *Blades of the High and Low* campaign. Fifteen-year-old youngest daughter of a border baron and senior squire at the [[RoyalKnightAcademy]]. Level 6 [[SpellbladeGestalt]] (Fighter + Bladesinger Wizard). Secretly the reincarnation of the [[WarriorQueen]] who unified the surface kingdoms a thousand years ago — but she rejects that life's burden of governance and seeks freedom, mastery, and worthy opponents instead.

## Traits

- Calm under pressure; direct, concise, impatient with empty ceremony; animated about swordplay, tactics, training, and [[AetherPrimordialEnergy|Aether]].
- Morally serious despite wanting freedom — does not casually accept preventable civilian deaths or abuse of people under her protection.
- Afraid of stagnation, not responsibility itself; rejects responsibility that becomes permanent ownership of others' lives.
- Habits: tests balance points of utensils/sticks/weapons while thinking; studies posture, muscle tension, grip, and escape routes; trains before dawn; deliberately underperforms to avoid obligations or the wrong attention.

## Hidden power

Her [[AetherPrimordialEnergy|Aether]] bypasses [[Highlanders|Highlander]] bloodline restrictions on Lowlander casting, letting her function as a full gestalt. Publicly she reads as an unusually beautiful, talented, slightly strange young noble — not an ancient apex combatant. Reveals are governed by the [[WitnessedRevealMechanic]]: knowledge of her true ability spreads per witness, never globally.

## Contrast within the wiki

Unlike [[SaitaAethelgard]] (undercover imperial herald) or power-fantasy rulers elsewhere in the corpus, Valeria's concealment serves *escape from* authority rather than projection of it.
