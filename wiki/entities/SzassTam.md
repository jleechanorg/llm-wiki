# Szass Tam

Lich regent of [[Thay]] in Forgotten Realms canon. Cited in "[[2026-08-04-bumpkin-swordsman-rust-bound-blade|The Rust-Bound Blade]]" campaign bible as the archetypal example of one mythic-tier exception class to the [[Level12MortalCeiling]]: "ancient undead horrors who have bartered away their mortality" — beings operating at Levels 13–20, fundamentally unanchored from the standard mathematics of the Weave.
