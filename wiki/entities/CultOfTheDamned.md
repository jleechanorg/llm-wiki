# Cult of the Damned

In the [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|Deceiver's Crown]] alternate timeline, the Cult is **not** a death cult of raving fanatics — it is a mercenary priesthood trading favors for power, one of the sentient factions inside the [[SentientScourge]]. [[NocturneRavencrest]] negotiates with it through her envoy [[SylvanThePale]] as part of her shadow stack.
