# High Priestess Vespera (Elena Vance)

**Class**: Level 12 Mastermind Rogue. **Role**: Legal Strategist & Cult Administrator — Kara's representative, press secretary, and political advisor.

## Background

A former United Nations human-rights prosecutor who resigned after realizing international law was too slow to prevent genocides. She watched, in real time, the Malakor ethnic cleansing begin — and watched the Security Council debate whether the situation met the threshold for intervention. By the time the vote concluded, three villages had been depopulated. She submitted her resignation that evening and founded the **Sanctum of the Sun** — a hyper-organized global NGO that channels religious devotion toward Kara into legal accountability, refugee support, and disaster response.

## Defining Moment

Sitting in the Security Council chamber as the genocide she had been warning about began. Voting on a procedural motion while satellite feeds showed entire neighborhoods being cleared. **Choice**: continue to fight the slow legal battle, or quit and build something faster. **Perceived failure**: she chose correctly, but she will never stop hearing the names of the people who died during the vote.

## Relationship to Kara

Elena does not bow. Does not chant. Does not treat Kara as a goddess — she treats Kara as a *principal she represents*. She is the only person in the Inner Circle who argues with Kara about legal strategy in front of the press, who tells Kara when a decision will produce a worse outcome than inaction, and who has refused two direct orders (once during the ICC pre-trial motions, once during the Malakor aftermath cleanup) because the orders would have created legal exposure that would have lost the larger case.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- Provides advantage on Kara's Insight checks during courtroom encounters.
- Unlocks the **Lawyer Trap** counter-action against Lex Luthor's prosecution moves: once per session, Elena can negate a Lex-prepared legal trap by anticipating the doctrine in advance.
- Carries Kara's diplomatic signature — anything Elena signs is legally binding on Kara, which she uses strategically to commit Kara to compromises Kara would not personally endorse.
