# Nocturne, the Silent Blade

A distinct incarnation in the Nocturne protagonist line (see [[NocturneRavencrest]] and [[NocturneSosuke]] for prior variants). This version: 19 years old, Ranger ([[GloomStalkerConclave]]) Level 5, Haunted One / Disgraced Noble background, sworn to the [[Zhentarim]] and operating in post-crisis [[BaldursGate]].

## Identity

- **Title**: The Silent Blade
- **Archetype**: [[VenomousOrchid]] — spectrum between temptress (Lust, FMA) and slayer (The Predator)
- **Masks**: the Gilded Spider (social seductress, [[WeaponizedIntimacy]]) and the Butcher in the Dark (terror operative who uses gore as messaging)

## Drives

1. **Price of Perfection** — dominance through aggressive fee negotiation; overpayment = the client's submission.
2. **Dominion Through Fear and Desire** — goal of becoming urban legend in Baldur's Gate.
3. **Art of Ruin** — finds and shatters stress points in reputations, vaults, and bodies.

## Hidden Compulsion

The [[JohanExperiment]]: seduce moral paragons into betraying their codes to prove civilization is a lie, then execute them when they fail.

## Mechanics

Umbral Sight, Dread Ambusher, and Disguise Self used as an infiltration toolkit (personas: barmaid, noblewoman, distressed traveler).
