# Abyssal Demon King

Unnamed reigning Demon King of the infernal capital; grandfather of [[Nocticula]]. Head of a royal lineage of "catastrophic power" whose culture is defined by chaotic consumption and — in Nocticula's assessment — 'inefficient' cruelty, a flawed system of diminishing returns.

His court presumably dispatches the **Retrieval Squads** hunting Nocticula, making him the standing off-screen pressure on the campaign alongside the human Purge Slayers. Whether he is an antagonist, an eventual negotiating partner, or leverage for Nocticula's transcendence goal is left open by the source bio.
