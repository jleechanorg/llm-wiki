# Mal'Ganis

Nathrezim (dreadlord) of Warcraft canon, re-imagined in [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]] alternate timeline. **Did not die at [[ArthasMenethil]]'s hand in Northrend**: he anticipated the [[LichKing]]'s betrayal, left a sentient shadow-avatar in Lordaeron, and bound himself to [[NocturneRavencrest]] via the [[DualCustodyPact]] — as partner-in-finance, not master.

Described as "a Nathrezim who out-thought Frostmourne." He receives intelligence on Arthas's pursuit through Nocturne's shadow stack and is one pole of the campaign's central alliance; the pact's mutual-destruction clause keeps the partnership balanced.
