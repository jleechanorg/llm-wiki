## Summary
Small, jittery miller at the old stone bridge over the Silverflow. Houses the Guild's lifestone crates in his cellar, terrorized by giant rats that grew unnaturally large in the damp. Plays crucial role distracting Silverflow guards while Alexiel escapes with the lifestone. Alexiel later brings him before the King as a misleading proxy.

## Key Quotes
> "You are here for the... the problem? They are beneath the floor, Lady. They have chewed through the grain sacks and now they bite at the very stone." — Hobb, leading Alexiel to the rats (Turn 8)

> "It is a simple arrangement, Lady. Purely business. Master Daran provides the protection and the shipments, and I... well, I provide the space and the silence." — Hobb, explaining his relationship with Daran (Turn 14)

> "Mind the grain! If they tear, I'm ruined — watch your feet, they're everywhere!" — Hobb, panicked during the rat fight (Turn 9)
