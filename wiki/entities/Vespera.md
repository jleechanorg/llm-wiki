# Vespera

Chaotic Neutral half-elf bard at the [[BloomShadow|Bloom Shadow]] in [[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]] — the house "favorite" before [[NocturneSosuke]] arrived, and now her burning rival.

**Threat mechanic**: if she catches Nocturne sneaking out she screams for the guards unless silenced socially (Deception +7 about a wine errand, or Intimidation over a stolen tip jar) or physically — the module's escape-phase social obstacle.
