# The Disfavored

Elite, honor-bound legion of [[Kyros]]' army under [[GravenAshe]]; rivals of the [[ScarletChorus]]. In [[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension|The Architecture of Ascension]], Nocturne obsesses over Disfavored supply-line logistics as one of the "precision" outlets of the Wounded Architect psychology — competence as leverage over the faction.
