## Summary
Stout human woman with ink-stained fingers, registrar of the Adventurer League at the Tower of Venture in Larion. Gates copper rank, distributes contracts, refuses to be bypassed by charm, magic, or attempted heists.

## Key Quotes
> "There you go. Officially copper rank. Try not to lose them. Replacements cost five florins in a lecture you don't want to hear."

> "It's weird, it's damp, and it's all yours."

> "Touch that platinum board again, and I will personally see your charter suspended before the bells toll for lunch. Get out of my line."
