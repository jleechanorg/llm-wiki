## Overview
Darth Baras ("The Schemer") is one of two candidate leaders of the [[CouncilOfTwelve]] traditionalist faction in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]), alongside [[DarthMarr]]. A SWTOR-canon spymaster Sith Lord, here representing the intrigue wing of the Old Guard: the Council hates the [[GoldenLineage]] because inherited demigod children end the Sith meritocracy. Their method against [[NocturneOldRepublic|Nocturne]] is constant assassin "tests" and political maneuvering to discredit [[Vitiate]]'s heirs.
