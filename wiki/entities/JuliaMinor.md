# Julia Minor ("The Zealot")

Second sister (22) in the Gaia v3 [[PaxCaesaraEmpire]] campaign; daughter of [[JuliusCaesar]] and High Priestess of **Venus Genetrix** (the Julian ancestral cult). **Fanatical** — believes the family is literally divine and "unwittingly feeds your ego by worshipping the ground you walk on." Narrative function: an in-house divinity feedback loop for [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]]'s god-complex, echoing the divine-ascension arcs in [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]]-adjacent campaigns.
