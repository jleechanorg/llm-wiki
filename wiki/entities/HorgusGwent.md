## Overview
Horgus Gwent is a wealthy noble and regular client of the Bloom Shadow brothel who serves as the catalyst for the V4 revenge plot. He possesses a Soul Coin bearing Nocturne's sister Lyra's face and brings the Cassalanter Ascension Gala invitation that sets the story in motion. His remark "They bottled them... vintage currency" reveals the fate of Nocturne's sisters.

## 2026-08-04 v4 revision ([[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]])
- Pays a premium "for the irony of defiling a Sosuke" but genuinely believes Nocturne desires him — the module calls this her masterpiece of manufactured love.
- Won Lyra's Soul Coin "off Victoro's ledger-keeper" at a high-stakes table; invited to the Ascension as a ritual **Witness**.
- Scene 1 encounter: Commoner stats, prone and poisoned (drunk); three resolution lanes (seductive kill, cold theft, first magic manifestation). Listed afterward in the Dramatis Personae as "Deceased/Incapacitated. The catalyst."
