## Summary
Massive, muck-dripping sewer dweller (suedeweller) who claims the moss of the deep as his personal feeding ground. Scowls at adventurers, hisses them out of his tunnels, but accepts a short sharp nod from Dracos as a farewell.

## Key Quotes
> (hisses, points at drainage pipe, makes it clear he wants the singers out of his tunnels)

> "What do you call a fish with no eyes? A fash, safe hunting neighbor." — Dracos's farewell to him
