# Dragonstone

Island fortress-seat of the Blacks during the Dance of the Dragons and setting of *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]), 129 AC.

## In This Campaign

The Dragonmont — the island's volcano — hosts the riderless dragons that [[RhaenyraTargaryen]]'s Dragonseeds attempt to claim during [[TheSowing]]. The opening scene is set in a hellscape cavern of sulfur and heat where [[Vermithor]] laires, with Dragonkeepers controlling access and closing the trials each day. As a Valyrian-founded outpost it also grounds the [[NoblestDialect]] mechanic: the island is thick with Valyrian descendants susceptible to High Valyrian authority.
