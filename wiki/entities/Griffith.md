# Griffith

Antagonist of Kentaro Miura's *Berserk*; cited in the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign source as the "profound, transcendent ambition" component of the [[SerpentKingArchetype]] composite. His trajectory (sacrificing companions for apotheosis) maps directly onto the campaign's [[PortableAbsolutePower]] drive — allies as scaffolding for transcendence — and echoes the divine-ambition throughline of [[ProjectApotheosis]] and [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] in the wider campaign corpus.
