# Kara Zor-El / Supergirl — Level 1–30 Character Sheet

**Class System**: Gestalt Fighter (Eldritch Knight) / Monk (Way of the Open Hand). Wisdom-unified spellcasting (per player direction). Gearless (unarmed strikes ARE her Weapon Bond). Wisdom serves as both Monk Ki Save DC and Eldritch Knight Spell Save DC / Spell Attack rolls — a single unified stat engine.

**Alignment**: Neutral Good. **Background**: Off-World Survivor / Planetary Protector. **Archetype**: Gearless Solar Goddess & Arcane-Martial Hybrid.

## Unified Combat & Spell Statistics

- **Base Unarmored AC**: 10 + DEX (5) + WIS (5) = **20**. Reaches **23** at Level 30 via Epic ASI.
- **Layered Defense Engine**: Base 20 → Shield reaction (+5, AC 25) → Haste concentration (+2, AC 27) → Blur/Mirror Image (attackers have disadvantage) → Empty Body (resistance to all damage except force for 1 minute).
- **Hit Points at Level 20**: 224 HP (15 at Level 1 + 19 × 11).
- **Hit Points at Level 30**: 334 HP (15 at Level 1 + 29 × 11), +40 from Epic Boon of Fortitude at L27.
- **Speed**: 60 ft base walking → +30 ft Unarmored Movement → **90 ft**. Flying speed: 120 ft base, scales to **200 ft** at Level 30.
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies**: STR, DEX, CON, WIS (full +11 to +14 progression). At Level 14, Diamond Soul grants proficiency in ALL saving throws.
- **Spell Save DC & Ki Save DC** (unified): 8 + Proficiency Bonus + WIS (+5).

## Kryptonian Racial Traits (Integrated Gestalt Rules)

- **Solar Cell Physiology**: Full power under a yellow sun. In areas of Red Solar radiation, Monk abilities, Ki pool, and flight speed are suspended until exposed to yellow sunlight again.
- **Kryptonite Vulnerability**: Green Kryptonite within 30 feet inflicts the Poisoned condition and 1 level of Exhaustion per round. Bypasses damage resistance and reduces effective Gestalt level down toward Level 1 while within range.
- **Telescopic & X-Ray Vision**: True Sight up to 120 feet; darkvision up to 1,000 feet; can see through up to 10 feet of stone/wood/metal (blocked entirely by lead).
- **Super-Hearing**: Expertise (+10 passive) on Wisdom (Perception) checks involving sound across global distances. **The cost**: she hears every scream, every whispered prayer, every desperate heartbeat — every hour of every day, unless she is at the summit of Mount Everest.

## Aesthetic Profile & Gearless Mechanics

- **Radiant Presence**: Solar radiation causes her skin to emit a faint warm luminescence, eyes to shimmer like crushed sapphires, and hair to fall perfectly regardless of atmospheric re-entry or orbital combat.
- **Gaze of Awe**: Although her Charisma score is 12, her physical symmetry and divine stature grant innate advantage on Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) checks when interacting with human populations who perceive her as a walking goddess.
- **Effortless Cleanliness**: Dirt, blood, and grime burn off her invulnerable skin instantly due to her microscopic bio-electric aura. She requires no armor, robes, or protective gear to survive in vacuum, deep oceans, or heavy firefights.
- **Weapon Bond (Gearless Reflavor)**: Kara's hands count as bonded weapons; she can never be disarmed of her unarmed strikes.

### Synergy Matrix

- **War Magic (EK L7)**: Cast a Cantrip (e.g., Thunderclap / Light) → Make an Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action.
- **Improved War Magic (EK L18)**: Cast a 1st–4th level spell → Make an Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action.
- **Arcane Charge (EK L15)**: Teleport 30 feet when using Action Surge without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Flurry of Blows + Battle Master Maneuvers** (Way of the Open Hand): Execute 4–6 unarmed strikes per turn, inflicting Trip / Push / Disarm on each hit.
- **Abjuration Capstone Synergy** (Level 30): Casting Shield recharges 2 Ki points instantly.

## Signature Combat Loop (Level 20 example turn)

```
1. Bonus Action: Cast Haste (concentration) — +2 AC, double speed, extra action.
2. Action 1: Action Surge → Move 90 ft + Cast Greater Invisibility on self (4th level).
   Bonus action from War Magic: Unarmed Strike (1d10 + 5 mod + Superiority maneuver).
3. Action 2: Extra Attack 3 — 4 attacks @ 1d10+5 + Stunning Strike on each (4 Ki).
4. Flurry of Blows — 2 more attacks @ 1d10+5 + Open Hand Technique (prone / push / no reaction).
5. Reaction (later in round): Shield spell if hit — +5 AC.

Total per turn: 7 unarmed strikes (4 base + 2 Flurry + 1 War Magic), AC 27+,
all with Stunning Strike on a CON save DC 19, all carrying Open Hand control.
Ki spent: 4 (Stunning Strike on each) + 1 (Shield) = 5 of 20.
```

## The Loneliness Mechanic

A campaign-specific quasi-feature: at the start of each session, Kara makes a WIS save (DC 10, escalating by 1 per session) or gains the *Suffocating Worship* condition for the day — disadvantage on all Charisma checks against humans who know her identity, because they prostrate themselves instead of listening. The Inner Circle and Clark are immune. Mount Everest scenes auto-succeed.
