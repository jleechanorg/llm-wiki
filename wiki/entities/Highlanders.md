# The Highlanders

Ruling elite of the *Blades of the High and Low* setting. They inhabit floating plateau-cities built from fragments of the [[WarriorQueen]]'s research by her former advisors and generals. They monopolize high magic, advanced craftsmanship, and political authority over the lowland kingdoms, enforcing the [[HighlanderLowlanderDivide]]: Lowlanders are restricted by law and bloodline controls to half-caster or martial traditions.

Highlander elites, their mage-hunters, and inquisitors form part of the campaign's all-human threat roster (the setting contains no mindless monsters). [[ValeriaVex]]'s [[AetherPrimordialEnergy|Aether]] bypasses their restrictions entirely, making her existence a latent political threat to their monopoly — one more reason she conceals her ability via the [[WitnessedRevealMechanic]].
