# Lae'zel — "The Astral Scourge"

In the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath: L12+ Fighter (Battle Master) who rejected Vlaakith but found no allies in the corrupted [[BaldursGate]] of the [[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]]. Took to the Astral Sea wielding the Silver Sword of the Astral Plane in Helldusk Armour, leading a relentless, suicidal guerrilla war against both the Lich-Queen and the remnants of the illithid empire — a hardened, ruthless zealot of her own freedom. One of the few companions in [[DefinitiveCompanionFates]] who is neither ruler nor victim, but exile.
