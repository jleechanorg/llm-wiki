# Wyll Ravengard

Baldur's Gate 3 companion character (the Blade of Frontiers) appearing as an NPC in the [[Voyage]] post-BG3 campaign ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]). Slain by [[DrorRagzlin]]; his spark was then siphoned by [[NocturneSosuke]] as part of the [[SoulEssenceHarvesting]] pattern. First of four named harvest victims in the run, alongside [[DrorRagzlin]], [[PriestessGut]], and [[LarinnaDuskweaver]].
