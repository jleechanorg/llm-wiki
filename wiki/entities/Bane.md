# Bane

The Forgotten Realms greater deity of **tyranny, fear, and control** — and the primary divine victim of the [[AizenSosuke]] god campaign. The original chat lineage was even named "bane - main" after him.

## Role in the campaign

- Bane's portfolio (tyranny/control) is thematically adjacent to Aizen's own ambitions, making him the natural target for [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]]: Aizen consumes Bane's divine essence to ascend.
- His consumption is the trigger event that converts the mortal [[BaldursGate]] intrigue arc into the divine-mechanics arc of chat 2, where Aizen becomes the **God-King of Absolute Codification, Tyranny, and Control** — explicitly inheriting and rebranding Bane's portfolio.
- The **Divine Intervention: Consumed Will** mechanic (forcing mortals to reroll successful saves) is framed as a power derived from Bane's consumed essence.
- In the teen prequel ([[2026-08-04-aizen-teen-campaign-export]]), Bane's consumption is one of the explicitly nullified world-state facts ("Bane has not been consumed") — he is parked as an inactive future threat, marking the [[PrequelOriginCampaign]] rewind boundary.
