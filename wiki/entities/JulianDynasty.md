# Julian Dynasty

The ruling imperial house of the "Iron Eagle: Vengeance" timeline, established when [[JuliusCaesar]] seized power early and killed the Republic before 71 BCE. Members carry the [[Miasma]] ("The Divine Blood") and are mythologized as "not quite human" — [[AureliusCaesar]]'s supernatural beauty ([[BeautyAsWeapon]]) is treated as biological proof of the family's divine descent (Venus lineage, per the real gens Julia's claimed ancestry). Family doctrine is embodied in the Emperor's mandate: victory, solvency, and above all *no embarrassment*. Structural sibling of [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]] in the Aizen campaign line: a great house whose bloodline carries both supernatural power and crushing expectation.
