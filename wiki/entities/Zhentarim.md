# The Zhentarim (The Black Network)

Mercenary/criminal network of the Forgotten Realms and recurring faction across the wiki's [[BaldursGate]] campaigns.

## In the Silent Blade campaign (2026-08-04)

Employer and allegiance of [[NocturneSilentBlade|Nocturne, the Silent Blade]]. The campaign frames the Zhentarim as a terror-projection organization: Nocturne's brutality doctrine — displayed bodies, deliberately traumatized survivors — exists explicitly "to ensure that the survivors are too traumatized to ever oppose the Zhentarim again." Zhentarim Captains are also negotiation targets: making one pay double is characterized as an act of submission to the operative.

Operating theater: the chaotic rebuilding of Baldur's Gate ("chaos is a ladder"), alongside/against factions such as the [[FlamingFist]] and [[TheGuild]].
