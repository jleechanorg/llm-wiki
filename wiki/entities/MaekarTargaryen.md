# Maekar Targaryen

Youngest of King [[DaeronIITargaryen|Daeron II]]'s four fighting sons in ASOIAF canon; in campaign visenya v9 (209 AC) he holds "the thankless task of pacifying the Crownlands" as the crown's authority decays and Blackfyre-broken brotherhoods turn the roads into hunting grounds.

In the v9 alternate frame, his **youngest child is [[VisenyaTargaryen]]** — a departure from canon, where Maekar's youngest is Aegon ("Egg" of Dunk & Egg, cf. [[SerDuncanTheTall]]). He is the source of Visenya's barony grant: [[RooksRest|Rook's Rest]], awarded for her single-season pacification of the Stokeworth-to-Duskendale stretch.

Functionally the campaign's legitimizing authority figure: his mandate makes Visenya's ruthlessness state-sanctioned, and his canonical sternness frames a father who rewards results he cannot publicly love.
