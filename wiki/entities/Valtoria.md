## Overview
Lady Valtoria is a new sibling introduced in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]) — the Mad Scientist archetype of the [[GoldenLineage]]. She was name-checked in the played [[NocturneOldRepublic]] campaign as "Sibling - Alchemy."

## Key Details
- **Role:** Keeper of the **Sphere of Biotic Science**
- **Personality:** Obsessive, clinical, detached — "She doesn't want to rule the galaxy; she wants to dissect it."
- **Domain:** The **Genetic Vaults** on Korriban, creating **Sithspawn** — abominations of alchemy and technology used as shock troops
- **Plot hook:** She wants to study Nocturne's blood; she knows Nocturne is a "Prodigy" and constantly requests bone-marrow samples to replicate the power
