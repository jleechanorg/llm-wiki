# The Bloom Shadow

Upper City establishment in [[BaldursGate]] run by [[MadamZylra]]; the setting of Part 0 ("The Broken Cage") of the [[NocturneSosuke]] BG3 v3 campaign. [[NocturneSosuke]] and both sisters were sold here after the [[CassalanterFamily]] engineered [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]]'s bankruptcy during the 1492 DR Absolute crisis. Sessions 1–2 take place here (purple-silk chamber, the [[HorgusGwent]] encounter); the sisters are held in separate wings until they are moved to [[SunderStoneVilla]] as ritual collateral.
