# Daeron II Targaryen

King on the Iron Throne in 209 AC, the setting year of campaign visenya v9. Presented as "old and sick, his reign entering its final year" — canonically Daeron II ("Daeron the Good") dies in the Great Spring Sickness of 209 AC, which the campaign uses as its clock.

His waning reign defines the campaign's power vacuum: "the crown's reach ends where the Kingswood begins," the brotherhoods broken in the last Blackfyre Rebellion never disarmed, and enforcement has devolved to regional strongmen like Lord [[MaekarTargaryen]] and, in miniature, the player character [[VisenyaTargaryen]].
