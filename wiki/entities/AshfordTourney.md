# Ashford Tourney

The tourney at Ashford Meadow — canonically the setting of *The Hedge Knight*, where [[SerDuncanTheTall]] enters the historical record. Campaign visenya v9 explicitly dates itself to 209 AC as "the year of the Ashford Tourney," using it as the era anchor that places [[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s Crownlands campaign alongside the Dunk & Egg storyline and the final year of [[DaeronIITargaryen|Daeron II]]'s reign.

(Note: canonically the Ashford tourney occurs in 209 AC per the campaign's framing; the source uses it purely as a period signpost — no scene is set there in the excerpted sections.)
