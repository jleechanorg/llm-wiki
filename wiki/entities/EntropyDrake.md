# Entropy (spirit dragon)

[[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s invisible spirit dragon in the v6 campaign. Via the Tier 2 "Summon the Alpha" feature of the [[ApexWeaver]] class, Entropy can be manifested as a physical **Large Drake**; at level 10 it becomes a Permanent Steed. Thematically the externalization of her core drive — dismantling ordered systems ([[EntropyToll]]).
