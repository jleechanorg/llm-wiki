# The Witcher (setting)

Andrzej Sapkowski's fantasy world (Continent, Chaos-based magic system) as adapted by the Netflix series — the "Season 4" era anchors the Nocturne Witcher campaign timeline. Setting elements used by the campaign: [[TheRats]] gang as supporting cast, "the fundamental laws of Chaos" as the magic system the protagonist seeks to master ([[PortableAbsolutePower]]). Follows the campaign line's established pattern (per the user's campaign-preference profile) of adapting non-D&D fictional settings into D&D 5e mechanics — here a College of Swords Bard overlay.
