# House Baelarys

Lost sorcerous house recurring across the Visenya campaign line as the source of the [[ApexBloodline]] (spelled **Baelarys** in v4/v5, **Belaerys** in v2/v3 — see [[HouseBelaerys]]). In Visenya v5 its last known survivor was [[VaelaBaelarys]], found in the ruins of Volantis and married to [[RhaegarTargaryen]] in 287 AC; she vanished six months after bearing [[VisenyaTargaryen]], leaving the child as the house's only living evidence.
