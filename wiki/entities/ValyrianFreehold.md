# Valyrian Freehold

Setting of the [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]] campaign, depicted at its absolute zenith: "a glittering golden cage built upon a foundation of liquid magma, unmitigated slave labor, and blood-fleshed sorcery." Power is balanced among the Forty Families through dynastic breeding monopolies and the deterrent of hundreds of adult dragons above the Fourteen Flames.

That equilibrium has fractured into the 20-v-20 [[FortyFamiliesCivilWar]]. The Freehold's defining flaw is **institutional blindness**: obsessed with Essos dominance, it dismisses Westeros as worthless wilderness — the exact gap [[SarielAsh]] and [[HouseTargaryen]] exploit via the [[VanguardExodus]].
