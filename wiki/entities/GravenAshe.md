# Graven Ashe

Archon of War in [[TyrannyGame|Tyranny]], commander of the [[Disfavored]] legion. In [[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension|The Architecture of Ascension]], he is a primary manipulation target: Nocturne's [[CourtlyShadowMask|Courtly Shadow]] persona mirrors back "the submissive, dutiful son/daughter who listens to his war stories with reverence" as part of her [[DependencyLoopManipulation]]. His honor-and-blood worldview is exactly the "stagnation" the character means to obsolete.
