# Emperor Tiberius Aethelgard

Emperor of the [[AethelgardEmpire]] and father of [[SaitaAethelgard]] in *The Hollow Zenith* ([[2026-08-04-saita-v3-hollow-zenith]]). A Level 20 titan capable of leveling cities barehanded — the apex of the Empire's eugenics-bred royal bloodline. Referenced in Saita's inner monologue ("my father has boots older than his entire family tree") as the standard against which she measures both herself and the mayfly nobility of Faerûn. His Empire's hidden ruin — and Saita's need to prove her worth to him — drives the [[BurdenOfSupremacy]] compulsion.
