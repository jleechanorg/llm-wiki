## Overview
The Principate Senate is the traditionalist power base of the oldest families in the [CelestialImperium](CelestialImperium.md), composed of political intrigue masters managing the Empyrean Affliction under [Raziel](Raziel.md)'s guidance.

## Leadership
- Guided by: Lord Regent [Raziel](Raziel.md)
- Wing: Revisionist Scholars (progressive, questioning official doctrine under Magister Lucian Kantos)

## Key Facts
- Type: Traditionalist political power base
- Parent: [CelestialImperium](CelestialImperium.md)
- Members: Oldest families
- Focus: Political intrigue, managing Empyrean Affliction

- Part of: [CelestialImperium](CelestialImperium.md)
- Leads: [CelestialImperium](CelestialImperium.md)
