# Knights of the Shield

Secretive, influential faction in [[BaldursGate]] devoted to maintaining order and subtly guiding the city's destiny from behind the scenes. Introduced in the [[ShadowOverTheGate]] campaign frame as deliberately NOT always aligned with the [[FlamingFist]] — they run their own agendas.

- **Campaign start** ([[2026-08-04-aizen-mortal-campaign-shadow-over-the-gate-start|mortal chat]]): listed as one of five major factions.
- **Later arc** (chat 1 entries 601-900): [[AizenSosuke]] bypasses their warded manor with Knock and dominates the confrontation of wits before their agents can interrogate him.

Structural role: the "hidden legitimate order" foil to Aizen's own hidden-hand play — both operate from the shadows, making them natural rivals.
