# The Shattered Rose

Organization/domain named in the Nocturne Sosuke v6 campaign module *The Velvet Cage (Descent Edition)* ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-bg3-v6-velvet-cage]]). [[NocturneSosuke]]'s revealed Abyssal form — horns and wings "wreathed in shadow and starlight" — marks her as **Sovereign of the Shattered Rose**.

The v6 source names the title but does not (in the ingested portion) define the Rose's membership, territory, or role in the module's Infernal Politics layer; the document truncates before those sections. Treat details beyond the sovereign title as not-yet-established for v6, pending later ingestion.
