## Overview
The Prophetess is the mysterious Force user leading [[TheEclipsed]] nihilist faction in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]). The bible describes her only as "possibly a clone of Kreia or a new entity." Her faction's defining knowledge: they know Emperor [[Vitiate]] plans to eat the galaxy via [[TheHarvest]]. The source document cuts off mid-sentence while describing her faction's response to that knowledge, so her agenda is only partially specified.
