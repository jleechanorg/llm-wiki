# Oghma

Forgotten Realms god of knowledge, bards, and inspiration — the Binder of What Is Known. In the Divine Deception V0 campaign prompt ([[2026-08-04-god-campaign-prompt-v0-divine-deception]]) he has a specific mechanical role in the [[DivineDissonance]] detection ladder: at the **21–50% Suspicion** threshold, deities with Detection/Knowledge domains (Oghma, [[Savras]]) begin **passive scrying** on the player, who feels a "gaze" upon them. He is the tripwire between anomalies-dismissed-as-luck and active investigation by Inevitables/Solars.
