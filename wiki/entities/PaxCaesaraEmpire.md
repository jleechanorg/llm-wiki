# Pax Caesara (The Iron Eagle setting)

Setting of the [[2026-08-04-aurelius-v1-pax-caesara|Aurelius v1]] campaign: a Roman Republic that mutated into a **monolithic autocratic Empire** after [[JuliusCaesar]] survived the Ides of March, governed as a "Perpetual Dictatorship."

- **Year**: 40 BCE. Civil wars are crushed; the resulting peace is "a suffocating weight."
- **Texture**: a gilded, treacherous Rome — court politics, legions on the barbarian frontier, and dynastic succession intrigue among Caesar's sons.
- **Campaign function**: the prize itself. [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]]'s stated ultimate target is to turn the Empire ("The World Soul") into his personal pleasure garden.

Thematic sibling of the Rome-inflected faction machinery in [[FactionPoliticsRome]] and the imperial-court settings elsewhere in the corpus (e.g. [[CelestialImperium]]).
