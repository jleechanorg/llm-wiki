# Jaehaerys Waters / Targaryen (Jon)

The baby [[RhaegarTargaryen]] carried back from the Tower of Joy (284 AC) with Lyanna Stark's bones. To prevent the North rallying around "Lyanna's stolen son," Rhaegar publicly acknowledged him as his **bastard, Jaehaerys Waters**, later legitimized as Targaryen — claiming Lyanna begged him to protect the child. In Visenya v5 he is [[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s older half-brother and a latent succession-tension vector between the Crown and the sealed-off North of [[EddardStark]].
