# Commander Marcus Vance

**Class**: Level 12 Battle Master Fighter. **Role**: Chief of Security & Zealot Interceptor. Elena's older brother.

## Background

Former elite special-forces commander whose entire squad was lost during a rogue warlord's artillery strike in eastern Markovia. Marcus held the radio as each callsign went silent — nine soldiers he had trained, equipped, and commanded, killed in a single salvo. Supergirl arrived three minutes later and destroyed the artillery battery in two seconds. She could not save them. She could only have saved them by being there earlier. She held Marcus at the wreckage for an hour before he could speak.

## Defining Moment

The nine callsigns going silent in sequence. **Choice**: whether to keep commanding subsequent operations with the knowledge that human-speed response cannot match human-speed atrocity. **Perceived failure**: he keeps going. The squad is still with him — their names are the callsigns of every House Guard sub-unit he creates.

## The House Guard

Marcus commands the **House Guard** — an elite security wing of 240 Level 8–10 operatives (the brightest tactical talent he can recruit) dedicated to a single mission: prevent mortals from getting close enough to Supergirl to force her to use power on them. Their job is to absorb the first strike. Their job is to die so she does not have to choose. They know this. They volunteer anyway.

## Relationship to Kara

Marcus treats Kara the way a chief of staff treats a head of state — formal, direct, and unflinching. He is the only person in the Inner Circle who has physically blocked Kara's path to the front door when she tried to handle a stalker personally. He is also the only person who has ever made Kara laugh out loud — and he leverages that against her mercilessly when she gets too serious.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- Coordinates the covert-extraction vectors in **Scenario 3 (Sovereignty Accords / Geneva Trap)** and **Scenario A (Kahndaq Diplomatic Standoff)**.
- Once per session, can reroute an assassination attempt against Kara to target a decoy instead, provided he has had 24 hours of advance intel.
- Carries the **[[KryptonianAccords]]** portable denial system — a radio jammer that prevents any unauthorized Kryptonite triggering within 200 feet.
