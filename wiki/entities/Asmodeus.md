# Asmodeus

Lord of the Nine Hells (D&D canon), apex of the infernal hierarchy.

## Fate in Thay v1 ([[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]])

After dominating [[Zariel]], [[NocturneSosuke]] "moved through the Nine Layers like a plague of beauty, hollowing out Asmodeus until the Lord of the Nine was nothing more than a porcelain puppet speaking with her voice." His shell continues to rule nominally — one reason [[Ao]] could not simply kill Nocturne: her control structure had become load-bearing for the Lower Planes, forcing the [[GeometricFirewall]] quarantine instead.
