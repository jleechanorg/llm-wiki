# Martian Manhunter / J'onn J'onzz

**Class**: Level 20 Psionic Monk. **Apex Five member**. The campaign's wild card.

## Threat

Total telepathic intrusion and density shifting. Can bypass physical invulnerability by attacking the mind or by passing through Kryptonian flesh directly. The only entity on Earth who can read Kara's thoughts without her consent (and the only one who would consider doing so to be a breach of trust).

## Stance

Quiet, covert arbiter between Amanda Waller's coalition and the House of El. Has personally infiltrated the ARGUS black-site where the [[ProjectRedDust]] trigger codes are stored. Has not revealed what he knows to either side. Is waiting to see whether Kara will become a tyrant or a steward.

He watched Mars die. He watched his wife die. He watched his people die because the galaxy did not intervene in time. He understands Kara's position better than anyone — and he will not forgive her if she uses her power to make Earth another Mars.

## Mechanical Role in the Campaign

- **[[RedDustTriggerCodes]]** — J'onn knows the location of one of the five trigger code holders. If Kara earns his trust, he can hand her the codes without a fight. If he concludes she is too dangerous, he will erase her knowledge of the system entirely (with a targeted psychic block) and orchestrate her removal from Earth himself.
- **[[TelepathicTranslation]]** — once per scenario, J'onn can translate Kara's words directly into another language without her needing to learn it. Useful for the ICC trial where the prosecution may use interpreters to distort her statements.
- **[[DensityShift]]** — J'onn can phase through solid matter, including Kryptonian defenses. He is the only character who can sneak up on Kara. He uses this exactly once per campaign — at a critical moment that defines Kara's trust in him.
