# ChatGPT

OpenAI's conversational LLM product. In this wiki it appears as one of three review surfaces for the [[CampaignSummaryPrompt]] (2026-08-04), where it contributed the most structural feedback: chronology-handling instructions for timestamp-less transcripts, a minimum-impact guideline for bullet granularity ([[SignificanceThreshold]]), per-session retcon formatting ([[RetconDMNote]]), transcript chunking for long campaigns ([[TranscriptChunkingSummarization]]), and optional per-bullet sequence metadata.

Sibling reviewers on the same document: [[Gemini]] (via [[GoogleAIStudio]] and [[Cursor]]).
