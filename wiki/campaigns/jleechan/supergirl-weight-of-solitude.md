# Supergirl: The Weight of Solitude

A high-level D&D 5e Gestalt Campaign Sourcebook & Narrative Blueprint set in the post-2026 DC cosmic film universe.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & World Context

Following the cosmic events of the 2026 film universe (her travels with Ruthye and the defeat of Krem of the Yellow Hills), **Kara Zor-El (Supergirl)** has made Earth her permanent home alongside her cousin **Kal-El (Superman)**.

Unlike Clark, who anchors his humanity behind the bumbling journalist persona of Clark Kent, **Kara refuses to live a lie**. Believing that hiding behind a fake name and specs is fundamentally dishonest to both humanity and herself, Supergirl operates in the open with **no secret identity**.

### The Psychological Price of Absolute Transparency
Without a mortal disguise, Kara cannot enjoy normal human relationships, grab a casual coffee, or date without drawing targeted strikes or overwhelming citizens with dread and adoration. The world views her not merely as a superhero, but as a living deity walking among insects. 
- **Her only true peer**: Superman.
- **Her only human companions**: A select inner circle of "sane" cult members—elite humans (Level 12) who manage her public affairs and understand her moral vision without descending into fanatical violence.

### Core Mechanics & Power Reality
- **Invulnerability in Combat**: In raw physical combat, virtually no mortal on Earth can hurt Kara or Clark. Kara's AC is 20+ (Wisdom-based Unarmored Defense), her flight speed is functionally infinite under yellow sunlight, and her unarmed strikes (Flurry of Blows) hit with localized seismic force.
- **Wisdom as Core Ability**: Wisdom reflects Kryptonian sensory processing, super-hearing, and mental clarity under yellow solar absorption. Attacks, Unarmored Defense, and save DCs use Wisdom.
- **Vulnerabilities**: 
  - **Green Kryptonite**: Inflicts Poisoned + 1 level of Exhaustion per round within 30 ft, stripping effective Gestalt level down to Level 1.
  - **Red Solar Radiation**: Temporarily suppresses Monk abilities, flight, and invulnerability.
  - **Magic**: Bypass natural physical damage immunity.

## 3. The Apex Five (Earth's Level 20+ Terrestrial Ancients)

While peak humanity caps at Level 12, these five native Earth-bound entities represent the only strategic checkmates capable of matching or neutralizing Kryptonians:

1. **Doctor Fate (Level 22 Divine Archmage)**: Magic bypasses Kryptonian invulnerability entirely. Lord Nabu views Kryptonian cult worship as a disruption to the cosmic balance between Order and Chaos.
2. **Wonder Woman / Diana of Themyscira (Level 20 Demigod Fighter / Paladin)**: Wields god-killing weapons forged by Hephaestus that slice through Kryptonian skin. Refuses to submit to UN authority or Kryptonian intervention.
3. **Black Adam / Teth-Adam (Level 21 Sorcerer / Fighter)**: Sovereign ruler of Kahndaq wielding magic lightning and yellow-sun level physical power. Grants political asylum to Kryptonian cultists, daring Kara to invade a sovereign border.
4. **Swamp Thing / Alec Holland (Level 20 Archdruid / Avatar of the Green)**: Controls planetary biomass. Immune to physical destruction. Protects the biosphere if anti-Kryptonian weapons or military testing cause ecological collapse.
5. **Martian Manhunter / J'onn J'onzz (Level 20 Psionic Monk)**: Total telepathic intrusion and density shifting. Bypasses physical invulnerability by targeting the mind or passing through flesh.

## 5. Inciting Crisis: The Execution of Dictator Malakor

### The Incident
Six months ago, General Josip Malakor—dictator of Markovia—began ethnic cleansing while surrounding his palace with human shields and tying dirty-bomb triggers to his heart rate. The UN was paralyzed by vetoes; Clark refused to act without legal authorization.

Kara flew in at Level 20 speed, disarmed the bomb triggers in milliseconds, and executed Malakor with heat vision on live global television. She saved millions of lives in 3 seconds.

### The Backlash & Legal Trap
- **The Cult**: Celebrated Kara as a divine savior; membership exploded globally.
- **Lex Luthor, Amanda Waller & Batman**: Terrified by her total disregard for national borders and constitutional law.
- **The International Criminal Court (ICC)**: Issued an international arrest warrant and subpoena for Kara Zor-El for extrajudicial murder.

## 6. Main Narrative Arc & Major Scenarios

### Scenario 1: The ICC Trial in The Hague
- **Conflict**: The ICC demands Kara stand trial. Lex Luthor serves as special prosecutor.
- **Clark's View**: Urges Kara to attend peacefully, take off her cape, and accept human legal judgment to prove Kryptonians aren't tyrants.
- **Elena's View**: Urges Kara to boycott, arguing that submitting to corrupt political courts delegitimizes moral justice.
- **Gameplay**: A brutal legal and philosophical cross-examination on live television against Lex Luthor (Level 12 Mastermind).

### Scenario 2: The Solar Dawn Schism
- **Conflict**: An extremist splinter cult (*The Solar Dawn*) begins assassinating prosecutors and judges involved in Kara's indictment, leaving her House of El symbol in blood.
- **Gameplay**: Kara cannot punch the cultists without validating claims of tyranny. She must send Elena and Marcus to infiltrate the cult network and stop the terror cell.

### Scenario 3: The Sovereignty Accords & The Geneva Trap
- **Conflict**: The UN passes the *Kryptonian Accords*, declaring any unauthorized Kryptonian intervention an act of war. A massive earthquake strikes a non-signatory military regime that refuses foreign aid.
- **Dilemma**: Intervene and prove human law is a fiction, or stay silent while thousands die.

### Scenario 4: Project Red Dust (The MAD Contingency)
- **Conflict**: Batman, Lex Luthor, and Amanda Waller deploy an orbital network of 200 satellites loaded with pulverized Green Kryptonite and Red Solar emitters.
- **Stakes**: Mutually Assured Destruction—if a Kryptonian overthrows a government, the atmosphere becomes lethal to Kryptonians worldwide.

### Scenario 5: The Isolation of the Sun Goddess
- **Roleplay Moments**:
  - Private dialogues with Clark on Mount Everest—the only place quiet enough to talk without hearing billions of heartbeats.
  - Walking among humans who either fall to their knees or flee in terror.
  - Evaluating whether her Level 12 companions see her as a person or a god to be managed.
