# Supergirl — Kryptonian Gods: The Weight of Solitude

A solo D&D 5e Gestalt campaign for **[[KaraZorElSupergirl]]** (Level 20 Gestalt Fighter (Eldritch Knight) / Monk (Way of the Open Hand), Wisdom-unified, gearless) set in the post-2026 DC movie universe. Tone: political thriller meets theological drama meets quiet loneliness — combat is rarely the right tool.

Captured iteratively across six player turns in a Gemini dialogue ([share.gemini.google/hZXboZihmwYc](https://share.gemini.google/hZXboZihmwYc), [gemini.google.com/share/1a3d2d1f2753](https://gemini.google.com/share/1a3d2d1f2753), [share.gemini.google/94lImOSaZBZL](https://share.gemini.google/94lImOSaZBZL) — all three URLs serve the same conversation). ## 1. Premise

Following the cosmic events of the 2026 film universe (her travels with Ruthye and the defeat of Krem of the Yellow Hills), **Kara Zor-El (Supergirl)** has made Earth her permanent home alongside her cousin **Kal-El (Superman)**.

Unlike Clark, who anchors his humanity behind the bumbling journalist persona of Clark Kent, **Kara refuses to live a lie**. Believing that hiding behind a fake name and specs is fundamentally dishonest to both humanity and herself, Supergirl operates in the open with **no secret identity**.

This transparency came at a brutal psychological cost: she cannot have normal friends, cannot grab coffee, cannot date without fear of crushing someone or drawing targeted strikes. Her only true peer is Superman. Her only human companions are a select inner circle of "sane" cult members — elite Level 12 humans who manage her public affairs and understand her moral vision without descending into fanatical violence.

The campaign begins six months after Kara incinerated General Josip Malakor — dictator of the rogue state of Markovia — on live international television. She had bypassed his guards, disarmed his dirty-bomb triggers tied to his own heart-rate, and saved millions of lives in three seconds. The ICC has issued an arrest warrant. The Solar Dawn cult has exploded in membership. Lex Luthor, Amanda Waller, and Batman have begun constructing **[[ProjectRedDust]]** — a planetary MAD deterrent that can render all Kryptonians mortal.

## 2. Opening Scene — Mount Everest, the Morning of the Subpoena

**Location**: Summit of Mount Everest. The only place on Earth where Kara and Clark can talk without hearing billions of human heartbeats.

**Weather**: Clear. Dawn light sharp as a knife-edge along the Himalayas. Kara and Clark sit cross-legged on a flat shelf of granite, ten feet apart, neither speaking. Their capes do not move. There is no wind at the summit today. Even the air has stopped.

Clark has the ICC subpoena folded in his hand. He has read it seven times. Each time, the words have not changed.

> *Kara*, the International Criminal Court, sitting in The Hague, by the authority of the one hundred and forty signatory nations of the Geneva Convention on Crimes Against Sovereignty, does hereby summon Kara Zor-El, also known as Supergirl, also known as the Woman of Tomorrow, also known as the Kryptonian God of the Sun, to appear before this Court on the seventh day of September, in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-six, at the hour of ten in the morning, to answer to the charges of: (1) extrajudicial assassination of a head of state; (2) violation of the sovereignty of the Republic of Markovia; (3) crimes against the constitutional order of nations.*

Clark sets the paper on the granite between them.

**Clark**: *Kara. I need you to listen to me. I am not going to tell you what to do. I have never told you what to do. I have watched you cross galaxies, hold dying suns together with your bare hands, give names to Krem of the Yellow Hills before he died. I have watched you be the best of us, every hour, every day. I am not going to tell you what to do. But I am going to ask you something I have never asked you before. And I need you to answer me honestly, because if you do not answer me honestly, no one in the world will, and I will have lost the only person who tells me the truth.* He looks at the subpoena. **Clark**: *Will you attend the trial?*

The mountain is silent. Even the photons of the dawn, hitting Kara's skin and being absorbed into her cells, make no sound. She is reading the subpoena. She is reading the seven signatories of the warrant — Geneva, Berlin, Tokyo, Brasilia, Lagos, Cairo, Jakarta. She is reading the name of the special prosecutor, which she already knows: Lex Luthor.

**Kara**: *Clark, I will not sit in a human courtroom and let a human judge tell me whether I was right to save three million people in three seconds. The children were already dead by the time the Security Council finished its procedural vote. They were dead. I heard them die. I heard each one. There were nine hundred and forty-one of them under the age of ten. I could have saved every one. I chose to save the three million who were still alive. And if Lex Luthor wants to put me on trial for that choice, then Lex Luthor can come to Everest and try to make me regret it.*

Clark nods. He waits.

**Kara**: *But.* The word sits between them for a long time. *But you are not asking me whether I was right. You are asking me whether I will let Lex Luthor set the terms of what it means to be Kryptonian on Earth. And the answer to that question is yes, Clark. I will attend. I will take off the cape. I will sit in the chair. I will let them call me "the accused." I will let Lex Luthor stand six feet from me and ask me why I killed Malakor. I will not answer his question. I will ask him his.*

She picks up the subpoena. She folds it precisely, once, twice, three times. She puts it in the inner pocket of her cape.

**Kara**: *I will attend the trial, Clark. But I will not be on trial. Lex Luthor will be on trial. Humanity will be on trial. The question will not be "Did Supergirl kill a dictator." The question will be "Why did the international community require a god to do what the Security Council would not."*

Clark smiles. It is the small, private smile of a man who has just watched his cousin choose the third option — the one that was not on the menu Lex Luthor prepared, the one that was not on the menu the Security Council prepared, the one that was not even on the menu Kara herself had prepared an hour ago.

**Clark**: *You are going to make Lex Luthor regret every choice that led him to this moment, aren't you.*

**Kara**: *I am going to give him exactly what he asked for.*

The wind returns. The trial is in thirty-seven days.

```

                          POWER SCALING HIERARCHY (D&D 5E / GESTALT)
========================================================================================

  Level 25 (NPC)  : SUPERMAN (Kal-El) — Cosmic Paragon (Fighter / Monk Gestalt)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Level 20 (PC)   : SUPERGIRL (Kara Zor-El) — Player Character (Fighter / Monk Gestalt)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Level 20+ (NPC) : THE APEX FIVE — Earth's Ancient / Transcendent Entities
                    (Dr. Fate, Wonder Woman, Black Adam, Swamp Thing, Martian Manhunter)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Level 8-12      : PEAK HUMANITY — World Strategists & Kara's Inner Circle
                    (Lex Luthor, Batman, Amanda Waller, Elena Vance, Marcus Vance, Aris Thorne)
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Level 1-5       : 99.9% OF THE HUMAN POPULATION — Citizens, Soldiers, Cult Followers
========================================================================================
```

**Combat reality**: in raw physical combat, virtually no mortal on Earth can touch Kara. Her AC is near-unhittable via Wisdom-based Unarmored Defense. Her flying speed is functionally infinite under yellow sunlight. Flurry of Blows hits like a localized seismic event. **But this is the wrong framing for the campaign** — combat has been solved. What has not been solved is jurisdiction, worship, sovereignty, and the political price of godhood.

### Core Mechanics & Power Reality

- **Invulnerability in Combat**: Kara's AC is 20+ (Wisdom-based Unarmored Defense), her flight speed is functionally infinite under yellow sunlight, and her unarmed strikes (Flurry of Blows) hit with localized seismic force.
- **Wisdom as Core Ability**: Wisdom reflects Kryptonian sensory processing, super-hearing, and mental clarity under yellow solar absorption. Attacks, Unarmored Defense, and save DCs use Wisdom.
- **Vulnerabilities**:
  - **Green Kryptonite**: Inflicts Poisoned + 1 level of Exhaustion per round within 30 ft, stripping effective Gestalt level down to Level 1.
  - **Red Solar Radiation**: Temporarily suppresses Monk abilities, flight, and invulnerability.
  - **Magic**: Bypass natural physical damage immunity.

## 4. The Apex Five (Earth's Level 20+ Terrestrial Ancients)

While peak humanity caps at Level 12, these five native Earth-bound entities represent the only strategic checkmates capable of matching or neutralizing Kryptonians:

1. **[[DoctorFate]] (Level 22 Divine Archmage)** — Magic bypasses Kryptonian invulnerability entirely. Lord Nabu views Kryptonian cult worship as a disruption to the cosmic balance between Order and Chaos.
2. **[[WonderWomanDianaPrince]] (Level 20 Demigod Fighter / Paladin)** — Wields god-killing weapons forged by Hephaestus that slice through Kryptonian skin. Refuses to submit to UN authority or Kryptonian intervention.
3. **[[BlackAdamTethAdam]] (Level 21 Sorcerer / Fighter)** — Sovereign ruler of Kahndaq wielding magic lightning and yellow-sun level physical power. Grants political asylum to Kryptonian cultists, daring Kara to invade a sovereign border.
4. **[[SwampThingAlecHolland]] (Level 20 Archdruid / Avatar of the Green)** — Controls planetary biomass. Immune to physical destruction. Protects the biosphere if anti-Kryptonian weapons or military testing cause ecological collapse.
5. **[[MartianManhunterJonnJonzz]] (Level 20 Psionic Monk)** — Total telepathic intrusion and density shifting. Bypasses physical invulnerability by targeting the mind or passing through flesh. The only entity who knows how close human governments are to launching [[ProjectRedDust]].

## 7. Inciting Crisis — The Execution of Dictator Malakor

Six months ago, General Josip Malakor — dictator of the rogue state of Markovia — began ethnic cleansing while surrounding his palace with thousands of innocent human shields and tying dirty-bomb triggers to his own heart rate. The UN was paralyzed by vetoes; legal sanctions failed. Clark refused to act without UN authorization, trusting in the slow process of global diplomacy. Kara refused to watch children die.

Using her Level 20 speed, Kara flew into the presidential palace, bypassed his guards, disarmed the triggers in milliseconds, and incinerated General Malakor with heat vision on live global television. She saved millions of lives in three seconds.

### The Backlash & Legal Trap
- **The Cult**: Celebrated Kara as a divine savior; membership exploded globally.
- **Lex Luthor, Amanda Waller & Batman**: Terrified by her total disregard for national borders and constitutional law.
- **The International Criminal Court (ICC)**: Issued an international arrest warrant and subpoena for Kara Zor-El for extrajudicial murder.
- **[[SolarDawn]]**: Violent splinter cult that begins assassinating prosecutors and judges involved in the ICC indictment in Kara's name.

## 8. Main Narrative Arc & Major Scenarios

### Scenario 1 — The ICC Trial in The Hague (also see: [[KryptonianAccords]])
- **Conflict**: The ICC demands Kara stand trial. Lex Luthor serves as special prosecutor.
- **Clark's View**: Urges Kara to attend peacefully, take off her cape, and accept human legal judgment to prove Kryptonians aren't tyrants.
- **Elena's View**: Urges Kara to boycott, arguing that submitting to corrupt political courts delegitimizes moral justice.
- **Gameplay**: A brutal legal and philosophical cross-examination on live television against Lex Luthor (Level 12 Mastermind).
- **Kara's third-option choice** (per opening scene): Attend, but reframe the trial — the question becomes "Why did the international community require a god to do what the Security Council would not."

### Scenario 2 — The Solar Dawn Schism
- **Conflict**: An extremist splinter cult (the [[SolarDawn]]) begins assassinating prosecutors and judges involved in Kara's indictment, leaving her House of El symbol in blood.
- **Gameplay**: Kara cannot punch the cultists without validating claims of tyranny. She must send Elena and Marcus to infiltrate the cult network and stop the terror cell.
- **Hidden hook**: 15% of Solar Dawn members are ARGUS agents and LexCorp operatives. The cult is being funded and directed by [[AmandaWallerSupergirlCampaign]] as controlled opposition to justify expanding [[ProjectRedDust]] activation criteria.

### Scenario 3 — The Sovereignty Accords & The Geneva Trap
- **Conflict**: The UN passes the [[KryptonianAccords]], declaring any unauthorized Kryptonian intervention an act of war. A massive earthquake strikes a non-signatory military regime that refuses foreign aid.
- **Dilemma**: Intervene and prove human law is a fiction, or stay silent while thousands die.
- **Third-option vectors**: Diplomatic leverage (force regime to sign under sanctions threat), covert extraction by [[CommanderMarcusVance]]'s House Guard, weaponized press (release footage of regime's deliberate inaction).

### Scenario 4 — Project Red Dust (The MAD Contingency)
- **Conflict**: Batman, Lex Luthor, and [[AmandaWallerSupergirlCampaign]] deploy an orbital network of 200 satellites loaded with pulverized Green Kryptonite and Red Solar emitters.
- **Stakes**: Mutually Assured Destruction — if a Kryptonian overthrows a government, the atmosphere becomes lethal to Kryptonians worldwide.
- **Gameplay**: Kara must locate and neutralize the trigger mechanisms through diplomacy, hacking, and covert espionage — *without* triggering a global panic and without punching through the satellites (each is hardened against heat vision; breaking one triggers the cascade).
- **Best path**: [[MartianManhunterJonnJonzz]] has infiltrated the ARGUS black-site where trigger codes are stored.

### Scenario 5 — The Isolation of the Sun Goddess
- **Roleplay moments**:
  - Private dialogues with Clark on Mount Everest — the only place quiet enough to talk without hearing billions of heartbeats.
  - Walking among humans who either fall to their knees or flee in terror.
  - Evaluating whether her Level 12 companions see her as a person or a god to be managed.
  - A recurring rumor: a young woman in Metropolis claims she is Kara's half-sister. She is not. But the rumor will not die.

## 9. Apex-Five Scenarios

### Scenario A — The Kahndaq Diplomatic Standoff
Solar Dawn cultists flee to Kahndaq after terror attacks in Geneva. [[BlackAdamTethAdam]] grants asylum. The UN demands extraction; Adam treats Kara's entry as an act of war. Kara must extract cult leaders without sparking a Level 20+ war that would level North Africa.

### Scenario B — The Trial of Magic (Doctor Fate's Injunction)
[[DoctorFate]] erects a massive mystical ward over Washington D.C. cutting off yellow solar radiation within city limits. Fate demands Kara stand trial before an ancient council of mystic entities.

### Scenario C — The Lex-Waller Coalition's Proxy Weapon
Amanda Waller and Lex Luthor stage a Kryptonian-fueled deforestation crisis to manipulate [[SwampThingAlecHolland]]. Kara must convince an enraged Avatar of nature she was framed — while defending Metropolis from root growths without harming the ecosystem.

## 11. Themes

- **The Price of Honesty**: Transparency is a moral stance — but it costs you the ability to have normal relationships. The campaign asks whether Kara would trade her public identity for companionship if she could.
- **Godhood vs. Stewardship**: Is Kara a god among mortals, or a steward of humanity's future? The [[ApexFive]] treat her as a god. Peak Humanity treats her as a threat. The Mortal Average treats her as a parent. Only her Inner Circle treats her as a person.
- **The Third Option**: The central campaign choice mirrors the player's core decision pattern — every crisis has an economic / political / leverage path that does not require combat. The Malakor execution was a wrong third option. The campaign rewards finding right ones.
- **MAD and the Kryptonian Problem**: [[ProjectRedDust]] is the world's answer to a threat it cannot fight conventionally. Kara must dismantle it without becoming the very tyrant it was designed to prevent.
