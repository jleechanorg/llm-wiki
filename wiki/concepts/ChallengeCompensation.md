# Challenge Compensation

Encounter-design technique from the [[2026-08-04-aizen-bg3-v2-exiled-viper|Exiled Viper v2 campaign]]: deliberately engineering counters so a deliberately under-leveled PC (Level 4 [[AizenSosuke]]) stays *challenged but viable* in a world tuned for L12 BG3-era encounters and CR 5–10 organizational threats.

## Counter taxonomy

- **Social counters** — attack the PC's dominant axis (CHA 18 social predation): high-Wisdom interrogators (Lae'zel at DC15+ with advantage), Zhentarim captains with Detect Magic / True Seeing, anti-charm amulets, suspicious rivals.
- **Physical counters** — attack action economy and mobility: swarms of weak enemies ([[TheGuild]] cutpurses ×10), magic-resistant Absolute remnants, sewer ambushes that deny Blade Flourish room.
- **Intrigue counters** — attack the cover identity: family bounties on the exile, a scrying rival (Lysandra), moral traps (Isolde's guilt forcing suboptimal plays), a dedicated pursuer (Kaname).

## Reward and failure shaping

Explicit XP/loot ladder tied to scheme milestones (VIP blackmail = 300XP + Poisoner's Kit upgrade; Lae'zel interrogation = 500XP + Githyanki dagger; family bounty = 600XP + "Sosuke ledger"). Failure states are **narrative** (captured for a "breaking" reversal, debt collectors) rather than TPK — keeping the intrigue loop running instead of ending the campaign.

## Relation to the lineage

Inverse of the god-campaign problem: godhood campaigns must scale challenge *up* to threaten an overpowered PC; this scales threat *shape* sideways so a weak PC's strengths (deception, institutional leverage via the [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]]) remain the load-bearing survival mechanism.
