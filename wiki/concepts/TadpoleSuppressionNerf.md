# Tadpole Suppression Nerf ("Level 6 entity in a Level 1 cage")

Campaign-design device from [[NocturneSosuke]] v5: instead of a fresh Level 1 character, the protagonist is a **Level 6 warlock whose power is actively suppressed** by the illithid tadpole. The parasite isn't just a ticking-clock threat — it is the *dam* holding back the infernal energy absorbed in the [[Malcanthet]] pact ritual.

## Why it matters

- **Reframes the BG3 opening**: the standard "remove the tadpole" quest becomes "break the dam so my power floods back" — recovery, not growth.
- **Preserves character continuity** across campaign versions: v5 keeps the pact backstory from earlier Nocturne iterations while resetting mechanical level, an instance of the [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart]] / [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]] family.
- **Licenses asymmetry**: social/knowledge capabilities remain Level-6-flavored (she *remembers* being powerful) while combat statistics are Level 1, paired with [[MalcanthetsFavor]] to survive the gap and [[PredatorPresenceIntimidation]] to explain NPC deference.
