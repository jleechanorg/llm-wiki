## Overview
The New Peace is the current era in the World of Assiah, beginning after the [TreatyOfNexus](../entities/TreatyOfNexus.md) ended the [CelestialWars](CelestialWars.md).

## Timeline
- Year 10, Kythorn Day 1: Battle of Sacrifice Fields - [Alexiel](../entities/Alexiel.md) dies saving [SarielValArtorius](../entities/SarielValArtorius.md)
- Year 11, Kythorn Day 1: First anniversary of Alexiel's death
- Year 11, Kythorn Day 2: Current campaign start

## Key Facts
- Era: Post-Celestial Wars
- Current Year: 11
