# God Mechanics V1 — 7-Rank Divinity Ladder

The baseline god-mechanics framework (`god_mechanics_general.md` in [[PR8488]]): a **system-agnostic 7-rank ladder** from Quasi-Deity up through Greater Deity, defining what each divinity rank can do independent of any one setting.

In the God of Murder campaign it supplies the progression spine — the [[DarkUrge]] begins as a rank-1 Quasi-Deity anchored to [[BaldursGate]] and climbs toward Greater Goddess through deicide. V1 is deliberately setting-neutral so the same ladder can back Faerûn, Cyberpunk, Wuxia, Marvel, Naruto, or homebrew pantheons; the [[GodMechanicsV2Overlay]] extends it, and the Nocturne V2 spec specializes it for D&D 5e Faerûn. Sibling ascent-track mechanics in the wiki: [[AscensionMeter]], [[ProjectApotheosis]].
