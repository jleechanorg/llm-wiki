# Imperator (custom class)

Custom D&D 5e class (Noble/Sorcerous Origin) introduced for [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] in the Gaia v3 [[PaxCaesaraEmpire]] campaign. Design goal: a **"Prodigy" start** — "the smartest and most charismatic person in every room, but a single sword stroke could still end you" (HP 11, AC 14).

Level 1 features:
- **[[Auctoritas]] (Spellcasting)**: CHA-based; cantrips Guidance, Friends, Mind Sliver; two 1st-level slots; Command + Shield known.
- **Tactical Sense**: cannot be Surprised while conscious; bonus-action assessment of a visible creature (relative HP high/low/equal + immunities) — a mechanical embodiment of the INT 18 mastermind fantasy.
- **Variant Human feat: Actor** — advantage on Deception/Performance while impersonating, powering the "Gaia of the Gens Vibia" incognito identity.

Contrast with sibling builds: [[AureliusCaesar]] and the [[SerpentQueen|Serpent Queen]] use the [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|bard College of Swords]] chassis for the same dual-mask prodigy archetype; Imperator is a bespoke-class alternative that fuses the social toolkit directly with dynastic flavor rather than borrowing bard mechanics.
