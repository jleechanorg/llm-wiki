# Portable Absolute Power

Core drive from the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign: rejection of worldly power (land, titles, armies) as "a trap" built on uncontrollable emotional variables (fealty, morale), in favor of power that travels with the self — forbidden knowledge, intrinsic magical sources, raw treasury potential, mastery of the fundamental laws of Chaos. Terminal goal: "not to rule from a heavenly throne, but to become the throne — a new foundation for a reality of your own making."

This is the mortal-side articulation of the corpus-wide apotheosis throughline: [[ProjectApotheosis]], [[AscensionMeter]], and the Rank-A endpoint of [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] describe where this drive lands when it succeeds. Distinguishing feature vs. conventional power fantasy: administration and pageantry are treated as costs, not rewards — see the companion drive [[SurgicalDomination]].
