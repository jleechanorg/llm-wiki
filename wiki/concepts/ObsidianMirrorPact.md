# Obsidian Mirror pact

Pact mechanic from [[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]] replacing the standard warlock soul-contract with a **physical artifact and mutual surveillance channel**.

## Design

- **Object**: a shard of black glass "cold as the void between stars," framed in thorned iron. It reflects the patron's face, not the bearer's — a live window into the [[ShatteredRose]].
- **Two-way scrutiny**: [[NocturneSosuke]] watches [[SosukeAizen]] sleep, kill, and bleed; he watches her rule, studying her methods to replicate them. Intimacy and apprenticeship are the same channel.
- **Power dynamic — [[WeaponizedDesire]]**: power is granted out of boredom and hunger, not generosity; the bearer is pet, toy, potential lover, and fuel for the patron's ascension engine at once.
- **Material Anchor clause**: the bearer is the patron's only tether through the [[GeometricFirewall]] to the Material Plane, inverting leverage — the "weaker" party is structurally indispensable.

Contrasts with the parchment-and-devil contract the source explicitly rejects ("a devil who views you as a soul to be collected in fifty years").
