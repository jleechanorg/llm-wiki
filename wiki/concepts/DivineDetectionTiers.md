# Divine Detection Tiers

A risk-stratification mechanic from the [[AizenSosuke]] god-campaign (chat 1, entries 901-1147): pervasive use of divine power risks **attention from other deities**, graded by what kind of divine activity each observer is positioned to notice. Detection is a softer consequence class than direct divine combat — the cost is scrutiny and escalation risk, not immediate damage.

## The tiers (as ruled by the DM for mass telepathic conversion)

| Observer | Detection vector |
|---|---|
| [[Mystra]] | Minds / the Weave — large-scale telepathic imposition touches her portfolio |
| [[Shar]] | Domain conflict — expansion into shadow/control space she claims |
| [[Ao]] (Overgod) | Broad disruptions to the divine order itself |

## Design significance

- Creates a **stealth economy at divine scale**: Aizen's Divine Save DC 100 makes mortal resistance irrelevant ([[Telepathic-Domain-Usurpation]]), so the *only* remaining constraint is peer/overgod attention plus the [[Divine-Power-Points|DPP]] price.
- Includes a mundane-observer clause: puppeted cultists must not behave so uniformly perfectly that ordinary people infer divine control — a narrative-plausibility check mirroring [[UnusualEfficiencySignature]].
- Complements the [[Sanctuary-Imperative]] / [[Mask-of-Fragility]] family of constraint mechanics elsewhere in the wiki: all convert an overpowered protagonist's excess into a concealment-management game.
