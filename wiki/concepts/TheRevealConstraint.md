# The Reveal

The single balancing constraint on [[Nocticula]]'s build: using her full power or 'Masked' spell slots causes a mana spike that **temporarily shatters** her [[ManaVeilMastery|mana veil]], alerting any high-level mages or entities in the region to her true nature — which summons the Retrieval Squads and Purge Slayers her fugitive life exists to avoid.

This converts every hard encounter into a strategic decision — win quietly under-tier, or win instantly and start a manhunt — the defining loop of the [[HiddenPowerArchetype]]. Structurally identical to the detection-tax design in [[DivineDetectionTiers]]: escalating capability purchases escalating visibility.
