# Faction Web Restart

The specific move the Gemini DM makes when executing a [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart]]: rather than replaying old plot, it **reimagines the same faction roster with fresh internal tensions**, so continuity lives in the cast while the state machine resets.

In *Shadow over the Gate* ([[ShadowOverTheGate]]) the five-way web is:

| Faction | Role | Fresh tension |
|---|---|---|
| [[FlamingFist]] | Law/military under [[UlderRavengard]] | Internal divisions, potential corruption |
| [[TheGuild]] | Organized crime | Power vacuum / ambitious new leader |
| [[KnightsOfTheShield]] | Hidden influence | Not always aligned with the Fist |
| [[CultOfTheDeadThree]] | Chaos agent | Bane as subtle carried-over presence |
| [[HouseAizen]] | Rising power | Mysterious goals, cross-faction reach |

The design intentionally seats the player's faction ([[HouseAizen]]) as the one whose influence "potentially extends into all other factions" — the restart's tension generator. Carried-over threads (Bane) provide continuity hooks without carried-over state, distinguishing this from the full-rewind [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] and the prequel-style [[PrequelOriginCampaign]] restarts elsewhere in the Aizen lineage.
