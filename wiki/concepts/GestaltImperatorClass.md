# Gestalt Imperator Class

Homebrew D&D 5e archetype used in the [[2026-08-04-gaia-julia-v2-golden-republic|Golden Republic (Gaia Julia v2)]] campaign: a **gestalt** build taking the best features of two classes simultaneously.

## Composition

- **The Domina (Paladin — Oath of Conquest/Crown)**: Divine Smite burst damage, Aura of Conquest (10-ft fear freeze), Channel Divinity: Conquering Presence, and the CHA-to-all-saves aura that makes CHA 22 defensively load-bearing.
- **The Witch (Sorcerer — Divine Soul)**: Subtle Spell metamagic to cast without verbal/somatic components — framed as "essential for social domination," i.e., mind-affecting magic (Friends, illusions) undetectable in social scenes.

## Design intent

The gestalt exists to support the campaign's political-domination theme: the Paladin half wins open confrontations and radiates supernatural presence; the Sorcerer half wins drawing rooms invisibly. Compare [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild]] (the bard chassis used by the Aurelius v3 and BG1 Nocturne personas) — different mechanical skeleton, same social-predator function. Related power-inflation pattern in the same campaign family: Level 12 start with +3 gear in a nominally low-magic world, rationalized via First Founder artifact lore.
