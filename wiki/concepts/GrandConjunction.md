# Grand Conjunction

The central ritual of the Aizen god campaign's opening arc: an attempt by [[CorvanSosuke]] and [[SeraphinaSosuke]] to bring [[Bane]] physically into Faerûn, under the belief they could control and wield him.

## Mechanics (per Aizen's account)

- Channelers serve as conduits for the divine manifestation.
- Bane's actual design: consume the channelers as raw, unthinking fuel — the ritual is a trap for its casters.
- A counter-ritual reportedly exists (held by Seraphina) that can shatter the manifestation and banish Bane back to his plane, broken.

## Narrative function

The Conjunction gives [[AizenSosuke]] his origin catastrophe, his moral cover story, and his lever over [[BaldursGate]] factions — see [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]]. Note that all details are filtered through Aizen's deliberately constructed retelling to the [[KnightsOfTheShield]]; the wiki records his account, not ground truth.
