# Dual-Stack Gameplay

The core loop of [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]]: the player runs two parallel, decoupled campaigns in one character.

1. **Public Stack** — be the paragon: command the [[FourthStormwindExpeditionaryDetachment]], broker with [[HousePrestor]], out-argue the [[KirinTor]], win refugee loyalty, hold titles in daylight.
2. **Shadow Stack** — run the [[SentientScourge]]'s intelligence service: dispatch [[SylvanThePale]] to the [[CultOfTheDamned]], feed [[MalGanis]] intel, smuggle Pale Undead through Andorhal, negotiate with the dreadlord triumvirate, build the parallel state that becomes the [[PaleCrown]].

The stacks are insulated from each other by the [[SovereignDecouplingPrinciple]] — failure in one does not mechanically degrade the other; only politics change. This generalizes the public-virtue/shadow-sanction pattern seen across the Nocturne campaign lines (cf. [[NocturneSosuke]]'s Black Diamond persona) into an explicit two-track ruleset.
