## Overview
The Obsidian Circle is the Neo-Imperialist faction in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]), led by High Marshal [[Arcann]].

## Ideology
"Total Loyalty to the Dragon": abolish the Dark Council entirely and replace it with a military hierarchy led by Emperor [[Vitiate]]'s children — the [[GoldenLineage]] as ruling junta.

## Goal
Total war with the Republic. No treaties, no spies — just annihilation. (Direct ideological opposite of [[Thexan]]'s Diplomatic Corps within the same family.)

## Connections
- Rival factions: [[CouncilOfTwelve]] (traditionalists), [[TheEclipsed]] (nihilists)
- Named for Arcann's **Obsidian Armada**
