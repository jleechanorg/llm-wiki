# Pax Julia Timeline (The Golden Republic)

Setting frame of the [[2026-08-04-gaia-julia-v2-golden-republic|Gaia Julia v2]] campaign: an alternate Rome dated **82 BCE**, styled "The Golden Republic (Pax Julia Alternate)." Tone is low-magic Game-of-Thrones gritty realism; the only "magic" is rare artifacts of the First Founder (Aeneas/Romulus) "forged with lost techniques that defy physics."

## Position among the player's Roman alternates

| Campaign | Timeline | Divergence |
|---|---|---|
| Aurelius v1 "Pax Caesara" | 40 BCE | Caesar survives the Ides, God-Emperor |
| Aurelius Caesar v3 "Iron Eagle: Vengeance" | 71 BCE | Caesar seizes power early; Third Servile War |
| **Gaia Julia v2 "Golden Republic"** | **82 BCE** | Julian dynasty already ascendant a generation early |

## Chronology conflict

The module places [[Spartacus]] in the ludus of [[Batiatus]] in 82 BCE — historically a ~73 BCE situation, and 71 BCE in the sibling v3 campaign. The Pax Julia Alternate relocates the Spartacus material roughly a decade early rather than tracking either chronology; treat dates across the Aurelius/Gaia family as per-campaign, not shared canon.
