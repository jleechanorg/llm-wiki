# Empyrean Affliction

Latent hereditary madness in the pure-blooded celestial lines of the [[WorldOfAssiah]], first appearing as "the First Rot" in Year 400. Manifests as megalomania, paranoia, or cold cruelty — evidence that the Creator's "perfect design" is fundamentally flawed.

Cosmologically paired with the [[EngineeredCrucibleWar]]: the Creator's crucible was meant to test and cull these very bloodlines, so the Affliction reads as the disease the [[AbyssalWar]] was engineered to select against — or the damage the endless war itself inflicts. Explains the "mad, stagnant" state of the [[CelestialImperium]] by Year 1509.
