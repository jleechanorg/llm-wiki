# College of Swords Shadow Build

The Level 4 character build carrying the [[2026-08-04-aizen-bg3-v2-exiled-viper|Exiled Viper v2 campaign]]: a RAW 5e College of Swords Bard optimized entirely for social predation, with combat as a concealed fallback.

## Build sheet

- Half-Elf, Entertainer (Criminal Variant) background; CHA 18 / INT 16 / DEX 14 / STR 8; Spell DC 15; Expertise in Deception + Persuasion.
- **Spell loadout as predation toolkit**: Charm Person, Suggestion, Detect Thoughts, Silvery Barbs, Disguise Self — every pick serves the [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]] pipeline.
- **Combat identity**: AC 15, rapier + offhand dagger with Blade Flourish, Mobile Flourish hit-and-run; the STR 8 slender frame deliberately "hides lethality."

## Design constraint

Explicit rule: "no magical power-ups — just raw skill, crime, and psychological predation." Power growth comes from leverage and institutional position, with viability maintained by the campaign's [[ChallengeCompensation]] design.

## Known contradiction

The profile header labels the character "Wizard — School of the Mirror Flower" (a Bleach kyoka-suigetsu homage) while the entire stat block is RAW 5e Bard. The Bard sheet is treated as authoritative; the Wizard label is flavor drift from the [[AizenSosuke]] source material.
