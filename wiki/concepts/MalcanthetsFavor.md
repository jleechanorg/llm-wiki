# Malcanthet's Favor (The Plot Armor)

House mechanic from the [[NocturneSosuke]] v5 campaign that **replaces the standard death-save mechanic for levels 1–3**:

- At 0 HP the character does not fall unconscious; she drops to **1 HP** and becomes **Incorporeal** (Sanctuary-like) until the start of her next turn — can move, cannot attack or cast.
- Visual: dissolves into violet smoke and reforms in a safer spot.
- **Limit: once per long rest** ("[[Malcanthet]] will not save you twice").

## Design reading

This is diegetic plot armor: the patron's protection is framed as *entertainment value*, making survivability itself a reminder of the pact's conditionality. It solves the fragility problem of a CON 8 Level 1 start ([[TadpoleSuppressionNerf]]) without removing stakes — one save per rest, then real death rules resume. Compare [[ChallengeCompensation]] and [[ConsequenceEnforcement]] as sibling mechanics balancing overpowered/underpowered protagonist frames.
