# Belaerys Allure

Signature risk-reward mechanic of [[VisenyaBelaerys]] in *The Apex of Ash* (campaign visenya v2).

**Effect**: Any NPC who sees Visenya's true face must pass a **DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be Charmed**.

**Cost (*The Prickle*)**: Each use grants the **Unfriendly Remnants** — the hostile trackers of [[HouseBelaerys]]'s legacy — a **+5 bonus to locate her**.

Design notes:
- Converts "Exceptional Beauty" from flavor text into an economy: influence now vs. exposure later. The player must ration their strongest social weapon.
- Member of the [[BeautyAsWeapon]] family, but distinguished by making the weapon *always loaded* (triggering on sight, not intent) — hence the straw hat and [[FunctionalObscurity]] as the default posture, and revealing the face as a deliberate spend.
- Pairs with Royal Insight (INT/WIS for social checks) so the charm opens doors and intellect exploits them — consistent with the serpent-mastermind INTJ profile across the user's campaigns.
