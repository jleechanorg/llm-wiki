# Subtle Apotheosis

Signature mechanic of the [[DragonLordSubclass]] in *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]).

## Rules

- Grants the **Subtle Spell** Metamagic for free.
- Spells that influence the mind (Command, Suggestion, Charm Person) or affect Dragons require **no Sorcery Points** for Subtle Spell — the casting is invisible, read by observers as "divine luck" or "royal authority."

## Function

This is the deniability engine that lets [[VisenyaBelaerys]] exert apex control while keeping the [[AscensionMeter]] low: the opening scene's Option A (subtle Calm Emotions on [[Vermithor]], credited to the dragon sparing [[GerrickTheSmith]]) is the mechanic's canonical use. It operationalizes the wiki-recurring theme of manipulation laundered as fortune — social dominance without attributable magic.
