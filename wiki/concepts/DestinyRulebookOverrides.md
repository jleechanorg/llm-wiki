# Destiny Rulebook Overrides

Custom stat system layered over D&D 5e in the [[Aizen]] god campaign, replacing or supplementing standard AC with a richer defensive vocabulary. Documented on [[AizenSosuke]]'s Level 10 sheet in [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-1-301-600|chat 1 entries 301-600]]:

| Stat | Value (Aizen L10) | Standard-5e analogue |
|---|---|---|
| Effective Defense | 20 | closest to AC-as-hit-target |
| Dodge Rating | 7 | no direct analogue (avoidance) |
| Armor Rating | 20 | armor-derived protection |
| Armor Mitigation | 10 | damage reduction (5e has none by default) |

Standard AC 18 coexists on the same sheet (Holy Armor +4, Bulwark of the Righteous +3), so the overrides add a parallel resolution layer rather than cleanly replacing 5e defense.

## Significance

- Splitting avoidance (Dodge) from mitigation (Armor Mitigation) imports MMO/video-game defense design into tabletop resolution.
- Parallel stat systems multiply bookkeeping surface — a contributing factor to the [[DMBookkeepingTrustGap]] pattern, since the DM must keep two defense models consistent per level-up.
- Related homebrew layers in the same lineage: [[Divine-Power-Points]], [[DynamicSituationalModifiers]], [[TieredSuccessOutcomes]].
