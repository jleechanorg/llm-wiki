# Player-Directed EXP Grant

Pattern where the player directly requests experience points and the DM computes and applies **exactly the XP needed for the next level**, rather than awarding XP through play (encounters, milestones, roleplay). Levels become an on-demand resource the player pulls, inverting the normal DM-paced progression economy.

## Canonical instance

In [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-1-301-600|Aizen god campaign chat 1 (301-600)]], the player at Level 9 (56,660 XP) requested EXP via the shorthand "add itg"; after a miscommunication detour, the DM added precisely 7,340 XP to reach the 64,000 threshold for Level 10 (Paladin 2 / Bard 8).

## Failure mode: shorthand elaboration

Ambiguous player shorthand invites DM overreach — here the DM initially interpreted "add itg" as a request for an "Infernal Taint" custom class bonus and began negotiating thematic consistency with the [[Bane]] Child-of-Bane heritage. This is the [[Whispers of the Realm]] pattern applied to mechanics: the DM narratively elaborates instead of asking a clarifying question.
