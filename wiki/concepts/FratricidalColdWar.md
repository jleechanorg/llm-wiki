# Fratricidal Cold War

Campaign-spine structure from [[2026-08-04-aurelius-v1-pax-caesara|Aurelius v1]]: the central long-game conflict is a "ruthless fratricidal cold war" among the sons of [[JuliusCaesar]] for sole rights to the name Caesar.

- **Setup**: elder brothers hold administration and foreign thrones (positional power); [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]] climbs the Cursus Honorum by force (earned/coerced power). The asymmetry manufactures conflict without open war.
- **Cold-war constraint**: because the Empire is at "peace" under the God-Emperor, sibling elimination must run through politics, prestige, proxies, and legion glory rather than direct violence — matching the campaign's three declared arenas (court, frontier, family).
- **Corpus parallels**: dynastic-spare framing echoes [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]]'s "the Spare" protagonist setup in [[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor|Aizen Thay v1]]; succession-by-demonstrated-supremacy echoes the ascension logic of [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]].
