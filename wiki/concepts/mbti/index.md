# MBTI Personal Growth Index

The 16 MBTI personality type pages, grouped by Keirsey **temperaments** (NT, NF, SJ, SP). Each page summarizes cognitive function stack, traits, strengths, weaknesses, career matches, relationship patterns, **and an explicit Personal Growth direction**. ## By Temperament

### Analysts (NT) — logic, systems, strategy
Analysts — driven by logic, systems, and strategy. Intuition + Thinking.

- [[INTJ]] (Architect) — Independent, strategic, driven to improve systems; long-range planners.
- [[INTP]] (Logician) — Logical, original, abstract thinkers; quiet theorists of systems and ideas.
- [[ENTJ]] (Commander) — Strategic, decisive, assertive; natural leaders who drive long-term plans to completion.
- [[ENTP]] (Debater) — Quick, clever, theoretical; enjoy new ideas and intellectual debate.

### Diplomats (NF) — values, meaning, human potential
Diplomats — driven by values, meaning, and human potential. Intuition + Feeling.

- [[INFJ]] (Advocate) — Quietly forceful with original ideas and strong values; visionary idealists.
- [[INFP]] (Mediator) — Idealistic, loyal to values; seek meaning and inner harmony, deeply authentic.
- [[ENFJ]] (Protagonist) — Charismatic, empathetic leaders; inspire and develop others toward their potential.
- [[ENFP]] (Campaigner) — Warm, imaginative, possibility-driven; see life as a web of connections.

### Sentinels (SJ) — duty, structure, continuity
Sentinels — driven by duty, structure, and continuity. Sensing + Judging.

- [[ISTJ]] (Logistician) — Quiet, serious, success through dependability; methodical, analytical, hard-working.
- [[ISFJ]] (Defender) — Quiet, kind, conscientious; great memory for details, harmony-oriented caretakers.
- [[ESTJ]] (Executive) — Decisive, organized, direct; implementers who enforce rules and traditions.
- [[ESFJ]] (Consul) — Warm, conscientious, cooperative; community-builders who value harmony.

### Explorers (SP) — action, presence, adaptability
Explorers — driven by action, presence, and adaptability. Sensing + Perceiving.

- [[ISTP]] (Virtuoso) — Tolerant, flexible, quiet observers; troubleshooters with calm detachment.
- [[ISFP]] (Adventurer) — Gentle, sensitive, harmonious; live in the present with strong aesthetic sense.
- [[ESTP]] (Entrepreneur) — Energetic, pragmatic, realistic; flexible problem-solvers who act in the moment.
- [[ESFP]] (Entertainer) — Spontaneous, enthusiastic, people-focused; bring fun and energy to the present.
