# Silver Tongue

[[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s signature social mechanic: charisma so high it functions like mind control. Built from Glibness (passive, always-convincing), Expertise in Persuasion flavored as subsonic resonance ("her words feel like a law of nature"), Suggestion as "the Voice of the Dragon," and Detect Thoughts as "Royal Insight" micro-expression reading. She "rewrites reality" conversationally — gaslighting kings and Hands into believing her "White Lies" are their own ideas.

An instance of the wiki-wide [[BeautyAsWeapon]]-adjacent pattern: social dominance mechanics standing in for overt magic in a low-magic setting.
