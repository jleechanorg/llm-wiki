# Conceptual Debate Victory

Non-combat resolution mechanic where a cosmic-scale conflict is decided by **winning a metaphysical argument** rather than a fight. The canonical instance: [[AizenSosuke]] vs [[Ao-the-Overgod]] in the god-campaign summary.

## How it works

- The challenger presents a competing axiom for how reality should be ordered (Aizen: a single absolute will, replacing eternal good/evil conflict).
- The opponent's own nature is the vulnerability: Ao, as a being of pure law, is **obligated to accept a demonstrably superior theorem**. Losing the debate means being subsumed by the winning framework — "retired," not killed.
- Outcome grants authority transfer at a scale combat cannot: Aizen becomes The Absolute without a fight.

## Design significance

Caps the escalation ladder of [[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]] — the final boss is beaten by argument, matching the operator's documented preference for manipulation-over-violence protagonists. Sets up [[The-Grand-Game]] as the post-victory gameplay loop.
