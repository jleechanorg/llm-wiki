# Weaponized Intimacy

Tradecraft doctrine from the Silent Blade campaign: seduction used **for access, not romance**, where every intimate gesture doubles as reconnaissance.

## Canonical examples (from the source)

- Touching a marksman's arm *to gauge his muscle density*.
- Whispering a joke to a guard *to smell the ale on his breath* (sobriety check).
- Letting targets believe **they** are the pursuers, inverting the predator/prey frame.

## Function

Lowers target guard with desire so that "when you strike, they are looking at your lips, not your hands." The mechanism is the operational core of the [[VenomousOrchid]] archetype's Gilded Spider mask and feeds target selection for the [[JohanExperiment]].
