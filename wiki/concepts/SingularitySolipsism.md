# Singularity Solipsism

The designed core flaw of [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] in the [[2026-08-04-gaia-julia-v2-golden-republic|Golden Republic (Gaia Julia v2)]] campaign: she "genuinely believes she is the only 'real' person in the world. Everyone else is scenery."

## Function in the campaign design

- **Motivation engine**: her stated driver is "profound ennui" — absolute power is boring without friction — so she plays with lives (herding, slaughtering, or breaking-and-keeping mortals like [[Spartacus]]) to manufacture stakes.
- **Mechanical hook**: the sheet ties it to WIS 14's caveat that "arrogance sometimes blinds you to 'lesser' threats" — a DM-usable blind spot in an otherwise near-unbeatable Level 12 build.
- **Pattern position**: the most extreme statement yet of the player's recurring Lawful Evil dual-mask archetype ([[BeautyAsWeapon]], [[DecadentConqueror]]); earlier Aurelius variants frame mortals as pieces on a board, while this one denies their reality outright.
