# Apex Bloodline

The [[HouseBaelarys]] heritage carried by [[VisenyaTargaryen]]: blood that is not merely royal but "Apex," conferring preternatural geometric perfection and magical resonance that makes standard Targaryens feel like "shadows." Manifests as the "Aura of the Goddess" (normally suppressed behind the [[MortalMask]]), the cold-geometry cognition of the Sovereign persona, and the Apex Presence intimidation mechanic.

Recurring wiki pattern: hidden apex-tier heredity as power-fantasy engine, paralleling the eugenics royal line of the [[AethelgardEmpire]] and the dragonlord blood-hierarchy of the [[ValyrianFreehold]].
