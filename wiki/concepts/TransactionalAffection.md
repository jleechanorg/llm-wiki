# Transactional Affection

Attachment model written into [[Alexiel]]'s bible: raised in a lab where she was "fed only when she performed," she does not parse love — only Debt and Ownership. Kindness from [[MasterOganDe]] triggers "what does he want?"; saving [[JaxPadawan]]'s life creates an expectation of service, not gratitude. Paired with explicit narcissistic fragility (humiliation and disfigurement read as loss of power).

**Design role**: gives the DM a deterministic lens for every social scene — any NPC generosity becomes a probe of the conditioning, and genuine unconditional care is the campaign's real challenge content. Cross-campaign: the affect-as-instrument stance matches the [[SosukeAizen]] line ([[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]], [[PreemptiveFrameControl]]) but grounded in trauma conditioning rather than chosen philosophy.
