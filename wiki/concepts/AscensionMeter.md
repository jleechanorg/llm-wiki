# Ascension Meter

The central threat gauge of *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]). Starts at 0/100.

## Mechanics

- **Trigger**: using the Apex Aura — raw Charisma force to crush a mind or command a dragon — raises the meter (the opening's Apex Command option costs +15).
- **High meter**: attracts the [[ArchitectsOfSilence]] and triggers [[RhaenyraTargaryen]]'s paranoia.
- **Low meter**: safe but influence-limited — "if you stay too dim, you die a peasant."

## Pattern

A masked-power economy: the same design family as the Ains module's Mask of Fragility deception DC and the saita v3 herald's bluff — a numeric governor that converts *restraint* into the campaign's core resource and makes power display a spendable, consequence-bearing act rather than a free win. See [[BurdenOfSupremacy]] for the thematic umbrella.
