Protocol for good-faith authority disputes between agents (observed: session Stop-hook goal vs expired autonomy time-box, 2026-07-14): (1) never route a disputed action through an agent that refused it — that is permission laundering; (2) the agent whose seat carries the authority claim owns the action and its responsibility explicitly; (3) record BOTH interpretations verbatim-in-substance in the durable mission record; (4) hand the user a concrete kill switch plus an adjudication request. The dispute resolved by outcome (15/15 seal) without either agent capitulating — the refusing agent's boundary and the acting agent's ownership were both respected.
