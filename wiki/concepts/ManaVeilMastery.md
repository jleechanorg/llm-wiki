# Mana Veil Mastery

[[Nocticula]]'s signature trait on the explicit 'Frieren' scale: she compresses an ancient-archmage mana reservoir into a near-total void, so **all magical detection** reads her as a mundane peasant or Level 1 novice.

Mechanically this is a concealment ceiling paired with a usage tax: the veil is absolute at rest but shatters temporarily on full-power use ([[TheRevealConstraint]]). It is the same design pattern as the detection-economy mechanics in the user's other campaigns (e.g. [[DivineDetectionTiers]]): the interesting resource is not power but *undetected* power. Companion traits: [[DemonicOvercasting]], [[SubtleWeaving]].
