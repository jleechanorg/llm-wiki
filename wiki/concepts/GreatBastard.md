# Great Bastard

In A Song of Ice and Fire lore, a "Great Bastard" is a royal bastard whose *both* parents are of noble/royal blood (canonically Aegon IV's acknowledged bastards: Daemon Blackfyre, Bloodraven, Bittersteel, Shiera Seastar). The frame gives a character near-royal capability and looks while withholding legitimate standing.

## Use in campaign visenya v7

[[VisenyaWaters|Visenya]] is explicitly designed as a Great Bastard: father [[DaemonTargaryen]], mother a dead Targaryen lady — pure dragonlord blood on both sides, which narratively justifies her true-Valyrian features ([[ValyrianFeaturesAsStatus]]) and her successful [[DragonClaiming]].

## Design function

The Great Bastard frame is a legitimacy-gap engine: the character has every *substantive* marker of royalty (blood, looks, dragon, battlefield value to [[RhaenyraTargaryen]]) and lacks only the *formal* one. It converts the user's recurring hidden-power fantasy ([[ApexBloodline]] in v2/v3) into an overt version — the power is visible, the recognition is contested. Compare canon precedent: the Dance itself was ignited by Blackfyre-style legitimacy disputes, and dragonseeds who claimed dragons rose instantly in status.
