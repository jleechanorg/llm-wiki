## Overview
The Eclipsed are the Nihilist faction in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]), led by [[TheProphetess]] — a mysterious Force user, possibly a clone of Kreia or a new entity.

## Defining knowledge
They are the faction that **knows** Emperor [[Vitiate]] plans to consume the galaxy via [[TheHarvest]] — knowledge hidden from both the [[CouncilOfTwelve]] and the general Empire.

## Caveat
The source document cuts off mid-sentence while describing this faction ("They know the Emperor plans to eat the galaxy. And th—"), so their response to that knowledge — resist, accelerate, or exploit — is unspecified in the ingested text.
