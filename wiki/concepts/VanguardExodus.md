# Vanguard Exodus

The campaign-spanning objective of [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]]: a daring pre-collapse migration of [[HouseTargaryen]] (the "41st House") across the Narrow Sea to resource-rich, untouched Westeros, guided by [[SarielAsh]]'s pedigree and executed while the [[FortyFamiliesCivilWar]] consumes the [[ValyrianFreehold]].

The strategic core is **arbitrage on institutional blindness**: the Freehold prices Westeros at zero ("muddy, worthless wilderness inhabited by sub-mortal kings playing with timber and iron"), so the one actor who values it correctly captures an entire continent uncontested. The end state is "an unassailable, permanent empire" with an unchained sky for the [[LevelScaledDragonCompanion]].

Re-frames ASOIAF canon's Targaryen escape (Daenys the Dreamer's prophecy) as deliberate INTJ strategy rather than prophetic luck — consistent with the operator's serpent-mastermind campaign preference profile.
