# The Sowing (of the Dragonseeds)

The historical event framing *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]): with the Dance of Dragons begun, Queen [[RhaenyraTargaryen]] invites commoners of suspected Valyrian descent ("Dragonseeds") to attempt to claim the riderless dragons of the Dragonmont on [[Dragonstone]], to match the Greens' firepower.

## In This Campaign

The Sowing is both the opportunity and the clock: Dragonkeepers close the trials each day (the opening scene's time pressure), failed claimants are incinerated ([[Vermithor]] has just burned a whip-wielding knight), and success means entering Rhaenyra's court as a *used asset* — the political stage on which the [[AscensionMeter]] tension plays out. For [[VisenyaBelaerys]] it is cover: a legitimate public path to a dragon that hides an Apex heir's restoration inside a peasant's lottery.
