# Venomous Orchid

Character archetype defined in the Silent Blade campaign for [[NocturneSilentBlade|Nocturne]]: an operative who moves along a spectrum between the irresistible temptress (explicit reference: Lust from *Fullmetal Alchemist*) and the terrifying, unstoppable slayer (The Predator). The signature claim: she dismantles *people*, not just systems — "peeling back their defenses with a lover's whisper or a butcher's knife."

## Structure: two perfectly-fitted masks

1. **The Gilded Spider** — the social interface. Stands at the *center* of the room rather than the shadows; deploys [[WeaponizedIntimacy]] so targets look at her lips, not her hands.
2. **The Butcher in the Dark** — the operative interface. Rejects "clean kill" codes; treats gore as strategic messaging on behalf of the [[Zhentarim]].

## Relation to prior wiki archetypes

Sibling to the [[DecadentConqueror]] (Gaia Julia line) and the [[SirensLeverage]] mechanic family: all monetize desire as a control lever. Distinct in that the Venomous Orchid pairs desire with *horror* as co-equal levers ("fear and lust") rather than desire alone, and adds the corruption-testing engine of the [[JohanExperiment]].
