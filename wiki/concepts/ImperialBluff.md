# The Imperial Bluff

Signature mechanic of [[SaitaAethelgard]] in *The Hollow Zenith* ([[2026-08-04-saita-v3-hollow-zenith]]).

**Upside**: whenever Saita successfully uses Charisma (Intimidation or Deception) to force an enemy to back down from a *physical* confrontation, she gains Temporary HP equal to her Charisma modifier + her Level — her ego literally feeds her survivability.

**Downside**: if her true identity or the [[AethelgardEmpire]]'s weakness is successfully called out or exposed in a scene, she suffers a point of Exhaustion from the psychological dissonance.

Design function: makes social manipulation the campaign's real combat system for a Level 1 character whose lore-strength cannot yet be expressed mechanically. Belongs to the same mechanic family as other identity-tension resources in the campaign corpus — compare [[Miasma]]-style strain trackers and the Mask of Fragility deception-DC mechanic in the Ains Sanctuary module: all convert *maintaining a false self-presentation* into a scored resource with a defined failure cost. Sits atop [[WeaknessMaskingDiplomacy]] as its per-scene mechanical expression.
