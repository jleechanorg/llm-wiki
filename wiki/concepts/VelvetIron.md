# Velvet Iron

Signature always-on mechanic of the [[CollegeOfTheSiren]] build in Gaia Julia v5: the character effectively has **Subtle Spell metamagic permanently active for all Enchantment and Illusion spells**, allowing Dominate Person or Suggestion to be cast mid-conversation with no verbal, somatic, or visible component.

## Why it matters to the campaign frame

The entire v5 premise (a hidden Level 12 caster posing as a Level 1 commoner among enemies) depends on castings being undetectable — a single witnessed spell would collapse the "Livia" masquerade. Velvet Iron is therefore the load-bearing mechanic of the inverted-hostage plot, the magical counterpart to the nonverbal command channel used with [[TheLotusTen]]. The source document cuts off mid-description of this section, so any additional riders are unrecorded.
