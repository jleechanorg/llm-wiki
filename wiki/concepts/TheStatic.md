# The Static (The Vaylin Echo)

Narrative-psychological mechanic in the [[2026-08-04-alexiel-swtor-siren-master-bible|Alexiel campaign]]: the character is "never alone." The Static is the blended psychic residue of [[Vitiate]]'s dream connection and [[Vaylin]]'s insanity — a low-frequency hum like grinding tectonic plates that insists mercy is weakness and urges destruction for its own sake. Coping mechanism: humming melodies, which unnerves classmates (a tell the table can play against the [[MaskOfTheInnocent]]).

**Design role**: an always-on Dark Side pressure that needs no tempter NPC — corruption as ambient inheritance. It also explains the stat line (WIS 10 "clouded by the Static"), making the character mechanically weakest against exactly the influence genre she practices.
