# Preemptive Frame Control

Signature tactic of the serpent-mastermind protagonist profile: **win a confrontation of wits before it begins** by controlling what the opposition believes the situation *is* — who is interrogating whom, what the stakes are, and which threat matters.

## Mechanics observed

- In [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-1-601-900]], [[AizenSosuke]] bypasses the [[KnightsOfTheShield]] wards with Knock, arriving on his own terms; the agents who intended to interrogate him instead receive a briefing.
- The frame is set *before* any contested roll or negotiation: by the time the Knights' agents speak, the encounter's subject is Bane's threat to their mission, not Aizen's classification as threat-or-asset.

## Relationship to other patterns

- Delivery vehicle: [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]] (the constructed fact/fiction blend).
- Strategic payoff: [[UnwittingAllyRecruitment]] (rivals act as allies).
- Recurs across the campaign lineage — see [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-summary]] for how this tactic scales from faction rooms to divine politics.
