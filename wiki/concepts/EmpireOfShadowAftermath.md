# Empire of Shadow — the Aftermath campaign frame

Campaign frame from the v0 setup doc "BG3: The Aftermath — A Campaign of Chaos & Survival": rather than starting after a heroic ending, play begins in the ruins of a **victory that changed nothing structurally** — "the saviors of Baldur's Gate did not liberate the city; they merely reorganized its chains."

## Structure

1. **Crisis resolved**: the Netherbrain is dead (a rotting mass in the Chionthar).
2. **Victors become the setting**: the [[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]] partitions [[BaldursGate]] into three tyrannies — blood (Astarion's vampiric economy), shadow (Shadowheart's Sharran theocracy), arcana (Gale's Netherese experiments).
3. **Fates sealed**: all other legends are locked into endpoints via [[DefinitiveCompanionFates]].
4. **Player space**: survival and resistance from below — the implied protagonists are ordinary survivors navigating three overlapping empires, with [[Karlach]] and the reforming [[Raphael]] as ready-made faction seeds.

## Relation to existing patterns

This is the fullest realized instance of [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] in the wiki, and a sibling to the Aizen-lineage [[ShadowOverTheGate]] setup — same city, same appetite for evil-victor worldbuilding, different continuity and ruling faction.
