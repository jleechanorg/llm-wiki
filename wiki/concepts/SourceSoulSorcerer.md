# Source Soul Sorcerer

Homebrew sorcerer subclass in "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse" — a **dark adaptation of the Divine Soul Sorcerer** representing the protagonist's monopolized Source after consuming [[LucianTheDivine]] and [[DallisTheHammer]].

## Key properties
- Access to **Sorcerer, Paladin, AND Cleric spell lists**, all Charisma-based (DC 17 at L12)
- Font of Magic: 12 Sorcery Points at L12; Metamagic scaling (2 → 3 options by L14)
- Fuels [[SourceSmite]] conversions and the Veil Striker ambush rider
- Paired with Oath of Conquest Paladin in the [[GestaltTier4Progression]] chassis
- L15 feature named "Revelation of Source" (details truncated in source document)
