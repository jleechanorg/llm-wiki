# Unwitting Ally Recruitment

Manipulation pattern where a rival or hostile faction is converted into a functional ally **without its knowledge or consent**, by framing the manipulator's agenda as an instance of the faction's own institutional mission.

## Structure

1. **Model the target faction's mission** — what it believes it exists to protect (for the [[KnightsOfTheShield]]: "order" and "balance").
2. **Construct a threat narrative** ([[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]]) in which the manipulator's enemy attacks exactly that mission.
3. **Deliver it as intelligence, not a request** — the faction acts on it as if self-directed, spending its own resources on the manipulator's goals.

## Canonical instance

In [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-1-601-900]], [[AizenSosuke]] casts the [[GrandConjunction]] and [[Bane]]'s pending manifestation as an assault on the balance the Knights preserve — turning an intelligence faction that came to classify him as a threat into an asset aligned against his enemy.

## Distinction from adjacent concepts

- Unlike simple deception, the payload can be mostly true; what is engineered is the *framing* and the target's inferred self-interest.
- Complements [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]] (the content) — this concept is the *aim* of that content: alliance formation without negotiation.
