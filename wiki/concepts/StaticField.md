# Static Field

Signature passive of the [[ApexWeaver]] class in campaign v6. When [[VisenyaTargaryen]] is focused or amused, local reality warps:

- Passively drops room temperature by 20 degrees (fictional tell: ozone-and-mint chill).
- Permanent **Advantage on Intimidation and Insight**.
- **Disadvantage on Stealth** unless magically muffled — a built-in cost that makes the incognito "Lady Elyse" mask mechanically fragile.
- Lower-level enemies **automatically fail insight checks** against her lies, mechanizing the campaign's power-disparity premise.
