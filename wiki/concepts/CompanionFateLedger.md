# Companion Fate Ledger

Campaign-setup device where **every major companion/NPC from the prior story receives a definitive, fixed fate** before play begins, instead of an open or ambiguous epilogue. The world state is grim and settled; the campaign starts *inside* the consequences.

## How it works in Empire of Shadow

The *Empire of Shadow* premise ([[2026-08-04-bg3-empire-of-shadow-dark-triumvirate]]) assigns each BG3 companion an epithet and endpoint: [[Laezel]] the Astral Scourge, [[Wyll]] the Broken Blade, [[Karlach]] the Mind Flayer Rebel, [[Minthara]] the High General, [[Halsin]] the Shadow-Touched, [[Jaheira]] the Risen Martyr, [[Minsc]] the Stone Sentinel, [[Withers]] the chronicler, [[Raphael]] the reforming essence.

## Why it matters as a design pattern

- **Fixed world state over branching epilogue** — the DM commits to one canon, which makes faction play legible and stakes concrete.
- **Built-in hooks** — each fate is a quest seed: free the petrified hero, join the sewer rebel, intercept the chronicler's book.
- **Tone-setting** — mostly-tragic fates announce a [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] where victory did not mean salvation.

Sibling pattern to [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] (Aizen lineage): both restart a known cast, but the ledger *fixes* outcomes forward while a clean-slate reset *rewinds* them.
