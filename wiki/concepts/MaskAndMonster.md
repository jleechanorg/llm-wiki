# The Mask and the Monster

Thematic pillar of the [[WorldOfAssiah]] campaign: the psychological toll of pretending to be a remorseless killer while secretly harboring "a desperate, hidden empathy." [[Alexiel]] must sustain a monstrous public persona inside a regime practicing the [[FiftyPercentCullingDoctrine]], while the [[HighPersistenceWorldState]] world remembers every spared child.

Structural inverse of [[Mask-of-Fragility]] (Ains performing weakness to hide power): here the character performs cruelty to hide compassion. Both belong to the operator's recurring persona-management taste seen across the Aizen ([[KyokaSuigetsu]], [[PreemptiveFrameControl]]) and Ains campaigns.
