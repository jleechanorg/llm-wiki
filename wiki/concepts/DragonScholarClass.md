# Dragon Scholar (Class)

Custom wizard subclass built for [[VisenyaBelaerys]] in *The Apex of Ash* (campaign visenya v2). Power is a manifestation of the Purest Valyrian Blood: where Targaryens merely command dragons, the Dragon Scholar has intellectual and intuitive mastery of the "Draconic Weave" predating the Iron Throne. Primary abilities INT (18) and WIS (16).

**Key features**:
- **[[SubtleApotheosis]]** — componentless, invisible casting at full caster progression.
- **Royal Insight** — Persuasion/Deception/Intimidation use INT or WIS instead of CHA.

**Five-tier progression (L1–30)**:
1. *Dragon Scholar* (1–5) — subtle casting, Royal Insight, damage spells removed for low-magic balance.
2. *Hatched Soul* (6–10) — dragon companion hatches via narrative quest and levels alongside her; mastery of the "Noblest Dialect" of High Valyrian.
3. *The Uncrowned* (11–16) — permanent True Sight; zero-visibility upcasting.
4. *Apex Sovereign* (17–20) — +10 to INT-based checks; subtle 9th-level utility/illusion.
5. *Ascendant God-Queen* (21–30) — epic boon *Eternal Apotheosis*: re-weave reality once per day; resistance to all non-magical damage.

Another instance of the recurring 30-level ascension template in this user's campaign bibles (compare the tier ladders in the Saita and Sariel documents and [[AgeProgressionByLevel]]).
