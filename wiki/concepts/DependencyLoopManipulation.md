# Dependency-Loop Manipulation

Control technique defined in [[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension|The Architecture of Ascension]]: front-load unsolicited, measurable value (reorganizing party inventory for 15% weight efficiency while others sleep; researched herbal remedies that beat magical healing) to make oneself indispensable — explicitly "not to be loved, but to create a dependency loop."

Stated internal logic: "If I am essential, they cannot discard me. If I control the supply of solutions, I control the demand of their w[ants]" (source truncates here). Supply-side control of problem-solving as a power base — the economic framing distinguishes it from ordinary ingratiation.

Executed through the [[CourtlyShadowMask]]; targets in this module are [[GravenAshe]] and the [[VoicesOfNerat]]. Thematic kin to [[SirensLeverage]] and [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]] in the BG3 branches — the campaign line's recurring pattern of converting service into leverage.
