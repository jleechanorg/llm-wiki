# Decadent Conqueror

Master personality archetype introduced in the v6 Iron Eagle personality bible for [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]]. Labeled an **ESFP–ENTJ hinge ("The Performer / The King")** — a fusion of sensory-performer charisma and command-drive ambition.

## Structure

- **Visual signature**: perpetual tactile contact — running a hand over marble, smelling wine, a hand on a subordinate's shoulder. Every touch is framed as a **gesture of Ownership**: "When you touch something, you are claiming it."
- **Two perfectly-fitted masks**:
  - *Prince of Pleasure* (public): intoxicating warmth, loud laughter, open weeping at tragedies, ambition draped in "Passion" and "Patriotism" — "You make tyranny look fun."
  - *Golden Tyrant* (true): Lawful Evil absolute Entitlement. "You do not calculate; you crave." Order is beautiful, Chaos is ugly; punishment is the wrath of a jealous god denied his due; sadism is emotional — watching hope break validates supremacy.
- **Supporting mechanics**: [[BloodOfVenus]] (beauty as armor, [[BeautyAsWeapon]]), the [[SensoryFeast]] compulsion, and the [[Miasma]] divine-blood power source under which the character self-identifies as "the Devouring Maw."

## Relation to other archetypes

Sibling of the [[ShadowPrince]] (the same character's operational cover archetype) and a tonal inversion of the calculating serpent-mastermind persona seen in [[SerpentQueen]] campaigns — appetite-driven heat rather than cold manipulation, while sharing the dual-mask Lawful Evil chassis.
