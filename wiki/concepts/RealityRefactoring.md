# Reality Refactoring

The flavor conceit of [[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension|The Architecture of Ascension]] (subtitle: "The Refactoring of Reality"): the protagonist is mechanically a Bard (College of Swords, for Blade Flourish + Charisma) but roleplayed as an Order of Scribes Wizard. She does not sing — she "recites mathematical proofs that force reality to correct itself," using her blade as a conductor's baton/focus.

Extends to perception: constantly scanning geometric flaws in masonry, caloric inefficiency in a guard's gait, "the probability lines of the Edict's magic" ([[EdictOfExecution]]). [[Terratus]] is treated as a flawed schematic to be corrected; the endgame ambition is rewriting the laws of magic itself.

Mechanics/roleplay split is a signature of the [[NocturneSosuke]] line (cf. [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild]], [[CollegeOfPerdition]]) — Bard chassis re-skinned to whatever power fantasy the branch requires.
