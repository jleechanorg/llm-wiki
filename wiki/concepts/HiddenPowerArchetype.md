# Hidden Power Archetype

Recurring protagonist pattern across the user's campaigns, made fully explicit in the [[Nocticula]] bio (which names its own "'Frieren' Scale"): a character with overwhelming capability that is (1) mechanically concealed from all detection, (2) usable at levels far above apparent tier, and (3) taxed by a detection/visibility cost on full use rather than by resource attrition.

Components in the Nocticula instance: [[ManaVeilMastery]] + [[DemonicOvercasting]] + [[SubtleWeaving]], gated by [[TheRevealConstraint]]. Sibling instances: the [[NocturneSosuke]] / [[SosukeAizen]] concealed-mastermind builds, the [[DivineDetectionTiers]] visibility economy, and the Gaia Julia "bored living god" line ([[BoredGodGaze]]). The archetype pairs with an INTJ optimizer psychology ([[OptimalOutcomeReasoning]]) and delivers the user's core fantasy: strategic dominance exercised from behind a mask, where the drama is *when to be seen*, not whether one can win.
