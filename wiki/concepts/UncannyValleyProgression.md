# Uncanny Valley Progression

Level-keyed appearance-evolution system introduced in the Velvet Cage v7 module ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-bg3-v7-velvet-cage]]). Explicit design theme: **"You do not grow monstrous; you grow perfect"** — power gain is rendered as escalating refinement of a humanoid form past the point of plausibility (too symmetrical, too smooth, too still), rather than the genre-standard accretion of claws, scales, or bulk.

## The six stages

1. **The Ruin (L0)** — genuine victim presentation; fragility is real.
2. **The Porcelain Doll (L1–4)** — environment stops marking the body (no grime, sweat, shivering); first "too perfect" tells.
3. **The Uncanny Valley (L5–9)** — living-marble skin, wounds crack rather than bleed, gliding movement, eye-glow on casting.
4. **The Predator (L10–14)** — voluntary blinking; fragility is now legible as a trap; spectral horns/wings flash on social crits.
5. **The Sovereign (L15–19)** — passive environmental effects (floating hair, persistent ozone/brine scent, lingering light trails).
6. **The Ascended (L20)** — statue-brought-to-life apotheosis.

## Design function

Each stage pairs a visual change with a **third-party social read** ("too pretty for this mud" → "something is wrong with that girl, but I can't look away" → instinctively lowered voices), so character level leaks into the fiction as NPC perception rather than a hidden number. This converts appearance into a leveled information channel and escalating soft-control tool — the progression-mechanic counterpart of [[BeautyAsWeapon]] (static charisma-as-armor) and the inversion of [[SosukeAizen]]'s Mask of Fragility, which conceals power instead of radiating it. Compare [[AgeProgressionByLevel]] in the sibling campaign lines, which keys chronological presentation rather than perfection to level bands.
