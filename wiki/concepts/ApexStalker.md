# Apex Stalker

The v9 protagonist chassis for [[VisenyaTargaryen]]: a **Level 6 gestalt of Gloom Stalker Ranger 6 and Assassin Rogue 6**, explicitly positioned against the v6 bard/mastermind gestalt ("Why Gloom Stalker / Assassin and Not Bard / Mastermind") — the same power-fantasy protagonist re-expressed as a predator instead of a manipulator.

## Design pillars

- **Darkness supremacy**: Umbral Sight (60 ft in magical + nonmagical darkness) + Blind-Fighting + Shadowy Dodge make her "unfair in low light"; urban-night Natural Explorer keys her to the Crownlands' bandit terrain.
- **Alpha-strike economy**: Assassinate (advantage vs. un-acted creatures; auto-crit on Surprised) + Alert (+5 initiative, can't be surprised) + Sneak Attack 6d6 + Stalker's Flurry — the build wins fights in round one or before they start.
- **Detection asymmetry**: double Expertise (Stealth/Perception at +11) + Skulker (hide while lightly obscured, immune to blindsight/tremorsense) — "She cannot be unseen; she is unseen."
- **Durability as afterthought**: Uncanny Dodge and Iron Mind only; the build's defense is never being targeted.

Augmented by the homebrew [[StressLineSight]] tier ladder, which extends the chassis past level 6 toward Sovereign/Demi-God tiers. Compare [[ApexWeaver]] (v5 line) for the sibling "Apex"-branded progression concept.
