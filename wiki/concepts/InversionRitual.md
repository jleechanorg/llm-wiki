# Inversion Ritual

Climax mechanic of [[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]]: instead of stopping the villains' ritual, the Level 1 protagonist **steps into the circle and changes the beneficiary** — stealing the [[CassalanterFamily]]'s pact with [[Zariel]] for herself. The module is blunt about why it's not a fight: "You are Level 1. You will die."

## Structure — complex skill challenge (4 successes before 3 failures)

1. **The Approach** — Stealth DC 13 or Deception (pose as an acolyte).
2. **The Disruption** — Arcana DC 14 (spot the mathematical flaw in the summoning) or Performance DC 15 (counter-melody).
3. **The Offer** — Persuasion DC 16 to Zariel's avatar: "They offer you coin... I offer you competence. I will eat the Absolute for you." Lyra's Soul Coin as down payment grants +5.
4. **The Anchor** — CON or CHA save DC 12 to hold the redirected power (failure: 2d6 fire, thrown back).

Success turns the room's gold to ash, strips the Cassalanters' protection, and instantly grants the [[CollegeOfInfernalContracts]] subclass with a jump to Level 2/3.

## Design significance

A clean pattern for **theft-of-power climaxes**: the villain's win condition becomes the player's, resolved entirely through the character's actual competencies (social + intellect) rather than combat the level budget can't support. Consistent with the campaign line's larger arc where Nocturne co-opts rather than destroys power structures ([[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]], [[BeautyAsWeapon]]).
