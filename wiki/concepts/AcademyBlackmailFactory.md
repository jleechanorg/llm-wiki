# Academy Blackmail Factory

Campaign pattern from the [[2026-08-04-aizen-bg3-v2-exiled-viper|Exiled Viper v2 campaign]]: converting a trusted institution (the [[ArcaneAthenaeum]]) into a systematic leverage-production pipeline.

## The pipeline

1. **Access via cover identity** — pose as low-status insider (meek junior scribe) inside an under-supervised institution (post-Absolute faculty shortage).
2. **Chemical softening** — spike communal resources (teas) with Essence of Lethe + the custom narcotic "Whisperdust" (euphoric dependency "mimicking [[Mystra]]'s touch").
3. **Extraction** — confessions from noble heirs; debts enforced with the Suggestion spell.
4. **Recycling** — broken marks feed "the Garden" as fodder, spies, or puppets.
5. **Trap dressing** — a "study group" as velvet trap, with Minor Illusion + Prestidigitation faking privacy wards.

## Why it matters as a design pattern

It lets a **Level 4 mortal** ([[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild]]) project campaign-scale power without magical power-ups — power comes from institutional position and compromised people, not character level. The campaign's [[ChallengeCompensation]] counters (True Seeing, anti-charm amulets, high-WIS interrogators) exist specifically to threaten this pipeline. Expression of the player's documented serpent-mastermind preference: control through manipulation rather than force.
