# High-Persistence / Low-Drift World State

Campaign-design contract stated in the [[WorldOfAssiah]] guide: play is built on "exceptionally high persistence and very low drift." Player actions in one theater permanently alter supply lines, frontlines, and the continental strategies of NPC commanders ([[Raziel]], [[Lucifer]]); "the world remembers every burned village, every spared child, and every unleashed spell."

As an LLM-DM instruction, this is a consequence-durability directive of the same family as [[ConsequenceEnforcement]] and [[DMBookkeepingTrustGap]] mitigations: it demands the DM track ripple effects instead of resetting state between scenes, and forbids narrative drift away from established facts. Gives mechanical teeth to the [[MaskAndMonster]] pillar, since merciful and cruel choices both leave permanent records.
