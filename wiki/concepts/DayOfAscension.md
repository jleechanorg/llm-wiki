# The Day of Ascension (44 BCE)

The [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] timeline's **point of divergence** from real history: the plot to assassinate [[GaiusJuliusCaesar]] on the Ides of March fails. Guided by a [[FatedMiasma]] vision, Caesar strikes preemptively, exposing the conspiracy in the Senate. The conspirators are arrested; in the ensuing panic and popular outrage Caesar receives emergency powers and **the Republic is suspended** — the first day of the god-empire.
