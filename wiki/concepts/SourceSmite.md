# Source Smite

Custom mechanic from "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse": the gestalt protagonist may **convert Divine Smite damage to pure Necrotic or Force by spending Sorcery Points**, fusing the Paladin and [[SourceSoulSorcerer]] halves of the [[GestaltTier4Progression]] chassis into one resource economy.

## Riders
- **Veil Striker (L13)**: attacks from hiding or while invisible add +2d8 Source Smite damage ("un-stabilized power flares violently") — mechanically rewards the campaign's stealth-predator fantasy
- Delivered through Finesse weapons (dual obsidian rapiers / shadow daggers), i.e. Dexterity-carried smites
