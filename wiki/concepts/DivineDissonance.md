## Description
The core tension mechanic in the Divine Leverage System. Using True Power while wearing a Mortal Mask creates a detectable disturbance in reality's fabric. If Dissonance rises too high, the deception fractures, alerting the Pantheon to an intruder. Each Layer has a Safe Limit that can be exceeded at risk.

## Quantified V0 formulation ([[2026-08-04-god-campaign-prompt-v0-divine-deception]])

The Divine Deception V0 prompt upgrades this from per-layer Safe Limits to a single hidden **0–100% meter** the engine runs in the background.

**Triggers:** (1) *Metric Mismatch* — Rank A damage (100+) from a Layer 1 mortal form makes the Weave tremble; (2) *Probability Violation* — auto-succeeding a check mathematically impossible for the current Mask; (3) *Direct Observation* — spending True [[Divine-Power-Points|DPP]] in the presence of another Greater Deity's avatar.

**Thresholds:** 0–20% *Safe* (dismissed as strange luck / wild magic) → 21–50% *Suspicion* (passive scrying by [[Oghma]] and [[Savras]]; the player feels a "gaze") → 51–80% *Investigation* (an Inevitable or Solar is dispatched; gods may test the mask with combat) → 81–100% *Exposure* (the mask fractures; the Pantheon recognizes an unsanctioned Super-Deity).
