# Crown of Karsus (reforged)

The Netherese artifact that [[Gale-of-Waterdeep]] forged anew in the [[2026-08-04-bg3-empire-of-shadow|Empire of Shadow]] timeline, achieving "terrifying pseudo-divinity."

- Powers Gale's floating citadel (carved from Ramazith's Tower) and his experimental Netherese weaving, which mutates citizens and tears holes in reality.
- Pseudo-divinity — not full godhood — leaves an exploitable gap: Karsus's own precedent (Karsus's Folly) implies the crown's power is inherently unstable, a built-in defeat vector for parties.
- Contrast with the Aizen campaign's sanctioned ascension model ([[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]], [[Ao-the-Overgod]] oversight): Gale's power is stolen infrastructure with no divine charter, and [[Mystra]]'s response is conspicuously unstated in the v0 document.
