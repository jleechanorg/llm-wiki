## Overview
The Celestial Wars were the divine rebellion led by [Lucifer](../entities/Lucifer.md) (Morning Star) that shattered the cosmic order, creating the current multi-polar world of Assiah.

## Key Facts
- Leader: [Lucifer](../entities/Lucifer.md)
- Outcome: Shattered cosmic order, created multi-polar world
- Result: [CelestialImperium](CelestialImperium.md) vs [ShatteredHost](../entities/ShatteredHost.md) conflict
- Ended by: [TreatyOfNexus](../entities/TreatyOfNexus.md)

## Historical Context
- Led to creation of [SevenApostates](../entities/SevenApostates.md)
- Resulted in current faction landscape
- Post-war period: Year 11 of the New Peace
