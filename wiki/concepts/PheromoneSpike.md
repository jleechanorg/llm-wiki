# Pheromone Spike

Named ability referenced in the Nocturne Sosuke v6 module ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-bg3-v6-velvet-cage]]). At the Uncanny Valley stage (levels 5–9) of the [[PorcelainPerfectionProgression]], using Pheromone Spike — like spellcasting — floods [[NocturneSosuke]]'s pupils with intense pink luminescence.

The ingested portion of the source names the ability and its visual tell but does not define its rules text (action economy, save DC, effect). The name and context imply a biochemical charm/influence effect consistent with the module's [[BeautyAsWeapon]] design and with siren-type kits elsewhere in the campaign family ([[CollegeOfTheSiren]]). Mark full mechanics as pending later sections of the v6 document.
