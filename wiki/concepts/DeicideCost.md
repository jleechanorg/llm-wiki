# Deicide-Cost

The fourth pillar of the [[GodMechanicsV2Overlay]]: god-killing carries an explicit, budgeted cost rather than being a climactic freebie. This converts the God of Murder campaign's endgame — the [[DarkUrge]]'s plane-spanning war on the Faerûnian pantheon — into an **economy of escalation**: each consumed spark (starting with [[Bhaal]]'s) must be paid for, so the ascent up the [[GodMechanicsSevenRankLadder]] is a sequence of priced acquisitions, not a montage.

Design function: it is the anti-snowball brake that keeps a Quasi-Deity-to-Greater-Goddess arc playable across a whole campaign, the same role [[EntropyToll]] and [[EssenceDevourer]]-style costs play in the wiki's other ascension frameworks.
