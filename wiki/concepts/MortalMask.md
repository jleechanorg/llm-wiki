# Mortal Mask

[[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s deliberate suppression of her [[ApexBloodline]] "Aura of the Goddess" — self-luminous silver-gold hair, crystalline violet eyes — to pass as a mere mortal princess or, on the Road to Ashford, the squire "Silver." Dropping the mask is itself a weapon: **Apex Presence** terrifies enemies into submission without a drawn blade (mechanically Command / Fear / Hypnotic Pattern).

Sibling mechanic to the "Mask of Fragility" dynamic-deception DC in the Ains/Overlord campaign — the user's campaigns repeatedly build tension from a godlike being maintaining a fragile mortal cover.
