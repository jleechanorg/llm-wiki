# Sovereign/Girl Duality

The central character engine of the Visenya v4 campaign. [[VisenyaTargaryen]] is split between:

- **The Sovereign (The Apex)** — cold geometry (people as variables, peace as a structure), the ruthless "Silver Butcher," the [[SilverTongue]], and cosmic dread of the Long Night driving a unify-the-world-by-any-means agenda.
- **The Girl (The Ditchbond)** — agonizing distance from humanity, craving simple honest connection ([[Ditchbond]]) with [[SerDuncanTheTall]], and protective love expressed *through manipulation* of her own family ("the Furnace that burns so they stay warm").

The duality operationalizes the user's recurring moral-corruption theme: power that isolates, and humanity retained only through deliberately chosen ordinary bonds.
