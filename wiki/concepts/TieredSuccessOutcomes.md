# Tiered Success Outcomes

Resolution mechanic observed in the [[Voyage]] engine ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]): instead of binary pass/fail, skill checks resolve on a graduated scale — **"Basic Success", "Mixed Results", "Success", "Great Success"**.

This is the PbtA-style partial-success ladder grafted onto a 5e/BG3 chassis. Combined with [[DynamicSituationalModifiers]], it lets the engine express narrative nuance (success-with-cost, degraded outcomes) that a binary DC check flattens.

Relevant to worldarchitect.ai design: a tiered outcome scale gives the narrator more hooks for consequence generation than pass/fail, and the Voyage playtest suggests players read it as fair rather than arbitrary.
