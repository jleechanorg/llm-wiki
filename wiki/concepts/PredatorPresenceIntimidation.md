# Predator-Presence Intimidation ("The Uncanny Valley")

Narrative hook from [[NocturneSosuke]] v5 answering the question: why do hardened NPCs like [[Zevlor]] or Aradin heed a physically unimposing sixteen-year-old? Because she reads as **wrong** — "the feeling of a bird staring at a snake." The air drops ten degrees on eye contact; Intimidation rolls are whispered, with the target feeling "the weight of the Abyss pressing against their skull," rather than shouted.

## Design reading

Decouples Intimidation from STR/size and grounds it in the [[Malcanthet]] pact residue, letting a STR 8 / CON 8 character run a fear-based social game at Level 1. Sibling of [[BeautyAsWeapon]] and [[BoredGodGaze]] in this wiki's recurring toolkit of presence-based social mechanics for the serpent-mastermind archetype.
