# Essence of Lethe

A memory-hazing agent used by teen [[SosukeAizen]] in the [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]] operation at the [[ArcaneAthenaeum]]: spiked into communal teas so that marks cannot reliably reconstruct what they said or did while under [[WhisperdustNarcotic|Whisperdust]]. Named for the mythological river of forgetfulness. Its function is evidentiary erasure — the victims' own memories become unreliable, leaving Aizen's ledger as the only durable record of their compromises, an early mortal-tier instance of the [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]] pattern.
