# Victim-to-Predator Transformation

Narrative pattern named explicitly in the [[NocturneSosuke]] BG3 v3 campaign ("This scene establishes your transformation from victim to predator"): the protagonist's origin trauma is not backstory color but the mechanical and moral engine of the arc. Survival under exploitation teaches the character to weaponize others' assumptions — the abuser's contempt becomes an intelligence-extraction surface (skill challenges replacing the abuse itself, with the source document mandating Session-0 boundary discussion and offering fade-to-black handling).

Corpus significance: this is the darkest-origin variant of the recurring serpent-mastermind protagonist profile (see the Sariel, Gaia Julia, and prior Nocturne campaign lines). Where the Gilded Trap campaigns start the manipulator at full power infiltrating downward, v3 Nocturne builds the manipulator upward from powerlessness, making moral corruption a consequence of survival rather than a starting trait. The declared theme list (survival → revenge → power acquisition → moral corruption → infernal bargains) is effectively this transformation stated as a five-step sequence.
