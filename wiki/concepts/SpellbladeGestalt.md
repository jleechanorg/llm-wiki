# Spellblade Gestalt

Character-build mechanic from *Blades of the High and Low*: [[ValeriaVex]] runs Fighter and Bladesinger Wizard simultaneously as one integrated combat style, not two parallel class lists.

## Integration requirements (from the campaign bible)

- Weapon attacks create openings for [[AetherPrimordialEnergy|Aether]] techniques.
- Defensive reactions enable stronger ripostes or repositioning.
- Concentration and action economy force real choices.
- Aether escalation may reveal more power than intended (hooks into [[WitnessedRevealMechanic]]).
- Mundane mastery lets her win while preserving the squire disguise.

## Balancing philosophy

The GM must NOT arbitrarily reduce the gestalt to an ordinary level-6 character. Challenge comes from opponents with specialized preparation, difficult objectives, terrain, time pressure, protection choices, and counterplay — stronger opponents should challenge a specific habit, stance, resource, or objective, never secretly inflate their numbers. This parallels the guardian-ceiling balancing in the Ains Overlord module and contrasts with raw-stat supremacy designs like [[TiberiusAethelgard]]'s L20 apex bloodline.
