# The Muse

The core compulsion mechanic of the Gaia Julia v5 campaign, mechanizing the character's addiction to emotional subversion (the [[SymphonyOfRuin]] drive):

- **Discord (failure penalty)**: being forced into crude violence — open brawling, shouting, getting hit — inflicts **psychic damage equal to character level**. Violence "offends her artistry."
- **Harmony (success bonus)**: successfully charming/dominating a target or defusing a combat encounter through words or acting grants a **d12 Inspiration die** usable on AC or saving throws.

## Design lineage

The Muse belongs to the same design family as v3's [[SanctuaryImperative]] (psychic strain tracking moral weight) but inverts the valence: where the Sanctuary Imperative punishes moral transgression, the Muse punishes *inelegance* — an aesthetic constraint, not an ethical one. Both use psychic damage as the enforcement currency, making personality mechanically binding rather than roleplay-optional. Pairs with [[VelvetIron]] as the reward-side enabler of the social-first playstyle.
