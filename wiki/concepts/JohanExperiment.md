# The Johan Experiment

The hidden compulsion driving [[NocturneSilentBlade|Nocturne]] in the Silent Blade campaign, named for Johan Liebert of *Monster*: a standing experiment to prove that **"civilization is a lie."**

## Protocol

1. **Select a paragon** — the pillar of the community: faithful priest, loyal soldier, loving husband.
2. **Apply pressure** — seduce or tempt them into betraying their defining code (the Paladin's oath, the merchant's loyalty to friends).
3. **Judge** — when they fail the test ("and they always fail"), execute them as punishment.

## Design role

This converts the campaign from a mission-of-the-week mercenary structure into a serial *moral vivisection* engine: every NPC of virtue is a latent test subject, and the DM's example prompt (a merchant trading codes for a promised "reward") shows the test mid-execution. Corruption here is proof-seeking, not profit-seeking — distinguishing it from the transactional corruption of [[CollegeOfPerdition]]-style mechanics and the empire-scale moral engineering of the [[DecadentConqueror]] line.
