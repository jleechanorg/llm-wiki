# Telepathic Banite Conversion

The specific application of [[Telepathic-Domain-Usurpation]] analyzed in entries 901-1147 of the [[AizenSosuke]] god-campaign: mass-persuading **all former [[Bane]] followers in Baldur's Gate** to serve Aizen, justified by the player as domain entitlement ("It's my domain, should be allowed") and ruled permissible by the DM under Absolute Codification / Domain Influence.

## The DM's pro/con ledger

**Pros**
- Divine Save DC 100 → unresistable, guaranteed loyalty
- Simultaneous city-wide imposition in one action
- Absorbs cultist knowledge, hidden caches, and existing networks intact
- Avoids overt conflict with the [[FlamingFist]]
- Directly usurps Bane's tyranny/control domain remnants ([[DivineAscensionByConsumption]])

**Cons / costs**
- **50 [[Divine-Power-Points|DPP]] per wave** of targets (Major Domain Manipulation pricing)
- Divine detection risk from [[Mystra]], [[Shar]], and [[Ao]] ([[DivineDetectionTiers]])
- Behavioral tells: converts must not act so perfectly coordinated that mundane observers infer divine control

## Significance

A clean example of the campaign's late-stage design pattern: raw success is guaranteed, so the DM relocates all tension into **resource metering and concealment risk**. The segment ends mid-sentence with the player pivoting to an alternative strategy ("How about this strategy. Neg..."), leaving the decision unresolved at the export boundary.
