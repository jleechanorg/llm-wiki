# Sovereign Shadow Tracker (Dignitas & Leverage)

Custom subsystem from campaign visenya v8 that turns [[VisenyaBelaerys]]'s clandestine influence over Planetos into a single tracked number:

`Sovereign Power (SP) = (Active Spies × 2) + (Blackmail Caches × 10) + (Subverted Officers × 15) − (Public Disclosures × 20)`

## Tiers

1. **The Ghost in the Ledger (0–35)** — localized whisper network; +2 Stealth/Deception; treated as anonymous merchant asset.
2. **The Unseen Thumb (36–75)** — controls secondary port-tariffs; intercepts Black/Green council communications; +3 passive Insight; retinue actions at half gold cost.
3. **The Sovereign Eclipse (76–99)** — owns local bureaucracy; can forge execution warrants, redirect supply trains, or move Gold Cloak cohorts on DC 15 Charisma (Deception).
4. **The Terminal Administrator (100)** — "Absolute reality lock"; DC to subvert any mortal lord drops to baseline 5.

## Design notes

- The weightings encode a doctrine: a subverted officer (15) outvalues seven spies, and one public disclosure (−20) erases a subverted officer plus a spy ring — secrecy is the scarcest asset.
- The status bar labels (0 SP "Outcast" → 100 SP "Admin") make the endgame explicitly administrative, matching the Visenya line's INTJ serpent-mastermind fantasy of ruling systems rather than thrones.
- Sibling mechanics elsewhere in the corpus: [[SirensLeverage]] (leverage-as-currency), [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]] (industrialized blackmail-cache production), and the [[AscensionMeter]] family of hidden-progress trackers.
