# Century of Silence

Era framing from [[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]]: the campaign opens **+100 years post-Ascension**, in the century following "the Sovereign's Vanishing" — the sealing of [[NocturneSosuke]] by [[Ao]]'s [[GeometricFirewall]].

- Official history is a **manufactured lie**: the world believes heroes solved the Crisis of the Absolute; the truth (a hostile takeover by a sixteen-year-old) is suppressed — an instance of the [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]] pattern applied at civilization scale.
- Nocturne's Material-Plane empire collapsed instantly on sealing; [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]] was hunted to extinction, producing the exile protagonist [[SosukeAizen]].
- The timeskip is a **campaign-reset device**: it clears the board of the overpowered prior protagonist while keeping her reachable as patron and lore — a sibling technique to [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart]] and [[PrequelOriginCampaign]], but running the clock forward instead of back.
