# Mundane Illusion Disguise

Non-magical concealment technique from the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign: deliberately applied grime, matted hair, ill-fitting leathers, and a sullen withdrawn demeanor used to make a conspicuous person forgettable — "a ghost in plain sight." The term "mundane illusion" marks the design point: the effect of an illusion spell achieved with zero magical signature, so it cannot be dispelled or detected.

Structural role: the outermost layer of the [[SerpentKingArchetype]] fortress, beneath which sit the two social masks ([[DataProbeSocialEngineering]]). Corpus parallel: [[MaskOfFragility]]-style dynamic deception in the shy-lich campaign and the layer system of [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] — the Nocturne line consistently makes *concealment of true capability* a first-class mechanic rather than backstory.
