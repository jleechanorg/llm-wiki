# Valyrian Features as Status

A narrative device where a character's true-Valyrian appearance (silver-gold hair, purple eyes, "very strong royal features") functions as an unforgeable public credential of dragonlord blood — one that can contradict their formal social rank.

## In campaign visenya v7

[[VisenyaWaters|Visenya]] is a bastard, but she "stands out" because she looks *more* royal than many trueborn Targaryens. Every observer who sees her face is forced to acknowledge the blood of [[DaemonTargaryen]] and her Targaryen mother before any herald reads her status. Combined with her claimed dragon ([[DragonClaiming]]), appearance and capability outvote paperwork.

## Relation to existing concepts

This is a legitimacy-flavored sibling of [[BeautyAsWeapon]] (documented across the user's campaign corpus): where BeautyAsWeapon uses appearance as a *manipulation tool*, ValyrianFeaturesAsStatus uses it as a *credential* — passive, always-on, and politically destabilizing because it invites comparisons a bastard is not supposed to invite.
