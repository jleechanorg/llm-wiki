# Gestalt Tier System

System architecture declared by "[[2026-08-04-bumpkin-swordsman-rust-bound-blade|The Rust-Bound Blade]]": **Gestalt Tier-2 / Tier-3 Sandbox Integration**. Gestalt characters advance in two classes simultaneously, so a "Level 12 Gestalt" ([[MasterVaelen]]'s locked output) is meaningfully stronger than a Level 12 single-class character — the source's Perception Paradox diagram explicitly contrasts "Level 20 Mythic Gestalt" against the "Level 12 Single-Class" global mortal peak.

Within the [[Level12MortalCeiling]] framework, the Gestalt distinction lets the campaign express fine-grained power gaps at the top of the mortal band without breaking the hard cap, supporting the grounded martial-arts-noir tone where small mechanical edges decide duels between elite combatants.
