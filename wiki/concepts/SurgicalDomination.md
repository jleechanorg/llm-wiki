# Surgical Domination

Core drive from the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign: satisfaction located in "the singular, perfect moment of absolute control" rather than sustained rulership — "the difference between a queen who rules a city and a sorceress who can stop every timepiece in that city with a single thought." A whispered Bardic suggestion that makes a hero kneel for one heartbeat is valued above a year on a throne.

Mechanical hook: Bardic magic (suggestion/compulsion effects of the [[CollegeOfSwords]] bard chassis) is the delivery vehicle, making this a build-defining drive rather than pure flavor. Pairs with [[PortableAbsolutePower]] (why thrones are rejected) and is exercised through [[DataProbeSocialEngineering]] (how targets are mapped before the strike).
