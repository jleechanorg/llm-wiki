## Overview
Bladesinger is a wizard subclass in D&D 5e that combines arcane magic with martial combat, specializing in swordplay while maintaining spellcasting abilities.

## Aizen Sosuke's Build
- **Level**: 6
- **Primary Stat**: Intelligence 18 (+4)
- **Hit Points**: 42/42
- **Armor Class**: 16 (19 during Bladesong)
- **Speed**: 30 ft. (+10 ft. during Bladesong)

## Class Features
- **Bladesong**: Coda of the Scalpel — magical enhancement of combat prowess
- **Extra Attack**: Recursive Strike
- **Weapon Proficiencies**: Light armor, simple weapons, rapiers
- **Skills**: Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Perception, Persuasion

## Feats
- War Caster
- Bladesinger Training (Extra Attack, Bladesong)
