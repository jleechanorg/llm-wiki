# Faction Structural Profile

Sub-Template B of [[CampaignBibleTemplateV4]]: the mandatory format for every major faction or organization.

1. **Nomenclature and Heraldry** — official name, common alias, sensory visual signature (colors, sigils, architecture, uniforms)
2. **Infrastructure and Domain** — HQ (name, location, vibe), asset/manpower bracket as a broad scale, primary resource engine
3. **Leadership Dossier** — the current leader gets a full [[StandardCharacterArchitecture]] profile
4. **Internal Operational Culture** — unwritten codes, internal power struggles, and the fate of members who fail or betray
5. **The Hook** — the precise structural vulnerability, historical scandal, or leader trauma the protagonist can exploit to dismantle or control the faction

## Why it matters

Item 5 is the load-bearing design choice: every faction ships with a documented attack surface, hard-coding factions as objects of protagonist leverage rather than neutral scenery. Combined with the leader's six-tier profile (fears, secrets, unconscious beliefs), the template guarantees a manipulation route into any organization — visible in practice in factions like [[ShatteredRose]], [[HouseVitiate]], and [[CelestialImperium]] across the ingested campaign bibles.
