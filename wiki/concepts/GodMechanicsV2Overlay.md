# God Mechanics V2 Overlay

The 2026-07-21 extension (`god-mechanics-v2-general.md` in [[PR8488]]) layered on the [[GodMechanicsSevenRankLadder]]. Explicitly **setting-agnostic** — the spec calls out translatability to Cyberpunk, Wuxia, Marvel, Naruto, and homebrew pantheons. Four additions:

1. **Hidden bands** — divinity tiers carry hidden information rather than open stat blocks, preserving mystery about how strong a rival god actually is.
2. **Auto-win ladder** — at sufficient rank gap, contests resolve automatically without dice; the mechanical twin of the narrative [[SubmissiveDeath]] doctrine.
3. **Per-dawn menu** — divine actions are budgeted as a daily menu, turning godhood into a resource-allocation loop instead of free-form omnipotence.
4. **[[DeicideCost]]** — killing a god has an explicit price, making pantheon war an economy rather than a boss rush.

A companion **Nocturne V2** spec binds the overlay to D&D 5e Faerûn with Nocturne-specific tier structure and multipliers, linking this framework to the [[NocturneSosuke]] campaign lineage.
