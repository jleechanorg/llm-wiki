# The Sovereign Ledger

ASCII stat-block tracker used in "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse" to summarize the [[GestaltTier4Progression]] chassis at a glance:

- Class chassis: Gestalt Paladin (Conquest) / Sorcerer (Source Soul)
- Starting level 12 ("Adjusting to the Torrent of Divinity"), max level 30 ("The God-Slayer Apotheosis")
- Primary stats: CHA 20 (+5), DEX 18 (+4)
- Spell access: Sorcerer + Paladin + Cleric lists, Charisma-based

Belongs to the same family of custom in-fiction trackers as v8's Sovereign Shadow Tracker and the [[AscensionMeter]] — this player's campaigns consistently externalize power state into named ledgers/meters.
