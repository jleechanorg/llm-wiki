# Rite of Profane Ascension

The Cazador-designed vampiric ritual that [[Astarion]] completed in the [[2026-08-04-bg3-empire-of-shadow|Empire of Shadow]] timeline, transforming him from spawn into the Vampire Ascendant.

- Mechanical consequence in this campaign: daylight-immune vampire lord with a spawn army large enough to replace [[BaldursGate]]'s Parliament of Peers.
- Narrative consequence: the single divergence that anchors the whole [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] — Astarion ascended is the seed tyrant around whom [[Minthara]] and the city's underworld consolidated.
- Thematically parallel to the divine-ascension mechanics in the Aizen campaign ([[Divine-Power-Points]]): a ritual shortcut that converts a mortal companion into a strategic-scale power.
