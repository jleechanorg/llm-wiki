# Pax Caesara

The setting of *The Iron Eagle*: a divergent timeline where [[JuliusCaesar]] survived the Ides of March, the Republic became a monolithic autocratic Empire branded "Perpetual Dictatorship," and by 40 BCE the crushed civil wars have produced a peace described as "a suffocating weight."

Key properties:
- **Gilded oppression**: order and beauty as state aesthetics; rebellion framed as ugliness.
- **Dynastic succession war**: the God-Emperor's sons fight a cold war for the throne rather than open civil war.
- **Cursus Honorum as ladder**: the traditional Roman career path persists but is gamed by ambition rather than seniority.

Continues the wiki's Roman-politics campaign lineage ([[FactionPoliticsRome]]) and the broader pattern of dark-authority settings ([[CelestialImperium]], [[CenturyOfSilence]]).
