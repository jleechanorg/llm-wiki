# March of Broken Chains (Year 1412)

Early [[CenturyOfSchism]] campaign in which [[Lucifer]], needing mass infantry to break [[Raziel]]'s lines, shattered the [[CelestialImperium]]'s slave colonies (established under the [[EdictOfChains]]) and absorbed the freed mortal races into his Host.

Dual-natured by design: genuine emancipation of a millennium-enslaved population *and* a cold manpower calculation — the setting's "Mathematics of Slaughter" pillar applied to liberation itself. The ingested Part 1 excerpt truncates mid-description of this event.
