## Overview
The Harvest is Emperor [[Vitiate]]'s endgame agenda in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]): a ritual to consume the galaxy, for which he needs his children — the [[GoldenLineage]] — to first break the galaxy's will. It extends SWTOR canon's Ziost-style world-consumption to a galactic scale and doubles as the family's internal doom: Vitiate plans to consume all but the strongest of his own children.

## Narrative function
- Gives every family member's domain (military, diplomacy, alchemy, hunting, justice) a shared dark purpose
- Motivates [[TheEclipsed]], the one faction that *knows* the plan
- Creates the PC's core dilemma: serve the Harvest, subvert it, or (as in the played [[NocturneOldRepublic]] campaign) consume the consumer
