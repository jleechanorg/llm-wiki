# Dragon Lord (Apex Bloodline) Sorcerer Subclass

Homebrew class chassis for [[VisenyaBelaerys]] in *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]). Explicitly a **refactor** of an earlier campaign version into a Charisma-based Sorcerer: magic as bloodright ("the raw, terrifying heat of the Fourteen Flames demanding obedience") rather than learned skill.

## Components

- **Base**: Sorcerer; primary stat Charisma 18.
- **[[SubtleApotheosis]]**: free Subtle Spell Metamagic, zero-cost on mind-influence and dragon-affecting spells.
- **[[NoblestDialect]]**: Advantage on CHA checks in High Valyrian vs Valyrian descendants and dragons.
- **[[AscensionMeter]]**: 0–100 threat gauge pricing overt power use.

## Design Note

The subclass mechanizes the campaign's social premise: every feature either *hides* power (Subtle Apotheosis), *converts* power to social currency (Noblest Dialect), or *prices* revealing it (Ascension Meter). Compare the D&D-adaptation-of-everything pattern across the user's campaign bibles.
