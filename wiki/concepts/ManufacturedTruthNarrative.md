# Manufactured Truth Narrative

Mechanic where a hidden director fabricates a public account of cosmic events and installs it as consensus reality. In the god-campaign summary, [[AizenSosuke]] commands the newly elevated [[GaleOfWaterdeep]] to broadcast across Faerûn that Gale defeated and consumed [[Mystra]] — a claim the source presents as scripted propaganda, not observed fact (the second commanded declaration is truncated in the source).

## Properties

- The narrative's *broadcaster* is genuinely powerful (Gale really is a Greater Deity), which launders the lie — audiences verify the messenger's divinity, not the claim.
- Serves [[The-Grand-Game]]: manufactured history is the raw material of the director's restarted conflict.
- Wiki-level caveat: pages about affected entities (e.g. [[Mystra]]) must record the claim as in-fiction propaganda, not as world-state.
