## Overview
Project Apotheosis is the central premise of the Nocturne Old Republic campaign line. The House of the Dragon bible defines it at origin: a **eugenics and Force-breeding program** initiated by Emperor [[Vitiate]] to create superior biological vessels — his children, the [[GoldenLineage]] — because he grew tired of the Dark Council's infighting. In the played campaign, it becomes the elevation arc by which [[NocturneOldRepublic|Nocturne]] transcends via essence consumption.

## Goals
- Breed vessels superior to the Old Guard Sith, replacing the Dark Council meritocracy with inherited demigods
- Strengthen and purify the Vitiate bloodline ([[GoldenLineage]])
- Prepare instruments to break the galaxy's will ahead of [[TheHarvest]]

## Methods
- **Force-breeding / eugenics:** engineered children as "vessels"
- **Essence Consumption:** the played campaign's [[EssenceDevourer]] mechanic — consuming defeated Force users to unlock power tiers
- **Culling:** Vitiate plans to consume all but the strongest of his children

## Connections

- [[HouseVitiate]] — Project lead; [[Valtoria]] — replication research on Nocturne's marrow
