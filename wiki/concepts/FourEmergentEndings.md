# Four Emergent Endings

The structural promise on campaign visenya v9's title card: **"Four emergent endings. One system. Player chooses the resolution."**

Rather than a linear plot, the campaign commits up front to (a) a bounded ending space of four outcomes, (b) a single unified mechanical system driving all of them (the [[ApexStalker]] chassis + [[StressLineSight]] tier ladder), and (c) explicit player authority over which resolution fires — endings *emerge* from play but are *chosen*, not stumbled into.

This is a design evolution within the Visenya line: v5/v6 encode tension through constraint systems (Sanctuary-style trackers, power-disparity dice) while v9 encodes it through a declared resolution contract, trading GM-side surprise for player-side strategic planning toward a known ending space. The excerpted sections name the contract but do not enumerate the four endings; the tier ladder's Demi-God gate ("the First Song's prison door opens") is the likely hinge for at least one of them.
