# The Sovereign Voice (class)

[[GaiaJuliaCaesar]]'s class in [[2026-08-04-gaia-julia-v7-pax-caesara-golden-cage|Gaia Julia v7]]: a **College of Eloquence Bard** stripped of all performer flavor. Power comes from the quickened blood of Venus Genetrix; her "art" is flawless psychological manipulation and the imposition of divine will on reality — no song, no lute.

## Signature mechanic

**[[Auctoritas]] (The Weight of the Divine)** — spells have no visible verbal or somatic components, manifesting instead as pheromonal shifts, crushing atmospheric pressure, and perfectly pitched tones that bypass the conscious mind. Functionally an always-on Subtle Spell, directly parallel to v4's [[VelvetIron]] and mechanically sibling to the v3 [[ImperatorClass]]'s Auctoritas casting.

## Progression shape (Levels 1-30)

Tiers: **Prodigy** (1-5) → **Manipulator** (6-10) → **Demagogue** (11+; document truncates at level 14). Load-bearing picks:

- L1: Divine Boon *Find Greater Steed* (Celestial Aquila) — the imperial eagle as literal mount.
- L3: *Silver Tongue* (minimum 10 on Persuasion/Deception) + Expertise in both — floors social rolls so the fantasy is never punctured by a bad die.
- L10 Magical Secrets: **Wall of Force + Modify Memory**; L11: **Mass Suggestion** — containment plus memory-editing plus crowd control, the mechanical skeleton of the campaign title's "Golden Cage."

Part of the campaign line's pattern of building bespoke 30-level social-domination chassis per version: [[CollegeOfTheSiren]] (v4), [[ImperatorClass]] (v3), Sovereign Voice (v7).
