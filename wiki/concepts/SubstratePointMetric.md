# Substrate Point (SP) Metric

The unified scale/resource system of the [[PanSubstrate]]: **1 SP = 1,000 universes**, and SP triple-functions as territory measure, spendable currency (fueling "Substrate Rewriting" and [[LogicSiege]] expenditure), and the PC's multiversal HP (Logic Rot deals SP damage). Geographic tiers run Neighborhood (0.01 SP) → District (1) → City (100) → Province (1,000) → Nation (10,000) → Continent (100,000). The paired stat is **God Power (GP)** — the PC's Intelligence attribute permanently shunted into a check modifier — so the economy is GP-for-checks, SP-for-fuel-and-life. Starting SP converts the PC's existing universe count at 1,000:1, defaulting to 1 SP.

**Design significance**: collapsing territory, wealth, and hit points into one number makes every expenditure an existential wager — spending to win a siege literally spends your life total.
