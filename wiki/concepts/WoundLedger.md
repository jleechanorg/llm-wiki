## Summary

The **Wound Ledger** is Visenya v9's central mechanical and narrative object. A red-leather-bound journal in which she records the name, manner, and date of every person she has personally killed. The ledger replaces v6's Entropy Toll: where Entropy Toll made her suffer Exhaustion from *boredom* if she didn't manipulate, the Wound Ledger is the **virtue-into-vice inversion** — every kill *is* a sin, recorded in a literal book.

## Mechanics

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Visenya personally kills a sentient creature | She must enter the name in the ledger before sunrise, or suffer 1 Exhaustion |
| Each new month, 1d20 roll | 1–4: Bleed Trigger (DC 14 WIS save or 1 Exhaustion; success = Temp HP equal to WIS mod) |
| Ledger fills a full page (10 names) | Visenya gains *one* permanent Inspiration die (the weight settles) |
| Ledger is destroyed or stolen | Visenya loses all class features that require *filing* — including Stress-Line Sight — until she recreates it. She will *kill* to recover it. |

## Why This Replaces the Entropy Toll

- v6 Entropy Toll: suffer Exhaustion from *boredom* when she *doesn't* manipulate. Healing: Temp HP equal to CHA mod when she shatters an ego. → *External*: the cost is inflicted by the world.
- v9 Wound Ledger: suffer Exhaustion from *remembering* when the names "rise." Healing: Temp HP equal to WIS mod when the ledger's weight settles. → *Internal*: the cost is inflicted by herself.

The pivot is intentional. v9's apex is not a godling puppeteer healing off other people's ego-shattering. She is a **godling executioner** healing off her own remembered dead. The ledger makes her a *sympathetic* monster — the math turns against her as the campaign progresses.

## The Mechanical Endgame

By Level 11+ the ledger's bleeds become punishing (1d20 roll, 1–4 chance of Exhaustion per month; by Level 15 the math turns: *every kill costs more than it gains*). By Level 20, the Wound Ledger is the **mechanical pressure that forces the endgame**: she must either retire the blade, lose the ledger to an enemy, or be destroyed by the weight of her own record. The v9 endgame is *the ledger decides*.

## What the Ledger Is Not

- **Not magical.** Leather, paper, ink. It can be stolen, burned, dropped in a river. *It does not protect itself.* (This makes it vulnerable — and therefore dramatic.)
- **Not a resurrection anchor.** Killing an NPC whose name is in the ledger does not bring them back.
- **Not a moral authority.** The ledger is not a *judgment* — it is a *record*. The moral weight is the campaign's; the ledger is just the book she writes in.
