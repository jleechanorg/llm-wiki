# Four Layers of Existence

The identity-stack mechanic of the Divine Deception V0 prompt ([[2026-08-04-god-campaign-prompt-v0-divine-deception]]): the Rank A player projects one of four nested personas, each with its own public stat block, constraint, and detection risk. Switching layers is a **free action with narrative consequences**.

| Layer | Persona | Public stats | Constraint | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mortal Shell | Class HP/AC/slots | Lethal damage negated by spending True DPP on probability ("the arrow hit a coin in my pocket") | High — any divine power here spikes [[DivineDissonance]] |
| 2 | False Deity (Rank Y) | Lesser/Intermediate deity | Power must match the fabricated persona's portfolio "Brand" | Moderate |
| 3 | Primary Mask (Rank Z) | Constrained Greater Deity | Must act within this identity's divine compacts | Low — but exceeding the "Transcendent Template" alerts [[Ao]] |
| 4 | Hidden Truth (Rank A) | Never shown unless the player "Drops the Mask" | Exceeds Transcendent Greater Deities | Ao quietly sanctions it while it serves "Absolute Codification" |

## Evolution of the pattern

This is the four-layer successor to the three-layer [[DivineDeceptionProtocol]] (Mask / Persona / Source) from the divine-ascension-ceremony source — the V0 upgrade splits the god-facing identity into a *fabricated* god (Layer 2) and an *officially seated* god (Layer 3), so the player can burn a disposable persona without exposing their seat at the Divine Table. It is the most elaborate instance yet of [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]], moving the dual-state tracking from mortal level-masking to the divine bracket. Quantified stat rows per layer live in the [[QuantifiedDivineReferenceTable]].
