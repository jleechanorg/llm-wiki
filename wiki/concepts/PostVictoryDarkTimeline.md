# Post-Victory Dark Timeline

Campaign-design pattern where the canonical victory (the Netherbrain's defeat) is kept, but every post-victory choice resolves to its darkest option, producing a fixed grim world state as the starting point for new play.

## Characteristics (as used in [[2026-08-04-bg3-empire-of-shadow|Empire of Shadow]])

- **Victory without liberation**: the threat dies but "the city was merely reorganized its chains."
- **Definitive fates**: every legacy character gets one closed, mostly tragic outcome (petrified [[Minsc]], spawn-raised [[Jaheira]], Avernus-trapped [[Wyll]]), which prevents continuity ambiguity and gives new parties clear stakes.
- **Villain-protagonist gravity**: heroes-turned-tyrants ([[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]]) rather than external invaders.
- Sibling pattern to [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] (Aizen campaign): both reuse a known cast/setting, but this pattern *fixes* a dark end-state instead of resetting to a clean one.
