# Abyss Knight

Custom character class from [[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]]. The bible states [[SosukeAizen]] is "not a Warlock in the traditional sense" but "a special Abyss knight class."

- **Differentiator**: the power source is a living ancestral patron ([[NocturneSosuke]]) courted through the [[ObsidianMirrorPact]], not a contract with a soul-collecting devil.
- **Flavor**: martial "knight" framing plus Abyssal channel — power flows from the sealed [[ShatteredRose]] through the bearer as Material Anchor.
- v1 gives no stat block or level table; mechanics are so far defined narratively only. Joins the campaign family's tradition of bespoke builds ([[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild]], [[PowerlessMastermindBuild]]).
