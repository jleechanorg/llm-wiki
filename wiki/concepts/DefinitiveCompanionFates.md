# Definitive Companion Fates

A campaign-setup technique observed in the *Empire of Shadow* aftermath document: **every major figure from the prior crisis is assigned a single definitive, often tragic, endpoint** before play begins — no ambiguity, no "whereabouts unknown". Rulers get statted builds and named gear ([[Astarion]], [[Shadowheart]], [[GaleOfWaterdeep]], [[Minthara]]); the rest get sealed fates: exile ([[Laezel]]), damnation ([[Wyll]]), hidden resistance ([[Karlach]]), corruption ([[Halsin]]), undeath-as-trophy ([[Jaheira]]), petrification ([[Minsc]]), withdrawal ([[Withers]]), and deferred return ([[Raphael]]).

## Why it works as design

- **Closes the sequel-hook loophole**: the world state is load-bearing, not a menu of unresolved threads.
- **Turns beloved NPCs into terrain**: each fate is simultaneously lore, a faction, and an adventure site (Szarr Palace, House of Grief, the sewers, Cloakwood).
- **Signals tone contract**: definitive tragic fates tell players up front this is a [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] survival campaign, not a heroic continuation.

Related pattern: the [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] used in the Aizen lineage resets state while keeping cast; this technique instead *freezes* the cast into fates to build a new status quo.
