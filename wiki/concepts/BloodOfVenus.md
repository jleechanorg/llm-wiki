# Blood of Venus

Section of the v6 Iron Eagle personality bible defining [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]]'s physical perfection as a manifestation of the Julian line's mythic descent from Venus. The character is "a biological anomaly — a creature of gold and light": spun-electrum hair, porcelain skin with inner bioluminescence, liquid-amber eyes; "Statues of Apollo look crude and unfinished when you stand next to them."

## Mechanical/narrative framing

- **Armor, not vanity**: veterans hesitate to strike ("it feels like vandalism"); matrons and rivals forgive cruelties because the face implies divine justification. A concrete instance of the recurring [[BeautyAsWeapon]] pattern across this player's campaigns.
- **Auctoritas as Heat**: presence manifests thermally — a happy mood makes the room "a summer day"; anger is "the door of a furnace."
- **Propaganda function**: living proof "that the Julians are not quite human" — beauty as dynastic legitimacy.
