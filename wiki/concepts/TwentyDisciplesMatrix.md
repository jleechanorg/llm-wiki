# Twenty Disciples Matrix

Faction scaffold of "[[2026-08-04-bumpkin-swordsman-rust-bound-blade|The Rust-Bound Blade]]": over twenty years, hundreds of students entered [[MasterVaelen]]'s rustic Western Kingdom dojo, but because he calibrates the "beginner" curriculum to his own broken Level 20 baseline ([[VaelenCalibrationCrisis]]), 99% wash out — locals treat the dojo as an eccentric, brutally demanding gamble.

Exactly **twenty students** possessed the rare biology and psychological stubbornness to graduate. They split into distinct ideological and mechanical networks across the continent:

- **The Good Circle (10)**: the publicly recognized premier martial elite of the Western Kingdom; their fame sustains the dojo's recruit stream. (Source truncates mid-description — individual rosters not captured.)
- Remaining graduates (~10) belong to other, presumably darker or neutral, networks not preserved in the truncated source.

The matrix converts a hermit-mentor backstory into a geopolitical web: every major power bloc plausibly contains a Vaelen graduate near the [[Level12MortalCeiling]], all tracing lineage to one hidden anomaly.
