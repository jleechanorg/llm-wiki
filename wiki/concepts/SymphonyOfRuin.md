# Symphony of Ruin

The stated core compulsion of [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] in the v5 campaign: an addiction to **emotional subversion** — making enemies trust and love her before destroying them, because "simply killing them is boring; breaking their soul is art." Mechanically enforced by [[TheMuse]] (violence literally damages her; social victories empower her).

## Structure of the pattern

1. **Mask selection** — present the persona the target's psychology demands (damsel for the protector [[Spartacus]], victim-witness framing to isolate [[Crixus]]).
2. **Trust construction** — scripted vulnerability and confession to build a paternal or protective bond.
3. **Proxy action** — sabotage executed through [[TheLotusTen]] or unwitnessed castings ([[VelvetIron]]), never attributable to her.
4. **Engineered betrayal** — the target's own code or comrades become the destruction mechanism ("have his best friend stab him for me").

Sits in the same conceptual family as [[BeautyAsWeapon]] and the [[DecadentConqueror]] archetype from the v6 bible; the campaign's inverted-hostage premise makes this pattern the primary game loop rather than villain background color.
