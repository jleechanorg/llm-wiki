# Prequel Origin Campaign

Campaign-design pattern instantiated by *The Architect of Destiny* ([[2026-08-04-aizen-teen-campaign-export]]): take a character whose endgame is already established in prior campaigns (God-King [[SosukeAizen|Aizen]]) and start a **fresh campaign** at the beginning of their life, exploring "the factors that shape" the known destination.

## Structural moves in the Aizen instance

1. **Explicit world-state reset** — the export enumerates what has *not yet* happened: no ascension, Bane unconsumed (see [[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]]), Cult of the Absolute distant/inactive. This is a targeted form of [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart]].
2. **Downscaled character sheet** — Level 1, standard array, mundane equipment; the adult's powers (e.g. [[KyokaSuigetsu]]) exist only as thematic class suggestions (Illusion wizard, Whispers bard, Mastermind rogue).
3. **Psychology ported in 'nascent' form** — the adult personality document is restated with every trait marked developing/unconscious, giving the player a destination to grow toward rather than a state to play.
4. **Aging mechanic** — [[AgeProgressionByLevel]] binds the formative-years window to levels 1–10.

## Tension inherent to the pattern

Because the endpoint is fixed, dramatic stakes shift from *whether* the character becomes the mastermind to *how and at what cost* — the export's stated themes ("awakening of ambition," "corrupting influence of forbidden knowledge," "subtle lines between good and evil") all target the how. Continuity risk is the flip side: successor documents like [[2026-08-04-aizen-bg3-v2-exiled-viper-level4]] have already drifted from this one on age, class, and social position.
