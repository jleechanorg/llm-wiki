# Sentient Scourge

The worldbuilding premise of [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]]: every Scourge unit — ghoul, abomination, crypt fiend, necromancer, lich, dreadlord, banshee — possesses will, hierarchy, ambition, politics, factional loyalty, and private grievances.

Consequences:

- Necropolises are **cities**, not spawn buildings.
- The [[CultOfTheDamned]] is a mercenary priesthood, not a fanatic death cult.
- The [[LichKing]] is a coalition-propped tyrant, not a god.
- The Scourge becomes a negotiable political landscape — which is what makes [[NocturneRavencrest]]'s shadow stack (and ultimately the [[PaleCrown]]) possible: you can run an intelligence service and build a parallel state inside a faction that has politics.
