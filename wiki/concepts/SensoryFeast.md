# The Sensory Feast

Core compulsion defined in the v6 Iron Eagle personality bible for [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]]: the character is bored by dry reports and silent rooms and **craves Intensity** — e.g. wanting to *feel* the weight of a Legion marching at his command. The universe is framed as "a banquet table set specifically for you"; other people are either invited guests or courses to be consumed (the "Devouring Maw" self-image under the [[Miasma]]).

This compulsion is the motivational engine of the [[DecadentConqueror]] archetype — the character climbs the Cursus Honorum "not because it is logical, but because the view from the top is the only one that satisfies." It gives the GM a lever: sensory-rich stakes hook the character; abstract or bureaucratic stakes bore him into risk-taking.
