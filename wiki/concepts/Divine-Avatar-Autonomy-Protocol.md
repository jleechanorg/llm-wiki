# Divine Avatar Autonomy Protocol

Governance contract from the [[Aizen]] god campaign defining how a god's mortal-realm avatar operates without moment-to-moment direction. The avatar is a "perfect instrument" with Level 20 intellect that autonomously handles missions, adapts to unforeseen circumstances, and exploits opportunities.

## The two-tier contract

1. **Escalation tier — important decisions**: The avatar must telepathically confer with the divine consciousness before acts that significantly alter the strategic landscape, carry high risk, or spend substantial resources — initiating major conflicts, deposing high-ranking rulers, handling powerful artifacts, or directly confronting factions like the [[Flaming Fist]] or Knights of the Shield.
2. **Autonomy tier — everything else**: The avatar exercises its own judgment, and proactively reports at least daily, with more frequent reports for critical developments, major successes, or complications.

## Notable pattern

The protocol is structurally an **agent-delegation contract**: bounded autonomy, defined escalation triggers, and unprompted heartbeat reporting — the same shape as human-in-the-loop AI agent oversight. It preserves the player's ultimate control while offloading routine play, complementing the campaign's other player-authority mechanics like [[PlayerDirectedExpGrant]] and [[DestinyRulebookOverrides]].
