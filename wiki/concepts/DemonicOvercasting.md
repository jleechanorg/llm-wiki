# Demonic Overcasting

[[Nocticula]]'s bloodline trait: her Abyssal royal descent (from the [[AbyssalDemonKing]]) grants unusually efficient access to the Weave, letting her consistently wield spells and spell slots **two tiers higher** than her apparent level.

Combined with [[ManaVeilMastery]] this creates the campaign's core asymmetry — an opponent who scouts her sees a novice and meets an archmage. The balancing cost is externalized to [[TheRevealConstraint]] rather than to spell failure or resource drain, keeping the mechanic narrative rather than attritional.
