# Gestalt Tier-4 Progression

Character framework from "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse": a **Level 12 to 30 gestalt sheet** granting the features, spellcasting progression, and benefits of TWO full classes simultaneously at every level — no multiclass splitting. In this campaign: modified Oath of Conquest Paladin × [[SourceSoulSorcerer]], plus a third column of custom "Tyrant Divine" unlocks per level (Source Vision, Veil Striker, Cleansing Shadow, Dark Pinions, ...).

## Structure
- Custom hit dice (12d12 "Tyrant Divine" chassis at L12)
- Start L12 = "Adjusting to the Torrent of Divinity"; cap L30 = "The God-Slayer Apotheosis"
- Per-level table with three columns: Paladin features / Sorcerer features / custom divine unlock
- Tracked via [[TheSovereignLedger]]

## Comparison within the wiki
- Higher floor and ceiling than the Ains module (guardian ceiling L12–16) and Visenya v6 (L6 prodigy) — this is the collection's first explicit epic-tier (post-20) progression matrix
- Same gestalt power-stacking instinct as the Visenya v6 "bard-mastermind gestalt" frame
