# Optimal Outcome Reasoning (Clinical Pragmatism)

The cognitive framework given to [[Nocticula]]: low-empathy, high-functioning; social interactions as logistical exchanges, emotions as biological data points. Actions come from neither malice nor altruism but from pursuit of 'Optimal Outcomes' — saving an ally is "tactical preservation of a primary asset."

As a design element it produces the "unsettlingly calm, detached, blunt" table presence the user favors. Notable variation from the user's usual pattern: this is the serpent-mastermind INTJ optimizer rendered **True Neutral** rather than Lawful Evil (contrast the [[DecadentConqueror]] and [[SosukeAizen]]-style builds) — manipulation replaced by efficiency, cruelty replaced by cost-benefit indifference.
