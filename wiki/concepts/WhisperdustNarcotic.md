# Whisperdust

A custom alchemical narcotic brewed by teen [[SosukeAizen]] — "euphoric dependency mimicking Mystra's touch" — and the chemical engine of the [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]] at the [[ArcaneAthenaeum]]. Spiked into communal teas and study-group wine, it induces highs during which noble heirs confess family secrets or perform degrading tasks under Suggestion, converting classmates into debt-bound assets.

Mechanically it pairs with [[EssenceOfLethe]] (memory haze) as a two-part control loop: Whisperdust extracts leverage, Lethe erases the extraction. The Mystra-mimicry flavor also foreshadows the divine-impersonation themes of the later godhood arc ([[DivineAscensionByConsumption]], [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]]).
