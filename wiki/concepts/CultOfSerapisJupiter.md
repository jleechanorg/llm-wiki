# Cult of Serapis-Jupiter

The syncretic religion blending Roman and Egyptian beliefs that becomes the **de facto state religion** during [[Caesarion]]'s Golden Age (14 BCE – 10 AD) in the [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] timeline.

- Grows from seeds planted by [[CleopatraVII]] as regent (40–30 BCE) and Caesar's temples to Venus Genetrix and Isis (29 BCE).
- The religious face of the empire's Roman-Egyptian fusion, alongside [[Alexandria]] as second capital and the deification of [[GaiusJuliusCaesar]] as *Divus Julius*.
