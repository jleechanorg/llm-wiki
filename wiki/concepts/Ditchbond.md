# Ditchbond

The Visenya v4 campaign's term for the simple, honest, un-manipulated connection an Apex being needs to stay anchored to the earth. Because [[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s [[ApexBloodline]] creates agonizing distance from humanity, her bond with [[SerDuncanTheTall]] — mud, road, squire-work, no thrones — is not sentimentality but structural necessity: the one relationship she does not conduct like an equation. Its fragility (Dunk does not know who "Silver" is) is a standing dramatic fuse.
