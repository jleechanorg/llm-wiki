# Aether (primordial energy)

Magic system of the *Blades of the High and Low* campaign. Aether is a primordial energy that predates and bypasses the [[Highlanders]]' legal and bloodline controls on Lowlander spellcasting. It is fluid, luminous, and adaptive, manifesting as force, light, resonance, or precise physical enhancement.

The [[WarriorQueen]] mastered it a millennium ago; fragments of her research became the Highlanders' plateau-cities. [[ValeriaVex]] possesses it innately, which is what allows her to function as a full [[SpellbladeGestalt]] despite being a Lowlander squire.

## Design notes

- Aether escalation is a *tell*: using more of it than intended can reveal hidden capability, feeding the [[WitnessedRevealMechanic]].
- It integrates with martial play rather than replacing it — weapon attacks create openings for Aether techniques, and mundane mastery is the disguise-preserving alternative to Aether use.
