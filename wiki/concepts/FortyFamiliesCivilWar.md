# Forty Families Civil War

The inciting geopolitical situation of [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]]: the [[ValyrianFreehold]]'s Forty Families have split **exactly 20-v-20**, and open war — "an inferno of tearing claws and atomic-grade dragonflame" above the Fourteen Flames — is hours away at campaign start.

Function in the design:
1. **Clock pressure**: the exodus must launch before the skies burn.
2. **Thematic proof** of the campaign's core claim — structural rot of an insular aristocratic autocracy (blood-feuds, vanity, breeding monopolies) destroying itself.
3. **Cover**: total war among the great houses is the distraction that lets the mocked 41st House ([[HouseTargaryen]]) and [[SarielAsh]] slip away in the [[VanguardExodus]].

A player-facing analogue of canon ASOIAF's Doom-adjacent instability, replacing cataclysm with self-inflicted political collapse.
