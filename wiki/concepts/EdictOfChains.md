# Edict of Chains

Founding-era decree of the Seraphim in the [[WorldOfAssiah]]: all "chaotic" mortal races — elves, dwarves, orcs, goblins, humans — must be chained and used as labor. Millions died building [[Aeterna]] under it.

It supplies the [[CenturyOfSchism]]'s decisive lever: in Year 1412 [[Lucifer]] shattered the slave colonies ([[MarchOfBrokenChains]]) and absorbed the freed races into his Host — emancipation as both moral cause and infantry logistics, characteristic of the setting's "Mathematics of Slaughter" framing.
