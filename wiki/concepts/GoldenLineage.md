## Overview
The Golden Lineage is the collective identity of Emperor [[Vitiate]]'s bred children in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]). Publicly they are "The Imperial Princes and Princesses," demigods of the Dark Side who serve as the Emperor's Enforcers; to the Dark Council they are an existential threat — monsters bred to replace the Old Guard.

## Structural tension
- **Internal:** dysfunctional, competitive, deadly — every sibling jockeys for favor knowing Vitiate plans to consume all but the strongest ([[TheHarvest]])
- **External:** the [[CouncilOfTwelve]] sees inherited power as the death of the Sith meritocracy ("power should be taken, not inherited")

## Roster
[[Arcann]] (military), [[Thexan]] (diplomacy), [[Valtoria]] (alchemy), [[Draven]] (beasts), and the PC [[NocturneOldRepublic|Nocturne]], with [[Senya]] as Matriarch.
