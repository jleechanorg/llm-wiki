# Weaponized Desire

Thematic power system from [[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]], distilled in [[NocturneSosuke]]'s law learned at sixteen: **"Love is a weapon, and desire is a leash."**

## Applications in the mythos

- **Origin**: trafficked in [[BaldursGate]]'s Upper City, Nocturne inverted victimhood into predation — she seduced the Nautiloid's illithid parasite instead of fleeing it, "transforming from a victim into a predator so exquisite that the monsters of the dark forgot to attack her."
- **Conquest**: the Audit of the Hells replaced battle with rewritten loyalty ([[Zariel]]) and adoration-husking ([[Asmodeus]]); the Abyss summit ended in a soul-fueled Mass Dominate over the Demon Lords.
- **Patron dynamic**: the same law governs the [[ObsidianMirrorPact]] — power as courtship, the bearer held by desire rather than contract.

The purest systematization yet of the manipulation-as-mechanics thread running through the player's campaigns ([[PreemptiveFrameControl]], [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]], [[KyokaSuigetsu]]).
