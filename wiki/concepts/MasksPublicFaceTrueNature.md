# The Masks: Public Face vs True Nature

A [[CampaignTemplate]] Section 2 requirement: every protagonist is defined by a **Public Face** (how they manipulate the world) versus a **True Nature** ("the monster beneath"). Section 5 extends the same device to siblings, who each get their own "Mask".

This duality is the engine of the corpus's social gameplay: the Ains module's [[Mask of Fragility]] (dynamic deception DC) and Visenya v6's incognito-in-the-North premise are both direct mechanizations of it. It also matches the user's documented campaign taste profile (serpent-mastermind protagonists whose presented persona is strategic tooling). Related: [[CoreCompulsionMechanic]] — the compulsion is what strains the mask.
