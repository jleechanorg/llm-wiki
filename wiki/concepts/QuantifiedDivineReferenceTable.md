# Quantified Divine Reference Table

The combat-resolution spine of the Divine Deception V0 prompt ([[2026-08-04-god-campaign-prompt-v0-divine-deception]]): every divine rank gets an explicit stat row so the engine can resolve god-tier combat numerically instead of narratively.

| Rank | Divine HP | AC | DPP | Attack | Leg. Resist | Avg dmg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mortal (Layer 1) | class HP | class AC | 0 (slots) | class mod | 0 | class dmg |
| Lesser (Rank Y) | 15 | 18 | 5 | +3 | 1 | 1d20+1 |
| Intermediate | 30 | 19 | 20 | +5 | 3 | 1d20+3 |
| Greater (Rank Z) | 100+ | 20–21 | 100+ | +7 | 5 | 1d20+6 |
| **True Rank A (player)** | 180+ | 25+ | 200+ | +12 (auto-hit) | 9+ | 1d20+20 |
| [[Ao]] | ∞ | ∞ | ∞ | ∞ | ∞ | ∞ |

## Attached resolution rules

- **Auto-success**: the player passes all mortal-scale checks (DC ≤ 30) without rolling.
- **Fog of War**: Rank A cannot read the true minds of other gods — only surface thoughts and avatars (the one hard epistemic limit on the player).
- **Damage mitigation**: 1 True [[Divine-Power-Points|DPP]] narratively negates any hit taken in a lower Layer.

## Design role

The table is what makes [[DivineDissonance]] enforceable: "Metric Mismatch" detection only works because each Layer of the [[FourLayersOfExistence]] has published numbers to mismatch against. It also hard-caps the power fantasy with an infinite-stats Ao row, echoing the balance-enforcer role Ao plays across the god campaigns ([[DivineDetectionTiers]], [[GeometricFirewall]]).
