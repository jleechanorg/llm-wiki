# Logic Siege

The Sovereign Protocol's replacement for physical combat: conflict between [[Sovereign]]s resolves via competing [[SovereignLogic]]s. **Formula**: `d20 + GP + SP expenditure vs. Target Defense`. Success "rotates" the target's reality to match the attacker's theme (conversion, not destruction); failure causes **Logic Rot** — SP damage against the attacker's own [[SubstratePointMetric|SP pool]], which doubles as their HP. Because SP is spent to boost the roll *and* lost on failure, every siege is a double-or-nothing resource gamble. The session-start state throws the PC directly into a defensive wave of Logic Sieges at Aggro 8/10 (see [[AggroMeter]]).
