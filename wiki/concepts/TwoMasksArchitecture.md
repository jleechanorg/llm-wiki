# Two Masks Architecture

A character-design pattern from the BG1 Nocturne campaign: the protagonist's personality is "a fortress, constructed with two masterfully crafted outer walls that conceal the true, singular nature of the woman within."

## The two masks

1. **Dutiful Ward / Courteous Advisor** — default public interface. Soft, musical, deliberately quiet voice (forces others to lean in); flawless etiquette that puts others off-balance. Every kind gesture is a **data probe**: pressure applied to another personality's system to map weaknesses and leverage.
2. **Dispassionate Strategist** — deployed when planning is required. Presents multi-faceted win-win plans aligned with allies' stated goals (wealth, survival, knowledge) while banking an unstated private benefit. Allies feel like co-architects; structurally they are scaffolding to be dismantled when utility expires — no apology, no remorse, only "a quiet, logical pivot."

## Design function

The pattern operationalizes social manipulation as a game loop: mask selection is the action economy, and probe results feed the strategist. It is the **mundane, low-level analogue** of the magical deception mechanics elsewhere in the mythos (e.g. [[MaskOfFragility]]'s dynamic deception DC in the Ains campaign). Used by [[SerpentQueen]]; ancestral variants appear across the [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]] family ([[Alexiel]]'s porcelain-doll prisoner mask, [[NocturneSosuke]]'s courtly personas).
