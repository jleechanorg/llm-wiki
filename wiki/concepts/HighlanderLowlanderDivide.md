# Highlander/Lowlander Divide

Central social structure of *Blades of the High and Low*. The [[Highlanders]] — heirs of the [[WarriorQueen]]'s advisors, living in floating plateau-cities built from her research — monopolize high magic, advanced craftsmanship, and political authority. Lowland kingdoms are restricted **by law and bloodline controls** to half-caster or martial traditions.

## Narrative function

- Makes [[ValeriaVex]]'s innate [[AetherPrimordialEnergy|Aether]] inherently transgressive: a full-caster Lowlander is not just rare but structurally illegal, giving every use of the [[WitnessedRevealMechanic]] political stakes beyond personal reputation.
- Supplies the all-human antagonist roster (mage-hunters, inquisitors, Highlander elites) in a setting with no mindless monsters.
- Irony: the oppressive order descends from the Warrior Queen's own research — Valeria's past life built the cage her current life chafes against.
