## Summary
The wide, fast river that cuts through the emerald fields of Highvale. Defines the geography and economy of the region. Its name derives from the silver-leaf stamps used on lifestone crates moved along its length. The river has flooded sub-levels under the old mill, seepage in the manor cellars, and patrol routes for the Silverflow guards (who answer to the Crown). Smuggling along the Silverflow is the foundation of the Tethlar economic recovery.

## Notable Instances
- Turn 0: The river is described as a 'sluggish rush' near the manor
- Turn 13: Hobb's mill sits on the river; the lifestone crates are kept in the wet cellar where 'the Silverflow seeps through the stone'
- Turn 21: 'Silverflow guards' patrol the bridge every hour
- Turn 130: 'Silverflow guards' become the target of Alexiel's corruption operation
