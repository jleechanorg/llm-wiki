# The Burden of Supremacy

Core compulsion of [[SaitaAethelgard]] in *The Hollow Zenith* ([[2026-08-04-saita-v3-hollow-zenith]]): an utter obsession with proving her worth to her family — above all Emperor [[TiberiusAethelgard]] — and ensuring the [[AethelgardEmpire]] does not fall.

Narrative function: converts the campaign's geopolitical stakes into personal psychological stakes. The [[ImperialBluff]] mechanic is its scored expression — ego-feeding Temporary HP when supremacy is successfully performed, Exhaustion when the performance cracks. The compulsion also explains why a supremacist who privately despises Faerûn's "mayflies" tolerates the humiliation of bargaining with them: the bloodline's survival outranks her pride, which is itself a supremacy calculation.

Comparable family-approval drives appear in the [[GoldenLineage]] children of [[ProjectApotheosis]] (Nocturne Old Republic line) — both make a superhuman scion's self-worth contingent on a patriarch's judgment.
