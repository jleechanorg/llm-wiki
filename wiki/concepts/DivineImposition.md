# Divine Imposition (Consumed Will)

The capstone house mechanic of the [[Aizen]] god campaign's divinity layer, defined in the chat-2 campaign export.

## Mechanic

- **Divine Save DC 100** — described as "Absolute & Unbeatable for all mortals."
- When Aizen influences a mortal, **he does not roll**. The target makes a Divine Saving Throw using the *lower* of their Wisdom or Charisma, **at Disadvantage**, against DC 100.
- If the target somehow succeeds, **Divine Intervention: Consumed Will** (once per turn) forces a reroll taking the lower result.
- Companion rule: all of Aizen's mortal-realm ability checks (Investigation, Perception, Arcana, History, Insight, Survival) **auto-succeed** — treated as DC 100 with no dice rolled.

## Significance

This is the mechanical endpoint of the campaign's manipulation fantasy: the mortal arc's contested Deception/Persuasion play ([[Mask-of-Fragility]], [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]]) is replaced by a layer where mortal resistance is definitionally impossible. Dice are formally abolished for the protagonist's social and knowledge actions. The remaining game tension is relocated upward — only deity-tier actors ([[Mystra]], [[Elminster]] as her Chosen, rival gods) sit outside the auto-win envelope, which is why the export tracks a separate divine stat block (DR 750, DAC 25, DPP economy) for peer-level conflict.
