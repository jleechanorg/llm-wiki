# Level-Scaled Dragon Companion

Signature mechanic of [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]]: [[SarielAsh]]'s dragon **completely bypasses traditional draconic growth constraints** (age-based size in ASOIAF canon), instead scaling directly with her character level until reaching a hard ceiling of **exactly twice the size of Balerion the Black Dread** — a deliberately world-bending endpoint.

Design notes:
- Converts the dragon from a setting element into a personal progression track, the same pattern as the six-stage level-gated evolution tables in the Nocturne lines ([[AgeProgressionByLevel]]).
- The "unchained sky" for this dragon is the stated win condition of the [[VanguardExodus]] — companion scaling and campaign goal are the same arc.
- Risk it must manage: a companion that outgrows every canonical deterrent (hundreds of adult Freehold dragons) collapses late-game challenge unless opposition scales politically rather than militarily.
