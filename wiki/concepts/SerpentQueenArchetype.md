# Serpent-Queen Archetype

The personality architecture of [[Sariel]]: a "philosopher-queen in waiting, a master of systems who views the world... as a flawed schematic in desperate need of an editor with a steady hand and a merciless red pen." Explicitly constructed as a four-way blend: [[Griffith]] (transcendent ambition) + [[TywinLannister]] (cold strategic pragmatism) + [[AizenSosuke]] (intellectual/charismatic superiority) + [[JohanLiebert]] (philosophical nihilistic evil).

Structure: a fortress of two outer masks ([[TwoMaskFortress]]) concealing a singular inner nature driven by [[PortableAbsolutePower]], [[SurgicalDomination]], and supremacist certainty.

Cross-campaign: this is the modern-setting instantiation of the player's recurring serpent-mastermind fingerprint — sibling to the [[DecadentConqueror]] (Rome) and the ascension-focused builds cataloged in [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]]. The distinguishing variation here is the *servant-class cover identity* (bodyguard) instead of the sovereign/noble covers of prior campaigns.
