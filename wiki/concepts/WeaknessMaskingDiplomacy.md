# Weakness-Masking Diplomacy (the Herald's Bluff)

Core premise-pattern of *The Hollow Zenith* ([[2026-08-04-saita-v3-hollow-zenith]]): a genuinely superior but currently crippled power (the [[AethelgardEmpire]], gutted by its Cadet Branch civil war) must extract resources, alliances, and vassal states from targets it cannot actually fight, by projecting an illusion of unbroken invincibility. The campaign describes itself as "geopolitical gaslighting and supreme arrogance" — the player systematically buys, manipulates, and breaks [[BaldursGate]] rather than saving or conquering it.

Structural properties:
- **Asymmetric stakes**: discovery of the bluff means not embarrassment but annihilation ("you will be slaughtered").
- **Reputation as borrowed capital**: threats are backed by a reputation the herald cannot physically honor yet.
- **Mechanical binding**: enforced per-scene via [[ImperialBluff]] (Temp HP for successful bluffs, Exhaustion for exposure).
- **Aesthetic discipline**: extends to retainers (see [[KaelenAethelgard]]) — projecting invincibility governs even armor-cleaning.

Distinct from the wiki's other dual-identity premises ([[NocturneSosuke]]'s survival-horror concealment, the Ains module's Mask of Fragility): here the concealed fact is *state-level* weakness, making the bluff a geopolitical instrument rather than personal cover.
