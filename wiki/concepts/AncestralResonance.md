# Ancestral Resonance

The magic-system conceit of the Visenya v4 campaign: in a low-magic Westeros, [[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s Level 6 **Baelarys Weaver** (modified Swords Bard) casts no flashy spells — her "magic" is manipulating the "marrow" of the world through voice and will, inherited from [[HouseBaelarys]]'s dragon-binding Apex Conductors. Blade Flourish becomes "Geometric Combat" (calculated trajectory-stepping), Suggestion becomes the "Voice of the Dragon," Mending/Prestidigitation becomes "The Polish."

A clean example of the D&D-5e-adapts-everything pattern in the user's campaigns: mechanical chassis retained, presentation rewritten to fit the licensed setting's magic level.
