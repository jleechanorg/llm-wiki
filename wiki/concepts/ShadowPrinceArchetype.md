# Shadow Prince Archetype

Campaign-design archetype from "Iron Eagle: Vengeance" v3: a royal-blooded operative who moves through a war zone under a **deliberately underestimated civilian cover**. [[AureliusCaesar]]'s implementation:

- **Public face**: spoiled, hedonistic merchant ("Aurelius Varenus") — the cover buys free travel, bribery access, and intelligence from enemies who would never suspect a "civilian".
- **True nature**: Imperial prodigy with a black-ops squad, a war chest, and a secret mandate from the throne.

The archetype separates *social camouflage* from *actual capability* and makes the gap itself the power fantasy. It is the same engine as [[SosukeAizen]]'s "the Spare" persona and [[Alexiel]]'s porcelain-doll prisoner framing — this household's recurring pattern of masks that invite underestimation — but tuned to seduction-by-magnificence ([[BeautyAsWeapon]]) rather than pity. The bible names the specific hinge "The Decadent Conqueror" (ESFP performer mask over ENTJ king core).
