## Overview
College of Perdition is a Bard subclass in the Nocturne campaigns, representing Nocturne's connection to the Abyss and infernal powers. It features Thrall mechanics, Soul Thief progression, and Pheromone Spike abilities. The college evolves into "College of the Abyssal Siren" as Nocturne grows in power.

In the 2026-08-04 Velvet Cage v4 revision ([[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]]), the character sheet still names "Future College of Perdition" and the climax says "College of Perdition Unlocked," but the module's addendum supersedes it with the revised [[CollegeOfInfernalContracts]] ("Legion Update") under the doc's later-text-takes-precedence rule.
