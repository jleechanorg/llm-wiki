# Triumvirate of the Absolute's Bane

The three-way tyranny ruling the Sword Coast in the [[2026-08-04-bg3-empire-of-shadow|Empire of Shadow]] campaign: [[Astarion]] (blood — economy, politics, underworld, night), [[Shadowheart]] (shadow — Lower City Sharran theocracy), and [[Gale-of-Waterdeep]] (arcana — floating citadel, Netherese experimentation).

## Design notes

- Each member has a distinct domain, seat of power, and enforcement mechanism, giving parties three parallel villain tracks with built-in inter-faction friction ([[Minthara]]'s Night Watch explicitly suppresses "Sharran incursions").
- The name inverts their heroic title: they killed the Absolute, then became the new chains — the core irony of the [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] frame.
- Contrasts with the single-mastermind structure of the [[Aizen]] god-campaign; here power is split and unstable, which is the campaign's main lever for intrigue.
