# Transcendent Greater Deity

The custom top divine rank in the [[AizenSosuke]] god campaign's homebrew divinity ladder, and the explicit **one-year in-fiction goal** declared in the chat 2 campaign export.

## The ladder

- Ascension via [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]] (consuming [[Bane]]) makes Aizen a **Nascent Greater Deity** at character Level 12.
- The stated objective is to consolidate cosmic dominion and climb to **Transcendent Greater Deity** within one year — turning the post-ascension game into a divine-politics progression loop measured in [[Divine-Power-Points]], portfolio control, and inter-deity negotiation (e.g. the prepared [[Elminster]]/[[Mystra]] contact).

## Why it matters

Even after dice are abolished for the protagonist ([[DivineImposition]] DC 100), the campaign retains a progression treadmill by moving the goalposts to a higher divine tier — the player's power fantasy is structured as bureaucratic consolidation ("Absolute Codification"), not combat.
