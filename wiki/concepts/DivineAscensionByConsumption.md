# Divine Ascension by Consumption

The ascension model used in the [[Aizen]] god campaign: godhood is obtained not by apotheosis quest or divine grant but by **consuming the essence of an existing deity** — here, [[Bane]], whose portfolio of Tyranny and Control Aizen absorbs and rebrands as **Absolute Codification, Tyranny, and Control**.

## Structure

- **Trigger**: character Level 12 (Paladin 2 / Bard 10, 113,440 XP) — the mortal build's completion doubles as the ascension threshold.
- **Result**: Nascent Greater Deity rank with a distinct divine stat block (Divine Resilience 750, Divine Armor Class 25, Divine Power Points 825/day, PB +6 "Transcendental," inherent divine Charisma 30) layered over a preserved mortal disguise (HP 59, AC 18).
- **Inherited will**: the [[DivineImposition]] mechanic is explicitly named "Consumed Will" — the stolen god's coercive nature becomes the ascendant's signature power.
- **Ladder continues**: the stated campaign goal is **Transcendent Greater Deity within one year**, implying consumption/consolidation is repeatable, not terminal.

## Pattern significance

Consumption-ascension matches the player's established serpent-mastermind preference (see campaign-preferences profile): power is taken from a rival tyrant rather than earned from a patron, and the theft is then legitimized through systematizing language ("Codification"). It mirrors Aizen's Bleach source material (the Hōgyoku transcendence arc) transposed into Forgotten Realms cosmology, with Kyōka Suigetsu retained across the ascension boundary.
