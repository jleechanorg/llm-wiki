## Overview
The Iron Dominion is a ruthless corporate-mercenary state in the Faerun setting, located just beyond Baldur's Gate's sphere of influence. It operates as an institutionalized military-industrial complex where morality is an expensive luxury and survival is dictated by blood, gold, and absolute structural hierarchy.

## Political Structure

### Nine-Tier Ranking System
1. **King** — Supreme autocratic leader
2. **Grand General** — High military council members
3. **General** — Regional warlords commanding armies
4. **Captain** — Strategic commanders of major installations
5. **Lieutenant** — Company commanders
6. **Sergeant** — Tactical squad leaders (Aizen's rank)
7. **Soldier** — Standard infantry
8. **Apprentice** — Military cadets
9. **Slave** — Stripped of legal personhood

### The Five Great Houses
- **House Sosuke** — Ancient nobility, apex tacticians and war-mages
- **House Vane** — Industrial masters, weapon forging
- **House Draconis** — Frontline executioners, heavy assault
- **House Malakar** — Shadow architects, intelligence and assassinations
- **House Kaelen** — Maritime lords, slave trade infrastructure

## Economy
Primary revenue streams:
- High-stakes military contracts
- Calculated blackmail
- Systemic enslavement
- Targeted political assassinations
- Territorial subjugation
