# Serpent-King Archetype

Personality template defined in the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign: "a philosopher-queen in waiting, a master of systems who views the world as a flawed schematic in desperate need of an editor." Explicitly constructed as a four-way composite: [[Griffith]] (transcendent ambition) + [[TywinLannister]] (strategic pragmatism) + [[SosukeAizen]] (intellectual/charismatic superiority) + [[JohanLiebert]] (philosophical nihilism).

Structure: a fortress personality with two outer walls (the masks — see [[DataProbeSocialEngineering]] for the probe layer) concealing a singular inner nature, plus three core drives: [[PortableAbsolutePower]], [[SurgicalDomination]], and supremacist certainty (scholarly detachment toward all other actors).

Relation to corpus: this is the "serpent-mastermind INTJ" preference documented in the user's campaign-preference profile, and the mask mechanics parallel [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] and [[DivineDeceptionProtocol]] from the god-campaign line — same deception architecture at mortal scale.
