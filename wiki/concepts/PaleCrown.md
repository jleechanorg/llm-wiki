# The Pale Crown

The endgame of [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]]: [[NocturneRavencrest]]'s planned kingdom — **neither human nor Scourge but a new thing**, a living-undead nation held together by the dual authority of public virtue and shadow sanction, with a single throne answered to by every other power.

Its feeder infrastructure is built through the shadow stack of [[DualStackGameplay]]: Pale Undead smuggled through Andorhal, deals with the [[CultOfTheDamned]] and the dreadlord triumvirate, and a parallel state grown inside the Plague Lands. "Absolute Sovereignty" — pawn of neither mortal kings nor demonic lords — is the stated win condition.
