# The Shattered Rose

[[NocturneSosuke]]'s private sub-plane in the Abyss, from [[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]] — simultaneously her throne and her prison.

- Before the sealing: her seat of dominion, "a kingdom of dead gods and enslaved demons."
- After [[Ao]]'s [[GeometricFirewall]]: a pocket dimension outside of time. She retains absolute rule over the devils and demons within but cannot touch the Material Plane.
- Sole window out: the Obsidian Mirror ([[ObsidianMirrorPact]]) held by [[SosukeAizen]], through which she watches him — and he studies her.

Narratively, the Shattered Rose converts an off-scale NPC (Level 60 sovereign) into a bounded resource: infinite power on the far side of a keyhole only the protagonist can open.
