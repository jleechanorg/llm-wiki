# Engineered Crucible War

Narrative pattern in which the setting's apparent existential enemy is secretly a mechanism deployed by the setting's own creator/authority to endlessly test and cull its subjects. In the [[WorldOfAssiah]], the [[AbyssalWar]]'s demons are the Creator's "violent terrarium" for the Empyrean bloodlines — revealed to [[Lucifer]] by a dying Demon Prince in Year 1400.

The pattern converts a war-against-evil setting into a rebellion-against-heaven setting: the revelation reframes 900 years of heroism as complicity, and the most decorated defender becomes the revolutionary. Related to [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]] (authority fabricating the frame others act within) and the operator's recurring taste for god-indicting protagonists ([[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]]).
