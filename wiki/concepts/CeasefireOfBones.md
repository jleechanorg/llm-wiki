# Ceasefire of Bones

The 284 AC settlement in Visenya v5: after [[RhaegarTargaryen]] returned Lyanna Stark's bones (and publicly claimed her son as his bastard [[JaehaerysTargaryen]] to deny the North a rallying cause), a grieving [[EddardStark]] **refused to bend the knee but agreed to a ceasefire** — closing the Neck and turning the North into an isolationist kingdom that trades with Braavos and ignores the Iron Throne. A truce bought with a corpse and a legitimacy lie, not reconciliation: it freezes rather than ends the war, and Jon/Jaehaerys is the fault line along which it can shatter.
