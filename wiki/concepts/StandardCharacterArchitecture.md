# Standard Character Architecture

Sub-Template A of [[CampaignBibleTemplateV4]]: a mandatory six-tier profile executed for **every** generated character — protagonist, retinue, parents, siblings, faction leaders.

1. **Core Identity** — name, archetype/role, social standing/title, alignment, MBTI type
2. **Psychology** — core motivation (the "Why"), greatest fear rooted in a *specific formative trauma*, 3–5 temperament traits
3. **Behavior and Speech** — physical stress tells, explicit speech patterns (tone, cadence, vocabulary, pacing), public reputation
4. **Complete Backstory** — the Defining Moment (precise sequence of action, choice, perceived failure), chronological relevant history, and Deep Secrets unknown to all other characters
5. **Persona vs. Repressed Interior** — the outward manipulative mask vs. private self-critique when alone
6. **Unconscious Beliefs** — 2–4 absolute subconscious axioms (e.g. "Power is the only objective truth")

## Why it matters

The tier structure bakes *dramatic irony* into every NPC by construction: public persona, hidden interior, and secrets are separately specified, giving a manipulation-oriented protagonist guaranteed leverage surfaces. This explains the uniform psychological depth of NPCs across the Nocturne campaign bibles ([[NocturneSosuke]], [[NocturneRavencrest]], [[NocturneOldRepublic]]) — it is a template contract, not per-campaign authorship. Tier 6's unconscious-belief axioms echo the profile in the campaign-preferences analysis of serpent-mastermind play.
