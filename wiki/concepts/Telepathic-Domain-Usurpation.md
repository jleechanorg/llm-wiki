# Telepathic Domain Usurpation

Strategy from the [[Aizen]] god campaign: converting a rival deity's surviving worshippers by direct telepathic imposition of will, thereby absorbing the rival's domain remnants. Aizen applies it to [[Bane]]'s former cultists, asserting dominion over tyranny/control by making the dead god's followers his own.

## The DM's pro/con ledger

**Pros**: unresistable loyalty (Divine Save DC 100); simultaneous district-wide control without physical presence; absorption of cultist knowledge, caches, and networks; no overt conflict with the [[Flaming Fist]]; direct usurpation of the rival domain.

**Cons**: 50 [[Divine Power Points]] per wave for large-scale imposition; detection risk from [[Mystra]] (mental manipulation), [[Shar]] (domain conflict), and [[Ao]] (broad disruption); and a *behavioral subtlety* requirement — puppets acting "too perfectly" would betray divine control to mundane observers.

## Design note

The mechanic frames conquest as an efficiency/stealth trade rather than a combat problem, consistent with the campaign's serpent-mastermind throughline: the optimal move is invisible, administrative, and total. It extends the mortal-tier social-manipulation toolkit ([[Mask of Fragility]], [[Whispers of the Realm]]) to divine scale.
