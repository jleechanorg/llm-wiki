# Companion Contract Recruitment

Companion framework from [[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]]: the Level 9-10 BG3 origin characters are not co-equal party members but **Assets the Level 1 protagonist must Contract** — each defined by an attraction flag (does [[SirensLeverage]]'s +2 apply?), a leverage hook, and explicit contract terms.

## Examples

- **[[Astarion]]**: blood-for-kills contract; leverage = knowing he's a spawn before he tells you.
- **Gale**: artifacts-for-theory tutoring; leverage = promised look at the Book of Vile Darkness.
- **Lae'zel**: creche intel (possibly a lie) for service; leverage = winning (or cheating) a duel.
- **[[Shadowheart]]**: shared anti-Absolute cause; leverage = keeping her Shar worship secret.
- Immune seats: Jaheira (persuadable only via "protecting the city") and Minsc (pure of heart; sees a damsel, "not realizing you are the villain of this story").

## Mechanics

Party of protagonist + 3; remainder wait at a camp that upgrades from the [[ElfsongTavern]] to reclaimed Sosuke Manor. Approval decay leads to abandonment or betrayal. Romance grants buffs (Lover's Comfort: 1d8 temp HP after a shared long rest; max-approval lovers give Advantage on saves within 10 ft). At College levels, companions become the elite always-Active tier of the [[CollegeOfInfernalContracts]] Black Ledger.

## Significance

Inverts the BG3 power fantasy: the socially clumsy high-level combatants need a **Face**, and the mechanically weakest character owns the roster through blackmail, secrets, and manufactured desire — the recurring serpent-mastermind pattern ([[BeautyAsWeapon]]) expressed as a recruitment system.
