# A Night of Unrest

Opening scenario of [[ShadowOverTheGate]]: a seemingly ordinary evening in [[BaldursGate]] over weeks of accumulating unease — strange disappearances, escalating gang violence, whispers of ancient cults.

Four parallel PC entry hooks (see [[NarrativeHookScenarios]]):
1. **Elfsong Tavern brawl** — Guild gang vs a disciplined, ritual-tattooed gang at the [[ElfsongTavern]]; artifact theft attempt mid-chaos.
2. **Dockside abduction** — unnaturally fast shadowy figures; an eye-like symbol left in the cobblestones.
3. **Noble's plea** — minor noble's vanished guard, a "growing darkness," lurking figures.
4. **Flaming Fist patrol** — missing persons who were critics of the power structure; a stressed patrol leader, eerily quiet routes.

All hooks are bound by one common thread: the antagonists' "unusual efficiency" — [[AizenSosuke]]'s hidden hand ([[SubtleInfluenceCampaignHook]]). The design ensures any starting choice converges into the same faction intrigue.
