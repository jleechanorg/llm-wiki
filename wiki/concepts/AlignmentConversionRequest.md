# Alignment Conversion Request

A campaign-setup pattern observed in *Pax Caesara* v2: the player prefaces a fully-authored campaign bible with a modification directive — **"Change the campaign below and make me lawful good"** — while the bible text beneath remains written as "a campaign of Lawful Evil conquest."

## Why it's interesting

- The document captures a campaign **mid-conversion**: requested alignment and authored text contradict until the DM rewrites the bible.
- Conversion-by-directive is cheaper than re-authoring: the player keeps the setting ([[PaxCaesara]]), the character chassis ([[AureliusJuliaCaesar]], [[WeaponizedBeauty]], dual masks), and asks only for the moral valence to flip.
- The interesting design question it poses: which traits survive the flip? Charisma, beauty, and ambition are alignment-neutral; the "Devouring Maw" entitlement core and emotional sadism cannot — a Lawful Good rewrite must replace the core compulsion, not just relabel it.

## Relation to other patterns

Sibling of [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart]] / [[CampaignContextMigration]] (both revise an existing campaign rather than starting fresh), but narrower: it revises the *moral contract* while preserving world and chassis.
