# Subtle Weaving

[[Nocticula]]'s specialization in casting without somatic or verbal components — effectively an always-on Subtle Spell metamagic — letting her manipulate reality while remaining a "ghost" even in the heat of battle.

Tactically it is the third leg of her concealment stack: [[ManaVeilMastery]] hides the reservoir, [[DemonicOvercasting]] hides the ceiling, and Subtle Weaving hides the *act* of casting, so observers cannot even attribute effects to her. Only [[TheRevealConstraint]] pierces the stack.
