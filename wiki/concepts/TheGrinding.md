# The Grinding

Visenya v5's name for the steady-state of [[TheLongDiscord]]: not peace, but a **seasonal rhythm of border skirmishes, raids, and failed treaties** (290–298 AC). The Crown fights a perpetual counter-insurgency against the **Stag Lords** (Baratheon loyalists in the Stormlands) and the **River Wolves** (Tully/Stark guerillas in the Riverlands). No battles are decisive; the war is an economic bleed that has bankrupted the realm. As a campaign mechanic it supplies endless low-stakes conflict texture while the real game — succession, prophecy, the [[ApexBloodline]] — plays out in King's Landing.
