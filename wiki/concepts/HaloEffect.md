# Halo Effect

Defensive class feature of [[Alexiel]]'s [[VoidExperimentClass]] build. As a Reaction, the character meets an attacker's eyes; the attacker makes a WIS save (DC 14) or hesitates, taking Disadvantage on the attack roll.

**Design role**: converts the character's non-combat statistic (CHA/appearance) into effective AC, letting an 8-HP, AC-12 frame survive Level 1 combat. It is the defensive mirror of [[SirensCall]]: both weaponize how others perceive the character rather than the character's own physical capability — the core [[PowerlessMastermindBuild]] trick of making the opponent's psychology do the work.
