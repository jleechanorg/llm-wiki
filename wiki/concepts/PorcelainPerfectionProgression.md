# Porcelain Perfection Progression

Physical-evolution mechanic from the Nocturne Sosuke v6 module ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-bg3-v6-velvet-cage]]). Design thesis: **"You do not grow monstrous; you grow perfect."** Character level drives refinement of a humanoid form toward the uncanny valley — too symmetrical, too smooth, too still — instead of adding claws/scales.

## Stage table

| Levels | Stage | Marker |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | The Ruin | Drowned-victim wreckage; pitiful and fragile |
| 1–4 | The Porcelain Doll | Grime stops sticking; bone-china skin; no sweat; glass-bead eyes |
| 5–9 | The Uncanny Valley | Living-marble skin; wounds crack and seal, don't bleed; gliding gait; pink eye-flood on casting |
| 10–14 | The Predator | Voluntary blinking; wind-proof hair; fragility legible as a trap; spectral horns on social crits |
| 15–19 | The Sovereign | Personal updraft; perpetual ozone/brine scent; lingering light-trail eye-glow |
| 20 | The Ascended | Statue-brought-to-life apotheosis (source truncates here) |

## Significance

- Inverts standard D&D fiendish-transformation tropes: horror comes from *perfection*, not corruption — a body-horror register suited to the module's Survival Horror / Gothic Noir tone.
- Sibling mechanic to [[AgeProgressionByLevel]] (level-indexed embodiment) and an expression of [[BeautyAsWeapon]]: each stage increases the gap between perceived fragility and actual lethality.
- Ties visual tells to mechanics: pink eye-luminescence telegraphs spellcasting and [[PheromoneSpike]] use; horn/wing flashes trigger on Intimidation/Persuasion critical successes.
