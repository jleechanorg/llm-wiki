# Social Armor Wardrobe

Mechanic from the [[2026-08-04-gaia-julia-v2-golden-republic|Golden Republic (Gaia Julia v2)]] campaign: clothing treated as **equipment with stat effects**, giving the social game the same crunch as combat.

## The two tiers

1. **"Middle-Tier" disguise** (equipped): respectable-but-new-money teal stola and historyless gold jewelry → Advantage on Deception to appear harmless. Purpose-built to bait a specific mark ([[Batiatus]]) into believing the wearer is exploitable.
2. **True Julian Regalia** (hidden in baggage): Tyrian purple silk gown + gold laurel diadem → Advantage on Intimidation against Roman officials; wearing it is itself a plot action (revealing royal identity).

## Design significance

The wardrobe encodes the campaign's dual-mask structure into inventory: which outfit is "equipped" *is* which persona is active. This mechanizes the [[BeautyAsWeapon]] pattern seen across the player's campaigns (cf. the BG1 Nocturne profile hiding beauty under grime) — appearance is a controlled tactical resource, not flavor. Sibling combat gear follows the same logic: silent gladius, stealth-optimized mithral corset, and Boots-of-Elvenkind sandals build an assassin loadout wearable under a stola.
