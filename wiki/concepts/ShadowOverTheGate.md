# The Shadow over the Gate

Campaign title [[Gemini]] gives the mortal-era Aizen campaign: D&D 5e in [[BaldursGate]] (Lower City, Upper City, outskirts). Themes: intrigue, shifting loyalties, power struggles, the nature of good and evil, the corrupting influence of ambition.

**Five-faction web**: [[FlamingFist]] (state force under [[UlderRavengard]]), [[TheGuild]] (organized crime), [[KnightsOfTheShield]] (secret order), [[CultOfTheDeadThree]] (Bane/Bhaal/Myrkul), and House Aizen ([[AizenSosuke]]) — the new rising power whose influence "could extend into any or all of the other factions."

**Design pattern**: the title itself is the tell — the "shadow" is Aizen. The campaign is architected so every faction conflict has a second, hidden layer ([[SubtleInfluenceCampaignHook]]), and the opening ([[ANightOfUnrest]]) offers four entry points that all converge on the same signature.

Born as a [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart]] of the "bane - main" chat; the arc later continues through Level 12 faction play, divine ascension, and the [[CampaignContextMigration]] into chat 2.
