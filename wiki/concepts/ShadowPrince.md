# Shadow Prince

Operational archetype used in the Iron Eagle: Vengeance campaigns (v3 and v6): a royal operates under a **spoiled, hedonistic merchant** cover identity (Aurelius Varenus, grain and wine) that lets him travel freely, bribe officials, and spy on enemies "who would never suspect a 'civilian.'"

- **Public face**: harmless decadence — the cover works because it is *plausible* decadence, matching the character's genuine tastes.
- **True nature**: a Level 6 [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]] Bard — duelist, manipulator, [[Miasma]] wielder — executing a secret imperial mandate.

The Shadow Prince is the *operational* mask; the [[DecadentConqueror]] bible (v6) defines the *psychological* mask structure beneath it (Prince of Pleasure vs Golden Tyrant). Together they form a three-layer identity stack: cover identity → public persona → true nature.
