# Soul Tithe Inversion

The prologue-climax mechanic of the [[NocturneSosuke]] BG3 v3 campaign: instead of disrupting the [[CassalanterFamily]]'s soul-tithe ritual to [[Zariel]], the party **inverts** it, redirecting the soul siphon from the intended sacrifices (the caged Sosuke sisters) onto the ritual's own sponsors upstairs, who are "consumed by their own pact."

Mechanical decomposition (a multi-actor skill challenge rather than a combat win):
- **Persuasion DC 18** — convince [[Vaximus]] to target the nobles instead of merely stopping the ritual.
- **Arcana DC 15 (Vaximus)** — recalculate the "infernal mathematics" to invert the target.
- **Performance DC 18 (Nocturne)** — anchor the ritual personally, becoming its focal point.

Design significance: the victim becomes the ritual's beneficiary, and the campaign's stated starting level (9) is the payoff of this single act — an unusually large level jump that encodes "stolen infernal power" as the character's foundation. This is the corpus's clearest instance of the recurring pattern in which the protagonist wins by redirecting an enemy's own machinery rather than overpowering it (compare [[Conceptual-Debate-Victory]] and the false-hostage inversion in the Gaia Julia v4/v5 Gilded Trap line).
