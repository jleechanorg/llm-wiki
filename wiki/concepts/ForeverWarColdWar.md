# The Forever War (cold war)

Setting frame for campaign v6: [[RhaegarTargaryen]]'s victory at the Trident seventeen years ago preserved the dynasty, but the rebellion never fully resolved — it cooled into "a tense, continent-wide cold war of politics and veiled threats" between the crown and a stabilized-but-resentful North. The premise turns Winterfell into an intelligence sandbox: [[VisenyaTargaryen]]'s incognito visit is effectively a one-girl loyalty audit of the Warden of the North, with annexation-or-existence as the implied stake.
