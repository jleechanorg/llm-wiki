# Dread-Wyrm Shadow

Custom **gestalt-tier hybrid archetype** granted to [[SarielAsh]] at Level 6 in the [[2026-08-04-sariel-valyria-embers-of-valyria|Embers of Valyria]] campaign. Explicitly exempts the protagonist from "standard restrictions of a low-level adventurer," granting **absolute social and physical disruption**:

- Stealth/wetwork assassin kit ("Dread-Wyrm Assassin") for localized tactical operations.
- [[BeautyAsWeapon]] as a passive social-warfare aura — unarmored presence mechanically destabilizes nobility.
- Bonded [[LevelScaledDragonCompanion]] replacing normal draconic growth curves.

Fits the campaign library's recurring pattern of bespoke overpowered-by-design protagonist classes (cf. [[ImperatorClass]], the Nocturne Ascendant profiles) — the power fantasy is front-loaded, and tension comes from political containment rather than combat scarcity.
