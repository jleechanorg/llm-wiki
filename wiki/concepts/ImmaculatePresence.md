# Immaculate Presence

Passive feature of the Emerald Eclipse protagonist ([[GestaltTier4Progression]], L12): flawless divine features grant **advantage on all Charisma checks**, and enemies within 30 feet have **disadvantage on saves against being Charmed or Frightened**.

## Function in play
- Backs the CHA 20 (+5) social-domination pillar — "words manipulate royal courts, beauty intoxicates mortals"
- Synergizes with Oath of Conquest's fear engine (Scornful Rebuke at L15)
- Continues the recurring player-taste motif of beauty/charisma as strategic weaponry seen across the Visenya line and the manipulation-focused concepts ([[SirensLeverage]])
