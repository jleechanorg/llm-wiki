# The Noblest Dialect

Feature of the [[DragonLordSubclass]] in *The Apex of Ash: The Sowing* ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v3-apex-of-ash]]): Advantage on **all Charisma checks** when speaking High Valyrian to Valyrian descendants or to Dragons.

## Function

Encodes bloodline authority as a linguistic register — [[VisenyaBelaerys]]'s voice "resonates in the bones of those who hear it." The opening scene's Apex Command option collapses the mechanic to a single word: "Lykirī" (Calm), spoken to [[Vermithor]], makes the Bronze Fury bow rather than merely stop. Because [[Dragonstone]] and Rhaenyra's court are dense with Valyrian descendants, the feature doubles as a court-intrigue tool, not just a dragon-taming one.
