# Entropy Reversal

The ultimate endgame goal of the Sovereign Protocol: rewriting thermodynamic laws across **all realities** of the [[PanSubstrate]] to match the PC's specific thematic vision ([[SovereignLogic]]), thereby preventing heat death. It frames the entire 1,000-Sovereign competition as a race to decide *whose theme the multiverse's physics permanently adopts* — victory is not conquest of rivals but authorship of the laws they must live under.
