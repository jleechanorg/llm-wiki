# Witnessed Reveal Mechanic

Core hidden-power rule of *Blades of the High and Low*, governing how [[ValeriaVex]]'s concealed strength interacts with the world. Six-step loop:

1. Valeria appears limited or merely exceptional.
2. An opponent makes assumptions based on what they have seen.
3. Valeria chooses: keep hiding, reveal a small portion, or fully escalate.
4. A witnessed reveal creates surprise.
5. Observers become surprised, intimidated, cautious, respectful, or determined to prepare a counter.
6. Later encounters reflect what each individual actually witnessed — not what the entire world magically knows.

## The anti-pattern it forbids

A reveal must never become a **global suspicion meter**: one witness may be intimidated while an unseen rival still treats Valeria as an ordinary squire. Knowledge of her ability is tracked per witness, making information itself a tactical resource.

## Comparisons in the corpus

Sibling mechanic to [[MaskOfFragility]] (Ains module — dynamic deception DC) and the herald bluff of [[SaitaAethelgard]], but distinctive in demanding per-NPC epistemic state rather than a single campaign-wide deception value. For a state-tracking engine like [[WorldArchitectAI]], this implies per-NPC knowledge fields rather than one global flag.
