# Abyssal War (Years 500–1409)

Nine-hundred-year war between the [[CelestialImperium]] and demonic hordes that tore planar rifts across the northern hemisphere of the [[WorldOfAssiah]]. A "relentless meat grinder" in which [[Lucifer]] and [[Raziel]] rose as the Imperium's champions.

Its hidden nature is the setting's central twist: per the Revelation of Year 1400, the demons are not invaders but the Creator's deliberate mechanism — the war an engineered, endless crucible to test the Empyrean bloodlines and cull the weak ([[EngineeredCrucibleWar]]). This truth destroyed Lucifer's faith and caused the [[CenturyOfSchism]].
