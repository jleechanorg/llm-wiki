# Functional Obscurity

The default operating stance of [[VisenyaBelaerys]] in *The Apex of Ash* (campaign visenya v2): a quiet, soot-smudged girl in an oversized straw hat and rough traveler's clothes, performing the role of traumatized victim so that the world's most dangerous bloodline reads as background scenery.

Design notes:
- Obscurity here is *functional*, not passive — it is the resource that [[BelaerysAllure]] spends down (each face reveal buys influence at the price of traceability via *The Prickle*), and the cover under which [[SubtleApotheosis]] operates freely.
- Enforced diegetically by [[DaenerysTargaryen]]'s command to hide her face, giving the concealment an in-world patron and a standing source of chafing against Visenya's "Breaking Authority" compulsion — the mask is simultaneously protection and a cage.
- Recurring pattern across the 2026-08 campaign bibles: [[SaitaAethelgard]]'s undercover herald persona and the *Mask of Fragility* mechanic solve the same problem (apex being must pass as harmless) with a deception-DC framing instead of a resource-spend framing. Related: [[BeautyAsWeapon]], [[Miasma]]-era masking concepts.
