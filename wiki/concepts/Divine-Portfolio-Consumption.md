# Divine Portfolio Consumption

Homebrew apotheosis mechanic in the Aizen god-campaign: defeating a deity's avatar (by combat or battle of will) lets the victor **seize and consume the deity's portfolio**, absorbing its divine domain and rank.

## Instances in the summary arc

1. **Tyranny** — [[AizenSosuke]] defeats [[Bane]]'s avatar in a battle of will → nascent God of Tyranny.
2. **Death** — Aizen ambushes [[Myrkul]]'s avatar → Greater Deity.

## Properties

- Stacking: portfolios accumulate; each consumption raises divine rank.
- Escalation trigger: serial consumption attracts higher-order attention ([[Ao-the-Overgod]]), pivoting the campaign from combat to metaphysics ([[Conceptual-Debate-Victory]]).
- Related: [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]] and [[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]] siblings in the reset campaigns reuse this mechanic; the soul-scale analogue is [[SoulEssenceHarvesting]].
