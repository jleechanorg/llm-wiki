# Data-Probe Social Engineering

Mechanic/behavior pattern from the Nocturne Witcher S4 campaign: every social gesture — offering first watch, mending [[Reef]]'s strap, praising one efficient movement in [[Mistle]]'s knife-work, defusing loot squabbles by redirecting attention — is framed as "a data probe, a gentle pressure applied to the system of another's personality to see how it reacts, where its weaknesses lie, and what it can be leveraged for."

Two-mask delivery system: the **Quiet Rat / Courteous Advisor** (disarming gentility, soft voice as attention-control, out-of-place noble etiquette) gathers data; the **Dispassionate Strategist** converts it into "win-win" plans carrying a private unstated benefit. Allies experience co-architecture; structurally they are scaffolding.

Cross-links: the Public-vs-True dual-reality bookkeeping in [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] and [[DivineDeceptionProtocol]] is the divine-scale version of the same pattern; [[SirensLeverage]] is the adjacent seduction-flavored variant in the Nocturne line.
