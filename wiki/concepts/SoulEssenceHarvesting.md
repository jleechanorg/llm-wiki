# Soul Essence Harvesting

Power-progression pattern in the [[Voyage]] BG3 campaign ([[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]): [[NocturneSosuke]] uses her abyssal lineage to consume the souls of powerful defeated entities, grafting their magic, martial vigor, and divine sparks into her own essence.

## Ledger in this campaign

| Victim | What was taken |
|---|---|
| [[WyllRavengard]] | Fading spark + eldritch energy (psychic siphon) |
| [[DrorRagzlin]] | Raw martial essence |
| [[PriestessGut]] | Psychic energy threads (emerald/violet) |
| [[LarinnaDuskweaver]] | Translucent essence wisp (victim aged/withered) |

## Cross-campaign pattern

This is the same consumption-ascension mechanic the player pursued in the Aizen god campaign as [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]] / [[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]] — a stable player preference (see the serpent-mastermind campaign-preferences profile): power gained by predatory absorption of named powerful beings, with vivid physical desecration of the victim, independent of platform or character.
