## Overview
Siren's Leverage is a signature trait of Nocturne Sosuke that provides a +2 bonus to Charisma checks (Deception and Persuasion) against humanoids who are sexually or romantically attracted to females. This makes her a "porcelain weapon" built for social combat in the Nocturne campaigns.

The 2026-08-04 Velvet Cage v4 ([[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]]) frames it as "a tactical biological weapon honed over 730 days of survival," confirms it stacks with Expertise, and operationalizes it across the whole module: every BG3 companion carries an explicit attraction flag determining whether the +2 applies (Jaheira and Minsc are the immune seats), and the Upper City random-encounter table has a per-row "Seduction Bonus?" column ([[BeautyAsWeapon]], [[CompanionContractRecruitment]]).
