# The Draconic Command & Scarcity Ledger

Custom subsystem from campaign visenya v8 governing [[Vermithrax]]. Its premise inverts the usual dragon-as-trump-card trope: because [[VisenyaBelaerys]] is a Level 6 baseline outsider, the dragon is a **constrained, expensive, discoverable asset**, not a free win button.

## Components

- **Command Check**: complex aerial maneuvers or targeting a specific non-infantry asset (e.g. a scorpion battery) costs a bonus action and a vocal High Valyrian command — Charisma (Animal Handling) vs base DC 14, +4 flat from the "Sovereior Singularity" trait [sic in source; likely "Sovereign Singularity"].
- **Logistical Upkeep**: exactly 1,000 gp/month for protein and concealment security (Medium-sized dragon).
- **Famine Inversion / *Famine of Fire***: treasury at 0 at end of an operational loop → dragon breaks containment, hunts smallfolk cattle → immediate −20 regional popularity/stability → localized counter-audit by [[JacaerysVelaryon]]'s investigators.

## Design notes

- Couples the economic layer to the secrecy layer: going broke doesn't just stall the player, it *actively generates discovery risk*, threading treasury management into the same exposure economy the [[SovereignShadowTracker]] tracks (where a Public Disclosure costs −20 SP — a deliberate numeric rhyme with the −20 stability hit).
- Loot economy is sized against it: a single Celtigar Harbor Ledger tribute (5,000 gp, see [[HouseCeltigar]]) buys five months of dragon concealment.
