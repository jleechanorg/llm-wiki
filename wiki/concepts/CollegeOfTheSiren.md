# College of the Siren

Custom Bard college used by [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] in the v5 campaign, half of a **Bard/Paladin Gestalt** at Level 12 (masquerading as a Level 1 commoner).

Distinguishing features:

- **No instrument**: the character's **Voice is the spellcasting focus**; magic is flavored as psionic and pheromonal, woven into speech and movement rather than performed music.
- **[[VelvetIron]]**: permanent Subtle Spell on Enchantment/Illusion — the subclass's defining stealth-casting feature.
- Governed by the [[TheMuse]] compulsion economy (psychic damage for crude violence, d12 Inspiration for social victories).

Compare v6's [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild]]: the same character line built as a combat-capable Sword bard versus v5's pure social-engineering Siren — the two subclasses encode the two halves of the [[DecadentConqueror]] archetype (blade versus whisper).
