# Kyōka Suigetsu

The signature **absolute-hypnosis** power of Bleach's Sosuke Aizen, imported wholesale into the [[AizenSosuke]] D&D 5e campaign as a persistent character ability that survives every restart, rebuild, and even ascension.

## How it works in the campaign

- Grants total sensory illusion control over anyone who has witnessed its "release," letting Aizen dictate what observers perceive — the mechanical backbone of the [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]] and [[Mask-of-Fragility]] plays of the mortal arc.
- In the chat 2 divine export it **remains active in Aizen's mortal disguise form**, stacking with [[DivineImposition]]'s DC 100 saves: perception control at the narrative layer, save-DC impossibility at the rules layer.

## Why it matters

It is the clearest crossover artifact in the campaign lineage: a fiction-derived power the LLM DM ([[Gemini]]) honors as a standing rule rather than a spell, illustrating how the player negotiates persistent custom mechanics ([[DestinyRulebookOverrides]]) on top of 5e.
