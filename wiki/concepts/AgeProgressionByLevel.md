# Age Progression By Level

House rule from the Aizen prequel campaign ([[2026-08-04-aizen-teen-campaign-export]]): **for every level the character gains, they age 1 year**, applied up to Level 10, after which age follows the campaign timeline normally. In the Aizen instance: Level 1 = age 10, Level 10 = age 20.

## Design function

The rule fuses mechanical progression with narrative coming-of-age: leveling *is* growing up, so the DM never has to arbitrate time-skips — XP milestones dictate them. It also caps the childhood arc (the interesting formative window of a [[PrequelOriginCampaign]]) at exactly nine levels before reverting to normal-time play.

## Known inconsistency

The v2 *Exiled Viper* sheet ([[2026-08-04-aizen-bg3-v2-exiled-viper-level4]]) presents Aizen at **age 16, Level 4**, but this rule yields age 13 at Level 4 (10 + 3 levels gained). Either the rule was revised between documents, off-screen years passed without levels (contradicting the pre-L10 coupling), or the v2 sheet silently rebased the ages.
