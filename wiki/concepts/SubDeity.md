# Sub-Deities

Under the Sovereign Protocol upgrade, key existing NPCs from the source campaign — partners, parents, lieutenants — are immediately elevated to **Sub-Deities** of the PC's new Hegemony, each assigned a specific portfolio (War, Logistics, Culture, Research) **matched to their personal history**. Assigning these portfolios is part of the mandatory opening objective ("stabilize the Sovereign Core") alongside surviving the first [[LogicSiege]] wave.

**Design significance**: continuity-preserving ascension — the campaign's social graph survives the jump to god-tier scope with relationships intact but re-expressed as divine administration, keeping earned NPC bonds mechanically relevant inside the [[SovereignHierarchy]].
