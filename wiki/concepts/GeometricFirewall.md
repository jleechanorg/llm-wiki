# Geometric Firewall

Campaign mechanic from [[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]]: [[Ao]]'s response to a player character who becomes too structurally entangled with reality to destroy.

## Mechanics

- **Trigger**: a being stops "playing the Great Game" and starts "deleting the board" — [[NocturneSosuke]] preparing to invade Mount Celestia.
- **Constraint**: destruction is impossible when the target has woven themselves into the fabric of the planes (killing Nocturne would collapse the Hells and Abyss into a black hole).
- **Effect**: sever the connection between the target's domain and the Material Plane; seal the target in a pocket dimension outside of time (the [[ShatteredRose]]) with internal dominion intact.
- **Loophole**: a **Material Anchor** — a mortal pact-bearer ([[SosukeAizen]] via the [[ObsidianMirrorPact]]) — remains the sole channel through the firewall, making the anchor the campaign's central strategic asset.

## Pattern significance

Extends the [[DivineDetectionTiers]]/[[DivineImposition]] escalation ladder with a terminal rung: not smiting but *quarantine*. Also a DM-side design solution to the runaway-ascension problem documented in [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]] — the overpowered character is preserved as lore and patron rather than retconned.
