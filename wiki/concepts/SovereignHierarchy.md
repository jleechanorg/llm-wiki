# Sovereign Hierarchy

The Sovereign Protocol's dynamically generated 1,000-entity ranking of [[Sovereign]]s, tone-matched to the source campaign and background-simulated each turn for top factions:

| Rank | Title | Holdings |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | **Emperors** | Continents (100k+ SP) — multiversal overlords; 2–3 authored to oppose the PC's [[SovereignLogic]] |
| 11–100 | **Conductors** | Nations (10k+ SP) — galactic-tier |
| 101–1000 | **Administrators** | Cities (100+ SP) — local entities; the PC starts in or below this bracket |

The PC's existing organization is elevated to a **Hegemony**, and established NPCs (partners, parents, lieutenants) become [[SubDeity|Sub-Deities]] with history-matched portfolios (War, Logistics, Culture, Research) — preserving the prior campaign's relationship web as the new realm's government.
