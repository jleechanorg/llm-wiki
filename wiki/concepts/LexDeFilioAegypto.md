# Lex de Filio Aegypto

The 43 BCE law by which [[GaiusJuliusCaesar]] formally legitimized [[Caesarion]] — his biological son with [[CleopatraVII]] — making him a Roman citizen and undisputed heir in the [[2026-08-04-pax-julia-eagles-brood-campaign-guide|Pax Julia]] timeline.

- Passed alongside Caesar's divorce of Calpurnia and marriage to Cleopatra.
- **Political fault line**: outrages the conservative aristocracy; wildly popular with the plebeians, who are captivated by the Egyptian queen's glamour and wealth.
- Establishes the legal foundation for the Julian-Ptolemaic dynasty and (with the 42 BCE death of [[GaiusOctavius]]) forecloses any alternative succession.
