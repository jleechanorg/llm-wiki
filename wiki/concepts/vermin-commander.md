## Summary
Level-2 ability unlocked by Nikolai (wood elf ranger) in the first Voyage dev playthrough. Lets the user command a swarm of rats to gather, perform menial tasks, or attack targets. Became the viral moment of the stream — the rat swarm later shredded three bandits in a single skirmish.

## Description (in-game)
> "I can compel someone to gather, hurt themselves, or perform menial tasks within a small area. Turn it in the past."

## Notable Moments
- Nikolai's initial rat summoning (whistle + rat-tide out of the floorboards)
- The rat heist in the Tower of Venture (rats scramble up Hestia's robes, steal a silver contract)
- 8-rat-per-bandit swarm attack in the Wolfwood (man howls, "defensive lines breaking instantly under the weight of the tiny, coordinated hunters")

## Naming Lore
- Named rats: Whiskers, Gray-face, Witter, Spot, Fred
- Unnamed: "rats one through end"
