# Mask of the Innocent

[[Alexiel]]'s public persona at the Jedi Academy: the "Repentant Child" — folded hands, deepest bows, perfect Code diction, a single calculated tear when caught (stated 90% success rate) — while the internal monologue stays cold and predatory (apologizing to [[Atris]] while assessing her podium's structural weak point).

**Design role**: the deception layer of the [[PowerlessMastermindBuild]]. Direct sibling of [[Mask-of-Fragility]] from the Ains campaign (which formalizes the same idea as a dynamic Deception DC); here it is pure roleplay contract plus the supervising institution's suspicion as the opposing force. The recurring pattern across campaigns: the protagonist's true stat is the gap between how they are perceived and what they are.
