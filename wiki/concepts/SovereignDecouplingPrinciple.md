# Sovereign Decoupling Principle

The central design pillar of [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]]: a hard mechanical separation between **intrinsic personal power** and **institutional/reputational power**.

## The rule

- Personal combat stats — smites, AC, spellcasting, lethality — are intrinsic to the character ([[NocturneRavencrest]]) and completely independent of public opinion.
- Exposure of treason costs only institutional power: rank, lands, titles, army command, political protection.
- Exposed or exalted, the character remains fully combat-capable; the campaign "never reduces personal power; it only changes the politics."

## Why it exists

Explicitly framed as the answer to campaign-creation pitfall **P24**, which forbids global aggregate suspicion trackers ("the realm suspects", "the court has grown suspicious"). Under this principle nothing aggregates: reputation is per-NPC witness persistence, combat power is intrinsic, and **the two never share a meter**.

## Related

Complements [[DualStackGameplay]] (the two decoupled stacks) and contrasts with the de-power openings of the [[NocturneSosuke]] BG3 v5 line, where losing power is the premise rather than the excluded failure mode.
