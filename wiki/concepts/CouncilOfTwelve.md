## Overview
The Council of Twelve is the Traditionalist faction in the House of the Dragon bible ([[2026-08-04-nocturne-old-republic-house-of-the-dragon]]) — the Dark Council old guard, led by [[DarthMarr]] (The Patriot) or [[DarthBaras]] (The Schemer).

## Ideology
The Empire exists to serve the Sith Code, not one man. They hate the [[GoldenLineage]] because bred demigod heirs end the Sith meritocracy: **power should be taken, not inherited.**

## Goal & methods
Prove the Emperor's children incompetent or heretical, forcing [[Vitiate]] to return power to the Council. Method against the PC: constant assassin "tests" of [[NocturneOldRepublic|Nocturne]].

## Connections
- Rival factions: [[ObsidianCircle]] (loyalists), [[TheEclipsed]] (nihilists)
