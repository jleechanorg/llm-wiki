# Powerless Mastermind Build

A character-design pattern from the [[2026-08-04-aizen-bg3-v2-exiled-viper-level4|Exiled Viper v2 sheet]]: keep a godhood-tier villain's *psychology* while stripping his *power*, enforced by strict RAW legality. Teen [[SosukeAizen]] is built with fully documented 27-point buy, racial bonuses, and a single Level 4 ASI — "No magical power-ups — just raw skill, crime, and psychological predation."

The pattern has three legs: (1) **RAW transparency** — every number is derived on-sheet, preventing DM/player drift toward the protagonist-favoritism the later campaigns fight via [[DestinyRulebookOverrides]]; (2) **capability substitution** — empire-building shifts from spell power to CHA-based social domination ([[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild]]) plus crime infrastructure ([[AcademyBlackmailFactory]]); (3) **calibrated opposition** — [[ChallengeCompensation]] scales threats so Level 4 stays tense against a post-BG3 world of L12 encounters. It is the mechanical expression of the prequel's [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] premise.
