# The Miasma

Device used in [[2026-08-04-aurelius-v1-pax-caesara|Aurelius v1]]: character sheets are written in two registers — "to the world, you are X" (public mask) versus "**to the Miasma**, you are Y" (true nature). For [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]] the pair is "The Sun of Rome" vs "The Devouring Maw."

- **Function**: the Miasma register is the address space of the character's real motives, visible to the DM/narrator layer but hidden from in-world observers — formalizing the corpus-wide two-mask construction (public persona / private predator) into an explicit sheet convention.
- **Relation to other campaigns**: same structural slot as the mask/true-self splits in [[2026-08-04-alexiel-swtor-siren-master-bible|Alexiel's Siren bible]] ("Wolf in the Fold") and the [[Mask of Fragility]] mechanic in the Ains campaign. Whether "the Miasma" is a named in-world cosmological entity in this setting (as opposed to purely a narration register) is not established by this v1 document.
