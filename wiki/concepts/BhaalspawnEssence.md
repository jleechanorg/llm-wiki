# Bhaalspawn Essence

The inherited divine bloodline of the dead god Bhaal, carried by [[SerpentQueen|the Serpent Queen]] in the BG1 Nocturne campaign ("the intrinsic power of your own mysterious bloodline"). Canonical to the [[BaldursGate]] saga; in this campaign it is reframed as a **research subject and ascension resource** rather than a tragic curse.

- Listed alongside forbidden knowledge and treasury potential as a pillar of [[PortableAbsolutePower]].
- Marks this incarnation's distinct route to divinity within the [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]] family: **inherited** essence, versus the engineered gestalt of [[Alexiel]] ([[VoidExperimentClass]]) and the consumption routes of [[NocturneSosuke]] ([[DivineAscensionByConsumption]], [[EssenceDevourer]]).
- [[Gorion]]'s guardianship in [[Candlekeep]] implicitly exists because of this bloodline, giving the protagonist's study of him an ironic double edge.
