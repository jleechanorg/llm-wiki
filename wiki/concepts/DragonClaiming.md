# Dragon Claiming

The act of bonding an unridden dragon — in Dance-of-the-Dragons-era campaigns, the single largest discontinuous jump in a character's military and political weight. A claimant goes from person to strategic asset in one scene.

## Across the Visenya campaign line

- **v3 (The Sowing)**: [[VisenyaBelaerys]] claims **Vermithor**, one of the largest living dragons, during Rhaenyra's dragonseed call.
- **v7**: [[VisenyaWaters|Visenya]] claims a **medium-sized dragon** — a deliberate calibration downward from v3's apex mount, trading raw scale for a more contested, growth-oriented arc while still making her "a serious battle power" for [[RhaenyraTargaryen]].

## Design notes

Using the claim as the *opening* scene (v7's explicit request) front-loads the power fantasy: the campaign starts at the moment of transformation rather than building toward it. The medium dragon size acts as an implicit level-appropriate cap — consistent with the wiki's observed pattern of tiered power gating in this user's campaigns (cf. [[AgeProgressionByLevel]], guardian level ceilings in the Ains module).
