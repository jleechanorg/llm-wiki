# Courtly Shadow Mask

The default social interface of Nocturne in [[2026-08-04-nocturne-tyranny-architecture-of-ascension|The Architecture of Ascension]]: soft-spoken, hyper-competent, intentionally unassuming, run on a "self-reliant" attachment script ("I'll give you access to my world, but I remain the orchestrator").

Two mechanisms:
1. **[[DependencyLoopManipulation]]** — front-loading value until targets cannot function without her.
2. **The Nihilistic Mirror** ([[JohanLiebert]]) — reading subconscious desires and reflecting them back so the target feels deeply seen, lowering defenses to expose the internal "switch" (trauma/desire) that makes them controllable. Demonstrated per-target: reverent child to [[GravenAshe]], admiring chaos-prodigy to the [[VoicesOfNerat]].

Sibling of the [[MaskOfFragility]] mechanic from the BG3 v2 module — same mask-architecture pattern, tuned for court politics instead of survival horror. Pairs with the "Deceptive Grime" visual layer (beauty deliberately obscured — inversion of [[BeautyAsWeapon]], holding beauty in reserve as a reveal).
