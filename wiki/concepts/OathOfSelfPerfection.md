# Oath of Self-Perfection (The Apex Predator)

Homebrew paladin archetype from the WorldAI campaign-prompts index, used by [[Gladius]] at level 5. The oath is sworn **to the self**: no fealty to gods or external powers, only to the character's own potential and "the brutal philosophy of self-maximization". Divine features are systematically re-skinned as willpower/intellect:

## L5 kit

- **Core aptitude**: Intelligence — **EP (Energy Point) pool = INT score × 2**
- **Fighting Style (Battle-Hardened)**: +1 Defense in armor
- **Extra Attack**: standard
- **Perfected Strike** (replaces Divine Smite): on melee hit, spend EP for force damage — 2d8 for 5 EP, +1d8 per additional 2 EP, max 5d8
- **Aura of Unyielding Dominance** (replaces Aura of Protection): 10-ft aura, advantage vs Frightened/Charmed for self+allies; enemies have disadvantage resisting your Intimidation
- **Apex Senses** (replaces Divine Sense): action → advantage on Perception/Investigation + pinpoint hidden/invisible creatures within 30 ft; 1 + INT-mod uses per long rest
- **Willpower Infusion** (adapted Lay on Hands): pool = 5 × character level; 1 pt heals 5 HP; 5 pts remove one Fatigue level; a Primary Action option is cut off mid-sentence in the source

## Design lineage

Part of the wiki's homebrew apex-class family — cf. [[ApexWeaver]], [[ImperatorClass]], and the "oath to the self" theme that recurs across the player's power-fantasy builds (see [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] for the divine-tier version). Uses the [[DestinyRuleset]]'s EP economy rather than spell slots.

## Known defect

The class is INT-keyed but was attached in-source to [[Gladius]], a character explicitly built with very low INT/WIS — and the section header reads "Nocturne's Class", indicating copy-forward from a [[NocturneRavencrest|Nocturne]] campaign document. Either the aptitude should be re-keyed (STR/CON) for Gladius or the stat line contradicts the kit.
