# Century of Schism (Years 1409–1509)

The present era of the [[WorldOfAssiah]]: a celestial civil war exactly 100 years old at campaign start. Triggered when [[Lucifer]], after the Revelation that the [[AbyssalWar]] was the Creator's engineered crucible ([[EngineeredCrucibleWar]]), declared the [[CelestialImperium]] a false idol (1409), purged loyalists from his legions in one night, and marched on [[Aeterna]].

Key early event: the [[MarchOfBrokenChains]] (1412), where Lucifer shattered the Imperial slave colonies to absorb freed mortals as infantry. The war is fought as "mathematics of slaughter" — logistics, attrition, and calculated terror including the [[FiftyPercentCullingDoctrine]] — rather than heroic charges. A 900-year demon war followed by a 100-year civil war gives both sides ancient, traumatized, uncompromising immortal commanders (the campaign's "Weight of Immortality" pillar).
