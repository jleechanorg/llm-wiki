# The Long Discord

The era-name for Visenya v5's central divergence: [[RhaegarTargaryen]] won the Battle of the Trident (283 AC) but **shattered only the rebellion's leadership, not its spirit**. Fifteen years later (298 AC) the war has never ended. Westeros is partitioned — Targaryens hold King's Landing and the southern breadbasket; the North, Riverlands, and Stormlands are entrenched hostile nations. The Iron Throne is a bankrupt military junta's command center, the Iron Bank circles, and the smallfolk pray the dragons do not look at them. Its steady-state combat rhythm is [[TheGrinding]]; its northern frontier was frozen by the [[CeasefireOfBones]].

Design note: this inverts the usual "Rhaegar wins" wish-fulfillment AU — victory produces a dystopian occupation state, giving the Apex protagonist ([[VisenyaTargaryen]]) a broken system to out-think rather than a court to charm.
