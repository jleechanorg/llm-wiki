# Divine Power Points (DPP)

Resource currency governing divine-scale acts in the [[Aizen]] god campaign. DPP converts the god tier's otherwise unbounded power into a budgeted economy with meaningful trade-offs.

## Documented pricing (entries 901-1147)

- **Free**: influencing individual common mortals — automatic, no cost.
- **50 DPP per wave/group**: Major Domain Manipulation — widespread, simultaneous telepathic imposition over numerous "higher echelons" or a large organization (e.g. all remaining [[Bane]] cultists not already controlled). See [[Telepathic Domain Usurpation]].

## Design role

DPP is the campaign's answer to the god-tier balance problem: with Divine Save DC 100 making individual resistance impossible, the constraint moves from *can it be done* to *what does scale cost*, paired with a second non-monetary cost — divine detection risk from [[Mystra]], [[Shar]], and [[Ao]]. Together they keep strategic choice alive after ascension, analogous to how the [[Sanctuary Imperative]] priced moral weight at the mortal tier.
