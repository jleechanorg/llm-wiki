# Subtle Influence Campaign Hook

DM technique in [[ShadowOverTheGate]]: regardless of which of the four [[ANightOfUnrest]] entry scenarios the PCs take, they encounter the same signature — antagonists (gang members, abductors, cultists) displaying "unusual efficiency" and discipline uncharacteristic of ordinary criminals.

The signature is the perceptible edge of [[AizenSosuke]]'s hidden coordination: House Aizen's influence "could extend into any or all of the other factions," so the mastermind is seeded as an environmental pattern before being introduced as a character. Players discover the villain by noticing statistical anomalies in enemy behavior — a mystery-first reveal structure.

Related: [[NarrativeHookScenarios]] (the four convergent entry points), [[PreemptiveFrameControl]] (Aizen's own version of controlling perception before contact).
