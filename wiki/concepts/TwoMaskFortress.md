# Two-Mask Fortress

[[Sariel]]'s dual-persona system — "a fortress constructed with two masterfully crafted outer walls that conceal the true, singular nature of the woman within."

1. **Silent Servant** (public): soft musical voice that forces the powerful to lean in; etiquette too noble for a bodyguard, deliberately off-balancing; anticipatory service to [[NohTaeNam]]. Every servile gesture doubles as a *data probe* mapping the weaknesses of other personalities.
2. **Dispassionate Strategist** (private): beautifully structured win-win plans that align with the master's stated goals while banking unstated private benefits; post-hoc flattery ("praise his courage") that makes the puppet feel like co-architect.

Related: [[MaskOfFragility]] (the Ains campaign's deception-DC analog — dynamic mask mechanics recur across campaigns) and [[BeautyAsWeapon]] (here inverted: beauty concealed as camouflage rather than deployed as leverage).
