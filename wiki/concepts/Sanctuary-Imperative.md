# Sanctuary Imperative

Dual-track morality mechanic from the [[2026-08-04-shy-lich-overlord-v2|shy-lich Overlord module]]: **Psychic Strain** accrues from morally heavy acts (unnecessary slaughter is explicitly punished), while **Divine Serenity** rewards non-lethal problem-solving. Because the module's Universal Sentience rule makes every NPC fully sentient, there are no "free" kills — all violence carries meterable moral weight for [[Ains-Ooal-Gown]].

**Design role**: where [[Mask-of-Fragility]] prices *visible* power and [[Whispers-of-the-Realm]] prices *noticed* power, the Sanctuary Imperative prices power's *moral use* — a conscience dial completing the module's three-dial tension system. Contrast the corruption-direction meters elsewhere in the corpus (e.g. [[EntropyToll]], [[AscensionMeter]]), which typically reward transgression; this one rewards restraint.
