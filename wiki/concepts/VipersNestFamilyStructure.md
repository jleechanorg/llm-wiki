# The Viper's Nest Family Structure

A [[CampaignTemplate]] Section 5 requirement with unusually rigid arithmetic: the protagonist's family must contain **two Titan parents (Level 20+)** who overshadow the PC, plus **exactly two older brothers and two older sisters**, each with a distinct personality, a Mask ([[MasksPublicFaceTrueNature]]), and a defined dynamic with the protagonist — Rival, Ally, or Pawn.

The structure guarantees the protagonist starts as the youngest and nominally weakest member of a powerful house, making the power fantasy an *ascent within the family* rather than from obscurity — visible in the Visenya campaigns' positioning of [[VisenyaTargaryen]] as a child prodigy inside a ruling dynasty overshadowed by figures like [[RhaegarTargaryen]]. The "Pawn" option also builds manipulation targets directly into the starting cast.
