# Gloom Stalker Conclave

D&D 5e Ranger subclass functioning as the wiki's recurring **ambush-predator chassis** for stealth-assassin protagonists.

## Kit as used in the Silent Blade campaign

- **Umbral Sight** — darkvision invisibility to darkvision; "you can see them, but they cannot see you."
- **Dread Ambusher** — bonus first-round action/attack; framed as an "explosion of violence" that ends fights pre-emptively.
- **Disguise Self** (Gloom Stalker spell list) — reframed from concealment to *infiltration personas* (barmaid, noblewoman, distressed traveler) that close to knife range.

## Recurrence across campaigns

Second consecutive Gloom Stalker ingest: Visenya v9 (2026-08-04) runs a Level 6 Gloom Stalker/Assassin gestalt with [[StressLineSight]]; the Silent Blade runs a straight Level 5 Gloom Stalker with social-infiltration emphasis. The repeated selection indicates a stable player preference for first-strike stealth chassis, with variation expressed through the *social* layer (apex-stalker hunting vs [[WeaponizedIntimacy]]) rather than the combat layer.
