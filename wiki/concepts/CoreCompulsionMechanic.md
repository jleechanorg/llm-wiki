# Core Compulsion Mechanic

A [[CampaignTemplate]] Section 2 requirement: the protagonist must have a specific psychological hunger (example given: "Breaking Authority") expressed as a **mechanic**, not just flavor — with an explicit failure penalty and success bonus (template example: Failure Penalty = Exhaustion, Success Bonus = Temp HP).

This binds the character's psychology to the dice: indulging the compulsion is mechanically rewarded, being denied it mechanically costs. Instances across the corpus include the Ains module's [[Sanctuary Imperative]] (psychic strain tracking moral weight) and the sadism-dice mechanics noted in the Visenya v6 bible — both are compulsion mechanics instantiated for their protagonists. Related: [[MasksPublicFaceTrueNature]], which the compulsion threatens to expose.
