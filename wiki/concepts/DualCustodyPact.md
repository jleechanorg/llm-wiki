# Dual-Custody Pact

The alliance structure at the heart of [[2026-08-04-nocturne-warcraft3-deceivers-crown|The Deceiver's Crown]]: [[MalGanis]] bound to [[NocturneRavencrest]] **as partner-in-finance, not as patron/servant**.

## The single clause

When one party tries to dominate the other, **both die**. Custody of the alliance is shared; sovereignty is the prize each pursues without being able to seize it by force.

## Design significance

Inverts the standard warlock-patron power gradient (contrast the [[Malcanthet]] patron relationship in the [[NocturneSosuke]] v5 line): the mutual-destruction clause makes the pact a symmetric game-theoretic standoff rather than a leash, which keeps a sixteen-year-old mortal and an elder Nathrezim at the same table for a whole campaign.
