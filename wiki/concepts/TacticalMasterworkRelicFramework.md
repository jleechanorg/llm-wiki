# Tactical Masterwork / Relic Framework

Sub-Template C of [[CampaignBibleTemplateV4]]: the four-part spec for magical or masterwork items in a campaign's panoply and loot tables.

1. **Item Name and Classification** — true name, legendary titles, base item type
2. **Aesthetic and Material** — exact appearance, components, weight, age, tactile feel
3. **Mythic Origin** — who forged it, the battles/betrayals it witnessed, how it was acquired
4. **System Metrics (5e integration)**:
   - *Passive Property* — a continuous narrative/sensory/exploratory boon
   - *Active Tactical Feature* — high-impact ability with explicit action-economy cost, variable daily uses or recharge dice, and scaling DC calculations
   - *Narrative Side Effect / Behavioral Urge* — a thematic drawback or psychological pull imposed on the attuner

## Why it matters

The framework forbids mechanically flat loot: every relic must carry lore weight, crunchy 5e math, AND a psychological cost. The mandatory behavioral-urge clause means items double as character-pressure devices — the same corruption-through-power theme the campaign bibles pursue narratively (e.g. the descent mechanics in the [[NocturneSosuke]] Velvet Cage line).
