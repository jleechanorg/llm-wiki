# Consent-Gated Time Skips

Campaign-prompt pattern from the [[Gladius]] merc-brute prompt in the WorldAI campaign-prompts index. Three coupled rules:

1. **Engine-initiated**: the DM engine identifies sensible narrative points to offer a **one-year time skip** — pacing responsibility sits with the engine, not the player.
2. **Explicit consent required**: the skip only executes after the player explicitly agrees; proposing is free, executing is gated.
3. **Autonomous simulation during the skip**: the character is assumed to be "autonomously doing sensible things" off-screen, and the engine must simulate those outcomes — the skip advances the world-state and the character's holdings, not just the calendar.

## Why it matters

This is a player-authority mechanic in the same family as the plot-arc-choice-without-spoilers directive in the [[Shadowheart]] prompt: the engine surfaces meaningful decisions but never takes irreversible narrative actions unilaterally. For accumulation-goal campaigns (power/wealth/influence), rule 3 is load-bearing — a skip that only advances time without simulating autonomous accumulation would silently zero out a year of the character's core activity.
