# Veil Monopoly

Cosmological premise of "Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse": by consuming [[LucianTheDivine]] and [[DallisTheHammer]], the protagonist **monopolized Rivellon's cosmic lifeblood** — the Veil that keeps the world intact is no longer anchored by a pantheon or by sacrifices of the pure, but **tethered exclusively to the protagonist's living soul**.

## Campaign implications
- Built-in stakes: the world's integrity depends on the tyrant's survival, making the PC both apex threat and load-bearing pillar
- Structural sibling of other "world tied to the protagonist" engines in the collection ([[ProjectApotheosis]], the Ains Sanctuary Imperative) — the player repeatedly selects frames where personal power and cosmic responsibility are the same stat
