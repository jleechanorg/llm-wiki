Proof Assistant is a generic term for formal verification tools like [Lean](Lean.md), [Coq](Coq.md), and Isabelle that enable interactive theorem proving. They allow users to state theorems and prove them mechanically.

## Key Properties
- **Interactive**: Human and AI collaborate to construct proofs
- **Machine-checked**: Every step of the proof is verified by the tool
- **Expressiveness**: Can represent complex mathematical structures and program properties
- ** [Lean](Lean.md) and [Coq](Coq.md) are the most widely used in AI research contexts

- [FormalVerification](FormalVerification.md) — proof assistants are the tools of formal verification
