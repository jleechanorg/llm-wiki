# Sensory Feast Compulsion

Core drive/flaw defined for [[AureliusCaesar]] in the v3 bible: the character is structurally **bored by abstraction** (dry reports, silent rooms) and compelled toward **Intensity** — feeling the weight of a marching legion, tactile claiming of objects and people, vintage wine smelled before drunk. The personality spec calls his inner identity "the Devouring Maw", "a singularity of Appetite": the universe as a banquet where everyone is guest or course.

As a design pattern, this gives a Lawful Evil character a *non-strategic* motivation — he climbs the Cursus Honorum "not because it is logical, but because the view from the top is the only one that satisfies" — deliberately distinguishing him from the calculating INTJ masterminds of this user's other campaigns ([[user_campaign_preferences]] pattern). The compulsion doubles as a DM hook: intensity-craving is an exploitable vulnerability that pure calculation would not have. The source document truncates mid-way through this section.
