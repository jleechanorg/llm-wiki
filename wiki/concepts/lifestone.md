## Summary
Refined, violet-veined crystal of immense arcane value. The contraband that drives the Alexiel campaign's smuggling plot. Sourced from the Sunken Vaults and dark-elf mines, stamped with the jagged-sun crest of House Tethlar (or the rival Sunken Vaults houses), moved via Daran's Guild through the Silverflow tunnels. Pure lifestone 'hums with a low frequency that Alexiel feels in the marrow of her bones.' Highly refined lifestone is described as the 'blood of the earth.'

## Notable Instances
- Turn 8–24: The first lifestone crate is recovered from Hobb's cellar, triggering the central smuggling plot
- Turn 18: Alexiel discovers the crates bear a noble house crest tied to Tethlar debt notes
- Turn 32: Lifestone trade formalized at 12% commission
- The 25-Gloomstalker deployment (Turn 130) is partly justified by the need to protect lifestone shipments
