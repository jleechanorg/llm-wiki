## Definition
Multi-layer concealment system allowing nascent deities to hide their true power from pantheon detection. Required for survival in [[DivineAscensionCeremony]].

## Layers (original three-layer form)
1. **Layer 0 - The Mask**: Mortal identity, mortal physics apply
2. **Layer 1 - The Persona**: Fabricated deity identity with church and myth (unlocked at Tier 2)
3. **Layer 2 - The Source**: True cosmic self, revealed only on "Drop the Mask"

## Key Mechanic
Using divine power while wearing the Mortal Mask creates **Divine Dissonance** — detectable by pantheon entities.

## V0 four-layer successor

The god-campaign prompt upgrade ([[2026-08-04-god-campaign-prompt-v0-divine-deception]]) expands this to the [[FourLayersOfExistence]]: it splits the god-facing identity into a disposable **False Deity** (Rank Y) and an officially seated **Primary Mask** (Rank Z), quantifies [[DivineDissonance]] as a 0–100% meter with four detection thresholds, and adds explicit per-rank stat rows ([[QuantifiedDivineReferenceTable]]) with [[Ao]] as silent sanctioner of the hidden Rank A truth.
