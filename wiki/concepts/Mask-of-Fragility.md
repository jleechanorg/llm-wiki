# Mask of Fragility

Deception mechanic from the [[2026-08-04-shy-lich-overlord-v2|shy-lich Overlord module]] that turns "pretending to be weak" into a live skill system rather than narration:

- **Base DC scales with the PC's spell save DC** — the stronger [[Ains-Ooal-Gown]] actually is, the harder his weakness act is to sell.
- **Visible power use imposes penalties** (the module's DPP-style penalty pool), so every overt spell or feat degrades the mask.
- Maintaining cover is resolved by **deception checks** against the dynamic DC.

**Lineage**: the mechanical core of the wiki's masked-power family — cf. [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] (the strategic pattern), [[WeaknessMaskingDiplomacy]], and [[BeautyAsWeapon]]'s note that this campaign line weaponizes *weakness* where others weaponize allure. Failures here feed the [[Whispers-of-the-Realm]] aggro track.
