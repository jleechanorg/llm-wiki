# Cross-Platform Ascension Pattern

Meta-observation from [[2026-08-04-bg3-voyage-campaign-summary]]: the player's consumption-based power-accretion arc reproduces **identically across unrelated platforms and characters**, which shifts its attribution from platform affordance to stable player preference.

## Evidence

- **Gemini / Aizen lineage**: [[SosukeAizen]]'s god campaigns — [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]], [[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]], [[SoulEssenceHarvesting]] of divine sparks.
- **Voyage / Nocturne**: [[NocturneSosuke]] harvests four named souls ([[WyllRavengard]], [[DrorRagzlin]], [[PriestessGut]], [[LarinnaDuskweaver]]) to graft their magic and sparks — same predator-ascension arc, different engine, different character, same [[house-sosuke]] lineage naming.

## Implication

When a behavior pattern survives a platform change, it is a **player signature**, not an engine artifact. This matches the documented campaign-preference profile (serpent-mastermind, manipulation, moral corruption) and means AI-DM platform evaluations by this player will consistently stress-test soul-consumption, essence-grafting, and epic-tier scaling mechanics — a useful known-load for comparing platforms like [[Voyage]], [[AIDungeon]], and the [[Gemini]] DM setup.
