# Campaign Bible Nine-Section Structure

The mandated skeleton every campaign bible generated by the [[CampaignTemplate]] must follow, in order:

1. **Campaign Intro** — title, world twist, power-fantasy Hook ("conquering, not saving").
2. **Character Personality** — archetype with MBTI hinge, sensory signature, [[MasksPublicFaceTrueNature]], [[CoreCompulsionMechanic]], interaction shorthand, 3 inner monologues.
3. **Character Class** — renamed/Gestalt class, one game-breaking unique mechanic (e.g. [[Auctoritas]], "Soul Thief"), Levels 1–30 progression with Epic Boons.
4. **Assets & Retinue** — rank, wealth, safehouses, blackmail leverage, a named Panoply of 3–4 items, 3 NPC subordinates with Loyalty rationales.
5. **Family** — the [[VipersNestFamilyStructure]].
6. **Factions** — 10 noble houses with exploitable Hooks, 10 friendly tool-factions, 10 structural/humanoid antagonist factions.
7. **World Lore** — timeline with Divergence Point, mythos, current crisis, 3 escalating arcs.
8. **Gazetteer & Mechanics** — 4 stages with Vibe + Hazard, 2 custom systems, 8-entry narrative-power loot table.
9. **Starting Scene** — atmosphere, immediate hook, A/B/C authority-demonstrating first choice.

## Diagnostic value

When a wiki source is a partial campaign document (e.g. the Visenya v8 "Section 8" continuation), this structure identifies which sections exist elsewhere and what a complete bible should contain. Shared features across campaigns (alt-history divergence points, sibling rival webs, custom trackers, relic tables) are template requirements, not per-campaign inventions.
