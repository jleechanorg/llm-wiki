# College of Infernal Contracts (Revised — "The Legion Update")

Homebrew Bard subclass from [[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage]], the revised form of [[CollegeOfPerdition]] for the 2026-08-04 Velvet Cage module. Design thesis: "power is a pyramid scheme" — the bard manages an *organization*, not a party.

## Features

- **Level 3 — The Black Ledger**: unlimited creatures can sign as **Signatories** (1-minute willing/coerced ritual, invisible mark); but **Active** Signatories are capped at Bard level + CHA modifier, prepared per long rest (~6-7 at L3, ~25 at L20).
- **Level 3 — Terms of Service**: Clause of Collateral (Reaction: transfer incoming damage to an Active Signatory as force damage, take 0), Clause of Extension (cast through a Signatory's position/line of sight), Clause of Tithe (intercept half of any healing a Signatory receives).
- **Level 6 — Breach Protocols**: *Liquidation of Assets* — cast without a spell slot by dealing 10 × spell level unreducible necrotic to a Signatory; if they drop to 0 HP the spell upcasts by one level. Plus Non-Compete (Signatories can't willingly attack you).
- **Level 14 — Hostile Takeover**: Golden Parachute (at 0 HP, drain any Active Signatories within a mile to full, voiding their contracts) and Forced Arbitration (no-Concentration Dominate that makes the target a temporary Signatory — enabling the loop of fueling spells with the dominated boss's own HP).

## Analysis

The in-document meta note is explicit about the exploit surface: sign a village, activate the 20 healthiest each morning, and walk around "with 200+ buffer HP at level 5." Narratively justified as a "Spark of Archdevil authority" stolen from [[Zariel]] via the [[InversionRitual]] — gang leader → cult leader → general, "From Ruin to Archdevil." Companions recruited under [[CompanionContractRecruitment]] serve as the elite always-Active tier.
