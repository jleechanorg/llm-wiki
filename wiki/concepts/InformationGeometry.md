# Information Geometry

The v6 campaign's reskin of the [[ApexWeaver]]'s power source: instead of magical songs, [[VisenyaTargaryen]] perceives "the stress lines, pulse rates, and mathematical architecture of the physical and social world." Spellcasting is framed as applied analysis. This continues the Visenya line's pattern of grounding overwhelming power in cognition rather than spectacle — v2's subtle-magic wizard, v3's blood-instinct sorcery, v6's pure information dominance — keeping the [[ApexBloodline]] hidden in social spaces.
