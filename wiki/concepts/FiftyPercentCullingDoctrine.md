# The 50% Culling Doctrine

War doctrine named in the [[WorldOfAssiah]] campaign guide's "Mathematics of Slaughter" pillar: warfare in the [[CenturyOfSchism]] is logistics, attrition, and the *calculated application of terror*, of which the 50% Culling Doctrine is the named instrument. Part 1 introduces the doctrine by name; its detailed mechanics presumably follow in later installments.

Its function in play is to force [[Alexiel]] into the [[MaskAndMonster]] bind — complicity in institutional atrocity versus hidden empathy — with every sparing or burning permanently recorded by the [[HighPersistenceWorldState]] world model.
