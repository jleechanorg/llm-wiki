# The Grand Game

Post-victory narrative framework from the Aizen god-campaign: having perfected reality as The Absolute, [[AizenSosuke]] is **bored by a solved world** and restarts conflict as a game with himself as secret director.

## Structure

- The winner does not rule openly; he authors events from hiding while the world believes other actors are driving history.
- First move in the summary: elevate [[GaleOfWaterdeep]] to God of Magic and script his public narrative (the false [[Mystra]]-consumption claim — see [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]]).
- Solves the "what does a campaign do after total victory" problem: play continues one level down, with the PC as DM-within-the-fiction.
