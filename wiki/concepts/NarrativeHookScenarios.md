# Narrative Hook Scenarios

Campaign-opening design pattern: offer N parallel starting scenarios, each placing a PC in a different social stratum and city location, all engineered to converge on one plot. In [[ShadowOverTheGate]]'s [[ANightOfUnrest]] opening, the four hooks cover the tavern floor ([[ElfsongTavern]] brawl), the docks (abduction), the Upper City (noble's plea), and the institutions ([[FlamingFist]] patrol) — criminal, mercantile, aristocratic, and state perspectives on the same conspiracy.

Convergence is enforced by a shared clue signature (the [[SubtleInfluenceCampaignHook]] "unusual efficiency" thread) rather than by railroading locations, so player agency in the opening is real but the campaign spine is protected.
