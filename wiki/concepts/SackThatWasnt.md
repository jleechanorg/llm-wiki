# The Sack that Wasn't

Visenya v5's second divergence point: with [[RobertBaratheon]] dead, [[TywinLannister]] arrived at King's Landing in 283 AC **to secure the city for the victors instead of sacking it** — pledging to the living [[RhaegarTargaryen]] at the price of Hand of the King **for life**. Consequences cascade: no Sack, no planned murder of Elia (she dies later in a skirmish; Rhaegar executes the Mountain to appease Dorne), [[AerysIITargaryen]] imprisoned rather than killed, and a Lannister power center permanently welded into the junta. Illustrates the campaign line's signature move: one battlefield reversal rewritten into institutional consequences rather than a clean happy ending.
