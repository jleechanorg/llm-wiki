# Whispers of the Realm

Narrative-aggro mechanic from the [[2026-08-04-shy-lich-overlord-v2|shy-lich Overlord module]]: the outside world's awareness of [[Nazarick]] and its master advances through **four escalating states**:

1. **Quiet Ignorance** — nobody is looking.
2. **Restless Border Whispers** — rumors circulate.
3. **The Gathering Storm** — organized investigation/mobilization.
4. **The Eclipse** — full realm-scale confrontation.

Power leakage — failed [[Mask-of-Fragility]] checks, overt displays, large operations — pushes the state forward; discretion holds or lowers it.

**Lineage**: a member of the wiki's family of visibility-tension dials — [[AggroMeter]] (multiversal expansion velocity) and [[AscensionMeter]] both cite it as kin — all pricing power gain in enemy attention, but this one is discrete-state and narrative-triggered rather than a 0-10 numeric meter.
