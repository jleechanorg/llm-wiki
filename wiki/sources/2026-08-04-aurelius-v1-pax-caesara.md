# Campaign Bible: The Iron Eagle — Pax Caesara (v1)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign aurelius v1" — a v1 campaign intro plus master personality sheet for a Roman alternate-history campaign. Point of divergence: **Julius Caesar survived the Ides of March**; by 40 BCE the Republic has mutated into a monolithic autocratic Empire under "Perpetual Dictatorship," ruled by [[JuliusCaesar]] as living God-Emperor. The player character is [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]], the God-Emperor's youngest and most ambitious son.

## Campaign frame

- **Setting**: [[PaxCaesaraEmpire]], 40 BCE. Civil wars crushed; the peace is described as "a suffocating weight." A gilded, politically treacherous Rome.
- **Premise**: Explicitly a **Lawful Evil conquest campaign** — "You are not a hero saving the world; you are a tyrant-in-training perfecting the art of rule."
- **Three arenas**: (1) court politics of gilded Rome, (2) commanding legions against barbarian tides, (3) a [[FratricidalColdWar]] against his own brothers to prove sole worthiness of the name Caesar.
- **Advancement axis**: climbing the Cursus Honorum by "forcing the world to acknowledge your supremacy" rather than waiting his turn.

## Character architecture — Aurelius Julia Caesar

The sheet uses the same public-mask / true-self dual-layer construction seen across this campaign family, here framed as "to the world" vs "to the [[TheMiasma|Miasma]]":

- **Public face — "The Sun of Rome" / Prince of Pleasure**: the most vibrant, charismatic member of the Julian dynasty; lavish tastes, loud laughter, open weeping at theater tragedies; sells ambition wrapped in "Passion" and "Patriotism." People follow his warmth, not his fear.
- **True nature — "The Devouring Maw" / Golden Tyrant**: a singularity of Appetite and absolute Entitlement. "The universe is a banquet table set specifically for him." He crushes rebellion because chaos is *ugly* — an aesthetic offense — and his sadism is emotional: he wants to watch hope break because it validates supremacy.
- **Archetype**: **ESFP-ENTJ hinge** ("The Performer / The King"), labeled the [[DecadentConqueror]]. Tactile and sensory — constantly touching statues, wine, subordinates' shoulders as gestures of *ownership*.
- **The Blood of Venus**: described as the most beautiful man in the Empire — spun-electrum hair, porcelain skin with inner bioluminescence, liquid-amber eyes. This is systematized as [[BeautyAsWeapon]]: veterans hesitate to strike him ("it feels like vandalism"), rivals forgive cruelties, and his Auctoritas manifests as literal *heat* (summer-day warmth when pleased, open-furnace when angry).
- **Core compulsion — the [[SensoryFeast]]**: craves Intensity over administration; wants to *feel* a legion marching at his command, *smell* Senate fear, *taste* a rival's despair. Ultimate stated target: "The World Soul" — turning the Roman Empire into his personal pleasure garden (document truncates mid-word here).

## Position in the campaign corpus

This bible extends the user's established tyrant/mastermind protagonist family (see [[user-campaign-preferences]] themes: manipulation, moral corruption, power-fantasy infrastructure) into a historical-Rome skin, and pairs with the earlier Rome-flavored faction work in [[FactionPoliticsRome]]. Notably it **breaks the INTJ serpent-mastermind norm** documented across prior campaigns ([[SosukeAizen]], [[Alexiel]]): Aurelius is an ESFP-ENTJ *sensualist* tyrant whose power instrument is charisma, appetite, and weaponized beauty rather than cold calculation — appetite-driven evil versus scheme-driven evil. The dual-mask device (public performer / private predator) and the "Miasma" true-self framing are continuous with the rest of the corpus.
