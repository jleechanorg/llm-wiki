# BG3 campaign: Empire of Shadow — *The Aftermath* (v0)

**Source**: Campaign setup document (v0) titled "BALDUR'S GATE 3: THE AFTERMATH — A CAMPAIGN OF CHAOS & SURVIVAL". A world-state bible for a post-Netherbrain dark timeline: the Netherbrain is dead, but the saviors of [[BaldursGate]] "did not liberate the city; they merely reorganized its chains." This is a pure instance of the [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] pattern — victory over the crisis villain followed by the victors becoming the new tyrants — and of [[DefinitiveCompanionFates]]: every major BG3 figure is assigned a definitive, usually tragic, endpoint.

## The Triumvirate of the Absolute's Bane

Three former companions carve the Sword Coast into empires of blood, shadow, and arcana ([[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]]):

- **[[Astarion]] (The Ascended Lord)** — L12+ Rogue (Thief)/Ranger (Gloom Stalker). Completed the [[RiteOfProfaneAscension]]; rules the night from the rebuilt Szarr Palace, controlling economy, politics, and underworld. Bhaalist Armour + Crimson Mischief/Bloodthirst dual blades. His vampiric spawn replace the Parliament of Peers in the Upper City.
- **[[Shadowheart]] (Mother Superior of Loss)** — L12+ Cleric (Trickery)/Paladin (Oathbreaker). Fully embraced Shar, slaughtered the Nightsong, usurped the House of Grief. Dark Justiciar Half-Plate, Viconia's Walking Fortress, Spear of Evening. Lower City is a Sharran theocracy: memories extracted via the Mirror of Loss, dissenters dragged to the Shadowfell.
- **[[GaleOfWaterdeep]] (God of Ambition)** — L12+ Wizard (Evocation)/Sorcerer (Wild Magic). Reforged the [[CrownOfKarsus]] into pseudo-divinity; floats above the city in a citadel carved from Ramazith's Tower (Robe of the Weave, Markoheshkir), using the populace as test subjects for experimental Netherese weaving.

## Fates of the remaining companions

- **[[Laezel]] (The Astral Scourge)** — L12+ Battle Master; rejected Vlaakith, wages a suicidal Astral Sea guerrilla war against both the Lich-Queen and illithid remnants with the Silver Sword of the Astral Plane and Helldusk Armour.
- **[[Wyll]] (The Broken Blade)** — L12+ Warlock (Fiend)/Paladin (Devotion); pact unbroken, father dead via Astarion's political maneuvering, trapped in Avernus as Mizora's attack dog, soul calcifying into a Lemure.
- **[[Karlach]] (The Mind Flayer Rebel)** — L12+ Barbarian (Wild Magic)/Illithid; became illithid to kill the brain, now hides in the sewers eating the brains of Astarion's rogue spawn, humanity fading.
- **[[Minthara]] (The High General)** — L12+ Paladin (Vengeance); Astarion's military enforcer commanding the "Night Watch" legion of spawn and Drow (Armour of Persistence, Mace of the Believer).
- **[[Halsin]] (The Shadow-Touched)** — spirit broken as Sharran darkness corrupted Thaniel; roams the Cloakwood as a rabid shadow-touched beast.
- **[[Jaheira]] (The Risen Martyr)** — organized the Harpers against the Triumvirate, was ambushed by Astarion and Minthara, slain, and raised as a mindless vampire spawn serving drinks at Astarion's galas.
- **[[Minsc]] (The Stone Sentinel)** — petrified by Gale for being "disruptive to the arcane equilibrium"; stands as a garden ornament beneath Gale's floating tower.
- **[[Withers]] (Jergal)** — disgusted, abandoned the party; records the Sword Coast's collapse into the Book of the Dead from a forgotten tomb.
- **[[Raphael]]** — slain in the House of Hope, but his essence is slowly reforming in the Nine Hells, plotting (source truncates mid-sentence here).

## Relationship to the Aizen campaign lineage

This is a **separate continuity** from the [[AizenSosuke]] campaigns (e.g. [[ShadowOverTheGate]]): both are player campaigns set in post-crisis Baldur's Gate, but here the city is ruled by the Triumvirate rather than by Aizen's factions. The shared aesthetic — evil victors, definitive tragic fates, level-12+ statted rulers — matches the player's documented taste profile (serpent-mastermind, moral corruption, D&D adapting everything).
