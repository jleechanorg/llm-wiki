## Summary

User asked a custom Gemini **Gem** (lightweight replica of WorldArchitect.AI, labeled "World AI") to analyse why the Vespera Thul BG3 campaign was not challenging, then restart it under an *Ascendant Difficulty Protocol*. The Gem diagnoses four root causes (paper-tiger NPCs, social-mechanics inflation, uncapped faction power, and uncapped resource acceleration) and imposes a new rule set: contextual social DC scaling (45–65+ for peer-tier and cosmic targets), Crown-of-Ambition risk/reward mask enforcement with Dissonance Overflow on exposure, soul-essence and Divine Dissonance as explicit spend-currencies, and a fully sentient Cult of Ambition instead of mindless automatons. The user plays through ~75 turns ending at the Evershade Grand Gala planning block, choosing Debt-Trap / Sovereign Marriage / Gala-Ultimatum options for the Lord Rivington contract.

## Key Claims (difficulty analysis)

- High-tier NPCs in the prior run had CR/level 18+ but were narratively bypassed or god-moded, skipping their mechanical AC / social HP; the new run requires ≥220 HP and Legendary Resistance for CR 12+ targets.
- Charisma-based checks were hitting 35+ with stacked Advantage and flat modifiers, making social success near-guaranteed. The new run scales DCs to 45–65+ for peer-tier (Level 25+) and cosmic (Level 30+) targets with Legendary Social Resistance.
- Faction Power received a +7.5M Void-Titan Singularity modifier that catapulted Vespera to Global Rank #2 in a single step, with the father (Archmage Malichor) absorbing 100% of Divine Dissonance as a public beacon. The new run is mid-mask: active Level 5 Initiate mask must not exceed its operational stat boundaries, or Dissonance Overflow risks Mystra/Celestial-Host exposure.
- Uncapped full-caster gestalt + unlimited spell slots <9th level + automated soul harvest eliminated bottlenecks; the new run reintroduces resource scarcity.
