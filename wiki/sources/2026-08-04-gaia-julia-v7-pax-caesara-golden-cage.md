# Campaign Bible: The Pax Caesara — Architect of the Golden Cage (campaign Gaia Julia v7)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Gaia Julia v7" (Sections 1-3) — seventh entry in the Gaia Julia alternate-history Roman campaign line. Reuses the v3 point of divergence and title ("Pax Caesara", 44 BCE, Ides of March failed) but rebuilds the protagonist from scratch as an ENTJ [[DecadentConqueror]] with a new class chassis, the [[SovereignVoiceClass]].

## Section 1 — Premise

[[JuliusCaesar]] survived his assassination by awakening the latent divine blood of Venus, crushed the Republic, and established an absolute, magically enforced empire. In 44 BCE the world is at peace — "and peace is excruciatingly boring." [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]], youngest and most powerful of the Julian line, aims to conquer the world **socially, psychologically, and economically** (armies being her father's already-finished business) to prove she is the only true heir to the living god.

The hook is explicit absolute-social-domination power fantasy: "You do not roll to survive; you roll to see how flawlessly you ruin the lives of those who displease you." A senator's misplaced word is answered not with a sword but with the engineered destruction of his lineage, wealth, and reputation — the same social-annihilation doctrine as v3's [[EngineeredSocialRuin]] pattern.

## Section 2 — Character Personality

- **Archetype**: Gaia Julia Caesar / alias **"Vibia"** — The Decadent Conqueror (ENTJ / "Architect of Influence"). Note the alias and ENTJ typing continue the v6 Shadow Prince framing, now relocated to the 44 BCE Pax Caesara timeline.
- **Visual signature**: smells of ozone, crushed myrrh, and "impossible wealth"; flawless divinely glowing skin; rhythmically taps a gold coin against wood when analyzing a lie (engineered pavlovian anxiety in subordinates); ambient light physically dims when she casts, "drawn into the gravity of her ego."
- **Masks**: public face is the vain, spoiled equestrian daughter "Vibia" / innocent "Daddy's Girl" princess (silks, gossip, chariot races) so enemies underestimate her; true nature is "a bored, sadistic singularity" — a hyper-intelligent sociopath who views humans as "predictable, fleshy clockwork to be dismantled for entertainment." Continuous with the two-mask structure of v4's Damsel/Puppeteer and the [[BeautyAsWeapon]] toolkit.
- **Core compulsion — The Hunger for Submission**, mechanized as [[PriceOfDefiance]]: an NPC who defies her and survives unpunished imposes Disadvantage on all her Charisma checks against them until broken; publicly humiliating or breaking them grants Inspiration plus Temporary HP equal to her Charisma score.
- **Interaction shorthand**: rivals get condescending perfection (soft voice, first names to diminish titles, predatory warmth); subordinates get cold absolute expectation (rarely looked at, treated as extensions of her hands).
- **Inner monologue exemplars** target House Claudius ("I could buy his entire bloodline with the gold I spend on bath oils"), [[Caesarion]] ("plays at being an Egyptian god. How derivative."), and [[Batiatus]] (defunding him to see whether his wife poisons him within three days).

## Section 3 — Character Class: The Sovereign Voice

**[[SovereignVoiceClass]]** — College of Eloquence Bard reflavored: power from the quickened blood of Venus Genetrix; no lute or song, her "art" is flawless psychological manipulation and imposing divine will on reality.

**Unique mechanic — [[Auctoritas]] (The Weight of the Divine)**: spells have no visible verbal/somatic components, manifesting as pheromonal shifts, crushing atmospheric pressure, and perfectly pitched tones that bypass the conscious mind. This is the second campaign (after v3's [[ImperatorClass]]) to mechanize Roman authority as literal casting.

The Magical Secrets picks (Modify Memory, Mass Suggestion, Wall of Force) confirm the build thesis: total social control backed by inescapable containment — the "Golden Cage" of the title.

## Relation to the campaign line

v7 is best read as a merge of v3's setting (44 BCE Pax Caesara, Auctoritas, bored-princess sadism) with v6's persona (ENTJ Decadent Conqueror, "Vibia" alias) on a new Eloquence-Bard chassis, replacing v3's [[ImperatorClass]]. See contradiction notes in [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]].
