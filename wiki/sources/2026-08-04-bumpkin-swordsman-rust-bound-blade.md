# Campaign Bible: The Rust-Bound Blade (campaign bumpkin swordsman)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign bumpkin swordsman" — a grounded geopolitical campaign bible set in the Western Heartlands of Faerûn in the post-[[BaldursGate]]-3 era. Tone: "Grounded Martial Arts Noir, Geopolitical Cold War, Narrative Realism." System architecture: [[GestaltTierSystem|Gestalt Tier-2 / Tier-3 sandbox integration]]. **Note: the source document is truncated mid-Section 2.1 (The Good Circle); disciple rosters beyond the faction split are not captured.**

## Section 1: Cosmic parameters — the Axioms of Power

The setting enforces a rigid power topology via the [[Level12MortalCeiling]]:

- **Common baseline**: 95% of the sentient population lives and dies between Levels 1–3; a veteran guard or career mercenary captain is Level 5–6.
- **Mortal pinnacle (Level 12)**: the absolute hard ceiling of mortal capability — biology, psychology, and standard spiritual discipline intersect there. A Level 12 individual is "a walking nation-state" able to rout conventional battalions or dictate kingdoms' military policy. Fewer than fifty active Level 12 individuals exist across the Western Heartlands.
- **Mythic tier (13–20)**: reserved for cosmic anomalies — direct Chosen of major deities (e.g. [[Elminster]]), ancient undead who bartered away mortality (e.g. [[SzassTam]]), or extraplanar masterminds — "fundamentally unanchored from the standard mathematics of the Weave."

## The anomaly: Master Vaelen

[[MasterVaelen]] is a structural glitch in the continental balance of power: a **pure martial prodigy** (no divine bloodline, no Thayan arcane engineering) who naturally shattered the Mortal Ceiling and achieved a flawless **Level 20 Gestalt** by age twenty, serving as a frontline General guaranteeing [[Thay]]'s battlefield dominance during its territorial expansion twenty years ago.

- **The Sunder and the Anchor**: crushed by guilt over industrialized slaughter for the Red Wizards, Vaelen deserted Thay, fled west to the rustic borderlands outside Baldur's Gate, and buried his identity. Twenty years of guilt act as a permanent subconscious psychological anchor.
- **The Calibration Crisis** ([[VaelenCalibrationCrisis]]): his internal matrix is hardwired to a Level 20 Mythic baseline while trauma locks his output to the global mortal maximum of **Level 12 Gestalt**. He experiences his Level 12 self as a "sluggish, rusty, middle-aged failure" — while observers see "a walking god who manipulates kinetic energy with a casual footstep." This Perception Paradox drives his intense, earnest humility.

## Section 2: The Disciples Matrix (the Twenty Graduates)

Vaelen runs a modest rustic dojo in the Western Kingdom, calibrating his "beginner" curriculum to his own broken Level 20 baseline — so 99% of students wash out from exhaustion, confusion, or injury, and locals consider the dojo an eccentric, brutal gamble. Exactly **twenty students** had the biology and stubbornness to graduate; they split into distinct ideological/mechanical networks across the continent ([[TwentyDisciplesMatrix]]):

- **The Good Circle (10)**: publicly recognized as the premier martial elite of the Western Kingdom; their fame drives the steady stream of ambitious new students to the dojo. (Source truncates mid-description of this faction.)
- Remaining factions (implied ~10 more graduates in other networks) are not captured in the truncated source.
