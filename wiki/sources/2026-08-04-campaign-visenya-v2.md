* Title: The Apex of Ash: Shadow of the Mother
* The Concept: Set during the liberation of Astapor and the march on Meereen, you are Visenya Belaerys. While the world believes Daenerys Targaryen is the "Last Dragon," you are the true Apex—the final scion of a Valyrian family so magically potent they attempted Apotheosis to become gods, an act that triggered the Doom. Rescued by Daenerys from a high-end Lysene pleasure house in Slaver’s Bay, you now walk beside her as a "penniless" freedwoman. Daenerys, struck by your "Exceptional Beauty," suspects you are a lost Targaryen. She has vowed to protect you but commands you to hide your face, fearing your radiance will ignite the madness of men and the jealousy of queens.

* Age: 16
* The Hook: You are the ultimate prize and the ultimate threat. Most who look upon you are immediately "extremely attracted" to your otherworldly Belaerys features. While Daenerys draws the world’s eye with her dragons, you are the shadow-weaver, using 18 Intelligence and subtle sorcery to manipulate the war from her side.

While other Targaryens merely command dragons, you are a scholar of the Draconic Weave, possessing an intellectual and intuitive mastery of magic that predates the Iron Throne.
+4
               * Primary Ability Scores: Intelligence (18) and Wisdom (16).
+1
               * Unique Subclass Mechanic: Subtle Apotheosis. You possess full spellcaster progression and the Subtle Spell feature. You can cast spells without any somatic or verbal components, making your magic completely invisible to observers in this low-magic setting.
+4
               * Special Feature: Royal Insight. You benefit significantly from your high Wisdom and Intelligence. Your Charisma-based checks (Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation) use your Intelligence or Wisdom modifier.

Section 9: Starting Scene: The Rescue from the Red House
                                          * Setting: The air in the Astapor pleasure house is thick with the scent of cheap incense and the copper tang of blood. The "Red House" is in flames as the Unsullied break the doors.
                                          * The Hook: You stand in a corner, your silver-gold hair matted with soot, clutching a tattered silk robe. Daenerys Targaryen enters, her dragons circling the burning rafters. She stops dead when she sees you. The "Exceptional Beauty" of your face, even through the dirt, strikes her like a physical blow. Ser Jorah, standing behind her, forgets his sword, his eyes fixed on you with a sudden, overwhelming heat.
                                          * The Action: Daenerys reaches out a hand. "You... you have the look of my mother. Who did this to you?"
                                          * A) The Royal Command: Say nothing, but use Subtle Casting to make the flames around her part, demonstrating your "Dragon Affinity" without a word.
