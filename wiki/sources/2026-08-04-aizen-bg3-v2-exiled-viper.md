# Aizen BG3 Campaign v2 — The Exiled Viper

**Source**: "Campaign aizen bg3 v2" character profile update. A v2 restart of the [[AizenSosuke]] Baldur's Gate campaign lineage (sibling to [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-1|the god campaign]] and its [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] pattern), but pointed in the **opposite power direction**: instead of godhood, Aizen is rebuilt as a 16-year-old **Level 4 mortal** with an explicit "no magical power-ups — just raw skill, crime, and psychological predation" constraint.

## Character frame

- **Name**: Sosuke Aizen, age 16, publicly a "gifted commoner orphan under state wardship" — actually an exiled noble.
- **Class**: [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords Bard]], Half-Elf, Entertainer (Criminal Variant) background. Note: the profile header contradicts itself by saying "Wizard — School of the Mirror Flower" (a Bleach kyoka-suigetsu nod) while the entire stat block is RAW 5e Bard.
- **Stats**: CHA 18 / INT 16 / DEX 14, Spell DC 15, Expertise in Deception + Persuasion. Spells lean fully into social predation: Charm Person, Suggestion, Detect Thoughts, Silvery Barbs, Disguise Self.
- **Combat identity**: AC 15, rapier + offhand dagger with Blade Flourish; Mobile Flourish hit-and-run. Slender frame (STR 8) "hides lethality."

## The Asset: the Arcane Athenaeum

Aizen attends the [[ArcaneAthenaeum]] (crumbling prestigious academy in the Upper City) posing as a meek junior scribe. In reality it is an [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]]: post-Absolute faculty shortages let him spike communal teas with Essence of Lethe and his own alchemical narcotic **"Whisperdust"** (euphoric dependency "mimicking Mystra's touch" — see [[Mystra]]), extracting confessions and Suggestion-enforced debts from noble heirs. Broken students feed "the Garden" as fodder, spies, or puppets. His "study group" is a velvet trap built from Minor Illusion + Prestidigitation fake privacy wards. Key figure [[ElowenThorne|Professor Elowen Thorne]] is introduced but the export truncates mid-description.

## Challenge Compensation design

The profile explicitly engineers [[ChallengeCompensation]] so a Level 4 PC stays viable against post-BG3 chaos (L12 BG3-era encounters, CR 5–10 org thugs):

- **Social counters**: high-Wisdom interrogators (Lae'zel DC15+ with advantage), Zhentarim captains with Detect Magic/True Seeing, anti-charm amulets, rivals who suspect the "witch-boy."
- **Physical counters**: swarms of weak enemies ([[TheGuild]] cutpurses ×10), magic-resistant Absolute remnants, environmental sewer ambushes that deny flourish room.
- **Intrigue counters**: family bounties on the exile, a rival (Lysandra) who scries his moves, moral traps where Isolde's guilt forces suboptimal plays, Kaname as a pursuer.
- **Reward table**: explicit XP/loot ladder (VIP blackmail = 300XP + Poisoner's Kit upgrade; Lae'zel interrogation = 500XP + Githyanki dagger; family bounty = 600XP + "Sosuke ledger"). Failure states are narrative (captured for a "breaking" reversal, debt collectors) rather than TPK.

## BG3 cast integration

[[Gale-of-Waterdeep|Gale]] appears as a friendly INT-contest foil; Wyll as an antagonist whose Paladin smite counters charm; Lae'zel as an interrogation threat; the Zhentarim field devils immune to charm.

## Relation to the campaign lineage

This is the third distinct power-tier for the same player persona: godhood ([[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-2|god campaign]]), lich sovereignty (Ains sanctuary module), and now street-level mortal predation — all expressing the same serpent-mastermind fantasy of control through manipulation rather than force, consistent with the player's documented campaign preferences.
