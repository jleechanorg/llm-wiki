# Campaign Bible: Rivellon Under the Emerald Eclipse (DOS2 campaign)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Dos 2 campaign" — a [[DivinityOriginalSin2]] post-endgame anthology in which the player character chose absolute tyranny at the apex of the First Garden, consumed both [[LucianTheDivine]] and [[DallisTheHammer]], and monopolized the cosmic lifeblood of [[Rivellon]]. The Veil remains intact but is now tethered exclusively to the protagonist's living soul (the [[VeilMonopoly]]). Continues the wiki's apex-power-fantasy campaign lineage (cf. [[ApexWeaver]], [[ProjectApotheosis]]).

## Premise: The God-Shadow

Rather than a lumbering juggernaut, the divine chassis manifests as a silent apex predator — a high-elven or perfected Eternal silhouette in dark supple medium armor, dual finesse blades flashing with the stolen radiance of the gods. Ascension was pursued through speed, grace, and absolute stealth.

## Chapter I: Mechanical Architecture

The character uses a **Level 12–30 [[GestaltTier4Progression|Gestalt character sheet]]**: at every milestone the character gains the features, spellcasting progression, and benefits of BOTH classes simultaneously — no multiclassing or level splitting:

- **Paladin side**: modified **Oath of Conquest Paladin** (absolute martial authority)
- **Sorcerer side**: **[[SourceSoulSorcerer]]** (dark adaptation of Divine Soul; infinite cosmic reservoir; access to Sorcerer, Paladin, AND Cleric spell lists, all Charisma-based)

### Level 12 base statistics
- Hit Dice: 12d12 (custom "Tyrant Divine" chassis) + CON mod
- Saves: Charisma +9 (with Aura), Constitution +4
- AC 19 (Half-Plate 15 + Dex 2 + Fighting Style 1 + protection item 1)
- Stealth +12 or higher
- **Charisma 20 (+5)** — [[ImmaculatePresence]] at peak; spell save DC 17
- **Dexterity 18 (+4)** — Finesse weapons (dual obsidian rapiers / shadow-infused daggers) carry Divine Smites

Tracked on **[[TheSovereignLedger]]** stat block: Gestalt Paladin (Conquest) / Sorcerer (Source Soul), start L12 ("Adjusting to the Torrent of Divinity"), max L30 ("The God-Slayer Apotheosis").

## Design notes

- Third distinct "start-above-mortals" chassis in the collection: contrasts the L12–16 guardian ceiling of the Ains/Nazarick module and the L6 prodigy frame of Visenya v6 — this one begins at L12 and scales to L30 apotheosis.
- Matches the established player taste profile: stealth-charisma apex predator, moral corruption via consuming the setting's divine authorities, and monopoly-of-power framing rather than party-balanced play.
