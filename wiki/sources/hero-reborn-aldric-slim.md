# Hero Reborn — Aldric of the Last Oath (slim, male mirror)

## Section 1 — Campaign Intro

He killed a queen to save a continent. A thousand years later he woke in the body of a Margrave's fifth *son* — quiet, polite, dark-haired, eyes down — and has been counting the people she was right about ever since. Aldric the First, the Hero-King, ended a 40-year war by cutting down the woman who burned half the world. He kept his word: the daughter lived. She woke up 12 years ago and discovered the cost. Ash was not mad. She was *made* into a monster by the necessity of winning a war his side started.

**Opening:** Imperial Academy of Aetheria, present day. Mushoku Tensei cadence → Witcher moral-grey → Frieren melancholy (he outlived everyone he saved) → political-thriller escalation as the cold war's other side comes into focus. Same Academy, same dormitory row; his suitemate is a quiet silver-haired girl whose name he keeps pretending he does not recognize.

**Act III:** one scene where Aldric almost does what Aldric the First did — strike the killing blow against Ash, accept the demon Court's deal, sign the imperial assassination order — and chooses differently. The antagonist is not the Demon Queen. The antagonist is the version of himself who believed the clean-cut lie.

**CANON-PRIORITY:** Same as Ash of Aetheria. Old magic > modern. Demons = sentient morally-grey, NOT monsters. Modern L10 ≈ old-magic L5. **The two bibles are mutually-canon in the same campaign.** Ash and Aldric can play parallel campaigns; hooks collide. No mindless monsters; threats come from people.

## Section 2 — Personality (INFJ — DM notes only)

Quiet, polite, eyes down. Hides. **Core Compulsion: "Do not become the lie again."** Three inner-monologue seeds: (1) "She was smiling when I cut her down," (2) "Mirael survived. I kept my word. What did the word cost her?", (3) "I will not sign that order." Coping: letters to the dead (never sent), private 2,114-name casualty ledger, INFJ doorslam when the lie gets loud. True self: a king who wanted to be a farmer. Arc: certainty → doubt → accountability.

## Section 3 — Class & Subclass

**Gestalt — Paladin 12 / Warlock 12 (Pact of the Blade, the old deal).** True ceiling L25 (old oath) gated behind the **Oath** + the **Pact**.

- **The Oath (passive, L1):** Righteous Aura. Lies to his true identity auto-fail unless cast by L15+. *Same mechanic as Ash's Veil, inverted — concealment that *strengthens* his paladin auras when he lies, weakens them when he tells the truth.*
- **The Pact (active, L5+):** Each L5+ cast = Charisma save DC 10+spell level. Fail = his patron whispers the name of someone he killed for "the greater good." Success = patron goes quiet one turn (bonus action).
- **Sword of the Last Oath (Pact Weapon substitution):** The blade he used to kill Astheria, reforged. Sentient (LE, calls him "Boy-King"). +3 greatsword, radiant to fiends, necrotic to oath-breakers, psychic to himself when drawn without intent.
- **Hero's Memory (L17 unlock, Oath-drop):** Once/long rest, cast a spell from his first life. L9 = **Oath of the Last Stand** (abjuration that *holds a single person alive past their death*). Oath drops for all in 100y.
- **Pact Escalation (L6 unlock):** Cast one tier higher by paying 1 Pact Point. Starts at 0. Earns PP by *telling the truth about his identity* — each truth believed by a named NPC = 1.

Starting spells: standard 5e prepared, max L6 per old-oath cap. **No spells above L6** — the patron is the previous-life patron, *not* a new one; calling higher spells re-binds the old contract.

## Section 4 — Assets & Retinue (starting gold: 0)

- **The Hero's Blade (sealed)** — reforged greatsword, in the Academy reliquary *next to* the Ash-Crown. He feels it from 3 floors away. Has not drawn it.
- **The Sealed Confession** — the Hero-King's last written words, locked in the Imperial vault. He does not know the Headmistress has a copy.
- **Mirael's Letter (lost)** — Mirael wrote him a thank-you after the war. Never delivered. Academy lost-mail vault.
- **Walking stick ("First Farmer" in Old Aetherian)** — from a tutor he does not remember. Older than the Academy.

**Three starting NPCs (each trips one specific scene — "clinic not harem"):**
- **Captain Reyna (47, human veteran, Academy security)** — Loyalty 8/10. Same Reyna as Ash's. Her late husband from a line Astheria killed. *Trains Aldric with the same blade work she trains Ash — same cruelty.* *Function: "tutor who reminds him of the killing."*
- **Cassian Aldric-Vale (13, great-grandson of Hero-King Aldric I)** — Loyalty 5, oscillates. Earnest, awkward, no idea his great-grandfather is three rooms away. *Function: "the descendant who would either forgive him or hate him."*
- **The Chamberlain (40s, human, Imperial spymaster, Aldric II's hand)** — Loyalty 0/10. Already hunting Ash. Has a standing order to find anyone who "carries the Hero's shadow." *Function: "the assassin who would be his closest ally if he weren't the target."*

## Section 8 — Gazetteer & Mechanics

### Rank Tables (same as Ash)

**Imperial Nobility:** Esquire → Landed Knight → Baron → Viscount → Count → Marquis → Duke → Archduke → King (historical, reserved 400y — *the King seat is "Aldric the First's," never refilled*) → Emperor (Empress Cassia XI reigning) → **World Emperor (mythic)** — vacant 800y. Last holder: Astheria's father.

**Martial:** Squire → Knight-Errant → Knight of the Realm → Knight Commander → Paladin → High Paladin → Holy Marshal → **Sword Saint (mythic)** — one alive (Sword-Saint of Karn). *The Hero-King was a Sword-Saint.*

**Mage:** Apprentice → Adept → Magister → Archmagister → High Magus → Imperial Magus → **Veilwarden (mythic)** — 3 alive >400y → **Archmage of the Hidden Tower (mythic)** — none alive 800y.

### Quad-Pillar Stats (mirror of Ash's)

| Pillar | Floor | Ceiling | Floor effect | Ceiling effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Righteousness** (people who believe him just) | 0 | 3 | Safe — quiet boy | Knightly Orders rally; Aldric II loses legitimacy |
| **Hidden Apex** (old-oath power used) | 0 | 25 (L25) | Cast nothing above L1 | Cast Oath of the Last Stand; the blade wakes |
| **Doubt** (named NPCs who suspect) | 0 | ∞ | Anonymous | The Hero-King returns; world fractures |
| **Witnesses** (NPCs who know he's more) | 0 | ∞ | Oath intact | Oath irrelevant; he is *known* |

Pillars independent. High Righteousness + low Doubt = world trusts him, nobody knows why. High Doubt + low Righteousness = world fears him, no legitimacy.

### XP Rule (per User Preference #8)

`XP = 0.34 × (next_level_threshold − current_level_threshold)` per die roll. Standard D&D 5e cumulative XP. NO XP during god mode / time-skips / modals. Bonus damage rolls do not separately award XP. Example L4→L5 (distance 3800): `0.34 × 3800 = 1292 XP/die`.

### Demon-vision mechanic (mirror of Ash's)

Demons see soul silhouettes. Aldric's silhouette = a king in armor holding a blade over a kneeling woman. The Quiet Brother has *not yet seen it* — but the silhouette is loud. When he does, campaign forks.

### Names-as-Contracts mechanic

Mirror of Ash's. **The Hero-King's true name is the most dangerous name in the world** — saying it wrong binds any demon Court member for one full day and triggers the Chamberlain's kill order. Every Act needs one name beat.

## Section 9 — Continuity Hooks (no endings)

1. **The Hero's Blade in the Reliquary Vault** — Aldric feels it from 3 floors away. *The Ash-Crown is in the same vault.* Veil and Oath drop simultaneously if both owners look.
