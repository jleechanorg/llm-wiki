# Aizen god campaign — campaign genesis

Opening segment of the Aizen god-campaign transcript (Gemini 2.5 Flash ULTRA, "Dungeon master" Gem). The chat index shows the campaign's full arc structure: **mortal → v1m → v2m → v3m → godhood**, with pinned chats "Aizen godhood (previously bane - main)" and "aizen godhood 2". The player asks the DM to read the prior "bane - main" chat for context but start a **new** D&D 5e campaign in [[BaldursGate]] featuring [[AizenSosuke]] — same characters and factions, clean world state. This is the [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] pattern: continuity of cast, reset of state.

## Campaign frame

- **Title**: *The Shadow over the Gate* ([[ShadowOverTheGate]])
- **System**: D&D 5e; **Setting**: Baldur's Gate — Lower City, Upper City, immediate outskirts
- **Themes**: intrigue, shifting loyalties, power struggles, the nature of good and evil, corrupting ambition

## Major factions (reimagined)

- **[[FlamingFist]]** — city military/police under Grand Duke [[UlderRavengard]]; internal divisions and potential corruption
- **[[TheGuild]]** — organized crime; a power vacuum or ambitious new leader may be emerging
- **[[KnightsOfTheShield]]** — secretive faction guiding the city's destiny from behind the scenes; not always aligned with the Flaming Fist
- **[[CultOfTheDeadThree]]** — hidden cult of [[Bane]], Bhaal, and Myrkul sowing chaos; Bane flagged as a significant, subtle presence
- **[[HouseAizen]]** — new rising power fronted by Aizen; goals shrouded in mystery, considerable resources and charisma, influence potentially extending into every other faction

## Starting point: "A Night of Unrest"

Weeks of unease — strange disappearances, escalating gang violence, whispers of ancient cults. The DM offers four PC starting scenarios ([[NarrativeHookScenarios]]):

1. **Elfsong Tavern brawl** — gang fight at the [[ElfsongTavern]] (Guild-aligned gang vs a disciplined gang with ritualistic tattoos) covering the attempted theft of a dark-pulsing arcane artifact
2. **Dockside abduction** — shadowy figures with unnatural speed abduct a merchant/Flaming Fist officer, leaving an **eye symbol** etched in cobblestones
3. **Noble's plea** — minor Upper City noble whose guard has vanished, terrified of a "growing darkness," offering a reward
4. **Flaming Fist patrol** — a Fist PC investigates missing persons who were critics of the city's power structure; the patrol leader is unusually stressed

## The common thread

All four hooks share deliberate signatures — "unusual efficiency" among the antagonists — seeding Aizen's hidden hand behind ostensibly unrelated events ([[SubtleInfluenceCampaignHook]]). The transcript cuts off mid-description of this common thread.
