# Campaign Module: The Velvet Cage (Nocturne BG3 v4, 2026-08-04 revision)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign noctune bg3 v4" (raw text preserved at [[2026-08-04-campaign-noctune-bg3-v4]]). D&D 5e (2024 ruleset), starting **Level 1**, tone: psychological horror / noir / revenge. This is a revised v4 of the module first ingested 2026-04-12 (older slug `nocturne-bg3-v4`); the doc's own precedence rule is "later text overrides earlier text." Play begins turn-by-turn with the Horgus Gwent scene.

## Premise

[[NocturneSosuke]], 18, is the "Black Diamond" of [[MadamZylra]]'s Upper City brothel, the [[BloomShadow|Bloom Shadow]] — 730 days after the [[CassalanterFamily]] ruined [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]] and sold her into it. Her sisters [[Elara]] and [[Lyra]] were reported dead "of illness"; the truth arrives when regular client [[HorgusGwent]] drunkenly drops a **Soul Coin minted with Lyra's face** — the Cassalanters "bottled" the sisters as vintage currency — along with an invitation to the **Cassalanter Ascension Gala** ("The Second Tithe"), the midnight ritual renewing the pact with [[Zariel]] that powers their fortune by sacrificing ruined nobility.

## Structure

1. **Scene 1 — The Tuesday Regular**: unarmed, AC 12, 7 HP vs. a drunk, prone Horgus; three resolution lanes (seductive kill with improvised hairpin-stiletto, cold theft, or first manifestation of bard magic as social engineering).
2. **Scene 2 — The Red Ledger**: Lyra's coin grants Inspiration; the invitation reveals the ritual, its hidden Temple of the Golden Sun, and the choice: flee / suicide run / **the Contract** (steal the ritual, don't stop it).
3. **Locations**: Bloom Shadow escape (rival [[Vespera]], ogre bouncer [[Grum]], Zylra's blackmail Black Ledger behind a DC 20 lock + Sleep glyph), Upper City travel with a d6 encounter table (Steel Watch, Astarion cameo, judging ox easter egg), then the **Cassalanter Villa heist**: masquerade gala → library puzzle (imp familiar Rattigan) → Temple of the Golden Sun.
4. **Climax — the [[InversionRitual]]**: explicitly *not* a combat ("You are Level 1. You will die"). A 4-successes-before-3-failures skill challenge to step into the circle and swap the ritual's beneficiary from the Cassalanters to Nocturne, pitching competence to Zariel's avatar (Lyra's coin = +5 down payment). Success strips the Cassalanters' protection and unlocks the subclass at Level 2/3.

## Character & mechanics

- Level 1 Bard, STR 8 / DEX 14 / CON 8 / INT 16 / WIS 10 / CHA 16 — a "porcelain doll that has learned to hate"; beauty framed as tactical infrastructure ([[BeautyAsWeapon]]).
- **[[SirensLeverage]]**: +2 to Deception/Persuasion vs. humanoids attracted to females; stacks with Expertise.
- **Survivor's Stash**: hairpin stiletto, 125 GP, Potion of Sleep, improvised thieves' tools, miniature blackmail book (Advantage on Intimidation vs. houses Gralhund/Phylund/Caldwell), pageboy disguise.
- **[[CollegeOfInfernalContracts]]** ("Legion Update", revised from [[CollegeOfPerdition]]): the Black Ledger binds unlimited Signatories with Active slots = Bard level + CHA; Clauses of Collateral (damage transfer), Extension (proxy casting), and Tithe (healing interception); Level 6 Liquidation of Assets burns minion HP for free spell slots; Level 14 Hostile Takeover chains Dominate into contracts. Meta-analysis in-doc: "walking around with 200+ buffer HP at level 5."
- **Companion system** ([[CompanionContractRecruitment]]): the Level 9-10 BG3 origins are recruited as contracted *Assets*, not co-equal party members — every companion (Astarion, Wyll, Gale, Karlach, Lae'zel, Shadowheart, Halsin, Minthara, Dark Urge; Jaheira and Minsc immune to the +2) gets an attraction flag, a leverage hook, and a contract. Camp starts at the [[ElfsongTavern]], upgrading to reclaimed Sosuke Manor.

## World state & remnants

Synchronized to late BG3 Act 3: refugee crisis, Gortash's coronation in 3 days, the Absolute squeezing the city — the Cassalanters are racing to complete the tithe tonight. Surviving Sosuke splinters seed future arcs: collaborator Uncle Valerius Sosuke (tax collector for the Cassalanters), gutter-fence Cousin "Rat" Jace, institutionalized warlock Great-Aunt Ophelia, and Hellrider refugee Isolda Sosuke.

## Continuity notes

The Thay v1 bible ([[2026-08-04-aizen-thay-v1-nocturne-ancestor]]) treats this exact brothel arc as [[NocturneSosuke]]'s 1492 DR ancestral backstory ("Love is a weapon, and desire is a leash"), while this module plays it in the present at age 18 — the [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]] tension. The v4 climax text says "College of Perdition unlocked," but the later addendum details College of Infernal Contracts; by the doc's precedence rule the Infernal Contracts version governs.
