# Campaign Start: Military Prosecutor Doberman (Sariel)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Campaign military prosecutor Doberman" — a D&D 5e campaign start adapting the recurring serpent-mastermind personality profile into the modern K-drama setting of *[[MilitaryProsecutorDoberman|Military Prosecutor Doberman]]* (Korean military/corporate legal drama). This is the first ingested campaign in a contemporary Korean setting rather than Rome, Faerûn, or an isekai frame — the same master-manipulator archetype ported to boardrooms and courts-martial instead of arenas and empires.

## Player character: [[Sariel]]

- **Age**: 26. **Class**: Bard ([[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]], re-flavored for a modern setting).
- **Appearance/cover**: jarring chaebol-heiress beauty deliberately concealed — anonymous black bodyguard suit, severe bun, mask of silent subservient efficiency. "A ghost in plain sight, standing three paces behind your charge." Direct application of the [[BeautyAsWeapon]] pattern, inverted: beauty is *hidden* as camouflage rather than displayed as leverage.
- **Archetype**: the **[[SerpentQueenArchetype|Serpent-Queen]]** — a "philosopher-queen in waiting" who views corporate and military law as a flawed schematic needing a merciless editor. Explicitly blended from four villain templates: [[Griffith]] (transcendent ambition), [[TywinLannister|Tywin]] (cold pragmatism), [[AizenSosuke|Aizen]] (intellectual/charismatic superiority), and [[JohanLiebert|Johan Liebert]] (philosophical nihilistic evil).

## The Two Masks ([[TwoMaskFortress]])

1. **The Silent Servant** — default public interface. Soft musical voice forcing generals to lean in; flawless noble etiquette that feels *wrong* for a bodyguard; anticipates her charge [[NohTaeNam|Noh Tae-nam]]'s petty needs before he thinks of them. Every servile gesture is framed as a **data probe** — pressure applied to another personality's system to map weaknesses.
2. **The Dispassionate Strategist** — private planning mask. Presents win-win interlocking plans that solve Noh Tae-nam's problems while banking unstated private benefits (studying a prosecutor's methods, testing [[IMDefense|IM Defense]]'s legal armor). She praises his "courage" after he executes her suggestions; he is scaffolding "destined to be dismantled the instant his utility expires."

## Core drives

- **Personal Transcendence / [[PortableAbsolutePower]]**: rejects titles and thrones as traps (companies need administration, armies need morale — messy uncontrollable variables). Hungers instead for portable absolute power: blackmail ledgers, state secrets, untraceable offshore treasuries. End goal phrased as touching divinity — "not to rule from a boardroom, but to *become* the boardroom." Continuous with the ascension endgames of prior campaigns ([[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]], [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]]).
- **[[SurgicalDomination]]**: no desire to be Chairwoman; the thrill is the singular frictionless imposition of will — a whispered word that stops a general's career, a Bardic-magic "suggested" idea that makes brilliant lawyer [[YongMoonGu|Yong Moon-gu]] unknowingly adopt her strategy.
- **Supremacist Certainty**: views allies (Noh Tae-nam) and enemies ([[DoBaeMan|Do Bae-man]]) alike with a scholar's detached amusement toward "fascinating but limited fauna"; listens to prosecutors argue like an adult hearing children debate. (Source text truncates mid-sentence here.)

## Cross-campaign significance

Same player-taste fingerprint as the Gaia Julia / Aurelius Caesar / Sosuke lines: female master-manipulator PC, hidden true nature behind a servile mask, Bard College of Swords chassis, ascension-through-systems ambition, Lawful Evil patience. The novel elements are (1) a fully modern low/no-magic setting where Bardic magic is re-flavored as social suggestion, and (2) the servant/bodyguard cover identity replacing the noble/royal cover of prior campaigns.
