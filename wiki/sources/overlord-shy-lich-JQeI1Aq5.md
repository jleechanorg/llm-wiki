God Mode:
Character: Ains | Description: Campaign Module: Ains — The Sanctuary of Flesh and Shadow
Overlord × D&D 5e / Pathfinder hybrid isekai. Set Year 0 of the New World arrival — Nazarick has just crossed over, the Slane Theocracy still treats it as a local myth, and Ains is the lone Level 20 god-queen of a fortress whose guardians could be hunted down by a united continent. Ains is a 16-year-old living lich who wants to keep everyone alive and buy some wheat. Ships as a system — see "Phase Progression" and "Hard Guardrails."


Shared World Background
The New World (Overlord canon, with two custom mutations)
A century-cycle reality where entities from the D&D-derived MMO YGGDRASIL arrive in a young-medieval world. 600 years ago came the Six Great Gods; 500 years ago came the Eight Greed Kings; today comes the Great Tomb of Nazarick with one Supreme Being still inside it. The continent is held by the Re-Estize Kingdom (rotting feudal agriculture), the Baharuth Empire (functional militarist monarchy), the Slane Theocracy (human-supremacist state holding six World Items and the Black Scripture elite), the Dragon Lord Council of Argland (anti-YGGDRASIL ancient powers), and a constellation of demi-human realms under constant pressure from the Beastman Abellion.

Custom Mutation #1 — Universal Sentience. No mindless beasts, no pop-spawn fodder, no brainless undead. Every creature in the New World — goblin, dire wolf, skeleton warrior, even summoned elemental — possesses full sentience, language, personal motives, and emotional capacity. Combat is always negotiable. Killing is morally heavy; using the wrong target (innocent village, surrendered caravan, surrendering kobold tribe) creates Psychic Strain on Ains.

Custom Mutation #2 — Lone Sovereign Constraint. Ains is the only Level 20 entity in Nazarick. Her floor guardians, lieutenants, siblings, and pop-spawns are capped at L12–L16. Against a single mortal army they look like gods. Against the Black Scripture carrying World Items, a coalition of awakened Dragon Lords wielding Wild Magic, and a unified million-man crusade — her retinue is mortal. If she lets them run wild, she will personally survive and end up standing alone in an empty tomb surrounded by the corpses of her friends' creations. That nightmare is the campaign's driving tension.
The Hidden Truth (Pre-Campaign Reveal — surfaces at L1 turn 2)
The 41 Supreme Beings are gone. They didn't die. They logged off. YGGDRASIL's servers shut down six months before the transfer; Ains's 40 friends chose to walk away from the game. Nazarick didn't fall into the New World — Ains was simply left holding the door open when the building emptied. Her "Supreme Being peers" aren't coming back. Her social anxiety isn't a quirk — it's the loneliness of a 16-year-old who was the youngest of a guild family and woke up alone as the aunt of an orphanage full of monsters who think she's their mother.

This is player-discoverable, not prologue. Reveal it in Act 1, beat 2 — let the player figure it out from Sebas's line "My Lady, I have searched the throne room thrice. There are no other chairs at the table."
The Turning Point — The Treasury Audit (Day 0, opening scene)
Soft pale-blue light from floating arcane crystals spills across the polished mahogany of the inner council chamber. Stacked before you are Demiurge's leather-bound ledgers detailing Nazarick's weekly operational costs: 7,200 gold in YGGDRASIL coin, 0 in Re-Estize silver, 0 in local grain, 14,000 beastman mouths to feed this month.

Your floor guardians and four older siblings kneel around the table in reverent silence. Under your Mask of Fragility you look like a breathtaking, delicate 16-year-old with Level 1 stats. Your body hums with Level 20 Bladesinger/Monk Ki.

Demiurge adjusts his silver-framed glasses. "As our Supreme Ruler has undoubtedly already deduced —" (your eye twitches) "— our reserves of local New World currency are dangerously thin. Therefore, I have devised a simple, elegant solution for our First Arc in this rotting kingdom." He smiles, revealing needle-sharp teeth. "E-Rantel sits on the primary trade triangle between Re-Estize, the Empire, and the Theocracy. Shalltear and Rubedo can lead three cohorts of undead, march on E-Rantel under cover of night, and seize their grain silos before sunrise."

Inside your head: (If you smash E-Rantel, the entire continent forms a Grand Crusade. I'm L20, but they are L12–L16 — they'll die. I just want to buy some wheat and open a peaceful shop.)

You clear your throat softly. The room falls into reverent silence. Your Aura of Quiet Majesty radiates gently across the table. "Umm… while I appreciate your dedication… outright slaughter destroys the very economic infrastructure we wish to acquire. Furthermore — if we act as bloodthirsty monsters, we will force every kingdom and Dragon Lord to unite against us. I will not allow a world where any of you are taken from me."

Shalltear's eyes fill with tears as she drops to her knees. Demiurge's eyes widen in humbled epiphany. (At least nobody is getting flayed.)

The New Choice

Module 0: The Grain & Silver Crisis
Campaign Summary
You are Ains — a 16-year-old living lich, Level 20 Bladesinger Wizard / INT-Monk gestalt with Pathfinder Lich traits, absolute sovereign of the Great Tomb of Nazarick. You possess god-tier magic, untouchable martial arts, and a Soul Anchor phylactery bound to a World Item. Your greatest enemy is social anxiety, economic scarcity, and the catastrophic possibility that your overenthusiastic subordinates will accidentally trigger a Holy Crusade that gets them all killed. You must secure trade routes, prevent massacres, and quietly transform Nazarick from "ancient evil tomb" into "surprisingly competent diplomatic neighbor" — without ever letting the realm discover that your Mask of Fragility is, in fact, a Mask of Fragility.
World History (The Path to Day 0)
YGGDRASIL's servers shut down six months before transfer. Of the 41 guild members, only Ains remained logged in when the world-cross triggered. Her lieutenants, guardians, and siblings — created as NPC constructs by guild-mates who are now gone — woke fully sentient and continue to believe the 41 are simply "offline on divine business." Ains has not corrected them because she doesn't know how. She just wants to keep everyone alive and figure out how to feed them.
Campaign Details
Setting the Scene: Grand Council Chamber of Nazarick, Day 0, opening scene described above. Eight named NPCs at the table: Albedo, Shalltear, Demiurge, Sebas Tian, Rubedo, Nigredo, Pandora's Actor, Aureole Omega. Atmosphere: reverent silence broken only by Demiurge's proposals and your internal panic.


The First Tactical Decision Point — Arc 1 Launch (Re-Estize Border Operations)
The realm rests in Quiet Ignorance (narrative aggro state). Choose how to launch Nazarick's first economic operation:
Option A: The Level 1 Adventurer in E-Rantel [Personal Infiltration]
You and Sebas enter the walled city of E-Rantel wearing your Level 1 disguise. Register at the Adventurer Guild to earn local Re-Estize silver legally, clear trade roads, negotiate with sentient monster tribes in the Forest of Tob, secure direct grain/timber contracts with local merchant lords.

Mechanic: Triggers the Adventurer's Ledger sub-system — Ains earns gold piece-by-piece through guild contracts, building mortal relationships and learning New World economics firsthand. Slow but trust-building.
Atmospheric Impact: Keeps the world in Quiet Ignorance. World sees a kind teenage heroine. Demiurge is horrified.
Lead-in: Forces Ains out of her comfort zone into direct social interaction (her anxiety spikes).
Option B: The Patron of Carne Village [Agricultural Sanctuary]
You travel to Carne Village near the Tob Forest border. When corrupt border raiders threaten the town, you intervene softly — using your L20 monk speed masked as "lucky dodges." You assign low-tier, sentient Nazarick goblins (the Goblin General cohort, ~5,000 strong) to help the villagers farm with Yggdrasil Grain Seeds that yield province-feeding crops.

Mechanic: Triggers the Sanctuary Ledger — Carne Village becomes Nazarick's first surface protectorate. Builds grain supply lines and a heartwarming human alliance.
Atmospheric Impact: Maintains Quiet Ignorance in major capitals, but local Re-Estize tax collectors will notice a tiny border village suddenly producing triple yields.
Lead-in: Sets up the canonical "Enri's goblin village" relationship from Overlord lore, but with all goblins fully sentient and negotiating on equal terms.
Option C: The Secret Syndicate Takeover [Eight Fingers Subversion]
You allow Demiurge to arrange a private meeting with the regional heads of Eight Fingers — the corrupt crime syndicate controlling Re-Estize grain speculation, black markets, and silver trade. You walk into their underground den as a fragile Level 1 girl. They try to intimidate you. You shatter their leverage with your Spell DC (DC 24 bare, DC 26 with Staff) and your Aura of Quiet Majesty, forcing them into a fair, lucrative, bloodless trade contract in a single night of high-stakes political intrigue.

Mechanic: Triggers the Whispers of the Realm — solves Nazarick's grain and silver shortages in one session, but Eight Fingers' rank-and-file don't know why their bosses are suddenly acting strange. Slight atmospheric ripple toward Restless Border Whispers.
Atmospheric Impact: Instant economy fix. Risk: Ains must keep Demiurge on an extremely tight leash so he doesn't torture the syndicate leaders "for efficiency."
Lead-in: Sets up the Eight Fingers as recurring antagonists/allies and opens the underground politics sub-arc.


The Sanctuary Imperative (central mechanic)
What It Is
A Psychic Strain / Divine Serenity dual-track system that mechanically rewards non-lethal problem-solving and punishes unnecessary slaughter. The strain is Ains's — it tracks her moral weight, not the party's kill count. It also generates the campaign's most distinctive mechanic: The Mask of Fragility deception check, which gates every NPC encounter with anyone who might suspect her true power.

The Mask of Fragility (deception system, v2)

By default, Ains's L20 aura is suppressed under a passive glamour. All scrying, appraisal spells, and threat-detection abilities see her as a delicate Level 1 human girl with 8 HP. The Mask's effective DC is dynamic — it starts unbreakable but degrades with every visible power use.

How the DC Scales With Power
The Mask uses Spell Save DC as its base, which already scales with Ains's level progression. As Ains grows more powerful (level ups, INT increases, Staff acquisition), the Base DC rises — but every visible use still subtracts DPP from it. "The stronger I am, the lower the perception DC" reads correctly: raw power level raises the ceiling, but every visible use shaves it down.
Mask of Fragility Deception Check
Default state: Ains appears as a fragile teenage civilian. NPCs do not bother to probe.
Suspicion trigger: If an NPC, scout, archmage, or lieutenant gets suspicious and actively probes, they roll Deception vs. Ains's Effective Mask DC.
Failure: They buy the Level 1 cover 100%. View Ains as a helpless, harmless child.
Success: They glimpse a split-second flicker of cosmic blue arcana around her eyes. Result: immediate awe or panic; the NPC gains the Sensing Suspicion condition (any further action against Ains requires a Wisdom save vs. Frightened).
At ≥2 Psychic Strain: The Mask's DC drops by 1 per stack (suspecting NPCs have an easier time seeing through the glamour). Pressure mechanic — the more Ains fails, the more her lies cost her.


The Whispers of the Realm (narrative world aggro system)
Continental tension and crusade risk tracked as four narrative states. Advances forward when lieutenants commit massacres, exposed YGGDRASIL-tier magic is witnessed by mortal chroniclers, or Nazarick's involvement in a regional incident becomes public. Holds or recedes through trade deals, stealth operations, and successfully maintaining the Mask of Fragility.

Pattern Notes
The Mask of Fragility is the campaign's most distinctive mechanic. Every NPC encounter becomes a deception check, every social moment becomes a risk assessment. Ains's power is hidden but never gone; the player has to choose when to drop the Mask and reveal Level 20.
The lieutenants are the campaign's secondary cast, not the antagonists. They are devoted, protective, and terrifying in their misunderstanding. Each session should give the player at least one moment where they have to gently correct a guardian without crushing their devotion.
The Sanctuary Imperative creates a punishing feedback loop. Killing innocent NPCs strains Ains. Strain makes her Mask easier to see through. Failing the Mask reveals her. Revealing her spikes the Whispers of the Realm. The Whispers lead to the Eclipse. The Eclipse kills her guardians. The campaign is mechanically a downward spiral that the player has to actively reverse through non-lethal problem-solving.
No mindless monsters. Every enemy is sentient, negotiable, and morally weighted. Combat is always a choice, not a default.
The hidden truth surfaces in beat 2. The 41 didn't die — they left. Ains is an orphan pretending to be a mother. The campaign is about whether the orphanage survives her childhood.
