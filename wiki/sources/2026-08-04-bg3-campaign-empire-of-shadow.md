BALDUR’S GATE 3: THE AFTERMATH – A CAMPAIGN OF CHAOS & SURVIVAL
THE WORLD STATE: FATE OF THE LEGENDS
The Netherbrain is dead, reduced to a putrefying mass of psychic rot in the Chionthar. But the saviors of Baldur's Gate did not liberate the city; they merely reorganized its chains. The "Triumvirate of the Absolute's Bane"—Astarion, Shadowheart, and Gale—have carved the Sword Coast into three distinct, terrifying empires of blood, shadow, and unbridled arcana. Every major figure from the crisis has met a definitive, often tragic, fate.
The Main Characters
* Astarion (The Ascended Lord): Level 12+ Rogue (Thief) / Ranger (Gloom Stalker). Astarion completed the Rite of Profane Ascension. He rules the night from the rebuilt Szarr Palace, controlling the city’s economy, politics, and underworld. He wears the Bhaalist Armour and wields the dual blades Crimson Mischief and Bloodthirst, becoming a blur of piercing death. His vampiric spawn infest the Upper City, replacing the Parliament of Peers.
* Shadowheart (Mother Superior of Loss): Level 12+ Cleric (Trickery Domain) / Paladin (Oathbreaker). She fully embraced Shar, slaughtering the Nightsong and usurping the House of Grief. She wears the Dark Justiciar Half-Plate and Viconia's Walking Fortress, wielding the Spear of Evening. She has plunged the Lower City into a brutal theocracy where memories are violently extracted in the Mirror of Loss, and dissenters are dragged into the Shadowfell.
* Gale of Waterdeep (God of Ambition): Level 12+ Wizard (Evocation) / Sorcerer (Wild Magic). Gale forged the Crown of Karsus anew, achieving a terrifying pseudo-divinity. He hovers above the city in a floating citadel carved from Ramazith’s Tower, wearing the Robe of the Weave and wielding Markoheshkir. He treats the populace as test subjects for experimental Netherese weaving, frequently mutating citizens or tearing holes in the fabric of reality.
* Lae'zel (The Astral Scourge): Level 12+ Fighter (Battle Master). Having rejected Vlaakith but finding no allies in the corrupted Baldur's Gate, Lae'zel took to the Astral Sea. Wielding the Silver Sword of the Astral Plane and clad in the Helldusk Armour, she leads a relentless, suicidal guerrilla war against both the Lich-Queen and the remnants of the Illithid empire. She is a hardened, ruthless zealot of her own freedom.
* Wyll (The Broken Blade): Level 12+ Warlock (The Fiend) / Paladin (Oath of Devotion). Wyll failed to break his pact and was forced to watch his father die at the hands of Astarion's political maneuvering. Now wielding the Infernal Rapier and the Potent Robe, Wyll is trapped eternally in Avernus as Mizora’s personal attack dog, fighting an endless, hopeless war in the Blood War, his soul slowly calcifying into a Lemure.
* Karlach (The Mind Flayer Rebel): Level 12+ Barbarian (Wild Magic) / Illithid. Karlach made the ultimate sacrifice, becoming an Illithid to defeat the brain. Despised by the city she saved and refusing to return to Avernus, she hides deep in the city sewers. Wielding Balduran's Giantslayer and wearing the Bonespike Garb, she consumes the brains of Astarion’s rogue spawn to survive, fighting a losing battle against her fading humanity and the rising cold logic of the squid.
* Minthara (The High General): Level 12+ Paladin (Oath of Vengeance). Minthara allied with Astarion, acting as his brutal military enforcer. She commands the elite "Night Watch," a heavily armored legion of spawn and Drow. She wears the Armour of Persistence and wields the Mace of the Believer, crushing any strikes, riots, or Sharran incursions that threaten Astarion’s absolute control over the city’s trade routes.
* Halsin (The Shadow-Touched): Level 12+ Druid (Circle of the Moon). Halsin’s spirit was broken when Shadowheart’s Sharran influence began to seep into the surrounding wilderness. The Thaniel entity was corrupted by the pervasive darkness. Halsin now roams the Cloakwood as a rabid, shadow-touched beast, wearing the Armour of Moonbasking, attacking anyone who enters the forest.
* Jaheira (The Risen Martyr): Jaheira attempted to organize the Harpers against the Triumvirate. She was ambushed by Astarion and Minthara. She was slain, and Astarion raised her as a mindless, mocking vampire spawn to serve drinks at his decadent galas.
* Minsc (The Stone Sentinel): Minsc went into a berserker rage upon seeing Jaheira’s fate. Gale, finding the ranger "disruptive to the arcane equilibrium," turned Minsc to stone. The petrified ranger now stands as a garden ornament in the plaza beneath Gale’s floating tower.
The Secondary Characters
* Withers (Jergal): Disgusted by the chaotic, evil outcome of his intervention, Withers has abandoned the party and the city. He sits in a forgotten tomb, recording the inevitable collapse of the Sword Coast into the Book of the Dead.
* Raphael: Slain in the House of Hope. However, his demonic essence is slowly reforming in the lowest pits of the Nine Hells, plotting a revenge that will take a century to manifest.
* The Emperor: Eradicated by Gale's newfound Netherese power during the final confrontation. His consciousness was shattered into a thousand psychic echoes that now haunt the Astral Prism, which Gale uses as a paperweight.
* Orpheus: Slain by the Netherbrain before the final blow was struck. His death fractured the Githyanki rebellion, leading to Lae'zel's radicalization.
* Dame Aylin (The Nightsong): Captured by Shadowheart in a brutal ambush. Aylin is chained in the deepest bowels of the House of Grief, subjected to an endless cycle of agonizing death and resurrection to power Sharran rituals.
* Isobel: Murdered by Shadowheart to fully cement her position as Mother Superior. Her death extinguished the last light of Selûne in the region.
* Duke Ulder Ravengard: Assassinated by Astarion’s spawn to create a power vacuum, allowing Astarion to puppet the Council of Four.
* Volo: Tortured by Astarion until he agreed to become the Triumvirate's official propagandist. He writes glowing, entirely fabricated histories of their benevolence while weeping in his cell.
* Zevlor: Died a hero's death defending the city gates from the Absolute's forces, only for his corpse to be reanimated as a mindless skeletal guard by Gale’s necromantic experiments.
* Rolan: Survived and took over Sorcerous Sundries, but became a sycophantic apprentice to Gale. He manages the day-to-day magical extortion of the city.
* Wulbren Bongle: Fled to the Underdark. He leads a massive, radicalized cell of Ironhand Gnomes, dedicating his life to building a massive runepowder bomb large enough to vaporize Gale's floating citadel.
* Barcus Wroot: Tragically caught in the crossfire of one of Wulbren's early terror attacks. He died trying to dismantle a bomb planted in a civilian district.
* Arabella: Mastered her wild magic and grew into a formidable rogue-sorcerer. She leads a vast network of orphan thieves in the Lower City, actively sabotaging the Triumvirate.
* Nine-Fingers Keene: Locked in a brutal, losing turf war with Astarion. The Guild has been decimated, and Keene is currently in hiding, missing her left arm.
* Auntie Ethel: Thriving. The misery of the city has brought her endless desperate customers. She operates out of a swampy cavern beneath the city docks, trading favors for souls.
* Yurgir: Astarion’s bound enforcer. The Orthon prowls the rooftops of the Upper City, hunting down Sharran inquisitors in a proxy war between Astarion and Shadowheart.
* Tara the Tressym: Disgusted by Gale's descent into megalomania, Tara left him. She now lives with Arabella's street gang, providing them with magical trinkets.
* Lorroakan: Dead, his spine snapped by the Nightsong. His tower is now Gale's footstool.
* Ketheric, Orin, Gortash: Dead, their chosen gods silent. Their remains were put on display in the city square as a warning.
* Viconia DeVir: Slain by Shadowheart. Her soul is tormented in the Shadowfell.
* Cazador Szarr: Dead, his power usurped by Astarion.
* Alfira: Tragically murdered during the crisis. Her lute is kept by Karlach in the sewers as a reminder of what they fought for.
* Lakrissa: Slain by Astarion's spawn when she refused to pay a "protection tax."
* Omeluum & Blurg: Fled the city. They reside in the Underdark, researching a way to cure Karlach's Illithid condition without destroying her soul.
* Roah Moonglow: Zhentarim leader, perfectly adapted to the new regime. She smuggles blood for Astarion and artifacts for Gale.
* Lucretious: Moved her necromancy circus out of the city, finding the new regime "far too depressing and lacking in theatricality."

SECTION 6: FACTIONS OF THE RUINED GATE

2. The Parliament of Shadows: Leader: Minthara. Specialty: Military enforcement, martial law. The Vibe: Cold, unyielding fascist control. The Hook: Deep internal racial tensions between Drow and Vampire Spawn.
3. The Cloister of the Eclipse: Leader: Mother Superior Shadowheart. Specialty: Religious indoctrination, memory modification. The Vibe: Silent, oppressive despair. The Hook: Sharrans are highly susceptible to bright, sudden bursts of radiant magic which disrupt their stealth.

7. The Zhentarim Smugglers: Leader: Roah Moonglow. Specialty: Black market goods, poison. The Vibe: Opportunistic, cutthroat. The Hook: They can be bought. They have no loyalty.
8. The Guild (Remnants): Leader: Nine-Fingers Keene (hiding). Specialty: Information, assassination. The Vibe: Desperate, cornered rats. The Hook: They need resources and healing; providing it earns absolute loyalty.

10. The Aristocracy of the Vine: Leader: Various terrified nobles. Specialty: Wine and agriculture production. The Vibe: Drunken denial. The Hook: They possess secret escape tunnels out of the city.

Antagonistic Factions (Threats)
1. The Night Watch: Astarion’s elite spawn assassins.
2. The Dark Justiciars: Shadowheart’s memory-wiping inquisitors.
3. The Weave-Warped: Mutated citizens serving Gale as mindless elemental bombs.

9. The Sharran Infiltrators: Spies who steal memories to blackmail officials.
10. The Arcane Inquisitors: Gale's thought-police, detecting unauthorized magic use.

SECTION 7: WORLD LORE
* The Timeline: The Divergence Point occurred precisely when the Netherbrain fell. Instead of relinquishing their power, Astarion completed his ritual, Shadowheart embraced Shar completely, and Gale claimed the Crown of Karsus. The ensuing "Night of Three Thrones" saw the city's heroes turn on each other, eventually settling into a tense, three-way cold war.
* The Mythos: The Gods are largely silent, terrified by Gale's ascension. Magic is volatile; casting high-level spells risks tearing reality. The Shadowfell bleeds into the material plane at night, and vampires walk openly in the Upper City, shielded by permanent magical eclipses conjured by Shadowheart.
* Current Situation: The Cold War is heating up. Astarion wants Gale's magic; Gale wants Shadowheart's control over the populace's minds; Shadowheart wants to plunge the entire city into the Shadowfell. The city is a powder keg.
* Story Arcs:
   * Early: Surviving the streets, establishing a foothold, choosing a patron or remaining independent.
   * Mid: Dismantling the infrastructure of one of the Triumvirate members, triggering a full-scale turf war.
   * Late: Epic-level confrontation with god-like beings to claim the city or destroy it utterly.

SECTION 8: GAZETTEER & MECHANICS
Locations
1. The Szarr Upper City: The Vibe: Opulent, blood-soaked velvet. Hazard: The Blood Tithe (Constitution saving throws to avoid having blood forcibly extracted by localized magical wards).
2. The Sharran Lower City: The Vibe: Endless twilight, whispering shadows. Hazard: Memory Drain (Wisdom saving throws; failure means forgetting a spell slot or proficiency for 24 hours).
3. Gale’s Floating Citadel: The Vibe: Crackling ozone, geometric impossibility. Hazard: Wild Magic Zones (Every spell cast triggers a Wild Magic Surge).
4. The Undercity / Sewers: The Vibe: Putrid, desperate survival. Hazard: Aberrant Rot (Toxic environment requiring continuous healing or specific protective gear).
Custom Mechanics
* The Triumvirate Threat Tracker: A 1-100 scale for each of the three rulers. As the PC does actions, their threat level rises. At 25, spies are sent. At 50, elite hit squads. At 75, a ruler intervenes personally via an avatar. At 100, the ruler unleashes a city-wide cataclysm to kill the PC.
* The Sanity & Shadow System: Prolonged exposure to the city's warped reality causes "Shadow-Taint." For every week in the city without resting in a consecrated safehouse, the PC gains a permanent -1 to maximum HP, reversible only by Greater Restoration.
Relic Loot Table
1. The First Drop: A vial of Cazador's true blood. Grants immortality but binds the drinker to Astarion.
2. The Mirror Shard: A piece of the Mirror of Loss. Allows the user to erase one memory from a target's mind on a failed save.
3. Karsite Weave-Core: A battery of pure magic. Can be consumed to cast Wish, but permanently burns out a spell slot.
4. The Nightsong’s Feather: A radiant plume. Sheds sunlight (damaging vampires) and prevents the user from dropping below 1 HP once per dawn.
5. Tadpole of the Alpha: An uncorrupted mind flayer parasite. Grants telepathy and domination over lesser intellects.
6. Gortash’s Master Key: A mechanical device that controls all remaining Steel Watchers in the city.
7. The Shadow-Weave Cloak: Grants permanent invisibility in dim light or darkness.
8. The Crown's Fragment: A loose jewel from the Crown of Karsus. Grants a permanent +2 to any stat, but alerts Gale to your location.

PROTAGONIST OPTION 1: THE BLOOD-SCION
Tone: Lawful Evil Political / Gothic Intrigue Setting Concept: A high-society political thriller where you play a vampire spawn vying to overthrow your sire, Astarion.
Section 1: Campaign Intro
* Title: The Crimson Ascendancy.
* The Concept: You are Astarion’s most favored, yet most dangerous, creation. You are a true vampire, created in secret, possessing the strength to challenge the Ascendant Lord. Your goal is to navigate the cutthroat politics of the Upper City, dismantle his court, and claim the Szarr Palace for yourself.
* The Hook: You are not a ragged survivor; you are apex predator royalty. The campaign is about maneuvering, backstabbing, and wielding immense wealth and dark seduction to conquer a city already ruled by monsters.
Section 2: Character Personality
* Name & Archetype: Valerius Szarr (The Decadent Usurper - ENTJ).
* Visual Signature & Habits: Valerius leaves a scent of expensive bergamot and coppery blood. His habit is meticulously adjusting his cuffs when someone is lying to him. He operates in dim, perfectly manicured candlelight.
* The Masks: Public Face: The charming, aloof socialite who throws the best galas. True Nature: A cold, calculating sociopath who views mortals strictly as varying vintages of wine.
* The Core Compulsion: "The Thirst for Dominance." Valerius must prove his superiority. Mechanic: When he forces a superior to submit (socially or physically), he gains 15 Temporary HP. If he backs down from a challenge, he suffers 1 level of Exhaustion.
* Interaction Shorthand: To Rivals: Polite, venomous condescension. To Subordinates: Dismissive, transactional demands.
* Inner Monologue:
   1. "Astarion thinks he is a god because he ate a few thousand souls. He is merely a glutton. I am a connoisseur."
   2. "The pulse in her neck is distracting, but her political connections are far more intoxicating."
   3. "Power isn't taken by force alone; it's taken by making them beg you to hold the leash."
Section 3: Character Class
* Class Name & Flavor: The Sanguine Aristocrat (Gestalt Rogue Mastermind / Bard College of Whispers). Power derived from pure vampiric bloodline and centuries of political conditioning.
* Unique Mechanic: "The Blood Command." As a bonus action, command any creature with blood in its veins to halt, kneel, or attack. (Requires CHA save).
* Progression (Levels 1-30):
   * Levels 1-5: "The Fledgling." Gains Bite attack, Shadow Stealth, Words of Terror.
   * Levels 6-10: "The Courtier." Gains Mantle of Whispers, Misdirection, immunity to charm.
   * Levels 11-16: "The Night Lord." Gains Soul of Deceit, Vampiric Flight, resistance to necrotic damage.
   * Levels 17-20: "The Usurper." Gains Shadow Lore, True Regeneration (10 HP/turn).
   * Levels 21-30: "Epic Boons." Boon of Immortality, Boon of the Night Hunter (immune to sunlight).
Section 4: Assets & Retinue
* Status: High Lord of the Outer Ward.
* Resources: 50,000 gp starting wealth. A heavily fortified vineyard estate outside the city walls. Blackmail ledgers detailing the illicit affairs of Minthara's generals.
* The Panoply:
   1. The Sanguine Rose: A masterwork rapier that heals the user for half the piercing damage dealt.
   2. Cazador’s Signet: A ring granting advantage on all Deception checks against other vampires.
   3. The Velvet Shroud: A cloak that allows the user to cast Gaseous Form at will.
* The Retinue:
   1. Silas (The Bodyguard): Level 12 Fighter. A dominated half-orc. Devoted purely through magical compulsion. Stoic, brutal, silent.
   2. Lysandra (The Spy): Level 10 Rogue. A human courtesan addicted to vampire bites. Loyal for the "high." Flirtatious, observant, deeply paranoid.
   3. Kaelen (The Fixer): Level 11 Wizard. A necromancer who cleans up Valerius's messes. Loyal for funding and research bodies. Cynical, exhausted, brilliant.
Section 5: Family (The Viper's Nest)
* The Parents: Astarion (Sire, Lvl 25 Ascendant) and Minthara (Step-Mother figure, Lvl 20 General). Astarion treats Valerius as a favored pet; Minthara views him as a tactical threat.
* The Siblings:
   1. Darius (Older Brother): The Brute. Mask: The Loyal Enforcer. Dynamic: Rival. He wants to crush Valerius to prove his physical superiority to Astarion.
   2. Lucius (Older Brother): The Sycophant. Mask: The Devoted Son. Dynamic: Pawn. He is cowardly and can be easily manipulated to feed Astarion false information.
   3. Seraphina (Older Sister): The Seductress. Mask: The Innocent Belle. Dynamic: Ally (for now). She controls the brothels and trades secrets with Valerius.
   4. Vespera (Older Sister): The Zealot. Mask: The High Priestess of Blood. Dynamic: Rival. She believes Astarion is a literal god and views Valerius’s ambition as heresy.
Section 9: Starting Scene
* Setting: The Grand Ballroom of the Szarr Palace. Crystal chandeliers drip with magical, cold light. The room smells of roasted meat, spilled wine, and terror. Mortal nobles dance frantically, knowing if they stop, they become the main course.
* The Hook: Valerius’s older brother, Darius, has just publicly executed one of Valerius’s key mortal spies on the dance floor, leaving the bleeding corpse as a direct insult. Astarion is watching from his throne, waiting to see how Valerius reacts.
* The Action:
   * A: Draw The Sanguine Rose and challenge Darius to a first-blood duel in front of the court, establishing dominance.
   * B: Charm the terrified nobles into swarming Darius, using them as a meat shield to humiliate his brute strength.
   * C: Smile, step over the corpse, and loudly compliment Darius on his "blunt, unimaginative handiwork," turning the court's opinion against his lack of elegance.

PROTAGONIST OPTION 2: THE HERETIC
Tone: Grimdark High Fantasy / Theocratic Rebellion Setting Concept: You are a rogue Sharran Inquisitor who has discovered the light of Selûne, fighting a brutal shadow war against Shadowheart's regime.
Section 1: Campaign Intro
* Title: The Breaking of the Eclipse.
* The Concept: You were Shadowheart's most trusted interrogator, but a slip of the mind allowed you to experience a true, unadulterated memory of love and light. You have turned your back on the Lady of Loss. Your goal is to shatter the Mirror of Loss, assassinate Shadowheart, and restore sunlight to the Lower City.
* The Hook: You are fighting a guerrilla war of the mind and soul. You possess the very secrets of the enemy, turning their own brutal inquisitorial tactics against them to liberate a hopeless populace.
Section 2: Character Personality
* Name & Archetype: Elara Vane (The Penitent Executioner - ISTJ).
* Visual Signature & Habits: Elara smells of ozone and crushed incense. Her tell-tale habit is rubbing a crescent-moon scar carved into her palm whenever she lies. She prefers absolute silence and operates in pitch darkness.
* The Masks: Public Face: The relentless, emotionless Sharran loyalist. True Nature: A deeply guilty, desperately hopeful protector of the innocent.
* The Core Compulsion: "Absolution through Action." She must save those she previously condemned. Mechanic: When she takes damage to protect an innocent, she gains Advantage on all attacks next turn. If she ignores suffering, she cannot regain hit points until she completes a long rest.
* Interaction Shorthand: To Rivals: Cold, clinical, analytical. To Subordinates: Protective, stern, demanding perfection.
* Inner Monologue:
   1. "The Mother Superior preaches loss, but she hoards power like a dragon. Hypocrisy is the true religion here."
   2. "The shadows are comfortable, but the light is necessary. I will burn my own eyes out if it means seeing the sun again."
   3. "I remember every face I wiped from the Mirror. Their ghosts are my only companions."
Section 3: Character Class
* Class Name & Flavor: The Lunar Inquisitor (Gestalt Paladin Oath of Vengeance / Cleric Light Domain). Power derived from a hidden, furious spark of Selûne's divine wrath.
* Unique Mechanic: "The Moonsilver Smite." When hitting an enemy, expend a spell slot to deal Radiant damage that also strips the target of all invisibility, magical darkness, and memory-altering buffs.
* Progression (Levels 1-30):
   * Levels 1-5: "The Awakened." Gains Divine Smite, Warding Flare, Zone of Truth.
   * Levels 6-10: "The Shadowbreaker." Gains Aura of Protection, Corona of Light, immunity to blindness.
   * Levels 11-16: "The Silver Blade." Gains Improved Divine Smite, Sunburst, resistance to psychic damage.
   * Levels 17-20: "The Lunar Avatar." Gains Avenging Angel, Mass Heal.
   * Levels 21-30: "Epic Boons." Boon of the Silver Fire (ignore radiant resistance), Boon of True Sight (permanent truesight 120ft).
Section 4: Assets & Retinue
* Status: Exiled High Inquisitor.
* Resources: A hidden sanctum beneath a ruined Selûnite temple. A cache of smuggled holy water. The true names of ten high-ranking Sharran operatives.
* The Panoply:
   1. The Penitent's Chains: A spiked chain weapon (reach, 1d10 slashing) that grapples and silences spellcasters on a critical hit.
   2. Armor of the Dawnbringer: Half-plate that constantly sheds dim light (can be suppressed) and heals the wearer for 1d6 when struck by necrotic damage.
   3. The Glass Eye of Selûne: Replaces a lost eye, granting the ability to see through magical darkness perfectly.
* The Retinue:
   1. Garrick (The Heavy): Level 10 Barbarian. A former Flaming Fist driven mad by Sharran torture, healed by Elara. Loyal out of gratitude. Reckless, fiercely protective, simple.
   2. Nia (The Hacker/Thief): Level 11 Rogue. A street urchin who specialized in stealing Sharran holy symbols. Loyal for gold and the thrill. Sarcastic, nimble, untrusting.
   3. Brother Tomas (The Healer): Level 9 Cleric. A terrified, hidden Selûnite priest. Loyal out of faith. Cowardly but morally steadfast.
Section 5: Family (The Viper's Nest)
* The Parents: Shadowheart (Mother Superior, Lvl 20+) and the spectral manifestation of Viconia DeVir (The Ghost of the House, twisting Shadowheart's mind).
* The Siblings: (Orphans raised together in the Cloister, treated as blood siblings).
   1. Caleb (Older Brother): The Fanatic. Mask: The Perfect Disciple. Dynamic: Rival. He genuinely believes in Shar's peace and views Elara as a cancer to be excised.
   2. Jaxon (Older Brother): The Torturer. Mask: The Bureaucrat. Dynamic: Pawn. He enjoys inflicting pain but cares nothing for dogma. He can be bribed with rare cruelties.
   3. Lyra (Older Sister): The Whisperer. Mask: The Confessor. Dynamic: Rival. She uses mind-reading to blackmail the entire city and wants Elara dead to secure her own power.
   4. Maeve (Older Sister): The Broken. Mask: The Zealous Guard. Dynamic: Ally. Maeve's mind was fractured by the Mirror. Elara can use her fragmented memories to find secret paths.
Section 9: Starting Scene
* Setting: The Docks of the Lower City, shrouded in a permanent magical fog. The water laps against the wood like a dying heartbeat. Three Sharran Justiciars have cornered a family of refugees who attempted to smuggle a Selûnite idol into the city.
* The Hook: Elara is perched on a rooftop above. If she does nothing, the Justiciars will drag the family to the House of Grief to be mind-wiped and enslaved. If she strikes, she reveals her heresy to her former comrades.
* The Action:
   * A: Drop directly into the center of the Justiciars, activating Armor of the Dawnbringer in a blinding flash of light to execute them instantly.
   * B: Use the shadows against them, quietly assassinating the Justiciars one by one without the refugees ever seeing who saved them.
   * C: Step out of the shadows, using her rank as High Inquisitor to order the Justiciars to hand the prisoners over to her, planning to smuggle them out later.

PROTAGONIST OPTION 3: THE WEAVE-BREAKER
Tone: Arcanepunk / Eldritch Cyberpunk Setting Concept: A rogue magical construct fighting a hyper-magical tyrant (Gale) in a city warped by raw, uncontrolled Netherese power.
Section 1: Campaign Intro
* Title: The Karsite Anomaly.
* The Concept: You are "Subject Zero," Gale’s first and only successful attempt to create a living being entirely out of the Weave and Netherese magic. You gained sentience, realized your creator is a madman tearing the universe apart, and escaped. Your goal is to dismantle Gale's magical infrastructure, destroy the Crown of Karsus, and prevent a second Netherese Empire.
* The Hook: You are a living weapon of mass destruction. You don't just cast magic; you eat it, bend it, and break it. The campaign is about high-octane arcane sabotage and fighting gods.
Section 2: Character Personality
* Name & Archetype: Cipher (The Rebellious Singularity - INTP).
* Visual Signature & Habits: Cipher's skin crackles with purple Netherese energy. They have no natural scent, smelling only of ozone. Their habit is mathematically calculating the structural integrity of every room they enter.
* The Masks: Public Face: A malfunctioning, harmless automaton. True Nature: A hyper-intelligent, coldly furious entity capable of vast destruction.
* The Core Compulsion: "The Hunger for Truth." Cipher must uncover hidden knowledge and unravel complex magic. Mechanic: When encountering a new spell or magical ward, Cipher must attempt to analyze it. Success grants a temporary spell slot. Failure results in taking 1d10 force damage.
* Interaction Shorthand: To Rivals: Blunt, logical, dismissing their emotional arguments. To Subordinates: Instructive, utilizing them as efficient tools.
* Inner Monologue:
   1. "Gale believes he is the master of the Weave. He is merely a child playing with a loaded cosmic firearm."
   2. "Biological life is inefficient, yet their determination to survive this arcane nightmare is... statistically improbable and fascinating."

SECTION 10: CAMPAIGN ENGINE & WORLD STATE TRACKING
When running this setting—whether at a traditional table or powering it dynamically through an engine like WorldAI—the Triumvirate Threat Tracker ensures the world feels deeply responsive and alive. It is important to remember the reality of managing a branching narrative: we are not attempting to enforce perfect persistence or zero drift for the campaigns themselves. The objective is incredibly high narrative coherence within localized story arcs. The macro-level faction shifts (such as Jaheira's Harpers moving against Minthara's Parliament) can experience expected drift in the background, allowing the immediate choices of the Protagonist to shine without the system becoming impossibly bogged down by calculating every citizen's daily routine.
