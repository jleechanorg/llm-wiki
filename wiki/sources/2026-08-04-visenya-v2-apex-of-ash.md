# Campaign Bible: The Apex of Ash — Shadow of the Mother (campaign visenya v2)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign visenya v2" — an A Song of Ice and Fire campaign bible set during the liberation of Astapor and the march on Meereen. Player character: [[VisenyaBelaerys]], age 16, the last scion of [[HouseBelaerys]], hidden in plain sight beside [[DaenerysTargaryen]]. Part of the ongoing Visenya campaign line (a Visenya V6 transcript was previously ingested; this is the v2 campaign-bible document).

## Premise

While the world believes Daenerys Targaryen is the "Last Dragon," Visenya is the true Apex — final survivor of a Valyrian family so magically potent they attempted Apotheosis to become gods, an act this canon says triggered the Doom of Valyria. Rescued by Daenerys from a high-end Lysene pleasure house in Slaver's Bay, Visenya travels as a "penniless" freedwoman. Daenerys suspects she is a lost Targaryen, vows to protect her, and commands her to hide her face — fearing her radiance will "ignite the madness of men and the jealousy of queens."

## Character

- **Name/Archetype**: Visenya Belaerys, "The Silver Dragon" — "The Reluctant God-Queen" (INTJ).
- **Visual signature**: "Exceptional Beauty" that makes standard Targaryens look plain; purest silver-gold hair; violet eyes so deep they appear black in low light. Habit: tracing a hidden Signet Ring when "the threads of the Weave begin to pull."
- **Masks** (dual identity):
  1. *The Freedwoman* — soot-smudged girl in an oversized straw hat, playing traumatized victim to maintain [[FunctionalObscurity]].
  2. *The Apex Heir* — sole survivor of the Fourteen Flames' most powerful line; views the Targaryens as lesser "usurpers" of the true fire.
- **Core compulsion**: "Breaking Authority" — loathes anyone who believes they own her or the world.
- **Interaction shorthand**: reverent warmth + tactical advice toward Daenerys; cold clinical detachment toward admirers ([[JorahMormont]]) that only fuels their obsession.
- **Signature inner monologue**: "She calls herself the Mother of Dragons, yet she does not know what it is to be the flame."

## Mechanics

- **[[BelaerysAllure]]**: any NPC who sees her true face must pass a DC 18 Wisdom save or be Charmed; using it triggers *The Prickle* — the Unfriendly Remnants gain +5 to track her location. A direct beauty-as-liability/leverage loop in the [[BeautyAsWeapon]] family.
- **Class**: [[DragonScholarClass]] (Wizard subclass) — power manifests from the Purest Valyrian Blood; scholar of the "Draconic Weave" predating the Iron Throne. INT 18 / WIS 16.
- **[[SubtleApotheosis]]**: full spellcaster progression + always-on Subtle Spell — no verbal/somatic components, magic completely invisible in a low-magic setting.
- **Royal Insight**: Charisma-based social checks (Persuasion, Deception, Intimidation) use INT or WIS instead.
