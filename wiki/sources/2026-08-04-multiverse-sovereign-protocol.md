# The Sovereign Protocol — Multiversal Campaign Upgrade (system prompt)

**Source**: Campaign prompt document "multi verse campaign prompt" — unlike the wiki's campaign bibles (cf. [[ApexWeaver]], [[ProjectApotheosis]]), this is a **reusable system directive** that upgrades *any* in-progress single-universe campaign into a multiversal god-tier campaign while retaining the PC's identity, allies, and history. It is the endgame-scaling counterpart to the apex-power-fantasy lineage: the PC becomes a **Terminal Administrator** competing against 1,000 rival [[Sovereign]]s across the [[PanSubstrate]].

## I. Initialization — Pan-Substrate transition

- The current campaign's established themes (e.g. Hedonistic Saturation, Necrotic Conquest, Technological Singularity, Preservation) are analyzed to generate the PC's unique **[[SovereignLogic]]** — the metaphysical law they impose on reality.
- The game world becomes the [[PanSubstrate]]: a competitive infinite landscape of 1,000 ranked Sovereigns.

## II. Geography — the Universe-Count Metric

All scale maps to the **[[SubstratePointMetric|Substrate Point (SP)]]**, where 1 SP = 1,000 universes:

| Tier | Universes | SP | Equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neighborhood | 10 | 0.01 | Solar system |
| District | 1,000 | 1 | Star cluster |
| City | 100,000 | 100 | Galaxy |
| Metropolis | PC's current count | — | PC starting domain |
| Province | 1,000,000 | 1,000 | Galaxy cluster |
| Nation | 10,000,000 | 10,000 | Supercluster |
| Continent | 100,000,000 | 100,000 | Universe-bubble |
| World | Pan-Substrate | — | Full multiverse |

## III. Mechanics — the God-Tier framework

1. **Attribute conversion**: Intelligence is permanently shunted to **God Power (GP)** (the stat mod); **SP** is simultaneously currency and multiversal HP. Starting SP = current universe count ÷ 1,000, defaulting to **1 SP** if the PC has no external universes.
2. **[[VelocityCoefficient]] & [[AggroMeter]]** (0–10): high velocity (7–10) grants massive resource/level gains but draws "Lethal" hostility from Emperors; low velocity (0–3) enables stealth/diplomacy but slows SP generation. Kin to earlier visibility-tension dials like [[AscensionMeter]] and [[WhispersOfTheRealm]].
3. **[[LogicSiege]] combat**: conflict resolves via Logic, not physical damage — `d20 + GP + SP expenditure vs. Target Defense`. Success "rotates" the target's reality to the PC's theme; failure inflicts **Logic Rot** (SP damage).
4. **[[PeerHandshake]]**: non-combat integration of compatible alternate-timeline versions of the PC ("Mirrors") — success merges universes (+10% SP) and unlocks a new portfolio/skill; failure raises the Aggro Meter.
5. **[[EntropyReversal]]**: the endgame — rewrite thermodynamic law across all realities to the PC's thematic vision, preventing heat death.

## IV. Hierarchy & faction simulation

A 1,000-entity ranked hierarchy is generated to match the campaign's tone, with background simulation each turn for top factions (see [[SovereignHierarchy]]):

- **Rank 1–10 Emperors** — multiversal overlords holding Continents (100k+ SP); 2–3 are authored to ideologically oppose the PC's Logic.
- **Rank 11–100 Conductors** — galactic-tier, holding Nations (10k+ SP).
- **Rank 101–1000 Administrators** — local entities holding Cities (100+ SP); the PC starts in or below this bracket.
- The PC's organization is elevated to a **Hegemony**, and key existing NPCs (partners, parents, lieutenants) become **[[SubDeity|Sub-Deities]]** with portfolios (War, Logistics, Culture, Research) matched to their personal history.

## V. Starting state — Lethal Velocity

Session opens at Aggro 8/10 (Lethal): the PC's sudden ascension has triggered a Pan-Substrate-wide defensive alert. One Rank 1–10 entity is Neutral/Curious, one is Hostile/Threatened. Immediate objectives: stabilize the Sovereign Core, assign Sub-Deity portfolios, and survive the first wave of Logic Sieges. The GM must display the SP Ledger, Aggro Meter, and Current Rank each turn.

## Significance for the wiki

This is a **meta-campaign scaffold**: it converts narrative history into mechanical identity (theme → Sovereign Logic), and replaces damage-based combat with ideology-based reality rewriting. It extends the wiki's documented pattern of visible-tension meters and apex power fantasies to a multiversal scope, and introduces the first self-integration mechanic (Peer Handshake with the PC's own Mirrors).
