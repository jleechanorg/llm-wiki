## Summary
A complete 175-turn solo TTRPG campaign log from the Voyage platform, set in the world of LARION. The half-elf warlock Alexiel of the Tethlar line returns from exile to reclaim her family's ruined estate (Riverside Manor), discovers a smuggling racket in her cellars, seduces and politically binds her rival Lady Ashbury, corrupts the Crown's surveyor, sends shadow-entities to spy on the King, summons 25 Gloomstalkers, and ultimately absorbs the power of a Demon King's heart. Single-player campaign with heavy social/political focus.

## Key Claims
- Alexiel of House Tethlar (half-elf mage/warlock) reclaims Riverside Manor from near-bankruptcy on Day 237
- Daran, Hand of the Thieves' Guild for the Silverflow, agrees to a 12% commission deal for use of the manor cellars
- Lady Ashbury becomes a political and romantic ally after a multi-turn seduction/negotiation sequence
- A 20-natural magic attack (Turn 12) and a 20-natural absorption roll (Turn 113) are the two critical combat successes
- The 25 Gloomstalkers are deployed in batches of 5 — 5 with Alexiel, 5 with King's men, 5 with surveyor, 10 elsewhere
- The patron is abyss-themed; Alexiel's pact magic produces violet-shadow effects, shadow-implosion, and shadow-binding
