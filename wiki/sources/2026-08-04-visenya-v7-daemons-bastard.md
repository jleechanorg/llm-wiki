# Campaign Setup: Visenya v7 — Daemon's Great Bastard

**Source**: Campaign setup prompt "campaign visenya v7" — the seventh iteration of the ongoing Visenya campaign line. This version reframes the player character as [[VisenyaWaters|Visenya]], **bastard daughter of Prince [[DaemonTargaryen]]** and a dead Targaryen lady, making her a [[GreatBastard]] (both parents of royal Valyrian blood despite illegitimate birth).

## Character premise

- **Identity**: Visenya, age 16, acknowledged bastard of [[DaemonTargaryen]]; her mother was a Targaryen lady who has died, so both bloodlines are dragonlord stock.
- **Appearance as narrative device**: she "stands out" because she looks like a true Valyrian with very strong royal features — her face itself broadcasts royal blood her birth status denies her (see [[ValyrianFeaturesAsStatus]]).
- **Opening scene requested**: she has **just claimed a medium-sized dragon** (see [[DragonClaiming]]) and is now "a serious battle power" on [[RhaenyraTargaryen|Rhaenyra]]'s side — placing the campaign in the [[DanceOfTheDragons]] civil war (Blacks vs Greens, ~129-131 AC).

## Position in the Visenya campaign line

This is the 7th distinct framing of the Visenya player character:

| Version | Identity | Era |
|---|---|---|
| v2 | [[VisenyaBelaerys]], hidden Apex beside Daenerys | Slaver's Bay |
| v3 | [[VisenyaBelaerys]], Sorcerer at the Sowing claiming Vermithor | Dance, 129 AC |
| v4 | [[VisenyaTargaryen]], trueborn daughter of Maekar, rides with Dunk | 209 AC |
| **v7** | **Visenya, Great Bastard of Daemon** | **Dance, ~129 AC** |

v7 shares the Dance-era setting with v3 but swaps the hidden-power Belaerys frame for an **openly legitimacy-coded bastard**: instead of concealing an Apex bloodline, the drama engine is the gap between her royal looks/dragon and her bastard status. Consistent with the user's documented [[BeautyAsWeapon]] / power-fantasy campaign taste: the character is visibly exceptional from scene one.

## Key claims

1. Player character is a bastard ("great bastard") of Daemon Targaryen and a deceased Targaryen lady.
2. She has strong true-Valyrian royal features that make her conspicuous.
3. First scene opens immediately after she claims a medium-sized dragon at age 16.
4. She is aligned with Rhaenyra's Blacks as a serious battle asset.
