## Section 9: Starting Scene

### Morning (06:00:00)

The wind off the Gullet does not blow; it claws. It comes howling over the black, jagged obsidian ramparts of Dragonstone carrying the bitter, icy sting of sea-salt and the heavy, sulfurous breath of the Dragonmont—a perpetual, choking gray smoke that stains the morning sky the color of a bruised lung.

You stand upon the edge of the Sea Dragon Tower’s high terrace, the cold stone beneath your scavenged leather boots slick with the damp mist of the narrow sea. Your silver-gold hair, untied and magnificent, whips like a banner of ancient Valyrian silk against the dark rock, the pale morning light catching its threads until it seems to glow with its own inner, ancestral fire. Your features—the high, haughty cheekbones of the Old Freehold and eyes like cold, brilliant flints of amethyst—are set in a mask of absolute, clinical focus.

Down below, in the lower training yard, the world is a chaotic, bleeding canvas of mud, ash, and old iron.

The Sowing of the Seeds has just reached its violent, terrifying conclusion. Prince Lucerys is dead, his flesh and his mount swallowed by the narrow sea near Storm's End, and the Queen’s grief has turned the air of Dragonstone into an absolute furnace of rage. The dragonseeds have just emerged from the smoking vents of the volcano, their smallfolk blood tested against the fire, and the lower yard is thick with the loud, drunken bravado of the commoners who survived the transition.

You can hear Hugh Hammer’s voice—a gravelly, low-born roar that bounces off the citadel walls like a smith’s hammer hitting an anvil. He stands near the entrance of the dragon-pits, a massive tankard of sour ale sloshing over his raw-hide jerkin, his thick arms spread wide as he boasts to a circle of terrified, awestruck gold cloaks. Behind him, resting its massive, bronze-scaled head on the blackened stone, is Vermithor. The Bronze Fury is a mountain of ancient malice, his great nostrils huffing plumes of white-hot steam that turn the morning mist into a boiling fog.

"The Old King's mount!" Hugh bellows, his laughter coarse and heavy with wine. "He looked into my face and he saw the iron in my blood! The maesters can keep their scrolls and their names—the beast answers to the hammer now! When Criston Cole sees this bronze shadow over his vanguard, he’ll be dropping his lance into the mud before I even whisper *Dracarys*!"

A few paces away, slumped against an old trebuchet frame, Ulf White lets out a loud, wet cackle. His pale hair is matted with tar and grease from the shipyards, his eyes bloodshot from a night spent draining the citadel’s lower cellars. He points a trembling, salt-crusted hand toward the pale, silver-winged form of Silverwing resting on the outer ridgeline. "The silver queen!" Ulf laughs, his voice thick with ale. "She’s as smooth as silk and twice as deadly, Hugh! The prince Jacaerys talks of 'wing-formations' and 'flight-geometry' as if the sky were a map of the Reach, but a dragon doesn't care for a prince's ink! We have the fire, smith! We are the true lords of this island now!"

They are nuclear weapons in the hands of children. They treat the apex predators of the world like prize hounds, their commands born of raw volume, low-born arrogance, and the temporary certainty of wine. They believe that because they have mounted the giant beasts, the sky has surrendered to their ignorance.

A low, rhythmic vibration through the soles of your boots cuts through their shouting.

Behind you, crouching on the narrow obsidian shelf of the tower’s parapet, **Vermithrax** stirs. He is Medium-sized—scaled perfectly to your Level 6 baseline—a searing hatchling of charcoal-black scales and wings the color of cooling lava. He does not possess the mountain-scale bulk of Hugh’s Bronze Fury or the long, silver wingspan of Ulf’s queen. He is lean, agile, a predator built for the high-velocity dive and the surgical strike. But through your unique inheritance—**The Sovereign Singularity**—the bond between you is not a matter of crude shouting or brute force. You claimed him *during* the chaos of the Sowing, walking into the fire alongside the seeds, yet you did not burn, and you did not shout.

You reach out with your mind, your *Empathetic Bond* catching the sharp, predatory pulse of his excitement. He doesn't see a master; he feels the frequency of his own blood. You do not cheat the rules; you speak the High Valyrian of your mother’s scrolls. Your command is a matter of *Resonance*. Through vocal directives and precise physical cues in the saddle, you add your full **Charisma modifier (+4)** to his hit accuracy and saving throws. You have turned a Medium dragon into a surgical execution device. You don't strike hard; you strike *first*, and you strike *exactly where the throat is open*.

The heavy oak doors of the Sea Dragon Tower groan open behind you. Prince Jacaerys Velaryon steps out onto the terrace, his face pale and his posture rigid with the stress of a king who has just realized he has invited the smallfolk into his mother's bedchamber. He looks down at Hugh and Ulf, his jaw tight, his violet eyes tracing the irregular, dangerous movements of their dragons.

You stand level with him, your silver hair gleaming, completely independent of the Targaryen bloodline—an unknown, pure-blooded phantom of the Freehold whose very presence forces his **Insecurity Variable** to increase by +10%. He turns to you, his eyes wide with a profound, diagnostic shock. He has memorized every lineage scroll from Old Valyria to King's Landing, yet your face—a perfect, terrifying mirror of the Conqueror's own queens—carries a majesty that is entirely absent from the royal records.

"They are boasting in the yard, Visenya," Jace says, his voice a low rasp that carries only to you. He stands beside you, his dark hair plastered to his forehead by the mist, his gaze continually snapping back to the flawless symmetry of your profile. "My mother has given them the gold, and the Sea Snake has promised them the ships. But they do not look at her crown with reverence. They look at Vermithor and they see their own claim to the sky. And you..." He pauses, his throat bobbing as he swallows hard, overawed by your unlined features. "You are an enigma. No scroll names your house, no septon recorded your birth, yet you walk out of the Dragonmont looking more like a queen of the Old Freehold than any trueborn heir on this island. My mother is blind with rage over Luke's death, and she sees only numbers. But I see the threat."

He looks at Vermithrax, who holds his hover with a silent, terrifying discipline that makes the larger dragons look clumsy and wild. "You are the only one who can command them without a shout, Visenya. Teach them the law of the sky, or let the fire consume us all."
