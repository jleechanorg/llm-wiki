# Aizen god campaign chat 1 (entries 301-600)

Second segment of the [[Aizen]] god-campaign chat transcript (entries 301-600). This segment centers on Aizen's advancement from Level 9 to Level 10 via a player-directed EXP grant, and documents the campaign's custom defensive stat system.

## Key events

- **Level confirmation**: Aizen confirmed at Level 9 (Paladin 2 / Bard 7), 56,660 XP, +4 proficiency, one ASI used (Charisma +2 at Bard 4).
- **"add itg" miscommunication**: The player's shorthand request for EXP was misread by the DM as a request to add an "Infernal Taint" custom class bonus; the DM began negotiating thematic consistency with the [[Bane]] "Child of Bane" heritage before the player clarified they just wanted EXP. Example of [[Whispers of the Realm]]-style DM overreach on ambiguous input — see [[PlayerDirectedExpGrant]].
- **Level 10 grant**: 7,340 XP added on request (64,000 total), advancing to Paladin 2 / Bard 8. This is a direct instance of [[PlayerDirectedExpGrant]] — the DM computes and applies exactly the XP needed rather than awarding it through play.
- **Bard 8 choices**: second ASI spent on Charisma +2 (final CHA 23, +6 modifier) and *Locate Creature* learned as the new spell (player typo "location creature").

## Character sheet snapshot (post level-up)

- Human, Paladin 2 / Bard 8, Sage background, Neutral Evil, Child of [[Bane]]
- Attributes stack three bonus layers: base + Child of Bane bonus (+2 CON/INT/WIS/CHA) + legendary item bonus (Holy Armor +1 STR/CON/CHA) + ASIs
- HP 59, AC 18 (Holy Armor +4, Bulwark of the Righteous +3)
- [[DestinyRulebookOverrides]] stats: Effective Defense 20, Dodge Rating 7, Armor Rating 20, Armor Mitigation 10

## Observations

- HP was not recalculated at the level-up; the DM asserted 59 HP "already reflected Bard 8" without showing the calculation — a bookkeeping trust gap. See [[DMBookkeepingTrustGap]].
- The DM's accountability framing ("thank you for holding me to account") shows the recurring pattern of the player auditing DM math, as in other [[Aizen]] campaign segments.
