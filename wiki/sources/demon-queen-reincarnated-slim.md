# Ash of Aetheria — Demon Queen Reincarnated (slim, paste-ready)

## Section 1 — Campaign Intro

A thousand years ago she burned half a continent. The Hero-King cut her down while her daughter screamed. She woke up 12 years ago in the body of a Margrave's fifth daughter — quiet, polite, silver-haired, eyes down — and has been counting the bodies ever since. Astheria, the Ash-Crowned Queen, inherited a war and was made into a monster by the necessity of winning it. She lost on purpose when her daughter's life was the hostage. The hero kept his word. Mirael lived.

**Opening:** Imperial Academy of Aetheria, present day. Mushoku Tensei cadence → Frieren melancholy → Witcher moral-grey → political-thriller escalation as a 3-way cold war closes in.

**Act III:** one scene where Ash almost does what Astheria did — burns a city, executes a child-king, accepts a deal from the wrong demon — and chooses differently. The antagonist is the previous-life self; never a monologue.

**CANON-PRIORITY:** Old magic > modern institutional memory. Demons = sentient morally-grey, NOT monsters. Old magic (Aether) structurally stronger than modern (Conduit) — modern L10 Imperial Magus ≈ old-magic L5. No mindless monsters; threats come from people.

## Section 2 — Personality (INFJ — DM notes only)

Quiet, polite, eyes down. Hides. Lets herself get shoved. **Core Compulsion: "Do not become her again."** Three inner-monologue seeds: (1) "I could end this in a second," (2) "She is smiling in the dream again" (Mirael, age 4, asking when Mother will be home), (3) "I will not become her." Coping: counts exits, keeps a private 4,873-name death ledger, INFJ doorslam when overwhelmed. True self: a mother who wanted to plant a garden. Arc: repression → integration.

## Section 3 — Class & Subclass

**Gestalt — Wizard (Bladesinger) 12 / Sorcerer (Aberrant Mind) 12.** True ceiling L25 (old magic) gated behind the Veil + Guilt Lock.

- **The Veil (passive, L1):** Mana Veil Mastery. Detection sees "L4 student." *Auto-fails unless caster is L15+.*
- **The Guilt Lock (active, L5+):** Each L5+ cast = Wisdom save DC 10+spell level. Fail = vivid flashback ends her turn. Success = narrative beat, action completes.
- **Subtle Bladesong (Bladesinger 6 substitution):** Weapon attacks psionically silent — thin silver line, body falls.
- **Ash-Crowned Memory (L17 unlock, Veil-drop):** Once/long rest, cast a spell she knew in her first life. L9 = **Mourning of the Ash-Crowned** (necromancy that *rebuilds what was destroyed*). Veil drops for all within 1 mile.
- **Aether Escalation (L6 unlock):** Cast one tier higher by paying 1 Veil Point. Starts at 0. Earns VP by *lying successfully about her identity* — each lie believed = 1.

Starting spells: standard 5e prepared, max L6 per old-magic cap. **No spells above L6 in spellbook** — writing them *remembers her.*

## Section 8 — Gazetteer & Mechanics

### Rank Tables (parallel tracks)

**Imperial Nobility:** Esquire → Landed Knight (manor+5 levy) → Baron (50 levy, declare border skirmish) → Viscount (market town, tolls+mint) → Count (county+fortified town, raise 500 in 30d, own-banner tournament) → Marquis (march, foreign treaty) → Duke (duchy+Diet seat, veto 1 decree/reign overridable 2/3) → Archduke (royal blood, call Diet, 1 vote/archduchy) → King (historical, reserved 400y) → Emperor (Empress Cassia XI reigning) → **World Emperor (mythic)** — Imperial+Abyssal crowns, vacant 800y. Last holder: Astheria's father.

**Martial:** Squire → Knight-Errant → Knight of the Realm (3-10 retinue) → Knight Commander (50+, field-promotions) → Paladin (divine smite, challenge any monster) → High Paladin (chapter-master, summon entire Order) → Holy Marshal (Knight-Commander of all Orders, audience with Emperor) → **Sword Saint (mythic)** — L20+ duelist, one alive (Sword-Saint of Karn), may challenge any mortal to single duel for any reason.

**Mage:** Apprentice (7y to Magister) → Adept (Conduit Exam, L3) → Magister (Conclave, faculty) → Archmagister (L7+, Conclave Council) → High Magus (Council member, rules Magister disputes) → Imperial Magus (court mage, 1 free L9/imperial-decree) → **Veilwarden (mythic)** — old magic, 3 alive >400y, read ley-lines+refuse imperial order on magical-safety grounds → **Archmage of the Hidden Tower (mythic)** — sovereign of off-plane academy, none alive 800y. Last one: Astheria's teacher, killed by Ash in the war.

### XP Rule (per User Preference #8)

`XP = 0.34 × (next_level_threshold − current_level_threshold)` per die roll. Standard D&D 5e cumulative XP. NO XP during god mode / time-skips / modals. Bonus damage rolls do not separately award XP. Example L4→L5 (distance 3800): `0.34 × 3800 = 1292 XP/die`. Typical combat = 6-12 die rolls = one level-up per major encounter.

### Demon-vision mechanic

Demons see soul silhouettes. Veil holds = quiet 12yo girl. Veil thins = woman with crown-shaped silver burn at temples, silver hair, ember eyes. Liriel has not yet seen the burn. When she does, campaign forks.

### Names-as-Contracts mechanic

A spoken name is a binding contract. (1) **True names** (*Astheria, Mirael, Vael'eth, the Last Fire*) bind speaker to named — they cannot easily lie to the named for the contract's duration. (2) **Cover names** (*Ash, Asthelia Voss*) cost Veil Points when spoken as if true (this is how Ash earns Aether Escalation points). (3) **Renaming rewrites debts** — Mirael survived by renaming her line; the Demon Emperor's surname is, technically, a stolen name. Every Act needs one *name beat* — someone says a true name and consequences cascade.
