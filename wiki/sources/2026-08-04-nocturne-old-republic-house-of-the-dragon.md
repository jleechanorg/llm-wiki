# Campaign Bible: The House of the Dragon (Nocturne Old Republic)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign nocturne old republic" — a Star Wars: The Old Republic alternate-universe campaign bible for the [[NocturneOldRepublic]] line. Premise: Emperor [[Vitiate]] never abandoned the Sith Empire; he evolved it via **[[ProjectApotheosis]]**, a eugenics and Force-breeding program producing superior biological vessels — his children, the [[GoldenLineage]] ("The Imperial Princes and Princesses"). The player is one of these children (Nocturne), publicly a demigod Enforcer, privately a threat the [[CouncilOfTwelve]] wants destroyed. The document text is cut off mid-sentence in the Eclipsed faction section.

## The Family (Golden Lineage)

Dysfunctional, competitive, deadly — all jockeying for the Emperor's favor, knowing he plans to eventually consume all but the strongest of them.

- **The Patriarch — Emperor [[Vitiate]]**: Absolute monarch, resides in the Citadel of Storms above Kaas City. Agenda: **[[TheHarvest]]**, a ritual to consume the galaxy; his children must first break the galaxy's will. Treats his children "like prize hounds — feeding the strong, beating the weak."
- **The Matriarch — High Justice [[Senya]]** (explicit retcon: NOT a Zakuul Knight; former Commander of the Imperial Guard). Runs the Sphere of Justice secret police hunting disloyal Sith. Loves the PC coldly; urges restraint of the PC's "Vaylin side" because losing control means destruction by Vitiate.
- **The Firstborn — High Marshal [[Arcann]]**: Supreme Commander of the Imperial Military and the Obsidian Armada. Lost arm and half his face during the Sacking of Coruscant taking a blow for the Emperor (retcon: not fighting the Outlander); wears a Sith-alchemy cybernetic mask. Sees Nocturne as a "broken vessel" that should have been discarded. Leads the [[ObsidianCircle]].
- **The Twin — Lord [[Thexan]]**: Grand Vizier / Head of Diplomacy; controls the Sith Diplomatic Corps and spies in the Republic Senate. The only sibling kind to Nocturne — suspected ploy. Cleans up Arcann's messes.
- **New Sibling — Lady [[Valtoria]]** (The Alchemist): Keeper of the Sphere of Biotic Science; runs the Genetic Vaults on Korriban creating Sithspawn shock troops. Wants to dissect the galaxy, not rule it; constantly requests Nocturne's bone marrow to replicate the "Prodigy" power.
- **New Sibling — Lord [[Draven]]** (The Beastmaster): Warden of the Untamed; controls the Beast Riders, keeps a pet Terentatek named "Glory," prefers Dxun/Korriban wilds. The Emperor's hunter for fleeing targets; respects Nocturne because "he smells the predator in you."

## The Factions (Civil War in the Shadows)

1. **[[CouncilOfTwelve]]** (Traditionalists) — led by Darth [[DarthMarr|Marr]] (The Patriot) or Darth [[DarthBaras|Baras]] (The Schemer). Believe the Empire serves the Sith Code, not one man; hate the Golden Lineage as the death of the meritocracy ("power should be taken, not inherited"). Goal: prove the Emperor's children incompetent/heretical; they constantly send assassins to test the PC.
2. **[[ObsidianCircle]]** (Neo-Imperialists) — led by Arcann. Total loyalty to the Dragon; abolish the Dark Council for a military hierarchy of the Emperor's children. Goal: total war with the Republic, no treaties.
3. **[[TheEclipsed]]** (Nihilists) — led by [[TheProphetess]], a mysterious Force user, possibly a Kreia clone or new entity. They know the Emperor plans to eat the galaxy. *(Source text cuts off here mid-sentence.)*
