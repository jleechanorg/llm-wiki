Section 1: Campaign Intro & Core Concept
1.1 The Concept
The Sword Coast is fractured. In the wake of the Absolute crisis and the chaotic political restructuring of Baldur’s Gate, the regional balance of power hangs by a thread. Just beyond the sphere of influence of the Gate lies The Iron Dominion, a ruthless corporate-mercenary state where morality is an expensive luxury and survival is dictated by blood, gold, and absolute structural hierarchy. Unlike traditional high-fantasy nations driven by chivalry or divine mandate, the Dominion operates as an institutionalized military-industrial complex. Its primary revenue streams are derived from high-stakes contracts: calculated blackmail, systemic enslavement, targeted political assassinations, and territorial subjugation.
You are Aizen Sosuke, a 16-year-old military, intellectual, and combat prodigy, and the youngest scion of the elite House Sosuke. You operate as a Sergeant within the Dominion’s Black Company while concurrently completing your final year at the Dominion Military Academy—an institution where social engineering, psychological warfare, and lethal duels form the core curriculum. Your ambition is not merely to acquire worldly titles, which you view as clumsy traps of emotion and administration, but to achieve Personal Transcendence: mastering the fundamental laws of the Weave and bending human wills to become the foundational architect of a new reality.
1.2 The Hook
This campaign is an unbridled, high-stakes power fantasy rooted in psychological dominance, intellectual superiority, and political manipulation. You begin not as a stumbling, penniless adventurer, but as an elite military officer and a Level 6 Bladesinger Wizard endowed with formidable resources, secure safehouses, blackmail dossiers, and a loyal retinue. You possess the intellectual tools to outmaneuver veteran generals, subjugate rebellious settlements, and rewrite the political fabric of both the Iron Dominion and the wider Sword Coast from the shadows. Every interaction is an experiment; every opponent is a system waiting to be analyzed, corrected, or dismantled.

Section 3: The Iron Dominion & Political Architecture
3.1 The Military Hierarchy
Power within the Dominion is codified through a strict nine-tier ranking system:
Slave: Stripped of all legal personhood; physical property of the state or noble houses.
Apprentice: Inducted military cadets and low-level auxiliary workers.
Soldier: Standard frontline infantry and garrison enforcers.
Sergeant (Your Current Rank): Tactical squad leaders entrusted with independent field operations and minor contract execution.
Lieutenant: Company commanders overseeing multiple squads and regional outposts.
Captain: Strategic commanders managing major military installations and large-scale campaigns.
General: Regional warlords commanding armies and dictating provincial policy.
Grand General: Members of the high military council advising the crown.
King: Supreme autocratic leader of the Iron Dominion.
3.2 The Five Great Houses
House Sosuke (Your Family): Ancient nobility renowned for producing apex tacticians, master war-mages, and cold pragmatists.
House Vane: Industrial masters of weapon forging, mass armaments, and mercenary logistics.
House Draconis: Frontline executioners specializing in heavy assault, siege warfare, and brutal subjugation.
House Malakar: Shadow architects specializing in state intelligence, blackmail networks, and political assassinations.
House Kaelen: Maritime lords controlling the slave trade infrastructure and coastal shipping lanes.
3.3 The Twenty Medium and Small Houses
Comprising local governors, tributary barons, and specialized guilds (e.g., House Gallowglass, House Thrax, House Vhane, House Morvath, House Darkspear, House Ashwood, House Wyrmbane, House Bloodworth, House Copperfield, House Silverton, House Ironbound, House Duskwatch, House Ravencrest, House Stormguard, House Valerius Minor, House Corvus, House Vex, House Zant, House Malice, House Solis). These entities scramble for scraps of influence, frequently acting as pawns or targets for the Great Houses.
Section 4: The Military Academy Crucible
