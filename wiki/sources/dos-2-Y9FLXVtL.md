## CHAPTER I: THE MECHANICAL ARCHITECTURE OF THE GOD-SHADOW
When you stood at the apex of the First Garden and chose the path of absolute, unyielding tyranny, you did not merely claim a title. By consuming Lucian the Divine and Dallis the Hammer, you systematically monopolized the cosmic lifeblood of Rivellon. The Veil remains intact, but it is no longer anchored by a pantheon of squabbling deities or the fragile sacrifices of the pure; it is tethered exclusively to your living soul.
Because you chose to pursue your ascension through blinding speed, immaculate grace, and absolute stealth, your divine chassis does not manifest as a lumbering, iron-clad juggernaut. Instead, you are a silent, breathtaking apex predator—a high-elven or perfected Eternal silhouette clad in dark, supple medium armor, slipping through the shadows with a pair of flawlessly forged finesse blades that flash with the stolen radiance of the gods.
To represent this mythic tier of power, your character utilizes a **Level 12 to 30 Gestalt Character Sheet**, blending the absolute martial authority of a modified **Oath of Conquest Paladin** with the infinite cosmic reservoir of a **Source Soul Sorcerer** (a dark adaptation of the Divine Soul). You do not multiclass or split your levels; at every milestone from your starting point at Level 12 up to your ultimate transcendence at Level 30, you gain the features, spellcasting progression, and raw mechanical benefits of both classes simultaneously.

## CHAPTER III: THE UNIQUE PARADIGM OF THE SENTIENT VOID
One of the most profound consequences of your max-evil campaign in *Divinity: Original Sin 2* and your subsequent consumption of Dallis the Hammer is the radical transformation of the Void itself. When you absorbed Dallis, you did not just inherit her memories; you seized the ancient, forbidden biological and magical secrets of the **Eternals**—the original, immortal rulers of the world before they were cast into the dark by the treacherous Seven.
As a result, the relationship between the material plane and the Void has undergone a terrifying, seductive evolution. The higher-level entities crossing the fraying Veil are no longer mindless, chitinous insects or slavering, multi-eyed monstrosities covered in rot. Instead, they are the ancient Eternals themselves, twisted by the primordial darkness of the Void but restored to a state of flawless, hauntingly beautiful humanoid grace.
```
                    =================================

              and Tactical Fodder            Diplomacy & Sedition
```

These high-tier Voidwoken lords possess an aristocratic, cruel elegance. They dress in heavy, shimmering silks woven from shadow-thread, wear armor forged from non-reflective obsidian, and speak with voices that sound like dark music. They do not wish to merely reduce Rivellon to a wasteland of ash; they view mortals as cattle, but they view *you* as the true, perfect evolution of their ancient race. They have come to the material world to see if you are a master worthy of their allegiance, or a false king to be dragged down into the dark.
This creates a highly stylized, dark aesthetic for the entire campaign. Evil is not ugly, decaying, or desperate in this world; it is immaculate, wealthy, and breathtakingly attractive. The horror of your empire does not lie in skeletal ghouls wandering the streets, but in the absolute, quiet perfection of your court, where dissenters are erased from existence without a sound, and the common folk are seduced into giving up their freedom by the sheer, intoxicating beauty of their new masters.
## CHAPTER IV: THE GEOPOLITICAL POWER BALANCE
Although you are an untouchable, individual powerhouse capable of slaughtering any mortal champion in a one-on-one duel, your empire begins the campaign in a highly volatile, fragile state. By executing every single magistrate, paladin, and bureaucrat who remained loyal to the memory of Lucian the Divine, you effectively decapitated the infrastructure of Rivellon.
Arx is an architectural masterpiece, but its treasury is low, its administrative offices are empty, and its territory is currently limited to the capital city and its immediate perimeter. At the start of the campaign, your faction, **The Eternal Throne**, ranks at the bottom of the world's top five major geopolitical forces. You must play a high-stakes game of dark diplomacy, tactical assassinations, and resource management to rebuild your vanguard while keeping your rivals at bay.

## CHAPTER V: POTENTIAL CORE CAMPAIGN NARRATIVE ARCS
### Act I: The Hollow Throne & The Silver Crisis
The campaign opens in the high-spire city of Arx, roughly six months after your bloody ascension at the First Garden. The spires of the grand cathedral have been re-decorated with banners of emerald green and absolute black, bearing your personal crest of the crescent moon and the broken sword. Yet beneath this immaculate surface, your empire is starving.
By purging Lucian’s loyalists, you shattered the logistics of the realm. The **Silver Crusade**, a fanatical military coalition unified under the command of **Grand Paladin Hardwin** (a Level 18 Vengeance Paladin), has established a total economic embargo in the northern territories. They have locked down the grain routes, intercepted tax convoys, and hanged your newly appointed local enforcers. Arx is weeks away from a famine, and the common folk are beginning to whisper that a false, monstrous deity sits upon the throne. Worse, the **Umbral Court**—the wealthy syndicate of rogue Sourcerers and Lone Wolf assassins—is covertly financing the Crusade to keep you distracted while they search the ruins of the world for ancient, anti-divine weaponry.
Because you lack a massive standing army, you cannot wage a conventional war of attrition. You must rely on your high Dexterity, your medium armor, and your *Source Vision* to operate as a divine shadow.

The first major plot movement involves an infiltration of the historic fortress of Fort Joy, which Hardwin has converted into a massive, heavily fortified staging ground for his impending march on Arx. You and your elite companions—**Malady**, serving as your spymaster, and **General Kaelen**, your Eternal warlord—slip into the fortress under the cover of a magical tempest. Using *Source Vision*, you peer through the solid stone walls of the keep, tracking the exact locations and movements of the Paladin captains.
You do not fight them in the courtyard. Instead, you slide through the corridors like a ghost, using *Subtle Spell* to cast *Silence* and *Hold Person*, executing the leadership in absolute darkness before they can sound their alarms. Every blade strike is infused with your *Source Smite*, converting radiant energy into cold, un-resistible force.
When you finally corner Grand Paladin Hardwin in his war room, the confrontation serves as your first major geopolitical milestone. You are faced with a definitive choice: you can slaughter him brutally, using your newly unlocked **Deathfog Radiance** to reduce his entire guard to ash and raising them as undead thralls to march back to Arx as a grim warning, or you can leverage your *Immaculate Presence* and high Charisma to psychologically break his fanaticism, seducing him into believing that your absolute rule is the only thing preventing the universe from dissolving into the Void.

The climax of Act I occurs when the Umbral Court attempts to capitalize on the chaos by deploying a massive mercenary armada to blockade the harbor of Arx, using a stolen Eternal relic known as the *Aeteran’s Shard* to create a magical field that neutralizes standard spellcasting. To break the blockade, you utilize your **Ghost-Walk of the Veil**.
You dissolve your body into pure emerald light, gliding invisibly across the surface of the harbor and passing directly through the wooden hulls of their flagships. You materialize silently within their powder rooms, your rapiers flashing once in the dark before you vanish back into the Veil. The night sky over Arx burns with the brilliant fire of exploding war galleys. By the end of this act, the Silver Crusade is broken, the Umbral Court has been forced to pay massive financial tributes to your treasury, and The Eternal Throne rises to **Rank 3** on the global ladder.
### Act II: The Court of Silk and Ash
With the immediate mortal rebellions crushed and the treasury replenished by the Umbral Court’s stolen gold, the nature of the campaign shifts from an open, bloody civil war to an elegant, high-stakes cold war. The fabric of the Veil is growing thin. The God King, recognizing that you cannot be easily broken by conventional military might, alters his strategy. He deploys **The Seductive Vanguard**—a massive court of high-tier, breathtakingly beautiful Eternal nobility—across the threshold of the Void.
They do not launch an invasion of swords and fire. Instead, they construct a magnificent, sprawling city of shifting obsidian architecture and white silk on the borders of the Reaper's Coast. Led by **Emissary Isara**, an ancient Eternal of haunting grace and terrifying political intellect, they begin a campaign of cultural seduction. They offer the war-weary local mortal lords everything your fragile empire cannot yet guarantee: limitless agricultural yields, protection from rogue monsters, and ancient secrets of life-extending magic. They are systematically stealing the allegiance of your subjects without spilling a single drop of blood.

To refuse the invitation would reveal weakness to your subjects; to attend and simply slaughter the hosts would trigger an immediate, apocalyptic invasion from the Void before your administration is stable enough to survive it.
You attend the gala alongside Malady and the Red Prince, dressed in custom, immaculate formalwear designed to match the dark aesthetic of the Eternal lords. While the Red Prince occupies the attention of the court with grand displays of his dragon-backed lineage, you use your high Dexterity to slip into the private quarters of the palace. Along the way, you leverage your Level 22 feature, **Source-Beast Seduction**. The Eternal nobles, fundamentally attuned to the cosmic energy of the world, find themselves physically intoxicated by the massive, concentrated volume of Source residing within your flesh. You do not merely negotiate; you seduce. You discover that many of these beautiful Voidwoken do not wish to return the God King to absolute power—they are terrified of his eternal malice and have grown fond of the luxuries of the material world.
Behind the scenes, a radical faction of Isara’s zealots attempts to assassinate Malady using a specialized dagger that rots the soul. You intercept them within the labyrinthine silk gardens of the spire. The combat is resolved in single rounds of absolute, high-initiative violence: your finesse blades cut through their silk attire before they can utter a single verbal component or call for aid.
The climax of Act II occurs when you turn the God King's weapon against him. Having subverted Isara’s top generals through psychological manipulation and the promise of a shared empire, you orchestrate a coup during her final court session. Left completely isolated, Isara attempts to rip open a massive rift to the Void to consume the palace.
You deploy **The Great Dampening**, instantly choking the flow of magic within the room. She watches in absolute horror as her spells fizzle into meaningless sparks. You step forward from the shadows, claim her life, and absorb her remaining essence. The Seductive Vanguard defects to your side, bending their knees to you as their true Divine ruler. The Eternal Throne claims the **Rank 1** position on the world stage.
### Act III: The War for the Cosmos
The God King’s patience has reached its absolute limit. His political maneuvers have failed, his vanguard has defected, and you have consolidated the entire planet under an immaculate, tyrannical peace. The skies over Rivellon turn the color of bruised iron as the Veil begins to tear open completely. This is no longer a localized political conflict; it is an existential war for the cosmic ledger.
As a Level 26 to 30 powerhouse, you now command an unstoppable empire comprising the remnants of the mortal races, elite dragon-riders, and legions of beautiful, defected Voidwoken lords. Yet the God King is unleashing his full, primordial reserves, tearing massive cosmic rifts directly above the world's major population centers, pouring forth titanic horrors alongside armies of monstrous, un-deceived Voidwoken.

Utilizing your **Source Vision**, you decipher her uncompleted masterpieces: the *Veil Anchors*, ancient mechanical towers capable of weaving the fabric of reality back together through raw force.
While your army holds the perimeter against waves of primordial horrors, you move through the shadows of the facility, utilizing your 9th-level arcana—such as *Time Stop* and *Wish*—to systematically activate the anchors while your rapiers slice through any entity that manages to breach the inner sanctum.

### Arc A: The Call of the Cosmic Deep
#### The Catalyst
By consuming the God King and absorbing the total volume of Rivellon’s Source, your magical signature becomes a blazing star in the dark fabric of the multiverse. You are no longer a local deity hidden away behind a fragile Veil; you are a cosmic anomaly—a single, stealth-focused Tyrant Divine who has proven capable of systematically executing and eating an entire pantheon.
Entities from worlds far beyond Rivellon—planar travelers, elder evils of the deep astral sea, and ancient interstellar pantheons—take immediate notice of the sudden extinction of the God King.
#### The Threat
A collective of foreign deities, terrified that your empire will eventually expand beyond the boundaries of Rivellon to hunt their own spheres of influence, forms a massive cosmic coalition. They do not attempt a traditional invasion; instead, they launch a permanent quarantine spell. They deploy cosmic anchors designed to phase your entire planet out of time and space, locking Rivellon in an isolated, frozen stasis demiplane where no Source can regenerate.
#### Potential Gameplay Trajectory
To save your world from permanent isolation, you must transition your empire into an inter-planar superpower. You lead your beautiful Voidwoken nobility and the Red Prince’s dragon-riders into the Astral Sea, constructing massive skyships forged from obsidian and fueled by pure Source. The campaign shifts from a defensive struggle for Rivellon into an offensive crusade across foreign planes. You utilize your high-stealth mechanics to slip into the heavens of rival gods, systematically assassinating their solar captains and dismantling their cosmic machinery to ensure your absolute freedom.
### Arc B: The Internal Echoes (The War Within)
#### The Catalyst
You hold the souls, memories, and egos of Lucian the Divine, Dallis the Hammer, the Seven Gods, and the God King within your single, consolidated consciousness. While your individual will is absolute, the sheer psychological mass of these conflicting divine entities begins to manifest as a literal, physical fracture within your empire. You are an un-contested monarch, but your mind is a crowded graveyard.
#### The Threat
Phantoms of Lucian’s light and the God King’s original monstrous form begin to manifest spontaneously across the provinces of Rivellon. These are not real resurrections; they are physical projections formed from the rogue thoughts, nightmares, and stresses of your own subconscious mind.
Your subjects begin witnessing "Miracles of the Pure Light" in distant villages, sparking a new, ghostly rebellion. This insurgency is uniquely terrifying because your *Source Vision* cannot track its origin from the outside—the signatures trace back directly to your own throne room.
#### Potential Gameplay Trajectory
This arc represents a deeply psychological, introspective dark fantasy dungeon crawl. To save your empire from being torn apart by your own thoughts, you and your inner circle must utilize a specialized ritual designed by Malady to enter your own subconscious mind. Your mind is represented as a massive, non-Euclidean level-30 dungeon where your memories have taken the form of lethal traps and elite guardians.
You must use your high-Dexterity stealth to hunt down and assassinate the ghosts of the people you consumed, moving through the memories of Fort Joy, the Blackpits, and the First Garden within your own brain, executing the phantoms of Lucian and the God King one last time to achieve true, un-fractured, and eternal peace.
