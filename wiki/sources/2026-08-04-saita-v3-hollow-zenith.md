# Campaign Bible: The Hollow Zenith (campaign saita v3)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign saita v3" (internal version marker v0) — subtitled *"A Herald's Bluff in the City of Blood."* A new campaign line introducing the [[AethelgardEmpire]] and player character Princess [[SaitaAethelgard]], operating undercover in a recovering [[BaldursGate]] after the fall of the Absolute.

## Premise

The [[AethelgardEmpire]] is a eugenics-driven civilization of apex predators whose royal bloodline holds world-shattering physical and magical superiority — Emperor [[TiberiusAethelgard]] is a Level 20 titan, and standard nobles reach Level 12 before age forty. To Aethelgards, Faerûn's citizens are "fragile, fleeting mayflies." But an apocalyptic civil war against a treacherous Cadet Branch has secretly gutted the Empire: legions decimated, treasuries empty, infrastructure ruined. To survive, Aethelgard expands into the Sword Coast, targeting post-Absolute Baldur's Gate.

The player is **Princess Saita Aethelgard**, youngest of the royal line and only **Level 1** ("an infant by their standards"), sent as "Herald Saita Kross" to secure resources, alliances, and vassal states while projecting the illusion of an unbroken, invincible Empire. If the Sword Coast realizes Aethelgard is too weak to conquer them, she will be slaughtered — the core tension of [[WeaknessMaskingDiplomacy]].

## The Hook

Explicitly framed as "a high-wire act of geopolitical gaslighting and supreme arrogance": not saving Baldur's Gate but systematically buying, manipulating, and breaking it to feed the starving Empire. The power fantasy is a character with a god's ego and world-breaker biological potential, temporarily constrained to Level 1 fragility — conquest through hyper-competent social manipulation and terrifying bluffs, "where rolling high on Deception feels like holding a knife to a king's throat."

## Character: Princess Saita Aethelgard / Herald Saita Kross

- **Archetype**: "The Patient Apex" (INTJ/ENTJ hinge) — see [[PatientApexArchetype]]. Lawful Evil; morality dictated entirely by bloodline survival and supremacy.
- **Visual signature**: impeccably tailored midnight-blue velvet with hidden imperial silver threads; weaponized posture with hyper-efficient kinetic economy; bored tell of balancing a razor dagger on her index fingertip; scent of ozone, crushed pine, and cold iron (the biological signature of Aethelgard blood).
- **Masks**: public face **Herald Kross** — weary, pragmatic, charming diplomat for a distant superpower who makes demands sound like generous favors ("merely doing paperwork for a god"); true nature **Princess Saita** — a supremacist godling viewing non-Aethelgardians as sub-sentient tools, boiling with humiliation at having to bargain with "fragile, short-lived creatures."
- **Core compulsion**: [[BurdenOfSupremacy]] — obsession with proving her worth to her family and preventing the Empire's fall.

## Core Mechanic: The Imperial Bluff

See [[ImperialBluff]]. Successfully using Charisma (Intimidation or Deception) to force an enemy to back down from physical confrontation grants Temporary HP = Charisma modifier + Level (ego fed). If her true identity or the Empire's weakness is exposed in a scene, she suffers a point of Exhaustion from crushing psychological dissonance.

## Named NPCs and factions

- **Duke Ravengard** ([[UlderRavengard]]) — target of veiled threats about legions teaching Baldur's Gate's "masons how to build walls that actually hold."
- **Kaelen** ([[KaelenAethelgard]]) — Saita's subordinate, ordered to project invincibility, not butchery.
- **[[FlamingFist]]** — dismissed in inner monologue as slow-moving steel she could kill through even while dormant, "but no... we need their iron."
- **Patriars** — Baldur's Gate nobility, mocked for "ancient" fifty-year-old bloodlines ("my father has boots older than his entire family tree").

## Relation to other campaign lines

This is another instance of the recurring "hidden-superior scion undercover in Baldur's Gate" premise family (compare the [[NocturneSosuke]] Velvet Cage line and the imperial-scion framings around [[CelestialImperium]]): a Level 1 start with a lore-superhuman ceiling, dual public/private identity masks, a Charisma-driven signature mechanic that converts social wins into survivability, and exposure of the true identity as the core failure state.
