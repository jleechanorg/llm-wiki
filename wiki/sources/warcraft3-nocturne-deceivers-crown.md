## Summary

A publication-ready solo D&D 5e campaign bible set during Warcraft III's alternate Third War. The Scourge is **fully sentient** (this is the campaign's central spine — they have will, hierarchy, ambition, and politics, NOT mindless beasts). The protagonist is **Lady Nocturne Ravencrest**, age 16, daughter and heir of Count Marcus Ravencrest of Stormwind, a military prodigy and Level 6 Paladin who publicly serves the Alliance Remnant while secretly holding a pact with the dreadlord **Mal'Ganis** (a Nathrezim whose sentient shadow-avatar survived his Frostmourne death). The campaign explores dual-life gameplay: climbing both the public noble ladder (Landed Knight → Emperor) and the shadow court ladder (Bound Blade → Prince of the Pact) while never letting the two faces fully collide.

## The Sovereign Decoupling Principle

This is the user's most important design pillar — and the explicit response to the "no global aggregate trackers" pitfall. **Personal combat stats (HP, AC, spell DC, smites, class features, relic weapons) are intrinsic to Nocturne's prodigial strength and dark pact. They are completely independent of public opinion. If her treason is exposed, she loses institutional power — noble rank, lands, titles, army commands, political protection — but her personal combat stats and class capabilities remain 100% intact.** Exposed or exalted, she is an absolute terror on the battlefield.

## The Pact (locked terms)

Mal'Ganis's three demands:
1. Help unmake Arthas Menethil — not as a simple corpse, but as a tool or a broken thrall.
2. Treat the Scourge as sentient beings with their own hierarchies. Some will be destroyed. Many more will be brought to heel.
3. Deliver a human kingdom, corrupted quietly from within, ready to become something better than either the noisy Scourge or the failing Alliance.

Shared long-term conspiracy: use the Third War to rise in both human and shadow power → defeat or enslave portions of the Scourge rather than fight them as monsters → break or dominate Arthas → seize shadow control of a major human kingdom → transition it into a demon-dominated nation under Mal'Ganis's (and Nocturne's) influence — quiet, ordered, and far more useful than the noisy Scourge.

## Divergence from Warcraft canon

During the aftermath of the Culling of Stratholme, Arthas sailed to Northrend in pursuit of Mal'Ganis. In the original timeline, Mal'Ganis was struck down by Frostmourne. In this timeline, Mal'Ganis anticipated the Lich King's betrayal. He left a sentient shadow-avatar behind in the Northern Kingdoms and bound himself to Lady Nocturne Ravencrest, creating a dual-custody alliance aimed at usurping both the Alliance of Lordaeron and the Scourge hierarchy.

## Custom Sacred Oath — Oath of the Deceiver

Full Paladin chassis (Divine Smite, Lay on Hands, Divine Health, Aura of Protection, spellcasting). Replaces standard Sacred Oath features:
- **Oath Spells:** disguise self / wrathful smite (3rd), mirror image / hold person (5th), fear / crusader's mantle (9th), greater invisibility / death ward (13th), dominate person / holy weapon (17th, may deal necrotic or cold damage instead of radiant).
- **Channel Divinity (3rd):** *Mantle of the Honorable* (Public — action, 10 min, advantage on Persuasion/Deception; allies within 30 ft gain THP = Paladin level + Cha mod) and *Touch of the Deceiver* (Shadow — bonus action, 1 creature within 30 ft takes 2d8 + Cha mod necrotic OR cold, WIS save or frightened 1 min; can force target to treat an ally as enemy).
- **Aura of False Virtue (7th):** +Cha save bonus in 10-ft radius (30 ft at 18th); +1 AC and saves while ≥ 5 trusting allies are in the aura. **Intrinsic — remains active regardless of public reputation or discovery (per the Sovereign Decoupling Principle).**
- **Soul of Two Masks (15th):** Divine Smite may deal necrotic or cold damage. Regain 1 expended Channel Divinity use when reducing a living creature to 0 HP with no witnesses.
- **Paragon of the Deceiver (20th):** 1 min — resistance to all damage; +1d8 necrotic and +1d8 cold on melee; Channel Divinity 1/turn without expending; creatures within 30 ft must WIS save or kneel/flee.
- **Epic Progression (L21–25):** Domain of the Twin Crowns / Rune Sovereign / Veiled Command / Unbroken Vessel / Princess of Two Worlds.
- **Apotheosis (L26–30):** Divine stat cap raised to 30; Ascendant God-State mechanics.

## Dual Ranking System

**Public Rank — Stormwind / Alliance Remnant:** Landed Knight → Baron → Count → Marquis → Duke → Archduke → King → Emperor. Each rank grants mechanical benefits (aura ranges, troop call-ins, AC bonuses, Persuasion advantages, second actions, doubled benefits at the apex).

**Shadow Rank — Court of Mal'Ganis:** Bound Blade → Agent → Hand → Dark Knight → Dread Baron → Shadow Count → Night Marquis → Prince of the Pact. Each grants shadow-side benefits (frost/necrotic smite access, pact resources, ability to hide death-knight effects, undead summons, dreadlord army interventions).

**Hybrid Unlocks (examples):** Duke + Dread Baron = share aura benefits with shadow agents. King + Night Marquis = issue commands that appear legitimate but carry hidden shadow compulsion. Emperor + Prince of the Pact = true dual sovereign; campaign's endgame opens.

## Retinue (3 NPCs)

1. **Captain Sir Kenneth Drake** — Veteran Stormwind Officer (Paladin/Fighter), second-in-command, Public Shield. Stoic, honorable, deeply tired, protective. Believes Nocturne is the reincarnated spirit of ancient martial chivalry.
2. **Sylvan the Pale** — Sentient Undead / Former Lordaeron Scholar, Master of Intelligence & Scourge Liaison, Shadow Liaison. Soft-spoken, impeccably groomed, pale skin like polished marble, chillingly polite.
3. **Lyra Moon-Whisper** — High Elf Priestess of the Light, Field Medic & Spiritual Advisor, Unwitting Asset. Earnest, empathetic, anxious, deeply pious. Saved by Nocturne during the retreat from Stratholme.

## Custom Mechanics

1. **Dual Authority System & Decoupling Architecture** — two parallel resource metrics (Public Dignitas 0-100 and Shadow Favor 0-100). What each controls; what happens if reduced to 0 (loss of institutional power but zero impact on personal combat stats). Equilibrium Bonus: when PD and SF are within 10 points, gain Tactical Supremacy (+Cha mod to initiative and squad mobility).
2. **Cohort Warfare Engine** — Cohort Combat Value formula (CCV = Unit Strength + Commander Cha mod + Tactical Rank). Living vs Pale Undead unit characteristics (Living: high morale, food logistics, fear vulnerability. Pale Undead: immune to fear/poison, high armor, requires secret deployment). Combined Tactics: Feigned Retreat — living troops fall back while hidden Pale Undead flank.
3. **Mystery Tracker (v1.2.0)** — *The First Scourge Defector* (a sentient undead claims to know Frostmourne's location — Nocturne must decide whether to feed intel to Mal'Ganis, Stormwind, or keep it for herself). Plus retinue-targeted mysteries (e.g., *The Ledger of Andorhal* hits Drake's paternal-rejection insecurity; *The Priestess of the Light* hits Sylvan's truth-vs-decency conflict).

## Starting Scene

Hearthglen courtyard, midnight. Three sentient Scourge prisoners bound in holy iron. Captain Drake awaits orders to execute them publicly. Sylvan whispers from the shadows — Mal'Ganis wants them alive. The First Action Choice: A (Public Execution +10 PD -10 SF), B (Private Interrogation +10 SF, risk Inquisitorial check), or C (Dual Subterfuge — mock escape, execute one publicly, recruit two in shadow; +5 PD +5 SF).

## Acts

Act I — The Northern Game. Establish foothold, secure refugees, ascend to Baroness / Agent.
Act II — Knives in the Dark. Manipulate Arthas's return, undermine rival Dreadlords, consolidate sentient undead.
Act III — The Dual Crown. Corrupt a major human kingdom from within; claim High Noble and Shadow Court rank.
Act IV — The Quiet Kingdom. Enslave / break Arthas, establish the Pale Nation, finalize Apotheosis.

## Design Philosophy Notes (per user P24)

- Hidden apex → surprise → intimidation (per-NPC, not aggregate).
- No global aggregate trackers — the Sovereign Decoupling Principle is the explicit response.
- No forced ruler-progression arc.
- Custody/confinement is a playable consequence.
- Conditional exhaustion / responsibility cost.
- Warcraft canon is the source material — flavor from Warcraft, no copying of named techniques or storylines from canon.
- Per-NPC state over aggregate state (the Dual Authority System reflects this).
