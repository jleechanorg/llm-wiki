## Part 1 — The Campaign Bible (BG3 / Faerûn specific)

Post-Netherbrain [[BaldursGate]], 1492 DR. A **female [[DarkUrge]]** — gestalt Gloomstalker Ranger + Assassin Rogue, level 12 — drained the [[Netherbrain]] and the Crown of Karsus of their primordial energy, marched into the Undercity Temple of [[Bhaal]], and **consumed her own father's divine spark**. She is now the incubating Goddess of Murder, ruling a terrified Baldur's Gate through the bloodless doctrine of the [[SanguineArchitecture]] — [[SubmissiveDeath]] and Tragic Betrayal — rather than open slaughter. Campaign arc: the slow ascent from nascent **Quasi-Deity** anchored to a single city to **Greater Goddess** waging plane-spanning deicide against the Faerûnian pantheon. Module 0 (*The Apotheosis*) opens first-person, present-tense atop the Balduran statue, the Netherbrain's carcass flaking off in the salt wind — the same apex-of-power turning-point framing used across the wiki's power-fantasy campaign line (cf. [[ApexWeaver]], [[ProjectApotheosis]]).

## Parts 2–4 — The God Mechanics Framework

1. **V1 General** ([[GodMechanicsSevenRankLadder]]): system-agnostic baseline built on a **7-rank divinity ladder** (Quasi-Deity → Greater Deity tiers), designed to be portable beyond D&D.
2. **V2 General** ([[GodMechanicsV2Overlay]]): extends V1 with **hidden bands**, an **auto-win ladder** (rank-gap resolution without dice), a **per-dawn divine action menu**, and an explicit **[[DeicideCost]]** economy for killing gods. Explicitly setting-agnostic — translatable to Cyberpunk / Wuxia / Marvel / Naruto / homebrew pantheons.
3. **Nocturne V2 specialization**: D&D 5e Faerûn binding of the V2 overlay with Nocturne-specific tier structure and multipliers, connecting this framework to the wiki's existing Nocturne lineage ([[NocturneSosuke]], [[house-sosuke]]).
