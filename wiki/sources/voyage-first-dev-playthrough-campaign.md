## Summary
Full formatted campaign summary (~12.5k words) of the first ever Voyage dev playthrough livestream on the Larion world, featuring the four-person dev team playing copper-rank adventurers through the Tower of Venture, a sewer moss encounter, the Wolfwood bandit skirmish, and a final Q&A on the platform's features and roadmap. Source: `~/Downloads/voyage_gameplay_stream/transcript_plain.txt`. Reformatted into Reddit-style sections (Setup, Beats 1–9, Q&A, Highlights, System Notes, What's Next) with all original dialog preserved.

## Key Claims
- Voyage is the next-gen TTRPG/AI-dungeon successor from the AI Dungeon / Latitude team (Nick, Ryan, Brian, Kobe in the playthrough).
- The platform uses a multi-agent "World Engine" with simultaneous player input (not turn-based), NPC autonomy, and persistent inventory/rank progression.
- Copper → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum rank ladder is gated by contract completion; the devs themselves spent the session trying (and failing) to bypass Hestia the registrar.
- The rat-summoning ability ("Vermin Commander") was the viral moment of the stream — 24+ named/unnamed rats acting as a combat swarm.
- Featured NPCs: Hestia (registrar, "It's weird, it's damp, and it's all yours"), Ulla Finehair (dwarf justiciar), Kragroff (sewer dweller, "what do you call a fish with no eyes?"), Daran Ashby (scarred human warrior).
- Daily-quest feature was pitched on-stream by Ryan as a "short quest everyone gets the same one" — moss monster day was floated as a template.
