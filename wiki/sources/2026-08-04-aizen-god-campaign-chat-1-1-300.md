# Aizen God Campaign — Chat 1, Entries 1-300

**Source**: Gemini 2.5 Flash Ultra "Dungeon master" Gem chat export ("Aizen godhood (previously bane - main)" chat lineage), entries 1-300 of chat 1.

## What this is

The opening of a fresh D&D 5e campaign in [[BaldursGate]] featuring [[SosukeAizen]] as the central power figure. The player explicitly requested a [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart]]: reuse the characters and factions from the prior "bane - main" chat, but reset all state. The DM ([[Gemini]]) proposes **Campaign Title: The Shadow over the Gate** (see [[ShadowOverTheGate]]).

## Campaign frame

- **System**: D&D 5e; **Setting**: Baldur's Gate (Lower City, Upper City, outskirts).
- **Core themes**: intrigue, shifting loyalties, power struggles, the nature of good and evil, the corrupting influence of ambition — squarely in jleechan's documented serpent-mastermind preference profile.

## Factions (reimagined for the restart)

1. **[[FlamingFist]]** — city military/police under Grand Duke [[UlderRavengard]]; internal divisions and potential corruption.
2. **[[TheGuild]]** — organized crime syndicate; a power vacuum or ambitious new leader may be emerging.
3. **[[KnightsOfTheShield]]** — secretive influence faction guiding the city from behind the scenes, not always aligned with the Fist.
4. **[[CultOfTheDeadThree]]** — hidden Bane/Bhaal/Myrkul cult sowing chaos; **Bane is flagged as a significant, subtle presence** (continuity with the prior "bane - main" campaign).
5. **[[HouseAizen]]** — new rising power; Aizen as a powerful individual or head of a highly influential organization, goals shrouded in mystery, resources + charisma, influence potentially extending into all other factions.

## Opening structure: "A Night of Unrest"

An undercurrent of unease — disappearances, gang violence, cult whispers. Four possible PC starting hooks (an instance of [[NarrativeHookScenarios]]), designed to intertwine:

1. **[[ElfsongTavern]] Brawl** — Guild gang vs a disciplined gang with ritualistic tattoos; attempted theft of a dark-pulsing arcane artifact.
2. **Dockside Abduction** — shadowy figures with unnatural speed abduct a merchant/Fist officer, leaving an **eye-like symbol** etched in cobblestones (Aizen signature).
3. **The Noble's Plea** — minor noble's guard has vanished; "growing darkness"; lurking figures.
4. **Flaming Fist Patrol** — missing persons who were critics of the city's power structure; stressed patrol leader; eerily quiet routes.

## The common thread

Across all hooks, antagonists exhibit **unusual efficiency** — the DM's mechanism for [[SubtleInfluenceCampaignHook]], formalized here as the [[UnusualEfficiencySignature]]: Aizen's hand is visible only as a pattern of competence and coordination, never as direct presence. The chunk ends mid-description of this thread.
