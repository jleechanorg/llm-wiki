# Campaign Bible: The Blood Dragon of Rook's Rest (campaign visenya v9)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign visenya v9" — the ninth entry in the Visenya campaign line, this time set in **209 AC**, the year of the [[AshfordTourney]], during the Dunk & Egg era (cross-reference [[SerDuncanTheTall]]). Advertised structure: *"Four emergent endings. One system. Player chooses the resolution."*

## Premise (Section 1: Campaign Concept)

King [[DaeronIITargaryen|Daeron II]] sits the Iron Throne, old and sick, his reign entering its final year — but the crown's reach ends where the Kingswood begins. The brotherhoods broken in the last Blackfyre Rebellion never disarmed, and the roads of the Seven Kingdoms have become hunting grounds. Lord [[MaekarTargaryen]], youngest of Daeron's four fighting sons, has the thankless task of pacifying the Crownlands.

His youngest child — the player character [[VisenyaTargaryen]], sixteen, "the unwanted great-granddaughter of the Conqueror through three generations of spares" — has been awarded the barony of [[RooksRest|Rook's Rest]] near King's Landing for clearing the Stokeworth-to-Duskendale stretch of every bandit, cutthroat, and broken knight in a single campaigning season. The smallfolk call her **"the Blood Dragon"** and whisper her name in the same breath as the Doom of Valyria. She is not loved; she is feared, and she has weaponized the fear.

**The Hook**: A sixteen-year-old princess with a de facto seat at the small council of every village headman between the Blackwater and the Narrow Sea — because she hanged the last three. She holds a barony no older lord will acknowledge, rides with a household guard of broken men who would die for her, and "has killed more people than the average Kingsguard knight has spoken to." The fun is the asymmetry of a baronial magnate in miniature who has discovered ruthlessness is the only dialect the Crownlands understand.

## Build: [[ApexStalker]] gestalt (Ranger 6 / Rogue 6)

Explicitly framed as "Gloom Stalker / Assassin and Not Bard / Mastermind" — a deliberate archetype pivot from the v6 bard/mastermind gestalt.

**From Ranger (Gloom Stalker Conclave 1-6):** Favored Enemy (humans — "she can read intent at a glance"), Natural Explorer (urban at night), Blind-Fighting style, **Umbral Sight** (60 ft vision in magical AND nonmagical darkness — flagged "Critical"), Iron Mind (WIS saves), Extra Attack, Stalker's Flurry (bonus-action reroll attack on a miss), Shadowy Dodge (impose Disadvantage when attacked in dim light/darkness).

**From Rogue (Assassin 1-6):** Expertise in Stealth + Perception (both +11 — "She cannot be unseen; she is unseen"), Sneak Attack 6d6, Cunning Action, **Assassinate** ("The centerpiece" — advantage vs. creatures that haven't acted; auto-crit on Surprised targets), ASI: Alert (+5 initiative, cannot be surprised), Uncanny Dodge, second Expertise + Skulker feat (hide while lightly obscured; undetectable by blindsight/tremorsense).

## Homebrew: [[StressLineSight]] (v9 exclusive, not PHB 5e)

Always-active perception of the physical world as "stress lines" — the trajectory a guard's gaze will trace in the next 3 seconds, the moment a beam will crack, the refraction angle of light off an arbalest bolt. Scales by tier:

| Tier | Levels | Range / active uses |
|---|---|---|
| Cub | 1-5 | 15 ft passive; lineage dormant |
| Stalker | 6-10 | 60 ft passive + 1 Stress Line/round |
| Apex Predator | 11-15 | 120 ft passive + 2 Stress Lines/round |
| Sovereign | 16-19 | 240 ft passive + 3/round + Sovereign Sight (planar geometry — "the shape of magic as well as physics") |
| Demi-God | 20+ | 480 ft passive + unlimited — "The First Song's prison door opens" (see Magic Barrier System, Section 13) |

Passive: permanent Advantage on sight-based Perception in dim light/darkness at all tiers. Active (bonus action): declare a Stress Line — DEX (Stealth) check against an unaware creature in range (source truncates mid-rule).

## Structure

The campaign promises [[FourEmergentEndings|four emergent endings]] under one system, with the player choosing the resolution — a structural contract distinct from the tracker/ledger subsystems of v8. A **Magic Barrier System** (Section 13, tied to "The First Song's prison") is referenced but not included in this excerpt.

## Relation to the Visenya line

Ninth revision: v5 = 298 AC junta-era Apex child; v6 = 14-year-old Level 6 bard/mastermind incognito in the North; v8 = Dance-era [[VisenyaBelaerys]] gazetteer/mechanics. v9 relocates the character concept to 209 AC as [[MaekarTargaryen]]'s daughter — each version is a fresh alternate frame around the same serpent-mastermind power-fantasy core, here re-skinned from social manipulation to predatory stealth.
