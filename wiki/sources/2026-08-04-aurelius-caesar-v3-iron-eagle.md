# Campaign Bible: The Iron Eagle: Vengeance (Aurelius Caesar v3)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign aurelius caesar v3" — a v3 campaign intro + personality bible for a Roman alternate-history campaign set in **71 BCE during the Third Servile War**, in a divergent timeline where the Republic is already dead: [[JuliusCaesar]] seized power early and rules as **Emperor**.

## Premise and hook

The player character is [[AureliusCaesar]] (16), "the Prodigy of the Julian Line" and the Emperor's youngest, most ambitious son. Publicly he travels as **Aurelius Varenus**, a wealthy merchant scion dealing in grain and wine. He is dropped into the chaos of the [[Spartacus]] revolt — a mess created by "incompetent Praetors like [[Glaber]]" — with:

- 50,000 gold in a backpack
- a squad of black-ops Praetorians
- a secret Imperial mandate: *"End the rebellion. Save the gold. Do not embarrass the family."*

Mechanically he is a **Level 6 [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords Bard]]** — duelist, manipulator, and wielder of the **[[Miasma]]** ("The Divine Blood"). This is the [[ShadowPrinceArchetype]]: hedonistic civilian cover used for free travel, bribery, and espionage, wrapped around a royal operative.

## Personality architecture (the two masks)

The bible dedicates most of its length to a dual-identity personality spec:

- **Public face — "The Prince of Pleasure" / "Sun of Rome"**: the most vibrant, charismatic Julian; laughs loud, weeps at the theater, drinks deep, sells ambition as "Passion" and "Patriotism". Gilded armor, perfumed tent — he "makes tyranny look fun".
- **True nature — "The Golden Tyrant" / "the Devouring Maw"**: a **Lawful Evil** core of absolute Entitlement. The universe is a banquet set for him; people are invited guests or courses to be consumed. He crushes rebellion because chaos offends his *aesthetic*, and his sadism is emotional — he wants to watch hope break because it validates his supremacy.

Archetype label: **The Decadent Conqueror**, an explicit **ESFP-ENTJ hinge** (Performer / King) — a notable departure from the INTJ serpent-mastermind archetype dominating this user's other campaigns (see [[user_campaign_preferences|campaign preferences]] pattern).

## Key mechanics/motifs

- **[[BeautyAsWeapon]] — "The Blood of Venus"**: he is framed as "the most beautiful man in the Empire"; perfection functions as armor (veterans hesitate to strike him — "it feels like vandalism") and as coercive charisma (being noticed by him is "an intoxication").
- **Auctoritas as Heat**: his presence physically reads as temperature — summer-day warmth when pleased, open furnace door when angry.
- **Tactile ownership**: constant touching of objects and people as a gesture of *claiming*.

## Relationship to the campaign family

This is the third iteration of the Aurelius Caesar campaign line and continues the household pattern of playing beautiful, morally corrupt manipulators embedded under cover identities (cf. [[SosukeAizen]] in the Nocturne-ancestor line, [[Alexiel]] in the SWTOR/Assiah lines). The College of Swords chassis links it directly to [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild]]. Where the Aizen/Alexiel protagonists weaponize fragility and pity, Aurelius weaponizes overwhelming beauty and warmth — the same deception engine with inverted polarity.

## Notable internal inconsistency

The personality section names him "Aurelius **Julia** Caesar" (feminine gens form) while the intro uses "Aurelius Caesar" / cover "Aurelius Varenus" — likely a drafting artifact across the three names.
