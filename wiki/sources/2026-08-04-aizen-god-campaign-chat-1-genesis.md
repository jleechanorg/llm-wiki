# Aizen god campaign chat 1 — campaign genesis

Opening of the Aizen god-campaign chat transcript ([[Gemini]] 2.5 Flash ULTRA, "Dungeon master" Gem). The player asks the DM to look at the prior "bane - main" chat for context but start a **new** D&D 5e campaign in [[BaldursGate]] featuring [[AizenSosuke]] — same characters and factions, but a clean state. This is the [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] pattern: continuity of cast, reset of world state.

## Campaign frame proposed by the DM

- **Title**: *The Shadow over the Gate* ([[ShadowOverTheGate]])
- **System**: D&D 5e; **Setting**: Baldur's Gate (Lower City, Upper City, outskirts)
- **Themes**: intrigue, shifting loyalties, power struggles, the nature of good and evil, corrupting ambition

## Major factions (reimagined)

- **[[FlamingFist]]** — city military/police under Grand Duke [[UlderRavengard]]; internal divisions and potential corruption.
- **[[TheGuild]]** — organized crime syndicate; a power vacuum or ambitious new leader may be emerging.
- **[[KnightsOfTheShield]]** — secretive faction guiding the city's destiny from behind the scenes; agendas not aligned with the Flaming Fist. (Later becomes the campaign's principal counter-intelligence antagonist — see the entries 601-900 segment.)
- **[[CultOfTheDeadThree]]** — hidden cult of [[Bane]], Bhaal, and Myrkul sowing chaos; Bane flagged as "a significant, subtle presence," continuous with Aizen's Child-of-Bane heritage in later segments.
- **[[HouseAizen]]** — new rising power; Aizen as a powerful individual or head of a mysterious, resource-rich organization whose influence "could extend into any or all of the other factions."

## Starting point: "A Night of Unrest"

The city simmers with disappearances, gang violence, and cult whispers ([[ANightOfUnrest]]). The DM offers four [[NarrativeHookScenarios]] for PC entry: (1) an [[ElfsongTavern]] brawl with a dark-artifact theft, (2) a dockside abduction leaving an eye-like symbol, (3) a minor noble's plea about vanished guards and a "growing darkness," (4) a Flaming Fist patrol investigating missing critics of the power structure. The common thread across all hooks is Aizen's subtle influence — antagonists exhibiting an [[UnusualEfficiencySignature]] (unusual efficiency and discipline).
