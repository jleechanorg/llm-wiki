# Aizen God Campaign — Chat 2 (godhood continuation)

**Source**: Gemini chat export "aizen godhood 2" (https://g.co/gemini/share/dd09d4ab6b5f, played June 9 2025, published June 12 2025). Continuation chat of the [[AizenSosuke]] god campaign after chat 1 hit its length limit.

## What this is

The player performs a [[CampaignContextMigration]]: opening a fresh [[Gemini]] chat and re-supplying (1) overall campaign information, (2) the full prior chat log, (3) reference files (A Song of Ice and Fire books, explicitly **style-only** — no ASOIAF characters or places), and (4) the original campaign prompts, so play continues "seamlessly." The DM ([[Gemini]]) acknowledges the handoff protocol before receiving the definitive campaign export.

## Campaign state at handoff — "Aizen: The God-King of Absolute Codification (Definitive)"

- **Premise**: [[AizenSosuke]] is the newly ascended **God-King of Absolute Codification, Tyranny, and Control**, having achieved godhood via [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]] — consuming the divine essence of [[Bane]]. Goal: consolidate cosmic dominion and reach **Transcendent Greater Deity** status within one year.
- **Divine rank**: Nascent Greater Deity; ascension triggered at character Level 12 (Paladin 2 / Bard 10, 113,440 XP).
- **Mortal disguise**: operates in mortal form (HP 59/59, AC 18, full spell slots, 6 Bardic Inspiration, 7 Divine Sense, 10 Lay on Hands, Great Weapon Master, Extra Attack) while retaining full divine power. **[[KyokaSuigetsu]]** remains active — the Bleach-derived absolute-hypnosis signature carried into the D&D frame.
- **Divine stat block** (custom system layered over 5e): Divine Resilience 750/750, Divine Armor Class 25, [[Divine-Power-Points|Divine Power Points]] 715/825 daily, PB +6 "Transcendental," inherent divine Charisma 30 (+10), temporarily boosted to an effective 35 (+12) specifically for an anticipated interaction with [[Elminster]] and [[Mystra]].
- **[[DivineImposition]]**: Divine Save DC 100 ("Absolute & Unbeatable for all mortals"). Aizen does not roll to influence mortals; targets save (lower of WIS/CHA) at Disadvantage vs DC 100, and even a success can be forced into a reroll via Divine Intervention: Consumed Will. Divine ability checks in the mortal realm auto-succeed (treated as DC 100) — the campaign formally abolishes dice for the protagonist's social/knowledge play.
- **Divine Awareness / Cosmic Insight**: passive multi-planar detection of major cosmic events and divine beings; unparalleled grasp of cosmic law and inter-deity politics.

## Why it matters for the wiki

This export is the cleanest statement of the campaign's endgame power fantasy: the [[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]] and [[Mask-of-Fragility]] manipulation play of the mortal arc culminates in a rules layer where opposition is mechanically impossible (DC 100, no rolls). It also documents the player's practiced workflow for LLM-DM continuity across context-window boundaries — the export is itself a player-authored save file. Fits the established serpent-mastermind profile: power consolidation through consumption of a tyranny god ([[Bane]]), then bureaucratic ("Absolute Codification") rather than martial dominion.
