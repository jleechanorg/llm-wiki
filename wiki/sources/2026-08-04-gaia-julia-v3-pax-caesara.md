# Campaign Bible: The Pax Caesara — The Daughter of the Phoenix (Gaia Julia v3)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign gaia julia v3" — a D&D 5e, low-magic / Game-of-Thrones-style campaign guide plus Level 1 character sheet. Point of divergence: on the Ides of March, [[JuliusCaesar]] did not die — he caught Casca's wrist, broke it, and unleashed a wave of [[Auctoritas]] that brought the Conspirators to their knees. Current date: **44 BCE, post-civil war**. Themes: imperial decadence, sibling rivalry, "the bored gaze of a living god."

## World state (The Phoenix Ascendant)

- **The Empire**: exists in all but name. Caesar is *Dictator Perpetuo*; the Senate is "terrified clerks rubber-stamping" his decrees. See [[PaxCaesaraEmpire]].
- **The Peace**: civil wars over — Pompey dead, Cato dead, the world "pacified under the Julian boot."
- **The Problem**: "Peace is boring." With no external enemies, the House of Caesar turns inward; the court is a nest of vipers competing for the aging God-King's favor.

## The Family: The Brood of Venus

The player character is [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] (16), youngest of five. Caesar's "Little Star" — he loves her because she (seemingly) asks for nothing; "He does not realize he fathered a Singularity."

- **Father — Emperor Caesar** ([[JuliusCaesar]]): the Living God. Charismatic, ruthless, and tired.
- **Mother — [[Calpurnia]]**: the Empress. Superstitious and protective; "senses your darkness but refuses to name it."
- **[[Caesarion]] (21), "The Foreigner"**: son of Cleopatra, backed by Egypt; arrogant, mystical, cruel; believes he is the reincarnation of Horus. Gaia finds him "derivative." Views her as a Roman doll to be married off.
- **[[Octavian]] (19), "The Wolf"**: adopted blood-nephew turned son; the Administrator who "runs the city while Caesar runs the world." Cold, sickly, utterly brilliant — "calculates human lives like an abacus." He fears Gaia: "his instincts scream that you are the predator in the room."
- **[[JuliaMajor]] (25), "The Socialite"**: face of the court, manages festivals and games; vain and shallow "but with a steel trap for gossip."
- **[[JuliaMinor]] (22), "The Zealot"**: High Priestess of Venus Genetrix; fanatical family-divinity worship that "unwittingly feeds your ego."

## Character profile: Gaia Julia Caesar

- **Alias**: "Gaia of the Gens Vibia" (incognito). **Age**: 16. **Alignment**: Lawful Evil — "The Sadistic Voyeur."
- **The Drive**: bored daughter of the greatest man in history; has slipped away from the Imperial retinue to [[Capua]] seeking "raw, unscripted violence." Wants to build a "toy army" of gladiators and one day prove that she — not Caesarion, not Octavian — is the true heir to the fire.

## Level 1 mechanics ("Prodigy" start)

- **Class**: [[ImperatorClass|Imperator]] (Noble/Sorcerous Origin); Background: Imperial Scion (custom Noble); Race: Variant Human; explicitly *not yet* "a world-ending Singularity" — "a single sword stroke could still end you."
- **Abilities**: STR 8 / DEX 16 / CON 12 / **INT 18** / WIS 10 / **CHA 18**. HP 11; AC 14 (Fine Leather Stola + Dex), 16 with Shield; Initiative +3; Prof +2.
- **[[Auctoritas]] (Spellcasting)**: CHA-based. Cantrips: Guidance, Friends, Mind Sliver. Two 1st-level slots; known spells Command ("Kneel") and Shield ("a flicker of the Miasma deflects a blow" — links this build to the [[Miasma]] cosmology used across sibling campaigns).
- **Tactical Sense**: cannot be Surprised while conscious; bonus-action assess reveals relative HP and immunities.
- **Feat**: Actor — Advantage on Deception/Performance when impersonating (sheet text truncates mid-sentence in the source).

## Relationship to sibling campaign variants

This is the third distinct protagonist-variant of the "Caesar survives / Julian god-dynasty" frame: [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]] v1 (*Pax Casara*, 40 BCE, youngest **son**), [[AureliusCaesar]] v3 (*Iron Eagle: Vengeance*, 71 BCE under Emperor Julius during the Third Servile War), and now Gaia v3 (44 BCE, youngest **daughter**). All three share the [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]] signature: teenage prodigy heir, dual-mask incognito identity ([[BeautyAsWeapon]]-adjacent concealment), Lawful Evil mastermind alignment, and gladiator/violence patronage as the opening playground.
