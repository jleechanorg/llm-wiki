# Campaign Bible: From Ruin to Archdevil (Nocturne BG3 v3)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign noctune bg3 v3" — the v3 iteration of the [[NocturneSosuke]] Baldur's Gate 3 campaign line. Framed as "a College of Perdition Origin Story": a 70–100 session arc from level 9 (after the prologue's ritual subversion) to level 20 plus epic progression to 25. Themes declared up front: survival, revenge, power acquisition, moral corruption, infernal bargains.

## Premise

[[NocturneSosuke]] is a 16-year-old half-elf of ruined [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]], bankrupted by rivals during the 1492 DR Netherbrain/Absolute crisis in [[BaldursGate]]. The [[CassalanterFamily]] orchestrated the ruin through debt manipulation, and Nocturne and two sisters (Elara, 18; Lyra, 14) were sold to [[TheBloomShadow]], an Upper City establishment run by [[MadamZylra]]. The campaign's stated arc is the transformation from victim to predator: what begins as survival becomes a calculated climb that culminates in stealing an archdevil's ritual and claiming infernal authority.

## Part 0 — The Broken Cage (Sessions 1–5, levels 1–3)

- **Session 1 (The Velvet Cage)**: establishes the survival mindset, brothel social dynamics, and [[MadamZylra]] as a cold businesswoman; a "special guest," merchant [[HorgusGwent]], is tied to the family's destroyers. Skill DCs 10–13 for composure, deception, and reading intentions.
- **Session 2 (The Predator's Game)**: an abuse scene the document itself flags for Session-0 boundary discussion, with explicit fade-to-black guidance. Mechanically it is an intelligence-extraction skill challenge (Performance/Deception/Persuasion/Sleight of Hand DCs 12–18): Nocturne learns the sisters are held at [[SunderStoneVilla]]'s Whispering Cellar as "Refined Collateral" for a Cassalanter soul tithe to [[Zariel]], and steals Horgus's keys, framing an Amnian merchant for the theft. The doc frames this as the pivot from enduring to weaponizing others' assumptions.
- **Session 3 (The Chess Move)**: planning montage; recruits [[Vaximus]] (paranoid infernal-mathematics scholar hiding from the [[Zhentarim]]), Kaelen (disgraced [[FlamingFist]] ranger), and Vesper (street monk owing House Sosuke a debt) with 15gp of saved coin. Level 2 milestone.
- **Sessions 4–5 (The Whispering Cellar)** — flagged "start the game here": stealth/social/combat heist into [[SunderStoneVilla]] during ritual preparation (Stealth DC 13–15, Deception DC 16, Cassalanter guard encounters, a CR 9 bone devil if the alarm is raised). The climax is the **[[SoulTitheInversion]]**: Vaximus recalculates the ritual's infernal mathematics (Arcana DC 15) while Nocturne anchors it (Performance DC 18), redirecting the soul tithe from the caged sisters onto the Cassalanter nobles upstairs, who are consumed by their own pact.

## Relationship to prior versions

This is the third Nocturne BG3 iteration (see [[NocturneSosuke]]); v3 sharpens the origin into a prologue-heist structure with an explicit "start here" checkpoint at Sessions 4–5 and names the [[CollegeOfPerdition]] as the destination subclass identity, with the endgame escalated to archdevil ascension and epic levels 20–25.
