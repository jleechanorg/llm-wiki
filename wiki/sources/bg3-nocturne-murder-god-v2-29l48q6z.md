Module 0: The Apotheosis & The Post-Purge Reality
Setting: Baldur's Gate, Year 1492 DR, weeks after the fall of the Netherbrain.
POV: First-person, present-tense, Nocturne atop the Balduran statue.
The high platform above the Chionthar River is cool, but the stone still holds the phantom warmth of psionic death. The colossal carcass of the Netherbrain hangs across the Balduran statue's shoulders like a decaying cloak, its tissue flaking into salt-wind and grey ash. Below, the Upper City cheers. The patriars host banquets, the Flaming Fist clears the rubble, and the commoners pray to the High Elf hero who saved their minds from the Absolute.
They do not know what you carried down from that high platform.
You did not break the Crown of Karsus. You treated the Netherbrain as an operating matrix—a vast, perfect database of intent—and drained it. You pulled the reality-warping battery of the Crown directly into your own soul, layer by patient layer, while the brain's husk twitched and dimmed. You carry that silence inside you now. However, the raw, cosmic magic of the Crown proved too vast for a mortal vessel to wield safely; you integrated and locked its Karsite spellcasting matrix deep within your core, rendering its wizardry unusable until your physical shell completes its full divine ascension at Level 30.
When you descended into the Undercity Temple of Bhaal, you did not go as a daughter seeking a blessing. You turned the stolen energy of the Crown against your father. You stripped the Lord of Murder of his portfolio—murder, submissive death, planned homicide—and tore his divine spark from his throat. You drank it down until his form dissolved into cinders.
In the weeks that followed, you executed the most volatile phase of your architecture: The Purge of the Redeemers. You realized that your former companions—Jaheira, Minsc, Karlach, Wyll, and Halsin—would never accept a world built on submissive death. One by one, through surgical ambushes and tragic betrayals, you executed them. You harvested their heartbreak, converting the severance of those profound bonds into explosive spikes of divine essence.
You are now a Divine Rank 0 Quasi-Deity—a nascent Goddess of Murder, anchored within Baldur's Gate. You hold a public reputation as a legendary savior, a private treasury of 100,000gp, and a secret court of evil allies who bow to your strength. But the world has begun to react. The High Harpers in Waterdeep, the Order of the Gauntlet in Elturgard, and the Chosen of Mystra have felt the sudden, terrifying vacuum left by the deaths of their champions.
The campaign begins at this exact nexus: you hold the city's love, the Undercity's throne, and the blood of Faerûn's greatest heroes on your blades.

Greatest Fear: Loss of Agency / Being Mastered. Having escaped Bhaal's blood-madness and the Netherbrain's psionic hive-mind, her primary phobia is being chained or manipulated by another cosmic power (Shar, Mystra, or the Triad).
Key Personality Traits: Analytical, Breathtakingly Magnetic, Predatory, Guarded, Strategic, Morally Conflict-Prone.

Unconsciously touches her throat or traces the line of her collarbone when contemplating a murder target (a memory of Bhaal's spark ignition).

Refuses to wear gauntlets during private audiences, preferring her bare skin to feel the temperature of blood and stone.

Speech Patterns: Silken, clinical, and reassuringly warm when wearing her Savior mask; low, quiet, and telepathically resonant (Voice of the Inheritor) when addressing her court or victims.
Reputation: The Savior of the Gate. Nationally celebrated as the High Elf hero who shattered the Absolute. Unanimously beloved by commoners, respected by Duke Ravengard, and revered by the Flaming Fist.
IV. Backstory
Defining Moment: Draining the primordial energy of the Netherbrain and the Crown of Karsus atop the Balduran statue, then descending into the Undercity Temple of Bhaal to slay her father, tear out his divine spark, and consume his portfolio.
Relevant History: Created as Bhaal's purest Bhaalspawn, Nocturne was betrayed by Orin, infected with an Illithid tadpole, and forced to rebuild her identity. She led a band of heroes and monsters to defeat the Absolute, secretly using the crisis to position herself as Bhaal's true successor and the ultimate arbiter of death.
Secrets: She has fully consumed Bhaal's spark and integrated the Crown's matrix, making her a nascent Quasi-Deity (Divine Rank 0). She is actively building an Assassins Guild and cultivating Sharran and Bhaalist cults beneath the city while maintaining her public role as Baldur's Gate's shield.

Bounded Accuracy Enforcement: Natural 1s always fail, and Natural 20s always succeed. DCs scale logically (DC 15 = Medium, DC 20 = Hard, DC 25 = Very Hard, DC 30 = Mortal Ceiling).
