# Campaign Bible: The Embers of Valyria (Sariel Valyria)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Campaign Sariel Valyria" — an A Song of Ice and Fire prequel-era campaign bible set at the absolute zenith of the [[ValyrianFreehold]], on the eve of a catastrophic 40-family civil war. Tone: Grimdark Imperial Gothic Opulence & Ruthless Political Pragmatism. Style: immersive sensory prose juxtaposing silver-gold opulence against black obsidian, sulfurous heat, and cold tactical calculation.

## Premise

The Freehold is "a glittering golden cage built upon a foundation of liquid magma, unmitigated slave labor, and blood-fleshed sorcery." The Forty Families' millennia-old oligarchy — balanced through breeding monopolies and hundreds of adult dragons as deterrent — has fractured into a 20-v-20 split; the [[FortyFamiliesCivilWar]] is hours from igniting the skies above the Fourteen Flames.

The core sociological theme is **the structural rot of an insular aristocratic autocracy**: the ruling families are so consumed by blood-feuds, vanity, and dominance over Essos that they dismiss Westeros as worthless muddy wilderness ruled by "sub-mortal kings playing with timber and iron." The protagonist's goal is to exploit that institutional blindness — reject her role as a disposable shadow weapon for her tyrannical father, and lead the mocked, impoverished "41st House" ([[HouseTargaryen]]) in a [[VanguardExodus]] across the Narrow Sea to forge a permanent empire on the untouched continent.

## The Hook — high-station power fantasy + stealth wetwork

- Start at **Level 6** as a combat prodigy with a custom **gestalt-tier hybrid archetype**: the [[DreadWyrmShadow]] ("Dread-Wyrm Assassin").
- **Beauty as a mechanical weapon**: her unarmored presence is a literal tool of psychological destabilization — structurally perfect beauty that paralyzes high-court nobility with insecurity and "exposes a dangerous, cross-house scandal with every glance" (continues the [[BeautyAsWeapon]] mechanic seen in other campaign lines).
- **[[LevelScaledDragonCompanion]]**: her dragon bypasses traditional draconic growth constraints entirely, scaling directly with character level until it reaches **exactly twice the size of Balerion the Black Dread**.
- Operational freedom backed by her father's cold indifference and a secret network of loyal siblings.

## Protagonist Profile: Sariel Ash

- **Name/Role**: [[SarielAsh]] — The Sovereign Shadow / Dread-Wyrm Assassin.
- **Standing**: Great Bastard of the Blood Feud; Sovereign Left Hand of [[HouseVaelaros]].
- **Alignment/MBTI**: Lawful Neutral (Pragmatic Sovereign); **INTJ (The Strategist)** — consistent with the operator's established INTJ serpent-mastermind protagonist taste profile.
- **Core motivation**: Absolute Autonomy — sever the invisible chains of blood-sorcery and lineage obligation so her talent and dragon are never again another sovereign's instruments.
- **Greatest fear**: Absolute containment — a bloodline lock or stagnant marriage contract suppressing her draconic essence, rooted in watching her mother [[DaenysBelaerys]] be politically erased and hidden as "an unviable third-born tool by an enemy house."
- **Temperament**: Calculated (every room is a tactical grid), Stoic, Ruthlessly Pragmatic (lives valued strictly by strategic utility), Fiercely Independent.
- **Tells**: stress manifests as a slow rhythmic finger-tap in the cadence of a resting dragon's heartbeat; when cornered tactically, a ring of molten gold around her violet pupils pulses with thermal light. Speech: deliberate, low-register, chillingly clear; eschews Valyrian honorifics; sparse high-economy vocabulary. (Document truncates mid-sentence in the speech-patterns section.)

## Relation to other campaign lines

Structurally parallel to the [[NocturneSosuke]] / [[NocturneRavencrest]] pattern: an INTJ dual-identity prodigy inside a decadent empire, weaponized beauty, a scaling personal power engine, and a sovereignty-versus-containment core conflict. Here the imperial parent is [[HouseVaelaros]] rather than [[HouseVitiate]]/[[ProjectApotheosis]], and the escape vector is geographic (exodus) rather than infernal or necropolitical. Distinct from the existing [[Sariel]] entity (angel persona in other campaign lines) — same first name, different character.
