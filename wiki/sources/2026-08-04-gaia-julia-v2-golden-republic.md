# Campaign Module: The Golden Republic (Gaia Julia v2)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign gaia julia v2" — a v2 campaign module + character profile for a Roman alternate-history campaign, the **Pax Julia Alternate** timeline, dated **82 BCE**. System is D&D 5e with a **Gestalt Imperator** archetype; tone is explicitly low-magic / Game-of-Thrones gritty realism where magic items are rare "world-shaking miracles" framed as lost artifacts of the First Founder (Aeneas/Romulus), not glowing magic. Campaign themes: political domination, sadistic intrigue, dynastic ascendancy.

## Premise and hook

The player character is [[GaiaJuliaCaesar]] (16), traveling under the alias **"Gaia of the Gens Vibia"** — a wealthy, provincial equestrian heiress. To [[Batiatus]] and [[Lucretia]] she plays a bored, naive, flirtatious rich girl with a taste for "rough" excitement; in truth she is a self-styled **Singularity** with "the biological majesty of a Goddess" who views mortals as cattle and marks rare specimens like [[Spartacus]] to be "broken and remade as pets." Her driving flaw is **Solipsistic Arrogance** — she genuinely believes she is the only "real" person in the world (see [[SingularitySolipsism]]).

This is the female-protagonist sibling of the Aurelius line of campaigns ([[AureliusJuliaCaesar]] v1 "Pax Caesara", Aurelius Caesar v3 "Iron Eagle: Vengeance") — same dual-mask Lawful Evil Julian-heir archetype, same Spartacus/ludus infiltration hook as v3, gender-swapped and re-dated. It continues the documented player pattern of [[BeautyAsWeapon]] and the serpent-mastermind persona.

## Character architecture (Level 12 "Singularity")

- **Class**: Level 12 **Imperator** — [[GestaltImperatorClass]], a gestalt Paladin (Oath of Conquest/Crown) × Sorcerer (Divine Soul).
- **Alignment**: Lawful Evil, self-described "Benevolent Tyrant / The Sadist."
- **Stats**: STR 10, DEX 20 (+5), CON 14, INT 16, WIS 14, **CHA 22 (+6, "God-Tier")** granting +6 to all saves via Paladin aura. HP 112, **AC 26** (19 armor + 2 Dex + 5 shield), Spell Save DC 21, Passive Perception 16.
- **Paladin side**: Divine Smite, Aura of Conquest (10-ft fear freeze), Channel Divinity: Conquering Presence (AoE fear).
- **Sorcerer side**: Subtle Spell metamagic for verbal/somatic-free casting — explicitly framed as "essential for social domination"; cantrips Prestidigitation (cleaning blood), Friends, Minor Illusion.

## Inventory as social/combat infrastructure ([[SocialArmorWardrobe]])

- **"Middle-Tier" disguise** (equipped): respectable teal wool stola + new-money gold jewelry — Advantage on Deception to appear harmless, explicitly designed to bait Batiatus into thinking he can exploit her.
- **True Julian Regalia** (hidden): translucent Tyrian purple gown "worth a small city" + gold laurel diadem — Advantage on Intimidation vs Roman officials; signals royalty in all but name.
- **Mors Celeris** ("Swift Death"): +3 finesse gladius of blackened meteoric iron, silent when drawn; +14 to hit, 1d6+8 piercing + Smite.
- **Shadow of the Palatine**: +3 mithral breastplate corset, AC 19, no Stealth disadvantage.
- **Aegis of the Eagle**: +3 obsidian sentinel buckler, Advantage on Initiative and Perception.
- **Heart of Venus**: Amulet of the Devout +3 (ruby with internal heat) — the stated source of her magical dominance.
- **Sandals of the Prowler**: silent steps, Advantage on Stealth.

All items are lore-justified as First Founder artifacts "forged with lost techniques that defy physics" to preserve the low-magic conceit.

## Timeline note

The module dates itself **82 BCE** yet stations [[Spartacus]] at the ludus of [[Batiatus]] — historically a 73 BCE (Third Servile War) situation, and the sibling Aurelius v3 campaign sets the same war in 71 BCE. The Pax Julia Alternate appears to compress or relocate the Spartacus material rather than track the historical or v3 chronology.
