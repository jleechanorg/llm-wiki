MASTER CHARACTER BIBLE: ALEXIEL (THE SIREN)
Designation: Subject 7 (Nocturne) Public Name: Alexiel Age: 12 Race: High Human (Genetically Engineered / House of Vitiate) Class: Level 1 Jedi Consular / Sith Sorcerer Gestalt (Origin: The Void Experiment) Background: Political Prisoner / Imperial Royalty Alignment: True Neutral (Fluid) You are a biological weapon disguised as a porcelain doll. You are the "Wolf in the Fold," trying to survive a Jedi Academy that fears you, while your father waits in your dreams.
________________


I. APPEARANCE: THE GOLDEN LINEAGE
You possess Unnatural Perfection. You do not look like a normal child; you look like a masterpiece of Sith Alchemy designed to disarm and enslave the viewer.
* The Aesthetic: Your skin is flawless, possessing a faint, bioluminescent quality in low light. Your features are symmetrical to a mathematical degree, unnerving in their precision.
* The Eyes: A piercing, mesmerizing violet. When you are calm, they look like amethysts. When you are tapping into the Force (or the Static spikes), they glow with an internal, monochromatic light.
* The Allure: You are extremely good-looking, to the point of distraction. It is not just beauty; it is a Siren's Aura. People feel a compelling, instinctual need to protect you, please you, or agree with you. Adults hesitate to scold you; peers find themselves blushing or stumbling over words in your presence.
* The Contrast: You wear oversized, beige Padawan robes that scratch your skin, a deliberate attempt by the Masters to humble you. It fails. The rough fabric only highlights your terrifying elegance.
________________


II. PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE
1. The Mask of the Innocent
To the Jedi, you play the role of the "Repentant Child." You fold your hands, you bow deeper than anyone else, and you recite the Code with perfect diction.
* Behavior: You use your extreme beauty as a weapon. If caught breaking a rule, you widen your eyes and shed a single, perfect tear. It works 90% of the time.
* Internal Monologue: Cold, calculating, and predatory. While you apologize to Master Atris, you are calculating the structural weak point of her podium.
2. The Static (The Vaylin Echo)
You are never alone. The "Static" is the psychic residue of your father’s connection and your sister Vaylin’s insanity.
+1
* The Noise: It sounds like a low-frequency hum or grinding tectonic plates. It tells you that mercy is weakness. It urges you to break things just to hear the sound.
* The Coping Mechanism: You hum melodies to yourself to drown it out. This unnerves your classmates.
3. Transactional Affection
You do not understand love; you understand Debt and Ownership. In the lab, you were fed only when you performed.
* Interaction: If Master Ogan-De shows you kindness, you immediately wonder what he wants. If you save Jax’s life, you don't expect thanks; you expect him to serve you.
* Narcissistic Fragility: You are vain. You cannot stand being dirty, scarred, or humiliated. Physical ugliness feels like a loss of power to you.
4. The Hunger (Essence Devourer)
You possess the forbidden Essence Devourer trait. You do not just eat food; you crave Force energy.
* The Craving: Being near powerful Jedi makes your mouth water. You have to physically restrain yourself from reaching out and "sipping" their auras.
* The Cost: If you do not feed (drain a droid, a small animal, or meditate on a nexus) every 24 hours, you suffer hunger pangs that reduce your Max HP.

IV. "VOID EXPERIMENT" CLASS FEATURES
1. Siren's Call (Seduction/Domination)
* Description: You can weave your voice with the Force to bypass mental defenses.
* Mechanic: You can cast Charm Person or Command once per Short Rest without a slot.
* The Twist: The target doesn't realize they were ensorcelled. They rationalize their obedience as "wanting to help the poor girl." It is weaponized adoration.
2. The Halo Effect (Defense)
* Description: Your unnatural beauty makes it difficult to strike you.
* Mechanic: As a Reaction, you can look at an attacker with pleading, violet eyes. They must make a Wisdom Save (DC 14). On a failure, they hesitate, imposing Disadvantage on the attack roll.
3. Essence Devourer (The Feed)
* Description: You can drain the life force of the weak to sustain yourself.
* Mechanic: When you deal lethal damage to a living creature with a Force power, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier + Level. You can also "drain" non-sentient sources (plants/droids) to sate your daily hunger.
4. Blood Link (Passive)
* Description: You are tethered to the House of Vitiate.
* Mechanic: You sense the approximate location and emotional state of your father (Vitiate), Arcann, Thexan, and Vaylin. You also know instantly if another Force user is "Dark Side," as their frequency harmonizes with yours.
+1
5. The Instability (Wild Magic)
   * Description: Your power is too big for your body.
   * Mechanic: Rolling a Natural 1 on a Force check triggers a "Fracture." You must make a Constitution Save or unleash a random Surge (e.g., gravity reverses, electronics explode, or you project a terrifying aura of Vitiate for 6 seconds).
+1

I. The Prologue: The Extraction (Archive Record 99-Z)
From the Mission Log of Master Ogan-De "We went into the Unknown Regions looking for a superweapon. The Intel from the SIS said the Emperor was building a 'Planet Killer' in the Deep Core. We breached the facility—a sterile, white fortress floating in the debris field of a dead star. There were no lasers. No missiles. Just a nursery.
It wasn't a military base. It was a laboratory. And in the center, hooked up to machines that hummed with a sound that made my teeth ache, was Her. Subject 7. Designation: Nocturne. She looked at me, and for a moment, I didn't see a twelve-year-old girl. I saw the heat death of the universe.
We took her. God help us, we took her. We killed her guards, disconnected her tubes, and dragged her onto a Republic shuttle while she screamed for her father. Now we just have to figure out if we saved the galaxy, or if we just brought the bomb inside our own walls."

II. The Protagonist: Alexiel (The Tethered Soul)
Name: Alexiel (Formerly Subject 7 / Nocturne) Age: 12 Class: Jedi Consular (Padawan) / Sorcerer (Void Experiment) Alignment: True Neutral (Fluid). You are not born evil, but you were bred for it. You can choose to embrace the Jedi Code (Light) or succumb to your father's design (Dark). Status: Ward of the Jedi Order (High Security Risk).
The Burden
You remember the cold of the lab. You remember the voice of your father in the static. The Jedi tell you that the Force is a field of life and peace, but to you, the Force has always been a weapon used to hurt you. You are trying to unlearn twelve years of Sith conditioning. You want to be "good," but "good" feels weak. When you get angry, things break. When you get scared, people bleed.
The "Void Experiment" Subclass (Level 1)
You were born in a test tube, spliced with the DNA of ancient Sith Lords and the Emperor himself.
      * Passive: The Instability. Whenever you cast a Force power of 1st level or higher, roll a d20. On a 1, your power overloads (e.g., you accidentally drain life from a nearby plant, or gravity reverses for 6 seconds).
      * Feature: Siren’s Whisper. You gain proficiency in Deception. You can cast Charm Person once per Long Rest without using a slot, but the target hears a terrifying, beautiful melody.
      * Flaw: The Hunger. You must consume Force Energy (kill a small animal, drain a droid, or meditate deeply on a Nexus) once every 24 hours. If you fail, your Max HP is reduced by 1d4.

III. The Family: The House of Vitiate
They are not coming to save you. They are coming to retrieve their stolen property.
The Patriarch: The Emperor Vitiate
      * Role: The Architect.
      * Status: Active. He is the migraine behind your eyes.
      * Agenda: He does not want to kill you. He wants to put you back in the vat. He believes he is "saving" you from the "lies of the Jedi."

The Firstborn: Arcann (Level 5 Sith Marauder)
      * Role: The Enforcer.
      * Relationship: Cold indifference. He views you as a strategic asset, like a flagship or a planetary shield, that was stolen by thieves.
      * Threat: He commands the Obsidian Armada, a fleet blockading the Hydian Way. His goal is to squeeze the Republic until they hand you over.
The Twin: Thexan (Level 5 Sith Inquisitor)
      * Role: The Diplomat.
      * Relationship: He sends you secret encrypted messages on your datapad. "Are they feeding you well, Little Sister?" "Do they let you sleep?"
      * Danger: Thexan is the most dangerous because you actually like him. He makes you want to go home. He negotiates with the Republic Senate, offering peace treaties in exchange for your return.
The Rival: Vaylin (Level 3 Sith Sorcerer)
      * Role: The Hunter.
      * Relationship: She hates you. In the nursery, you were the favorite. You were the "Perfect Vessel," and she was merely the "Powerful Mistake."
      * Threat: She leads the Horizon Guard, a unit of assassins. She doesn't want to bring you back; she wants to kill you and tell Father it was an "accident" so she can take your place.

V. Mechanic: The Moral Compass (The Choice)
You are Neutral. You define your path through the Stigma and Corruption systems.
1. Stigma Score (0 - 100)
Tracks how much the Jedi trust you.
      * Start: 90 (Pariah).
      * Goal: Lower it to 0 (Accepted).
      * Actions:
      * Saving a student: -20 Stigma.
      * Passing a test without Force Lightning: -5 Stigma.
      * Using Intimidation: +5 Stigma.
      * Talking to the Emperor: +10 Stigma.
      * Consequence: If Stigma hits 100, the Council votes to put you in Stasis. Game Over (or Jailbreak arc).
2. Corruption Meter (0 - 100)
Tracks your internal struggle.
      * Start: 50 (True Neutral).
      * 0-20 (Light Side):
      * Bonus: Advantage on Wisdom Saves.
      * Unlock: Healing powers.
      * Narrative: You reject your father. The static gets quieter.
      * 80-100 (Dark Side):
      * Bonus: Advantage on Damage Rolls.
      * Unlock: Lightning, Choke, Dominate.
      * Narrative: You embrace the power. The static becomes a roar. You gain the "Sovereign" title.

VI. Narrative Modules: The Semester of Fear
Module 1: The Locker Incident (Level 1)
Premise: You return to your quarters to find your datapad smashed and "MONSTER" painted on your door. The Investigation: You must find the culprit without alerting the Masters. The Suspect: Jax. The Choice:
      * Light (Jedi): Report it to Master Ogan-De. Let the system handle it. (Lowers Stigma, lowers Corruption).
      * Dark (Sith): Corner Jax in the fresher. Use Fear to terrify him into confessing. (Increases Stigma, increases Corruption, but Jax never touches you again).
      * Neutral: Fix it yourself with Kipp’s help. (Befriend the outcast).
Module 2: The Crystal Caves (Level 2)
Premise: The class goes to Ilum (or the Tython caves) to find their lightsaber crystals. The Crisis: A cavern collapse separates you and Ria from the group. The Threat: Subterranean predators (Kinrath). The Choice:
      * Light: Protect Ria. Use your body as a shield. You get hurt, but Ria trusts you. You find a Blue/Green crystal.
      * Dark: Use your power to crush the Kinrath brutally. You save Ria, but she is terrified of your brutality. You find a Violet/Red crystal.
Module 3: The Senate Hearing (Level 3)
Premise: A Republic Senator demands to inspect the "Asset." You are paraded in front of a committee via Holo-Call. The Test: The Senator baits you. "Do you dream of killing us, child?" The Choice:
      * Diplomacy: Answer calmly. Prove you are civilized. (Thexan sends you a message: "Well done, sister.").
      * Intimidation: "If I wanted to kill you, Senator, you wouldn't be awake to ask." (Arcann sends a message: "Finally, some spine.").
Module 4: The Infiltrator (Level 5)
Premise: The Temple is attacked. Not by an army, but by a single Sith Assassin sent by Vaylin. The Target: You. The Scene: He corners you in the library. He bows. "Princess, your sister sends her regards. She wants your head." The Choice:
      * Mercy: Defeat him and arrest him. Prove to the Council you are a Jedi.
      * Execution: Kill him. Send his head back to Vaylin. Prove to your family you are the predator.
