# Campaign alexiel assiah — *The Century of Schism* world guide, Part 1 (v2)

**Source**: Campaign document "campaign alexiel assiah" v2 — the first installment of a complete campaign guide for the [[WorldOfAssiah]] in Year 1509. It covers expanded world history, the cosmological backdrop of the [[AbyssalWar]], the realities of the [[CenturyOfSchism]] civil war, and (per its own outline) the geographical theaters of war. The player character is [[Alexiel]], operating in the Western Marches between two ancient commanders: [[Raziel]] (Imperium) and [[Lucifer]] (revolutionary Host).

## Campaign architecture

Designed for solo or small-group play with **exceptionally high persistence and very low drift** — an explicit [[HighPersistenceWorldState]] contract: Alexiel's actions in the Western Marches permanently ripple outward, shifting supply lines and frontlines and forcing both Raziel and Lucifer to adapt continental strategy. "The world remembers every burned village, every spared child, and every unleashed spell."

### Thematic pillars
- **The Weight of Immortality** — commanders are ancient, traumatized, and dangerously uncompromising after 1,000+ years of war.
- **The Mathematics of Slaughter** — war as logistics, attrition, and calculated terror (the [[FiftyPercentCullingDoctrine|50% Culling Doctrine]]).
- **The Mask and the Monster** — the psychological toll of performing remorseless killing while hiding desperate empathy; a direct sibling of the [[Mask-of-Fragility]] persona mechanics in the Ains/Aizen campaigns ([[MaskAndMonster]]).

## Grand timeline

### Age of Foundation (Years 1–500)
- The Creator and the Seraphim conquer primordial chaos and establish the [[CelestialImperium]] to anchor physical law.
- **The [[EdictOfChains]]**: all "chaotic" mortal races (elves, dwarves, orcs, goblins, humans) are enslaved; millions die building [[Aeterna]], the Imperial Capital — itself a continent-spanning magical ritual enforcing divine order.
- **The First Rot (Year 400)**: the [[EmpyreanAffliction]] — latent madness (megalomania, paranoia, cold cruelty) — appears in pure-blooded celestial lines, hinting the Creator's design is flawed.

### The Abyssal War (Years 500–1409)
- A ~900-year meat-grinder against demonic hordes flooding through planar rifts.
- [[Lucifer]] (the Morning Star, 900 years on the front lines) and [[Raziel]] (the Architect, grand strategist and master of magical defenses) rise as brothers-in-arms and the Imperium's greatest champions.
- **The Revelation (Year 1400)**: a dying Demon Prince reveals the demons are the Creator's deliberate mechanism — the war is an engineered, endless crucible to test and cull the Empyrean bloodlines for the absent god's amusement ([[EngineeredCrucibleWar]]).
- **The Silent Decade (1400–1409)**: Lucifer's faith shatters; he silently confirms the truth, converts his veteran legions, and prepares to turn against heaven.

### The Century of Schism (Years 1409–1509)
- **1409**: Lucifer declares the Imperium a false idol, purges Imperial loyalists in his ranks in one night, and marches on Aeterna.
- **1412, the [[MarchOfBrokenChains]]**: needing infantry to break Raziel's lines, Lucifer shatters the Imperial slave colonies and absorbs the freed mortal races into his Host. *(Source text truncates mid-sentence here — Part 1 continues beyond the ingested excerpt.)*
- Year 1509 (present): exactly 100 years of civil war; the earth is scarred by arcane artillery, skies gray with ash, mortals caught between "a mad, stagnant Imperium and a ruthless, revolutionary Host."

## Relation to other campaigns

Another instance of the operator's taste profile: morally gray masterminds, hidden-empathy masks, and grand strategic scope (compare [[SosukeAizen]]'s BG3 arcs and the [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] pattern). The Lucifer arc — beloved champion → revelation of a rigged cosmos → apocalyptic revolutionary — mirrors the [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]] preference for protagonists who indict the gods themselves.
