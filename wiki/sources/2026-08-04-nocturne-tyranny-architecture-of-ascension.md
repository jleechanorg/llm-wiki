# Campaign Module: The Architecture of Ascension (Nocturne × Tyranny)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign nocturne tyranny" — a new setting-branch of the [[NocturneSosuke]] campaign line, moving the protagonist out of [[BaldursGate]] and into [[Terratus]], the world of Obsidian Entertainment's [[TyrannyGame|Tyranny]] (The Tiers, Year 431 TR — "The Year of the Sword"). System: Tyranny / modified D&D 5e hybrid. Tone: psychological horror / political grand strategy / metaphysical thriller. Declared themes: "The Refactoring of Reality & The Burden of Competence."

**Note**: The source text is truncated mid-sentence in section II ("...I control the demand of their w"); only the character profile and the first mask are fully specified in this capture.

## Character Profile: Nocturne, Fatebinder

- **Name/Title**: Nocturne, [[FatebinderArchetype|Fatebinder]] — styled "The Ascendant Architect" / "The Ghost in the Machine."
- **Age**: 16 physically, "ancient intellectually" (contrast: 18 in the v4 [[2026-08-04-noctune-bg3-v4-velvet-cage|Velvet Cage]] iteration).
- **Class**: Bard ([[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]]) mechanically, roleplayed as an Order of Scribes Wizard — see [[RealityRefactoring]]. She does not sing; she "recites mathematical proofs that force reality to correct itself," conducting magic with her blade as a baton.
- **Background**: Haunted One ("The Survivor of Incompetence") / Courtier ("The Fixer").
- **Alignment**: Lawful Evil, "The Systematizer" — a self-authored Code held superior to [[Kyros]]' law because it is "based on optimization, not vanity."

## Aesthetic: The Deceptive Grime

Symmetrical beauty deliberately obscured under road dust and ill-fitting scavenged leathers — a "ghost in plain sight" the eye slides over. The tell is the eyes: they *scan* (masonry flaws, caloric inefficiency of a guard's gait, probability lines of Edict magic — cf. [[EdictOfExecution]]). When the mask slips, posture shifts to "terrifying, statuesque perfection": "You do not run; you arrive."

## Existential Archetype: The Wounded Architect

INTJ–ENTP hinge, "The God in Waiting." Views Terratus as a flawed schematic; oscillates between rigorous precision (Sigil pronunciation, surrender-contract wording, [[Disfavored]] supply logistics) and cosmic ambition to rewrite the laws of magic. The mask is total objectivity ("just the facts, show me the math" — the only adult in the room among Archons); the reality is a hypersensitive observer who "fears insignificance more than failure" and channels terror into productive machinery. Explicitly integrates Griffith (dream that burns away morality) and [[AizenSosuke|Aizen]] (contempt for stagnation — [[GravenAshe]], the [[VoicesOfNerat]], even Overlord [[Kyros]]): "a deity in chrysalis" who believes enough optimized variables solve the equation of life and finally make her safe.

## Mask 1: The Courtly Shadow (social persona)

Archetypes: [[JohanLiebert|Johan Liebert]] (the Mirror) + the anxious-preoccupied "Fixer." Core mechanic is [[DependencyLoopManipulation]]: front-load value (reorganize party inventory for 15% weight efficiency while others sleep; offer researched herbal remedies faster than magic) to become indispensable — "not to be loved, but to create a dependency loop." The Nihilistic Mirror reads subconscious desires and reflects them back: to [[GravenAshe]] she is the dutiful, reverent child; to the [[VoicesOfNerat]] the chaotic admiring prodigy. Goal: make targets feel so deeply seen they lower defenses, then locate the internal "switch" — the trauma or desire that makes them dance. Internal logic: "If I am essential, they cannot discard me. If I control the supply of solutions, I control the demand of their w[ants]" (text cut off here).

## Relation to the campaign line

This is the first non-Forgotten-Realms branch of the Nocturne line (v3–v5 are all [[BaldursGate]]-anchored). Constants carried over: teenage prodigy protagonist, [[BeautyAsWeapon|beauty/competence as instruments]], mask-based social play, Lawful Evil systematizer ethos, Aizen/Johan archetype stack. New here: the Tyranny political frame (Kyros' Archons, Disfavored vs [[ScarletChorus]]), the Fatebinder legal-authority role, and the Scribes-Wizard "reality as code review" flavor.
