# Aizen God Campaign — Chat 1, Entries 601-900

**Source**: Gemini DM chat export, entries 601-900 of chat 1 of the Aizen god campaign (in-world date 1492 DR, Flamerule 12, ~04:31). Sibling segments: [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-1-1-300|entries 1-300]], entries 301-600, entries 901-1147, chat 2, and the mortal-era chat. Spine document: [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-summary]].

## What happens

[[AizenSosuke]] (Level 12, CHA 23) bypasses the warded manor of the [[KnightsOfTheShield]] in [[BaldursGate]] using Knock, then seizes control of the encounter before the faction's agents can interrogate him — winning the confrontation of wits before it begins.

## Key events

- **Threat assessment**: Aizen models the Knights as an intelligence faction — individually weaker than him, collectively dangerous through information networks and subtle manipulation of other factions. Their goal is to classify him as threat or asset to their program of "order."
- **The constructed truth** ([[ManufacturedTruthNarrative]]): Aizen delivers a calculated blend of fact and deception to two Knight agents (an impeccably dressed woman with sharp eyes; a burly man in reinforced leather):
  - His parents [[CorvanSosuke]] and [[SeraphinaSosuke]] attempted the [[GrandConjunction]] — a ritual to physically manifest [[Bane]] in Faerûn, believing they could control him.
  - Bane betrayed them, intending to consume the channelers as fuel for his manifestation.
  - Corvan died "realizing the treachery"; Seraphina supposedly fled into hiding with a counter-ritual capable of shattering Bane's manifestation.
  - Aizen claims to have purged Bane's influence from the Sosuke Estate and a Heapside Ward cultist hub, with his brother [[MalachiSosuke]] purging another.
- **Framing** ([[UnwittingAllyRecruitment]]): Aizen positions the Bane threat as an attack on the very "balance" the Knights claim to preserve — recruiting a rival intelligence faction as unwitting allies by aligning his fabricated story with their institutional mission.

## Character notes

- Aizen is Level 12 in this segment; earlier segments track the ascent from Level 8 (see [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-summary]] for the full arc to The Absolute).
- Signature pattern: control what the opposition believes the situation *is* before any contest occurs — consistent with the serpent-mastermind protagonist profile across all campaigns in this lineage.
