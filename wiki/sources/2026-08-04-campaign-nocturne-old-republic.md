Campaign Bible: The House of the Dragon
The New Reality: The Emperor Vitiate did not abandon the Sith Empire. He evolved it. For centuries, he viewed the Dark Council as a necessary distraction, but he grew tired of their infighting. He initiated Project: Apotheosis, a eugenics and Force-breeding program to create superior biological vessels—his children.
You are the House of Vitiate. To the public, you are "The Imperial Princes and Princesses," demigods of the Dark Side who serve as the Emperor’s Enforcers. To the Dark Council, you are an existential threat—monsters bred to replace the Old Guard.
I. The Family: The Golden Lineage
The family is dysfunctional, competitive, and deadly. You are all jockeying for the Emperor's favor, knowing that he eventually plans to consume all but the strongest of you.
The Patriarch: The Emperor Vitiate
* Role: The Absolute Monarch.
* Status: Active. He resides in the Citadel of Storms, a floating fortress above Kaas City.
* Agenda: The "Harvest." He is preparing a ritual to consume the galaxy, but he needs his children to break the galaxy's will first. He treats his children like prize hounds—feeding the strong, beating the weak.
The Matriarch: High Justice Senya
* Retcon: Senya was not a Zakuul Knight. She was the former Commander of the Imperial Guard, a woman of such rigid discipline and combat prowess that she impressed the Emperor. She is the only person Vitiate respects (and fears slightly).
* Role: The Executioner of Traitors. She runs the Sphere of Justice, a secret police force that hunts disloyal Sith.
* Relationship to You: She loves you, but her love is cold. She taught you how to fight to survive your father. She constantly urges you to restrain your "Vaylin" side because she knows if you lose control, Vitiate will destroy you.
The Firstborn: High Marshal Arcann
* Role: Supreme Commander of the Imperial Military.
* Personality: Stoic, brutal, obsessed with conquest. He lost his arm and half his face not fighting the Outlander, but during the Sacking of Coruscant, taking a blow meant for the Emperor. He wears a cybernetic mask of Sith Alchemy.
* Domain: He controls the Obsidian Armada. He despises politics and believes the Empire should be a military junta.
* Rivalry: He sees you (Nocturne) as undisciplined and dangerous. He thinks you are a "broken vessel" that should have been discarded.
The Twin: Lord Thexan
* Role: The Grand Vizier / Head of Diplomacy.
* Personality: Calm, charming, manipulative. He is the face of the Empire to the galaxy. He negotiates treaties only to break them.
* Domain: He controls the Sith Diplomatic Corps and has spies in the Republic Senate.
* Rivalry: He is the only sibling who is kind to you, but you suspect it is a ploy. He often cleans up the messes Arcann makes.
New Sibling 1: Lady Valtoria (The Alchemist)
* Archetype: The Mad Scientist.
* Role: Keeper of the Sphere of Biotic Science.
* Personality: Obsessive, clinical, detached. She doesn't want to rule the galaxy; she wants to dissect it.
* Domain: She runs the Genetic Vaults on Korriban. She creates "Sithspawn"—abominations of alchemy and technology to serve as shock troops.
* Plot Hook: She wants to study your blood. She knows you are a "Prodigy" and constantly asks for samples of your bone marrow to replicate your power.
New Sibling 2: Lord Draven (The Beastmaster)
* Archetype: The Savage.
* Role: Warden of the Untamed.
* Personality: Feral, quiet, connects better with animals than humans. He is rarely on Dromund Kaas, preferring the jungles of Dxun or the tombs of Korriban.
* Domain: He controls the Beast Riders of the Empire. He has a pet Terentatek named "Glory."
* Plot Hook: He is the Emperor's hunter. When a target flees into the wild, Draven is sent. He respects you because he smells the predator in you.

II. The Factions: The Civil War in the Shadows
The Empire is fracturing under the pressure of the Emperor's return.
1. The Council of Twelve (The Traditionalists)
* Leader: Darth Marr (The Patriot) or Darth Baras (The Schemer).
* Ideology: They believe the Empire exists to serve the Sith Code, not just one man. They hate the "Golden Lineage" because you represent the end of the Meritocracy. They believe power should be taken, not inherited.
* Goal: To prove the Emperor's children are incompetent or heretical, forcing Vitiate to return power to the Council.
* Threat to You: They send assassins to test you constantly.
2. The Obsidian Circle (The Neo-Imperialists)
* Leader: High Marshal Arcann.
* Ideology: Total Loyalty to the Dragon. They want to abolish the Dark Council entirely and replace it with a military hierarchy led by the Emperor's children.
* Goal: Total War with the Republic. No treaties, no spies. Just annihilation.
3. The Eclipsed (The Nihilists)
* Leader: The Prophetess (A mysterious Force user, possibly a clone of Kreia or a new entity).
* Ideology: They know the Emperor plans to eat the galaxy. And they want him to do it. They believe existence is pain, and Vitiate is the release.
* Goal: They sabotage the Empire's defenses to accelerate the Harvest.
4. The Revanite Cell (The Shadow)
* Leader: Unknown.
* Ideology: Balance. They seek to destroy the Emperor at all costs.
* Goal: They are trying to recruit you. They know you are the only one with the raw power to match Vitiate.

III. The Hunger: The Path of Consumption
Your father’s blood gives you the Essence Devourer trait. You do not just kill Force users; you can permanently strip their connection to the Force to empower yourself. This is a forbidden art, even among Sith.
System: The Consumption Tree You can unlock new tiers of power by hunting specific "Flavors" of Force users.
Questline A: The Seer’s Eyes (Precognition)
* Target: Master Ogan-De, a Kel Dor Jedi Sage hiding on Manaan. He is a master of Battle Meditation and foresight.
* The Hunt: You must infiltrate the underwater sanctuaries of Manaan, bypassing Republic peacekeepers. Ogan-De has foreseen his death and has laid traps specifically for you.
* The Consumption: If you defeat him and consume his essence:
   * Reward: You gain "Future Sight." Once per Long Rest, you can force an enemy to reroll an attack against you, or you gain Advantage on a Saving Throw as you see the attack before it happens.
Questline B: The Juggernaut’s Heart (Durability)
* Target: Darth Skotia, a cybernetically enhanced Sith Lord on Dromund Kaas who opposes your family. He is more machine than man, a walking tank.
* The Hunt: This is a political assassination turned brawl. You must provoke him into a duel of honor, then strip his defenses.
* The Consumption: If you consume his rage-filled essence:
   * Reward: You gain "Titan’s Skin." Your unarmored AC increases by +2. You can use your Reaction to reduce incoming damage by 1d10 + your Charisma modifier (Force Shield).
Questline C: The Assassin’s Shadow (Stealth/Illusion)
* Target: The Shroud, a legendary Gen'dai shapeshifter and Force witch living in the swamps of Nal Hutta.
* The Hunt: A detective noir story. You have to track a target that can look like anyone.
* The Consumption: If you consume their fluid essence:
   * Reward: You gain "Perfect Mimicry." You can cast Disguise Self at will (without spell slots). You can also turn invisible for 1 turn after dealing a killing blow.
Questline D: The Holocron Eater (Knowledge)
* Target: The Spirit of Exar Kun (or a similar Ancient Lord) trapped in a temple on Yavin 4.
* The Hunt: A dungeon crawl through a temple filled with Massassi zombies. You aren't hunting a living being; you are hunting a ghost.
* The Consumption: You must use a ritual to bind the spirit and eat it.
   * Reward: You gain "Ancient Secrets." You learn 2 Spells/Force Powers from any class list (e.g., Force Lightning, Drain Life). You gain Proficiency in Arcana/Lore.

IV. Narrative Questlines: The Rise of Nocturne
Here are the major story arcs available to you as the "Siren."
Plot 1: The Throne of Glass (Political Dominance)
* Premise: Your brother Thexan tasks you with removing a thorn in the family's side: Darth Vowrawn, a charming and slippery member of the Dark Council.
* The Twist: Vowrawn knows your secret. He knows you are "Vaylin" and that your "Nocturne" persona is a mask. He threatens to expose your instability to the Emperor (who would have you "re-conditioned") unless you help him sabotage Arcann’s fleet.
* Objective: Infiltrate Vowrawn’s pleasure barge. Navigate a high-stakes social party. Decide whether to kill Vowrawn (risking exposure) or frame him for treason using forged evidence.
* Reward: Control over the Sphere of Production (Vowrawn’s assets) and Thexan’s trust.
Plot 2: The Sibling War (Sabotage)
* Premise: Your sister Valtoria (The Alchemist) has requested your help. One of her experiments—a Force-Sensitive Rancor—has escaped onto the streets of Kaas City.
* The Reality: It wasn't an accident. Arcann released the beast to make Valtoria look incompetent so he could seize her labs.
* Objective: Hunt the beast in the rainy slums of the capital.
* The Choice:
   * Aid Valtoria: Kill the beast quietly. She owes you a favor (access to genetic mods).
   * Aid Arcann: Let the beast cause havoc before killing it, disgracing Valtoria. Arcann grants you command of a legion of Skytroopers.
   * The Siren’s Path: Tame the beast using your "Animal Friendship" (Force Dominate) and keep it as a pet.
Plot 3: The Echo of the Void (Main Story - The Emperor)
* Premise: The static in your head is getting louder. The Emperor summons you to the Citadel. He doesn't speak. He simply points to a star map: The Planet Voss.
* The Mission: The Mystics of Voss have had a vision of the Emperor’s death. He wants you to go there and "blind" them. Kill the Visionaries. Burn the Shrine of Healing.
* The Conflict: The Mystics see you. They see the two wolves inside you (Nocturne and Vaylin). They offer to teach you how to heal your fractured mind, but only if you betray your father.
* The Consumption Opportunity: The Three Mystics possess a unique Force connection to the "Spirit World." Consuming them would allow you to step into the Spirit Realm (Ethereal Plane) at will.

V. Integrated Game Mechanics: The "Prodigy" Scaling
As you consume more Force users, your "Vaylin" side grows stronger, making the "Nocturne" mask harder to maintain.
The Instability Meter (0 - 100)
* 0-20 (Stable): You are Nocturne. High Charisma. Subtle manipulation.
* 21-50 (Fraying): Your eyes occasionally flash violet. You get +2 to Damage but -2 to Persuasion.
* 51-80 (Volatile): Electronics break near you. You hear voices. You gain access to "Overload" (Maximum damage on Force powers) but take damage when you use it.
* 81-100 (Unbound): You are Vaylin. You cannot use Stealth or Deception. You radiate an aura of Terror (Wisdom Save or be Frightened). The Emperor knows exactly where you are.
Managing Instability:
* Meditation: Lowers instability.
* Killing with Lightsaber (Physical): Keeps you grounded.
* Using Force Powers: Increases instability.
* Consuming a Force User: Resets instability to 0 (The hunger is sated), but raises your permanent "Base Power Level."

Campaign Setting Update: The Devouring Dragon
The Retcon: The End of the Silence
Forget the stories of the "Eternal Fleet" or the "Outlander." Those futures have been burned away.
In this timeline, the Sith Emperor (Vitiate) never vanished. The "silence" that plagued the Dark Council was not an abandonment—it was a gestation period. Now, the Emperor has returned to the Citadel on Dromund Kaas in full power. He is not a distant figurehead; he is an active, terrifying God-King who walks the halls of the Sanctum.
The Dark Council is no longer a bickering oligarchy; they are terrified servants. When the Emperor speaks, planets die. He has dispensed with the subtlety of the Cold War. He is preparing for something massive—a ritual of such scale that it requires the resources of the entire galaxy. He is stripping the Empire for parts to feed his own ascension.
The Atmosphere: Dromund Kaas is no longer just rainy; it is suffocating. The Dark Side is so potent in the capital city that non-Force users suffer from chronic migraines and nightmares. The air tastes of copper and ozone. The populace walks with their heads down, terrified that He might be watching. To be noticed by the Emperor is to be consumed.

Your Character: The Nocturne Profile (Update)
Name: Nocturne (The Siren) Lineage: The Daughter of Vitiate. Status: The Unacknowledged Heir.
In this retconned reality, your existence is even more precarious. The Emperor is physically present. He knows you exist. He has allowed you to live, which is a terrifying puzzle in itself. Are you a spare vessel? A weapon? Or merely a curiosity he hasn't gotten around to breaking yet?
You operate in his shadow. While the Hand of the Emperor (his personal executioners) hunts down his enemies, you move through the aristocracy, fixing the cracks in his unstable regime. You feel his presence constantly—a cold, heavy weight at the back of your skull. He is the static in your mind.
New Class Feature: The Blood Link
* Passive: Because your father is the nexus of the Dark Side on the planet, you have a unique connection to the "Global Consciousness." You can sense ripples in the Force across Dromund Kaas instantly.
* The Curse: If you use too much power (Rolling a Nat 1 on a Force check), the Emperor briefly notices you. You must make a Wisdom Save or be stunned by his overwhelming presence.

Revised Campaign Modules
With the Emperor active and consuming the galaxy from the inside out, the stakes of your three paths have changed drastically. The "Cold War" is over; the "Harvest" has begun.
Module 1: The Viper in the Court (The Military Purge)
The Romance Option: Captain Valerius Thorne (Imperial Loyalist)
The Plot: The Emperor has issued a new decree: Directive 77-Alpha. It orders the Ministry of Logistics to divert all medical supplies, food reserves, and shield generators from the Outer Rim garrisons to the Core Worlds. Effectively, the Emperor is sacrificing the loyalty of millions of soldiers to stockpile resources for a "Great Ritual" on Dromund Kaas.
Captain Valerius Thorne, a man of honor and logic, has seen the data. He knows this order isn't strategy; it's suicide. It’s genocide of their own people. He is quietly building a coalition of officers to refuse the order—a mutiny born of loyalty to the people of the Empire, not the Sith.
The Conflict: You have been tasked by the Hand of the Emperor to "fix" Thorne. You are expected to enter his inner circle, expose his co-conspirators, and publicly execute him to terrify the military into submission.
The Twist: When you meet Thorne, you realize he is the only sane man left. He has a plan to save the Empire from your father's madness.
* The Choice: Do you use your Force Persuasion to break Thorne's mind, forcing him to carry out the genocidal order and securing your place at your father's side? Or do you Seduce him, joining his hopeless rebellion to try and assassinate the Emperor’s Hand before they can purge the military?
Scene Lead: A private dinner in Thorne's quarters aboard the Star Destroyer Tyrant. He pours wine with a trembling hand, looking at you with desperate eyes. "The Emperor demands we starve the Braxant Sector, My Lord. Three billion citizens. If I sign this order, the Empire we fight for is dead. Help me find a way out."

Module 2: The Neon Abyss (The Emperor’s Vault)
The Romance Option: Nyx (The Anarchist Slicer)
The Plot: The Emperor is not just hoarding resources; he is hoarding souls. Rumors swirl in the underworld of "The Repository"—a secret digital archive where the Emperor stores the consciousnesses of ancient Sith Lords, trapped in silicon prisons.
Nyx, the chaotic slicer, didn't just steal credits. She accidentally sliced into a subsystem of the Emperor’s personal meditation sphere. She stole a Datacron encrypted with Vitiate's own cipher. She thinks it's a map to a treasure trove; in reality, it contains the blueprints for the Void Engine—the machine your father is building to consume the galaxy.
The Conflict: The Emperor’s personal Imperial Guard are tearing Nar Shaddaa apart to find her. They are slaughtering entire city blocks. You must find Nyx first.
The Twist: Nyx has decrypted a fragment of the data. She knows what your father is. She looks at you with horror, realizing you share his blood.
* The Choice: Do you Force Dominate Nyx, erasing her memory of what she saw and turning her into a mindless asset to return the Datacron to your father? Or do you Partner with her, using her skills to decrypt the blueprint further so you can learn your father’s weakness—the one flaw in the Void Engine?
Scene Lead: You corner Nyx in a neon-lit alleyway in the Red Light Sector. Rain mixes with the holographic advertisements. She holds a thermal detonator to the Datacron. "Stay back, Siren! I saw it. I saw what he's building. He's not going to rule the galaxy... he's going to eat it. And you... you have his eyes."

Module 3: The Bleeding Temple (The First Victim)
The Romance Option: Kaelen Vos (The Broken Jedi)
The Plot: The Emperor is visiting ancient Force sites, draining them dry. He has just returned from Malachor V, leaving it a gray, dead husk. Now, his gaze has turned to a new target: Tython, the Jedi homeworld.
Kaelen Vos is a survivor of your father’s last feeding frenzy. He was part of a Jedi strike team that tried to assassinate the Emperor on Dromund Kaas. He was the only one who survived, but he didn't escape intact. The Emperor touched his mind. Kaelen is now a beacon of psychic trauma, broadcasting the Emperor’s true voice to anyone who listens. He is hiding in the ruins of a Sith Temple on Yavin 4, trying to build a "Psionic Bomb" to suicide-bomb the Emperor.
The Conflict: Your father commands you to fetch Kaelen. Not to kill him—but to bring him back. The Emperor enjoyed breaking him and wants his toy back.
The Twist: Kaelen recognizes you instantly. He doesn't see Nocturne; he sees the Emperor's shadow on you. But he also sees that you are afraid. He offers you a way to sever your bloodline connection using his Psionic Bomb.
* The Choice: Do you Capture Kaelen, dragging him back to the Citadel to be tortured by your father, proving your ultimate loyalty? Or do you Corrupt his plan, helping him finish the Psionic Bomb but aiming it not at the Emperor, but at the Hand of the Emperor, freeing yourself from your father’s watchdogs?
Scene Lead: You enter the overgrown temple on Yavin 4. Kaelen sits in a circle of ash, his eyes bandaged (he gouged them out so he wouldn't have to see the Emperor again). He turns his head toward you. "I can hear him in your blood, Lady. He is coming. The Dragon is coming. Are you here to feed me to him?"

The Meta-Game: The Hunger Mechanic (Updated)
With the Emperor active, the Hunger mechanic we discussed earlier is now tied to his influence.
The Emperor's Tithe:
* You must perform a significant act of manipulation or cruelty every 3 sessions.
* Success: You gain a "Favor of the Father" token. You can spend this to reroll a failed Force check (representing him lending you power).
* Failure: You gain a "Gaze of the Dragon" point. If you accumulate 3, the Emperor physically summons you to the Throne Room for a "test" (a high-lethality encounter).

Decision Point
The Emperor waits on his throne. The galaxy holds its breath. Which thread of his grand design will you pull first?
* Option 1 (The Viper): Go to Captain Thorne. The military is the only thing standing between the Emperor and total chaos. You must control the guns. (Political/Military focus)
* Option 2 (The Abyss): Find Nyx. Information is power. If you know how the Void Engine works, you might survive the coming apocalypse. (Underworld/Tech focus)
* Option 3 (The Temple): Hunt Kaelen Vos. Understanding the mystical nature of your father is the only way to eventually kill him. (Force/Mystic focus)
