# House of the Dragon — Custom Campaign Template for WorldArchitect.AI

**Source**: User-requested campaign template for the public [WorldArchitect.AI](https://worldarchitect.ai) wiki. Player character is a **gender-ambiguous bastard Targaryen of [[DaemonTargaryen]]** — a "great bastard" (both parents dragonlord blood) in the mold of [[VisenyaWaters]] / the Visenya campaign line. Setting: the [[DanceOfTheDragons]] civil war (~129-131 AC). Designed to be pasted into the WorldArchitect.AI "Custom Campaign" wizard's description field (verified to accept ≥15,000 characters on the live site).

The character is intentionally **gender-ambiguous** — pronouns, body type, hair styling, and personal style are left as player-shaped variables so the same bible works for he/him, she/her, or they/them protagonists without rewriting.

## Why this template exists

The user has a documented preference profile (see [[2026-08-04-campaign-template-prompt-v3]] "Sovereign Solo" generator) for power-fantasy protagonists whose **visible exceptionalism** is the dramatic engine. The Visenya v7 framing ([[2026-08-04-visenya-v7-daemons-bastard]]) — a bastard of Daemon who looks unmistakably royal — fits that pattern. This template ports the Visenya v7 setup out of the line's existing campaign context and into a **standalone, beginner-friendly, publicly-shareable campaign bible** that anyone can paste into the WorldArchitect.AI "Custom Campaign" wizard.

# Section 1 — Campaign Intro (the hook)

**Title**: *House of the Dragon — The Bastard's Claim*

**World twist**: The Dance of the Dragons burns. King Viserys is dead. Queen Alicent's greens hold King's Landing. Princess Rhaenyra's blacks hold Dragonstone. Both factions tear at a kingdom that has never known dragonfire against dragonfire — until now.

You are a **bastard** of [[DaemonTargaryen]], Prince of the City, by a Targaryen lady whose name the court has been told to forget. Both your parents are dragonlord blood; on your mother's side you carry the lineage of Old Valyria, on your father's side the blood of the Conqueror himself. You have no seat, no inheritance, no name anyone is obliged to remember.

What you have is **the one thing the highborn fear**: a dragon who chose you.

This morning, on a black volcanic beach the maesters will not write about, a medium-sized she-dragon screamed your name. You walked into the smoke. You came out mounted. The greens will call it usurpation. The blacks will call it providence. The smallfolk will call it terrifying.

**Hook as power fantasy**: *You are not saving the realm. You are claiming your place in it — on dragonback, in fire, before anyone can stop you.*

# Section 2 — Character Personality (the mask + the truth)

## Core archetype
**The Apparent Right** — you look, walk, and speak like the trueborn heir everyone wishes you were. The court flinches when you enter a room because your Valyrian features (silver hair, lilac or violet eyes, sharp cheekbones, copper skin) mark you as Daemon's get. You are not subtle. You were never meant to be.

**Public face**: Quiet, courtly, deferential where it costs you nothing — the perfect "bastard trying to make a virtue of illegitimacy."

**True nature**: An apex predator who has decided that legitimacy is a story the powerful tell to the powerless, and that you are now powerful enough to write your own story.

## MBTI hinge (pick one)
- **ENTJ** — Strategic commander. Plays the long game. Uses people as levers.
- **ESTP** — Reactive opportunist. Acts first, justifies after. Charisma in combat and crowd alike.
- **INTJ** — Cold architect. Lets others move first, then dismantles them.

## Sensory signature
- **Voice**: Low and unhurried. Speaks Old Valyrian when angry; the highborn freeze because they forgot you knew it.
- **Hands**: Always clean, nails trimmed short — practical for dragon-handling and for the single sharp ring you wear that belonged to your mother.
- **Smell**: Woodsmoke and salt. You smell like the dragonpits because you refuse perfumes that hide where you came from.
- **Habit**: Tilts head 5° when listening — like a dragon deciding if prey is worth the breath.

## Core Compulsion Mechanic (the Game-Breaking Hook)
**[[BloodOfTheDragonCompulsion]]** — once per session, when your character visibly proves their royal blood in front of a hostile witness, they gain an action economy advantage:

- **Success**: One bonus action on the next turn (representing a burst of preternatural confidence and split-second combat instinct).
- **Failure / Overuse** (3+ uses per session): Roll a Wisdom save (DC 12). On a fail, gain a level of Exhaustion — the dragon's fire is in you, and it asks to be fed.

This is a custom mechanic; flag it to the DM up front so the AI Dungeon Master can track it.

## Interaction shorthand
- **Rivals**: Smile, compliment, then ask the question that makes them realize you already know everything they were trying to hide.
- **Subordinates**: Short sentences. State the task. Wait. They will either perform or be replaced.
- **Family**: The only people who can wound you. The only people you cannot lie to without losing yourself.

## Three inner-monologue thoughts (use any of these to seed the AI's voice)
1. *"They call me 'bastard' as if the word still has teeth. Let them. The dragon doesn't care what they call me. The dragon only knows my name."*
2. *"Mother died so I could live. Every breath I take is borrowed. The least I can do is make it expensive for the people who let her die."*
3. *"Rhaenyra is a queen. Aegon is a king. I am the one on a dragon. The realm will learn which title matters more."*

# Section 3 — Character Class (the rules-as-written payload)

**Class**: **Dragonrider Aristocrat** — a custom gestalt that combines:

- **Level 6 base**: Half the level of a standard Fighter (PHB) for HP; full Sneak Attack progression as a Rogue (1d6 → 3d6 by L6); Medium Armor + Shield proficiency.
- **Primary Attribute**: **Charisma 16+** (you lead, you persuade, you terrify). Secondary: **Dexterity 14+**.
- **Saving Throws**: Dex, Cha (proficient).
- **Skill Proficiencies**: Persuasion, Insight, Intimidation, History, Survival, Animal Handling.

## Signature game-breaking ability
**[[DragonsBond]]** — once per long rest, summon your dragon as a free action. The dragon is a bonded NPC ally with its own stat block (use **Young Dragon** from the Monster Manual as baseline; the dragon's CR scales with your level: Young at L1-5, Adult at L6-10, Ancient at L11+). You and the dragon share the following benefits:

- **Shared senses**: You can see through the dragon's eyes as a bonus action (range: line of sight from dragon's location; the AI will narrate what your dragon perceives).
- **Voice on the wind**: You can speak to your dragon telepathically within 10 miles per character level.
- **Mounted combat**: While mounted, you have advantage on all Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks to stay mounted, and the dragon's attacks count as your attacks for triggering Sneak Attack if you have an ally (your dragon) within 5 feet of the target.

**Note for AI**: if the campaign starts at L6 (recommended), begin with an **Adult Dragon** bond (CR 13, ~195 HP). The dragon has a name the player should pick — examples: *Vyraxes*, *Carmine*, *Maelghar*, *Aethon*, *Saeris*. (Gender-ambiguous dragon naming welcome.)

## Levels 1-30 progression
Levels are abstracted for a solo campaign — your power fantasy is **dragon-led**, not level-grinded. The AI DM should treat level-ups as **story beats**, not XP gates:

- **L1-5**: Establishing claim. Building name. First dragon-claim scene.
- **L6-10**: Court intrigue. Your dragon becomes a political weapon.
- **L11-15**: Major war scene. Storm of dragons. You are a battle-tyrant.
- **L16-20**: Throne turn. Either Rhaenyra's heir or your own claim.
- **L21-25**: Aegon-style conquest. Other kingdoms begin to fear the dragon name.
- **L26-30**: Mythic. You are a name future maesters argue about for 300 years.

If the AI tries to make you grind for XP, override with **THINK:** directive via God Mode: "Time-skip 6 months. Train under the dragon. You are now L6."

# Section 4 — Assets & Retinue

## Starting rank
**"Acknowledged bastard of Prince Daemon Targaryen, no land, no seat, no household knights."** The court gives you the respect fear buys. You live in a single tower room at Dragonstone (the blacks' base), with one trunk of belongings and one dragon.

## Wealth
**30 gold dragons** (about 6 months' wages for a household knight). Enough to bribe a steward, not enough to bribe a lord.

## Safehouses
- **The dragonpits at King's Landing** — if you can survive the greens long enough to land there.
- **Dragonstone's lower caves** — your dragon's lair. You sleep there sometimes; the heat keeps the dreams away.
- **A Velaryon cousin's manse in Driftmark** — neutral ground if the dance gets too hot.

## Blackmail & political leverage
You know three secrets when you arrive in King's Landing:
1. **Lord Larys Strong** is the true power behind Alicent — write this down only if you hear it confirmed by an NPC.
2. **The Hightower fleet** has been quietly hiring sellsails — treason if true.
3. **Mysaria's spy network** has a mole in the Kingsguard.

**Use these carefully**: blackmail is a weapon that only fires once. Spend it on the right lord.

## The Panoply (3-4 starting items)
1. **Mother's ring** — a Valyrian steel band set with a single ruby. Cold to the touch. Worthless to anyone who doesn't know what it is.
2. **A black cloak lined with crimson silk** — once Daemon's. You are 16 and wearing your father's clothes on purpose.
3. **A dagger of Valyrian steel** — a "loaned" piece from the dragonmont's armory. Officially unaccounted for. Officially yours.
4. **A book of Valyrian names** — your mother taught you to read High Valyrian; this is the only book of hers that survived.

## Starting companions (3 NPCs)
1. **Ser Ulwyck — "the Debtor"**: An ex-City Watch knight, age 40, owes your father a blood-debt he can never repay. Loyal because he has no other option. **Class**: Fighter 5. **Loyalty**: 7/10. **Personality**: Bitter, competent, dry-witted.
2. **Alys — "the Steward"**: A 14-year-old girl from Lys, bastard-born (no relation to you), sharp as a new knife. Runs your tower room, knows where every piece of silver in Dragonstone is. **Class**: Commoner (no combat). **Loyalty**: 9/10 (she has nowhere else to go). **Personality**: Quiet, observant, never speaks first.
3. **Maester Lyman — "the Apologist"**: An elderly maester who was on Driftmark when your mother died. He told you the truth about it when you were 8. He's the only adult in your life who has ever voluntarily given you information. **Class**: NPC. **Loyalty**: 8/10. **Personality**: Remorseful, pedantic, kind in a way that hurts.

# Section 5 — Family (the Viper's Nest)

Your family is **House Targaryen at its most dangerous**. Every relationship is a political weapon that also happens to involve people who share your blood.

## The Titan Father — Prince Daemon Targaryen
**Role**: Hegemon of the Crownlands. Rogue prince. The rogue prince. Husband to Rhaenyra (your half-sister-in-law via your father's marriage). Currently at Harrenhal with the Riverlords.
**Power**: Lvl 20+ Fighter/Champion. Rides Caraxes (the Blood Wyrm, biggest red dragon alive).
**Dynamic with you**: He has not acknowledged you in writing. He has not denied you. He sent you the black cloak without comment. **You are a test he is running**. **Loyalty to him**: 5/10 — you love him; you do not trust him. **Threat**: He could legitimize you and break you, or refuse to legitimize you and let the greens destroy you. He enjoys both possibilities.

## The Titan Mother — Lady Rhaenys Targaryen (née Velaryon)
**Role**: The Queen Who Never Was. Your mother's older cousin, the only other woman in the realm who knows what Targaryen blood on the wrong side of legitimacy costs.
**Power**: Lvl 14 Paladin (The Sea-Lion). Rides Meleys (the Red Queen).
**Dynamic with you**: She sees you. She will not protect you, but she will not deny you. **Loyalty to her**: 7/10. **Threat**: If you endanger Corlys's fleet line, she will kill you herself.

## The Four Older Siblings
1. **Rhaenyra Targaryen — "The Queen"**: Half-sister (your father's daughter by his first wife). She is the claimant to the throne. You are a pawn she could legitimize or sacrifice. **Mask**: "We are family." **Real dynamic**: Useful.
2. **Aegon II Targaryen — "The Usurper"**: Half-brother (your father's nephew, raised with him). The greens' king. He will kill you if he sees you. **Mask**: "I am merciful." **Real dynamic**: Enemy of the blood.
3. **Baela Targaryen — "The Drake's Daughter"**: Half-cousin (Daemon's daughter by Laena Velaryon). She and you are both dragonriders, both "of the line." **Mask**: "We are the same." **Real dynamic**: Rival / possible ally.
4. **Jacaerys Targaryen — "The Heir"**: Rhaenyra's eldest. Possible future king. Possible future corpse. **Mask**: "I am your brother." **Real dynamic**: Pawn he doesn't know he's playing.

## Mythos / magic system — Dragon Bond
**Dragons are not mounts. They are bonded souls.**

- A dragon chooses its rider, not the other way around. The claiming is mutual.
- A rider who loses their dragon loses a piece of themselves. Maesters document cases of riders who went "hollow-eyed" within a season of dragon-loss.
- The bond has a range: roughly 100 miles at first claim, extending to "anywhere on the same continent" by adulthood.
- Dragon eggs hatch only in extreme heat (volcanic vents, dragonpits, pyromancer fires).
- **Wild dragons** exist in the Doom of Valyria ruins and in the volcanic deeps of Slaver's Bay. They are not bonded. They are not friendly.

## Current crisis
**The realm has no king everyone accepts.** Rhaenyra holds Dragonstone, the Vale, the Stormlands, and most of the Riverlands. Aegon II holds King's Landing, the Reach, the Westerlands, and Oldtown. The Riverlands are burning. The dragon-counts:
- **Blacks**: Syrax (Rhaenyra), Caraxes (Daemon), Meleys (Rhaenys), Vermax (Jacaerys), Arrax (Lucerys), Tyraxes (Joffrey), Moondancer (Baela), Stormcloud (Aegon the Younger), **YOUR DRAGON**
- **Greens**: Vhagar (Aemond, the largest), Sunfyre (Aegon II), Tessarion (Daeron), Dreamfyre (Helaena)

## Three escalating arcs
1. **Early Arc — The Proving**: First dragon battle. Earn your place among the blacks. Your dragon is a new variable; both sides have to recalculate.
2. **Mid Arc — The Court**: Aegon II's court at King's Landing. You need a foothold inside enemy territory. Blackmail, seduction, and arson are all on the table.
3. **Late Arc — The Endgame**: After Vhagar kills your father's dragon at the Gods Eye, you take Caraxes's rider's role. The fate of the dynasty is on your shoulders — and your dragon's.

# Section 8 — Gazetteer & Mechanics

## Stage 1 — Dragonstone (Black HQ)
**Vibe**: Black volcanic rock, sea wind, dragon smell. The most dangerous place in Westeros for a lowborn.
- **Sub-location: The Dragonmont** — the volcano. Your dragon nests here.
- **Sub-location: The Sea Tower** — where Daemon holds court when he's not at Harrenhal. Rhaenyra's seat.
- **Sub-location: The Cellars of the Keep** — older stonework from the pre-Targaryen Valyrian colonists. Cold and damp; rumored to have a passage to the Dragonmont.
- **Hazard**: Other dragonriders' dragons are territorial. Your dragon is the smallest adult on the rock. Social friction is constant.

## Stage 2 — King's Landing (Green HQ)
**Vibe**: Smell of humanity, gold cloaks, the Red Keep towering. The Faith Militant in the streets.
- **Sub-location: The Dragonpit** — under Aegon II's control. Your dragon cannot land here.
- **Sub-location: The Street of Silk** — your best ally in the green capital.
- **Sub-location: The Red Keep** — Aegon II's throne room. To enter is to die.
- **Hazard**: The Hightower-controlled City Watch will execute any known bastard of Daemon on sight.

## Stage 3 — Harrenhal (the Riverlands)
**Vibe**: Dragonfire-charred ruins. The largest castle in Westeros, melted by dragonflame two generations ago. Now a war-camp.
- **Sub-location: The Hall of a Hundred Hearths** — Daemon's river council meets here. Every lord who pledges here becomes a target.
- **Sub-location: The Gods Eye** — the lake below. Where dragons will die.
- **Hazard**: Vhagar patrols this area. Aemond will try to kill any dragon that crosses his shadow.

## Stage 4 — Old Valyria (the Doom)
**Vibe**: Sulfur, ash, ghosts. The place dragons came from. Off-limits to men.
- **Sub-location: The Fourteen Flames** — volcanoes that have never stopped burning.
- **Sub-location: The Sorcerers' District** — pre-Doom Valyrian wizards left behind artifacts.
- **Hazard**: The Doom never ended. It just slowed down. **Don't go there.**

## 2 Custom Gameplay Systems

### System 1 — **BloodOfTheDragonCompulsion** (see Section 2)
- Once per session, gain a bonus action by visibly proving royal blood in front of a hostile witness.
- Failure penalty: 3+ uses per session = DC 12 Wisdom save or take Exhaustion.

### System 2 — **DragonRider'sHoard**
- Your dragon collects objects of significance to you. They do not eat them. They do not give them back.
- Mechanically, every major story beat triggers a dragon "gift" — an item from the hoard you can give to an NPC as proof of dragon-approval. **NPCs who receive a gift from a dragon are 50% more likely to honor any contract they sign in that moment.**
- DM cue: after each dragon-claim or battle victory, narrate a small object appearing at your dragon's nest.

# Section 9 — Starting Scene

**Atmosphere**: Black volcanic sand. Salt air. The horizon a thin line of grey. Behind you, the smoke column from the Dragonmont rises straight into a sky with no wind.

**Immediate hook**: Your dragon — *name your dragon here* — is screaming. Not the hunting scream, not the war scream. The **claiming scream**, the one that only fires once in a dragon's life when it accepts a rider. It is the scream that has not been heard on Dragonstone in twenty years.

The dragonkeepers are running. The maester is making the sign of the seven. From the Sea Tower, you can see a window open — someone is watching. It might be Rhaenyra. It might be one of her sons. It might be a crossbowman.

The dragon stops screaming. It looks at you. It is the size of a small inn. It is older than you. It knows you.

**Your A/B/C first decision**:

A) **Mount and fly to the Sea Tower** — make your claim a public one. Land on the balcony. Tell Rhaenyra what she has just received.

B) **Touch the dragon's snout first, mount second** — establish the bond silently, in private, before anyone with political weight knows. Walk into the keep as a bastard who is also a dragonrider, and let them wonder how.

C) **Speak to it in High Valyrian** — your mother's voice. The dragon will know. Everyone watching will know you are not a typical bastard.

The campaign begins on your answer.
