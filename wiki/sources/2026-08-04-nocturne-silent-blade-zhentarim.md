# Campaign Bible: The Silent Blade (campaign Nocturne merc zhentarim)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Nocturne merc zhentarim" — a new entry in the Nocturne campaign line (cf. [[NocturneRavencrest]], [[NocturneSosuke]]), this time as a 19-year-old [[Zhentarim]] mercenary operating in the chaotic rebuilding of [[BaldursGate]].

## Character Profile

- **Name**: [[NocturneSilentBlade|Nocturne]], titled "The Silent Blade"
- **Age**: 19
- **Class**: Ranger ([[GloomStalkerConclave]]) — Level 5
- **Background**: Haunted One / Disgraced Noble
- **Allegiance**: The [[Zhentarim]] (The Black Network)
- **Appearance**: Weaponized beauty — form-fitting black leather designed to draw the eye *before* vanishing; "a porcelain mask of perfection"; "to touch you is to bleed."

## Personality Architecture: The Venomous Orchid

The character operates on the [[VenomousOrchid]] archetype — a spectrum between the irresistible temptress (explicitly citing Lust from Fullmetal Alchemist) and the unstoppable slayer (The Predator). Core method: dismantle *people*, not just systems.

### The Two Masks

1. **The Gilded Spider (Seductress)** — social interface built on [[WeaponizedIntimacy]]. Seduction is used for *access*, not romance: every flirtation is a tactical assessment (touching a marksman's arm to gauge muscle density, whispering to a guard to smell the ale on his breath). Targets are made to believe they are the pursuers.
2. **The Butcher in the Dark (Operative)** — not bound by "clean kill" codes. Gore is a *communications medium*: bodies displayed, survivors deliberately traumatized so they never oppose the Zhentarim again. Horror as objective, not byproduct.

### Core Drives

- **The Price of Perfection** — aggressive negotiation as dominance ritual; making a Zhentarim Captain pay double is framed as *his* submission. Wealth is valued as leverage, not luxury.
- **Dominion Through Fear and Desire** — twin control levers; ambition is to become urban legend in Baldur's Gate, a name "whispered with a mix of arousal and terror."
- **The Art of Ruin** — satisfaction in finding the stress point of a reputation, a vault, or a body, and shattering it. Drawn to "no survivors" contracts.

## Mechanical Edge (Gloom Stalker toolkit)

- **Umbral Sight** — master of darkness; sees without being seen.
- **Dread Ambusher** — explosive first strike, ending fights before they begin.
- **Disguise Self** — used for infiltration personas (barmaid, noblewoman, distressed traveler) that close the distance to knife range.

## The Hidden Compulsion: The "Johan" Experiment

The campaign's psychological engine is the [[JohanExperiment]] — a compulsion to prove "civilization is a lie." Nocturne selects pillars of the community (faithful priest, loyal soldier, loving husband) and seduces them into betraying their own codes: the Paladin's oath, the honest merchant's friends. When they fail the test ("and they always fail"), the punishment is death. The name references Johan Liebert of *Monster* — corruption-as-proof, not corruption-as-profit.

## Setting Context

The campaign opens in the chaotic **rebuilding of Baldur's Gate** ("chaos is a ladder"), consistent with the wiki's post-crisis Gate settings (cf. [[BaldursGate]], [[FlamingFist]], [[TheGuild]]). The DM prompt fragment shows a mid-mission scene: a merchant surrendering codes while begging for a promised "reward."
