# Campaign Bible: The Gilded Trap — Blood on the Marble (Gaia Julia v5)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign Gaia julia v5" — the v5 iteration of the Gaia Julia Roman alternate-history campaign line. Tone: political wargame / reverse-horror thriller. Timeline: **73 BCE**; [[JuliusCaesar]] is already Dictator Perpetuo and the [[ThirdServileWar]] has erupted against his order, with [[Spartacus]] and [[Crixus]] burning the Italian countryside.

## Premise: the inverted hostage plot

[[GaiaJuliaCaesar]], 16-year-old daughter of the God-Emperor, travels to the countryside under the alias **"Livia of Capua"** ([[Capua]]) posing as a minor noble's daughter. When rebels sack the villa hosting her, they believe they have captured a terrified pampered noblegirl — a "moral dilemma" for their revolution. In fact they have captured a **Level 12 Bard/Paladin Gestalt** posing as a Level 1 commoner. The campaign inverts the hostage-rescue frame: the captive is the predator, and her win condition is turning the rebel leadership against itself and orchestrating the rebellion's collapse from inside its own command tent — social engineering as the primary weapon, not combat.

## Character design

- **Archetype**: The Black Widow / The Architect (ENFJ-ENTJ hinge) — continuous with the [[DecadentConqueror]] personality line from v6.
- **Two masks**: the public "Livia" facade (fragile, breathless damsel engineered to trigger protector instincts) versus the true Gaia (cold analyst who treats people as instruments and prizes control above all). This extends the v3 [[MaskOfFragility]] deception-DC idea into a full dual-persona structure.
- **Physical signature**: apparent fragility masking Strength 18; unnatural gold eyes tied to the [[Miasma]] / Venus bloodline motif shared with [[BloodOfVenus]]; a consciously releasable pheromonal scent trigger.
- **Interaction shorthand**: scripted manipulation lines per target — paternal-trust framing toward [[Spartacus]], monster-framing to isolate [[Crixus]], and nonverbal cues to [[TheLotusTen]], her embedded slave network.

## Mechanics

- **Class**: Bard, custom **[[CollegeOfTheSiren]]** subclass; the Voice is the spellcasting focus (psionic/pheromonal magic woven into speech), no instrument.
- **[[TheMuse]]** (core compulsion mechanic for the [[SymphonyOfRuin]] drive): crude violence inflicts psychic damage equal to level (Discord penalty); resolving encounters through charm, domination, or acting grants a d12 Inspiration die usable on AC or saves (Harmony bonus). Structurally the successor of v3's [[SanctuaryImperative]]-style morality-as-mechanics design, but inverted — it punishes violence for aesthetic rather than ethical reasons.
- **[[VelvetIron]]** (always active): permanent Subtle Spell metamagic on Enchantment and Illusion spells, allowing Dominate Person or Suggestion mid-conversation with no visible casting. The document cuts off mid-description of this mechanic.

## Version lineage note

v5 sits between v2 (82 BCE, Batiatus ludus) and v6 (71 BCE, Iron Eagle) in in-world chronology, sharing the Third Servile War frame with v6 but predating it, and sharing the hidden-power masquerade with v3's [[MaskOfFragility]]. Each version is an alternate campaign frame for the same character concept, not a continuous canon.
