# God Campaign Prompt Upgrade V0 — the "Divine Deception" Campaign Protocol

**Source**: Campaign system-prompt draft "god campaign prompt upgrade V0" — a DM/Universal-Engine override for a D&D 5e Forgotten Realms campaign in the [[BaldursGate]] 3 era. The player is a **Transcendent Entity (Rank A)** hiding in plain sight: a near-omnipotent deity playing as a "Smurf" in the divine bracket (see [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]]). The engine must track two simultaneous realities per scene — the **Public Reality** (the active Mask/Layer) and the **True Reality** (Rank A stats superior to Greater Deities).

## The Four Layers of Existence ([[FourLayersOfExistence]])

Layer switching is a free action with narrative consequences:

1. **Layer 1 — Mortal Shell**: standard adventurer with class HP/AC/slots; lethal damage is negated by spending True DPP on probability manipulation. Highest Dissonance risk.
2. **Layer 2 — False Deity (Rank Y)**: a fabricated god persona with its own portfolio and church; divine power allowed if it matches the persona's "Brand."
3. **Layer 3 — Primary Mask (Rank Z)**: the acknowledged seat at the Divine Table as a constrained Greater Deity; exceeding the "Transcendent Template" triggers suspicion from [[Ao]].
4. **Layer 4 — Hidden Truth (Rank A)**: exceeds Transcendent Greater Deities; Ao quietly sanctions this existence as long as it serves "Absolute Codification" and does not destabilize the Pantheon.

## Divine Dissonance detection system ([[DivineDissonance]])

A hidden 0–100% meter run in the background. Triggers: **Metric Mismatch** (Rank A damage in a mortal form — the Weave "glitches"), **Probability Violation** (auto-succeeding mathematically impossible checks for the current Mask), and **Direct Observation** (using True [[Divine-Power-Points|DPP]] before another Greater Deity's avatar). Thresholds: 0–20% dismissed as wild magic; 21–50% passive scrying by detection gods ([[Oghma]], [[Savras]]) — the player feels a "gaze"; 51–80% an Inevitable or Solar is dispatched and gods may test the mask with combat; 81–100% the mask fractures and the Pantheon recognizes an unsanctioned Super-Deity.

## Quantified Divine Reference Table ([[QuantifiedDivineReferenceTable]])

Combat is resolved against explicit per-rank stat rows: Mortal (class stats, 0 DPP), Lesser/Rank Y (DH 15, DAC 18, 5 DPP), Intermediate (DH 30, DAC 19, 20 DPP), Greater/Rank Z (DH 100+, DAC 20–21, 100+ DPP, 5 legendary resists), **True Rank A** (DH 180+, DAC 25+, 200+ DPP, +12 auto-hit, 9+ legendary resists, 1d20+20 damage), and [[Ao]] (infinite in every column). Combat rules: auto-success on all mortal-scale checks (DC ≤ 30); Fog of War — the player cannot read true minds of other gods, only surface thoughts and avatars; 1 True DPP narratively negates any hit taken in a lower Layer.

## Named pantheon

[[Mystra]], [[Shar]], and [[Helm]] are the detection-relevant Greater Deities alerted if the deception fractures; [[Oghma]] and [[Savras]] are the passive-scrying knowledge gods; [[Ao]] is the silent sanctioner and hard ceiling of the power table.
