## Premise and character framing

The v7 doc carries **two stacked profile headers** — "The Fallen Siren" and "The Ascendant" — reflecting the Descent Edition's core paradox: Nocturne is simultaneously the shipwrecked Level-0 castaway and the Sovereign of the [[ShatteredRose]] whose authority persists beneath the ruin. She is styled the "Vessel of Stolen Fire": a half-elven frame refined past mortal imperfection into living-marble stillness, with violet eyes that flood pink-crimson when she casts, commands a thrall, or steals a soul. Horns and retractable Abyssal wings can be manifested or hidden at will, letting her pass as a mortal noblewoman.

The **narrative hook** ("a quiet dissonance") defines her presence mechanically rather than cosmetically: no sulfur reek, only a pre-storm sea sharpness; people step back because "her stillness feels louder than their noise"; shadows deepen around her silhouette. Presence, not demand, produces submission.

## The Physical Evolution: Level 0–20

The doc's signature new system is a six-stage appearance-progression table keyed to level bands — the explicit design theme is **"You do not grow monstrous; you grow perfect"**, refinement into the uncanny valley rather than accretion of claws or scales (see [[UncannyValleyProgression]]):

| Levels | Stage | Signature change |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | The Ruin | Shipwreck victim: matted hair, clammy skin, shivering; reads as pitiful and fragile |
| 1–4 | The Porcelain Doll | Grime stops sticking; sweating and shivering cease; eyes reflect light "a little too perfectly" |
| 5–9 | The Uncanny Valley | Living-marble skin; wounds crack and seal rather than bleed; gliding movement; pink-flooded pupils on casting or **Pheromone Spike** |
| 10–14 | The Predator | Blinking becomes voluntary; fragility now reads as a trap; spectral horns/wings flash on Intimidation/Persuasion crits |
| 15–19 | The Sovereign | Gravitational presence (hair/capes float); persistent ozone-and-brine scent; eye-glow lingers, leaving light trails |
| 20 | The Ascended | Statue-brought-to-life apotheosis (source text truncates mid-description) |

This externalizes power level as social-read information: NPC reactions ("too pretty for this mud" → "something is wrong with that girl" → instinctively lowered voices) escalate with level, making the [[BeautyAsWeapon]] motif a leveled mechanic rather than static flavor.
