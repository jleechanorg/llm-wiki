# Aizen mortal campaign start — 'The Shadow over the Gate'

**Source**: Gemini chat export, opening of the mortal-era chat of the [[AizenSosuke|Aizen]] god campaign (Gemini 2.5 Flash ULTRA, "Dungeon master" Gem). The player's chat list shows the campaign lineage: "Aizen godhood (previously bane - main)", "aizen godhood 2", plus unrelated campaigns. This chat is the origin point of the entire Aizen arc that later reaches godhood in [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-2|chat 2]].

## What this is

The player instructs [[Gemini]]: "Look at the bane - main chat for context but start a new dnd campaign in baldurs gate with aizen. Same characters and factions but start with a clean state." This is a [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart]] — reuse the cast and faction web from a prior campaign but reset all state. Gemini responds with a full campaign frame.

## Campaign frame proposed by the DM

- **Title**: [[ShadowOverTheGate|The Shadow over the Gate]] — D&D 5e, set in [[BaldursGate]] (Lower City, Upper City, outskirts).
- **Core themes**: intrigue, shifting loyalties, power struggles, the nature of good and evil, the corrupting influence of ambition.
- **Factions (reimagined)**: [[FlamingFist]] under Grand Duke [[UlderRavengard]] (internal divisions, potential corruption); [[TheGuild]] criminal syndicate (possible power vacuum / new ambitious leader); [[KnightsOfTheShield]] (secretive behind-the-scenes order); [[CultOfTheDeadThree]] (Bane, Bhaal, Myrkul — with Bane a "significant, subtle presence," tying back to the bane-main origin chat); and **House Aizen** — a new rising power fronted by [[AizenSosuke]], mysterious goals, considerable resources and charisma, potentially infiltrating all other factions.
- **Opening**: [[ANightOfUnrest|A Night of Unrest]] — an ordinary evening over an undercurrent of unease: disappearances, escalating gang violence, cult whispers.

## Four starting hooks ([[NarrativeHookScenarios]])

1. **Elfsong Tavern brawl** — gang fight at the [[ElfsongTavern]] between a Guild-aligned gang and a disciplined gang with ritualistic tattoos; mid-chaos theft attempt of a dark-pulsing arcane artifact.
2. **Dockside abduction** — shadowy, unnaturally fast figures abduct a merchant/Flaming Fist officer, leaving an eye-like symbol etched in cobblestones.
3. **Noble's plea** — a frantic minor noble whose guard vanished hires help against a "growing darkness."
4. **Flaming Fist patrol** — a Fist PC investigates missing persons who were critics of the city's power structure; the patrol leader is stressed, routes eerily quiet.

## The common thread

All hooks share a signature of [[SubtleInfluenceCampaignHook|Aizen's subtle influence]]: antagonists display "unusual efficiency" and discipline regardless of entry point — the DM seeds House Aizen as the hidden hand before the PCs know it exists. (Export truncates mid-sentence here.)
