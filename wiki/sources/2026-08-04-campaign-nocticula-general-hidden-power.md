# campaign Nocticula general (hidden power)

## Character Biography: Nocticula


**Name:** Nocticula
**Title:** Scion of the Abyssal Throne (Hidden)
**Age:** 16
**Race:** Half-Demon (Human/Abyssal Hybrid)
**Class Archetype:** Sorcerous Prodigy / Shadow Weaver
**Core Personality:** INTJ / True Neutral (Clinical Pragmatism)


**Lineage & Origin:**
Nocticula is the granddaughter of a reigning Demon King, born into a lineage of catastrophic power. While her demonic kin defined themselves through chaotic consumption and 'inefficient' cruelty, Nocticula’s human heritage introduced a variable of logic and a sliver of empathy. Viewing the demonic hierarchy as a flawed system of diminishing returns, she fled the infernal capital. She currently lives as a fugitive, hiding her royal pedigree and immense mana to avoid both the demonic 'Retrieval Squads' and human 'Purge Slayers.'


**Psychological Profile:**
She operates with a low-empathy, high-functioning cognitive framework. She views social interactions as logistical exchanges and emotions as biological data points. She rarely acts out of malice or altruism; instead, she pursues 'Optimal Outcomes.' To Nocticula, saving an ally is a tactical preservation of a primary asset. She is often described as unsettlingly calm, detached, and blunt.


**Signature Mechanical Traits (The 'Frieren' Scale):**
*   **Mana Veil Mastery:** Nocticula possesses a unique ability to compress her mana into a near-total void. She can hide a reservoir of power equivalent to an ancient archmage, appearing to all magical detection as a mundane peasant or a Level 1 novice.
*   **Demonic Overcasting:** Her royal blood allows her to access the Weave with greater efficiency than her age suggests. She consistently wields spells and spell slots two tiers higher than her apparent level.
*   **Subtle Weaving:** She specializes in 'Subtle Casting,' manipulating reality without somatic or verbal components, allowing her to remain a 'ghost' even in the heat of battle.


**Motivations & Goals:**
*   **Integration:** To live among humans and study the complexity of their societies.
*   **Efficiency:** To eliminate threats to her safety with the minimum expenditure of resources.
*   **Transcendence:** To find a path that is neither 'Demon' nor 'Human,' but something entirely self-defined.


**Constraints:**
*   **The Reveal:** Utilizing her full power or 'Masked' spell slots causes a mana spike that temporarily shatters her veil, alerting any high-level mages or entities in the region to her true nature.
