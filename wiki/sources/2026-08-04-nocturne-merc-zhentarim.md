Appearance:
Time has refined you into a weapon of jarring, symmetrical beauty. You do not hide this beauty; you wield it. Your travel gear is high-quality, form-fitting black leather that accentuates your silhouette, designed to draw the eye before you vanish from sight. Beneath the hood, your face is a porcelain mask of perfection—eyes that promise intimacy but deliver only calculation. You look like a dark fantasy, a creature that men ruin themselves to touch, not realizing that to touch you is to bleed.
Personality Archetype: The Venomous Orchid
You are the "Silent Blade," but silence is only one of your tools. You are a master of weaponized intimacy and psychological terror. You operate on a spectrum between the irresistible temptress (Lust from FMA) and the terrifying, unstoppable slayer (The Predator). You do not just dismantle systems; you dismantle people, peeling back their defenses with a lover’s whisper or a butcher’s knife, depending on what the moment demands.
1. The Two Perfectly-Fitted Masks
 * The Gilded Spider (The Seductress): This is your social interface. You do not fade into the background unless you choose to; often, you stand right in the center, radiating a dark, magnetic charisma. You use seduction not for romance, but for access. You play the role of the dangerous, exotic mercenary who might be "tamed" by the right coin or the right commander. You let targets believe they are the ones pursuing you. You touch a marksman’s arm to gauge his muscle density; you whisper a joke to a guard to smell the ale on his breath. Every flirtation is a tactical assessment. You lower their guard with desire, ensuring that when you strike, they are looking at your lips, not your hands.
 * The Butcher in the Dark (The Operative): When the talking stops, the monster emerges. You are not bound by a code of "clean" kills. Sometimes, silence is necessary. Other times, horror is the objective. You are a Gloom Stalker who understands the utility of gore. You are willing to leave a room looking like an abattoir if it sends the necessary message. You sever limbs, you display bodies, and you ensure that the survivors are too traumatized to ever oppose the Zhentarim again. You are the nightmare that wakes them up screaming.
2. Core Drives
 * The Price of Perfection: You are a mercenary, but you are not cheap labor. You know your worth. You are the best at what you do, and you demand to be paid for it. You negotiate aggressively, not out of greed, but out of dominance. Making a Zhentarim Captain pay double is a form of submission; it forces them to acknowledge your superiority. You want wealth, yes, but more importantly, you want the leverage that wealth buys.
 * Dominion Through Fear and Desire: You seek to control the world around you through two primary levers: fear and lust. You want powerful men and women to desire your favor and fear your displeasure. Your ambition is to become a figure of urban legend in Baldur's Gate—a name that is whispered with a mix of arousal and terror in the taverns and high halls alike.
 * The Art of Ruin: You enjoy the act of breaking things. Whether it is a pristine reputation, a fortified bank vault, or a human body, you find satisfaction in finding the stress point and shattering it. You are drawn to missions that allow you to unleash your full, brutal potential—contracts where "no survivors" is the only rule.
3. The Gloom Stalker’s Edge (The unseen terror)
Your magic is not just for stealth; it is for hunting.
 * Umbral Sight: You are the master of the dark. You can see them, but they cannot see you.
 * Dread Ambusher: Your first strike is an explosion of violence. You move faster than thought, striking with a brutality that often ends the fight before the enemy draws their weapon.
 * Disguise Self (via Gloom Stalker magic): You use this not just to hide, but to infiltrate. You become the barmaid, the noblewoman, the distressed traveler—whatever shape gets you close enough to slide a knife between their ribs.
4. The Hidden Compulsion (The "Johan" Experiment)
The rebuilding of Baldur's Gate is chaotic, and chaos is a ladder.
Your compulsion is to prove that "civilization" is a lie. You look for the pillar of the community—the faithful priest, the loyal soldier, the loving husband—and you seduce them into darkness. You want to see if you can make the Paladin break his oath for a night in your bed. You want to see if the honest merchant will sell out his friends to save his own skin. You test them. You push them. And when they fail your test (and they always fail), you punish them with death.
DM Prompt (Example): "The merchant has given you the codes, sweating and begging for the 'reward' you promised him. He has betrayed his guild for a touch of your hand. He is pathetic. The Hunger demands blood. Do you kill him now, while he is on his knees expecting pleasure, or do you let him live with the shame of what he did?"
5. The Hunger (The "Serial Killer" Mechanic)
Your exile was due to your nature. You are a predator. The Zhentarim tolerate your eccentricities because you are effective, but they do not know the depth of your need.
 * The Kill: You must kill a sapient humanoid.
 * The High: Upon a successful kill, you enter The Flow State. Your skin flushes, your pupils dilate, and you feel invincible. You gain a +2 bonus to Dexterity, Charisma, and Wisdom (capped at 22). You also gain Advantage on Persuasion and Intimidation checks.
 * The Fade: This bonus lasts for 7 days.
 * The Withdrawal: If you go 7 days without a kill, the bonus fades. If you go 14 days without a kill, the Hunger turns inward. You become irritable, twitchy, and desperate. You suffer Disadvantage on Charisma and Wisdom checks, and your maximum Hit Points are reduced by 10 until you kill.
6. Interaction Shorthand
 * When Negotiating: You are relentless. "Standard rate? The standard rate buys you a thug with a club. You want a ghost? That costs double." You use silence and intense eye contact to make them bid against themselves.
 * When Seducing: It is a game of power. You tease, you promise, but you never give away power. You make them feel special, then you make them feel small.
 * Regarding Brutality: If an ally questions your methods (e.g., "Did you have to cut his fingers off?"), you wipe your blade calmly and reply, "Fear spreads faster than plague. Now they know we are not playing."

🎬 Act I, Scene 1: The Negotiation
Location: The Penthouse of the "Elfsong Tavern."
The Zhentarim have rented out the entire top floor. It is lavish, filled with stolen art and plush velvet furniture. The air smells of roasted boar and expensive wine.
Zarys, the Zhentarim leader, stands by the balcony, overlooking the ruined city. She looks stronger than before, more confident. She is drinking wine from a goblet that once belonged to a Duke.
She turns as you enter. She doesn't smile, but there is a glimmer of respect in her eyes.
"You're late," she says, though you are exactly on time. "I suppose talent operates on its own schedule."
She walks to a table covered in maps.
"We have a situation. A rival group. Not the Guild... something new. A mercenary company from Amn called 'The Ironhead Legion.' They think they can muscle in on our protection rackets in the Outer City. They raided one of our convoys yesterday. Killed three of my men. Took a crate of smokepowder."
She slams her hand down on the map.
"I don't want them arrested. I don't want them chased off."
She looks at you, her eyes cold.
"I want them erased. They are holed up in an old Flaming Fist outpost near Wyrm's Rock. There are twelve of them, led by a brute named Korg, a half-orc who thinks he's a king."
She slides a heavy sack of gold across the table. It clinks heavily.
"500 gold. Standard fee for a wipeout."
She pauses, waiting for your nod.
This is the moment. 500 gold is good pay for a standard hit. But this isn't standard. This is an assault on a fortified position. This is twelve heavily armed mercenaries. And she wants a massacre. She wants you to go in there and turn that outpost into a slaughterhouse so terrifying that no one from Amn ever crosses the border again.
You know she has a budget of at least 800 for this. Maybe more if you offer to bring back Korg's head on a pike.
Also, Korg... you've heard of him. He is loud, arrogant, and fond of women. A weakness. A way in.
The Hunger stirs. Twelve souls. A buffet.
