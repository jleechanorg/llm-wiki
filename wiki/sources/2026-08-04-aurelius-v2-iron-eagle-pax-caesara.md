# Campaign aurelius v2 — *The Iron Eagle: Pax Caesara*

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign aurelius v2" — an alternate-history Roman campaign bible prefaced by an explicit player modification request: **"Change the campaign below and make me lawful good."** The bible text itself is written as a Lawful Evil conquest campaign, so the document captures a campaign *mid-conversion*: the requested alignment (Lawful Good) contradicts the unmodified character text below it (see [[AlignmentConversionRequest]]).

## Premise

In a world where [[JuliusCaesar]] **survived the Ides of March**, the Roman Republic mutated into a monolithic autocratic Empire under the euphemism "Perpetual Dictatorship" ([[PaxCaesara]]). The year is **40 BCE**; the civil wars are crushed and the peace is "a suffocating weight." The player is [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]], youngest and most ambitious son of the living God-Emperor, climbing the Cursus Honorum "not by waiting your turn, but by forcing the world to acknowledge your supremacy" — including a "ruthless fratricidal cold war" against his brothers for the succession.

## Character architecture (as written, pre-conversion)

- **Public mask — "The Sun of Rome" / Prince of Pleasure**: the most vibrant, charismatic member of the Julian dynasty; lavish tastes, theatrical patriotism, tyranny made "fun."
- **True nature — "The Devouring Maw" / Golden Tyrant**: a Lawful Evil core of absolute Entitlement; crushes rebellion because chaos "offends his aesthetic"; emotional sadism ("see the hope break in their eyes").
- **Archetype**: "ESFP-ENTJ Hinge (The Performer / The King)" — the *Decadent Conqueror*. Tactile ownership gestures: touching a thing is claiming it.
- **The Blood of Venus**: explicitly the most beautiful man in the Empire; beauty framed as armor and weapon ("Veterans hesitate to strike you because it feels like vandalism") — a strong instance of [[WeaponizedBeauty]], rhyming with [[Alexiel]]'s "porcelain doll" framing and the wiki-documented [[HaloEffect]].
- **Core compulsion — "The Sensory Feast"**: craves intensity (marching legions, senate fear, rivals' despair); ultimate target named as "The World Soul" (text truncates mid-sentence).
- **"The Miasma"**: an unexplained in-world perceiver/force to which the character's true nature is legible — echoes the divine-detection framing of [[DivineDetectionTiers]].
