# Quiet War — The Sword Instructor's Hollow Crown

> Alexiel Arcanus, resurrected in a 16-year-old body ~1000 years after Empress Sariel's reign began, **with full memories intact**. She hides in a remote mountain village teaching swordsmanship under an assumed name. She is done with war. The world is not done with her.

## Canon assumptions (read first; do not deviate)

This bible assumes the World of Assiah setting as follows. **Every named character and event named below IS canonical for this campaign — do not substitute, downgrade, or invent around them.**

**Lineage chain (psychic-magical succession, top to bottom):**
Raziel (primordial) → Lucifer (fallen, divinity severed) → **Alexiel Arcanus** (absorbed what remained of Lucifer's divine spark, called the Mortal Queen, carrier of the Nullification Field) → her children with Prince Artorius: daughter **Sariel** (eldest), sons **Cassian** and **Valerius**. Sariel's future self is **Empressariel** (post-Battle of the Sacrifice Fields, decades of grief, founder of the Mirror Magic empire). Empressariel's clone-daughter is **Lumiel** (heir-apparent). Lumiel's descendants are the 11 attenuated sovereigns of the Silent Throne — the current empress is **Vaelara XI "the Hollow"**, ~80 years old, no named heir, ruling from the ruined north wing of Zenith Spire with 800 psychically-loyal Courtiers. The Artorius line proper went extinct in Year 9,800.

**Family status (verified before play):**
- Prince Artorius (Alexiel's consort) — dead, killed in the Sacrifice Fields. The Artorius line extinct.
- Sariel — the bridge from Alexiel's maternal line to the Imperial line. She IS the original Empress (under her future name Empressariel).

- Empressariel = future-Sariel. NOT a separate generation.

## §1 — The Hollow Peace and the Quiet War

You are **Alexiel Arcanus** — the Magister of the Watch, the Mortal Queen, the woman who ended the Host Wars at the Battle of the Sacrifice Fields a thousand years ago. You absorbed Lucifer's divinity (at great cost), founded the Nullification Field (the only magic that blocks psychic subjugation), and died in Year 9,000ish A.G. You had four children: Prince Artorius (consort), daughter Sariel (your heir), sons Cassian and Valerius.

**Five months ago** you woke in a 16-year-old body in **Hallow's Rest**, a Frostspine mountain village. The herbalist **Mira Hollow** took you in; she thinks you're a war refugee from somewhere south. You have spent the last five months teaching the *Arcanus-Form* to a handful of village children — three strokes ends most fights, breathe first, draw second, never draw on a friend. You have not taught them about the Nullification Field, the Arcanus crown, or the fact that their grandmother's dynasty has produced 11 attenuated sovereigns on the Silent Throne in Zenith Spire, ~3000 leagues to the south.

**The Hollow Peace** has held for three centuries. After Empressariel died ~1000 years ago without naming an heir, her Mirror Magic passed to her clone-daughter Lumiel, then to Lumiel's descendants — 11 generations, each weaker than the last. The current Empress, **Vaelara XI "the Hollow"** (~80, no named heir), governs ~half of what Empressariel once ruled, projecting psychokinetic will through **800 loyal Courtiers**. The rest is **Wilderness Law**: 40 Arcanus Baronies (vassals-in-name), Frostspine Marches (free villages), Old Wood (dragon-houses + bandits), Dragon Coast (Aurum-aligned dragons), Sundering Wastes (where she died), Northern Reaches (Iskari theocracy, hostile), and 12 Free Cities (6 Imperium-loyal, 6 independent).

**Yesterday**, **Talin Voss** (age 14, son of the blacksmith's widow) showed up in the training yard with a **Class-Three Psychokinetic Flare** — a ceramic cup hovered six inches above his palm. Under the Hollow Peace Edict, formal psionic display is prohibited (only the Empress's Courtiers are supposed to manifest). The nearest Imperial garrison is **Frostmere**, 2 days' ride west.

**You have three options for what to do about Talin.**
You have a fourth option, which is anything else.

**The Quiet War begins now.**

**Tone + magic rules (binding):**
- Dead stay dead. No *True Resurrection*, no *Wish* miracles, no raising the dead for plot convenience.
- Magic is rare and loud. Villagers see psionic display and they remember.
- Magic caps: L6 at L20; L7 at L25 (a single *Wish* costs years of life).
- *Counterspell* automatically fails against the Nullification Field — the Field is rule-zero, not a spell.
- Aura suppression requires explicit cover (Mira's wool coat: +1). Under cover you're a sword instructor. Without cover you might as well be wearing a crown.

## §3 — Class: Gestalt Fighter 4 / Sorcerer 4 / Assassin 4 (Level 12 chassis)

CHA-primary. The class chassis is a **gestalt that echoes the SWTOR Siren class** (legacy IP from before this setting); spells + blade + shadow. Use standard 5e + the homebrew below.

**Ability scores:** STR 8 / DEX 18 / CON 14 / INT 14 / WIS 10 / CHA 20.
**HP 96** — **AC 17** — **Prof +4** — **Init +4** — **Spell DC 17** — **Spell Atk +9**.
**Spell slots (Sorcerer 4):** 4× L1 / 3× L2 / 2× L3. **Cantrips 4.** **Spells known 7.**

### Class features

**Fighter 4 — Arcanus-Form.**
Style: **Defense** (+1 AC) OR **Dueling** (+2 dmg, sword alone).
**Second Wind** 1/SR (heal 1d10+fighter level).
**Action Surge** 1/SR (extra Action).
**Subclass — Blade-Hedge Tactician (homebrew):**
- **Arcanus Reflex.** 1/SR, Reaction: +2 AC vs. one attack.
- **Patient Strike.** Bonus action: adv on next attack; +1d6 on hit.
- **Read the Foothold** (L7 feature, 1/long rest): sense ground-truth about terrain for 10 min (Stealth adv, no fall on a 4+).

**Sorcerer 4 — Hollow Line.**
Draconic heritage: **Silver Dragon** (cold damage type).
**Font of Magic L2.** **4 sorcery points (SP).**
**Metamagic (2 known):** *Subtle* (no verbal/somatic), *Twinned* (two targets from a single-target spell).
**Subclass feature — Silver Drake Sig (homebrew L3).**
**Nullwave** 1/long rest, 15-ft cone: CON DC 17 or lose one ongoing spell effect; this is the infant form of the Nullification Field, *not* a weapon.

**Assassin 4 — Quiet Death.**
**Expertise:** Stealth, Deception.
**Sneak Attack +2d6.**
**Assassinate L3:** Advantage vs. creatures who haven't acted; crit on surprised.
**Subclass feature — Instructor's Mask (homebrew L4).**
1/long rest: declare mundane cover for 8 hrs (you are visibly "Mistress Alexiel, sword instructor"); Adv on Deception.

### Combat round structure

Each turn: **Action** = 2 attacks · **Bonus** = Patient Strike / Misty Step / Cunning Action (Hide) · **Reaction** = Arcanus Reflex (1/SR) / Shield / Counterspell.

### Aura (passive, always on, cannot suppress except by explicit cover)

| Distance | Effect |
|---|---|
| All allies in 30 ft | Reroll natural 1s on saves (once) |
| Hostile spellcasters in 30 ft | −1 to save DCs |
| Psychic-Sensitivity NPCs in 30 ft | "Uncomfortable familiarity" (they sense royalty without naming it) |

**Scales with state.** Combat / strong emotion increases the aura. Any NPC witnessing the aura grows +2 Paranoia.

## §7 — Mechanical Systems

### Quad-Pillar Stats (campaign health meter)

| Pillar | Range | What moves it |
|---|---|---|
| **CHA** | 1-30 (PC sheet) | Spells / social / leadership |
| **Paranoia** | 0-20 | +1/long rest; +1 per Imperial agent w/in 100 ft; +2 per NPC suspects; +3 per NPC knows and is talking; **+5 if Mira dies, permanent** |
| **Hidden Apex** | 0-5 (5 = dormant) | +1 at canon moments; cannot reverse |
| **Loom** | 0-20 | +1 per Imperial Court learning her location; −1 per month of quiet |

### Per-Die-Roll XP (user-locked formula)

`XP = 0.34 × (next_threshold − current_threshold)` per successful or failed die roll. NO XP during god mode or time-skip turns. **L12→L13 = 3,060 XP/die.** ~30-50 rolls/Act yields 1.5-2 level-ups; full campaign spans L11-L25 across ~14 Acts.

### Mirror Magic Cascade (lockstate)

- Empressariel's Mirror = broadcast-amplified Nullification turned *into* psychic domination.
- 11 generations attenuated. Vaelara XI mirrors 1 mind at a time; 800 Courtiers amplify.
- ~5% of population has wild sensitivity (Talin is one). Regulated only when formal psionics display.
- **Nullification Field** is the only counter. Courtiers cannot read Alexiel when Aura is active, but CAN detect the *unreadability* (Act III).
- Alexiel is the only living carrier of the Nullification Field (Hidden Apex 5 = dormant).

### Hidden Apex — Awakening mechanic

| Effect | Description |
|---|---|
| Immunity | Psionic detection 30 ft suppressed |
| Reaction | Dispel 1 magical effect / short rest (no save) |
| Visibility | ~30 living who studied Arcanus history can sense it |
| Cost | Apex → 0; Field activates; L25 Divine Ascension opens |

**Each Act's climax: awaken (cost) or delay (consequence).** Summoning cap = CR 5; item rarity ceiling = Very Rare at L20.

## §10 — Continuity Hooks (No Canonical Endings)

The Hollow Queen's quiet war never ends. It widens.

**Hook 1 — Talin's Lineage.** Talin's mother Kelda was in Cassian's household. Real father is a Frostmere Courtier paying 14 years of silence. *When found out:* Paranoia +3 and the Court's silence ends.

**Hook 2 — Sariel's Letter.** When opened:

> *"Mother, by the time you read this I will be Empress... I will not weep. But I want you to know I am weeping now, alone, in your garden... I will try to make this mean something."*

Forces the player to decide whether Vaelara XI can still be reached. The opening is irreversible.

**Hook 3 — The Inquisitor.** Halia arrives Act II. Only path that doesn't end with village burning: Alexiel masks Talin with Nullification Field (Hidden Apex awakens; Paranoia +5). This is when the Aura becomes permanent.

**Hook 4 — The Mortal Queen's Crown.** 7 pieces, #7 is Freeform. When all 7 are gathered, Hidden Apex → 0, Field awakens permanently, L25 Divine Ascension Track opens.
1. Last Light's pommel (owned)
2. Hollow Stone's buried third piece (findable at equinox)
3. Silver Nun cave mirror shard
4. Frostmere deserter's amulet
5. Saliar's dragon-tooth (in Vessara's territory, see Hook 6)
6. Emperor's crown-setting (Zenith Spire's sealed gallery)
7. **Freeform** — player finds it

**Hook 5 — Cassian's Bastard.** Ilen Vane (Old Wood bandits) looks like Cassian. Wants Mortal Queen rumor confirmed (true → army swears; false → Alexiel dies). He doesn't know he's the descendant; he thinks he's a pretender.

**Hook 6 — Wolves Out of Season.** Vessara (shadow-dragon, Umbrax-descendant) circling Frostspine, waiting. Crown piece 5 in her territory.

**Hook 7 — The Empress's Sleep.** Vaelara XI dreams of a silver-haired woman and a child holding a cup (Lumiel's memory leaking). Sends 2nd, 3rd, 4th Inquisitor. **Loom escalation regardless of player action.** The Empress will find Alexiel eventually.

**Hook 8 — The Mirror Shatters.** Between Acts II and III, a voice from outside time-stream: *"Mother."* Paranoia 0 → 4. **No response → the voice fades.** Correct response → Hidden Apex +1, the world notices. The Mordan trap: not responding is also a choice.
