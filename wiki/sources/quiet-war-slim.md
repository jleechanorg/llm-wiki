# Quiet War — The Sword Instructor's Hollow Crown

> Alexiel Arcanus, resurrected in a 16-year-old body ~1000 years after Empress Sariel's reign began, **with full memories intact**. She hides in a remote mountain village teaching swordsmanship under an assumed name. She is done with war. The world is not done with her.

**Open-Ended System (hard rule):** No canonical ending. No Final Verdict. No Ending Determinator matrix. §10's Continuity Hooks replace any pre-decided outcome. The player's choices are the campaign.

**CANON-PRIORITY:** *Assiah Wiki > Assiah v1 bible > Assiah chat exports > original SWTOR Siren bible.* Do not invent canon — every named character (Sariel, Lumiel, Raziel, Lucifer, Cassian, Valerius, Artorius, Aurum, Umbrax) has canonical entries; cross-check before narrating.

**CANON-CORRECTION (locked):** *Sariel Arcanus is the DAUGHTER of Alexiel Arcanus. Empressariel is the future version of Sariel after the Battle of the Sacrifice Fields and decades of grief. Psychic power descends: Raziel → Lucifer → Alexiel (absorbed Lucifer's divinity, Mortal Queen, Nullification Field) → Sariel → Empressariel (Mirror Magic) → Lumiel (clone-heir) → Vaelara XI "the Hollow" (current; 800 Courtiers).* **Alexiel is the Empress's mother; never narrate Sariel as the Empress's daughter.**

## §1 — The Hollow Peace and the Quiet War

You are Alexiel Arcanus — the Magister of the Watch, the Mortal Queen, the woman who ended the Host Wars at the Battle of the Sacrifice Fields. Full memories: Prince Artorius, daughter Sariel as a child, sons Cassian and Valerius, absorbing Lucifer's divinity, dying.

Five months ago you woke in a 16-year-old body in **Hallow's Rest**, a Frostspine mountain village. Herbalist **Mira Hollow** took you in; she believes you are a war refugee. You have taught the Arcanus-Form to village children — breathe first, draw second, three strokes ends most fights, never draw on a friend. You do not teach them about the Nullification Field, the Arcanus crown, or that their grandmother's dynasty has produced 11 attenuated sovereigns on the Silent Throne in Zenith Spire, ~3000 leagues away.

The **Hollow Peace** has been law for three centuries. After Empress Sariel died without naming an heir, her Mirror Magic passed to clone-daughter **Lumiel**, then to Lumiel's descendants — 11 generations, each weaker than the last. The current Empress, **Vaelara XI "the Hollow"**, governs ~half of what Sariel once ruled, projecting psychokinetic will through 800 loyal Courtiers. The rest is Wilderness Law: baronies, theocracies, dragon-houses, free cities, bandit-empires.

Yesterday, **Talin** (14) showed up with a **Class-Three Psychokinetic Flare** — illegal under the Hollow Peace Edict (Frostmere garrison: 2 days' ride). Report him → Inquisitors take him. Don't report and get caught → village burns. You are done with war. **The Quiet War begins now.**

**Tone:** Dead stay dead; magic rare+loud. **Magic caps:** L6 at L20, L7 at L25 (*Wish* costs life); no *True Res*, no *Meteor Swarm*; *Counterspell* auto-fails vs. Nullification Field.

## §7 — Mechanical Systems

**Quad-Pillar Stats** (campaign health meter): **CHA** 1-30 (sheet). **Paranoia** 0-20 (+1/long rest; +1 per Imperial agent w/in 100ft; +2 per NPC *suspects*; +3 per NPC *knows and is talking*; +5 if Mira dies, permanent). **Hidden Apex** 0-5 (5 = dormant; +1 at canon moments; cannot reverse). **Loom** 0-20 (+1 per Imperial Court learning; −1 per month of quiet).

**Per-Die-Roll XP** (user-locked): *XP = 0.34 × (next_threshold − current_threshold)* per successful or failed die roll. NO XP in god mode; bonus damage doesn't count. L12→L13 = 3,060 XP/die. ~30-50 rolls/Act; spans L12→L20 in ~14 Acts.

**Mirror Magic Cascade.** Sariel's Mirror = broadcast-amplified Nullification → psychic domination; 11 gens attenuated (Vaelara XI mirrors 1 mind; 800 Courtiers forward it). ~5% population has wild sensitivity flares — regulated only if *formal* psionics shown. **Nullification Field is the only counter; Courtiers can't read Alexiel's mind when Aura active, but CAN detect the unreadability** (Act III's central mechanic).

**Hidden Apex — Awakening.** Immune to psionic detection 30ft; dispel 1 magical effect/short rest as Reaction (no save); visible to ~30 living who studied Arcanus history. Feels like *recognition*, not power. Each Act's climax: awaken (cost) or delay (consequence). Summoning cap CR 5; item rarity ceiling Very Rare at L20.

## §9 — Starting Scene: The Hollow Stone, the Class-Three Boy, and the Three Options

Spring equinox light fades over the Hollow Stone. Mira is grinding lavender; Varn is hammering a hinge. **Talin Voss** stands in the middle of the training yard, eyes wide, both hands outstretched — a ceramic cup hovers six inches above his palm. His face is *terrified*.

"Mistress Alexiel — I didn't mean to — I just wanted to —"

The cup wobbles. The children back away. Mira looks up.

**A. Imperial Path — Report Him.** *"Talin. Take the cup down. We go to Frostmere tomorrow."* Council votes 3-2 that night.

**B. Maternal Path — Train Him Quietly.** *"Talin. Take the cup down. Tomorrow, before dawn, come to my cabin."* A month of quiet training, then the village asks questions.

**C. Wilderness Path — Send Him to the Old Wood.** *"Talin. Take the cup down. Pack a bag. Tomorrow we ride to the Old Wood."* Saliar the Younger is waiting at the tree line.

**D. [Freeform — anything else.]** AI DM adjudicates via Paranoia/Loom/Hidden Apex. *That is the point.*
