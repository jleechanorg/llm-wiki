# Campaign Bible: The Deceiver's Crown (Nocturne Warcraft 3, v1.0.0)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Campaign nocturne Warcraft 3" — a solo Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos / The Frozen Throne × D&D 5e hybrid campaign bible, v1.0.0. This is a new campaign line for the recurring "Nocturne" protagonist persona (see [[NocturneSosuke]] for the parallel Baldur's Gate 3 lines), here re-instantiated as [[NocturneRavencrest]] in an alternate-timeline Third War.

## Premise

An alternate-timeline Third War where the [[Scourge]] is **fully sentient** — every ghoul, abomination, crypt fiend, necromancer, lich, and dreadlord has will, hierarchy, ambition, politics, and private grievances ([[SentientScourge]]). The [[CultOfTheDamned]] is a mercenary priesthood trading favors for power, not a death cult of fanatics. Necropolises are cities. The [[LichKing]] is not a god but a tyrant propped up by a coalition of dreadlords, liches, and self-willed banshees who hate each other more than they hate the Alliance.

[[NocturneRavencrest]], sixteen, daughter of Count [[MarcusRavencrest]] of Stormwind, is Captain-Commander of the [[FourthStormwindExpeditionaryDetachment]]. By daylight she is a paladin of the [[SilverHand]] — proper, pious, beloved by soldiers, courted by [[HousePrestor]], admired by the [[KirinTor]]. By midnight she is the **Bound Blade of [[MalGanis]]**, sworn to a Nathrezim who out-thought Frostmourne.

## The Dual-Custody Pact

In this timeline [[MalGanis]] did not die at [[ArthasMenethil]]'s hand in Northrend. He anticipated the Lich King's betrayal, left a sentient shadow-avatar in Lordaeron, and bound himself to Nocturne — **as partner-in-finance, not master**. The [[DualCustodyPact]] has a single clause: if either party tries to dominate the other, both die. Custody is shared; sovereignty is the prize.

## Goal: Absolute Sovereignty — the Pale Crown

Nocturne refuses to be a pawn of mortal kings (Arthas, Terenas, the House of Nobles) or demonic lords (the Dreadlord Triumvirate, Kil'jaeden, the Lich King). Her endgame is the [[PaleCrown]]: a living-undead nation that is neither human nor Scourge, held together by dual authority of public virtue and shadow sanction, with her on its only throne.

## Dual-Stack Gameplay

The campaign's core loop is [[DualStackGameplay]]:

1. **Public Stack** — paragon play: lead the 4th Stormwind Expeditionary Detachment, broker with [[HousePrestor]] (Katrana Prestor — Onyxia in disguise), out-argue the Kirin Tor, win refugee loyalty, hold the Captain-Commander title in daylight.
2. **Shadow Stack** — run the sentient Scourge's intelligence service: dispatch [[SylvanThePale]] to broker with the [[CultOfTheDamned]], feed [[MalGanis]] intel on Arthas's pursuit, smuggle Pale Undead through Andorhal, negotiate with the dreadlord triumvirate, build a parallel state inside the Plague Lands.

## The Sovereign Decoupling Principle (design pillar)

The two stacks are mechanically decoupled by the [[SovereignDecouplingPrinciple]] — the user's most important design pillar. Personal martial prowess, divine/necrotic smites, AC, and spellcasting are **intrinsic** and completely independent of public opinion. Exposure of her treason destroys institutional power (rank, lands, titles, army commands, political protection) but leaves personal combat capability 100% intact. The document explicitly frames this as the answer to campaign-creation pitfall P24 (no global aggregate suspicion trackers): reputation is per-NPC witness persistence, combat stats are intrinsic, and the two never share a meter.

## Starting Assets

- Paladin smite + dreadlord shadow pact (paladin-sorcerer dual kit; the doc references level-30-scale endgame power)
- Noble rank and a regiment (the 4th Detachment)
- [[SylvanThePale]] — sentient undead retinue member with centuries of Lordaeron scholarship
- An unwitting high-elf priestess companion
- A veteran knight who believes Nocturne is the reincarnation of ancient chivalry

## Relationship to Other Campaign Lines

Same player archetype as the [[NocturneSosuke]] BG3 lines (v3 [[CollegeOfPerdition]] origin, v4/v5 Velvet Cage): a sixteen-to-eighteen-year-old protagonist who starts already inside a power structure, plays public virtue against shadow alliance, and pursues moral-corruption/sovereignty arcs — consistent with the documented [[user_campaign_preferences|serpent-mastermind preference profile]]. Key difference: this campaign starts **at the top** ("the power fantasy begins at the top and stays at the top") rather than from ruin or de-power.
