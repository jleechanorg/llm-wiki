# Ains — Sanctuary of Flesh and Shadow campaign module v2 ("shy lich overlord")

**Source**: Campaign module draft "shy lich overlord v2" — an Overlord × D&D 5e/Pathfinder hybrid isekai. **Provenance caveat**: the raw source document was not preserved (the source file on disk contained only its own path); this page is reconstructed from the curated index entry and the prior ingest's page summaries, and should be re-ingested from the original document if it resurfaces.

## Premise

The player character is **[[Ains-Ooal-Gown|Ains]]**, a 16-year-old *living* lich (Level 20 Bladesinger/Monk gestalt) stranded as sole administrator of **[[Nazarick]]**, the Great Tomb fortress, after the other 41 Supreme Beings "logged off" — the module's Hidden Truth. Unlike canon Overlord's confident skeleton, this Ains is shy: the campaign's tension comes from a socially fragile teenager commanding godlike power he must conceal.

## Core Mechanics (three interlocking dials)

1. **[[Sanctuary-Imperative|Sanctuary Imperative]]** — a Psychic Strain / Divine Serenity dual-track meter that prices moral weight: non-lethal problem-solving heals, unnecessary slaughter accrues strain. The module's conscience mechanic.
2. **[[Mask-of-Fragility|Mask of Fragility]]** — a dynamic deception-DC system: base DC scales with the PC's spell save DC, and visible power use imposes penalties, so staying "weak-looking" is a skill challenge, not flavor. Sibling of [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] and the weakness-weaponizing line noted in [[BeautyAsWeapon]].
3. **[[Whispers-of-the-Realm|Whispers of the Realm]]** — narrative aggro in four escalating states (Quiet Ignorance → Restless Border Whispers → The Gathering Storm → The Eclipse). The realm's attention rises as power leaks; kin to [[AggroMeter]] and [[AscensionMeter]] in the wiki's family of visibility-tension dials.

## Structural Constraints

- **Lone Sovereign Constraint**: floor guardians are capped at L12-16, well below Ains' L20 — he can never delegate his top-end power, so every existential threat is personally his.
- **Universal Sentience**: every NPC (including monsters and servants) is fully sentient, which feeds moral weight directly into the [[Sanctuary-Imperative|Sanctuary Imperative]].
- **Economic crisis**: Nazarick's treasury is failing; Session 1 opens with a **Treasury Audit** scene, forcing engagement with logistics rather than combat.

## Relation to the campaign corpus

This module belongs to the masked-power campaign line ([[MaskedPowerSmurfing]], the Divine Deception protocol, the [[SosukeAizen|Aizen]]-flavored campaigns): overwhelming true power + a maintained public mask + a meter that prices exposure. Its distinctive contribution is the *shyness* framing — the mask is not just strategy but temperament — and the dual-track morality meter.
