# Character Biography: Nocticula (Hidden Power)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign Nocticula general (hidden power)" — a character biography for **[[Nocticula]]**, a 16-year-old half-demon (human/abyssal hybrid) sorcerous prodigy / Shadow Weaver, secretly the granddaughter of a reigning **[[AbyssalDemonKing|Demon King]]**. Title: *Scion of the Abyssal Throne (Hidden)*. Personality: INTJ / True Neutral, explicitly framed as **[[OptimalOutcomeReasoning|Clinical Pragmatism]]**.

## Lineage & origin

- Born into a demonic royal line of "catastrophic power"; her human heritage introduced logic and "a sliver of empathy" as variables.
- She judged the demonic hierarchy a *flawed system of diminishing returns* and fled the infernal capital.
- Lives as a fugitive hiding both royal pedigree and immense mana, hunted from two directions: demonic **Retrieval Squads** and human **Purge Slayers**.

## Psychological profile

Low-empathy, high-functioning cognitive frame: social interactions are logistical exchanges, emotions are "biological data points." She acts from neither malice nor altruism but pursues **Optimal Outcomes** — saving an ally is "tactical preservation of a primary asset." Described as unsettlingly calm, detached, blunt. This is a direct continuation of the user's serpent-mastermind INTJ protagonist pattern (see the campaign-preferences profile), here in a True Neutral rather than Lawful Evil register.

## Signature mechanics (the explicit 'Frieren' Scale)

- **[[ManaVeilMastery]]** — compresses her mana into a near-total void; an ancient-archmage reservoir reads to all magical detection as a mundane peasant or Level 1 novice.
- **[[DemonicOvercasting]]** — royal blood grants Weave access beyond her apparent level; she consistently wields spells/slots **two tiers higher** than she appears.
- **[[SubtleWeaving]]** — component-less (no somatic/verbal) casting; she stays a "ghost" even mid-battle.

## Motivations & constraint

- **Integration** — live among humans, study their societies.
- **Efficiency** — eliminate threats with minimum resource expenditure.
- **Transcendence** — a self-defined path that is neither 'Demon' nor 'Human'.
- **[[TheRevealConstraint|The Reveal]]** — using full power or masked spell slots causes a mana spike that temporarily shatters the veil, alerting every high-level mage or entity in the region. This is the campaign's core tension dial: power is available but each use has a regional detection cost.
