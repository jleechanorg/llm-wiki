## Summary
A D&D 5e campaign set in Faerun's Iron Dominion — a ruthless corporate-mercenary state built on blood, gold, and absolute hierarchy. Protagonist Aizen Sosuke is a 16-year-old Bladesinger wizard, military prodigy, and youngest scion of House Sosuke. Campaign explores psychological dominance, political manipulation, and personal transcendence as the character rises through the Dominion's military academy while conducting shadow experiments on family and peers.

## Key Claims
- The Iron Dominion operates as an institutionalized military-industrial complex with a nine-tier ranking system from Slave to King
- Aizen is Level 6 Bladesinger with maxed INT (18), possessing Auctoritas (Sovereign Command aura) as a special ability
- The campaign enforces daily interactions with attracted female cadets as part of the character simulation rules
- Aizen has secretly catalogued psychological and magical vulnerabilities of all five siblings and both parents, with mental triggers planted for future activation
