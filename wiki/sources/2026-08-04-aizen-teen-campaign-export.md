# Aizen — *The Architect of Destiny* (teen campaign export)

**Source**: Campaign export document "Aizen - The Architect of Destiny". A fresh-campaign premise exploring the early life of [[SosukeAizen|Aizen]] before his ascent to God-King — "from a gifted prodigy within a powerful noble house to the calculating, supremely intelligent individual he is destined to become." This is the founding document of the [[PrequelOriginCampaign]] pattern for the Aizen continuity: the campaign deliberately rewinds the timeline to before every established endpoint.

## Character premise

Aizen begins as a **10-year-old human, Level 1**, a young scion of [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]]. The signature mechanic is the [[AgeProgressionByLevel]] rule: **each level gained ages him 1 year, up to Level 10 (age 20), after which age follows the campaign timeline normally.** Class is left as a player choice, with the document suggesting options that align with his future manipulative nature: Wizard (Abjuration/Enchantment/Illusion), Bard (Lore/Whispers), or Rogue (Thief/Mastermind).

Standard-array attributes: STR 10, DEX 14, CON 13, **INT 15** ("inherent genius"), WIS 12, CHA 14. Background: Sage preferred (over Noble or Acolyte) as best reflecting "intellectual hunger and access to forbidden knowledge." Equipment is deliberately mundane: quality noble clothing, books, writing implements, a personal journal, 10 gp.

## Personality architecture (nascent masks)

The profile ports the adult Aizen's psychology (from "azien personality.pdf") into an undeveloped form:

- **Core identity (nascent)**: views the world as "a complex system, ripe for understanding and, subtly, for imposition."
- **Developing masks**: (1) *The Inquisitive & Polite Young Noble* — public face that disarms and gathers information; (2) *The Obsessed Learner* — internal, consuming drive to comprehend everything with methodical precision. These are the childhood ancestors of the adult [[Mask-of-Fragility]] deception mechanic.
- **Nascent true self**: the "ultimate perfectionist, the cosmic editor, and the will that subtly bends others" — present but "raw, unrefined, and largely unconscious."
- **Fears**: being seen as unintelligent, failing to comprehend, "being confined by ignorance."

## Setting and factions

Set in [[BaldursGate]], Faerûn — childhood and adolescence primarily inside House Sosuke's Upper City operations, extending into Lower City academic, arcane, and clandestine circles. Background factions: [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]] (outwardly respectable, "morally ambiguous mercantile and esoteric interests"), the [[FlamingFist]] (backdrop of authority), [[TheGuild]] (underworld hinting at forbidden knowledge), and unnamed scholarly institutions/secret societies dealing in sanctioned and forbidden lore.

## Timeline reset

The export explicitly declares a [[CleanSlateCampaignRestart|fresh-campaign world state]]: the God-King Aizen has **not** ascended, Bane has **not** been consumed (see [[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]]), and the Cult of the Absolute is "a distant, unknown threat (or not yet active)." The campaign's stated purpose is to explore "the factors that shape a young Aizen."
