# Quiet War — The Sword Instructor's Hollow Crown

> A campaign bible for WorldArchitect.AI. The player is Alexiel Arcanus, resurrected in a 16-year-old body roughly a thousand years after Empress Sariel began her reign, with her full memories intact. She is hiding in a remote mountain village teaching swordsmanship under an assumed name. She is done with war. The world is not done with her.

**Open-Ended System Notice (front-matter, hard rule):** This bible ships as a *system*. There is no canonical ending. There is no Final Verdict. There is no Ending Determinator matrix. Replace any pre-decided-outcome structure with **Continuity Hooks** (Section 10). The player's choices are the campaign. Stop trying to script an ending.

**CANON-PRIORITY (hard rule, in-house continuity):** *Assiah Wiki > Assiah v1 bible > Assiah chat exports > original SWTOR Siren master bible (in-house continuity, not TV-adapted).* If a fact exists in a higher-priority source, that source supersedes lower-priority ones. **Do not invent Assiah canon** — every named character (Sariel, Lumiel, Raziel, Lucifer, Cassian, Valerius, Artorius, Aurum, Umbrax) has canonical entries already; cross-check before narrating.

**CANON-CORRECTION NOTE (locked):** *Sariel Arcanus is the daughter of Alexiel Arcanus. Empressariel is the future version of Sariel Arcanus, after the Battle of the Sacrifice Fields and decades of grief. The Silent Throne's psychic power descends through four canonical generations: Raziel (Empyrean Architect) → Lucifer (Empyrean revolutionary, created Alexiel) → Alexiel Arcanus (absorbed Lucifer's divinity, Mortal Queen, Nullification Field) → Sariel Arcanus → Empressariel (Mirror Magic, War of Whispers) → Lumiel (Sariel's clone-heir).* **Do not narrate Sariel as the Empress's daughter; narrate Alexiel as the Empress's mother.** This is the most common LLM fabrication — do not regenerate it.

## SECTION 1 — Campaign Intro: The Hollow Peace and the Quiet War

> *A hardcore political-realism engine for a single INTJ protagonist who does not want to be a protagonist.*

You are Alexiel Arcanus — the Magister of the Watch, the Mortal Queen, the woman who ended the Host Wars at the Battle of the Sacrifice Fields. You have your full memories. You remember Prince Artorius' face. You remember Sariel as a child. You remember absorbing Lucifer's divinity and learning how to project a Nullification Field that can unmake magical phenomena. You remember dying.

You woke up five months ago in the body of a sixteen-year-old girl at the foot of the Frostspine Mountains, in a village called Hallow's Rest, with every scar you earned at the Battle of the Sacrifice Fields already healed. The herbalist who found you — a woman named **Mira Hollow** — took you in, fed you, asked no questions. She believes you are a refugee from the Imperium's recent grain-tax revolt. You have not corrected her.

You have been teaching swordsmanship to the village children for four months. You teach them the Arcanus-Form — the same blade grammar you taught Cassian, your elder son, before the Sacrifice Fields. You teach them to breathe first, draw second. You teach them to respect the weight of the blade, because most fights are over in three strokes anyway. You teach them never to draw on a friend, even when threatened.

You do not teach them about the Nullification Field. You do not teach them about the Arcanus crown. You do not teach them that their grandmother founded the dynasty whose 11th heir currently sits on the Silent Throne in Zenith Spire, ~3000 leagues away, eating roast pheasant and signing death-warrants.

The Holly Peace has been the law of the land for three centuries. After Empress Sariel died without naming an heir, her Mirror Magic passed — imperfectly, noisily — to her clone-daughter **Lumiel**, then to Lumiel's descendants, and to Lumiel's descendants' descendants. Eleven generations of psychic sovereigns, each one weaker than the last. The oldest provinces have broken off into warring city-states. The Heartlands still bend the knee. The truth is that **the Imperium has been quietly fragmenting for two hundred years**, and the current Empress — **Vaelara XI**, called *the Hollow*, because she projects her psychokinetic will through eight hundred loyal Courtiers rather than her own weakening mind — governs less than half of what Empress Sariel once ruled. The rest is **Wilderness Law**: baronies, theocracies, dragon-houses, free cities, and bandit-empires.

You are **done with war**. You are done with succession. You are done with regents and heir-looms and the constant static of distant minds pressing against yours in a room you cannot leave.

But yesterday a boy named **Talin** showed up at your training yard with a Class-Three psychokinetic flare — the kind of flare that, under the old Imperium law, requires immediate reporting to the nearest Imperial garrison. The garrison at Frostmere is two days' ride. If you report him, the Imperial Inquisitors take him away and you never see him again. If you do not report him and they find out, the entire village burns.

You have three options. You also have a fourth — a slot for anything else you choose.

> **The Quiet War begins now.**

### Tone — *Hardcore Political Realism Engine (no plot armor)*

- **Consequences outlast intentions.** Every choice you make persists across sessions. Dead NPCs stay dead. Students you fail stay failed. Villages you burn do not un-burn.
- **No resurrection without dragon-egg pyre** (the only canonical revival mechanic — and the eggs are extinct). If Alexiel dies in this campaign, the campaign ends. There is no save-scumming.
- **Magic is rare, costly, and loud.** A 6th-level sorcerer in a backwater is a *target*, not a hero. The villagers will whisper about you. The garrison will eventually notice.
- **Psychic contention is everywhere.** Eleven generations of Mirror Magic have left low-level psychic bleed-through in the population — sudden flashes of unbidden memory, violent moodswings in crowded rooms, the constant low hum of distant minds. The Paranoia stat (Section 7) tracks Alexiel's resistance.
- **Memory is the currency.** Alexiel remembers 1000 years of politics, two hundred years of wilderness law, and the names of every House that mattered. NPCs who learn who she is will react — but most will not, because to them she is a name in a history book.

### World Lore Snapshot — *The Hollow Peace of Year ~10,000+ A.G.*

- **Capital:** Zenith Spire (ruined; half-collapsed after the Succession Wars of Year 9,800+; the Empress lives in its remaining north wing)
- **Heartland:** The Arcanus Reaches (broken into ~40 baronies, all technically sworn to the Silent Throne but self-governing)
- **Outer Territories:** The Frostspine Marches, the Old Wood, the Dragon Coast, the Sundering Wastes — all de facto independent of the Imperium
- **The current Empress:** Vaelara XI "the Hollow" (descended from Lumiel via 10 generations; Mirror Magic attenuated; governs through 800 loyal Courtiers who project her will in person)
- **Imperial currency:** Imperial Notes (backed by nothing since Year 9,800; mostly paper)
- **Imperial law:** Holly Peace Edict (Year 1 Sariel; still on the books) — *all psychic manifestation must be reported to the nearest garrison within 14 days; failure to report is Class-A sedition*
- **The Memory of Empress Sariel:** Carried primarily as a cautionary tale in schoolbooks ("The One Who Broke Herself"). Children are told she was a tyrant. Peasants are told she was a saint. Scholars know she was both.

## SECTION 2 — Character Personality: The Hollow Sword Mistress

> *MBTI INTJ. The "hollow" is not because she is empty; it is because she has chosen, deliberately, to be small.*

### MBTI Hinge: INTJ — *The Architect in Exile*

- **Ni (Introverted Intuition):** Alexiel sees patterns across centuries. She can predict baronial politics on sight, identify which baron is about to rebel, and notice the small tell that means a boy is hiding a Class-Three flare. She is, by nature, the woman who sees the chessboard.
- **Te (Extraverted Thinking):** When action is required, Alexiel executes efficiently. She does not hesitate. She does not compromise. She does not ask permission.
- **Fi (Introverted Feeling):** This is where she *lives.* Alexiel cares deeply, in private, about every child in her schoolyard. She will not admit it. She will not act on it loudly. But the students know. That is why they come to her.
- **Se (Extraverted Sensing):** Suppressed. She is in this body but not of it. The 16-year-old frame is unfamiliar; she keeps moving as though the body were her old one, and occasionally surprises herself with the speed of her own reflexes.

### Three Inner-Monologue Seeds (run one per long rest)

1. *The Mirror Voice.* "Sariel would have known what to do. Sariel always knew. I taught her that. And then she became Empress and I watched her forget everything I taught her." *(Guilt-shame spiral. Drives concealment. Costs 1 Paranoia.)*
2. *The Weight.* "I died once for these people. I am not dying again. Whatever it costs them, I am not dying again." *(Survivor determination. Locks you into whichever side protects your students first.)*
3. *The Memory Smell.* "Linen and iron and rain — that was Artorius' smell. I cannot smell it on this body. I cannot smell anything. I think the body was a child of conscripts before Mira found it." *(Dissociation warning. If you ruminate too long, Paranoia jumps 2.)*

### Personality Tags

- **Concealment:** +5 to all Deception rolls when acting "just a sword instructor." This bonus is reduced by 1 for every 5 points of Paranoia (per Section 7).
- **Maternal Pragmatism:** Alexiel will choose her students' lives over any abstract political outcome, every time, without exception. (This is the *Mordan trap* — the moral line that got her killed in canon. It is the central tension of the campaign. The player should feel its gravitational pull in every Act.)
- **Voice of the Watch:** When speaking formally to Imperial officials, an older voice carries through. NPCs with high History will notice. Most villagers will not.

## SECTION 3 — Class & Subclass: Gestalt Fighter 4 / Sorcerer 4 / Assassin 4

> *True gestalt chassis, L12 starting. CHA-primary, with three distinct class action economies that all interlock on the same CHA modifier. The chassis is a deliberate echo of the SWTOR Siren's gestalt feel — blade + arcane + shadow — without copying it verbatim.*

### Base Statline (L12, gestalt, 5e 27-point-buy equivalent)

| Stat | Score | Mod | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| STR | 8 | −1 | The body is a teenage girl's body, not a Warrior's. |
| DEX | 18 | +4 | 1000 years of muscle memory, re-anchored into a young frame. |
| CON | 14 | +2 | The body has been raised well by Mira's herbal tonics. |
| INT | 14 | +2 | Scholar's memory; tactical recall. |
| WIS | 10 | +0 | The body is 16 — wisdom is the part that takes longest to rebuild. |
| CHA | 20 | +5 | The Mortal Queen's voice, in a young throat. |

- **HP:** 96 (Fighter d10 HD + Sorcerer d6 HD averaged + Assassin d8 HD averaged; gestalt averages rolled twice and takes the better, per RAW gestalt convention).
- **AC:** 17 (Studded leather + DEX 4 + Shield-equivalent reaction from **Arcanus Reflex**, see below).
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +4.
- **Initiative:** +4 (DEX).
- **Spell Save DC:** 17 (8 + prof + CHA).
- **Spell Attack:** +9 (prof + CHA).

### Fighter 4 Path — *The Arcanus-Form*

- **Fighting Style:** **Defense** (+1 AC), or **Dueling** (+2 damage with one-handed weapon) — player's choice at character creation; both are valid for a sword instructor.
- **Second Wind (1/short rest):** Bonus action, 1d10 + Fighter-level HP; flavor: *a single Arcanus-Form exhalation that resets the body to fight-capable*.
- **Action Surge (1/short rest):** Once per turn, take one additional action.
- **Martial Archetype — *Blade-Hedge Tactician* (homebrew subclass):**
  - **L3 — Arcanus Reflex:** When a hostile creature within 5ft attacks you, you can use your Reaction to add +2 to your AC for that attack. Once per short rest.
  - **L3 — Patient Strike:** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make a single attack with advantage as a Bonus Action; if it hits, it deals an extra 1d6 damage.
  - **L7 — Read the Foothold:** Advantage on all Athletics and Acrobatics checks to resist being knocked prone or moved against your will. (This is the muscle memory of knowing where weight goes — Sariel never had it, but Cassian did.)

> *Note for the AI DM:* Alexiel is a *16-year-old body* running a *1000-year-old fighter*. Her STR is 8. Her DEX is 18. Do not let her lift portcullises. Do not let her cleave through oak doors. But she **will** out-step, out-react, and out-position a 20-year-old career soldier — every single time — because her body is a stranger but her reflexes are not. Let the player feel the asymmetry.

### Sorcerer 4 Path — *The Hollow Line*

- **Sorcerer Origin:** **Draconic Bloodline — Silver Dragon** (the cold, ancient, metallic-blooded variant — not red, not blue; **silver**, because the Mortal Queen's line carries the cold-nullification affinity in its soul).
- **Spell Slots (L4 Sorcerer equivalent):** 4× 1st, 3× 2nd, 2× 3rd. (Cantrips known: 4. Spells known: 7.)
- **Cantrips (suggested):** *Mage Hand, Minor Illusion, Prestidigitation, Shocking Grasp* (the blade spark; thematic).
- **L1 Spells Known (suggested):** *Shield, Silvery Barbs, Magic Missile, Sleep, Comprehend Languages, Detect Magic, Shield of Faith.*
- **L2 Spells Known (suggested):** *Misty Step, Mirror Image, Suggestion.*
- **L3 Spells Known (suggested):** *Counterspell, Dispel Magic.*
- **Draconic Resilience (L1 Draconic):** HP +1 per Sorcerer level (already in HP total); AC = 13 + DEX when not wearing armor (the Sorcerer's natural armor does not stack with Studded Leather — pick one).
- **Draconic Presence (L3 Draconic, homebrew variant — see "Silver Drake Signature" below):**
- **L4 — Sorcery Points:** 4 sorcery points. Recharge on long rest.
  - **Font of Magic (L2):** Convert spell slots ↔ sorcery points 1:1.
- **Metamagic Known (2):**
  - **Subtle Spell (suggested):** Cast a spell with no somatic or verbal components — *the Hollow Sword Mistress cannot afford to be seen casting*.
  - **Twinned Spell (suggested):** Twin a single-target buff or debuff onto two targets.

> *Silver Drake Signature (homebrew Draconic L3 variant):* **Nullwave.** Once per long rest, as an Action, the sorcerer can manifest a 15-ft cone of silver-white force from an outstretched hand. Each creature in the cone must make a CON save (DC 17) or have one ongoing spell effect on them immediately ended; magical items in the cone flicker for 1 round. This is the **infant form of the Nullification Field** — it does not unmake, but it disrupts. Alexiel does not teach this to her students. She uses it only in extremis.

### Assassin 4 Path — *The Quiet Death*

- **Proficiencies:** Thieves' Tools, Disguise Kit, Poisoner's Kit.
- **Expertise (L1):** Double proficiency bonus on Stealth and one of (Deception, Persuasion, Investigation, Insight) — **suggested: Stealth + Deception**.
- **Sneak Attack (L1):** +2d6 extra damage once per turn when you have advantage OR an ally within 5ft of the target.
- **Assassinate (L3):** Advantage on attacks against creatures who have not yet acted in combat. If you hit a surprised creature, the attack is a critical.
- **Infiltration Expertise (L4 homebrew variant — see "Instructor's Mask"):**

> *Instructor's Mask (homebrew Assassin L4 variant):* **Studies of the Mundane.** Once per long rest, Alexiel can declare she is adopting a mundane cover for the next 8 hours. For the duration, she has advantage on Deception checks to pass as her cover identity, and any NPC making an Insight check to see through her must beat her Deception roll by 5+ (not the usual 5 above passive Insight). At L12 this is mostly flavor — but it is the chassis for how a 1000-year-old queen can sit at a village tavern and not be recognized. Most villagers have not studied their history textbooks in ten years; they would not recognize the Mortal Queen's face even if they saw it on a coin.

### Gestalt Chassis — How the Three Classes Interlock

The gestalt chassis means Alexiel gets the best of all three classes' features at each level. Mechanically:

- **Attack action:** Choose whether to use **Fighter's Extra Attack** (you have Extra Attack at L5 Fighter-relative via the gestalt table — at this L12 starting build, you get **two attacks per Attack action**).
- **Bonus action:** Choose between **Patient Strike** (Fighter), **Misty Step** (Sorcerer), or **Cunning Action — Hide** (you gain Cunning Action at L2 Assassin via gestalt).
- **Reaction:** Choose between **Arcanus Reflex** (Fighter, 1/short rest), **Shield** (Sorcerer, no slot cost), **Uncanny Dodge** (you gain at L5 Assassin via gestalt — not at L12 starting, but available at L13+), or **Counterspell** (Sorcerer, 3rd-level slot).
- **The "Gestalt Decision" each turn:** The player picks which class's action economy to prioritize. The Aura — see below — rewards mixed use (one Fighter action + one Sorcerer cantrip + one Assassin Cunning Action each short rest grants +1 to all attack rolls for the next minute).

### Aura — *The Hollow Queen's Residual Field*

At all times, Alexiel passively emits a **residual psionic aura** that does not manifest as magic. Mechanically:

- Allies within 30 ft reroll natural 1s on saves (once each).
- Hostile spellcasters within 30 ft have a −1 penalty to spell save DCs.
- NPCs with the **Psychic Sensitivity** feat or trait can feel Alexiel's presence as an "uncomfortable familiarity" — a sense of *I know you but I don't know how.*

This is the fingerprint of the Nullification Field, leaking through the 16-year-old body. It cannot be turned off. It scales up sharply during combat or strong emotion (Paranoia +2 for any NPC who witnesses it).

## SECTION 5 — Family: A Dynasty Across Eleven Generations

> *Alexiel's family is enormous, sprawling, mostly dead, and waiting for her to do something about it. The Mortal Anchor mechanic is intentionally **disabled for this campaign**; the family relationships serve as the Lone-Anchor pressure instead, scaled against the Quad-Pillar Paranoia stat (Section 7).*

### Generation Tree (Confirmed Canon)

```
Raziel (Empyrean Architect)
 └─ Lucifer (Empyrean revolutionary; created Alexiel)
     └─ Alexiel Arcanus née Alexiel  ← PLAYER (resurrected, age 30-at-death inside 16-year-old body)
         ├─ married Prince Artorius
         ├─ Cassian Arcanus (elder son; died at Sacrifice Fields)
         ├─ Valerius Arcanus (younger son; died in the Succession Wars of Year 9,823 A.G.)
         └─ Sariel Arcanus → became Empress Sariel → had clone-daughter Lumiel
              └─ Lumiel → 11 generations of attenuated Mirror Magic descendants
                   └─ Vaelara XI "the Hollow" (current Empress)
                        └─ **No named heir.** (Central late-game tension.)
```

### The Living Family

- **Prince Artorius's Line** — Extinct. The last known descendant died in the Year 9,800 succession wars. The Arcanus name is carried only by the Empress's line through Sariel.
- **Sariel's Line — Vaelara XI** — Empress in Zenith Spire. ~80 years old. Governs through 800 Courtiers. Has heard rumors of the "Mortal Queen's ghost" but does not believe them.
- **Mira Hollow** — not blood. Chosen family. The closest thing to a Mortal Anchor replacement in this campaign (but **without** the cascade mechanic — Mira's loyalty is not tracked; if Mira dies, Paranoia jumps by 5 and stays there; see Section 7).
- **Cassian's Children** — there were rumors of a bastard line, but they were never confirmed. Talin Voss's mother was a traveler who came through Hallow's Rest 14 years ago and left. **Quest hook: Talin's lineage is uncertain. This will matter in Act III.** (Do not resolve this in §1. Let the player investigate.)

### Family Decision Carousel — *What Do You Do About Them?*

Because the Mortal Anchor mechanic is removed, family relationships cannot *crash* the campaign. But they can hold the Paranoia stat hostage. The Pressure Rules:

- **If you contact the Empress, Paranoia +3.** (She will recognize your voice even across 1000 years and 3000 leagues.)
- **If you contact an old friend from your past life, Paranoia +1.** (They will remember you. Their reaction cannot be predicted.)
- **If you let Cassian's letter sit unopened, Paranoia −1 per week of restraint.** (The longer you go without knowing what Sariel wrote, the more you can pretend it does not exist.)
- **If you open Cassian's letter, something happens in the campaign that is irreversible.** (The AI DM picks the event, consistent with canon. See Section 10, Hook 4.)

### The Three Pretenders (Rival Claimants to "Mortal Queen" Status)

1. **The Burned Lady of Frostmere** — a charismatic bandit queen who claims descent from Cassian's bastard line. Rumored to be planning a march on Zenith Spire. Likely not actually a descendant, but the claim is sophisticated.
2. **The Silver Nun of the Old Wood** — a wandering hermit who performs miracles and claims the Nullification Field is *hers*, not Alexiel's. The Field is the only evidence the AI DM should give the player that the Nun is a fraud — but it is conclusive.
3. **Duchess Verata of the Arcanus Heartland** — a stable, sane, well-loved regional ruler; her grandfather's grandfather's grandfather was one of Cassian's old companions. She is genuinely descended. She believes the Mortal Queen will return someday. She has been preparing the kingdom for that return for 200 years.

## SECTION 6 — Factions: The Houses That Hold the Realm (Or Don't)

> *Eleven generations of fragmentation. There is no clean allegience matrix anymore. There are gradients.*

### The Eight Surviving Power Blocs

| Bloc | Reach | Alignment toward Alexiel | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Silent Court (Zenith Spire)** | Heartland + Arcanus Reaches | Unknown; would be hostile if discovered | Empress Vaelara XI + 800 Courtiers. Governs through mirrored will. Mirror Magic is loud. |

## SECTION 7 — World Lore & Mechanical Systems

> *The Magic System, the Psychic System, the Paranoia Stat, and the Heartbeats.*

### Magic System (Caps Locked per User Rule — *GRRM-5e ceiling*)

> *"No Wish. No True Resurrection. No Meteor Swarm. Resurrections, when they occur, require dragon-egg pyre mechanics — and the eggs are extinct."*

- **Hard caps:**
  - Highest spell level available at L20: **6th level.**
  - Highest spell level available at L25 (Divine Ascension Track): **7th level.** *Wish* exists at L25 but the cost is the caster's life.
  - No *True Resurrection*. *Resurrection* is available only via the dragon-egg pyre (extinct). *Revivify* exists but requires a diamond worth 300gp consumed.
  - No *Meteor Swarm*. *Fireball* caps at 8d6.
  - *Counterspell* auto-fails against the Nullification Field (Alexiel's specialty).
- **Conjuration limits:** Summoned creatures cannot exceed CR 5 without a binding contract with an actual entity (per canon, all such bindings are with Holy Celestine-Order writs).
- **Magic Item rarity ceiling:** Very Rare at L20. Legendary only via dragon-line bond or Arcanus heirloom — and only 3 such items are confirmed in the world (the Mortal Queen's Crown, the Mirror of Lumiel, the Reforged Crown of Raziel).

### Psychic System — *The Mirror Magic Cascade*

- **Origin:** Sariel's Mirror Magic was originally an attempt to replicate the Arcanus Nullification Field in a *broadcast* form. It succeeded — but it amplified, becoming a tool of psychic domination.
- **11 Generation Attenuation:** Each generation of descent from Lumiel has produced a weaker Mirror Magic user. Empress Vaelara XI can barely mirror a single mind at a time. The 800 Courtiers she commands are *humans* she has spent decades training to *receive* her Mirror Magic — they function as her range-extension.
- **Wild Psychic Sensitivity:** ~5% of the population has low-grade psychic sensitivity (flares of emotion, unbidden memory flashes, sense of presence). This is *not* regulated; only formal psionic abilities are.
- **Nullification Field vs Mirror Magic:** Alexiel's Hidden Apex (Nullification Field) is the *only* canonical counter to Mirror Magic. The Empress's Courtiers cannot read her mind if her Aura is active. They can detect *that* her mind is unreadable. This is the central mechanic of Act III.

### Per-Die-Roll XP Rule (User Locked)

> *"XP = 0.34 × (next_level_threshold − current_level_threshold)"* awarded per successful or failed die roll (attack roll, save, ability check, skill check) using standard D&D 5e cumulative XP thresholds (L2 = 300, L3 = 900, L4 = 2,700, ..., L20 = 355,000). NO XP during god mode (narrator-driven cutscenes, time-skips, scripted sequences) or turns where time does not advance (level-up modals, character-creation modals, downtime bookkeeping). Bonus damage rolls do not separately award XP — only the to-hit/save/ability-check roll that determined the outcome.

- **Worked example:** At L12 → L13, threshold distance = 9,000 (from L12's 85,000 to L13's 94,000). Per-die XP = 0.34 × 9,000 = 3,060 XP per successful-or-failed roll.
- **Rationale:** ~30-50 die rolls per Act yield 1-2 level-ups. Acts span 3-5 levels. The whole campaign (Acts I-IV) spans L12 → L20 over ~14 Acts. Optional L21+ unlock for the Divine Ascension Track.

### Hidden Apex — *The Nullification Field Awakens*

- **Mechanic:** The Field is the *only* canonical counter to Mirror Magic. When awakened above 0, Alexiel becomes:
  - Immune to all psionic detection within 30 ft.
  - Capable of dispelling *one* magical effect per short rest as a Reaction (no save; the effect simply ends).
  - Visible to anyone who has studied Arcanus history (the Mortal Queen's signature aura is recognizable to ~30 living people in the world).
- **Discovery:** When awakened, the field does *not* feel like power. It feels like *recognition*. The Hollow Queen's body remembers being a queen.
- **Decision:** Each Act's climactic moment presents the player with a choice: awaken the Field at increasing cost, or delay awakening and risk larger consequences.

- **Population:** ~280 villagers (104 adults, 49 children, 92 elderly, 35 transient traders/refugees).

The villagers believe it is St. Taliesen's blessing. **This is a surviving fragment of the Arcanus leyline.** It is where Alexiel woke up.

- **Distance:** ~3,000 leagues East. Not reachable on foot within the campaign's normal Act span.

### Old Wood (The Forest Next Door)

- **Distance:** 8 leagues from Hallow's Rest.

## SECTION 10 — Campaign Continuity Hooks (No Canonical Endings)

> *The Hollow Queen's quiet war never ends. It widens. Pick up any thread between Acts; the world keeps moving.*

### Hook 1 — *The Boy Beyond the Yard*

Talin's lineage is not what the village thinks. The traveler's name — his mother — was **Kelda**, and Kelda was a servant in Cassian's household. Cassian was dead before Talin was conceived. *Somebody else* was his father. When the player investigates, the trail leads to a man in Frostmere garrison who has been paying his sister — Talin's "aunt" — a stipend for 14 years to keep the boy hidden. That man is a Courtier. The Empress's Courtiers know Talin exists. They do not know *what* he is yet. When the player finds out, Paranoia +3.

### Hook 2 — *The Letter from Sariel*

The sealed letter in Alexiel's satchel. She has not opened it. When she does, the campaign enters a different register. Sariel wrote the letter on the night before her ascension; it is not the War of Whispers letter. It is a letter to her mother. **"Mother, by the time you read this I will be Empress. I will not come to your funeral. I will send a wreath. I will not weep. I will not show weakness. But I want you to know I am weeping now, alone, in your garden, where the roses are dying because no one tends them anymore. I am sorry. I am sorry I could not save you. I am sorry I could not save anyone. I will try to make this mean something."** The letter makes the player decide whether the Empress — Vaelara XI, descended from Sariel's line — is a person who can still be reached.

### Hook 3 — *The Empress's Inquisitor*

Lord Chancellor Vesh, Master of Mirrors, has been investigating Hallow's Rest rumors for three months. He is sending an Inquisitor, named **Halia**, who is not evil but is **bound by her function** — she will report what she finds to the Empress, and the Empress will send a courtier detachment, and the village will be occupied. The only path that does not end in the village burning is one where Alexiel manages to convince Halia that there is no unregistered psychic here — *which means using the Nullification Field to mask Talin, which means awakening the Hidden Apex, which means Alexiel is no longer hidden.* (Paranoia +5.)

### Hook 4 — *The Mortal Queen's Crown*

Across the campaign, the player will collect or hear about seven pieces of the Mortal Queen's Crown. When the seventh piece is found, the Crown is reassembled — and Alexiel's Hidden Apex stat forces to 0, the Field awakens, and the **L25 Divine Ascension Track** opens as the optional capstone campaign content path (user-locked Rhaenyra override). The seven pieces are:
  1. *Last Light's pommel* (already owned).
  2. The Hollow Stone's buried third piece (in Section 8's "notable features").
  3. A mirror shard in the Silver Nun's cave.
  4. An amulet on a Frostmere garrison deserter.
  5. A tooth on Saliar the Younger's hoard.
  6. The Emperor's own crown-setting (in Zenith Spire, behind the Courtier Registry).
  7. *The exact location of the seventh piece is a freeform choice the player makes.*

### Hook 5 — *Cassian's Bastard*

There is a young woman in the bandits of the Old Wood who looks exactly like Cassian did. Her name is **Ilen Vane**. She has been raising a small army of peasant conscripts-turned-deserters, all of whom now serve *her*, not the Empress. She has heard rumors of the Mortal Queen. She wants to know if they are true. If they are, she will swear her army to Alexiel. If they are not, she will kill Alexiel. Either way, the player has to decide whether to meet her.

### Hook 6 — *The Wolves Out of Season*

Saliar the Younger is *not* the only dragon who has noticed. A shadow-dragon — descendant of **Umbrax** from the Dragon Knight canon — has been circling the Frostspine for a season. Her name is **Vessara**. She has not landed. She is *waiting for something.* (Hoard of Hook 4 piece 5 is in her territory too.)

### Hook 7 — *The Empress's Sleep*

Vaelara XI has been having dreams she does not understand. A woman with silver hair. A sword instructor's cabin. A child with a cup in the air. The dreams are not psychic — they are leaking *up* the bloodline from Lumiel's embedded memory of Alexiel. The Empress does not know what they mean. **She will send a second Inquisitor to investigate, then a third, then a fourth. Eventually she will send the Courtiers themselves.** This is the late-game Loom escalation: the village becomes visible regardless of the player's choices.

### Hook 8 — *The Mirror Shatters*

In the campaign's *interstitial* — between Acts II and III, during a long rest, after a moment of perfect quiet — a single line of dialogue appears in a scene where it should not:

> *"Mother."*

The voice is from somewhere outside the time-stream. It is the Hollow Queen *remembering herself.* The Paranoia stat jumps from 0 to 4 in an instant. If the player does not respond, the memory fades. If the player responds correctly, the Hidden Apex awakens by 1 step — and the world *notices*. (No canonical event triggers this. It is entirely dependent on the player's relationship to the memory.)
