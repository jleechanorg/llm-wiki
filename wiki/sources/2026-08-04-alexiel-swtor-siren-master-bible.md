# Alexiel SWTOR campaign — master character bible ("The Siren")

**Source**: Campaign document "campaign alexiel swtor" — a Level 1 master character bible for [[Alexiel]], designation Subject 7 (Nocturne), a 12-year-old genetically engineered High Human of the House of [[Vitiate]]. She is a Jedi Consular / Sith Sorcerer **gestalt** — an instance of the [[VoidExperimentClass]] chassis from the earlier SWTOR campaign family — surviving as a political prisoner inside a Jedi Academy that fears her, while her father Vitiate contacts her through dreams. Framing line: "a biological weapon disguised as a porcelain doll", the "Wolf in the Fold".

> **Content note**: The source document applies romantic/seduction framing to a 12-year-old character. This wiki page deliberately does not reproduce that framing; mechanics and psychology are documented in neutral, analytical terms (supernatural compulsion, protective-instinct manipulation, deception personas).

## Character architecture

- **Origin**: Lab-raised experiment ("fed only when she performed"), Imperial royalty, now a ward/prisoner of the Jedi. Deliberately humbled with rough oversized Padawan robes — which the text says fails.
- **Persona layer**: [[MaskOfTheInnocent]] — the "Repentant Child" act (perfect Code recitation, deep bows, a single calculated tear) covering a cold, predatory internal monologue. Cross-campaign sibling of [[Mask-of-Fragility]] from the Ains campaign and consistent with the player's documented [[PowerlessMastermindBuild]] / serpent-mastermind pattern.
- **Intrusion layer**: [[TheStatic]] — psychic residue of Vitiate's connection and [[Vaylin]]'s insanity; a grinding low-frequency hum urging cruelty, coped with by humming melodies.
- **Attachment model**: [[TransactionalAffection]] — love parsed as Debt and Ownership; kindness from [[MasterOganDe]] is immediately suspected as a transaction; saving a peer like [[JaxPadawan]] creates an ownership expectation, not gratitude. Explicit narcissistic fragility: being dirty/scarred/humiliated reads as loss of power.
- **Hunger layer**: [[EssenceDevourer]] — forbidden trait; craves Force energy near powerful Jedi, must feed (droid, small animal, or nexus meditation) every 24 hours or suffer Max-HP-reducing hunger pangs.

## Void Experiment class features

1. **[[SirensCall]]** — Force-woven voice bypasses mental defenses: Charm Person or Command 1/short rest without a slot; targets rationalize obedience as their own idea ("weaponized adoration"). The signature undetectable-influence mechanic.
2. **[[HaloEffect]]** — reaction defense: attacker makes a WIS save (DC 14) or hesitates, taking Disadvantage on the attack.
3. **[[EssenceDevourer]]** — lethal damage via Force power grants temp HP = CHA mod + level; can also drain non-sentient sources. (Source truncates mid-sentence here.)

## Analysis

This is the same design grammar as the wiki's other flagship campaigns: an outwardly weak, inwardly predatory protagonist whose real weapons are perception management ([[PreemptiveFrameControl]]) and undetectable influence ([[KyokaSuigetsu]] in the Aizen line, [[SirensCall]] here), embedded in an institution that both fears and supervises them (Jedi Academy ↔ Arcane Academy). The Essence Devourer feed-clock adds a resource-management corruption loop akin to [[DivineAscensionByConsumption]]. The document is a v0 bible: rich psychology, Level 1 mechanics, no scene log yet.
