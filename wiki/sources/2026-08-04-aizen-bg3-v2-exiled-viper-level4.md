# Aizen BG3 v2 — *The Exiled Viper* level 4 character sheet

**Source**: Campaign document "Campaign aizen bg3 v2" — a character profile update for the teen-era [[SosukeAizen|Aizen]] campaign in post-BG3 [[BaldursGate]]. This is the v2 mechanical foundation of the prequel arc seen in [[2026-08-04-aizen-teen-campaign-export|The Architect of Destiny]]: Aizen at age 16, Level 4, posing as a gifted commoner orphan under state wardship at the Arcane Academy.

## Header contradiction (see Contradictions)

The profile header lists "Level 4 (Wizard - School of the Mirror Flower)" — a nod to [[KyokaSuigetsu]] — but the entire stat block that follows is a **College of Swords Bard** build ([[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild]]). The bard build is the operative ruleset; the Wizard/Mirror-Flower line appears to be a vestigial flavor header from an earlier draft.

## The build (RAW point buy, Half-Elf, Level 4)

- **CHA 18 (+4)** (15 +2 racial, +1 ASI) — Spell DC 15, spell attack +7; the character's core weapon is social domination.
- **DEX 14 (+2)**, **INT 16 (+3)** (+1 ASI), CON 12, WIS 10, **STR 8 (-1)** — "slender frame hides lethality."
- Race: Half-Elf (Darkvision, Fey Ancestry, skills: Deception, Stealth). Background: Entertainer (Criminal Variant) — Disguise Kit, Poisoner's Kit.
- Features: Bardic Inspiration d6 ×5/day, Jack of All Trades, Expertise (Deception, Persuasion), Song of Rest; College of Swords: Two-Weapon Fighting, Blade Flourish.
- Spells: cantrips Vicious Mockery / Minor Illusion / Prestidigitation / Mage Hand; L1 Charm Person, Disguise Self, Silvery Barbs, Healing Word; L2 Suggestion, Hold Person, Detect Thoughts.
- Combat: AC 15 (17+ with Defensive Flourish), Rapier +6 (1d8+2+1d6 BI), bonus dagger +6, Mobile Flourish hit-and-run.

Explicitly **no magical power-ups** — "just raw skill, crime, and psychological predation." The design deliberately keeps godhood-era Aizen's psychology while stripping his power, matching the [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] / prequel pattern.

## Challenge Compensation table

The sheet includes a [[ChallengeCompensation]] section calibrating threats so a Level 4 PC stays viable against post-BG3 chaos (L12 BG3-companion encounters, CR5-10 org thugs):

- **Social/Enchant counters**: high-Wis foes ([[Laezel|Lae'zel]] interrogation at DC15+ with save advantage), anti-charm amulets, madness poisons; rivals suspect the "witch-boy" beauty.
- **Physical counters**: swarms ([[TheGuild]] cutpurses CR1/8 ×10), magic-resistant Absolute remnants, environmental sewer ambushes.
- **Intrigue counters**: family bounties (time pressure), rival intel ([[Lysandra]] has visions of his moves), moral traps ([[Isolde]] guilt forcing suboptimal plays).
- **Reward scaling**: VIP blackmail = 300XP + Poisoner's Kit upgrade; failures risk capture for a "breaking" reversal. Sample encounters: Zhentarim thug pack (400XP), garden escapee pursued by [[Kaname]] (300XP), family bounty tracked via Isolde (600XP + Sosuke ledger).
- **BG3 cameos**: friendly [[Gale]] (INT contests), antagonistic [[Wyll]] (Paladin smite vs. charm); [[Zhentarim]] counter with charm-immune devils.

## The Asset: the Arcane Athenaeum

Section VIII details [[ArcaneAthenaeum|the Academy]] as Aizen's primary asset — publicly a crumbling-prestigious school in the Upper City's Sea Ward where he plays meek 16-year-old scribe "Sosuke"; in reality a curated **blackmail factory and recruitment nexus** ([[AcademyBlackmailFactory]]). Post-Absolute faculty shortages give him lax oversight to spike communal teas with [[EssenceOfLethe]] (memory haze) and his own alchemical narcotic [[WhisperdustNarcotic|Whisperdust]] ("euphoric dependency mimicking Mystra's touch"). Drugged heirs confess family secrets, perform degrading tasks under Suggestion, or owe silence-debts; broken nobles become "Garden" fodder, spies, or puppets. The innocent "study group" is a velvet trap of spiked wine and illusory privacy wards. Key figure roster begins with [[ElowenThorne|Professor Elowen Thorne]] (document truncates mid-entry).
