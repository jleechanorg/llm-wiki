# Campaign Bible: The Iron Eagle: Vengeance (Gaia Julia Caesar v6)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign gaia Julia caesar v6" — the v6 iteration of the [[IronEagleVengeance|Iron Eagle: Vengeance]] Roman alternate-history campaign. It reuses the v3 frame (71 BCE, [[ThirdServileWar]], Republic already dead, [[JuliusCaesar]] as Emperor) and adds a much deeper master personality sheet: the **[[DecadentConqueror]]** archetype with the [[BloodOfVenus]] physical-zenith writeup and the [[SensoryFeast]] core compulsion.

## Campaign frame (identical to v3)

- **Setting**: 71 BCE, the Third Servile War. Divergence: Julius Caesar seized power early; he is Emperor, the Republic is dead.
- **Player character**: [[AureliusJuliaCaesar]] (16), "the Prodigy of the Julian Line", the Emperor's youngest, most ambitious son — sent to the front not to lead but to "fix" the mess created by incompetent Praetors like [[Glaber]].
- **Cover identity**: **Aurelius Varenus**, a wealthy merchant scion dealing in grain and wine — the [[ShadowPrince]] archetype (spoiled hedonist public face used to travel freely, bribe officials, and spy).
- **Assets**: 50,000 gold, a squad of black-ops Praetorians, and a secret imperial mandate: "End the rebellion. Save the gold. Do not embarrass the family."
- **Build**: Level 6 [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]] Bard; duelist, manipulator, wielder of the [[Miasma]] ("The Divine Blood").
- **Antagonist framing**: [[Spartacus]] is "just a messy stain you need to scrub out" — the rebellion as an aesthetic insult, not a moral question.

## New in v6: the Decadent Conqueror personality bible

- **Dual identity**: publicly "Aurelius Julia Caesar — the Sun of Rome / Passion of the Empire"; to the Miasma, "the Devouring Maw", a singularity of Appetite who treats the universe as a banquet table.
- **Archetype**: ESFP–ENTJ hinge ("The Performer / The King"). Visual signature: constant tactile contact as a gesture of **Ownership** — touching is claiming.
- **[[BloodOfVenus]]**: the character is framed as the most beautiful man in the Empire — electrum hair, bioluminescent porcelain skin, liquid-amber eyes. Beauty is explicitly armor and [[BeautyAsWeapon|a weapon]]: veterans hesitate to strike ("it feels like vandalism"), rivals forgive cruelties, Auctoritas manifests as literal Heat (rooms warm with his mood).
- **Two masks**: the *Prince of Pleasure* (public — loud laughter, open weeping at theater, tyranny made fun, gilded armor and perfumed tent as props) versus the *Golden Tyrant* (true — Lawful Evil absolute Entitlement; "Order is beautiful and Chaos is ugly"; emotional sadism that wants to watch hope break because it validates supremacy).
- **Core compulsion — [[SensoryFeast]]**: boredom with dry reports; craving for Intensity (the felt weight of a Legion marching at his command). The document cuts off mid-sentence in this section.

## Relation to prior versions

- v3 ([[2026-08-04-aurelius-caesar-v3-iron-eagle]]) established the identical campaign intro; v6's delta is the expanded personality bible (Decadent Conqueror, Blood of Venus, two-mask structure, Sensory Feast).
- The v1 Pax Caesara campaign ([[2026-08-04-aurelius-v1-pax-caesara]]) shares the dual-mask Lawful Evil heir architecture in a different timeline (40 BCE, Caesar survived the Ides).
- Continues the documented player pattern of [[BeautyAsWeapon]] and serpent-mastermind/tyrant protagonists across campaigns (see [[SerpentQueen]] in the BG1 Nocturne profile).

## Notable naming discrepancy

The source is titled "campaign **gaia** Julia caesar v6" (Gaia = feminine Roman praenomen), but the entire body text describes a male character ("the most beautiful **man** in the Empire", the Emperor's **son**). Either the title reflects a planned gender-flipped variant that this draft does not yet implement, or "gaia" is a campaign-line label rather than the character's name.
