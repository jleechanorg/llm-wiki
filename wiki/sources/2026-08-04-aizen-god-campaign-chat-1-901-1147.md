# Aizen god campaign chat 1 (entries 901-1147)

Fourth and final segment of the [[AizenSosuke|Aizen]] god-campaign chat 1 transcript (entries 901-1147). By this point Aizen has ascended to godhood ("The God-King") and operates through a mortal-realm avatar from the Sosuke Arcane Annex in [[BaldursGate]], 1492 DR (Flamerule 13). The segment codifies two governance systems: the avatar's autonomy contract and the economics of mass telepathic domain usurpation.

## Key events

- **Avatar autonomy protocol established**: Aizen's avatar is granted Level 20 intellect and full autonomous mission execution, with a two-tier reporting contract — telepathic conferral with the divine consciousness required only for "important decisions" (major conflicts, deposing rulers, powerful artifacts, direct confrontation with the [[FlamingFist]] or Knights of the Shield), plus unprompted daily status reports. See [[Divine-Avatar-Autonomy-Protocol]].
- **Telepathic Banite conversion analyzed**: The player asks whether mass-persuading all former [[Bane]] followers telepathically has downsides, reasoning "It's my domain, should be allowed." The DM confirms it is permitted under Absolute Codification / Domain Influence and enumerates a structured pro/con ledger.
- **Pros**: Divine Save DC 100 guarantees unresistable loyalty; simultaneous city-wide imposition; absorption of cultist knowledge, caches, and networks; avoids overt conflict with the [[FlamingFist]]; directly usurps Bane's tyranny/control domain remnants.
- **Cons/costs**: Major Domain Manipulation costs **50 DPP per wave** of targets ([[Divine-Power-Points]]); risk of divine detection by [[Mystra]] (minds), [[Shar]] (domain conflict), or [[Ao]] (broad disruptions); the puppeted cultists must not behave so perfectly that mundane observers infer divine control. See [[DivineDetectionTiers]].
- **Segment ends mid-sentence**: The player begins proposing an alternative strategy ("How about this strategy. Neg..."), truncated at the export boundary.

## Mechanics documented

- **Divine Save DC 100** — mortals cannot resist Aizen's imposed will; commoner influence is automatic and free.
- **DPP economy** — widespread simultaneous telepathic imposition over higher echelons or large groups is metered at 50 DPP per wave/group, distinguishing free ambient influence from paid [[Telepathic-Domain-Usurpation]].
- **Divine detection tiers** — pervasive divine will risks attention from Greater Deities and the Overgod, a softer consequence class than direct divine combat.
