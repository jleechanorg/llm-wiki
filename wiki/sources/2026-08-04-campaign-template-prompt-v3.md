# Campaign Template prompt v3 (with prompt v2 fragment)

**Source**: "Copy of Campaign template" — a meta-document, not a campaign itself. This is the reusable **master prompt** the user feeds to an AI to generate the campaign bibles seen throughout this wiki (the Visenya line v5–v8, the Ains/Overlord module, etc.). It instructs the AI to act as "an expert Narrative Designer and Game Master specializing in high-stakes, character-driven solo D&D 5e campaigns" and to produce a comprehensive Campaign Bible for a single powerful protagonist. A truncated **prompt v2** fragment at the end names the earlier revision: the [[SovereignSoloCampaignGenerator]] ("God-Mode" Solo D&D 5e campaigns).

## Structure: the nine-section Campaign Bible

The template mandates the [[CampaignBibleNineSectionStructure]], generated "in high detail" (explicitly: "Do not summarize; detail every section"):

1. **Campaign Intro** — thematic title, world twist, and a Hook framed as power fantasy ("You are not saving the world; you are conquering it").
2. **Character Personality** — psychological archetype (e.g. "The Decadent Conqueror" with an MBTI hinge like ESFP/ENTJ), sensory visual signature and habits, the [[MasksPublicFaceTrueNature]] duality, a [[CoreCompulsionMechanic]] (a psychological hunger with an explicit failure penalty / success bonus, e.g. Exhaustion vs Temp HP), interaction shorthand for Rivals vs Subordinates, and 3 sample inner-monologue thoughts.
3. **Character Class** — renamed or Gestalt class with lore-fitting power source, one game-breaking "Main Character" unique mechanic (examples named: "Auctoritas", "Soul Thief"), and a Levels 1–30 progression table with Epic Boons.
4. **Assets & Retinue** — starting rank, wealth, safehouses, blackmail/political leverage, a named 3–4 item "Panoply", and 3 NPC subordinates each with Name/Class/Role/Loyalty/Personality.
5. **Family (The Viper's Nest)** — the [[VipersNestFamilyStructure]]: two Titan parents (Lvl 20+) who overshadow the PC, plus exactly two older brothers and two older sisters, each with a Mask and a Rival/Ally/Pawn dynamic.
6. **Factions** — 10 Noble Houses (each with Specialty, Vibe, and an exploitable Hook/weakness), 10 friendly factions as tools, 10 antagonistic factions biased toward humanoid/structural threats over generic monsters.
7. **World Lore** — timeline with an explicit Divergence Point, mythos/magic system, current crisis, and 3 escalating story arcs (Early/Mid/Late).
8. **Gazetteer & Mechanics** — 4 key stages each with Vibe + sub-locations + a social or physical Hazard, 2 custom gameplay systems, and an 8-entry loot table of narrative-power Relics/Favors (no generic +1 items).
9. **Starting Scene** — atmospheric setting, immediate hook, and an A/B/C first decision that lets the player "demonstrate their authority immediately".

## Why this source matters to the wiki

This template is the **generator** behind the campaign-bible corpus: it explains why [[VisenyaTargaryen]] v5/v6, [[VisenyaBelaerys]] v8, and the Ains Overlord module all share the same skeleton — power-fantasy hook, masked mastermind protagonist with a compulsion mechanic, renamed Gestalt classes with a signature game-breaking ability (v8's Sovereign Shadow Tracker and the Ains [[Mask of Fragility]] are Section 8 custom mechanics; "Auctoritas" appears in the wiki as [[Auctoritas]]), Titan parents and sibling webs, 10/10/10 faction blocks, divergence-point alt-history timelines, gazetteers with hazards and relic loot tables, and A/B/C authority-demonstrating opening scenes. Tone and setting are parameterized ("Lawful Evil Political, Grimdark High Fantasy, Cyberpunk"), matching the user's documented preference profile for INTJ/ENTJ manipulation-focused protagonists.

The v2 fragment confirms the template lineage: prompt v2 ("Sovereign Solo" / "God-Mode") preceded the current sectioned v3.
