# Campaign Visenya v3

Here is the updated Campaign Bible for The Apex of Ash: The Sowing, refactored for a Charisma-based Sorcerer build. This fits your "Belaerys Blood" concept perfectly—your magic isn't learned from books; it is the raw, terrifying heat of the Fourteen Flames demanding obedience.
Campaign Bible: The Apex of Ash (Dance of the Dragons)
Setting: Dragonstone (The Sowing, 129 AC)
Player Character: Visenya "Silver" Belaerys
Class: Dragon Lord (Sorcerer Subclass)
Role: Secret Apex Heir / Rhaenyra’s Hidden Weapon
Section 1: Campaign Intro
 * Title: The Apex of Ash: The Sowing
 * The Concept: The Dance of Dragons has begun. Queen Rhaenyra, desperate to match the Green’s firepower, has called for "Dragonseeds" to claim the riderless dragons on the Dragonmont. You are Visenya Belaerys, the sole survivor of the Valyrian "Apex" line. You have come to claim Vermithor, the Bronze Fury, not because you are a bastard seeking glory, but because you are a queen seeking her throne.
 * The Hook: You possess a natural Charisma (18) that bends wills and reality alike. You must secure a dragon and rise in Rhaenyra's court without terrifying her into seeing you as a rival. If you shine too bright, you wake the Architects of Silence (Ancient Valyrian Hunters); if you stay too dim, you die a peasant.
Section 2: Character Dynamics
 * Name: Visenya (Alias: "Silver").
 * Visuals: Exceptional Beauty hidden beneath soot and wool. When you speak in the Noblest Dialect, your voice resonates in the bones of those who hear it.
 * The Anchor (Mortal Tie): Gerrick the Smith. A massive, kind-hearted brute of a Dragonseed. He believes you are just a clever peasant girl. He protects you from physical threats; you protect him from magical and political ones.
 * Rhaenyra Targaryen: She views you as a "lucky find"—a peasant with strange potential. She wants to use you. You plan to let her, until you are strong enough to lead.
Section 3: Class - Dragon Lord (Sorcerer Subclass)
 * Base Class: Sorcerer.
 * Subclass: Apex Bloodline.
 * Primary Stat: Charisma (18). Your magic is force of personality and bloodright.
 * Unique Mechanic: Subtle Apotheosis.
   * You gain the Subtle Spell Metamagic for free.
   * When casting spells that influence the mind (Command, Suggestion, Charm Person) or affect Dragons, you do not need to spend Sorcery Points to use Subtle Spell. The magic simply happens, appearing as "divine luck" or "royal authority."
 * Feature: The Noblest Dialect.
   * You have Advantage on all Charisma checks when speaking High Valyrian to Valyrian descendants or Dragons.
 * The Ascension Meter (The Threat Gauge):
   * Current: 0/100.
   * Trigger: Using your Apex Aura (Raw CHA force to crush a mind or command a dragon) raises the meter.
   * Consequence: High meter attracts Mage Slayers (The Architects) and triggers Rhaenyra’s paranoia. Low meter keeps you safe but limits your influence.
Section 4: The Starting Scene - The Bronze Fury
Location: The Dragonmont, Dragonstone.
Atmosphere: The cavern is a hellscape of sulfur and heat. Vermithor, the Bronze Fury—the second largest dragon in the world—coils around a mound of ash. He is old, angry, and lonely. He has just incinerated a knight who tried to use a whip on him.
The Situation: Queen Rhaenyra stands on the high ridge, her face a mask of frustration. "Is there no one?" she demands. The remaining seeds are cowering. Gerrick stands beside you, clutching his rusted hammer, trembling. "He's too big, Silver. We should go."
The Problem: The Dragonkeepers are moving to block the entrance, intending to end the Sowing for the day. If they do, you lose your chance to claim the Bronze Fury.
The Action: Gerrick takes a hesitant step forward to protect you, but Vermithor’s throat glows with magma-light. He prepares to fire.
 * A) The Blood-Link (Sorcerous Influence): Use Subtle Casting (Calm Emotions) to snuff out Vermithor’s rage instantly. To the onlookers, it looks like the dragon simply decided to spare the "brave smith." You then guide Gerrick to the dragon's flank, claiming you are just "helping." (Keeps Threat Low, Secures Gerrick as an ally).
 * B) The Apex Command (Raw Charisma): Step past Gerrick. Drop your mental shields and project your Apex Aura directly at Vermithor. Speak one word in the Noblest Dialect: "Lykirī" (Calm). The dragon won't just stop; he will bow. (Secures Vermithor instantly; Raises Ascension Meter +15; Rhaenyra sees your power).
 * C) The Illusory Echo: Use Minor Illusion to recreate the sound of King Jaehaerys’s old voice (Vermithor's previous rider). In his confusion, you and Gerrick rush the gap. (Risky; relies on Deception).
What is your command, Dragon Lord?
