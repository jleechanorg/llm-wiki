# Campaign Module: The Velvet Cage (Descent Edition) — Nocturne Sosuke v6

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign noctune bg3 v6" — the sixth iteration of the [[NocturneSosuke]] Baldur's Gate 3-adjacent campaign line. System: D&D 5e (2024 Ruleset Compatible). Tone: **Survival Horror / Gothic Noir / Infernal Politics**. Protagonist: Nocturne Sosuke, "The Black Diamond," age 16. The document carries an explicit precedence rule: *"If anything contradicts, assume things further below take precedence vs defined earlier"* — later sections override earlier ones.

## Character concept: the Vessel of Stolen Fire

Nocturne retains a delicate half-elven frame "perfected into something unsettlingly flawless": skin with the cool polished texture of living marble, pale as bone china, devoid of sweat or pores — "the beauty of a statue brought to life by a dark miracle." Signature features:

- **Face/hair**: sharp aristocratic features; raven-black hair in heavy silken waves that looks perpetually damp, "as if she has just emerged from the sea or a birthing pool."
- **Eyes**: deep violet at rest; when channeling power (casting, commanding a thrall, stealing a soul) they ignite with blinding pink-and-crimson luminescence that floods out the whites — "twin stars of Abyssal hunger."
- **Attire**: sheer midnight-blue silks; at campaign start torn and stained with blood/ichor/ash, worn as ruin-as-high-fashion (unmarred clothing arrives later per player note).
- **Hidden markings**: at-will manifestation of small elegant horns and retractable Abyssal wings; hidden, her silhouette reads as a mortal noblewoman; revealed, they are wreathed in shadow and starlight marking her as **Sovereign of the [[ShatteredRose]]**.

The narrative hook frames her as "a quiet dissonance": visually a shipwreck — a porcelain girl in ruined silk — yet her presence displaces air "like a rising tide," with a faint pre-storm sea-sharpness instead of sulfur, and stillness louder than others' noise.

## Core mechanic: [[PorcelainPerfectionProgression]] (Level 0–20 physical evolution)

The stated theme: "You do not grow monstrous; you grow perfect" — transformation refines the half-elven form into the uncanny valley rather than adding claws or scales.
