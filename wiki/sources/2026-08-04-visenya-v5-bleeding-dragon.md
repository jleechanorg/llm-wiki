# Campaign Bible: The Bleeding Dragon (campaign Visenya v5)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign Visenya v5" — an alternate-history A Song of Ice and Fire campaign bible in which [[RhaegarTargaryen]] killed [[RobertBaratheon]] at the Trident in 283 AC, producing not a restored dynasty but [[TheLongDiscord]]: fifteen years of grinding, unresolved civil war. Fifth entry in the Visenya campaign line (after v2 Slaver's Bay, v3 Dance-era Dragonstone, v4 Dunk & Egg).

## Premise

The year is 298 AC. Rhaegar's counter-thrust pierced Robert's throat at the Ruby Ford, but the rebellion's spirit survived its leader. Westeros is a broken continent: the Targaryens hold King's Landing and the southern breadbasket, while the North, Riverlands, and Stormlands are entrenched hostile nations. There is no peace — only [[TheGrinding]], a seasonal rhythm of border skirmishes, raids, and failed treaties. The Iron Throne is a command center for a military junta; the Red Keep smells of ozone, wildfire, and drying blood.

The player character is **Princess [[VisenyaTargaryen]]**, 12-year-old daughter of Prince Regent Rhaegar and the vanished sorceress [[VaelaBaelarys]] of lost [[HouseBaelarys]]. To the realm she is a child; to Maesters, Mages, and the Mad King she is the "Apex" ([[ApexBloodline]]) — a biological impossibility who "sees the code" the game of thrones is written in.

## Timeline of the Divergence (283–298 AC)

- **283 AC — The Ruby Ford**: Rhaegar takes Robert's hammer blow to the chest (cracked ribs) but kills him. The rebel vanguard under [[EddardStark]] goes berserk rather than routing; the exhausted Royalists cannot pursue. Stalemate. Rebels retreat to the Neck and Riverlands fortresses.
- **283 AC — The Sack that Wasn't**: [[TywinLannister]] arrives at King's Landing to secure it for the *victors*, pledges to the living Rhaegar, and extracts the price of **Hand of the King for life**. [[AerysIITargaryen]] orders the wildfire caches ignited; the wounded Rhaegar countermands it, declares his father "unwell," and confines him to the Maidenvault — King in title, prisoner in reality.
- **284 AC — The Tower of Joy**: Rhaegar returns from Dorne with Lyanna Stark's bones and a baby boy. He publicly acknowledges the child as his bastard **[[JaehaerysTargaryen|Jaehaerys Waters]]** (later legitimized) to prevent the North rallying around "Lyanna's stolen son." Eddard Stark refuses to kneel but agrees to the [[CeasefireOfBones]] and closes the Neck; the North becomes an isolationist kingdom trading with Braavos.
- **287 AC — The Baelarys Arrival**: Obsessed with the Prince That Was Promised prophecy and needing a third "head" (Elia Martell is dead — killed by the Mountain, whom Rhaegar executed to appease Dorne), Rhaegar's mystics locate **Vaela**, a House Baelarys survivor in the ruins of Volantis. They marry; Visenya is born 286 AC. Six months later Vaela **vanishes** from the Red Keep — official story a sickness, rumor an "ascension." Visenya is the only evidence she existed.
- **290–298 AC — The Grinding**: Perpetual counter-insurgency against the "Stag Lords" (Baratheon loyalists, Stormlands) and "River Wolves" (Tully/Stark guerillas, Riverlands). The realm is bankrupt; the Iron Bank circles like a vulture.

## Geopolitics of the Broken Realm

- **Crownlands ("The Dragon's Coil")**: Martial law. King's Landing overcrowded with refugees. The Gold Cloaks are a 6,000-man paramilitary force under Prince [[ViserysTargaryen]] — brutal, corrupt, loyal to the highest bidder. The Dragonpit is partially rebuilt by Rhaegar, though dragons are extinct (source truncates mid-sentence on its purpose).

## Recurring campaign-line patterns

Consistent with v2–v4: Visenya as hidden-Apex scion of a lost Valyrian sorcerous house, [[BeautyAsWeapon]]-adjacent manipulation framing, INTJ mastermind power fantasy, and a canon-divergent ASOIAF setting. Distinct here: youngest protagonist yet (age 12), father alive and ruling, mother a vanished sorceress, and a war-economy dystopia replacing courtly intrigue.

## Notable worldbuilding beats

- Aerys II alive, mad, and imprisoned — a latent wildfire-shaped Chekhov's gun.
- Jon Snow exists as Jaehaerys Waters/Targaryen, a legitimized rival-heir tension vector.
- Tywin as Hand-for-life: the junta's spine and an unremovable rival power center.
- The smallfolk "pray the dragons do not look at them" — the dynasty as occupier, not glory.
