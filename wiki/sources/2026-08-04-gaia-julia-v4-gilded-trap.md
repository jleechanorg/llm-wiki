# Campaign Bible: The Gilded Trap — Blood on the Marble (Gaia Julia v4)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign Gaia julia v4" — the v4 iteration of the Gaia Julia Roman alternate-history line. Tone: **Erotic Thriller / Reverse Horror / Political Wargame**. Year: **73 BCE**; the timeline has shifted so that [[JuliusCaesar]] is already **Dictator Perpetuo** while the [[ThirdServileWar]] erupts — [[Spartacus]] and [[Crixus]] are burning the Italian countryside.

## The Hook — inverted captivity

[[GaiaJuliaCaesar]], the 16-year-old daughter of the God-Emperor, travels to the countryside under the alias **"Livia of Capua"** ([[Capua]]) posing as a minor noble's daughter. The rebels sack the villa hosting her and believe they've captured a terrified, pampered noblegirl — a "moral dilemma" for their revolution. In truth they have captured a **Level 12 Siren (Bard/Paladin Gestalt)** — "a biological weapon of social engineering." The reverse-horror premise: *she is not the hostage; she is the predator playing with her food.* Her goal is to turn the rebel leadership against itself, seduce their champions into serving her, and orchestrate the rebellion's downfall from inside its own command tent.

## Character — the Two Masks

- **Archetype**: The Black Widow / The Architect (ENFJ-ENTJ hinge).
- **The Facade ("Livia")**: translucent Coan silk over a frame that *looks* breakable but hides Strength 18 "dense cords of steel"; sobbing, flinching, breathless high register engineered to trigger the "Protector" instinct in men like Spartacus.
- **The True Nature (Gaia)**: cold puppeteer who finds the rebellion "quaint," views people as instruments, and derives her deepest pleasure from **Control** — "watching a strong man break his own code to please you."
- **Signature details**: scent of crushed violets and ozone (a consciously releasable pheromonal trigger); a two-finger touch to the throat when genuinely amused; unnatural gold eyes ("The Miasma of Venus" — continuous with the [[Miasma]] / [[BloodOfVenus]] lore of earlier versions) usually kept downcast to appear submissive.

## Core Compulsion — [[SymphonyOfRuin|The Symphony of Ruin]]

An addiction to emotional subversion: make enemies love her before destroying them, because "simply killing them is boring; breaking their soul is art." Mechanized as **The Muse**:
- **Discord (failure penalty)**: forced Crude Violence (brawling, shouting, getting hit) deals Psychic Damage equal to her Level — it offends her artistry.
- **Harmony (success bonus)**: charming/dominating a target or defusing combat with words grants a **d12 Inspiration Die** usable on AC or Saving Throws.

## Class & Mechanics

- **Class**: Bard, custom subclass **[[CollegeOfTheSiren|College of the Siren]]**, Level 12 posing as a Level 1 Commoner. No instrument — **her Voice is the spellcasting focus**; magic is psionic and pheromonal, woven into speech and movement.
- **[[VelvetIron|The Velvet Iron]]** (always active): effectively permanent Subtle Spell metamagic for Enchantment and Illusion spells — she can cast Dominate Person or Suggestion mid-conversation with no visible tells. (Source document cuts off mid-description of this mechanic.)

## Social toolkit

- **To Spartacus**: weaves a trust/paternalism narrative — "You have the eyes of a father" — aiming to make him "my gladiator, not Rome's enemy" by finding the key to "the cage of morality he builds around himself."
- **To Crixus**: frames him as the frightening monster ("you scare me") to isolate him from the leadership — engineered sibling-rivalry inside the rebel command.
- **The [[LotusTen|Lotus Ten]]**: an embedded slave network she directs with a sharp intake of breath or a specific hum — "they know exactly which slave needs to spill wine on which guard."
- **Ruling philosophy vs her father's**: "My father rules by fear. I rule by love. Love is so much harder to rebel against."

## Relation to other versions

Shares the Third Servile War frame with [[2026-08-04-gaia-julia-caesar-v6-iron-eagle|v6 Iron Eagle]] (71 BCE, Emperor Caesar) but moves to 73 BCE with Caesar as Dictator Perpetuo, swaps the Shadow Prince / [[DecadentConqueror]] frame for direct infiltration of the rebel camp, and replaces v3's Level 1 sheet with an overpowered Level 12 deception premise (power level masked, not absent). The manipulation-through-performed-fragility toolkit continues [[BeautyAsWeapon]] and the [[MaskOfFragility|Mask of Fragility]] pattern from the Ains module.
