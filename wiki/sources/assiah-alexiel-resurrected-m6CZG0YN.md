Quiet War — The Baron's Youngest Daughter
A campaign bible for WorldArchitect.AI. The player is Alexiel Arcanus, resurrected in a twelve-year-old body. She is the youngest daughter of Baron Aldric Arcanus of Hollowmere, a border-town sword-master. She has full memories, is done with war, and carries a face carved into empire-wide monuments. Her family does not yet know what she is.
Open-Ended System Notice (hard rule): This bible ships as a system. There is no canonical ending, final verdict, or ending determinator matrix. Use Continuity Hooks (Section 10). Player choices drive the campaign.

§1 — CAMPAIGN INTRO: THE HOLLOW PEACE AND THE QUIET WAR
You are Alexiel Arcanus — former Magister of the Watch and Mortal Queen, who died at age thirty at the Battle of the Sacrifice Fields (Year 9,408 A.G.) via her Nullification Field to save 312 children, including her 15-year-old daughter Sariel. You are worshipped as a god (the Mother Who Burned, the Hollow Queen), but woke up eight months ago in a 12-year-old body in Hollowmere, a border-town of 900 souls.
Baron Aldric Arcanus (60) is your father — a retired Imperial sword-master and seventeenth cousin, seven times removed, of the imperial line. He runs the Arcanus Fencing School and took you in as a stray. You are his youngest daughter, Alexiel.
The year is ~10,420 A.G. The Arcanus Imperium rules half the world. Empress Vaelara XI (eleventh generation of Sariel's blood) rules from the Silent Throne, aided by 800 Courtiers. Hollowmere houses an Arcanus leyline (the Hollow Stone).
Your Fame is Your Shadow: Your name and visage are carved into monuments across the empire. Without scrying or remote magic, exposure happens strictly via visiting traders, pilgrims, and imperial tax-collectors who might recognize your face.
§2 — THE CANON: WHO ALEXIEL ARCANUS WAS BEFORE SHE DIED
§2.1 — Cosmological Frame
Raziel (Empyrean Architect) ruled the Eight Heavens. Lucifer (Morning Star) led the divine rebellion, losing and withdrawing after engineering Alexiel as his Prodigal Sword—a being perfectly mortal yet housing a Nullification Field that unmakes magic in a 30-ft radius.
§2.2 — Career & Family (Years 9,378–9,408)
Found at 17, you became Magister of the Watch at 20, ending the Host Wars. At 22, you married Prince Artorius. You had three children: Cassian (b. 9,389), Valerius (b. 9,389), and Sariel (b. 9,391).
§2.3 — The Battle of the Sacrifice Fields (Year 9,408)
Mordan held Sariel hostage, leveraging your absolute moral code regarding children. To stop his ritual, you projected your Nullification Field for 12 seconds, burning out your mortal body. Sariel and the children survived. You died.
§2.4 — The 1,012 Years Since
Years 9,408–9,432: Grieving Sariel studies your journals, creating Mirror Magic (Mother's Echo).
Years 9,432–9,475: Sariel takes the throne via the War of Whispers, establishing the Silent Peace.
Years 9,475–9,855: Sariel rules 380 years, marrying Lord Cassian Valerion, having daughter Lumiel.
Years 9,815–9,823: The Dragon Knight Ser Arion val Valerion revolts and takes the throne. Sariel returns, speaks with him privately for two hours, and executes him in a private audience, weeping.
Years 9,823–10,420: Sariel rules ~597 more years, dying at ~647. Eleven imperial generations follow, culminating in Vaelara XI.
