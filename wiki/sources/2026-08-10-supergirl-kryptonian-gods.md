## Captured design decisions (provenance for the bible)

- **Power hierarchy**: Kryptonian Gods (Superman 25, Supergirl 20) >> Apex Five (5 terrestrial Level 20+ beings — Doctor Fate, Wonder Woman, Black Adam, Swamp Thing, Martian Manhunter) >> Peak Humanity (Level 12 — Lex Luthor, Batman, Amanda Waller, plus Kara's Inner Circle) >> Mortal Average (Level 1–5).
- **Class**: Gestalt Fighter (Eldritch Knight) / Monk (Way of the Open Hand). Spellcasting stat unified to Wisdom. Gearless — Kara wears no armor, wields no weapons. Her unarmed strikes ARE her Weapon Bond.
- **Core stats**: STR 20 / DEX 20 / CON 20 / INT 12 / WIS 20 / CHA 12.
- **Aesthetic**: "Staggering Alien Perfection & Divine Luminance" — radiant presence, sapphire-shimmer eyes, hair that falls perfectly in atmospheric reentry, body sheds dirt/blood/grime automatically via bio-electric aura.
- **Trigger event**: Kara incinerated General Josip Malakor (dictator of Markovia) on live international television after he deployed ethnic cleansing with human shields and dirty-bomb triggers tied to his own heart-rate. ICC has issued an arrest warrant. Lex Luthor will serve as special prosecutor. Solar Dawn cult exploded in membership overnight.
- **Tone**: Social / political / theological. Combat is a known-ineffective tool; the campaign is about navigating worship, jurisdiction, sovereign immunity, cult schism, and human MAD deterrents — without losing one's soul.
