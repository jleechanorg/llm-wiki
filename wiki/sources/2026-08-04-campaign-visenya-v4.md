# Campaign Visenya v4

Based on the campaign logs, here is the updated, highly detailed profile for Visenya Targaryen. This version integrates the House Baelarys lore, her "Apex" bloodline, and the secret cosmic weight she carries while traveling with Dunk.
Character Profile: Visenya Targaryen (The Uncrowned Queen)
 * True Identity: Daughter of Prince Maekar, older sister to Aegon "Egg" Targaryen.
 * Secret Heritage: She is the last scion of House Baelarys, a "Top 5" family of Old Valyria known as God-Mages or Apex Conductors. While the Targaryens were dragonlords, the Baelarys were the architects who bound them.
   * The Secret: Her blood is not just royal; it is "Apex." She possesses a preternatural, geometric perfection and magical resonance that makes standard Targaryens feel like "shadows" in her presence.
 * Age: 16.
 * Appearance: Possesses an ethereal, "ultraviolet" beauty. When unmasked, her silver-gold hair seems to generate its own light, and her violet eyes hold a "crystalline" or "surgical" clarity. She usually suppresses this "Aura of the Goddess" to pass as a mere mortal princess (or a squire).
Personality: The Sovereign & The Girl
Visenya suffers from a profound duality born of her power:
 * The Sovereign (The Apex):
   * Cold Geometry: She views the world as a series of equations. People are "variables," conversations are "conductions," and peace is a "structure" she must build. She is willing to be ruthless (the "Silver Butcher") to ensure stability.
   * The Silver Tongue: She doesn't just persuade; she rewrites reality. Her charisma is so high it functions like mind control ("Glibness"). She can gaslit kings and hands into believing her "White Lies" are their own ideas.
   * Cosmic Dread: She knows threats exist beyond the Wall (The Great Other) and in the East (R'hllor). She believes she must hollow out the world and unite it under her absolute rule to survive the coming "Long Night."
 * The Girl (The Ditchbond):
   * Desperate Humanity: Because she is an "Apex" being, she feels an agonizing distance from humanity. She craves simple, honest connections (the "Ditchbond") with people like Dunk because they anchor her to the earth.
   * Protective Love: She loves her family (Maekar, Baelor, Egg, Daeron) fiercely, but she protects them by manipulating them. She views herself as the "Furnace" that must burn so they can stay warm.
Background: The Baelarys Legacy
 * The Mother's Secret: Visenya’s mother was not a minor noble, but a survivor of House Baelarys who escaped the Doom with the secrets of the Fourteenth Flames. She died of grief/sickness, leaving Visenya as the sole inheritor of the "Apex" blood.
 * House Peake's Betrayal: The Peakes destroyed the last Baelarys enclave in Westeros. They did not understand the magic they were killing; they only feared the "silver-gold sickness" and wanted the land. Visenya has systematically destroyed them in retaliation (turning them into vassals).
 * The Family Dynamic:
   * Maekar (Father): Loves her but fears her. He calls her the "Sun" and himself the "Anvil." He knows she is dangerous.
   * Baelor (Uncle): Respects her intellect but fears she is a tyrant in the making ("A Goddess's Playground"). He suspects she is stripping the world of free will.
   * Aerion (Brother): His madness is a reaction to her inferiority. He senses her "Apex" status and lashes out because he knows he is just an echo of her fire.
   * Daeron (Brother): The "Drunkard" drinks to drown out his dragon dreams, but Visenya is the only one who comforts him. He is her most loyal shield.
Class: Level 6 Baelarys Weaver (Modified Swords Bard)
In this low-magic setting, her "spells" are flavored as Ancestral Resonance—manipulating the "marrow" of the world through voice and will, rather than flashy magic.
Key Stats:
 * INT: 20 (The Architect/Scholar).
 * CHA: 18 (The Silver Tongue).
 * DEX: 16 (Feline Grace).
Subclass Features (Flavor):
 * Blade Flourish: Flavored as "Geometric Combat." She doesn't just swing a sword; she calculates the trajectory of the opponent's blade and steps where it won't be.
 * Silver Tongue (Expertise in Persuasion): Her words carry a subsonic resonance. When she speaks, it feels like a law of nature.
 * Apex Presence (Intimidation/Command): She can drop her "Mortal Mask" to reveal her true nature, terrifying enemies into submission without drawing a weapon (mechanically: Command, Fear, or Hypnotic Pattern).
Spells (The "White Lie" Kit):
 * Glibness (Passive/Flavor): She is always convincing.
 * Detect Thoughts: "Royal Insight"—reading micro-expressions.
 * Suggestion: "The Voice of the Dragon"—people feel compelled to obey.
 * Mending/Prestidigitation: "The Polish"—keeping her gear (and Dunk's) unnaturally perfect.

Class Features (Detailed)
Level 1: Apex Blood
Your reflexes are not born of speed, but of calculation.
 * Geometric Defense: While wearing no armor or light armor, your AC equals 10 + DEX mod + INT mod.
 * The Architect’s Mind: You may use Intelligence instead of Charisma for Deception, Intimidation, and Persuasion checks.
Level 1: Geometric Reality (Spellcasting)
You do not "cast" spells; you conduct the math of the universe.
 * Modifier: Intelligence.
 * Constraint: You cannot learn spells that deal direct damage (Fireball, Lightning Bolt) or summon creatures until Level 16. Your magic is limited to Illusion, Enchantment, Divination, and Transmutation.
 * Ritual Casting: You can cast any spell you know as a ritual.
Level 2: The White Lie
You bend the truth until reality agrees with you.
 * You gain proficiency in Deception and Insight.
 * Subtle Influence: When you speak to a humanoid for at least 1 minute, you can force them to make a WIS save against your Spell Save DC. On a failure, they are charmed by you for 1 hour or until you attack them. They do not know they were charmed.
Level 3: Tradition - The Silver Song (Modified Swords College)
You adopt the fighting style of the High Valyrian duelists.
 * Bonus Proficiencies: Scimitar, Rapier.
 * Fighting Style: Dueling (+2 damage when wielding a melee weapon in one hand).
 * Calculated Flourish: Whenever you take the Attack action, you can expend one use of your Leverage Die to perform a flourish (Damage is Psychic, not slashing—you hurt their mind with your precision).
   * Defensive Geometry: Add the Leverage Die to your AC until the start of your next turn.
   * Surgical Lunge: Add the Leverage Die to the damage roll. You push the target 5 ft plus a number of feet equal to your INT score.
Level 5: Font of Calculation
You recover all expended Leverage Dice when you finish a short or long rest.
Level 6: Extra Attack
You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.
Level 10: Divine Leverage
Your ability to tilt the odds becomes supernatural.
 * When you fail an ability check, you can expend one Leverage Die and add it to the roll after seeing the result but before the DM declares success/failure.
 * Subtle Arts: You can cast spells without somatic or verbal components by spending 1 Leverage Die.
Level 11: The Silver Butcher
You stop holding back your physical precision.
 * When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can expend a spell slot to deal extra Psychic damage. The damage is 2d8 for a 1st-level slot, plus 1d8 for each slot level higher than 1st (max 5d8). This represents striking nerve clusters and mental break points.
Level 14: Sovereign’s Command
Your words are absolute laws.
 * You can cast Mass Suggestion once per long rest without expending a spell slot.
 * Creatures who fail the save against your Enchantment spells lose their immunity to being Charmed for the duration.
Level 16: The Seal Breaks (High Magic Unlocked)
The "Low Magic" restriction is lifted. You tap into the dormant Baelarys volcanoes.
 * Unlock Evocation: You may now learn offensive spells (e.g., Fireball, Sunbeam, Disintegrate, Meteor Swarm).
 * Retroactive Learning: You may swap up to 3 known spells immediately for offensive spells.
Level 18: The Audit (True Sight)
You see the wireframe of the world.
 * You gain Truesight out to 60 feet.
 * You can detect any shapeshifter or illusion instantly.
 * System Admin: As a reaction when a creature you see casts a spell, you can make an Intelligence check (DC 10 + Spell Level). On a success, the spell fails (Counterspell without a slot). You can do this INT mod times per long rest.
Level 20: The Architect (Capstone)
You are a living conduit of Old Valyria.
 * Intelligence Score: Increases by 4 (Max 24).
 * Reality Anchor: You are immune to being Charmed, Frightened, or Possessed.
 * Perfect Geometry: Once per turn, if you miss an attack roll, you can treat the d20 roll as a 20.
Epic Levels (21-30): The God-Mage

Level 21: Divine Leverage (+20 Cap)
Your proficiency bonus scales past mortal limits (+7). Your stats may now exceed 20.
 * Feature: Increase INT and DEX caps to 24.
Level 22: Stellar Ignition
 * You can cast Sunburst or Incendiary Cloud by expending 2 Leverage Dice instead of a spell slot. The damage type is Radiant-Force (Violet Starlight).
Level 24: Reality Edit
 * You can cast Wish (simulating 8th level spells or lower) without risk of losing the spell, once per 7 days.
 * You can rewrite a non-magical event that happened in the last hour (e.g., "The guard did not see us"). The DM decides the DC.
Level 25: The 14th Flame (Avatar State)
You can assume the form of a "Humanoid Dragon" for 1 minute.
 * Flight: 60ft (Wings of violet light).
 * Immunity: Fire and Psychic damage.
 * Breath Weapon: A cone of violet fire (10d10 Force damage).
Level 27: Absolute Conduction
 * Your Spell Save DC increases by +5.
 * Your weapon attacks ignore all resistances and immunities.
Level 28: Subsonic Audit
 * You can strip a creature of its Divinity/Magic. On a hit, you can force a CON save (DC 25). On a failure, the target loses all spellcasting abilities for 1 minute.
Level 30: God Mode (The Erasure)
As described in the user file.
 * Feature: Once per campaign (or DM discretion), you can attempt to "Delete" a concept or entity.
 * Mechanic: You make a Reality Check (d20 + INT + Proficiency + Divine Leverage).
 * DC: 40 (Gods), 50 (Primordial Forces).
 * Effect: On success, the target is "audited" out of existence. They do not die; they were never there. The logic of the universe fills the hole.
