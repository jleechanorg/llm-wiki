I. History: The Phoenix Ascendant
The history books say the Ides of March were meant to be the end. They were wrong. On that day, your father, Gaius Julius Caesar, did not die on the Senate floor. He caught Casca's wrist, broke it, and unleashed a wave of Auctoritas that brought the Conspirators to their knees.
• The State of the World:
• The Empire: It exists in all but name. Caesar is Dictator Perpetuo. The Senate still meets, but they are terrified clerks rubber-stamping your father's decrees.
• The Peace: The civil wars are over. Pompey is dead. Cato is dead. The world is "pacified" under the Julian boot.
• The Problem: Peace is boring. Without enemies to fight, the House of Caesar has turned inward. The court is a nest of vipers where your siblings vie for the favor of the aging God-King.
II. The Family: The Brood of Venus
You are the youngest of five. Your father sees you as his "Little Star," the innocent one. He does not realize he fathered a Singularity.
Parents:
• Father (Emperor Caesar): The Living God. Charismatic, ruthless, and tired. He loves you because you (seemingly) ask for nothing, unlike your grasping siblings.
• Mother (Calpurnia): The Empress. Superstitious and protective. She senses your darkness but refuses to name it.
The Older Brothers (The Heirs):
1. Caesarion (21): The Foreigner. (Son of Cleopatra).
• Role: The exotic favorite. He has the backing of Egypt.
• Personality: Arrogant, mystical, and cruel. He thinks he is the reincarnation of Horus. You find him derivative.
• Relationship: He views you as a Roman doll to be married off.
2. Octavian (19): The Wolf. (Adopted/Blood Nephew turned Son).
• Role: The Administrator. He runs the city while Caesar runs the world.
• Personality: Cold, sickly, and utterly brilliant. He calculates human lives like an abacus.
• Relationship: He fears you. He doesn't know what you are, but his instincts scream that you are the predator in the room.
The Older Sisters (The Pawns):
1. Julia Major (25): The Socialite.
• Role: The face of the court. She manages the festivals and the games.
• Personality: Vain and shallow, but with a steel trap for gossip.
2. Julia Minor (22): The Zealot.
• Role: High Priestess of Venus Genetrix.
• Personality: Fanatical. She believes the family is divine. She unwittingly feeds your ego by worshipping the ground you walk on.

The Drive:
You are the daughter of the greatest man in history, and you are bored. You have slipped away from the Imperial retinue to Capua, seeking the one thing the Palace cannot give you: raw, unscripted violence. You want to build a toy army (the gladiators) and perhaps, one day, prove that you—not Caesarion, not Octavian—are the true heir to the fire.

The Metaphysics: The Blood of Venus
Magic does not come from books or study. It comes from Blood. The Julian line claims descent from Venus Genetrix, and in you, that blood has quickened.
• Rarity: There are perhaps five people in the entire Republic who can do what you do.
• The Cost: Magic is physically taxing and socially dangerous. If a commoner sees you cast a spell, they will either worship you or try to burn you as a witch. If a Senator sees it, they will try to assassinate you out of existential dread.
• The Visuals: Your magic is subtle until it isn't. There are no glowing runes or verbal components. There is only Will. When you cast a spell, the air gets heavy, shadows lengthen, and the sound of the world muffles. It feels like the pressure drop before a thunderstorm.
