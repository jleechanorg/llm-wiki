# Aizen Sosuke Campaign — Summarized Arc

Long-form summary of the Aizen god-campaign covering the full ascension arc from Level 8 mortal to hidden master of all reality. Companion detail chats: [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-1|chat 1]], [[2026-08-04-aizen-god-campaign-chat-2|chat 2]], and the mortal-era chat. This page is the **spine document** for the lineage that also includes the [[CleanStateCampaignReset]] restarts, the Exiled Viper low-level rebuild, and the Architect of Destiny prequel.

## Act 1 — The Rise to Power

- Protagonist: [[AizenSosuke]], Level 8 Paladin/Bard, heir to noble [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]] of [[BaldursGate]]. Motivation is ambition and control, not heroism.
- Investigation of a shadow conspiracy reveals a resurgent [[CultOfTheDeadThree]] (Bane, Bhaal, Myrkul) destabilizing the city.
- The cult's masterminds are Aizen's own parents: Lord Volcain Sosuke (champion of [[Bane]]) and Lady Seraphina Sosuke (sorceress). Aizen kills his father (patricide) and breaks — rather than kills — his mother, forcing her fealty as his first major subordinate.
- Bane's avatar manifests to punish him; Aizen wins a battle of will, seizes and **consumes the divine portfolio of Tyranny** — apotheosis into a nascent God of Tyranny ([[Divine-Portfolio-Consumption]]).
- He later ambushes and consumes the avatar of [[Myrkul]], adding the Death portfolio and becoming a Greater Deity.

## Act 2 — The Great Deception

- The double deicide draws [[Ao-the-Overgod]]. The confrontation is **not combat**: Aizen wins a metaphysical debate ([[Conceptual-Debate-Victory]]), presenting a "superior" definition of cosmic balance — a single absolute will instead of eternal good/evil conflict. Ao, a being of pure law, accepts the theorem and is subsumed — "retired," not killed. Aizen becomes **The Absolute**.
- Bored by a perfected reality, Aizen starts a grand game with himself as secret director ([[The-Grand-Game]]).
- He elevates [[GaleOfWaterdeep]] to Greater Deity as the new God of Magic, then commands him to broadcast two claims across Faerûn: that Gale defeated and consumed [[Mystra]], and a second declaration (source truncated mid-sentence).
