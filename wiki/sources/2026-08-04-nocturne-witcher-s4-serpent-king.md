# Campaign Profile: Nocturne — Witcher Season 4 Campaign

**Source**: Campaign character/personality document "Nocturne Witcher season 4 Campaign" — a new entry in the Nocturne campaign line (cf. [[NocturneRavencrest]], [[NocturneSosuke]], and the Zhentarim "Silent Blade" variant), this time transplanted into [[TheWitcher]] setting during the Season 4 era, embedded with [[TheRats]] bandit gang.

## Character Profile

- **Name**: Nocturne
- **Age**: 16
- **Class**: Bard ([[CollegeOfSwords]])
- **Cover identity**: A grimy, sullen, forgettable brigand — deliberate use of dirt and withdrawn demeanor as a "mundane illusion" to conceal a conspicuous appearance that would otherwise draw attention (see [[MundaneIllusionDisguise]]).
- **Archetype**: [[SerpentKingArchetype]] — a philosopher-queen in waiting who views the world as "a flawed schematic in desperate need of an editor." The document explicitly composites four fictional villain templates: [[Griffith]] (transcendent ambition), [[TywinLannister]] (cold strategic pragmatism), [[SosukeAizen]] (intellectual/charismatic superiority), and [[JohanLiebert]] (philosophical nihilism).

## The Two Masks

1. **The Quiet Rat / Courteous Advisor** — default public interface: soft musical voice that forces listeners to lean in, flawless etiquette that feels wrong for a bandit, small kindnesses (first watch, mending Reef's strap, praising [[Mistle]]'s knife-work). Every gesture is framed as a **data probe** — social pressure applied to map another person's weaknesses and leverage points (see [[DataProbeSocialEngineering]]).
2. **The Dispassionate Strategist** — planning mask: presents multi-faceted "win-win" plans aligned with the Rats' stated goals (wealth, survival) while embedding a private unstated benefit (studying a magical anomaly, observing a psyche under pressure, testing enemy defenses). Allies are "scaffolding... destined to be dismantled the instant their utility expires."

## Core Drives

- **Personal Transcendence** — rejects worldly power (land, titles, armies = messy emotional variables) in favor of *portable, absolute power*: forbidden knowledge, magical sources, mastery of Chaos, with the end goal of "becoming the throne" — divinity as foundation of a self-authored reality (see [[PortableAbsolutePower]]). Continuous with the divine-ambition line in [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]] and the god-campaign sources.
- **Surgical Domination** — no desire for public rulership; the thrill is the single frictionless moment of imposed will ("a whispered suggestion that makes a hero kneel for a single heartbeat" beats a year on a throne). See [[SurgicalDomination]].
- **Supremacist Certainty** — regards allies and enemies with a scholar's detached amusement ("charming... playing with wooden blocks while you are designing the city"); patiently lets others exhaust flawed strategies before intervening.

## Setting Notes

The supporting cast is [[TheRats]] of Witcher canon — [[Giselher]] (leader), [[Mistle]], [[Kayleigh]], [[Reef]] are named directly. "Season 4" anchors the timeline to the Netflix adaptation era. Mechanically the campaign is a D&D 5e overlay (College of Swords Bard, Bardic magic as suggestion/compulsion) on Witcher fiction — consistent with the campaign line's established pattern of D&D-adapting other settings.

## Relation to the Nocturne Line

Third distinct Nocturne incarnation in the wiki (Ravencrest noble, Zhentarim Silent Blade, now Witcher Rat). Shared invariants across incarnations: masked true capability, manipulation-first play, transcendence-over-throne ambition, and a beautiful-but-concealed protagonist. This variant is the youngest and least martially specced — power projection is social/arcane rather than martial.
