# Campaign Module: The Velvet Cage (Descent Edition) — Nocturne Sosuke v5

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign noctune bg3 v5" — the v5 iteration of the [[NocturneSosuke]] Baldur's Gate 3 campaign line. System: D&D 5e (2024-ruleset compatible). Tone: survival horror / gothic noir / infernal politics. Setting: the ravaged beach at the [[BaldursGate]] Nautiloid crash site.

**Editorial note**: This archive summarizes the mechanical and plot content only. The source document contains sexualized physical descriptions of the 16-year-old protagonist (including references to prior exploitation under a handler named [[Zylra]]); those passages are deliberately not reproduced here.

## Premise: the Level 1 cage

Minutes before the campaign opens, Nocturne was a Level 6 conduit of the Abyss holding a pact with [[Malcanthet]], Queen of Succubi — sealed by inverting the [[CassalanterFamily]] pact ritual. Then the Nautiloid took her. The campaign starts at **Level 1**, with an explicit in-fiction justification: the illithid tadpole is actively *suppressing* the absorbed infernal energy, not erasing it. She is "a Level 6 entity contained in a Level 1 cage," and the campaign arc is about **breaking the dam** so the stolen power floods back — see [[TadpoleSuppressionNerf]].

## Key mechanics

- **[[MalcanthetsFavor]] (plot armor, levels 1–3)**: replaces standard death saves. At 0 HP she instead drops to 1 HP and becomes Incorporeal (Sanctuary-like) until her next turn, dissolving into violet smoke and reforming elsewhere. Once per long rest — "Malcanthet will not save you twice." Explicitly framed as the patron finding her *too entertaining to break this early*.
- **[[PredatorPresenceIntimidation]]**: the narrative hook for why hardened NPCs (Zevlor, Aradin) heed a physically unimposing 16-year-old — her presence reads as *wrong* (bird-staring-at-a-snake response); Intimidation is whispered, backed by the felt weight of the Abyss, not shouted.
- **Glass-cannon statline**: STR 8 / CON 8 / DEX 14 — delicate frame, fluid predatory grace; violet Sosuke-bloodline eyes now sparking pink/crimson from Malcanthet's residue fighting the parasite.
- **Document precedence rule**: the source states later sections override earlier ones on contradiction; it contains two "I. Character Profile" sections (Descent-state vessel profile, then a fuller pre-crash physical profile), so the later text governs where they conflict.

## Continuity

Continues the [[NocturneSosuke]] / [[house-sosuke|House Sosuke]] line: the Cassalanter pact inversion and Malcanthet binding carry forward from prior versions, now grafted onto the BG3 opening (tadpole, ravaged beach, Zevlor's tieflings, Aradin's mercenaries). Fits the recurring [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]]: the serpent-mastermind archetype ported into a survival-horror de-power frame where social dominance substitutes for lost levels.
