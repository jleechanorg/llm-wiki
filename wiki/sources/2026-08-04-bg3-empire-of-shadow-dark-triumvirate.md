# BG3 Campaign: Empire of Shadow (v0)

Campaign premise document for a post-Netherbrain [[BaldursGate]] where the saviors became tyrants. The Netherbrain is dead in the Chionthar, but the city "merely reorganized its chains": the [[TriumvirateOfTheAbsolutesBane]] — [[Astarion]] (Ascended vampire lord), [[Shadowheart]] (Sharran Mother Superior of Loss), and [[Gale-of-Waterdeep]] (pseudo-divine wearer of the reforged Crown of Karsus) — split the Sword Coast into empires of blood, shadow, and unbridled arcana. This is a [[PostVictoryDarkTimeline]] setup with a [[CompanionFateLedger]]: every companion receives a definitive, mostly tragic fate, giving the campaign a fixed grim world state rather than an open epilogue.

## The Triumvirate

- **[[Astarion]]** — L12+ Rogue (Thief)/Ranger (Gloom Stalker); completed the [[RiteOfProfaneAscension]]; rules from the rebuilt Szarr Palace with vampiric spawn replacing the Parliament of Peers. Gear: Bhaalist Armour, Crimson Mischief + Bloodthirst.
- **[[Shadowheart]]** — L12+ Cleric (Trickery)/Paladin (Oathbreaker); slaughtered the Nightsong, usurped the House of Grief, runs a Lower City theocracy of [[Shar]] where memories are extracted via the Mirror of Loss. Gear: Dark Justiciar Half-Plate, Viconia's Walking Fortress, Spear of Evening.
- **[[Gale-of-Waterdeep]]** — L12+ Wizard (Evocation)/Sorcerer (Wild Magic); reforged the [[CrownOfKarsus]] into pseudo-divinity; floats above the city in a citadel carved from Ramazith's Tower, using citizens as test subjects for Netherese weaving. Gear: Robe of the Weave, Markoheshkir.

## Fates of the other companions

- **[[Laezel]]** (The Astral Scourge) — rejected Vlaakith; wages a suicidal Astral Sea guerrilla war against the Lich-Queen and Illithid remnants with the Silver Sword of the Astral Plane and Helldusk Armour.
- **[[Wyll]]** (The Broken Blade) — failed to break his pact; his father died to Astarion's political maneuvering; trapped in Avernus as Mizora's attack dog, soul calcifying into a Lemure.
- **[[Karlach]]** (The Mind Flayer Rebel) — became Illithid to defeat the brain; despised by the city, hides in the sewers eating the brains of Astarion's rogue spawn to survive.
- **[[Minthara]]** (The High General) — Astarion's military enforcer commanding the "Night Watch" legion of spawn and Drow; Armour of Persistence, Mace of the Believer.
- **[[Halsin]]** (The Shadow-Touched) — broken by Sharran corruption of Thaniel and the wilderness; roams the Cloakwood as a rabid shadow-touched beast.
- **[[Jaheira]]** (The Risen Martyr) — tried to rally the Harpers, was ambushed by Astarion and Minthara, slain, and raised as a mindless vampire spawn serving drinks at Astarion's galas.
- **[[Minsc]]** (The Stone Sentinel) — petrified by Gale for being "disruptive to the arcane equilibrium"; stands as a garden ornament beneath the floating tower.
- **[[Withers]]** (Jergal) — disgusted, abandoned the party; records the Sword Coast's collapse in the Book of the Dead from a forgotten tomb.
- **[[Raphael]]** — slain in the House of Hope, but his demonic essence is slowly reforming in the lowest pits of the Nine Hells (source text cuts off mid-sentence here).

## Relationship to other wiki campaigns

This is a distinct continuity from the [[AizenSosuke|Aizen]] god-campaign lineage (mortal → godhood → prequel chats), which also uses Baldur's Gate but with House Sosuke / Dead Three factions. Both share the pattern of Baldur's Gate as a stage for villain-protagonist power consolidation, consistent with the player's documented campaign preferences (manipulation, moral corruption, mastermind arcs).
