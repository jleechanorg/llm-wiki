# Campaign Bible: The Winter Web — A Blood Dragon's Vacation (campaign visenya v6)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Campaign Visenya v6" (Part 1, Sections 1-5) — the latest revision in the Visenya campaign line, repositioning [[VisenyaTargaryen]] as a **14-year-old Level 6 prodigy** vacationing incognito in a stabilized-but-resentful North. Distinct alt-history frame from v2-v4: here Prince [[RhaegarTargaryen]] killed Robert ("the Usurper") at the Trident seventeen years ago, the Targaryen dynasty stands, and the "Forever War" has cooled into a continent-wide cold war.

## Premise (Section 1)

- Princess Visenya Targaryen, the "Baelarys anomaly" ([[HouseBaelarys]]), Level 6 [[ApexWeaver]], known to terrified southern smallfolk as the **"Blood Dragon"** for clinically executing thousands of bandits and syndicates.
- She has left her post as **Lord Commander of the [[Blackguard]]** to travel the North under the pseudonym **"Lady Elyse Celtigar"**.
- Stated goal: not diplomacy with the Starks but to "mathematically map their resentments, vivisect their loyalties, and see how far you can bend the Warden of the North before he breaks."
- **The Hook — power disparity as the fun**: "You are a godling wandering through a sandbox of fragile, low-level mortals" surrounded by oblivious teenagers like [[RobbStark]] and [[JonSnow]]. "You are not saving the North; you are deciding whether it is mathematically useful enough to exist."

## Personality (Section 2)

- **Archetype**: The Bored Godling & The Vivisector (ENTJ) — creates chaos to feel the "spark" of genuine emotion (usually fear or pain) in others.
- **Signature habits**: unearthly posture, frictionless movement, absentmindedly traces geometric shapes into surfaces when bored; the air around her drops in temperature and smells of ozone and mint.
- **The Masks**: Public face "Lady Elyse" — polite, refined, slightly haughty, physically fragile (STR 8). True nature "The Blood Dragon" — cold sadistic calculator who views people as "dolls," radiating a subsonic dread aura via her Baelarys blood.
- **Core compulsion — Solving the Equation**, mechanized as the [[EntropyToll]]: witnessing a high-status NPC lie or assert false dominance without dismantling them within the scene costs 1 Exhaustion from boredom; shattering their ego grants Temporary HP equal to her CHA modifier.
- Inner-monologue samples target Robb's direwolf, seeding Catelyn against Jon ("Jon looks more Targaryen than Stark"), and the line "They think I am a guest. I am the architect of their winter."

## Class (Section 3)

- **Level 6 Apex Weaver (Bard/Mastermind Gestalt)** — power sourced from [[InformationGeometry]]: perceiving stress lines, pulse rates, and the mathematical architecture of the physical and social world (not magical songs).
- **Unique mechanic — [[StaticField]]**: passively drops room temperature by 20°; permanent Advantage on Intimidation and Insight; Disadvantage on Stealth unless magically muffled; lower-level enemies auto-fail insight checks against her lies.
- **Progression (Levels 1-30)**:
  - Tier 1 "The Cub" (1-5, unlocked): Silver Tongue (minimum roll 10 on Deception/Persuasion), Psychic Vivisection ([[SadismDice]] subtract from enemy saves), basic Information Geometry spellcasting.
  - Tier 2 "The Hunter" (6-10, current): Summon the Alpha (manifest invisible spirit dragon **Entropy** as a physical Large Drake), Aura of the Architect (add CHA mod to all saves); Permanent Steed at level 10.
