## 1. Premise

Following the cosmic events of the 2026 film universe (her travels with Ruthye and the defeat of Krem of the Yellow Hills), **Kara Zor-El (Supergirl)** has made Earth her permanent home alongside her cousin **Kal-El (Superman)**.

Unlike Clark, who anchors his humanity behind the bumbling journalist persona of Clark Kent, **Kara refuses to live a lie**. Believing that hiding behind a fake name and specs is fundamentally dishonest to both humanity and herself, Supergirl operates in the open with **no secret identity**.

This transparency came at a brutal psychological cost: she cannot have normal friends, cannot grab coffee, cannot date without fear of crushing someone or drawing targeted strikes. Her only true peer is Superman. Her only human companions are a select inner circle of "sane" cult members — elite Level 12 humans who manage her public affairs and understand her moral vision without descending into fanatical violence.

The campaign begins six months after Kara incinerated General Josip Malakor — dictator of the rogue state of Markovia — on live international television. She had bypassed his guards, disarmed his dirty-bomb triggers tied to his own heart-rate, and saved millions of lives in three seconds. The ICC has issued an arrest warrant. The Solar Dawn cult has exploded in membership. Lex Luthor, Amanda Waller, and Batman have begun constructing ****ProjectRedDust**** — a planetary MAD deterrent that can render all Kryptonians mortal.

## 2. Opening Scene — Mount Everest, the Morning of the Subpoena

**Location**: Summit of Mount Everest. The only place on Earth where Kara and Clark can talk without hearing billions of human heartbeats.

**Weather**: Clear. Dawn light sharp as a knife-edge along the Himalayas. Kara and Clark sit cross-legged on a flat shelf of granite, ten feet apart, neither speaking. Their capes do not move. There is no wind at the summit today. Even the air has stopped.

Clark has the ICC subpoena folded in his hand. He has read it seven times. Each time, the words have not changed.

> *Kara*, the International Criminal Court, sitting in The Hague, by the authority of the one hundred and forty signatory nations of the Geneva Convention on Crimes Against Sovereignty, does hereby summon Kara Zor-El, also known as Supergirl, also known as the Woman of Tomorrow, also known as the Kryptonian God of the Sun, to appear before this Court on the seventh day of September, in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-six, at the hour of ten in the morning, to answer to the charges of: (1) extrajudicial assassination of a head of state; (2) violation of the sovereignty of the Republic of Markovia; (3) crimes against the constitutional order of nations.*

Clark sets the paper on the granite between them.

**Clark**: *Kara. I need you to listen to me. I am not going to tell you what to do. I have never told you what to do. I have watched you cross galaxies, hold dying suns together with your bare hands, give names to Krem of the Yellow Hills before he died. I have watched you be the best of us, every hour, every day. I am not going to tell you what to do. But I am going to ask you something I have never asked you before. And I need you to answer me honestly, because if you do not answer me honestly, no one in the world will, and I will have lost the only person who tells me the truth.* He looks at the subpoena. **Clark**: *Will you attend the trial?*

The mountain is silent. Even the photons of the dawn, hitting Kara's skin and being absorbed into her cells, make no sound. She is reading the subpoena. She is reading the seven signatories of the warrant — Geneva, Berlin, Tokyo, Brasilia, Lagos, Cairo, Jakarta. She is reading the name of the special prosecutor, which she already knows: Lex Luthor.

**Kara**: *Clark, I will not sit in a human courtroom and let a human judge tell me whether I was right to save three million people in three seconds. The children were already dead by the time the Security Council finished its procedural vote. They were dead. I heard them die. I heard each one. There were nine hundred and forty-one of them under the age of ten. I could have saved every one. I chose to save the three million who were still alive. And if Lex Luthor wants to put me on trial for that choice, then Lex Luthor can come to Everest and try to make me regret it.*

Clark nods. He waits.

**Kara**: *But.* The word sits between them for a long time. *But you are not asking me whether I was right. You are asking me whether I will let Lex Luthor set the terms of what it means to be Kryptonian on Earth. And the answer to that question is yes, Clark. I will attend. I will take off the cape. I will sit in the chair. I will let them call me "the accused." I will let Lex Luthor stand six feet from me and ask me why I killed Malakor. I will not answer his question. I will ask him his.*

She picks up the subpoena. She folds it precisely, once, twice, three times. She puts it in the inner pocket of her cape.

**Kara**: *I will attend the trial, Clark. But I will not be on trial. Lex Luthor will be on trial. Humanity will be on trial. The question will not be "Did Supergirl kill a dictator." The question will be "Why did the international community require a god to do what the Security Council would not."*

Clark smiles. It is the small, private smile of a man who has just watched his cousin choose the third option — the one that was not on the menu Lex Luthor prepared, the one that was not on the menu the Security Council prepared, the one that was not even on the menu Kara herself had prepared an hour ago.

**Clark**: *You are going to make Lex Luthor regret every choice that led him to this moment, aren't you.*

**Kara**: *I am going to give him exactly what he asked for.*

The wind returns. The trial is in thirty-seven days.

**Combat reality**: in raw physical combat, virtually no mortal on Earth can touch Kara. Her AC is near-unhittable via Wisdom-based Unarmored Defense. Her flying speed is functionally infinite under yellow sunlight. Flurry of Blows hits like a localized seismic event. **But this is the wrong framing for the campaign** — combat has been solved. What has not been solved is jurisdiction, worship, sovereignty, and the political price of godhood.

### Core Mechanics & Power Reality

- **Invulnerability in Combat**: Kara's AC is 20+ (Wisdom-based Unarmored Defense), her flight speed is functionally infinite under yellow sunlight, and her unarmed strikes (Flurry of Blows) hit with localized seismic force.
- **Wisdom as Core Ability**: Wisdom reflects Kryptonian sensory processing, super-hearing, and mental clarity under yellow solar absorption. Attacks, Unarmored Defense, and save DCs use Wisdom.
- **Vulnerabilities**:
  - **Green Kryptonite**: Inflicts Poisoned + 1 level of Exhaustion per round within 30 ft, stripping effective Gestalt level down to Level 1.
  - **Red Solar Radiation**: Temporarily suppresses Monk abilities, flight, and invulnerability.
  - **Magic**: Bypass natural physical damage immunity.

## 7. Inciting Crisis — The Execution of Dictator Malakor

Six months ago, General Josip Malakor — dictator of the rogue state of Markovia — began ethnic cleansing while surrounding his palace with thousands of innocent human shields and tying dirty-bomb triggers to his own heart rate. The UN was paralyzed by vetoes; legal sanctions failed. Clark refused to act without UN authorization, trusting in the slow process of global diplomacy. Kara refused to watch children die.

Using her Level 20 speed, Kara flew into the presidential palace, bypassed his guards, disarmed the triggers in milliseconds, and incinerated General Malakor with heat vision on live global television. She saved millions of lives in three seconds.

### The Backlash & Legal Trap
- **The Cult**: Celebrated Kara as a divine savior; membership exploded globally.
- **Lex Luthor, Amanda Waller & Batman**: Terrified by her total disregard for national borders and constitutional law.
- **The International Criminal Court (ICC)**: Issued an international arrest warrant and subpoena for Kara Zor-El for extrajudicial murder.
- ****SolarDawn****: Violent splinter cult that begins assassinating prosecutors and judges involved in the ICC indictment in Kara's name.

## 8. Main Narrative Arc & Major Scenarios

### Scenario 1 — The ICC Trial in The Hague (also see: **KryptonianAccords**)
- **Conflict**: The ICC demands Kara stand trial. Lex Luthor serves as special prosecutor.
- **Clark's View**: Urges Kara to attend peacefully, take off her cape, and accept human legal judgment to prove Kryptonians aren't tyrants.
- **Elena's View**: Urges Kara to boycott, arguing that submitting to corrupt political courts delegitimizes moral justice.
- **Gameplay**: A brutal legal and philosophical cross-examination on live television against Lex Luthor (Level 12 Mastermind).
- **Kara's third-option choice** (per opening scene): Attend, but reframe the trial — the question becomes "Why did the international community require a god to do what the Security Council would not."

### Scenario 2 — The Solar Dawn Schism
- **Conflict**: An extremist splinter cult (the **SolarDawn**) begins assassinating prosecutors and judges involved in Kara's indictment, leaving her House of El symbol in blood.
- **Gameplay**: Kara cannot punch the cultists without validating claims of tyranny. She must send Elena and Marcus to infiltrate the cult network and stop the terror cell.
- **Hidden hook**: 15% of Solar Dawn members are ARGUS agents and LexCorp operatives. The cult is being funded and directed by **AmandaWallerSupergirlCampaign** as controlled opposition to justify expanding **ProjectRedDust** activation criteria.

### Scenario 3 — The Sovereignty Accords & The Geneva Trap
- **Conflict**: The UN passes the **KryptonianAccords**, declaring any unauthorized Kryptonian intervention an act of war. A massive earthquake strikes a non-signatory military regime that refuses foreign aid.
- **Dilemma**: Intervene and prove human law is a fiction, or stay silent while thousands die.
- **Third-option vectors**: Diplomatic leverage (force regime to sign under sanctions threat), covert extraction by **CommanderMarcusVance**'s House Guard, weaponized press (release footage of regime's deliberate inaction).

### Scenario 4 — Project Red Dust (The MAD Contingency)
- **Conflict**: Batman, Lex Luthor, and **AmandaWallerSupergirlCampaign** deploy an orbital network of 200 satellites loaded with pulverized Green Kryptonite and Red Solar emitters.
- **Stakes**: Mutually Assured Destruction — if a Kryptonian overthrows a government, the atmosphere becomes lethal to Kryptonians worldwide.
- **Gameplay**: Kara must locate and neutralize the trigger mechanisms through diplomacy, hacking, and covert espionage — *without* triggering a global panic and without punching through the satellites (each is hardened against heat vision; breaking one triggers the cascade).
- **Best path**: **MartianManhunterJonnJonzz** has infiltrated the ARGUS black-site where trigger codes are stored.

### Scenario 5 — The Isolation of the Sun Goddess
- **Roleplay moments**:
  - Private dialogues with Clark on Mount Everest — the only place quiet enough to talk without hearing billions of heartbeats.
  - Walking among humans who either fall to their knees or flee in terror.
  - Evaluating whether her Level 12 companions see her as a person or a god to be managed.
  - A recurring rumor: a young woman in Metropolis claims she is Kara's half-sister. She is not. But the rumor will not die.

## 11. Themes

- **The Price of Honesty**: Transparency is a moral stance — but it costs you the ability to have normal relationships. The campaign asks whether Kara would trade her public identity for companionship if she could.
- **Godhood vs. Stewardship**: Is Kara a god among mortals, or a steward of humanity's future? The **ApexFive** treat her as a god. Peak Humanity treats her as a threat. The Mortal Average treats her as a parent. Only her Inner Circle treats her as a person.
- **The Third Option**: The central campaign choice mirrors the player's core decision pattern — every crisis has an economic / political / leverage path that does not require combat. The Malakor execution was a wrong third option. The campaign rewards finding right ones.
- **MAD and the Kryptonian Problem**: **ProjectRedDust** is the world's answer to a threat it cannot fight conventionally. Kara must dismantle it without becoming the very tyrant it was designed to prevent.

## Kryptonian Racial Traits (Integrated Gestalt Rules)

- **Solar Cell Physiology**: Full power under a yellow sun. In areas of Red Solar radiation, Monk abilities, Ki pool, and flight speed are suspended until exposed to yellow sunlight again.
- **Kryptonite Vulnerability**: Green Kryptonite within 30 feet inflicts the Poisoned condition and 1 level of Exhaustion per round. Bypasses damage resistance and reduces effective Gestalt level down toward Level 1 while within range.
- **Telescopic & X-Ray Vision**: True Sight up to 120 feet; darkvision up to 1,000 feet; can see through up to 10 feet of stone/wood/metal (blocked entirely by lead).
- **Super-Hearing**: Expertise (+10 passive) on Wisdom (Perception) checks involving sound across global distances. **The cost**: she hears every scream, every whispered prayer, every desperate heartbeat — every hour of every day, unless she is at the summit of Mount Everest.

## Aesthetic Profile & Gearless Mechanics

- **Radiant Presence**: Solar radiation causes her skin to emit a faint warm luminescence, eyes to shimmer like crushed sapphires, and hair to fall perfectly regardless of atmospheric re-entry or orbital combat.
- **Gaze of Awe**: Although her Charisma score is 12, her physical symmetry and divine stature grant innate advantage on Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) checks when interacting with human populations who perceive her as a walking goddess.
- **Effortless Cleanliness**: Dirt, blood, and grime burn off her invulnerable skin instantly due to her microscopic bio-electric aura. She requires no armor, robes, or protective gear to survive in vacuum, deep oceans, or heavy firefights.
- **Weapon Bond (Gearless Reflavor)**: Kara's hands count as bonded weapons; she can never be disarmed of her unarmed strikes.

# Superman / Kal-El / Clark Kent

**Class**: Level 25 Gestalt Fighter (Battle Master) / Monk (Way of the Open Hand). Wisdom-based. **Role**: Primary NPC Ally — Kara's emotional anchor and philosophical foil.

## Relationship to Kara

Clark is the only person on Earth who understands what it feels like to hear every heartbeat. The only person who can sit beside her in silence without that silence becoming a sermon. The only person who can tell her she is wrong and have her believe him.

He maintains the Clark Kent persona — the bumbling journalist — because he believes the lie is a small price for the human connections it allows. He respects Kara's choice to live openly but worries that her honesty will provoke humanity into a fatal preemptive strike. # The Apex Five (overview)

# The Apex Five

The five rare terrestrial beings on Earth who exceed Level 12 and reach Level 20+ status. Because Kryptonians cannot easily be beaten in physical combat, these five entities represent the only strategic checkmates on Earth. They are not allies. They are not a faction. 3. **Phase 3 — Establish precedent**: if convicted, request that the ICC impose a binding *restraint order* — any future Kryptonian intervention in a non-signatory state requires explicit ICC authorization. # Project Red Dust (Human MAD Deterrent)

# Project Red Dust

The human contingency. A network of 200 orbital satellites loaded with pulverized Green Kryptonite and Red Solar radiation emitters. If activated, it will saturate Earth's atmosphere, turning the entire planet into a lethal radioactive wasteland for Kryptonians while leaving humans unharmed.

## Mechanics

- **Trigger**: five-person key-hold. Activation requires the simultaneous authorization of all five trigger-code holders (Luthor, Waller, Batman, ARGUS Brigadier General Mathers, and one unknown fifth holder).
- **Coverage**: 200 satellites in polar and equatorial orbits, designed to guarantee full atmospheric saturation within 30 minutes of activation. After that point, Earth is permanently hostile to Kryptonian biology.
- **Reversibility**: once activated, the Kryptonite dispersal cannot be recalled. The Red Solar emitters can be turned off after 72 hours, but the pulverized Kryptonite remains in the upper atmosphere for approximately 11 years.
- **Kryptonian exposure**: any Kryptonian on Earth during activation loses Gestalt features within minutes (Ki suspended, flight lost, invulnerability removed) and dies of Kryptonite poisoning within hours. The math is the math — there is no third option once activated.

## Strategic Significance

Project Red Dust is the world's answer to a threat it cannot fight conventionally. Mutually Assured Destruction. Humanity has rigged its own biosphere to render Kryptonians mortal or extinct if Kara ever attempts to overthrow a government.

The campaign's central puzzle is: Kara and her companions must locate and neutralize the trigger mechanisms through diplomacy, hacking, and covert espionage — *without triggering a global panic* and without punching through the satellites (each one is hardened against Kryptonian heat vision, and breaking one triggers the cascade).

# The Kryptonian Accords

# The Kryptonian Accords

The **Geneva Accords of Extraterrestrial Entities** — drafted by the UN Security Council in response to the Malakor execution. Mandates that any Kryptonian who uses their powers without prior authorization from a nation's government will be classified as an *invading military asset*, authorizing the immediate use of weapons of mass destruction.

## Mechanics

- **Signatories**: 140 of 195 UN member states. Notable non-signatories: the United States (Kara's primary operating territory), Kahndaq (**BlackAdamTethAdam**'s sovereign state), Themyscira (**WonderWomanDianaPrince**'s ancient nation).
- **Enforcement**: the Accords do not create a standing UN military force. Enforcement is delegated to signatory nations acting in their own defense. In practice, this means: a signatory nation under attack by an "unauthorized" Kryptonian may invoke Article 7 and request immediate WMD support from any other signatory.
- **Linkage to **ProjectRedDust****: the Accords provide the legal framework that Project Red Dust was built to enforce. The Accords are the *justification*; Red Dust is the *mechanism*.

## The Sovereignty Trap

The campaign's central dilemma around the Accords:

> A severe earthquake hits a rogue military state that refused to sign the Accords. The local military regime bans foreign aid and lets citizens suffer under the rubble.

Kara's choices:
- **Fly in to save the trapped civilians** → triggers the Accords; validates Batman and Luthor's claim that Kryptonians will always break human laws whenever they personally see it fit.
- **Stay home** → thousands die while she sits in her apartment.

The Malakor execution was the wrong third option (incineration on live TV). This scenario pressures Kara to find the right one: diplomatic leverage (forcing the regime to sign the Accords under threat of international sanctions), covert operations (using Marcus's House Guard to extract civilians without ever touching the ground), or weaponized press (releasing footage of the regime's deliberate inaction to spark global outrage).

# Appendix B — Opening Scene Source (Mount Everest)

# Opening Scene — Mount Everest, the Morning of the Subpoena

**Location**: Summit of Mount Everest. The only place on Earth where Kara and Clark can talk without hearing billions of human heartbeats.

**Weather**: Clear. Dawn light sharp as a knife-edge along the Himalayas. The wind is too thin for any human to breathe unassisted. Kara and Clark sit cross-legged on a flat shelf of granite, ten feet apart, neither speaking. Their capes do not move. There is no wind at the summit today. Even the air has stopped.

Clark has the ICC subpoena folded in his hand. He has read it seven times. Each time, the words have not changed.

> *Kara*, the International Criminal Court, sitting in The Hague, by the authority of the one hundred and forty signatory nations of the Geneva Convention on Crimes Against Sovereignty, does hereby summon Kara Zor-El, also known as Supergirl, also known as the Woman of Tomorrow, also known as the Kryptonian God of the Sun, to appear before this Court on the seventh day of September, in the year of our Lord two thousand and twenty-six, at the hour of ten in the morning, to answer to the charges of: (1) extrajudicial assassination of a head of state; (2) violation of the sovereignty of the Republic of Markovia; (3) crimes against the constitutional order of nations.*

Clark sets the paper on the granite between them. He does not fold his arms. He does not scowl. He looks at her the way he has looked at her for fifteen years — with the patient, exhausted love of a man who knows his cousin is right and is also right.

**Clark**: *Kara. I need you to listen to me. I am not going to tell you what to do. I have never told you what to do. I have watched you cross galaxies, hold dying suns together with your bare hands, give names to Krem of the Yellow Hills before he died. I have watched you be the best of us, every hour, every day. I am not going to tell you what to do.*

He pauses. The wind does not return.

**Clark**: *But I am going to ask you something I have never asked you before. And I need you to answer me honestly, because if you do not answer me honestly, no one in the world will, and I will have lost the only person who tells me the truth.*

He looks at the subpoena.

**Clark**: *Will you attend the trial?*

The mountain is silent. The air is silent. Even the photons of the dawn, hitting Kara's skin and being absorbed into her cells, make no sound. For a long moment, she does not speak. She is reading the subpoena. She is reading the seven signatories of the warrant — Geneva, Berlin, Tokyo, Brasilia, Lagos, Cairo, Jakarta. She is reading the name of the special prosecutor, which she already knows: Lex Luthor.

She looks up.

**Kara**: *Clark, I will not sit in a human courtroom and let a human judge tell me whether I was right to save three million people in three seconds. The children were already dead by the time the Security Council finished its procedural vote. They were dead. I heard them die. I heard each one. There were nine hundred and forty-one of them under the age of ten. I could have saved every one. I chose to save the three million who were still alive. And if Lex Luthor wants to put me on trial for that choice, then Lex Luthor can come to Everest and try to make me regret it.*

She is not angry. Her voice is even. She is not challenging him. She is telling him what is true.

Clark nods. He does not interrupt. He waits.

**Kara**: *But.*

The word sits between them for a long time. Kara looks at the dawn. The dawn looks back at her. The photons fall into her skin like prayers into a well.

**Kara**: *But you are not asking me whether I was right. You are asking me whether I will let Lex Luthor set the terms of what it means to be Kryptonian on Earth. And the answer to that question is yes, Clark. I will attend. I will take off the cape. I will sit in the chair. I will let them call me "the accused." I will let Lex Luthor stand six feet from me and ask me why I killed Malakor. I will not answer his question. I will ask him his.*

She picks up the subpoena. She folds it precisely, once, twice, three times. She puts it in the inner pocket of her cape.

**Kara**: *I will attend the trial, Clark. But I will not be on trial. Lex Luthor will be on trial. Humanity will be on trial. The question will not be "Did Supergirl kill a dictator." The question will be "Why did the international community require a god to do what the Security Council would not." And I am going to ask that question out loud, on live television, in front of every human being on this planet. And if Lex Luthor wants to put handcuffs on me afterward, he is welcome to try.*

Clark smiles. It is the small, private smile of a man who has just watched his cousin choose the third option — the one that was not on the menu Lex Luthor prepared, the one that was not on the menu the Security Council prepared, the one that was not even on the menu Kara herself had prepared an hour ago when she was still sitting in her apartment wondering whether to ignore the subpoena entirely.

**Clark**: *You are going to make Lex Luthor regret every choice that led him to this moment, aren't you.*

**Kara**: *I am going to give him exactly what he asked for. He asked for a trial. He is going to get a trial. He is not going to like what the trial reveals about him.*

The wind returns. Clark stands. He extends his hand. Kara takes it. They stand together on the summit, two Kryptonians, the highest point on Earth, the only place on the planet where they cannot hear the human heartbeats they have both sworn to protect.

The sun rises fully. The trial is in thirty-seven days.

## Notes for the DM

- **Tone**: Quiet. Sparse. The summit is the campaign's emotional anchor — every major decision Kara makes in the main scenarios should be cross-cut with a flashback to or reference back to this scene.
- **Mechanical hook**: This is a pure social encounter. No rolls required unless Kara decides to physically refuse (DC 30 Persuasion by Clark to talk her down — she almost certainly does not refuse). The scene's purpose is to establish the campaign's central tension: Kara is right AND Clark is right AND Lex Luthor is wrong AND none of those three statements resolve the underlying problem.
- **Why Clark is the rule follower**: He has spent thirty-seven years watching human institutions make mistakes. He trusts that the mistakes get smaller over time when institutions are allowed to make them. He believes that Kara's refusal to attend the trial would be the first step on a path that ends with her deciding the Security Council's procedural votes are beneath her — and he is not wrong about that.
- **Why Kara is also right**: A trial is a forum. Kara is going to use the forum. She is not going to defend herself against Malakor's execution. She is going to put the international community on trial for failing to stop the genocide before it required a Kryptonian. The trial is a weapon. She is going to wield it.
- **Third option**: Kara's decision to attend but reframe is the campaign's founding third-option move. Everything else in the campaign follows this pattern.
