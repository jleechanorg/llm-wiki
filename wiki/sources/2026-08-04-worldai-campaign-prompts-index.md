# WorldAI Campaign Prompts — master index document

**Source**: "WorldAI campaign prompts" — a working index/scratchpad of campaign system prompts for WorldArchitect.AI play. It lists the current [[NocturneRavencrest|Nocturne]] campaign line (personality prompt, campaign summary prompt, "campaign bg1 nocturne", "Nocturne - merc serial killer", "Nocturne Witcher season 4 Campaign", "Campaign military prosecutor Doberman") and carries two fully-written prompts under "misc": the Shadowheart campaign and the Gladius merc-brute solo campaign with a full homebrew class writeup.

## Prompt 1 — Shadowheart campaign (post-BG3 Sharran leader)

Play as [[Shadowheart]] after the events of BG3, in the timeline where **she sided with [[Shar]]** and now leads the Sharran sect at level 12 — a **Paladin 5 / Death Cleric 7** multiclass with best-in-slot gear from the video game. The prompt mandates:

- Guided **character creation** at session start
- **Plot-arc choices offered without full detail or spoilers**
- Player goals: amass **power, wealth, influence, networks, friends, companions** — the recurring accumulation-goal signature across this player's campaigns (cf. [[MaskedPowerSmurfing]], the Gladius prompt below)

## Prompt 2 — Merc solo campaign: Gladius the brute

Setting: [[BaldursGate]] after BG3 (same post-game slot as [[NocturneSilentBlade|The Silent Blade]] and the [[SosukeAizen|Sanguine Architecture]] line). Character: **[[Gladius]]**, an 18-year-old male mercenary — very physically attractive but hides it, exceptional physique/coordination/health, **"very low int and wisdom"**, resolves everything through combat or brute force.

- Personality directive: **evil ESFP, ultimate hedonist** — wealth, women, food, alcohol, social domination; **secretly abusive, enjoys dominating powerful people** (to be worked into the narrative). This is a deliberate archetype inversion of the campaign line's usual INTJ serpent-mastermind (see the campaign-preferences profile) — brute-force ESFP instead of manipulation-INTJ, but identical end goals (power + wealth).
- **[[TimeSkipConsent|Consent-gated time skips]]**: the engine proposes one-year time skips at sensible narrative points but must get explicit player agreement first, and must simulate the character's autonomous off-screen activity during the skip.
- Encounter density: plenty of BG3 and D&D lore characters.
- Rules: **default "destiny ruleset"** with D&D 5e as fallback for anything undefined ([[DestinyRuleset]]).
- Start: level 5 paladin using the custom class below.

## Homebrew class — Oath of Self-Perfection (The Apex Predator)

A godless paladin oath sworn **to the self**: self-maximization as the sacred principle (kin to [[ApexWeaver]] and the [[ImperatorClass]] in this wiki's homebrew family). Full L5 kit ([[OathOfSelfPerfection]]):

| Feature | Replaces | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| Fighting Style (Battle-Hardened) | — | +1 Defense in armor |
| Extra Attack | — | standard martial 2-attack |
| Perfected Strike | Divine Smite | EP-fueled force damage: 2d8 for 5 EP, +1d8 per extra 2 EP, max 5d8 |
| Aura of Unyielding Dominance | Aura of Protection | 10-ft aura: advantage vs Frightened/Charmed; enemies get disadvantage resisting your Intimidation |
| Apex Senses | Divine Sense | action: advantage on Perception/Investigation + pinpoint hidden/invisible within 30 ft; 1+INT-mod uses per long rest |
| Willpower Infusion | Lay on Hands | pool = 5 × level (25 at L5); 1 pt = 5 HP heal; 5 pts = remove one Fatigue level |

**Core aptitude**: Intelligence — **EP pool = Intelligence score × 2**. The document cuts off mid-sentence in Willpower Infusion's Primary Action option.

## Internal tensions (recorded as contradictions)

1. The class is titled **"Nocturne's Class"** but is attached to the **Gladius** prompt — likely copy-forwarded from a Nocturne campaign document.
2. The class keys everything to **Intelligence** (EP pool, Apex Senses uses) while Gladius is explicitly built with **"very low int and wisdom"** — as written, the brute would have a tiny EP pool and near-zero Apex Senses uses.
