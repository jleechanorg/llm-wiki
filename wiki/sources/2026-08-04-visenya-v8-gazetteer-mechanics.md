# Campaign Visenya v8 — Section 8: Gazetteer and Mechanics (Continuation)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign visenya v8" — a continuation section of the Visenya campaign line ([[VisenyaBelaerys]]), providing a location gazetteer entry, two custom subsystems, and a loot table. Set in the Dance of the Dragons era (references to the Black and Green councils, Prince [[JacaerysVelaryon|Jacaerys]]'s investigators, and "Relics of the Sowing").

## Location: The Nightfort Ruins (The Wall)

- **Vibe**: Ancient oppressive cathedral of fractured grey granite and weeping black ice beneath the Wall's shadow; air at "absolute zero" tasting of frozen pine-rot; tectonic silence broken by polar winds through bleeding weirwood roots.
- **Sub-locations**: The Cook's Hole, The Gate of the Black Gate, The Lord's Solar, The Ice-Cells.
- **Hazard — *The Primordial Cold***: the courtyard carries the Old Gods' baseline safeguard. Without an active frost-ward or winter gear: DC 16 Constitution save every 10 minutes; failure = 2d6 cold damage + one level of Exhaustion as frost freezes "blood-resonance" and locks joints. See [[TheNightfort]].

## Custom Mechanic 1: The Sovereign Shadow Tracker (Dignitas & Leverage)

Numeric model of Visenya's clandestine control of Planetos infrastructure — see [[SovereignShadowTracker]].

**Formula**: `SP = (Active Spies × 2) + (Blackmail Caches × 10) + (Subverted Officers × 15) − (Public Disclosures × 20)`

| Tier | SP Range | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–35 | The Ghost in the Ledger | +2 Stealth & Deception; factions treat her as anonymous merchant asset |
| 2 | 36–75 | The Unseen Thumb | Controls secondary port-tariffs; intercepts Black/Green council comms; +3 passive Insight; retinue actions cost 50% less gold |
| 3 | 76–99 | The Sovereign Eclipse | Owns local bureaucracy; forge execution warrants / redirect supply trains / move Gold Cloak cohorts on DC 15 Deception |
| 4 | 100 | The Terminal Administrator | "Absolute reality lock" — DC to subvert any mortal lord drops to baseline 5 |

Note the explicitly game-adminstrative framing at Tier 4 ("Admin", "reality lock") — consistent with the Visenya line's hidden-apex power fantasy.

## Custom Mechanic 2: The Draconic Command & Scarcity Ledger

Metabolic/logistical subsystem for the dragon [[Vermithrax]] — see [[DraconicScarcityLedger]]. Visenya is a **Level 6 baseline outsider**, so the dragon operates under resource limits and vocal command checks:

- **Command Check**: bonus action, vocal High Valyrian command; Charisma (Animal Handling) with a flat +4 from the **Sovereior Singularity** trait [sic — likely "Sovereign Singularity"]; base DC 14 for complex maneuvers or non-infantry targeting (e.g. scorpion batteries).
- **Logistical Upkeep**: 1,000 gp/month to feed and hide a Medium-sized dragon.
- **Famine Inversion**: Treasury at 0 at end of an operational loop → *Famine of Fire*: Vermithrax breaks containment to hunt smallfolk cattle; −20 regional popularity/stability; triggers a localized counter-audit by Prince [[JacaerysVelaryon|Jacaerys]]'s investigators.

## Relation to the Visenya campaign line

This is v8 of the Visenya series (v2 Slaver's Bay, v3 Dragonstone/Sowing, v4 Dunk & Egg previously ingested). It shares the Sowing-era Dance framing with v3 but names the dragon **Vermithrax** (v3 centered on claiming **Vermithor**) and pegs Visenya at Level 6 baseline. Mechanics rhyme with the wider corpus: [[SovereignShadowTracker]] is the Westeros analogue of [[SirensLeverage]]-style leverage economies and the [[AcademyBlackmailFactory]] blackmail-cache pattern.
