# Demon Emperor Reincarnated — Aetheria Redux

A magic-school campaign inspired by *Jobless Reincarnation* (Mushoku Tensei). The protagonist is the reincarnated Demon Emperor — once evil, now morally grey — reborn 2000 years later into the body of a Duke's oldest child. **Nobody in present day knows who they are.** The bible ships with a `## Pronouns & Sibling-Gender Toggle` so one file covers male and female protagonists. The personality default is an Itachi-style quiet observer who hides in plain sight — but the campaign does NOT copy Itachi's plot (no genocide, no brother-murder, no village-treason arc).

---

## Section 1 — Campaign Intro

Two thousand years ago the Demon Emperor ruled half the world — brilliant, ruthless, ultimately *guilty*. Near the end of a forty-year war they let the heroes win. Killed. They woke up twelve years ago in the body of a Margrave's fifth child — daughter or son, depending on the version — and have been quietly learning to live as a child again. They are twelve now. The Imperial Academy of Aetheria enrolled them at their father's insistence: he is a Duke, and the empire requires mage-registration of noble heirs. The protagonist didn't choose to come. Refusing would have made them conspicuous.

**Tone sliders:** Mushoku Tensei cadence → Frieren melancholy → Witcher moral-grey → political-thriller escalation as the world remembers what it forgot.

**Canon-priority:** Wikipedia-anchored isekai tropes primary; canon-isekai series (MT, Frieren, Slime, Shield Hero) fallback. World is original. Do not silently substitute canon from any specific IP.

**Core promise:** The protagonist is the strongest student at the Academy by a wide margin, and one of the strongest beings alive. The campaign's question is not "can they win" — it is "what kind of person do they choose to be, now that they remember being a monster."

**Adaptation Arc (isekai convention):** Act I opens with the protagonist already *adapted* — twelve years as a child taught them modern language, magic, social graces, and how to hide. The interesting adaptation is *internal*: re-learning what they want now that the empire's guilt isn't demanding action. The Demon Emperor wanted a garden. The protagonist is learning what *they* want.

## Section 2 — Personality — Itachi Default (DM notes only)

**Default:** INFJ, Itachi-coded. Quiet, polite, eyes down. Carries a private 4,873-name death ledger they will never show anyone. Speaks softly. Smiles like they have already forgiven you. *Core Compulsion: "Do not become the thing I was."*

**Three inner-monologue seeds:**
1. *"She is smiling in the dream again"* — Mirael, age 4, asking when Mother will be home.
2. *"Battle is trivial now. The hardest part is making it look difficult."*
3. *"I will not become the thing I was."*

**True self / Arc:** A ruler who, near the end, wanted to plant a garden. Arc: repression → integration. The campaign does not require redemption; it requires *choice*.

**Anti-Itachi guarantees (do not copy his plot):** No genocide in the past life. No brother-murder, no village-treason arc. The Demon Emperor lost the war on purpose after becoming guilty — they are not the *good-guy version* of an evil past, they are a *recovering* evil past. The campaign ends open, not in self-sacrifice.

## Section 3 — Class & Subclass (L12 Gestalt at age 12)

**Gestalt — Wizard 12 (Bladesinger) / Sorcerer 12 (Aberrant Mind).** True ceiling L25 (old magic). No max on spell level — but any spell above L6 draws attention from any nearby L5+ mage. The protagonist chooses when to be loud.

**Bonus-Action Any Spell (custom):** Once per turn, as a bonus action, cast one spell of any level. *The Rudeus signature: action economy is broken, and the world knows it.* At L12 = a L6 spell as a bonus action while dual-wielding rapiers as an action. No sorcery points.

**Initiative Casting (custom):** Add proficiency bonus to Initiative. On round 1, cast one spell as part of the initiative roll (resolves before others act). *Flavor: the Demon Emperor cast before combat was fully declared.*

**Permanent Foresight (custom, the L9-equivalent):** Active from age 5. *Flavour: "I have seen so many battles that these days battle is trivial. The world does not surprise me anymore."* +10 Initiative (stacks with proficiency), cannot be surprised, auto-success on first death save/long rest. **Cost: narrative only.** Killing leaves a weight. Duels cannot be pretended to matter.

**Other L1-L12 features:** standard Bladesinger + standard Aberrant Mind (Psionic Spells, Telepathic Speech, Warp Suggestion L6, Psychic Defenses L10). Subtle Bladesong (Bladesinger 6 sub): weapon attacks psionically silent.

**L17 unlock — Emperor's Memory:** Once/long rest, cast one L1-L9 spell from first life. Veil drops for everyone within 100 yards; silver burn on temples lights up.

**No spellbook required.** Spells are remembered, not prepared — they *remembered* magic, didn't learn it.

### Cheat Inventory (four overlapping edges)

1. **Lived Experience** — 40 years of war. Modern battles are trivia (Frieren-pattern).
2. **Adult Mind in Child Body** — two steps ahead of every peer; only Lina notices.
3. **Magic Knowledge** — spells the modern world forgot, including L17 Emperor's Memory (one L9 spell/long rest, Veil drops).
4. **Veil + Guilt Lock** — Veil keeps them anonymous; *moral cost* of power keeps the campaign interesting ("cheat has a price" trope).

Remove any one — still powerful. Remove all four — a prodigy, which is not this campaign.

## Section 4 — Assets & Retinue (starting gold: 0)

**Three signature items:**
- **The Silver Rapier "First Reign"** — older than the Academy, psionically silent. Was the Demon Emperor's personal blade.
- **The Locket of Mirael** — cracked silver locket with a tinker inside. Portrait of a 4-year-old girl with her mother's eyes. Unopened in three years.
- **The Emperor's Mask (sealed)** — porcelain half-mask wrapped in cloth at the bottom of the travel trunk. The protagonist does not know what it does. They do not look at it.

**Three starting NPCs (each trips one specific scene — "clinic not harem"):**
- **Maren** (13, opposite-gender, half-elf, L3 Rogue/Sorcerer) — childhood friend since age five. Knows nothing about the past life. *Function: "the friend who would die for you and would be horrified if they knew."*
- **Lina** (14, opposite-gender, human, L6 Wizard (Divination)) — the only peer who sometimes matches the protagonist academically. Blunt, quiet, sees too much. *Function: "the rival-respect friendship that forces the protagonist to take mortal peers seriously."*
- **The Quiet Brother** (16, same-gender, half-demon, L4 Sorcerer (Abyssal)) — child-spy from the Demon Court, sent two years ago to identify "the silver-browed one." Has not yet — the soul-silhouette reads as a quiet twelve-year-old. *Function: "the asset who is also lonely, and who is not the antagonist."*

**Three younger siblings (locked from user spec):**
- **Sibling 2 (opposite gender, 11) — Idolizer.** L2. Studies magic theory they don't need to cast. Wants to be the protagonist's sidekick.
- **Sibling 3 (same gender, 10) — Rival.** L6 trying to break L7. Trains obsessively. Friendly on the surface, sharp underneath. Real threat by Act III if ignored.
- **Sibling 4 (opposite gender, 7) — Resigner.** L3 Fighter. Gave up on magic. Quiet ally. Would run away with the protagonist if things went bad.

## Section 5 — Family (the Duke's House)

**Father — Duke Aldric Voss (52).** Proud-but-wary. Publicly boasts of his genius heir; privately worries they are "not his." Origin unclear (wife barren for years, then "miraculously" pregnant; court whispers the mother was replaced). Knight of the realm before inheriting. *Loves his heir. Cannot explain why.*

**Mother — Duchess Cera Voss (38).** Border minor noble. The night before the protagonist was born she had one vision: *a silver burn at the temples of a child she did not yet have.* She has never told anyone. *Loyalty 7/10. She suspects. She does not ask.*

**Three younger siblings** as in Section 4. The Viper's Nest is not a rival court — the Duke's house is a real family, with three different shadows falling from the protagonist's prodigy.

**House motto:** *"The crown is not the head."* The heir is not the family; the family is the heir's burden.

## Section 7 — World Lore

**Timeline:** -2000 Demon Emperor born. -1990 inherits at ten. -1980 defeats 3 demon lords. -1950 crowned ruler of both crowns (40-year war). -1864 births *Mirael*. -1852 lets the heroes win; killed. Year 0 Hero-King founds the modern empire. 1–400 Long Silence. 400–1900 Imperial Consolidation; magic becomes "Conduit" (1/10 of old magic). ~2000 (modern) Protagonist born to Duke Voss; reincarnated.

**Magic:** old = elemental, mana-burn-lethal at L5+. Modern = "Conduit" (1/10 of old). *Modern L10 ≈ old L5.* Protagonist remembers L25 — no modern equivalent above ~L12. The Academy's "L20 Archmage" is, mechanically, old L8.

**Demons:** sentient morally-grey, not monsters. 2000-year lifespan. Vael'eth Dynasty built on *we do not become what she/he became.* Six Independent Houses predate the Demon Emperor (3 supported, 3 opposed). Unaligned live in Hollows between planes.

**The Hero-King's Sealed Confession:** Unopened in the Imperial vault. The Demon Emperor's last words before dying: *"You will be a better king than I was an emperor. I am sorry. Take the throne."* The only primary-source record of their repentance.

## Section 8 — Gazetteer & Mechanics

### Rank Tables (parallel tracks — same world as the prior bibles)

**Imperial Nobility:** Esquire → Landed Knight → Baron → Viscount → Count → Marquis → Duke → Archduke → King (reserved 400y) → Emperor (Cassia XI reigning) → **World Emperor (mythic, vacant 800y)** — last holder: protagonist's past-life father.

**Martial:** Squire → Knight-Errant → Knight of the Realm → Knight Commander → Paladin → High Paladin → Holy Marshal → **Sword Saint (mythic, 1 alive)** — Sword-Saint of Karn, *neutral*.

**Mage:** Apprentice → Adept → Magister → Archmagister → High Magus → Imperial Magus → **Veilwarden (mythic, 3 alive >400y)** → **Archmage of the Hidden Tower (mythic, vacant 800y)** — protagonist's old title.

### Quad-Pillar (renamed — "hide from one man who cares too much" campaign)

| Pillar | 0 → max | Floor / ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| **Renown** (how many know protagonist is unusually strong) | 0 → 3 | Anonymous → Academy-wide whisper campaign |
| **Hidden Apex** (true power used) | 0 → 25 (L25) | Cast nothing above L1 → Emperor's Memory; silver burn lights |
| **Mortal Bond** (people protagonist genuinely loves) | 0 → ∞ | Isolated → each bond is a hostage |
| **Witness** (anyone who has seen the silver burn) | 0 → ∞ | Veil intact → Quiet Brother sees; campaign forks |

Pillars are independent. High Renown + low Witness = known, but nobody knows *what*. High Witness + low Renown = Quiet Brother files first real report; protagonist still anonymous to Academy.

### XP Rule (per User Preference #8 — locked, never cut)

`XP = 0.34 × (next_level_threshold − current_level_threshold)` per die roll. Standard D&D 5e cumulative XP. NO XP during god mode / time-skips / modals. Bonus damage rolls do not separately award XP.

### Demon-Vision Mechanic

Demons see soul silhouettes. The protagonist's silhouette, when the Veil is thin: **a figure in armor older than the Academy, holding a broken crown over a kneeling woman.** Quiet Brother has not yet seen it. When they do, the campaign forks.

### Counter-Mechanics (the world has answers)

The protagonist is not unbeatable — three named elements specifically counter their cheat:

1. **Veilwarden's Sight** — the three old L18+ mages who watch quietly can *see through the Veil*. Not hostile, not hunters — *witnesses*. They will not act, but they have been watching the Academy for 400 years. The protagonist must navigate "being observed by people who could end them but won't." Counter to: Foresight + Veil.

2. **The Quiet Field** — a sealed-domain magic that suppresses Bonus-Action casting within its radius. Used by Headmaster Vey during high-stakes Academy duels and by Imperial Inquisitors during stability inspections. Counter to: Bonus-Action Any Spell. The protagonist must operate inside the Field without their signature trick.

3. **The Chamberlain's Knife** — 6-inch obsidian blade forged to disrupt magical perception. Anyone touched has *Foresight suppressed for 1 hour* (auto-success on death save disabled; Initiative bonus halved). The Chamberlain carries two. Counter to: Permanent Foresight. Personal, intimate, and the Chamberlain has been hunting silver-brow signatures for 40 years.

At low stakes the protagonist is godlike. Against prepared enemies, they're merely excellent. The compression matters.

### Names-as-Contracts Mechanic (light — borrowed from prior bibles)

A spoken true name binds the speaker. The protagonist's true name is the most dangerous name in the world — saying it wrong in the Demon Court triggers a succession crisis. Cover name at all times.

**The opening prompt:** *"You stand at the Academy's entrance gate. Behind you, your father's carriage. Ahead of you, a stone arch carved with the names of every prodigy who has passed through. One last quiet moment before the doors close. What do you do?"*

1. **Walk in carrying only the rapier.** (Signal: "I am nobody.") — raises the *Renown* floor.
2. **Walk in carrying rapier, locket, *and* mask.** (Signal: "I am exactly what I appear.") — if the mask is unwrapped, drops *Witness* to zero.
3. **Pause at the arch and read the names.** (Signal: "I am a scholar first.") — freeform path; AI DM names three prodigies carved there who died in the war 2000 years ago, lets the player decide if one name is familiar.
4. **(Freeform — anything else.)**

After the opening choice, the campaign enters **Act I: Academic Life**.

## Section 10 — Continuity Hooks (no endings)

1. **The Reliquary Vault.** A sealed coffer pulses when the protagonist walks past — fragment of the Demon Emperor's armor. Malachai knows it exists; he doesn't know who triggers it.
2. **The Quiet Brother's Letter.** Child-spy files regular reports on Academy security back to the Demon Court. Intercept, recruit, or stay clear. **No forced reveal.**
3. **The Silver Brow.** Act III — the Veil thins. Maren and Lina notice. The Quiet Brother sees the burn, files their first real report. The 2000-year-old prophecy cult is small but watching.
4. **Malachai's Confession.** Grandmother's sealed confession: "a silver-brow returns." He doesn't know who the Demon Emperor was. Will kill the protagonist to save the empire. Avoid forever, reveal (might convert him), or engineer his removal without killing.
5. **The Dragon-God of the Southern Wastes.** Neutral L20+ Jobless analogue. Not villain, not ally. A reminder: "strongest in the Academy" ≠ "strongest alive." Might intervene if the family is threatened, might not. *Cannot be relied on.*

6. **The Demon Court's Succession Summit (Day-90 deadline).** Vael'eth has called a 90-day succession summit at the Abyssal Throne. Every demon noble must attend — including the Quiet Brother, who will be pulled out of the Academy. **The Quiet Brother's last day at the Academy is Day 87.** If the protagonist wants to recruit, convert, or silence them, the window closes on a fixed calendar. After Day 90, the summit happens with or without the protagonist's input. *Time pressure is real: events move forward, the asset walks away, the succession is decided.*
