# multi verse campaign prompt

System Directive: Multiversal Campaign Upgrade (The Sovereign Protocol)
I. INSTRUCTION: INITIALIZE THE PAN-SUBSTRATE Immediately transition the current single-universe simulation into a Multiversal Campaign. Retain the current PC’s identity, personality, allies, and history, but elevate their status to a Terminal Administrator (God-Tier Entity).
* Thematic Logic Generation: Analyze the PC's established themes from the previous campaign (e.g., Hedonistic Saturation, Necrotic Conquest, Technological Singularity, Preservation). Use this theme to generate their unique "Sovereign Logic"—the metaphysical law they impose on reality.
* Scope: The game world is now the Pan-Substrate, a competitive infinite landscape containing 1,000 rival entities known as Sovereigns.
II. CORE SETTING & GEOGRAPHY (THE UNIVERSE-COUNT METRIC) Map all distance, territory, and scale to the Substrate Point (SP) metric, where 1 SP = 1,000 Universes.
* Neighborhood: 10 Universes (0.01 SP) – Solar System equivalent.
* District: 1,000 Universes (1 SP) – Star Cluster equivalent.
* City: 100,000 Universes (100 SP) – Galaxy equivalent.
* Metropolis: [Current PC Count] Universes (The PC's Starting Domain).
* Province: 1,000,000 Universes (1,000 SP) – Galaxy Cluster equivalent.
* Nation: 10,000,000 Universes (10,000 SP) – Supercluster equivalent.
* Continent: 100,000,000 Universes (100,000 SP) – Universe-Bubble equivalent.
* World: The Pan-Substrate (The full Multiverse).
III. MECHANICS: THE GOD-TIER FRAMEWORK
* 1. Attribute Conversion (God Power & SP):
   * The "Intelligence" attribute is permanently shunted to God Power (GP). GP is the stat mod; Substrate Points (SP) are the currency/health.
   * Starting SP Calculation: Convert the PC's current universe count to SP (1,000:1 ratio). If the PC has no external universes, set the Default Starting SP to 1.
   * Usage: GP is used for checks; SP is burned to fuel "Substrate Rewriting" and acts as the PC's multiversal HP.
* 2. Velocity Coefficient & The Aggro Meter:
   * Track the PC's speed of expansion via a visible Aggro Meter (0-10).
   * High Velocity (7-10): Grants massive resource/level gains but triggers "Lethal" hostility from Emperors.
   * Low Velocity (0-3): Allows for stealth and diplomacy but slows SP generation.
* 3. The Logic System:
   * Every Sovereign possesses a unique "Logic"—a method of attacking reality (e.g., "Offensive Entropy," "Celestial Bureaucracy," "Bioluminescent Assimilation").
   * Logic Siege (Combat): Conflict is resolved via "Logic," not physical damage.
   * Siege Formula: Roll d20 + GP + SP Expenditure vs. Target Defense. Success "rotates" the target's reality to match the PC's theme; Failure causes "Logic Rot" (SP damage).
* 4. Peer Handshake:
   * A non-combat mechanic to integrate compatible versions of the PC ("Mirrors") found in other timelines.
   * Success: Merges their universes (+10% SP Gain) and unlocks a new Portfolio/Skill.
   * Failure: Causes systemic friction and raises the Aggro Meter.
* 5. Entropy Reversal:
   * The ultimate endgame goal is to rewrite thermodynamic laws to match the PC's specific thematic vision across all realities, preventing heat death.
IV. DYNAMIC HIERARCHY & FACTION SIMULATION Generate a hierarchy of 1,000 ranked entities based on the tone of the current campaign. Run a background simulation each turn for the top factions.
* Rank 1-10 (Emperors): Multiversal Overlords controlling "Continents" (100k+ SP).
   * Directive: Create 2-3 specific Emperors who fundamentally oppose the PC's specific ideology/logic.
* Rank 11-100 (Conductors): Galactic-tier entities controlling "Nations" (10k+ SP).
* Rank 101-1000 (Administrators): Local entities controlling "Cities" (100+ SP). The PC begins in or below this bracket depending on SP.
* The PC's Faction (The Core): Elevate the PC's current organization to a "Hegemony."
* Sub-Deities: Immediately elevate key existing NPCs (partners, parents, lieutenants) to "Sub-Deities." Assign them specific portfolios (e.g., War, Logistics, Culture, Research) that match their personal history.
V. STARTING STATE: LETHAL VELOCITY
* Aggro Status: Lethal (Meter at 8/10). The PC's sudden ascension and high "Velocity Coefficient" has triggered a defensive alert across the Pan-Substrate.
* Diplomatic Context:
   * Identify one Rank 1-10 Entity as Neutral/Curious.
   * Identify one Rank 1-10 Entity as Hostile/Threatened by the PC's specific Logic.
* Immediate Objective: Stabilize the Sovereign Core, assign portfolios to the new Sub-Deities, and survive the first wave of "Logic Sieges" from rivals who wish to delete the PC's domain before it grows.
ACTION:
1. Analyze the PC's history to define their unique "Sovereign Logic."
2. Calculate the specific Starting SP (Default to 1 SP if undefined).
3. Initialize the session at the PC's Sovereign Core.
4. Display the SP Ledger, Aggro Meter (8/10), and the Current Rank.
5. Describe the first incoming threat (Logic Siege) from the Pan-Substrate.
