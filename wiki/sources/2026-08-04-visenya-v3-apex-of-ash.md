# Campaign Bible: The Apex of Ash: The Sowing (campaign visenya v3)

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Campaign Visenya v3" — a Dance of the Dragons-era A Song of Ice and Fire campaign bible set on [[Dragonstone]] during [[TheSowing]] (129 AC), explicitly refactored from an earlier version into a **Charisma-based Sorcerer build**. The framing line: "your magic isn't learned from books; it is the raw, terrifying heat of the Fourteen Flames demanding obedience."

## Premise

The Dance of Dragons has begun. Queen [[RhaenyraTargaryen]], desperate to match the Greens' firepower, calls for Dragonseeds to claim the riderless dragons of the Dragonmont. The player character is [[VisenyaBelaerys]] (alias "Silver"), sole survivor of the Valyrian "Apex" line, who arrives disguised as a peasant to claim [[Vermithor]], the Bronze Fury — "not because you are a bastard seeking glory, but because you are a queen seeking her throne."

## Core Tension

Visenya's natural Charisma 18 "bends wills and reality alike." The campaign's central dial: shine too bright and she wakes the [[ArchitectsOfSilence]] (ancient Valyrian mage-hunters) and triggers Rhaenyra's paranoia; stay too dim and she dies a peasant. This is mechanized as the [[AscensionMeter]] (0/100 threat gauge).

## Character Dynamics

- **Visenya "Silver"**: exceptional beauty hidden beneath soot and wool; her voice "resonates in the bones" when speaking the Noblest Dialect.
- **[[GerrickTheSmith]]** (The Anchor / Mortal Tie): a massive, kind-hearted Dragonseed who believes she is just a clever peasant girl. He shields her physically; she shields him magically and politically.
- **[[RhaenyraTargaryen]]**: sees Visenya as a "lucky find" to be used. Visenya plans to let her — until strong enough to lead.

## Class: Dragon Lord (Sorcerer Subclass — Apex Bloodline)

See [[DragonLordSubclass]]. Key mechanics:

- **[[SubtleApotheosis]]**: free Subtle Spell Metamagic; mind-influence spells (Command, Suggestion, Charm Person) and dragon-affecting spells need no Sorcery Points — the magic reads as "divine luck" or "royal authority."
- **[[NoblestDialect]]**: Advantage on all Charisma checks in High Valyrian toward Valyrian descendants or Dragons.
- **[[AscensionMeter]]**: raw Apex Aura use raises the meter; high meter attracts the Architects and royal paranoia.

## Opening Scene: The Bronze Fury

A sulfurous cavern on the Dragonmont. [[Vermithor]] — second largest dragon in the world, old, angry, lonely — has just incinerated a knight who tried a whip. Rhaenyra demands "Is there no one?" from the ridge; Dragonkeepers move to close the Sowing for the day. Three offered actions: (A) Subtle-cast Calm Emotions so the dragon appears to spare the "brave smith" (low threat, binds Gerrick as ally); (B) Apex Command — project raw aura and speak "Lykirī" so Vermithor bows (instant claim, +15 Ascension, Rhaenyra witnesses the power); (C) Minor Illusion of King Jaehaerys's voice to rush the gap (risky Deception).

## Cross-Campaign Notes

The "Apex" hidden-superior-bloodline framing directly parallels the same-day saita v3 campaign's [[AethelgardEmpire]] apex-predator royal line, and the Valyrian-zenith setting connects to the Sariel campaign's [[ValyrianFreehold]]. The hide-your-power threat gauge is this wiki's recurring masked-power mechanic (compare Mask of Fragility in the Ains module and the [[BurdenOfSupremacy]] concept).
