# Campaign bg1 nocturne — "The Serpent Queen" character profile

**Source**: Campaign setup document "campaign bg1 nocturne" — a Level 1 character profile that ports the Nocturne serpent-mastermind persona into the [[BaldursGate]] opening. The player character is [[SerpentQueen|the Serpent Queen]] (real name hidden/Charname), a 16-year-old Bard (future [[CollegeOfSwordsShadowBuild|College of Swords]]) living as a scribe-acolyte in [[Candlekeep]] on the Sword Coast, foster daughter of [[Gorion]] and an implied carrier of the [[BhaalspawnEssence]].

## Character architecture

- **Concealed asset**: jarring symmetrical beauty deliberately hidden under grime, rough acolyte robes, and a sullen withdrawn demeanor — a "mundane illusion" making her a forgettable ghost among the stacks.
- **Composite villain archetype** ([[SerpentQueenArchetype]]): explicitly named blend of Griffith (transcendent ambition), Tywin Lannister (cold strategic pragmatism), [[SosukeAizen|Aizen]] (intellectual/charismatic superiority), and Johan Liebert (philosophical nihilistic evil).
- **[[TwoMasksArchitecture]]** — two perfectly-fitted outer personas concealing the true self:
  1. *The Dutiful Ward & Courteous Advisor* — soft musical voice that forces listeners to lean in, flawless etiquette; every kindness (copying scrolls, mending bindings, humming) is a **data probe** testing another personality's weaknesses and leverage points.
  2. *The Dispassionate Strategist* — presents win-win interlocking plans aligned with allies' stated goals while banking an unstated private benefit; allies are scaffolding "destined to be dismantled the instant their utility expires," with no apology and no remorse.

## Core drives

1. **Personal Transcendence** — rejects worldly power (land/titles/armies = messy emotional variables) in favor of [[PortableAbsolutePower]]: forbidden knowledge in Candlekeep's catacombs, the Bhaalspawn bloodline, pure treasury potential. End goal: not to rule from a heavenly throne but to *become* the throne — a new foundation for a reality of her own making.
2. **Surgical Domination** — no desire for public queenship; the thrill is the singular perfect moment of frictionless control. "A whispered *Suggestion* that makes a hero kneel for a single, soul-crushing heartbeat is infinitely more satisfying than sitting on a throne for a year."
3. **Supremacist Certainty** — views allies and enemies alike with the detached amusement of a scholar observing "fascinating but limited fauna"; listens to Harpers ([[TheHarpers]]), [[Zhentarim]], and [[Gorion]]'s moral lectures as data, not persuasion.

## Relationship to the wider Nocturne mythos

This is another instance of the [[CrossPlatformAscensionPattern]]: the same serpent-mastermind chassis previously instantiated as [[NocturneSosuke]] (Bleach-verse ascendant), [[SosukeAizen|Aizen Sosuke]] (Thay ancestor-patron sequel), and [[Alexiel]] (SWTOR "Siren" and Assiah campaigns), here re-seated at Level 1 in the canonical BG1 opening. Distinctives of this port: divinity is *inherited* (Bhaalspawn essence) rather than engineered or consumed, and the concealment layer is mundane (grime and demeanor) rather than magical — a low-level analogue of [[MaskOfFragility]]-style deception mechanics.
