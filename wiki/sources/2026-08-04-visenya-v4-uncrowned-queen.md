# Campaign Bible: Visenya Targaryen v4 — The Uncrowned Queen

**Source**: Campaign setup document "Campaign Visenya v4" — an updated, highly detailed character profile for [[VisenyaTargaryen]], integrating [[HouseBaelarys]] lore, her "Apex" bloodline, and the secret cosmic weight she carries while traveling with [[SerDuncanTheTall]] in the Dunk & Egg era of Westeros (the Road to Ashford, 209 AC).

## Identity

- **True identity**: Daughter of Prince [[MaekarTargaryen]], older sister to [[AegonTargaryenEgg|Aegon \"Egg\" Targaryen]]. Age 16.
- **Secret heritage**: Last scion of [[HouseBaelarys]], a "Top 5" family of Old Valyria known as God-Mages or Apex Conductors — where Targaryens were dragonlords, the Baelarys were the architects who *bound* the dragons. Her blood is [[ApexBloodline|\"Apex\"]]: preternatural geometric perfection and magical resonance that makes standard Targaryens feel like "shadows."
- **Appearance**: Ethereal "ultraviolet" beauty; unmasked, her silver-gold hair generates its own light and her violet eyes hold "crystalline" or "surgical" clarity. She suppresses this "Aura of the Goddess" behind a [[MortalMask]] to pass as a mortal princess — or, currently, a squire named "Silver."

## Personality: The Sovereign & The Girl

Visenya's core tension is a [[SovereignGirlDuality|profound duality]]:

**The Sovereign (The Apex)**
- *Cold Geometry*: the world as equations — people are "variables," conversations "conductions," peace a "structure" to build. Willing to be ruthless ("the Silver Butcher") for stability.
- *[[SilverTongue|The Silver Tongue]]*: charisma so high it functions like mind control (Glibness); she gaslights kings and Hands into believing her "White Lies" are their own ideas.
- *Cosmic Dread*: she knows of the Great Other beyond the Wall and R'hllor in the East, and believes she must hollow out and unite the world under absolute rule to survive the coming Long Night.

**The Girl (The Ditchbond)**
- *Desperate Humanity*: Apex nature creates agonizing distance from humanity; she craves simple honest connection ([[Ditchbond|the \"Ditchbond\"]]) with people like Dunk who anchor her to the earth.
- *Protective Love*: loves Maekar, Baelor, Egg, and Daeron fiercely — but protects them by manipulating them, casting herself as "the Furnace that must burn so they can stay warm."

## Background: The Baelarys Legacy

- **The mother's secret**: Visenya's mother was a [[HouseBaelarys]] survivor who escaped the Doom carrying the secrets of the Fourteen Flames; she died of grief/sickness, leaving Visenya sole inheritor of the Apex blood.
- **[[HousePeake]]'s betrayal**: the Peakes destroyed the last Baelarys enclave in Westeros — not understanding the magic, only fearing the "silver-gold sickness" and coveting the land. Visenya has systematically destroyed them in retaliation, reducing them to vassals.
- **Family dynamic**: [[MaekarTargaryen]] loves but fears her ("the Sun" to his "Anvil"); [[BaelorTargaryen]] respects her intellect but suspects she is a tyrant stripping the world of free will ("A Goddess's Playground"); [[AerionTargaryen]]'s madness is a reaction to sensing he is an echo of her fire; [[DaeronTargaryen]] drinks to drown his dragon dreams and is her most loyal shield — she alone comforts him.

## Mechanics: Level 6 Baelarys Weaver (Modified Swords Bard)

Low-magic setting: her "spells" are flavored as [[AncestralResonance]] — manipulating the "marrow" of the world through voice and will.

- **Stats**: INT 20 (Architect/Scholar), CHA 18 (Silver Tongue), DEX 16 (Feline Grace).
- **Blade Flourish** → "Geometric Combat": calculating the opponent's blade trajectory and stepping where it won't be.
- **Silver Tongue** (Expertise in Persuasion): words carry subsonic resonance, landing like laws of nature.
- **Apex Presence**: dropping the [[MortalMask]] terrifies enemies into submission without a drawn weapon (mechanically Command / Fear / Hypnotic Pattern).
- **"White Lie" kit**: Glibness (passive flavor), Detect Thoughts ("Royal Insight" — micro-expressions), Suggestion ("Voice of the Dragon"), Mending/Prestidigitation ("The Polish" — keeping her and Dunk's gear unnaturally perfect).

## Current Campaign Status

- **Setting**: The Road to Ashford, 209 AC.
- **Cover**: traveling incognito as "Silver," a squire to [[SerDuncanTheTall]].
- **Objective**: help Dunk win the Ashford tourney — not for glory, but to prove a "True Knight" can exist.
